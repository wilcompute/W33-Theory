\section{Projector correspondence, completed defect, and compact-resolvent frontier}

Let $A$ be the adjacency operator of $W(3,3)$. Its primitive rational
projectors are
\[
P_{12}=J/40,\qquad
P_2=\frac23I+\frac16A-\frac1{15}J,\qquad
P_{-4}=\frac13I-\frac16A+\frac1{24}J.
\]
They have ranks $1,24,15$. Hence the nonconstant cohomological packet
\[
(\mathbb Q^2\otimes\operatorname{im}P_2)
\oplus
(\mathbb Q^2\otimes\operatorname{im}P_{-4})
\]
has rank $48+30=78$. Multiplication by $120$ clears the denominators of both
nonconstant projectors. This is an explicit W33 projector-level
correspondence; it is not yet a map from the independent Pass-637 conductor
module.

For a critical-line zero pair $\frac12\pm i\gamma$, put
$x=\frac12$ and $h=2\delta$. Its contribution to the second difference of
$H=-\xi'/\xi$ at $1$ is
\[
\frac{4h^2x(3\gamma^2+h^2-x^2)}
{(\gamma^2+x^2)(\gamma^2+(x-h)^2)(\gamma^2+(x+h)^2)}.
\]
This is positive for every actual nontrivial ordinate and
$0<|\delta|<1/2$. Thus RH implies positivity of the completed Casey defect,
subject to the usual justified symmetric Hadamard summation. The converse is
false: an off-line reflected quartet can also have positive values for this
single scalar functional.

Define
\[
\Xi(z)=\xi\!\left(\frac12+iz\right),\qquad
E_{11}(z)=\Xi(z)+\frac{i}{\log 11}\Xi'(z).
\]
Global Hermite--Biehler status of this one fixed function would force the zeros
of $\Xi$ to be real and is therefore an RH-level target. Finite grid tests do
not establish the global inequality.

For the elliptic packet
\[
M_W=H^1(E_2)^{\oplus24}\oplus H^1(E_{-4})^{\oplus15},
\]
modularity gives the degree-$78$ automorphic product
\[
L(M_W,s)=L(E_2,s)^{24}L(E_{-4},s)^{15}.
\]
Its local factor at $11$ is the nontrivial W33 Ihara polynomial, but this
degree-$78$ Euler product cannot be identified directly with the degree-one
Riemann zeta function.

Finally, the leading Riemann--von Mangoldt inverse
\[
t_n=\frac{2\pi(n-7/8)}{W((n-7/8)/e)}
\]
defines a compact-resolvent Weyl scaffold with the correct $T\log T$ counting
class. Repeating every level with all $39$ nonconstant W33 states would
multiply the zero density by $39$; W33 must therefore enter as an internal
fiber or interaction rather than as independent spectral multiplicity.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
These statements do not prove classical RH, global Hermite--Biehler status,
or an adelic transfer from the W33 elliptic packet to completed $\zeta$.
