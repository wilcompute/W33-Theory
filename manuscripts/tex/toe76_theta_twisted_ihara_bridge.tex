\section*{The native eleven-site theta graph, twisted Ihara zeta,
  and the two regular W(3,3) graph-RH scales}
\noindent\textbf{Scope.}
The collinearity graph, the Levi incidence graph, and the eleven-site
theta subgraph are three distinct graphs.  Only the first two are
regular Ramanujan graphs.  None of these theorems implies the classical
Riemann hypothesis.

\paragraph{The two regular parents.}
The collinearity graph is the strongly regular graph
\(\operatorname{srg}(40,12,2,4)\), with adjacency spectrum
\(12^1,2^{24},(-4)^{15}\).  Its Ihara inverse is
\[
Z_{40}(u)^{-1}
=(1-u^2)^{200}(1-u)(1-11u)
 (1-2u+11u^2)^{24}(1+4u+11u^2)^{15}.
\]
The nonprincipal poles have modulus \(11^{-1/2}\), so this
12-regular graph is Ramanujan and satisfies graph RH.

The Levi point--line incidence graph has 80 vertices, 160 edges,
regular degree four, and adjacency spectrum
\[
4^1,(-4)^1,(\sqrt6)^{24},(-\sqrt6)^{24},0^{30}.
\]
In particular \(\sqrt6<2\sqrt3\), giving a second regular
Ramanujan and graph-RH theorem, now with critical circle
\(\lvert u\rvert=3^{-1/2}\).
Its exact inverse Ihara function is
\[
\boxed{
Z_{80}(u)^{-1}
=(1-u^2)^{81}(1-9u^2)
 (1+9u^4)^{24}(1+3u^2)^{30}.}
\]
Direct sparse integer calculation on the 320 oriented Levi edges gives
\(\operatorname{tr}(B_{80}^n)=0\) for \(1\le n<8\) and
\(\operatorname{tr}(B_{80}^8)=25920=16\cdot1620\), i.e.\
1620 undirected primitive octagons.

\paragraph{The theta subgraph and its magnetic twist.}
The native \(\Theta(4,4,4)\) patch is formed by subdividing each
edge of a three-parallel-edge dipole into four edges.  It has eleven
vertices, twelve edges, three primitive octagons, two degree-three
hubs and nine degree-two vertices.  It is therefore \emph{irregular}.
Fix unitary arm phases \(1,z_1,z_2\), with
\(z_j=e^{i\phi_j}\), and define
\(s=\lvert1+z_1+z_2\rvert\).
The twisted 24-by-24 nonbacktracking Hashimoto matrix \(B_\phi\)
has the exact determinant
\[
\boxed{
\det(I-uB_\phi)=
 (1-u^8)\bigl[1+(4-s^2)u^8+4u^{16}\bigr].}
\]
Indeed, contracting the four-edge arms turns the transition operator
into the dipole's \(6\times6\) directed-edge transfer.  With
\(K=J_3-I_3\), \(Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,z_1,z_2)\),
and \(x=u^8\), its determinant is
\[
\det(I-xKZ^{-1}KZ)
=(1-x)\bigl[1+(4-s^2)x+4x^2\bigr].
\]
The same identity follows independently from the \emph{irregular}
Bass formula
\[
\det(I-uB_\phi)
=(1-u^2)^{12-11}
  \det\bigl(I-uA_\phi+u^2(D-I)\bigr).
\]
It is directly checked on all eleven vertices and 24 directed edges
in \texttt{analysis/w33\_20261014\_toe76\_theta\_twisted\_ihara.py}.

For zero twist \(s=3\),
\[
Z_{\Theta,0}(u)^{-1}=(1-u^8)^2(1-4u^8).
\]
For cube-root (120-degree) arm phases \(s=0\),
\[
\boxed{
Z_{\Theta,120^\circ}(u)^{-1}=
(1-u^8)(1+2u^8)^2.}
\]
The twisted nontrivial quadratic factor is tempered when
\(s\le 2\sqrt2\), in which case its roots have
\(\lvert u\rvert=2^{-1/8}\); this is the regular
three-edge dipole's twisted \(2\sqrt2\) bound after
the substitution \(t=u^4\).  It is \emph{not}
the main W33 circle \(11^{-1/2}\), nor the Levi circle
\(3^{-1/2}\), and the irregular theta graph is
\emph{not} being claimed to satisfy the ordinary
regular-graph Ramanujan definition.

\paragraph{Fermion--geodesic coincidence and controls.}
The theta graph's exact noninteracting five-fermion energy
\[
E_0(s)=-\sqrt2-\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{3+s}}-
                 \sqrt{5+2\sqrt{3-s}}
\]
is minimized globally when \(s=0\), at
\((\phi_1,\phi_2)=(2\pi/3,4\pi/3)\) and its transpose.
The same phase choice sets
\(\operatorname{tr}(B_\phi^8)=8(s^2-3)=-24\),
whereas the untwisted trace is \(+48\).
Thus a common holonomy invariant controls an exact
fermion optimum and a twisted Ihara-geodesic cancellation.
The three theta octagons are only \(3/1620=1/540\)
of the Levi primitive octagons; this ratio alone
does not select a physical \(540\)-component model.

This result holds for \emph{any} equal-arm
\(\Theta(4,4,4)\), not uniquely for a W33 subgraph.
Additional W33-embedding-sensitive operators or measurements,
as well as a justified dynamical gauge sector,
are needed before claiming new fundamental physics.
