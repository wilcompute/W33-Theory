\section{The $1+24+15$ Packet, Elliptic Motive Candidate, and Completed Boundary}
\label{sec:casey_w33_motive_global}

\subsection{Primitive adjacency packets}

The Bose--Mesner algebra of the $W(3,3)$ collinearity graph is generated by
$I,A,J$ with
\[
 A^2=8I-2A+4J,\qquad AJ=JA=12J,\qquad J^2=40J.
\]
Its three primitive spectral idempotents are
\[
 P_{12}=\frac1{40}J,
\]
\[
 P_2=\frac23 I+\frac16 A-\frac1{15}J,
 \qquad
 P_{-4}=\frac13 I-\frac16 A+\frac1{24}J.
\]
They are pairwise orthogonal, sum to $I$, and have traces
\[
 \operatorname{tr}P_{12}=1,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{tr}P_2=24,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{tr}P_{-4}=15.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathbb Q^{40}=V_{12}\oplus V_2\oplus V_{-4}
\]
is a canonical automorphism-invariant decomposition.

\subsection{An explicit rank-$78$ global candidate}

Let
\[
 E_2:y^2=x^3+x-1,
 \qquad
 E_{-4}:y^2=x^3+x+2.
\]
Define the direct-sum motive candidate
\[
 M_W=H^1(E_2)^{\oplus24}\oplus H^1(E_{-4})^{\oplus15}.
\]
It has rank $2(24+15)=78$. At $p=11$ the curves have Frobenius traces
$a_{11}(E_2)=2$ and $a_{11}(E_{-4})=-4$, so
\begin{equation}
 \det(1-\operatorname{Frob}_{11}u\mid M_W)
 =(1-2u+11u^2)^{24}(1+4u+11u^2)^{15}.
 \label{eq:w33_motive_local_11}
\end{equation}
Thus the local Frobenius characteristic polynomial of $M_W$ is exactly the
nontrivial W33 Ihara inverse polynomial.

\begin{remark}[Claim boundary]
Equation~\eqref{eq:w33_motive_local_11} is an exact local realization. It does
not yet show that a variety naturally constructed from $W(3,3)$ has
cohomology $M_W$, nor that the full $L$-function of $M_W$ is Riemann's
completed zeta function.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Finite all-prime census}

For every common good prime $p\le10000$, direct point counting was performed
for both curves. The trace pair
\[
 (a_p(E_2),a_p(E_{-4}))=(2,-4)
\]
occurs only for $p=11$ in this range. Their rational $j$-invariants
$6912/31$ and $432/7$ are nonintegral, hence both curves are non-CM. The
traces differ at $p=5$, so the curves are not $\mathbb Q$-isogenous. The
normalized trace streams have empirical correlation
\[
 -0.0075713990\ldots,
\]
and their first six Sato--Tate moments agree closely with the non-CM targets.
These are finite computations, not an all-prime uniqueness theorem.

\subsection{Renormalized boundary formula}

Put $L(s)=-\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$ and $a=\sigma-\tfrac12$. The prime cocycle
\[
 \mathcal P(a,\delta)
 =L(2(a-\delta))-2L(2a)+L(2(a+\delta))
\]
converges absolutely only for $a-|\delta|>\tfrac12$. Writing
$\varepsilon=\sigma-1$, its finite part at the proposed boundary is
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{FP}\mathcal P(\delta)
 =L(1-2\delta)+L(1+2\delta)+2\gamma.
 \label{eq:prime_boundary_fp}
\end{equation}
Let
\[
 \mathcal A(s)=\frac1s+\frac1{s-1}-\frac12\log\pi
 +\frac12\psi(s/2)
\]
and
\[
 a_0=1-\frac12\log\pi-\frac\gamma2-\log2.
\]
The archimedean finite part is
\[
 \operatorname{FP}\mathcal A(\delta)
 =\mathcal A(1-2\delta)+\mathcal A(1+2\delta)-2a_0.
\]
For $H=-\xi'/\xi$, the completed difference is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_\xi(\delta)
 =H(1-2\delta)-2H(1)+H(1+2\delta)
 =\operatorname{FP}\mathcal P(\delta)
  -\operatorname{FP}\mathcal A(\delta).
 \label{eq:completed_boundary_defect}
\end{equation}
The prime and archimedean pole counterterms cancel exactly. On the registered
grid $\delta=0.001,\ldots,0.489$, the prime finite part is negative while
$\mathcal C_\xi(\delta)$ is positive and approximately quadratic. This is a
numerical coercivity certificate, not a proof of positivity for all defects.

\subsection{A fixed norm-$11$ de Branges candidate}

Let
\[
 \Xi(z)=\xi\!\left(\frac12+iz\right),
 \qquad
 c_{11}=\frac1{\log11},
 \qquad
 E_{11}(z)=\Xi(z)+ic_{11}\Xi'(z).
\]
The Hermite--Biehler gap and a normalized $12\times12$ reproducing-kernel
minor are positive on the registered upper-half-plane sample. Injecting the
off-real quartet $z=\pm14\pm0.2i$ into $\Xi$ makes the same fixed construction
fail at $z=14+0.15i$, where both the gap and kernel diagonal are negative.
Thus the finite test is sensitive, but finite sampling does not prove that
$E_{11}$ is globally Hermite--Biehler.

\subsection{Pre-registered higher-moment no-go}

A three-channel prime-indexed W33 tower with damping channels $s=2,3,4$ was
trained only on the classical moments $S_2,S_4,S_6,S_8$. Exact interpolation
requires weights
\[
 (w_2,w_3,w_4)
 =(0.0020658500\ldots,-0.1912572831\ldots,1.1891914330\ldots),
\]
so positivity fails. The untouched errors are $28.45\%$ at $S_{10}$ and
$118.66\%$ at $S_{12}$. The best positive fit already misses the training
moments and then misses $S_{10}$ and $S_{12}$ by $79.19\%$ and $308.66\%$.

Moreover, its proposed ordinates have the form
\[
 t_{n,j}=\frac{c\theta_j}{\log n},
\]
which accumulate at zero. Hence the model has no first positive ordinate and
no finite counting function below a fixed height. It cannot be a
compact-resolvent Hilbert--Polya operator.
