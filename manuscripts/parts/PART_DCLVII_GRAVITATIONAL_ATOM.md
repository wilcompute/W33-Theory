# Part DCLVII — The Gravitational Atom: W33 Black Hole Spectrum

## Setup

A black hole in W33-Theory is not a continuous object but a collection of W33 vertices. A minimal black hole occupies one vertex with the full local neighborhood.

The area spectrum of a black hole in W33-Theory follows from the Bekenstein-Mukhanov quantum:
$$A_n = k \cdot n \cdot l_{Pl}^2 = 12n \cdot l_{Pl}^2 \quad (n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots)$$

This was derived in Part DXVIII. The spectrum is equidistant with step $\Delta A = k \cdot l_{Pl}^2 = 12\, l_{Pl}^2$.

## The Gravitational Atom Energy Levels

Using $S_{BH} = A/4$ and the W33 hierarchy:
$$E_n = \frac{m_{Pl}^2}{M_n} = \frac{m_{Pl}^2}{\sqrt{A_n/(4\pi)}} = m_{Pl} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{3n}}$$

The energy gap between levels $n$ and $n+1$:
$$\Delta E_n = m_{Pl} \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{3}} \left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}\right)$$

For large $n$ (classical black hole):
$$\Delta E_n \approx \frac{m_{Pl}}{2} \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{3}} n^{-3/2}$$

## The W33 Black Hole Mass Gap

The minimum non-zero mass black hole (one W33 vertex fully occupied) has mass:
$$M_{min} = m_{Pl} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{k}{4\pi}} = m_{Pl} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{12}{4\pi}} = m_{Pl} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{3}{\pi}} \approx 0.977\, m_{Pl}$$

The minimum black hole mass in W33-Theory is approximately one Planck mass, as expected.

## Hawking Radiation as W33 Graph Surgery

Hawking radiation in W33-Theory corresponds to edge deletion: a black hole loses one edge (one unit of area $k \cdot l_{Pl}^2$) per emission event, transitioning from the $n$-vertex to the $(n-1)$-vertex configuration.

The emission rate from vertex $n$ to $n-1$:
$$\Gamma_{n \to n-1} \propto \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta M}{T_H}\right)$$

where $T_H = m_{Pl}^2 / (8\pi M)$ is the Hawking temperature, and $\Delta M = E_n - E_{n-1}$.

**Falsifier F37:** The minimum black hole mass in W33-Theory is $M_{min} \approx 0.977\, m_{Pl}$. No black hole with mass below $m_{Pl}$ can exist. Any detection of a sub-Planck black hole (e.g., in LHC microscopic black hole searches) would falsify W33. Current LHC limits are far below this threshold.
