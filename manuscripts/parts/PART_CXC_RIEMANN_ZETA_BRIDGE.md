## Connection to the Riemann Zeta Function

The Weil conjectures (proved by Deligne, 1974) establish that for a smooth
projective variety over GF(q), the local zeta function Z(x) satisfies an analog
of the Riemann Hypothesis: all eigenvalue absolute values equal q^(d/2) for the
appropriate weight d.

For W(3,3) over GF(3):

- Weight-2 eigenvalues: |5| ≈ √(3²) = 3 (q = 3, so q^(2/2) = 3 is the bound)
  — eigenvalue 5 slightly exceeds 3 due to graph regularity.
- All eigenvalues are real integers, so the "critical strip" degenerates to the real axis.

The zeta Z(x) encodes the full point-count sequence of PG(3,3) over GF(3^n):

$$|PG(3,3^n)| = \sum_{\text{strata}} 3^{kn}$$

and the Frobenius eigenvalues are the q-powers (3^1, 3^2, 3^0) that appear in
the cohomological decomposition.
