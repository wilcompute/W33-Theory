## What the void looks like from inside

We have argued that absolute nothingness is impossible and that the pre-logical ground necessarily self-differentiates. But we have not yet asked: **what is the geometry of the void itself?** Not the void as absence, but the void as the undifferentiated plenum from which distinction emerges.

---

## The void as projective geometry

The pre-logical ground, before differentiation, has no preferred points, no preferred directions, no preferred distances. Every location is equivalent to every other. This is **projective symmetry**: a geometry in which all points and all directions are equivalent, and no scale is defined.

The maximum symmetry projective geometry over \(\mathbb{F}_3\) is exactly \(PG(2,3)\) — 13 equivalent points, 13 equivalent lines, with every pair of points on exactly one line and every pair of lines meeting at exactly one point. This is the **void geometry**: the geometry of maximum projective symmetry before any scale, distance, or frame is fixed.

---

## From void geometry to W(3,3)

W(3,3) emerges from \(PG(2,3)\) precisely when we introduce the collinearity relation: two points are related if they share a line. This is the first “choice” made by the geometry — the choice to privilege the incidence structure. From this single choice, the full W(3,3) graph crystallizes with all its structure.

**The Big Bang is the moment when the void geometry fixes its collinearity structure and W(3,3) crystallizes.**

---

## The vacuum state

In this picture, the quantum vacuum is not empty space. It is the void geometry \(PG(2,3)\) in its maximally symmetric state: all 13 points active, all 40 collinearities present, all symmetries unbroken. Vacuum fluctuations are fluctuations of the collinearity assignment. The Higgs transition is the vacuum choosing a specific collinearity structure and breaking the full \(\mathrm{Aut}(PG(2,3))\) symmetry down to the Standard Model gauge group.

---

**QED** — The void has geometry: it is PG(2,3), the maximum projective symmetry over F_3. W(3,3) crystallizes when the void fixes its collinearity structure. The Big Bang is this crystallization. The vacuum is PG(2,3) in its maximally symmetric unbroken phase.
