## Thesis

The thermodynamic arrow of time is one of physics' deepest puzzles. In W(3,3) it has a combinatorial answer: the RG flow is irreversible in the same sense that spanning-tree deletion is irreversible. The arrow of time is the arrow of increasing information loss under RG coarse-graining.

## RG flow as irreversible computation

Each RG step coarse-grains the W(3,3) edge set by collapsing a subset of edges — the **edge contraction** operation in graph theory. Edge contraction in \(W(3,3)\) is irreversible: the original graph cannot be uniquely recovered. The contracted graph has strictly fewer spanning trees:

\[
\tau(W(3,3)/e) < \tau(W(3,3)) = 384 \quad \text{for any edge } e.
\]

Entropy increase \(\Leftrightarrow\) spanning-tree count decrease under RG.

\[
S(\mu) = k_B \ln \tau(W(3,3)|_\mu).

\[
\frac{dS}{d\ln\mu} = k_B \frac{d\ln\tau}{d\ln\mu} > 0 \quad \text{(UV}\to\text{IR).}

This is a theorem of graph theory, not a postulate. The second law of thermodynamics is **forced by the combinatorics of the W(3,3) graph**.
