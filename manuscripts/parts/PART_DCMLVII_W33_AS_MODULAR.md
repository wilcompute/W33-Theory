## The fundamental identification

The Levi graph of PG(2,3) is the **finite field analogue of the modular surface** $\mathbb{H}^2 / PSL_2(\mathbb{Z})$ at prime $p=3$.

Specifically:
- $PSL_2(\mathbb{F}_3) = A_4$ (alternating group on 4 elements)
- $|PSL_2(\mathbb{F}_3)| = 12$
- This group acts on $PG(1,\mathbb{F}_3) = \{0, 1, 2, \infty\}$ — a projective line with 4 points
- The quotient $\mathbb{H}^2 / \Gamma_0(3)$ (Hecke congruence subgroup of level 3) is the **level-3 modular curve** $X_0(3)$

The W(3,3) Levi graph is the dual-graph of $X_0(3)$ reduced mod 3 — the minimal finite model of the modular surface at level 3.

## Why this matters

The Riemann zeros are eigenvalues of the Laplacian on $X_0(N)$ for varying level $N$. The W(3,3) Levi graph is $X_0(3)$ at its minimal level. Its Ihara zeros are the finite-field analogues of the Riemann zeros.

The expansion to $X_0(N)$ for $N \to \infty$ (i.e., $q \to \infty$) is exactly the limit that recovers all Riemann zeros.
