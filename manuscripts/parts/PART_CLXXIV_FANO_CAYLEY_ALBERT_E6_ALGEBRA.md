## 3. Multiplication rule

Choose an orientation of the Fano lines.  On each oriented line

\[
(a,b,c),
\]

set

\[
e_a e_b=e_c,
\]

cyclically, and reverse the sign anti-cyclically:

\[
e_b e_a=-e_c.
\]

Each imaginary unit squares to

\[
e_a^2=-1.
\]

Thus the heptad is not merely seven labels.  It is a full Fano multiplication table.
