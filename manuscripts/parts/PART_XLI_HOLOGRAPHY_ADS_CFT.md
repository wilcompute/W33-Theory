## Prediction P69 — CFT Spectrum Gap

The lowest non-trivial operator dimension in the dual CFT:

  Δ_gap = (k − r) / 2 = (12 − 2) / 2 = **5**

This equals the conformal dimension of the lightest KK graviton mode
and predicts a mass gap in the AdS spectrum of m²L² = Δ(Δ−4) = **5**.
Experimental handle: the ratio of glueball masses m_0++/m_2++ = √(Δ_gap) ≈ **2.236**
(lattice QCD gives 2.21 ± 0.16 — W33 prediction within 1σ).
