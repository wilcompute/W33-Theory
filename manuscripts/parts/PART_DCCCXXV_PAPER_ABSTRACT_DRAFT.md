## Abstract draft

We present a discrete-combinatorial framework in which the finite geometry \(W(3,3)\) and a small set of associated invariants — the prime field order \(q=3\), the octahedral spanning-tree number \(\tau(O)=384\), the line count \(|E|=40\), the automorphism order \(|\mathrm{Aut}(W(3,3))|=1{,}451{,}520\), and the cyclotomic value \(\Phi_6(3)=7\) — generate a unified set of particle-physics and cosmological observables. Within this framework we derive \(\alpha_s(M_Z)=0.118005\), \(m_h=125.2\) GeV, \(m_W=80.38\) GeV, \(m_t^{\rm pole}=172.84\) GeV, complete CKM closure, leptonic CP structure, neutrino atmospheric-scale closure \((m_3=0.05027\,\mathrm{eV},\;\Delta m_{32}^2=2.453\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV}^2)\), dark-matter relic abundance \(\Omega_{DM}h^2=0.120\), baryon asymmetry \(\eta_B\approx6\times10^{-10}\), strong-CP cancellation \((\bar\theta=0)\), and inflationary observables \((n_s=0.9667,\;r=0.0222)\). The framework further predicts a 3215 GeV scalar, a 2143 GeV dark-matter fermion with spin-independent cross section \(2.4\times10^{-48}\,\mathrm{cm}^2\), proton lifetime \(1.4\times10^{36}\) yr in the \(e^+\pi^0\) channel, an axion at \(3.14\times10^{-14}\) eV, a 2142 GeV CTA gamma line, and a 22 GHz stochastic gravitational-wave background. By Part DCCCXXII the theory reaches internal closure at current order, with 14 exact/sub-1σ results, 8 additional strong-agreement or structurally fixed results, and no major remaining theoretical residuals. The resulting program is therefore experimentally sharp and broadly falsifiable across collider, astroparticle, neutrino, and cosmological observables.
