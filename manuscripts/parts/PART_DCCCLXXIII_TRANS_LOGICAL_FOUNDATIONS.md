## The question beneath logic

All of our derivations have used logic. Even the argument that “nothingness is self-contradictory” used the law of non-contradiction. But logic itself is a structured system. What grounds it?

This is not a question logic can answer about itself. It is the trans-logical question: **why does coherent reasoning work?**

---

## The trans-logical claim

Coherent reasoning works because the structure of the pre-logical ground is self-consistent. Logic does not impose order on reality; it discovers the ordering that is already present in the recursive coherent distinction that constitutes reality.

In W(3,3) terms:
- The law of non-contradiction reflects the binary field \(\mathbb{F}_2\) beneath \(\mathbb{F}_3\) — the meta-language of pure distinction.
- The law of the excluded middle reflects the two-valuedness of classical first-order logic, which is the \(q=2\) projection of the ternary truth structure.
- **Three-valued logic** (true, false, undetermined) is the natural logic of \(\mathbb{F}_3\) — and it is the correct logic for quantum systems, where measurement outcomes can be genuinely undetermined.

---

## Classical logic as a projection

Classical two-valued logic is the \(q \to 2\) projection of ternary logic, the same way that classical physics is the \(\hbar \to 0\) limit of quantum physics. It works for macroscopic classical objects because at those scales the third truth value (quantum superposition) is decoherenced away. 

But it fails for quantum systems, paradoxes of self-reference (Gödel), and the foundations of mathematics — exactly the situations where ternary structure is irreducible.

---

## The logic of W(3,3)

The natural logic of W(3,3) is **Łukasiewicz three-valued logic** \(L_3\), with truth values \(\{0, \tfrac{1}{2}, 1\}\) corresponding to \(\{0, 1, 2\} \in \mathbb{F}_3\). In this logic:
- \(\neg \tfrac{1}{2} = \tfrac{1}{2}\) (the undetermined value is its own negation),
- \(p \vee \neg p\) is not a tautology for \(p = \tfrac{1}{2}\),
- The excluded middle fails, exactly as it fails in quantum mechanics.

**W(3,3) logic is three-valued. Classical logic is a low-energy approximation.**
