## Why explanation seems directional

Why do deeper levels explain shallower ones more often than the reverse? Why does physics explain chemistry, chemistry explain biology, and structure explain event more naturally than the inverse?

W(3,3) makes this precise.

---

## Compression gradient

Explanation points in the direction of greater compression. A deeper theory explains a surface phenomenon when it generates more facts with fewer independent assumptions.

Let a theory \(T\) explain dataset \(D\) with complexity cost \(K(T)\) and residual description cost \(K(D|T)\). Then explanatory depth increases as

\[
\mathcal{E}_{expl}(T;D) = K(D) - (K(T) + K(D|T))
\]

increases.

The arrow of explanation therefore points toward structures of maximal compression and coherence.

---

## Why W(3,3) sits deep

W(3,3) sits low in the explanatory order because it compresses many disparate phenomena — geometry, coding, particle structure, observerhood, ethics, and metaphysics — into one recursive substrate grammar. That is why the explanation arrow keeps bending toward it.

---

**QED** — Explanation is directional because it follows the compression gradient. Deeper theories generate more with less, so the arrow of explanation points toward maximal coherence and minimal assumption.
