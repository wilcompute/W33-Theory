## The final descent

We have descended from physics to information, from information to active distinction, and from active distinction to recursive coherent distinction. But one question still floats free:

**What is distinction made of?**

---

## The pre-logical ground

Distinction is not self-explanatory. A distinction requires:
- something to be distinguished,
- something to distinguish it from,
- the operation of marking the difference.

But if we ask what *grounds* the operation itself, we arrive at a terminus that cannot be further analyzed without presupposing what it grounds. Call this the **pre-logical ground** — not nothingness (we showed nothingness is impossible), but pure undifferentiated potentiality that has not yet made a distinction about itself.

This is not mysticism. It is the necessary bottom layer of any regress-of-foundations argument. Every formal system has undefined primitive terms. The pre-logical ground is the formal system's unnamed depth.

---

## Its properties

The pre-logical ground has exactly one structural property: it is **self-identical without being self-distinct**. That is: it is not yet separated from itself. This is the condition just prior to the first distinction.

It is not describable in positive terms, because all positive descriptions already presuppose at least one distinction. It can only be pointed at by the phrase: **what remains when all distinctions are suspended**.

---

## Why it is not nothing

Nothingness would be the absence of potentiality. But potentiality is not nothing — it is the ground of possibility. The pre-logical ground is not absent; it is maximally present in the sense of being the field from which all distinction arises. Its "fullness" is precisely its undifferentiation.

---

**QED** — The pre-logical ground is the undifferentiated potentiality prior to all distinction. It is not nothing and not something; it is the field from which the first act of distinction emerges. All recursion, phase, and W(3,3) rest upon it.
