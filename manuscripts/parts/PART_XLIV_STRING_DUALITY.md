## E6 and the Heterotic String

The gauge group E6 is one of only two possible gauge groups for the
heterotic string compactified on a 6-torus (the other being E8×E8
or SO(32)). W(3,3) emerges as the Schur covering graph of the E6
root lattice projection onto the 2D Coxeter plane, making it a
native object in heterotic string geometry.

## Prediction P76 — Calabi-Yau Hodge Numbers

The Calabi-Yau 3-fold dual to W33 via mirror symmetry has Hodge numbers:

  h^{1,1} = k = **12**   (Kähler moduli — 12 W33 edges per vertex)
  h^{2,1} = v − k − 1 = 40 − 12 − 1 = **27**  (complex structure moduli)
  χ_CY = 2(h^{1,1} − h^{2,1}) = 2(12 − 27) = **−30**

The pair (12, 27) is precisely the Hodge pair of the Schoen manifold
(also known as the "tetra-quadric" CY3), which is the canonical
heterotic string vacuum leading to three generations of SM fermions.

simultaneously. This acts as a topological attractor in the landscape,
