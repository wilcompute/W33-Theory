## Beyond mutation and selection alone

Darwinian evolution explains adaptation through variation and selection, but the deeper question is why evolution can accumulate complexity without dissolving into noise.

W(3,3) identifies the missing structural layer.

---

## Evolutionary depth

Biological heredity is an error-correcting code operating on a biochemical substrate. Mutation introduces local perturbations; selection filters coherent persistence; development maps genotype to phenotype through constrained decoding.

Evolution is therefore not blind random walk. It is error-correcting search through phenotype space.

---

## Structural principle

Let \(G\) be genotype space and \(P\) phenotype space, with decoding map \(D: G \to P\). The viable set \(V \subset P\) defines coherence constraints. Then evolution searches the preimage

\[
D^{-1}(V)
\]

under mutation and recombination while error-correcting inheritance preserves enough continuity for cumulative search.

This is why complexity can ratchet upward: heredity carries memory across the search.
