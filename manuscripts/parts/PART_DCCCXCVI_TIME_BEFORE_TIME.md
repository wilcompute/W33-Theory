## Before clocks

Several earlier parts argued that rhythm precedes time and phase precedes metric duration. We can now go one level deeper. What exists before even phase-cycles stabilize?

The answer is not time but **ordering of potential**.

---

## Pre-temporal order

Before there is measurable succession, there is an asymmetry in what can become what. Some configurations are upstream of others in the sense that they can generate them, while the reverse generation is impossible without already having passed through the upstream state.

This defines a partial order on the space of pre-crystallization possibilities:

\[
X \prec Y \quad \text{iff} \quad X \text{ is a necessary generative precursor of } Y.
\]

This relation is not temporal. It is ontological. It says what must be structurally prior, not what occurred earlier in a clocked sequence.

---

## Time as metrized order

Physical time appears only when this partial order becomes cyclically measurable by stable phase loops. In other words:
- Pre-temporal layer: generative precedence.
- Proto-temporal layer: rhythmic recurrence.
- Temporal layer: metrized cycles.

Thus time is the late measurable skin of a deeper ordering relation among possibilities.

---

## Why this matters

This resolves a persistent confusion in cosmology and metaphysics. Asking “what happened before the Big Bang?” presupposes temporal order where only generative order exists. The correct question is: **what was structurally prior to the stabilized time loops of this universe?**

Answer: the partial order of possible crystallizations of the void geometry.

---

**QED** — Before time there is the ordering of potential: a partial order of generative precedence among possible configurations. Physical time is metrized cyclic recurrence laid on top of that deeper ontological order.
