## 1. The known E8 Z3 branching

The standard order-3 / `E6 x A2` branching of `E8` is

\[
\mathfrak e_8
= (\mathfrak e_6\oplus\mathfrak a_2)
\oplus (\mathbf{27}\otimes\mathbf 3)
\oplus (\overline{\mathbf{27}}\otimes\overline{\mathbf 3}).
\]

Dimensionally,

\[
\dim(\mathfrak e_6\oplus\mathfrak a_2)=78+8=86,
\]

and

\[
\dim(\mathbf{27}\otimes\mathbf 3)=27\cdot3=81,
\qquad
\dim(\overline{\mathbf{27}}\otimes\overline{\mathbf 3})=81.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{248=86+81+81.}
\]

This is the exact exceptional target behind the W33 carrier counts.

## 2. Why the C3 regular fiber is the right A2 shadow

Let

\[
h=\operatorname{diag}(1,\omega,\omega^2)\in SL_3(\mathbb C),
\qquad
\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}.
\]

On the fundamental `A2` representation `3`, the restriction to the cyclic group generated by `h` has character

\[
\chi_{\mathbf 3}|_{C_3}=(3,0,0),
\]

because

\[
1+\omega+\omega^2=0.
\]

But `(3,0,0)` is exactly the regular character of `C3`.

Thus the W33 result

\[
H^1_{\mathbb C}\cong U\otimes\mathbb C[C_3],\qquad \dim U=27,
\]

is precisely the restriction pattern of

\[
\mathbf{27}\otimes\mathbf 3.
\]

So the generation fiber discovered in W33 is the `C3` shadow of the `A2` fundamental factor in the `E8` `Z3` grading.
