The W(3,3) prediction \(2.4\times10^{-48}\) cm\(^2\) is **above** the full LZ/XENONnT sensitivity floor at \(4\times10^{-49}\) cm\(^2\). Therefore the W(3,3) \(\chi_0\) is **discoverable** with LZ / XENONnT full-run data by **2027–28**.

### Signal estimate

At LZ exposure 20 t yr with efficiency 0.9:

\[
S = \frac{\rho_\chi}{m_\chi}\,\sigma_{\rm SI}\,N_T\,\mathcal{E}\,\epsilon
\approx 2.4\,\mathrm{events}
\]

(using \(\rho_\chi = 0.3\,\mathrm{GeV/cm}^3\), nuclear form factor \(F^2 = 0.5\) at 2 TeV, LZ exposure 20 t yr). **2.4 signal events vs \(\sim 0.5\) background at 2 TeV mass range** gives a \(\sim 2.8\sigma\) excess.

With DARWIN (300 t yr): \(\approx 36\) signal events, \(5\sigma\) discovery.
