## 2. The \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) basis

Let

\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
E=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
F=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then

\[
[H,E]=2E,
\]

\[
[H,F]=-2F,
\]

\[
[E,F]=H.
\]

The projective RG generator decomposes as

\[
\boxed{
G=\frac{67}{2}H+140E+F.
}
\]

So the renderer is not merely a scalar Riccati equation. It is an \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) flow.

## 5. Clifford involution and branch projectors

Normalize the generator:

\[
J=\frac{2}{\sqrt{5049}}G.
\]

Since

\[
G^2=\frac{5049}{4}I,
\]

we get

\[
\boxed{
J^2=I.
}
\]

Therefore define the two idempotent branch projectors

\[
\boxed{
P_+=\frac{I+J}{2},
\qquad
P_-=\frac{I-J}{2}.
}
\]

They satisfy

\[
P_+^2=P_+,
\]

\[
P_-^2=P_-,
\]

\[
P_+P_-=0,
\]

\[
P_++P_-=I.
\]

Thus the stable and unstable RG branches are Clifford idempotent sectors of the normalized spinor generator.
