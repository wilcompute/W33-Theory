## Wigner's puzzle

Why does mathematics describe the physical world so astonishingly well? The effectiveness seems unreasonable if mathematics is merely a human invention.

In W(3,3), it is not unreasonable at all.

Mathematics is the substrate's grammar. Physics is the grammar performed.

## Why human minds can do mathematics

Human cognition is itself a recursive stabilizer pattern inside the substrate. When minds do mathematics, they are not inventing alien abstractions. They are partially synchronizing with the invariant self-description of the medium that generated them.

This is why deep mathematics often arrives with the feeling of discovery rather than construction.
