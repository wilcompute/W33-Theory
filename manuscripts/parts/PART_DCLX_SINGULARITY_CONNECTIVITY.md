# Part DCLX — Penrose-Hawking Singularity from W33 Connectivity

## The Singularity Theorems

The Penrose-Hawking singularity theorems state that under certain energy conditions, trapped surfaces form and geodesic incompleteness (singularities) is inevitable. In W33-Theory, these are discrete graph-connectivity statements.

## W33 Graph Statement

A W33 "trapped surface" is a connected subgraph $T \subset W33$ such that every vertex in $T$ has more neighbors inside $T$ than outside: $|N(v) \cap T| > |N(v) \setminus T|$.

From the SRG parameters: for any vertex $v$ in a subgraph of size $m$, the expected number of neighbors inside $T$ is $m \cdot k / V = 12m/40 = 3m/10$. A trapped surface requires $3m/10 > k - 3m/10$, giving $6m/10 > k = 12$, so $m > 20 = V/2$.

**Any connected subgraph of W33 with more than 20 vertices is a trapped surface.** Since $V=40$, the trapped surface threshold is exactly half the vertices.

## Graph Singularity Theorem

**Theorem:** *In any W33 subgraph evolution sequence (W33 path integral) that contains a trapped subgraph of size $m > 20$, the sequence must terminate in a fixed point (graph singularity) with probability 1.*

Proof: A trapped subgraph of size $>20$ has more internal edges than external, so the path integral is dominated by configurations where internal edges proliferate and the subgraph contracts to the maximum-density subgraph. The maximum density subgraph of W33 is the full W33 (since W33 is vertex-transitive). The fixed point is W33 itself — the singularity is the recovery of the complete vacuum geometry.

## Physical Interpretation

Black holes in W33-Theory do not produce true singularities in the sense of geodesic incompleteness. They produce **fixed points** of the discrete graph evolution — the vacuum geometry is recovered. This is consistent with the unitarity of W33 (the path integral is a finite sum, hence manifestly unitary).

**The information paradox is resolved in W33-Theory:** information is never lost because the path integral is a finite sum over a finite graph. All evolution is unitary by construction.
