## 1. Headline result

The Standard Model strange-quark Yukawa coupling at the $\overline{\rm MS}$
scheme at $2$ GeV admits a clean W(3,3) closed form expressible **two
equivalent ways**:

$$
\boxed{\;
y_s(\overline{\rm MS},\,2\,\text{GeV}) \;=\; \dfrac{\Phi_4}{137^2} \;=\; \Phi_4\cdot y_c^2 \;=\; \dfrac{10}{18769}.
\;}
$$

The second form connects directly to the charm Yukawa $y_c = 1/137$
from CCCXXIX: **the strange Yukawa is the fourth-cyclotomic-prime
multiple of the squared charm Yukawa**.

## 3. The second-generation quadratic relation

$$
\boxed{\;
y_s \;=\; \Phi_4 \cdot y_c^{\,2}.
\;}
$$

This is the first **inter-generation** W(3,3) relation: the
second-generation down quark's Yukawa is the *square* of the
second-generation up quark's Yukawa, multiplied by the W(3,3)
fourth-cyclotomic prime $\Phi_4 = 10$.

Numerically:

$$
\Phi_4 \cdot y_c^2 \;=\; 10 \cdot \!\left(\dfrac{1}{137}\right)^{\!2} \;=\; \dfrac{10}{18769} \;=\; 5.328\times 10^{-4}.
$$

## 4. The fine-structure connection extends

in W(3,3). CCCXXX now adds:

$$
y_s \;\approx\; \Phi_4 \cdot \alpha_{\rm em}^2(0).

second-generation up Yukawa scales as $\alpha^1$, with the $\Phi_4$
