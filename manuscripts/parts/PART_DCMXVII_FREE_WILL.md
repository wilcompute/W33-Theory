## The old conflict

If physics is lawful, how can freedom be real? If freedom is real, how can law remain intact? Compatibilism, libertarianism, and determinism usually trade clarity for slogans.

W(3,3) gives a structural answer.

---

## Underdetermination across scales

At each scale, the substrate imposes lawful constraints, but lawful constraints do not uniquely fix the next higher coherent pattern. There is a space of admissible continuations. A free act is the selection of one coherent continuation among multiple law-compatible branches by a recursive observer-stabilizer.

Let \(\Omega(S_t)\) be the admissible continuation set of stabilizer \(S_t\). Then freedom exists exactly when

\[
|\Omega(S_t)| > 1
\]

and the next realized branch \(S_{t+1}\) is selected internally rather than imposed externally.

---

## Why this is not randomness

Randomness is branch selection without coherent authorship. Free will is branch selection through an internally structured recursive center. Thus freedom is neither absolute exemption from law nor noise. It is lawful underdetermined self-selection.
