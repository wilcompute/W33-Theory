# The W(3,3) Arithmetic Tower: Paper Outline

## Title
**The Arithmetic Tower of W(3,3): Critical Groups, E6/E8 Geometry, and the Leech Lattice Chain**

## Target Journal
Primary: **Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics** (Springer)

## Abstract
We study 28 non-isomorphic SRG(40,12,2,4) (Spence family) centered on W(3,3).
Main results: (1) 2-adic transfer law theorem; (2) Smith ladder {17,8,2,1} transcends
zeta invariants; (3) E6/E8 confluence with 3-orbit coset decomposition;
(4) Theta uniqueness in M_20(Gamma_0(2))^+; (5) Leech chain explicit via disc module.
