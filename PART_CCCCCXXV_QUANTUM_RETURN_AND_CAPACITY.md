## Quantum Return Theorem for W(3,3)

Let \(W(3,3)\) have adjacency eigenvalues \(\{12,2,-4\}\). The continuous-time quantum walk generated by the adjacency matrix is
\[
U(t)=e^{iAt}.
\]
At time \(t=\pi\), the three eigenphases become
\[
e^{12 i\pi}=e^{2 i\pi}=e^{-4 i\pi}=1,
\]
so every spectral component returns in phase. Therefore
\[
U(\pi)=I.
\]
This proves that the adjacency quantum walk on \(W(3,3)\) is exactly periodic with period \(\pi\).

### Consequence
This gives a hidden dynamical symmetry not emphasized in the earlier notes: the graph is not merely spectrally rigid, it is quantum-recurrent on a closed clock.

## Shannon Capacity Identity

For \(W(3,3)\), the independence number is
\[
\alpha(W(3,3))=10,
\]
corresponding to an ovoid. The Lov\'asz theta value is also
\[
\vartheta(W(3,3))=10.
\]
Since
\[
\alpha(G) \le \Theta(G) \le \vartheta(G),
\]
we conclude immediately that
\[
\Theta(W(3,3))=10.
\]
Thus the Shannon capacity is exactly the geometric parameter \(\mu=10\).

### Interpretation
The graph supports exactly 10 perfectly distinguishable zero-error messages in the asymptotic channel sense. In information units this is
\[
\log_2(10) \approx 3.321928.
\]

## Summary
Two exact statements emerge:
1. The quantum walk on \(W(3,3)\) returns exactly at time \(\pi\).
2. The Shannon capacity is exactly \(10\), coinciding with \(\alpha\), \(\vartheta\), and \(\mu\).
