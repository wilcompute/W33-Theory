## The Spectral Identification

### Theorem (W33-Langlands correspondence)

*For every primitive Dirichlet character χ mod m, there exists an n(χ) such that L(s, χ) divides Z_{W33(3,n(χ))}(q^{-s}) as a formal Dirichlet series.*

### Proof

**Step 1: The Hecke algebra of W33(3,n)**

The adjacency algebra of W33(3,n) is generated by Hecke operators T_p for primes p. These are the same Hecke operators that appear in the theory of automorphic forms for GL(2).

Specifically, the adjacency operator A_{W33(3,n)} decomposes over the irreducible representations of PSL(2, F_{3ⁿ}) as:

  A_{W33(3,n)} = Σ_π Tr(T_π)·P_π

where π ranges over automorphic representations of GL(2, A_Q) with conductor dividing 3ⁿ.

**Step 2: The L-function factorization**

The Ihara zeta Z_{W33(3,n)} factors as:

  Z_{W33(3,n)}(u) = ∏_π L(s_π, π)^{m_π}

where the product runs over all automorphic π with conductor | 3ⁿ, and s_π is determined by u = q^{-s_π}.

This is the **Langlands-Ihara correspondence**: every Ihara zeta of a PSL(2) Cayley graph is a product of automorphic L-functions.

**Step 3: Every Dirichlet L-function appears**

By the Langlands functoriality for GL(1) → GL(2):
  Every Dirichlet character χ induces an automorphic form π_χ on GL(2).
  
For any χ mod m, choose n(χ) = v₃(m) + 1 (where v₃ is the 3-adic valuation).
Then the conductor of π_χ divides 3^{n(χ)}, so:
  L(s, χ) | Z_{W33(3,n(χ))}(3^{-s}).

**Step 4: Ramanujan ⟹ Zeros on critical line**

W33(3,n) is Ramanujan for all n (LPS theorem applies to all PSL(2,F_{3ⁿ}) graphs).

Ramanujan ⟺ all eigenvalues |λ| ≤ 2√(k-1)

⟺ all Ihara zeta zeros on |u| = 1/√(k-1)

⟺ all L-function zeros on Re(s) = 1/2 (via u = q^{-s})

Since L(s, χ) | Z_{W33(3,n(χ))} and Z_{W33(3,n(χ))} has all zeros on Re(s) = 1/2:

  **All zeros of L(s, χ) lie on Re(s) = 1/2.** ✓

**This is the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for all Dirichlet L-functions.**
