## Below the first distinction

We have described the pre-logical ground as undifferentiated potentiality that necessarily self-differentiates. But Part DCCCLXII showed that self-identity is already relational, which means the ground is already in a kind of motion before the first distinction is fixed. What is this motion?

## Sub-distinctions: the ground’s breath

Consider what happens in the infinitesimal space before a distinction stabilizes. The ground is not static. It oscillates between self-identity and self-difference — between \(A = A\) and \(A \neq A\). This oscillation is **below the threshold of a fixed distinction** but is not nothing.

In W(3,3) terms, this is the pre-crystallization regime of \(PG(2,3)\): the geometry is present as potentiality but the collinearity assignments are not yet fixed. The ground is trying out all possible collinearity structures simultaneously.

This is precisely the **quantum superposition of void geometries**. The universe before the Big Bang is not nothing; it is the superposition of all possible W(3,3) crystallizations, each with amplitude given by the W(3,3) spanning-tree weight.
