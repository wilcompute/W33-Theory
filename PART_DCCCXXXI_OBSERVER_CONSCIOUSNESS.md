## Thesis

\[

## Measurement and collapse

- **Measurement** = \(\mathcal{O}(\mathcal{C}) \to \mathcal{O}(\mathcal{C}')\), i.e., stabilizer restriction as the configuration grows.

- **Complementarity** = two configurations with disjoint edge sets have commuting stabilizers.
