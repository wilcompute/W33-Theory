## Core Pitch

The Holonet is a runnable finite-geometry compute fabric where address, route, gate, memory coordinate, and audit target are the same object. The practical pitch is not "believe a Theory of Everything." The practical pitch is: fund a self-auditing SDK, a table-free routing benchmark, and two small photonic falsifiers that can either advance or kill the first hardware claims quickly.

## First 90 Seconds

Start here if an investor is listening:

1. AI and datacenter systems are becoming constrained by power, interconnect, and control overhead, not only by arithmetic throughput.
2. The Holonet attacks that overhead by replacing separate routing tables, memory maps, and control ledgers with one exact finite geometry, `W(3,3)`.
3. The repo is already executable: it verifies the geometry, audits claims, benchmarks routing, runs a browser packet demo, and packages a photonic contextuality experiment.
4. The near-term ask is disciplined: build the developer surface and run the next falsifiers. The upside is a new architecture for optical, quantum, and AI fabrics; the downside is bounded by tests that can fail fast.

We are asking you to underwrite the executable machine, the audit layer, and the next experiments that decide whether the physical story survives."

## Investor Objections And Answers

### "Is this just a Theory of Everything pitch?"

No. The financing plan should start with executable architecture and falsifiers. The broader physics program is upside, not the first underwriting assumption.

### "Where is the hardware?"

There are now two hardware-facing packets: the Witting contextuality discriminator and the frequency-bin phase-probe schedule. Both have raw-shot schemas and decision logic. Neither should be represented as completed bench data yet.

### "Why should I care about E6 or E8?"

Because those labels are not decorative in this version. They lock the selector fabric to a runtime budget: `120` sheets, `240` signed sheets, `216` slots per signed sheet, and `51840` total ticks. That is a diligence handle: a reviewer can run the script and check the closure before funding the harder physics.

### "What is the moat?"

The moat is the combination of exact finite geometry, runnable witnesses, audit discipline, compiler lowering, and a growing corpus of claim-linked artifacts. The important asset is not a single benchmark; it is the executable proof surface.

### "What would kill it?"

Four things should change the plan quickly:

- W(3) contextuality fails to separate from even-q controls.
- The frequency-bin phase probes do not reproduce the predicted calibration structure.
- The table-free benchmark loses its scaling argument under independent implementation.
- The audit ledger cannot keep frontier claims cleanly separated from exact and physical claims.

### "Is the quantum advantage already proven?"

No. The Clifford/classical finite fabric runs today. Quantum advantage remains priced through the magic/contextual layer and must be earned through the discriminator and later optical experiments.
