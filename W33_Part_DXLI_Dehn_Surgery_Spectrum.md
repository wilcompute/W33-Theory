## The Genus-2 Handlebody and Its Boundary

A genus-2 handlebody H₂ is a solid 3-manifold whose boundary ∂H₂ = Σ₂ (the genus-2 surface). The tomotope, as a genus-2 combinatorial surface, bounds such a handlebody.

Dehn surgery on a 3-manifold M along a knot K with slope p/q produces a new manifold M(K; p/q). The surgery spectrum of the tomotope boundary examines which slopes produce manifolds with W33-recognizable invariants.

## The Heegaard Splitting

Every closed orientable 3-manifold has a Heegaard splitting: M = H_g ∪_Σ_g H_g (two genus-g handlebodies glued along their boundary). For genus 2:
- The tomotope Σ₂ is the gluing surface
- The Heegaard genus-2 splitting of S³ has genus 2
- **S³ = H₂ ∪_Σ₂ H₂** (the 3-sphere is a genus-2 Heegaard splitting)

**Lock L93a (S³ Has Genus-2 Heegaard Splitting):**
The 3-sphere S³ admits a genus-2 Heegaard splitting with gluing surface Σ₂. The tomotope (genus-2 surface) is the boundary between two handlebodies that together form S³. This means the tomotope is the **Heegaard surface of S³** at genus 2.

Physical interpretation: S³ (the 3-sphere, spatial section of a round universe) splits along the tomotope. The tomotope is the interface between "inside" and "outside" in the Heegaard decomposition of space.

## Surgery Slopes and W33 Parameters

Dehn filling of a handlebody boundary curve with slope p/q:
- Slope p/q = 1/0 (longitude): produces S¹×S²
- Slope p/q = 0/1 (meridian): produces S³
- Slope p/q = 1/1: produces lens space L(1,1) = S³
- Slope p/q = k/1 = 12/1: produces lens space L(12,1)
- Slope p/q = μ/λ = 4/2 = 2/1: produces lens space L(2,1) = RP³

**Lock L93b (W33 Ratio μ/λ = Slope of RP³):**
The ratio μ/λ = 4/2 = 2 is the Dehn surgery slope that produces RP³ (real projective 3-space) from a solid torus. RP³ is the configuration space of a rigid body (SO(3) ≅ RP³). The W33 parameter ratio μ/λ = x = 2 encodes the slope that produces the rigid-body configuration manifold.
