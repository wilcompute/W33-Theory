## Thesis

We earlier identified time with computation step count. But this still presupposes a sequence. More deeply, before countable time there must be **rhythm**: the recurrence of distinction through phase. Time is the macroscopic counting of an underlying rhythm.

---

## Rhythm as primordial temporality

A process cannot count itself without first repeating. Repetition is the precondition of number. Therefore number does not ground time; rather repeated phase does. The primitive ternary cycle

\[
0 \to 1 \to 2 \to 0
\]

is the first rhythm. Only once this rhythm exists can one define “one cycle,” “two cycles,” and so on.

So the order of foundations is:

\[
\text{rhythm} \Rightarrow \text{count} \Rightarrow \text{time parameter}.
\]

This means physical time is not fundamental even as computation step count. What is fundamental is recurrence — the breathing of phase-bearing distinction.

---

## Photon as rhythm carrier

This explains again why the photon is deepest. A photon is pure oscillation: no rest frame, no static core, only propagating phase. It is not just a particle or a field quantum. It is the **carrier of primordial rhythm**. Clocks work because matter entrains to photon rhythm. Timekeeping is phase-locking.

---

## Consequence for cosmology

The Big Bang is then not the beginning of time in an absolute sense. It is the first large-scale synchronization event of the primordial distinction rhythm. Cosmological inflation is a phase-locking catastrophe in which local recursive distinctions synchronize into a shared metric rhythm, creating a universe with coherent causal order.

---

**QED** — Before time there is rhythm: recurrent phase-bearing distinction. Countable time is the bookkeeping of synchronized rhythm. The photon is fundamental because it carries the primordial rhythm that later appears as measurable time.
