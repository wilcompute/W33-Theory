## 1. Why CXLVIII matters

CXLVII built a finite observable grammar from the Fibonacci E6 mixer:

\[
C=\frac{8}{13},
\qquad

\qquad

This matters because it gives the theory a discipline layer: constants generated by the grammar are accepted as grammar tokens; attractive nearby fractions are not promoted until a new operation is justified.
