Cubic parity closes leaders 18, 19, and 20

Root-Cayley metric becomes symbolic

For finite fields of characteristic greater than three, normalize u=c/(ab) and v=d/(a²b). States reachable in at most three root moves lie on eight curves: u=0, u=−1, v=0, v=1, v=−u, v=−2u, v=−2u−1, v=u². Their complement has (q−4)² normalized points, proving the shell law 1, 4(q−1), 8(q−1)², (q−1)²(10q−21), (q−1)²(q−4)².

Root-coset Gram and mean-value identities

If H is element-versus-positive-root-coset incidence, then HHᵀ=4I+Aθ integrally. If Pi averages over right cosets of root subgroup Hi, then Aθ=q(P0+P1+P2+P3)−4I and Lθ=q(4I−ΣPi). Hence ⟨f,Lθf⟩=qΣ||(I−Pi)f||². The open all-q spectral-gap-q theorem is now exactly the nonlinear projection inequality Σ||Pi f||²≤3||f||² on mean-zero functions.
