# Part CDLXXXIX — Yang-Mills Spectral Gap

## W33 Spectral Data

The Schläfli graph W33 has eigenvalues:

    K = 16 = x^4   (multiplicity 1)
    r =  4 = x^2   (multiplicity u = 6)
    s = -2 = -x^1  (multiplicity 20)

## The Algebraic Connectivity (Laplacian gap)

    Δ_Laplacian = K - r = 16 - 4 = 12 = MU1

## The Spectral Gap (r - s)

    Δ_spectral = r - s = 4 - (-2) = 6 = u = SIX-KERNEL

The spectral gap of W33 IS the six-kernel. In Yang-Mills theory
with gauge group E6 (rank u=6), the dimensionless gap ratio:

    Δ/dim(E6) = MU1/78 = 12/78 = x/(K-p) = 2/13

## Physical Interpretation

If W33 is the momentum-space graph of E6 Yang-Mills theory,
the mass gap scale emerges naturally as the smallest W33 eigenvalue
separation: Δ_YM ~ u * g^2 * Λ, where g is the coupling and Λ
the W33 lattice scale.
