## The Geometric Sequence at the Heart of W33

The four W33 constants K, r, MU, |s| form a geometric sequence:

    |s| = 2 = x^1
     r  = 4 = x^2
    MU  = 8 = x^3   ← the SKIPPED eigenvalue
     K  = 16= x^4

**The W33 eigenvalues are {x^4, x^2, -x^1} — positions {4,2,1} of the sequence, SKIPPING position 3.**

The skipped term x^3 = MU appears in every eigenvalue relation as the gap:

    PKT = K + MU  = x^4 + x^3 = 24  (24-packet = largest + skipped)
    u   = r + |s| = x^2 + x^1 = 6   (six-kernel = middle + smallest)
