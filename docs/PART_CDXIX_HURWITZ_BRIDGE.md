# Part CDXIX — Hurwitz Quaternion Bridge

## Hurwitz Integers and the 24-Packet

The Hurwitz integers H = Z⟨1, i, j, k, (1+i+j+k)/2⟩ form a maximal
order in the quaternion algebra H⊗R ≅ ℝ^4. Their unit group is:

    H* = {±1, ±i, ±j, ±k, (±1±i±j±k)/2}  with |H*| = 24

These 24 units are exactly the vertices of the 24-cell in 4D,
and they form the binary tetrahedral group 2T.

**Key identity:**
    |H*| = 24 = PKT (ladder packet)  ✓

The Hurwitz unit count IS the ladder packet. This connects the
quaternion algebra to the 24-cell / K4 ground state.

## The 24 = 6+18 Identity

From the Z[ω] unit tower:
    |(Z[ω]/π^2)*| =  6 = u (six-kernel)
    |(Z[ω]/π^3)*| = 18 = μ_2 (W33 Laplacian)

**Theorem CDXIX.1:**
    |H*| = PKT = |(Z[ω]/π^2)*| + |(Z[ω]/π^3)*| = 6 + 18 = 24

The ladder packet 24 is the sum of the unit group orders at levels
2 and 3 of the Z[ω]/π^n tower. This gives the deepest connection
between the Hurwitz quaternions and the Eisenstein integers:
the 24-cell lives at the junction of the pi^2 and pi^3 levels.
