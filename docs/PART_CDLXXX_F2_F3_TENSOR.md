## W33 Lives at the Intersection of Two Prime Fields

W33 is simultaneously:
- A Cayley graph of **(Z/3Z)^3** — the additive group of F_3^3 (V=3^3=27 vertices)
- A graph whose **eigenvalues are all powers of 2** (the F_2 characteristic)

The six-kernel is the product of these two generating primes:

    u = x * p = 2 * 3 = 6

The 24-packet:

    PKT = MU * p = x^3 * (x+1) = 8 * 3 = 24
