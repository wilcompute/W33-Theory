## Albert Algebra (Exceptional Jordan Algebra)

The Albert algebra J₃(𝕆) (3×3 Hermitian octonionic matrices) has:

    dim(J₃(𝕆)) = 3×(8+1) = 3×9 = 27 = V(W33)  ✓

Decomposed: 3 × (μ+1) = p × p² = p³ = V(W33)  ✓

The Albert algebra IS 27-dimensional, exactly V(W33). The exceptional
Lie group E6 is the automorphism group of J₃(𝕆), acting on 27 dimensions
= V(W33) vertices.
