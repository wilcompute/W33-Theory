The Holonet Machine

A universal computer that is also its own network and memory, on the symplectic geometry W(3,3) = GQ(3,3). The classical layer is pure mod-3 integer arithmetic, so it runs right here in your browser — no install. Routing is gating is memory access.

Everything below is the real machine, computed live in JavaScript. The quantum advantage is the one thing a browser can't supply (the priced 9t dial).

1 · The network: route a packet

A node's address is a point of F34. Two nodes are linked iff their symplectic inner product B(x,y)=0 — one ALU operation. The address is the route; diameter 2.

    
Valid addresses are 4 base-3 digits, normalized (first nonzero = 1): e.g. 1000, 0100, 0010, 0001, 1011, 0121. There are 40.
