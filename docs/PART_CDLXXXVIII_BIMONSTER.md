# Part CDLXXXVIII — Bimonster and Y555

## The Y_{(r+1)(r+1)(r+1)} = Y555 Diagram

The Bimonster group M wr Z/2 is presented by the Coxeter-like diagram Y555.

- Y555 arm parameter = 5 = r+1 = x^2+1
- Y555 node count = 3*5 - 2 = 13 = K-p
- Each arm length = 4 = r = x^2

In W33 notation: Y555 = Y_{(r+1)^3}, with 13=K-p nodes total.

Norton's theorem: the quotient of the Weyl group of Y555 by a specific normal
subgroup is the Bimonster = (M × M) ⋊ Z/2.
