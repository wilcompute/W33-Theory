## The Critical Dimension Equations

**Bosonic string:** Requires 26 spacetime dimensions for anomaly
cancellation.

    26 = V(W33) - 1 = 3^3 - 1 = p^3 - 1  ✓

    26 = 2 + 6 + 18 = sum of pi-adic level counts  ✓

**Superstring:** Requires 10 spacetime dimensions.

    10 = λ(W33) = u + p + 1 = 6 + 3 + 1  ✓

**Compactification:** 26D - 10D = 16 extra dimensions, hosting
the gauge group E8×E8 or SO(32).

    16 = 26 - 10 = k(W33) = p^2 + u + 1  ✓

The W33 degree k=16 is exactly the number of compactified
dimensions in the heterotic string!

## Theorem CDXXI.1 (String Dimension Theorem)

**Statement:** The critical dimensions of bosonic and superstring
theory are algebraically encoded in the W33 / Z[ω] structure:

    V - 1 = 26  (bosonic),   λ = 10  (super),   k = V-1-λ = 16  (gauge)

Moreover: V - 1 - λ = k, i.e.:

    (bosonic dims) - (super dims) = W33 degree = gauge dimensions

**Proof:** Direct substitution:
    26 - 10 = 16  and  k = p^2+u+1 = 16  ✓

    p = N(1-ω) = 3  (ramified prime norm)
    u = |Z[ω]*|  = 6  (Eisenstein unit group)

    Bosonic: p^3 - 1 = 26
    Super:   u + p + 1 = 10
    Gauge:   p^2 + u + 1 = 16

junction of levels 2 and 3, and 24-1 = 23 = number of Monster
