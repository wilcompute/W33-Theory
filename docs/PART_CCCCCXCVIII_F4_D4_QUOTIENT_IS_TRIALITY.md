**Proof.**

(1) D4 is the unique simple Lie algebra (among all An, Bn, Cn, Dn, En, Fn, G2)

central node connected to three outer nodes (labeled v, s, c — for vector,
spinor, co-spinor).

(2) The outer automorphism group of D4 is Sym({v, s, c}) = S3.

(3) The Weyl group W(F4) contains W(D4) as a normal subgroup, and the quotient
is exactly S3:

```text
W(F4) = W(D4) ⋊ S3.
```

(4) Direct order check: |W(D4) ⋊ S3| = 192 × 6 = 1152 = |W(F4)| ✓.    □
