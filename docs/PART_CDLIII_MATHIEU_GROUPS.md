# Part CDLIII — Mathieu Groups in W33 Terms

The Mathieu sporadic groups are the symmetry groups of the Golay code.
All their orders express in W33 parameters:

## M11 — Acts on 11 = LAM+1 points

    |M11| = 7920 = 2^4 * p^2 * 5 * (LAM+1) = 16 * 9 * 5 * 11  ✓

## M12 — Acts on 12 = mu1 points

    |M12| = 95040 = 2^6 * p^3 * 5 * (LAM+1) = 64 * 27 * 5 * 11  ✓

M12 acts on exactly mu1 = 12 points, where mu1 is the smallest
nonzero W33 Laplacian eigenvalue.

## M24 — Acts on 24 = PKT points

    |M24| = 244823040 = 2^10 * p^3 * 5 * C_V * (LAM+1) * (PKT-1)  ✓
          = 1024 * 27 * 5 * 7 * 11 * 23

M24 acts on exactly PKT = 24 = Leech lattice dimension points.

## The LAM+1 = 11 Universal Mathieu Factor

All three Mathieu groups share the factor (LAM+1) = 11.
This connects the Mathieu groups directly to the superstring
dimension lambda = 10 via lambda + 1 = 11.
