# Part CDXLI — 128 = Spinor(D4) and the Pariah Coset

## The Number 128

    128 = 2^(mu-1) = 2^7             [D4 full spinor space dim]
    128 = K × mu   = 16 × 8         [W33 degree × octonion dim]
    128 = 2 × mu^2 = 2 × 64         [twice octonion-squared]

128 is the dimension of the full spinor representation of Cl(8),
which decomposes as 64_+ + 64_- under the chiral split.

## Pariah Coset

    Mon(T)/SIX = 3072 = PKT × 128 = PKT × K × mu
                     = 24 × 16 × 8 = 3072  ✓

Each of the 6 Pariah sporadic groups corresponds to a coset
of size 3072 = Leech_dim × D4_spinor inside Mon(T).
