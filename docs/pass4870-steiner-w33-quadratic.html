The quotient is the original substrate

The 120-pair relation is 40 disjoint K3 components, internally recovering the classical forty-triad partition. Collapse each component to one point. On those 40 fibers, declare two fibers adjacent when any Steiner pair belongs to the 2160 relation. Every adjacent fiber pair contributes all 3x3=9 pairs, so the lifted relation has 240x9=2160 edges.

quotient parameters: (v,k,lambda,mu) = (40,12,2,4)

An exact graph-isomorphism check identifies this quotient with the standard symplectic W(3,3) graph constructed from projective points of F3^4, and the induced projective symplectic group on the quotient has order 25,920.

Why the first bridge is quadratic

Pass4866 established Hom_PSp(H2,Q10)=0 and the reverse Hom space is also zero. Over F3, however, 2 is invertible, so homogeneous quadratic maps are equivalent to symmetric bilinear maps:

quadratic H2 -> Q10 <=> Hom_PSp(Sym^2 H2,Q10)

Because H2 is the 120-point permutation module, Sym^2 H2 decomposes into the diagonal permutation module plus the four unordered-pair orbit modules. For each transitive orbit module, Frobenius reciprocity reduces the Hom dimension to the Q10 fixed-space dimension of one orbit stabilizer.

Both dimensions occur exclusively on the W33-adjacency lift. Since Q10 is irreducible, every nonzero linear map in that two-dimensional Hom space is surjective as a linear map from Sym^2 H2 to Q10.

Evidence boundary: this is a finite characteristic-three association-module result. The two-dimensional Hom space does not choose a preferred quadratic map, physical coupling, normalization, or continuum gauge field. Extra structure would be required to make such a selection.
