## The Dual Heterotic Gauge Group Dimension = 496

Both heterotic string theories have gauge group dimension 496:

    E8×E8:  2 * dim(E8) = 2 * 248 = 496  ✓
    SO(32): 32*31/2 = 16*31 = K*(2K-1) = 496  ✓

In W33 terms:

    496 = K * Phi_6(u) = 16 * 31  ✓
    496 = 2 * mu * Phi_6(u) = 2 * 8 * 31  ✓

## The Key: 2K-1 = Phi_6(u)

    2*K - 1 = 31 = Phi_6(u)  ✓

The SO(32) characteristic prime 31 IS the sixth cyclotomic
polynomial evaluated at the six-kernel size u = 6.
This is why both heterotic theories have the same dimension:
both expressions equal K * Phi_6(u).

The duality of the two theories is the W33 symmetry K ↔ 2K
(under K → K/2 + 1/2 one goes from SO(32) to E8×E8 language).
