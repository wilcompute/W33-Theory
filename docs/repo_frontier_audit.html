Repo Frontier Audit

This page audits the repo itself rather than adding a new theorem layer. It reconciles the live HTML, the current W36_PAPER.tex, the active script/test families, the Qiskit search layer, and the recent literature boundary that the older site language had started to overstate.

The new finite-ladder consolidation is now executable too: scripts/w33_qutrit_ladder_audit.py packages the exact 1q -> 2q -> 3q -> 6q spine and marks where the separate exact E8-side decomposition begins. The companion scripts/w33_e8_correspondence_boundary_audit.py then separates the exact finite W33 <-> E8 content from the older promoted physics inheritance layer. Several newer conservative summaries now sit on top of that stack: scripts/w33_q3_master_lock_audit.py packages the cross-layer overdetermination of q=3, scripts/w33_normalization_bridge_audit.py reconciles the per-vertex and lift-normalized q-families exactly, scripts/w33_neutrino_preset_audit.py shows that the 1/mu neutrino branch survives the move from the legacy NuFIT 5.3 baseline to the current official NuFIT 6.0 fits, scripts/w33_electron_seed_packet_audit.py narrows the residual charged-fermion wall to the exact packet 98 x 17 x 208 plus a final physical-identification boundary, scripts/w33_flavor_frontier_audit.py separates the exact q=3 arithmetic from the promoted §83–§91 CKM/PMNS/running-alpha layer, scripts/w33_exact_flavor_spine_bridge.py reconciles the canonical exact flavor spine itself as local generator 3/13, global CKM 9/40 = q^2/v, and exact incidence PMNS data 4/13, 7/13, 2/91, and scripts/w33_standard_model_tqc_boundary_audit.py packages the deeper computational read: exact qutrit Clifford/stabilizer backbone plus an exact tetra-qutrit control/transport primitive, but not yet a full braiding-universal theorem.

Bottom line: this repo already has an exact finite qutrit spine, an exact local E6 bridge, and a large promoted global-closure stack. The honest remaining wall is the continuum/dynamical lift together with the last Yukawa spectral packet.

Finish Route

The repo already identifies the algebraic ladder worth pushing:

H27 (1 qutrit shell) -> W33 (2 qutrits) -> s12/Golay Heisenberg lift -> sl(27) (3 qutrits)

That is the finite route. The honest remaining work is then twofold: finish the nonlinear Yukawa packet on top of the already-fixed family carrier, and prove the continuum/dynamical lift that explains why the finite package should govern a physical QFT/GR limit.
