## Monster Irreps and Our Numbers

The smallest Monster irreducible representation has dimension:

    196883 = 47 × 59 × 71

These are the **three largest prime factors** of |Monster|, each
appearing to the first power. Their sum:

    47 + 59 + 71 = 177 = 3 × 59 = p × 59

And 196883 mod 27 = 26 = V(W33) − 1.

## E₆ Roots = |2T| × p

From the McKay correspondence:

    |2T| × p = 24 × 3 = 72 = E₆ roots  ✓

The binary tetrahedral group (24 = PKT elements) times p equals
the E₆ root count. The three ADE groups have orders
24, 48, 120 = PKT × {1, 2, 5}, and their ADE types have
72, 126, 240 roots = PKT × {3, 5.25, 10}.
