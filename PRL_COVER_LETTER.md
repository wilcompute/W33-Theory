- **α⁻¹ = k² − (|r| + |s| + 1) = 144 − 7 = 137** (0.026% from experiment)
- **sin²θ_W = |s|/(k + |s|) = 4/16 = 1/4 → 3/13 ≈ 0.2308** at the Z-pole (0.18% from PDG)

The uniqueness of SRG(40,12,2,4) — it is the *only* graph with these parameters up to isomorphism — means there are **no tunable parameters and no free moduli**.
