\section{The W33 Quadrangle Dual Visibility Theorem}
\label{sec:w33-quadrangle-dual-visibility}

The minimal support theorem identifies projective minimal $Z$ supports with ordinary
quadrangles of the generalized quadrangle $W(3,3)$.  If $Q$ is such a quadrangle,
then $Q$ has four cycle-edges.  Each edge lies on a unique totally isotropic line,
and each endpoint of that edge gives one point-line flag.  Hence every quadrangle
has an eight-flag boundary
\[
        F(Q)=\{(p,L):p\text{ is an endpoint of a cycle-edge }e\subset Q,
        \; L\text{ is the unique isotropic line containing }e\}.
\]
Thus
\[
        |F(Q)|=8.
\]

\begin{theorem}[W33 Quadrangle Dual Visibility Theorem]
For two distinct ordinary quadrangles $Q,Q'$ of $W(3,3)$, the $Z$-side overlap is
exactly the flag-boundary intersection number
\[
        \Omega_Z(Q,Q')=|F(Q)\cap F(Q')|.
\]
For every fixed quadrangle $Q$, the distribution over the remaining $1619$ quadrangles is
\[
        0^{1187},\qquad 1^{288},\qquad 2^{96},\qquad 3^{32},\qquad 4^{16}.
\]
Equivalently, the global unordered distribution is
\[
        0:961470,
        \qquad
        1:233280,
        \qquad
        2:77760,
        \qquad
        3:25920,
        \qquad
        4:12960.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Finite certificate]
The certificate reconstructs $W(3,3)$ from the alternating form on projective points
of $\mathbb F_3^4$, enumerates all ordinary quadrangles, attaches to each quadrangle
its eight incident endpoint-line flags, and computes all pairwise intersections.
The row distribution is independent of the chosen quadrangle and equals
\[
        0^{1187},1^{288},2^{96},3^{32},4^{16}.
\]
The ordered counts are
\[
        0:1922940,
        \quad
        1:466560,
        \quad
        2:155520,
        \quad
        3:51840,
        \quad
        4:25920,
\]
which divide by two to give the unordered distribution.  Therefore the $Z$-side
minimal logical overlap is exactly the intersection theory of quadrangle flag
boundaries.
\end{proof}
