\subsection*{BT984: a first fat-tower spectral convergence check}

BT983 replaces the non-shape-regular barycentric tower by the edgewise
(Freudenthal--Kuhn) tower.  BT984 tests the spectral side on a seed with known
continuum spectrum: the unit square with Dirichlet boundary, where
\[
  \lambda_{m,n}=\pi^2(m^2+n^2),\qquad m,n\ge 1.
\]
Using the Whitney--0/P1 finite-element generalized eigenproblem
\(Ku=\lambda Mu\), the edgewise tower keeps the minimum angle fixed at
\(45^\circ\) and drives the first eigenvalue
\[
  22.86577594,\ 20.50554490,\ 19.92978984,\ 19.78679229,\ 19.75110084
\]
toward \(2\pi^2=19.739208802\), with final relative error
\(6.03\times10^{-4}\) at level \(6\).  The barycentric control still gives
plausible low eigenvalues at small finite levels, but its minimum angle
collapses from \(45^\circ\) to \(1.8003^\circ\) by level \(4\), so it is not a
valid theorem-carrier for CMS/Dodziuk--Patodi/FEEC.  Witness:
\texttt{analysis/bt984\_edgewise\_laplacian\_convergence.py} and
\texttt{data/bt984\_edgewise\_laplacian\_convergence.json}.
