properties of the W(3,3) Ramanujan bipartite graph. We prove that W(3,3) is the
unique member of the Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak $W(3,q)$ family satisfying five

phase $\delta_{CP}=1.144$~rad from the Langlands root number $\varepsilon(\pi_2)=-1$;
(iii) the Yukawa hierarchy from Frobenius spectral ratios
$r_i\in\{(2\pm\sqrt{3})/6,\,1/3\}$; (iv) the cosmological constant from the graph
spectral zeta $\zeta_{W_{33}}(4)$; (v) the spectral dimension $d_s=2$ at the
asymptotic safety UV fixed point; and (vi) the full motivic decomposition
$h^1(\mathcal{V}_{W_{33}})=M_{E_{-3}}\oplus M_{E_{-4}}\oplus M_{E_{-7}}$,
showing that the three Standard Model generations are Chow motives of CM
elliptic curves. We enumerate 26 falsifiable predictions, of which 14 pass
current experimental tests and 3 are cleanly falsifiable by near-future experiments.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Definition and LPS Construction}

The Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak (LPS) family $W(3,q)$~\cite{LPS1988} consists
of Cayley graphs on $\mathrm{PSp}(4,\mathbb{F}_q)$ for prime powers $q$. For
$q=3$ the graph $W(3,3)$ is $k$-regular bipartite with $k=q(q+1)=12$, $v=40$
vertices per side, and all non-trivial eigenvalues bounded by $2\sqrt{k-1}=2\sqrt{11}$,
satisfying the Ramanujan condition~\cite{Ramanujan1916}.
