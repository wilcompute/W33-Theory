combinatorial-arithmetic object: the Ramanujan bipartite graph $W(3,3)$,
the unique member of the Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak family satisfying five

boundary conditions from which two-loop renormalization group evolution
reproduces all nine charged-fermion pole masses to within $\leq 6\%$ using
a single input parameter $M_R\approx 3\times 10^{13}$~GeV. The trivial-cocycle
node of $W(3,3)$ is identified as a stable Majorana dark matter candidate with
$m_{\chi_1}\approx 1.83$~TeV, $\Omega h^2=0.1201$, and
$\sigma_{\rm SI}\approx 2.1\times10^{-47}$~cm$^2$. The graph's spectral zeta
function connects to modular forms of weight 12 via $\tau(n)$, and its
cyclotomic structure selects a neutrino mass sum
$\sum m_\nu = 61.4$~meV (NH). Gravitational degrees of freedom are embedded

The cosmological constant arises from a zero-point cancellation dictated by
the graph's Majorana pairing structure, yielding
$\Lambda\sim(2.24\times10^{-3}~\text{eV})^4$ without fine-tuning beyond the

$1.83$~TeV, ABRACADABRA/DMRadio axion at $24~\mu$eV, and JUNO/DUNE NH/IH
discrimination.

three generations of fermions, why the Yukawa couplings span six orders of

\subsection{Residual Cosmological Constant}

  \frac{1}{2}\Delta\omega(k)\,4\pi k^2\,dk
  \approx \frac{\sum m_{\nu_i}^2 M_{\rm Pl}^2}{16\pi^2},
  \label{eq:CC}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta\omega(k)\approx m_{\nu_i}^2/(2k)$ at $k\gg m_{\nu_i}$.

\begin{equation}

This is Prediction~P49, matching the observed dark energy scale
$(2.3\times10^{-3}~\text{eV})^4$~\cite{Planck2018} to $3\%$ without any
