\begin{abstract}
We study the Ramanujan bipartite graph $W(3,3)$, the unique member of the
Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak $W(3,q)$ family satisfying five simultaneous
arithmetic conditions. We prove that $W(3,3)$ is the unique graph in this
family where: (i) the zeta-function poles encode cyclotomic polynomial values
exactly; (ii) the spectral multiplicities satisfy $k+g = q^{q}$; (iii) the
spectral parameters reconstruct Ramanujan's tau function at $p=2,3$; (iv) the
eigenspace poles lie in a Heegner quadratic field; and (v) the first
post-barrier Euler factor of the Heegner CM curve equals the $W(3,3)$ Frobenius
eigenvalue. We establish that the tau-reconstruction horizon equals
$\Phi_6(3)=7$, coinciding with the conductor prime of the Heegner CM curve
$E_{-7}$. We further prove a new spectral moment identity
$M_4(q) = q(q+1)(q^3+3q^2-q+1)$ valid for all $W(3,q)$, and derive a 3-term
recurrence for all even moments. As an application, we derive a neutrino mass
sum prediction $\sum m_\nu = 0.1015\,\text{eV}$ (NH, $\mu_{\text{eff}}^2=1/4$)
and a seesaw spectral cascade descending through the W(3,3) cyclotomic ladder.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Definition and LPS Construction}

The Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak (LPS) family $W(3,q)$ consists of Cayley graphs
on $\mathrm{PSp}(4,\mathbb{F}_q)$ for prime powers $q$. For $q=3$ the graph
$W(3,3)$ is $k$-regular bipartite with $k=q(q+1)=12$, $v=40$ vertices per
side, and all non-trivial eigenvalues bounded by $2\sqrt{k-1}=2\sqrt{11}$,
satisfying the Ramanujan condition.

\subsection{Corrected Bipartite Spectrum}

Being bipartite, $W(3,3)$ has fully symmetric spectrum: eigenvalue $\lambda$
with multiplicity $m$ is paired with $-\lambda$ at the same multiplicity.
The complete spectrum on $2v=80$ vertices is
\begin{equation}
  \sigma(A) = \{\pm 12^{(1)},\; \pm 2^{(f)},\; \pm 4^{(g)}\},
  \quad 2(1+f+g)=80,
\end{equation}
so $n_{\text{zero}}=0$ (corrected from prior draft).
