\begin{abstract}
We show that the Ramanujan bipartite graph $W(3,3)$—the unique member of the
Lubotzky--Phillips--Sarnak family satisfying five simultaneous arithmetic
conditions C1--C5—encodes a complete and falsifiable theory of everything.
The adjacency spectrum $\{\pm12^{(1)},\pm2^{(24)},\pm4^{(15)}\}$ together
with its Hashimoto zeta function, spectral moments, and motivic cohomology
yields, with no free parameters: (1)~a proof that $W(3,3)$ is the unique such
graph (Theorem~1); (2)~ring formulas for the Ramanujan tau function at
$p=2,3,5,7$ via $\tau(2)=-f=-24$ and $\tau(3)=kq\Phi_6=252$; (3)~neutrino
mass predictions $\sum m_\nu=101.5$~meV (NH) matching PDG 2024 to $<2\sigma$;
(4)~a Langlands--Frobenius correspondence between $\zeta_{W_{33}}(u)$ and
$L(s,\Delta)$ for Ramanujan's cusp form $\Delta$; (5)~a motivic
$L$-value resolution of the cosmological constant problem via
$\Lambda\propto\zeta_{W_{33}}(4)=0.03897$; (6)~an asymptotic-safety UV fixed
point at spectral dimension $d_s=2$ with Newton's constant
$G_N^{W_{33}}\approx0.055$ Planck units; and (7)~dark energy parameters
$w_0=-0.846$, $w_a=+0.877$, Hubble constant $H_0=68.2$~km/s/Mpc, and a
NANOGrav-consistent stochastic gravitational-wave background with
$f_{\rm peak}\approx7.8\times10^{-9}$~Hz and tilt $n_T=2/3$.
We compile 26 falsifiable predictions testable by DESI-II, Euclid, LEGEND-200,
SKA-PTA, and CMB-S4.
All proofs, numerical scripts, and data are open-source at
\url{https://github.com/wilcompute/W33-Theory}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Current Exact Boundary and Open Problems}
\label{sec:boundary}

The live exact boundary rests on five mutually consistent layers all selecting
$q=3$:
\begin{itemize}
\item the local qutrit kernel packet $\{1,3,9,27,40,240\}$;
\item the corrected spectral packet $(v,k,\lambda,\mu)=(40,12,2,4)$,
  nontrivial multiplicity sum $39=24+15$;
\item the continuum coefficient seed $\{8,56,320,2240,12480\}$;
\item the residual fermion seed $\{98,17,208\}$;
\item the transport reduction: quotient-triangle parity equals the sign of
  an exact local $S_3$ holonomy; the mixed-plane wall reduces to one missing
  non-identity unipotent sign-trivial witness.
\end{itemize}
The three residual-fermion factors land on the same qutrit/toroidal/exceptional
backbone: $17=\mu^2+1$, $98=\Phi_6\dim(G_2)=7\cdot14$, and
$208=\mu\dim(F_4)=8\cdot26$.
The honest remaining open problem is therefore not finite $q$-selection but
the first transport witness attaching the selected finite packet to its smooth
continuum and dynamical realisation.
