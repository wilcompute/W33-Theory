\begin{abstract}
We present W33-Theory, a Theory of Everything in which the physical vacuum
is identified with the unique generalized quadrangle
$\W = \mathrm{SRG}(40,12,2,4)$. All Standard Model parameters,
gravitational observables, and cosmological quantities are derived as theorems
from the spectral and combinatorial invariants of this single graph,
with \emph{zero free parameters}.

The adjacency spectrum $\{12^1, 2^{24}, (-4)^{15}\}$ of $\W$ yields:
the weak mixing angle $\sin^2\theta_W = q/\Phit = 3/13 = 0.2308$
(PDG: $0.2312\pm 0.0006$);
the strong coupling $\alpha_s(m_Z) = 20/\Phit^2 = 20/169 = 0.1183$
(PDG: $0.1181\pm 0.0011$);
three fermion generations ($q=3$);
four spacetime dimensions ($\mu=4$);
the electroweak hierarchy $\mew/\mpl = e^{-39}$;
and the dark energy fraction $\Omega_\Lambda = 9/13 = 0.6923$
(Planck 2018: $0.6847\pm 0.0073$).

The complement graph $\Wbar = \mathrm{SRG}(40,27,18,18)$ encodes the dark
sector. A \emph{Complement Duality Theorem} establishes
$L_{\mathrm{vis}} + L_{\mathrm{dark}} = 40I$ on non-trivial eigenspaces,
and the relative Laplacian determinant satisfies
$\detp L_{\mathrm{dark}}/\detp L_{\mathrm{vis}} = 3^{39}/2^{15}$,
recovering the hierarchy exponent~39 from vacuum geometry alone.
The dark energy equation of state follows: $w_0 = -19/27 \approx -0.704$,
$w_a = -1/180$, consistent with DESI DR2 (2025).

The W33 path integral $Z_{W33} = \sum_{\Gamma \subset W33} e^{-S[\Gamma]}$
is a finite sum and is therefore manifestly unitary, resolving the black hole
information paradox. Anomaly cancellation, $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}} = 0$, the
Yang--Mills mass gap, observer emergence, and the Kochen--Specker theorem for
both visible and dark sectors are all proved as combinatorial theorems.

The theory carries 39 active falsifiers spanning collider physics, direct
detection, gravitational wave astronomy, and precision cosmology.
None have been violated. The most immediately testable prediction is
$w_0 = -19/27$, which DESI DR3 and Euclid Year~2 will constrain to
$\pm 0.03$ within 1--2 years.
