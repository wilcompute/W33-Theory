\section{BT1113--BT1116 Weights, Couplings, and Seed-Independence}
\label{sec:bt1113-bt1116-weight-coupling-factorization}

The Koide-style generation-weight family
\[
w_g=1+\epsilon\cos\left(\theta+\frac{2\pi g}{3}\right)
\]
is a controlled traceless perturbation of the symmetric generation projector.  The projector observes the quadratic ratios $p_g=w_g^2/\sum_h w_h^2$.  If one naively identifies $w_g^2$ with charged-lepton masses, the Koide ratio becomes
\[
Q=\frac{\sum_g w_g^2}{(\sum_g w_g)^2}=\frac13+\frac{\epsilon^2}{6},
\]
so exact $Q=2/3$ would require $\epsilon^2=2$.  Thus the positive reservoir-weight ansatz is not itself the exact charged-lepton mass vector; an additional mass/Yukawa map is needed.

The coupling closures likewise remain conventions until an additional physical closure is supplied.  Channel-equal normalization gives
\[
g_1:g_2:g_3=1:1:1,
\]
whereas sector-equal energy gives
\[
g_1^2:g_2^2:g_3^2=4:\frac43:\frac12.
\]
These are falsifiable algebraic ledgers, not derived numerical Standard Model couplings.

Finally, the spectral-action product factorization explains which continuum outputs are already closed.  For an almost-commutative product $M\times F$,
\[
\Theta_{M\times F}(t)=\Theta_M(t)\Theta_F(t),
\qquad
\Theta_F(t)=N_F-t\operatorname{Tr}D_F^2+\frac{t^2}{2}\operatorname{Tr}D_F^4-\cdots .
\]
Dimensionless ratios formed at the same heat-kernel order share the same manifold integral, so the seed factor cancels.  Hence finite ratios such as $m_H^2/v^2=14/55$ and $\lambda_H=7/55$ are $F=W(3,3)$ invariants.  By contrast, the dimensionful gravitational scales involve distinct seed integrals, $a_0(M)$ and $a_2(M)$, so $\Lambda$ and $1/G$ require the quantitative curved $K3$ spectral-action computation.
