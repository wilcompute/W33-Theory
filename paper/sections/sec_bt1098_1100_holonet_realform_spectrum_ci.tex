\[
A_{12}=\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3),
\]
with trace pairing $\langle X,Y\rangle=-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(XY)$.  Runtime packet coordinates are normalized using anti-Hermitian Pauli and Gell--Mann generators,
\[
W_a=i\sigma_a/\sqrt2,
\qquad
C_a=i\lambda_a/\sqrt2.
\]
Thus the $1+3+8$ packet has an orthonormal real basis before physical coupling constants are applied.

The reservoir readout matrix has exact spectrum.  For
\[
K:T_{66}\to A_{12},
\]

\[
KK^*=\frac13 I_{12}+\frac13 J_{12}.
\]
Thus one packet direction has strength $13/3$, while the eleven transverse packet directions have strength $1/3$.  The remaining $54$ domain directions are in the kernel.  Operationally, this says the readout has one enhanced generation-uniform header direction, eleven transverse gauge-packet directions, and a kernel consisting of matter-bookkeeping and trace redundancies.
