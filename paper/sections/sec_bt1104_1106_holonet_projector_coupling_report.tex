\section{BT1104--BT1106 Holonet Projector and Couplings}
\label{sec:bt1104-bt1106-holonet-projector-coupling-report}

The holonet reservoir now has an explicit projector onto the readout-active packet directions.  The projector
\[
P_K=K_{\mathrm{white}}^*K_{\mathrm{white}}
\]
has rank $12$ and kills the $D_9$ cycle-sum matter-bookkeeping blocks.  Across the three fixed $F_{13}$ headers it uses the same rational block $B$, so the readout is generation-averaged rather than generation-selected.

The runtime gauge packet also carries symbolic coupling slots.  With
\[
\widehat Y=g_1Y,
\qquad
\widehat W_a=g_2W_a,
\qquad
\widehat C_a=g_3C_a,
\]
the packet brackets are
\[
[\widehat Y,\cdot]=0,
\qquad
[\widehat W_a,\widehat W_b]=-\sqrt2\,g_2\epsilon_{abc}\widehat W_c,
\qquad
[\widehat C_a,\widehat C_b]=-\sqrt2\,g_3f_{abc}\widehat C_c.
\]
Thus the holonet packet has separated readout geometry, bracket algebra, and symbolic coupling slots.
