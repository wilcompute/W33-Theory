\[
\boxed{8,8,24,24,24,24,24,24.}
\]

\[
\boxed{24(-1)^n.}
\]

\[
\boxed{E_4F_nE_4=(2+(-1)^n)E_4.}
\]
Equivalently, the scalar sequence is
\[
3,1,3,1,\ldots
\]
and the protected trace sequence is
\[
243,81,243,81,\ldots .
\]
Therefore the full endpoint recurrence separates into three compatible pieces:
\[
\boxed{
