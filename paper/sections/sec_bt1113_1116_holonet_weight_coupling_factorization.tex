\section{BT1113--BT1116 Holonet Weight, Coupling, and Seed Boundary}
\label{sec:bt1113-bt1116-holonet-weight-coupling-factorization}

The runtime generation weights are a controlled projector perturbation, not yet a mass formula.  The Koide-style family
\[
w_g=1+\epsilon\cos\left(\theta+\frac{2\pi g}{3}\right)
\]
preserves the shared trace-free packet to first order.  However, identifying $w_g^2$ directly with charged-lepton masses would require $\epsilon^2=2$ for exact Koide, outside the small positive-weight regime.  The holonet therefore keeps generation weights as runtime projector data until a separate mass/Yukawa map is derived.

The packet couplings have two useful closures to test: channel-equal $g_1:g_2:g_3=1:1:1$ and sector-equal energy $g_1^2:g_2^2:g_3^2=4:4/3:1/2$.  These are runtime normalization hypotheses, not final constants.

The continuum boundary is now clean.  Heat-kernel factorization for $M\times F$ makes dimensionless finite ratios seed-independent, while dimensionful gravity constants remain seed-dependent.  Thus the holonet finite packet ratios belong to the $W(3,3)$ finite triple, while the remaining gravity computation belongs to the curved $K3$ seed.
