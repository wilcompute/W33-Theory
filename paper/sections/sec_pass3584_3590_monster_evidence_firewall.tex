\subsection{Monster evidence firewall and the $81$-sector restriction problem}
\label{sec:monster-evidence-firewall}

The Monster-facing arithmetic of the $W(3,3)$ substrate contains several exact identities,
but these must be separated from structural claims.  The projective symmetry group is
\[
PSp(4,3)\cong U_4(2),\qquad |PSp(4,3)|=25920,
\]
and the full $E_6$ Weyl extension has order
\[
|W(E_6)|=51840=2\,|PSp(4,3)|.
\]
Both orders divide $|\mathbb M|$, and subgroup work on the Monster documents subgroups
isomorphic to $U_4(2)$.  This is a genuine subgroup direction, not merely an order
coincidence.  It does not yet identify the concrete forty-point permutation model used in
this work with a fixed subgroup in a computational Monster realization.

The exact arithmetic shadow includes
\[
196883=47\cdot59\cdot71,
\qquad
196884=1+196883=196560+18^2,
\]
and
\[
744=3\cdot248,
\qquad
1728=12^3,
\qquad
24=12\cdot2,
\qquad
248=240+2\cdot4.
\]
These identities are retained as exact search constraints.  They are not promoted to
mechanisms for moonshine, the Griess product, the Leech lattice, or photonic dynamics.

The next falsifiable structural target is an explicit embedding and class-fusion certificate
\[
\iota:PSp(4,3)\hookrightarrow\mathbb M.
\]
Such a certificate must give concrete Monster generators, verify the W33 relations and
order, determine conjugacy-class fusion, and restrict a specified Monster module.  The
central representation-theoretic question is whether the protected W33 Steinberg module
\[
\dim H_1=81
\]
occurs in that restriction, with what multiplicity, and with what integral form.  No
occurrence claim is made here.

We therefore enforce the evidence ladder
\[
\text{arithmetic}\;<\;\text{documented subgroup fact}\;<\;
\text{explicit intertwiner/class fusion}\;<\;\text{physical interpretation}.
\]
A lower-level identity may not be narrated as a higher-level mechanism without the missing
map and its executable certificate.
