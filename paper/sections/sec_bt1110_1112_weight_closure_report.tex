\label{sec:bt1110-bt1112-weight-closure-report}

The W33 generation ledger $27\oplus27\oplus27$ and the three $D_9$ bookkeeping blocks determine the symmetric baseline
\[
w=(1,1,1),
\qquad
M_w=\frac13\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^T.
\]
They do not by themselves determine a mass hierarchy.  A controlled traceless perturbation compatible with the Koide-style phase ledger is
\[
w_g=1+\epsilon\cos\left(\theta+\frac{2\pi g}{3}\right),
\qquad g=0,1,2,
\]
which satisfies $\sum_g(w_g-1)=0$ at first order.  This preserves the BT1108 trace-free quotient structure while allowing generation splitting.

For the gauge couplings, the reservoir geometry forces only positivity of $g_1,g_2,g_3$.  The most conservative baseline closure is the unified packet scale
\[
g_1:g_2:g_3=1:1:1.
\]
The best exploratory sector-energy closure equalizes the total contribution of the $1$, $3$, and $8$ summands:
\[
g_1^2=3g_2^2=8g_3^2,
\qquad
 g_1^2=4,\quad g_2^2=\frac43,\quad g_3^2=\frac12
\]
under the trace normalization $g_1^2+3g_2^2+8g_3^2=12$.  These remain normalization choices, not yet W33-derived coupling constants.
