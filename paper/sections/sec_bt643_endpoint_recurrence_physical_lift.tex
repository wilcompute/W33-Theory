\subsection{Endpoint Recurrence, Physical Lift, and Tetrahedral Parity Sheet}
\label{subsec:bt643-endpoint-physical-lift}

The folded Hashimoto endpoint theorem can now be separated into three layers.
First, the terminal distance-four endpoint sequence
\[
(F_n)_{fg}=e_n,\qquad \rho(f,g)=4,
\]
is governed by the full W33 Hashimoto polynomial
\[
P(x)=(x-11)(x-1)(x+1)(x^2-2x+11)(x^2+4x+11).
\]
The initial values are
\[
(e_0,\ldots,e_{10})=(0,0,3,28,268,3000,33195,365480,4020568,44210368,486310803),
\]
so the cubic endpoint is
\[
e_3=28=\mu=v-k.
\]
The full recurrence residual vanishes identically for the tested endpoint range,
while deleting the \((x+1)\)-factor leaves
\[
\boxed{24(-1)^n.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\frac{P(x)}{x+1}\bigg|_{x=-1}=2688=16\cdot168,
\qquad
\frac{24}{2688}=\frac1{112}.
\]
Thus the omitted sheet is not arbitrary: it is a normalized sign component of the
endpoint quotient.

Second, the Hodge-protected physical block compresses the endpoint dynamics to
\[
\boxed{
E_4F_nE_4=3E_4\quad(n\;\mathrm{even}),
\qquad
E_4F_nE_4=E_4\quad(n\;\mathrm{odd}).
}
\]
Consequently the protected trace clock is
\[
243,81,243,81,\ldots
\]
while the full terminal endpoint grows by the Ihara recurrence.  At the cubic
step the endpoint mass is
\[
\boxed{
160\cdot81\cdot28=12960\cdot28=362880=9!.
}
\]
This is the precise lift: the full folded Hashimoto endpoint carries the large
Ihara recurrence, and the physical Hodge block sees its protected parity clock.

Third, the residual \(24(-1)^n\) has an explicit tetrahedral carrier.  Let
\(S_4\) act regularly on the \(24\) labelings of a tetrahedron.  The even subgroup
\(A_4\) has order \(12\), the odd coset has order \(12\), and
\[
S_4/A_4\cong C_2.
\]
The sign scalar on this regular \(24\)-dimensional carrier has trace powers
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{tr}((-I_{24})^n)=24(-1)^n.
}
\]
Therefore the endpoint recurrence, physical Hodge clock, and tetrahedral parity
packet fit into one exact stack:
\[
\boxed{
\text{full Ihara endpoint recurrence}
\longrightarrow
\text{Hodge }E_4\text{ clock}
\oplus
\text{tetrahedral }24(-1)^n\text{ sign sheet}.
}
\]
