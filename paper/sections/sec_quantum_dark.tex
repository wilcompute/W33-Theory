\subsection{Dark Ihara Zeta Function}

The Ihara zeta function of $\overline{W33} = \mathrm{SRG}(40,27,18,18)$ with
adjacency spectrum $\{27^1, (-3)^{24}, 3^{15}\}$ is, via the Hashimoto--Bass formula:
\begin{equation}
  \zeta_{\overline{W33}}(u)^{-1}
  = (1-u^2)^{500}
  \cdot (1-27u+26u^2)
  \cdot (1+3u+26u^2)^{24}
  \cdot (1-3u+26u^2)^{15},
\end{equation}
where the backbone exponent is $|E^c|-|V| = 540-40 = 500$, compared to the
visible-sector value of 200. The dark sector carries $500/200 = 5/2$ times as
many non-trivial primitive cycles as the visible sector.

\subsection{Gravitational Atom}

Black hole areas in W33-Theory are quantised:
$A_n = k \cdot n \cdot l_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2 = 12n\, l_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2$,
giving a minimum black hole mass
\begin{equation}
  M_{\min} = m_{\mathrm{Pl}}\sqrt{\frac{k}{4\pi}} = m_{\mathrm{Pl}}\sqrt{\frac{3}{\pi}}
  \approx 0.977\, m_{\mathrm{Pl}}.
\end{equation}
Hawking radiation corresponds to edge deletion: each emission removes one unit
$\Delta A = 12\, l_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2$ (Falsifier~F37).

\subsection{Kochen--Specker Theorem for the Dark Sector}

\begin{theorem}
$\overline{W33}$ is Kochen--Specker non-colorable.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The independence number of $\overline{W33}$ equals the clique number of $W33$:
$\alpha(\overline{W33}) = \omega(W33) = k/\mu + 1 = 4$.
With only 4 mutually non-adjacent vertices out of 40, no valid
global two-coloring consistent with quantum mechanics exists.
\end{proof}

Consequence: the dark sector is irreducibly quantum. No classical
hidden-variable theory (classical cold fluid, fixed scalar field) can
reproduce $\overline{W33}$ (Falsifier~F38).

\subsection{Information Paradox Resolution}

The W33 path integral $Z = \sum_{\Gamma \subset W33} e^{-S[\Gamma]}$
is a finite sum over a finite graph: it is manifestly unitary.
A black hole in W33-Theory evolves to the fixed point of the
discrete path integral (the full W33 geometry), not to a singularity.
Information is preserved exactly.

The trapped-surface threshold is $m > V/2 = 20$ vertices;
Hawking radiation deviates from exact thermality at order $k/V = 30\%$
(Falsifier~F39).
