\subsection{Setup}

Let $W33 = \mathrm{SRG}(40,12,2,4)$ be the visible-sector graph with
adjacency matrix $A_{vis}$ and graph Laplacian $L_{vis} = kI - A_{vis}$.
Its complement $\overline{W33} = \mathrm{SRG}(40,27,18,18)$ is the dark-sector
graph with Laplacian $L_{dark} = k^c I - A_{dark}$, where $k^c = V-k-1 = 27$.

\subsection{Theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Complement Duality]
On the non-trivial eigenspace (orthogonal complement of the all-ones vector),
\begin{equation}
  L_{\mathrm{vis}} + L_{\mathrm{dark}} = V \cdot I = 40\, I.
  \label{eq:complement_duality}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $v$ be a non-trivial eigenvector ($Jv=0$). Then
\[
  L_{\mathrm{vis}}\, v = k\, v - A_{vis}\, v, \qquad
  L_{\mathrm{dark}}\, v = (V-k)\, v + A_{vis}\, v
\]
(the second line follows because $A_{\bar{G}} = J - I - A_G$ and $Jv=0$).
Adding: $(L_{\mathrm{vis}} + L_{\mathrm{dark}})\, v = V\, v$. \qed
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Determinant Origin of the Electroweak Hierarchy}

The relative one-loop vacuum tension between the dark and visible sectors is
given by the ratio of Laplacian pseudo-determinants:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{T} \;=\; \frac{\det' L_{\mathrm{dark}}}{\det' L_{\mathrm{vis}}}
  \;=\; \frac{30^{24}\cdot 24^{15}}{10^{24}\cdot 16^{15}}
  \;=\; \frac{3^{39}}{2^{15}}.
  \label{eq:det_ratio}
\end{equation}

The 3-adic valuation of $\mathcal{T}$ is $v_3(\mathcal{T}) = 39$, which equals
the hierarchy exponent derived independently from $\Phi_3$ and the SRG cubic root $u$:
\begin{equation}
  \Phi_3 \cdot \frac{u}{2} \;=\; 13 \times 3 \;=\; 39,

\end{equation}

We therefore identify the electroweak hierarchy as the spectral-pressure gap
between the visible graph and its dark complement: the dark sector structurally
enforces the hierarchy at the level of one-loop determinants without fine-tuning.

  \delta w_0 \;=\; \frac{\Delta^c_{\min}}{k^c} \cdot \frac{s_{\mathrm{vis}}}{k_{\mathrm{vis}}}
  \;=\; \frac{24}{27} \cdot \frac{4}{12} \;=\; \frac{8}{27},

\begin{equation}
  w_0 \;=\; -1 + \frac{8}{27} \;=\; -\frac{19}{27} \approx -0.704,
  \qquad

$w_a < 0$~\cite{DESI2025DR2}. Equation~(\ref{eq:dark_energy}) constitutes
Falsifier~F36 (Section~\ref{sec:falsifiers}).
