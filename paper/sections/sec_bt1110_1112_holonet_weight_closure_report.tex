The runtime generation baseline is symmetric:
\[
w=(1,1,1).
\]
A controlled holonet splitting can be introduced by
\[
w_g=1+\epsilon\cos\left(\theta+\frac{2\pi g}{3}\right),
\]
which is first-order traceless across the three generation headers.  This lets the runtime perturb generation weights without destroying the shared trace-free readout packet.

The gauge packet admits symbolic coupling slots $g_1,g_2,g_3$.  The reservoir geometry keeps the projector invariant under the kinetic metric convention and therefore requires only $g_i>0$.  Two useful runtime closures are the unified packet scale $1:1:1$ and the sector-equal energy closure
\[
g_1^2:g_2^2:g_3^2=4:\frac43:\frac12.
\]
These are runtime conventions to test, not final derived constants.
