\section{BT1101--BT1102 Whitened Reservoir Readout and Gauge Bracket}
\label{sec:bt1101-bt1102-whitening-structure}

The reservoir readout $K:T_{66}\to A_{12}$ has row Gram matrix
\[
KK^*=\frac13 I_{12}+\frac13 J_{12}.
\]
Let
\[
P_u=\frac1{12}J_{12},
\qquad
P_t=I_{12}-\frac1{12}J_{12}.
\]
Then
\[
KK^*=\frac{13}{3}P_u+\frac13P_t,
\qquad
(KK^*)^{-1/2}=\sqrt{\frac3{13}}P_u+\sqrt3\,P_t.
\]
The whitened reservoir readout is
\[
K_{\mathrm{white}}=(KK^*)^{-1/2}K,
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{white}}K_{\mathrm{white}}^*=I_{12}.
\]
The domain projector is
\[
P_K=K^*(KK^*)^{-1}K=K_{\mathrm{white}}^*K_{\mathrm{white}},
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}P_K=12,
\qquad
\dim\ker K=54.
\]
Thus the enhanced uniform packet mode and the eleven transverse packet modes are normalized into an orthonormal readout coordinate system.

The normalized compact gauge packet has bracket
\[
A_{12}=\mathfrak u(1)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3),
\]
with $\mathfrak u(1)$ central.  For the anti-Hermitian trace-normalized basis
\[
W_a=\frac{i}{\sqrt2}\sigma_a,
\qquad
C_a=\frac{i}{\sqrt2}\lambda_a,
\]
one has
\[
[W_a,W_b]=-\sqrt2\,\epsilon_{abc}W_c,
\qquad
[C_a,C_b]=-\sqrt2\,f_{abc}C_c,
\]
where $[\lambda_a,\lambda_b]=2i f_{abc}\lambda_c$.  All cross-sector brackets vanish.  Physical gauge couplings may later rescale the three direct summands, but the normalized algebraic bracket is fixed.
