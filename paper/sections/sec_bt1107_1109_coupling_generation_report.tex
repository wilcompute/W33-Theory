\section{BT1107--BT1109 Coupling Constraints and Generation Weights}
\label{sec:bt1107-bt1109-coupling-generation-report}

Let
\[
G_A=\operatorname{diag}(g_1^2I_1,g_2^2I_3,g_3^2I_8)
\]
be the coupling-scaled packet metric and set
\[
K_g=G_A^{1/2}K_{\mathrm{white}}.
\]
Then
\[
K_gK_g^*=G_A.
\]
With kinetic metric $G_A^{-1}$ the domain projector is unchanged:
\[
K_g^*G_A^{-1}K_g=K_{\mathrm{white}}^*K_{\mathrm{white}}=P_K.
\]
Thus the reservoir geometry forces only $g_i>0$; numerical coupling ratios require an additional closure principle.  Optional closures include
\[
g_1^2+3g_2^2+8g_3^2=12,
\]
unified scale $g_1=g_2=g_3$, or sector-equal total energy $g_1^2=3g_2^2=8g_3^2$.

Generation splitting is introduced by positive weights $w_0,w_1,w_2$ and
\[
s=w_0^2+w_1^2+w_2^2.
\]
Replace the symmetric readout by
\[
K_w=s^{-1/2}[w_0\pi_{12}\; w_1\pi_{12}\; w_2\pi_{12}].
\]
After whitening, the domain projector has generation blocks
\[
P_w(g,h)=\frac{w_gw_h}{s}P_{\mathrm{tf},13},
\]
where $P_{\mathrm{tf},13}=\pi_{12}^T(\pi_{12}\pi_{12}^T)^{-1}\pi_{12}$.  This preserves $\operatorname{rank}P_w=12$ and keeps every $D_9$ direction in the kernel.
