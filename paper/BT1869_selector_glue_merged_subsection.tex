\subsection{Metric-canonical selector and the remaining sign-kernel lift}

The E8-side selector used by the runtime aperture stack is now canonical at every quotient level that is
visible on the mod-$2$ support shadow.  Smith-normal-form extraction first isolates the valuation-one,
rank-$8$ mod-$2$ E8 shadow.  Exact support recursion then lowers the old support-$76$ candidate to the
true support minimum $60$, with six minimizers.  The vertex metric selects minimizer $2$, and the
recovered chain-to-tetracode matrix independently selects the same minimizer in the tetracode metric
gauge.  The selected basis is
\[
  (3,68),\quad (4,42),\quad (38,65),\quad (90,144).
\]
These four pairs label the four runtime striations in the aperture table, the compiled trace schema, and
the shot protocol.

The tetracode quotient has also been sharpened.  The transported block-permutation quotient is $S_4$ of
order $24$; acting on the selected support-$60$ minimizer it has orbit size $24$, trivial stabilizer, and
intersection with the six support-$60$ minimizers only at the selected minimizer.  Thus the selector is
transported-$S_4$ rigid on the support shadow.

The local glue stabilizer has the exact tetracode-coordinate split
\[
  |G_{\mathrm{glue}}|=48=2\cdot24.
\]
The $24$-part is the transported $S_4$ quotient.  The remaining size-$2$ sign kernel is represented, at
the lattice-bookkeeping level, by the central inversion $-I$ in the full $A_2$ lattice automorphism group
$O(A_2)$ on each of the four $A_2$ planes.  This representative preserves the $A_2$ Gram form and fixes
the winner-$2$ support mask because it reduces to the identity mod $2$.  It is better described as a
central-inversion phase class in $O(A_2)/W(A_2)$, not as an ordinary element of the plain Weyl group
$W(A_2)$.
