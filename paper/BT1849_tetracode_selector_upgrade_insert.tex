\begin{result}[The E8 selector is metric-canonical and tetracode-rigid at the transported $S_4$ level]
The support-minimal E8-side selector is no longer merely a candidate.  The Smith-normal-form route first
extracts the valuation-one, rank-$8$ mod-$2$ E8 shadow; exact recursion lowers the selector from the old
support-$76$ candidate to support $60$ with six minimizers; the vertex metric selects minimizer $2$; and
the recovered chain-to-tetracode matrix independently selects the same minimizer in the tetracode metric
gauge.  The canonical selector is
\[
  (3,68),\quad (4,42),\quad (38,65),\quad (90,144).
\]
Moreover, transporting the tetracode block-permutation quotient $S_4$ through the recovered matrix gives
a $24$-element orbit with trivial stabilizer, and that orbit intersects the six support-$60$ minimizers
only at the selected minimizer itself.  Thus the runtime aperture stack uses a selector that is canonical
under both the vertex and tetracode metric gauges and rigid under the strongest explicit transported
quotient currently present in the repository.

\emph{Boundary.}  This closes the metric selector and transported $S_4$ quotient.  It does not yet close
the full local $A_2$/Weyl/glue stabilizer refinement; that requires an explicit transported local action
beyond the tetracode block-permutation quotient.
