\subsection{Exact support-minimal E8 selector update (BT950--BT952)}\label{subsec:bt952-exact-e8-selector}

The earlier support-76 selector candidate is superseded.  Let
\(H=\ker(A_2)/\operatorname{im}(A_2)\) be the rank-eight mod-2 chain shadow and
let
\[
  B(x,y)=\frac{x^T A y}{2}\pmod 2
\]
be the canonical divided alternating form.  The support of a class is measured
by the Hamming support of its chosen chain representative in the fixed BT925
coordinate gauge.

\begin{theorem}[Exact support-minimal hyperbolic selector]
Among all hyperbolic decompositions
\[
  H = \langle e_1,f_1\rangle \perp \cdots \perp \langle e_4,f_4\rangle,
  \qquad B(e_i,f_i)=1,
\]
the minimum total support is
\[
  \sum_{i=1}^4 \bigl(|e_i|+|f_i|\bigr)=60.
\]
There are exactly six unordered minimizing decompositions in the BT925 gauge,
and every minimizer has sorted support profile
\[
  [6,6,6,6,6,8,10,12].
\]
In particular, the previous profile
\([6,6,6,10,10,10,14,14]\) of total support 76 is not minimal.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Machine certificate]
BT951 performs Bellman recursion over symplectic subspaces of \(H\).  Each
state is encoded as a subspace of the 8-bit vector space.  At a state \(W\), the
verifier enumerates unordered hyperbolic pairs \((e,f)\subset W\) with
\(B(e,f)=1\), then recurses on the symplectic orthogonal quotient
\(W\cap e^\perp\cap f^\perp\).  The recursion memoizes subspaces and minimizes
support exactly.  The completed run explores 5914 subspace states and returns
minimum 60 with six unordered minimizers.  The six certificates are recorded in
\texttt{data/bt951\_exact\_support\_minimal\_selector.json}.
\end{proof}

The Smith-normal-form extractor BT950 also corrects the integral routing.  For
\(UAV=D\), the eight \(d_i=2\) torsion directions live on the left/codomain
basis and therefore pull back by columns of \(U^{-1}\).  These columns carry a
rank-eight hyperbolic divided form modulo two, but their raw divided integral
