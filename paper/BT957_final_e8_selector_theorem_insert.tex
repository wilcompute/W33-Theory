\subsection{The combined support--metric E8 selector (BT951--BT957)}\label{subsec:bt957-final-e8-selector}

The support-minimal selector is now exact, and the residual metric ambiguity is
resolved in both independent E8 gauges currently available in the repository.

\begin{theorem}[Support--metric E8 selector]
Let \(H=\ker(A_2)/\operatorname{im}(A_2)\) carry the divided alternating form
\[
  B(x,y)=\frac{x^T A y}{2}\pmod 2.
\]
Among all hyperbolic decompositions of \(H\), the exact minimum total support is
\(60\).  There are six unordered support-minimal decompositions in the BT925
coordinate gauge, all with sorted support profile
\[
  [6,6,6,6,6,8,10,12].
\]
All six share the hyperbolic pair \((90,144)\).  Testing these six minimizers in
the vertex E8 metric gauge and independently in the tetracode E8 metric gauge
selects the same minimizer:
\[
  \boxed{\bigl[(3,68),(4,42),(38,65),(90,144)\bigr].}
\]
In the vertex gauge this minimizer has score
\[
  \operatorname{tr}=38,\qquad \|G\|_F^2=444,\qquad \max |G_{ij}|=8,
\]
and in the tetracode gauge it has score
\[
  \operatorname{tr}=56,\qquad \|G\|_F^2=1320,\qquad \max |G_{ij}|=16.
\]
It is therefore the unique currently verified support-minimal and dual-metric
preferred selector.
\end{theorem}

\texttt{data/bt951\_exact\_support\_minimal\_selector.json},
\texttt{data/bt954\_metric\_selector\_among\_support60.json}, and

\end{proof}
