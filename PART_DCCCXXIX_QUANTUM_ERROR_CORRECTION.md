## Thesis

## The stabilizer construction

A quantum error-correcting code \([[n,k,d]]\) encodes \(k\) logical qubits into \(n\) physical qubits with distance \(d\). The stabilizer group \(\mathcal{S}\) is generated by Pauli operators that fix every codeword.

In W(3,3), identify:
- **Physical qubits:** \(n = |E| = 40\) photon edge-modes.
- **Stabilizer generators:** elements of \(\mathrm{Aut}(W(3,3))\) acting on the edge register.
- **Logical qubits:** the gauge-invariant sector.
- **Code distance:** \(d = q^2 + q + 1 = 13\) (points of \(PG(2,3)\), the minimum-weight undetectable error).

The \([[40, k, 13]]\) code structure means any error affecting fewer than 13 edge-modes is automatically corrected by the gauge symmetry. This is precisely why the SM is renormalizable: perturbative corrections up to order \(d-1 = 12\) are absorbed by counterterms, and only gauge-invariant quantities survive.
