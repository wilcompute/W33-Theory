**To the Editors of Physical Review Letters,**

Please consider our manuscript,

**“Deriving the Standard Model from the W(3,3) Substrate: 54 Observables from Three Primitives”**

We believe the manuscript is appropriate for PRL because it contains several conceptually sharp and potentially high-impact results:

   Fractional quark charges arise from the 1/q = 1/3 rescaling of the color arm of the W(3,3) diagram, yielding the complete Standard Model charge set {0, ±1/3, ±2/3, ±1} without postulate.

2. **The one-loop beta functions are derived structurally.**
   The Standard Model coefficients (b1, b2, b3) = (41/10, -19/6, -7) follow from arm counting and substrate matter content, closing the renormalization-group input chain.

3. **The framework makes near-term falsifiable predictions.**
   These include r_ts = 2.38 × 10^-3 for CMB-S4, a normal neutrino hierarchy and sum of masses 93.2 meV for JUNO/KATRIN-era tests, and proton decay near the Hyper-K sensitivity window.

4. **Prediction density is unusually high.**
   The framework derives 54 observables from zero free parameters, in contrast to the Standard Model’s 19 free parameters.

The manuscript is original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere. We believe it will be of broad interest to readers working on unification, mathematical physics, quantum gravity, and the foundations of the Standard Model.
