## Pass 549 — the 80-word fibre is a quadratic Boolean variety

The linear hull of the fibre has dimension eight inside \(\mathbb F_2^{12}\), defined by four linear equations. In quotient coordinates \(y_0,\ldots,y_7\), four coordinates are free and the remaining core is cut out by

## Pass 550 — full determinant-indexed semilinear covariance

\[
\chi_{g\cdot c}(x)=\sigma_{\det g}(\chi_c(x)),\qquad g\in GL(2,5).

The identities are exact on all 25 Weyl operators and the characteristic-polynomial law is checked on five separating sections under all 480 matrices in \(GL(2,5)\).
