## Core Theorem (Pass 5880)

Let B(G) be the Hashimoto (non-backtracking) operator on directed edges of G.

1. All non-trivial eigenvalues λ of B(G) satisfy |λ| = √3 = q^{1/2} where q = 3 (the valence minus 1 of a 4-regular cover), so W33 is **Ramanujan** in the sense of the non-backtracking spectrum.

3. This common log-modulus equals the **resonance decay rate γ** of the W33 photonic holonet, establishing `γ = (1/2) log 3` as a spectral invariant.

ζ_{W33}(u)^{-1} = (1 − u²)^{|E|−|V|} · det(I − u A + u² (D − I))
