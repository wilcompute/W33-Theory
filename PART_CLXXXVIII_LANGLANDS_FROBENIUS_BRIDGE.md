## §2  Frobenius Splitting Table

The splitting type of an odd prime $p \nmid 3$ is determined by $p \bmod 12$:

| $p \bmod 12$ | Layer | Interpretation |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1  | splits completely | all four ideals |
| 5  | Gaussian sheet only | splits in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, inert in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ |
| 7  | Eisenstein sheet only | inert in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, splits in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ |
| 11 | inert in both | single prime ideal |
| 2,3 | ramified | $p \mid 12$ |

This follows immediately from quadratic reciprocity:

- $p$ splits in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ $\iff$ $p \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$
- $p$ splits in $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ $\iff$ $p \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$
