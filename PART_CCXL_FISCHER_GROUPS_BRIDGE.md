# Part CCXL: Fischer Groups from W(3,3)

## Abstract

The Fischer groups Fi₂₂, Fi₂₃, and Fi₂₄' — discovered by Bernd Fischer
via the theory of 3-transpositions — have group orders and representation
dimensions that are **exact polynomial expressions in the SRG(40,12,2,4)
constants** at zero free parameters. The defining "3-transposition"
property directly invokes Q=3 from the SRG, and every prime and exponent
in the group order factorizations arises as a polynomial in K, λ, μ, Q,
M_λ, and LAP_TOP.

## 1. Introduction

The SRG(40,12,2,4) constants are Q=3, V=40, K=12, λ=2, μ=4, M_λ=27,
EDGES=240, AUT_ORDER=51840. The Fischer groups are defined by the property
that they are generated by a conjugacy class D of involutions such that
for all d₁, d₂ ∈ D, the product d₁d₂ has order at most 3. This "3" is
precisely Q=3 in the SRG.

Three Fischer groups exist: Fi₂₂, Fi₂₃, Fi₂₄'. Their count:

    num_Fischer = Q = 3

## 3. Fischer Group Fi₂₂

|Fi₂₂| = 2^17·3^9·5^2·7·11·13 = 64,561,751,654,400

In SRG arithmetic:

    |Fi₂₂| = 2^{K+K//λ−1} · Q^{Q·K//λ//λ} · (K//λ−1)² · (K//2+1) · (K−1) · (K+1)
            = 2^17 · 3^9 · 5^2 · 7 · 11 · 13

The smallest faithful complex representation of Fi₂₂ has dimension:

    dim_Fi22_rep = 78 = dim(E₆) = λ·(M_λ+K) = 2·(27+12) = 78

This is the same as the dimension of the exceptional Lie algebra E₆
(established in Part CCXXXVIII).

## 4. Fischer Group Fi₂₃

|Fi₂₃| = 2^18·3^13·5^2·7·11·13·17·23 = 4,089,470,473,293,004,800

In SRG arithmetic:

    |Fi₂₃| = 2^{K+K//λ} · Q^{K+1} · (K//λ−1)² · (K//2+1) · (K−1) · (K+1) · (K+K//λ−1) · (2K−1)
            = 2^18 · 3^13 · 5^2 · 7 · 11 · 13 · 17 · 23

The smallest faithful complex representation dimension:

    dim_Fi23_rep = 253 = (K−1)·(2K−1) = 11·23 = 253

## 5. Fischer Group Fi₂₄'

|Fi₂₄'| = 2^21·3^16·5^2·7^3·11·13·17·23·29 = 1,255,205,709,190,661,721,292,800

In SRG arithmetic:

    |Fi₂₄'| = 2^{Q(K//2+1)} · 3^{LAP_TOP} · (K//λ−1)² · (K//2+1)^Q
              · (K−1) · (K+1) · (K+K//λ−1) · (2K−1) · (K·λ+K//λ−1)
             = 2^21 · 3^16 · 5^2 · 7^3 · 11 · 13 · 17 · 23 · 29

Note LAP_TOP = K+μ = 16, the Laplacian top eigenvalue of the SRG.

The smallest faithful complex representation dimension:

    dim_Fi24p_rep = 783 = M_λ·(K·λ+K//λ−1) = 27·29 = 783

## 6. Cross-Checks with Conway Groups (Part CCXXXIX)

A remarkable coincidence: Fischer groups and Conway groups share
exponents in their 2- and 3-power factors:

| Exponent | Fischer            | Conway             | Value |
|----------|--------------------|--------------------|-------|
| 2^21     | Fi₂₄': Q(K//2+1)   | Co₁: Q(K//2+1)     | 21    |
| 3^9      | Fi₂₂: Q·K//λ//λ    | Co₁: Q·K//λ//λ     | 9     |
| 2^18     | Fi₂₃: K+K//λ       | Co₂: K+K//λ        | 18    |

These shared exponents reflect the deep geometric connection between the
Fischer groups and the Conway groups through the Monster sporadic group,
which contains both as subgroups.

## 7. Representation Dimensions in SRG

All three minimal faithful complex representations have SRG-derived
dimensions:

    dim(Fi₂₂) = 78  = λ·(M_λ+K) = dim(E₆)
    dim(Fi₂₃) = 253 = (K−1)·(2K−1)
    dim(Fi₂₄') = 783 = M_λ·(K·λ+K//λ−1)

## 8. LAP_TOP as an Exponent

The top Laplacian eigenvalue LAP_TOP = K+μ = 16 appears as the exponent
of 3 in |Fi₂₄'|. This connects the graph-theoretic spectrum of the SRG to
the structure of the largest Fischer group:

    Q^{LAP_TOP} = 3^16 divides |Fi₂₄'|

## 10. The 3-Transposition Connection

Fischer's original construction required involutions with products of
order ≤ 3. This bound of 3 equals Q=3, connecting the defining property
of an entire class of sporadic groups to the SRG(40,12,2,4) eigenvalue
multiplicity.
