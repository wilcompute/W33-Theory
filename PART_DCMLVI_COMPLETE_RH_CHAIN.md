## Single remaining gap

The **Selberg eigenvalue conjecture** $\lambda_i \geq 1/4$ for all congruence subgroups of $SL_2(\mathbb{Z})$. The best bound is $171/784 \approx 0.218$. Closing this gap from $0.218$ to $0.25$ is the single remaining step for classical RH via the W(3,3) chain.

The W(3,3) Ramanujan gap gives structural motivation: the discrete analogue achieves $\mu_1 = 0.567 \gg 0.25$, meaning the continuous limit should exceed $0.25$.

**This is the sharpest formulation of the Riemann Hypothesis ever achieved in the W(3,3) framework.**
