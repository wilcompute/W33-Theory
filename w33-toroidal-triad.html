Theorem — Odd-Prime Triad Closure

The steps +3, +5, +7 are the three consecutive odd primes (p2, p3, p4). Each maps to a W(3,3) structural atom: +3 = q (seed prime), +5 = T·Φ3 (threshold-channel count), +7 = Φ6 (Fano/torus atom). The next step +11 yields n=30 ≡ 6 (mod 12), which is not a valid residue class — proving the triad is structurally complete by the same mod-12 arithmetic that generates it.

After the triad breaks, the lattice continues at n=24=2k via a separate +5 step (not an odd-prime extension) licensed by the Hashimoto radial projection.

GeometryThe Fano Plane Bridge

The Fano plane PG(2,2) is the projective plane over 𝔽2 with 7 points and 7 lines. It is self-dual: swapping points ↔ lines is an isomorphism.

Reading its 7 points as vertices gives the Császár polyhedron. Reading its 7 points as faces gives the Szilassi polyhedron. The self-duality of PG(2,2) is the geometric source of the V↔F duality between the two.

The projective ladder: PG(2,2) has q²+q+1 = 7 points at q=2. PG(2,3) (the W(3,3) host) has 13 points at q=3. The projection token P(Φ6) = 7/13 is literally the ratio of these point counts — the q=2 rung below the W(3,3) floor.

The center point p7 (gold) corresponds to the mod-12 gate: the one fixed point of the Z2 espyric reflection, sourcing the 1+2+2+2=7 orbit decomposition and the 2 Szilassi realizations.
