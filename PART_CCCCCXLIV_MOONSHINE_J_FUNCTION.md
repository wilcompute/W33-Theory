# PART_CCCCCXLIV — Moonshine and the j-Function

## The Constant Term 744

The modular j-function has expansion:
\[
j(\tau) = q^{-1} + 744 + 196884q + 21493760q^2 + \cdots
\]
where \(q = e^{2\pi i\tau}\). The constant term 744 satisfies:
\[
\boxed{744 = |\mathrm{Aut}(W(2,2))| + f = 720 + 24,}
\]
where \(|\mathrm{Aut}(\text{Doily})| = |S_6| = 720\) and \(f = 24\) is the multiplicity of the positive eigenvalue \(r=2\) of W(3,3).

This is a **new observation**: the j-function constant term decomposes into the automorphism group of the predecessor geometry W(2,2) plus the spectral multiplicity of the successor geometry W(3,3). The two symplectic polar spaces GQ(2,2) and GQ(3,3) together encode the j-function's constant.

## The First Coefficient 196884

\[
196884 = 196883 + 1 \quad (\text{McKay’s observation for Monster moonshine}).
\]

Also:
\[
196884 = 25920 \times 7 + 15444,\qquad 15444 = 4 \times 3861 = 4 \times 3 \times 1287.
\]
This does not yield a clean relation, but McKay’s chain proceeds through the exceptional groups \(E_6, E_7, E_8\), whose Weyl group orders include 51840 = \(2|\mathrm{Aut}(W(3,3))|\).
