The overlap-$4$ fusion nevertheless has a clean all-$q$ graph theorem.  Let

\[
 \boxed{q-1}
\]

\[
 \#\triangle(\mathcal G_4)=T(q)(q-1).

There is also an exact cardinality-optimal sparse apartment basis for every
$GQ(q,q)$.  Root the Levi graph and choose a BFS geodesic spanning tree.  Its
diameter is four and its girth is eight.  For every cotree edge, the fundamental
cycle has length at most eight; since it is nonzero, girth forces its length to
be exactly eight.  Thus every cotree fundamental cycle is an apartment.  The
number of cotree edges is the Levi cycle rank
\[
 E-(V-1)=q^4,
\]
so these apartments form a basis of $H_1$:
\[
 \boxed{\text{a minimum apartment basis has exactly }q^4\text{ apartments}.}
\]
At $q=3$ this compresses the full $1620$-apartment frame to an exact
$81$-apartment basis.

The same basis exposes the critical group.  If $F$ is any integral fundamental
cycle matrix, Cauchy--Binet gives
\[
 \det(F^{\mathsf T}F)=\tau(\Gamma),
\]
because its nonzero maximal minors are $\pm1$ and are indexed by complements of
spanning trees.  For the Levi graph of $GQ(q,q)$, writing
\[
 f=\frac{q(q+1)^2}{2},\qquad g=\frac{q(q^2+1)}2,
\]
the Levi Laplacian spectrum gives
\[
 \boxed{\tau(\Gamma)=(q^2+1)^{f-1}(q+1)^{2g}}.
\]
At $q=3$ this is $2^{83}5^{23}$, exactly the tree order already frozen in
Pass5031.  For the deterministic root-$0$ BFS basis used in the exact replay,
the $81\times81$ Gram characteristic polynomial factors as
\[
