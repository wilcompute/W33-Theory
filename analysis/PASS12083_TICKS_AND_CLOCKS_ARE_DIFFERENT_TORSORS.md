# Pass 12083: ticks and clocks are different ℤ₃-torsors

Claude track. Completes 12072.

## The two torsors

Over each event there are three ticks and three clocks, and both sets are ℤ₃-torsors, but with different symmetry:

- **Ticks** are shifted by the Pauli translations: affine, event-dependent, the normal F₃⁴ of ASp(4,3).
- **Clocks** are shifted by the Eisenstein unit ω: central, acting at every event at once (12080).

## Obstructions to identifying them

1. Sp(4,3) is perfect, so 3 × Sp(4,3) has a unique complement to its centre.
2. |ASp(4,3)| = 4,199,040 does not divide |W(E8)|: the 3-parts are 3⁸ versus 3⁵.
3. Restricted to any copy of Sp(4,3), the ticks split 40 + 80 while the clocks form one orbit of 120 (12072).

## Reading

A clock is a cube root of unity in E8's Eisenstein structure. A tick label is a Pauli phase. They are equal in number
but carry inequivalent symmetry.
