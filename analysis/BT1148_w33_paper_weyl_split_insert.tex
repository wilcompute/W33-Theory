\begin{remark}[Ricci-flat $K3$ Weyl basket]
\label{rem:k3-weyl-signature-split}
For Ricci-flat $K3$, the corpus curvature slot is also the integrated Weyl slot.
With $\chi=24$ and $\tau=-16$, the signature relation gives
\[
  W_+^{\rm norm}+W_-^{\rm norm}=24,
  \qquad
  W_+^{\rm norm}-W_-^{\rm norm}=\frac32\tau=-24.
\]
Thus, in this orientation,
\[
  W_+^{\rm norm}=0,\qquad W_-^{\rm norm}=24,
\]
with the two entries swapped after orientation reversal.  Therefore the carrier
identity $C_4=18720$ fixes the integrated Ricci-flat Weyl basket/topological
slot, not an arbitrary pointwise Weyl density and not a non-Ricci-flat
higher-derivative deformation.
\end{remark}
