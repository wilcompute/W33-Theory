# BT1779 induced subgraph obstruction

The requested search for a 30-facet induced subgraph of the 600-cell facet-dual with 60 links and degree 4 has a structural obstruction.

The 600-cell facet-dual is connected and 4-regular. If an induced 30-facet subgraph also had degree 4 at every selected facet, then every full-dual neighbor of every selected facet would remain inside the selected set. The selected set would therefore be a union of connected components of the full dual graph. Since the full dual is connected with 600 facets, no proper 30-facet induced subgraph can have all 60 degree-4 links.

Conclusion: BT1776 was right. The BT1773 cycle is a backbone candidate, but the BT1767 completion graph cannot be an induced 30-facet full-neighbor subgraph. The remaining options are a non-induced selected-adjacency model, a larger facet set, or a projection/quotient from a 600-cell BC ring.
