# Exact dimension-six massive-vector matching in the W33 vacuum

The W33 light vacuum has nonzero tree-level baryon-violating four-fermion operators from massive gauge exchange. This is a separate calculation from the vanishing colored-triplet superpotential coefficients. The result uses the exact gauge orbit, the physical light quotient, and all internal generators of the contributing Standard Model weights. ## Matching and normalization

Use canonical matter kinetic terms and a positive relative adjoint scale `t` in the Hermitian field metric. For each external weight, take the Hessian kernel representatives and project orthogonally away from the broken gauge orbit using this metric. In a broken complex root block let `O` collect the gauge directions at the VEV and set `G = O† K O`, where `K` is the field metric. The replay checks that the transpose-root block gives the same Gram matrix under the exact generator identification. With a Hermitian vector field `a T + a* T†`, the mass term is `2 a† G a`. Eliminating the vector algebraically gives the current pairing `−J† G⁻¹ J / 2` in this convention. The certificate stores the reduced pairings before this common action factor and the common Weyl Fierz factor. This keeps the tensor normalization independent of spinor sign conventions.

For ordinary light matter, a positive unit of baryon number needs three quark fields with baryon charge `+1/3`. Each vector current has one dagger and one undaggered field. Hypercharge and the color contraction allow two structures with four ordinary fermions, `(Uᶜ† Q)(Eᶜ† Q)` and `(Uᶜ† Q)(Dᶜ† L)`, with the latter also receiving `(Dᶜ† Q)(Uᶜ† L)`. The two current assignments in each structure add after putting the fermions into the same Weyl Fierz order. The replay keeps the actual color and weak components of those currents, including the relative root signs. The result covers these ordinary-matter structures at zero external momentum. Higgsino external states are outside this calculation.

## The two vector sectors

The usual `SU(5)` block has one root per contributing weight with `6Y = ±5` and Gram `150t`. It receives its mass from the adjoint at this point. The second sector has `6Y = ±1` and two generators per contributing weight. Its Gram has diagonal `6t+2` and off-diagonal `−1`. In the trace-normalized gauge kinetic convention the two sectors have squared mass factors `25t` and `t+1/6, t+1/2`, respectively, multiplying the common squared gauge coupling and squared VEV unit.

The `QQUE` flavor map has rank six for every positive `t`. Its two exchanged weak/color assignments give the same unsymmetrized matrix after transporting the quark flavor basis. Each factor in that matrix is an invertible three-by-three current matrix. Their determinants are rational functions with strict coefficient signs on the positive ray. Adding the two assignments gives precisely the rank-six map on the symmetric two-quark flavor space. A fixed same-family coefficient also has strict sign throughout the ray.

At unit adjoint normalization the coherent `DUQL` flavor tensor has rank seven. In the metric of the physical light quotient, the squared norm of the second-sector contribution is about `468.42` times that of the usual `SU(5)` contribution. The interference term is constructive and is about `14.13` times the `SU(5)` squared norm. These are norms of the matching tensors. They are distinct from decay branching ratios.
