## Reading

- The prediction survives, but **under ΛCDM it is pushed to its lower edge**, and it is excluded by the frequentist ΛCDM bound.
- That same bound excludes all of normal ordering. So the conflict is between the oscillation floor and ΛCDM cosmology, not a discriminating test of no-AdS₃.
- In the repo's own preferred cosmology the window is unconstrained. Evolving dark energy beat ΛCDM out of sample in Passes 12593–12628, and the sparse-tower c′ result "overlaps the neutrino-mass anomaly" (Passes 12558–12592).
- The two arcs are therefore **coupled**: the neutrino window lives only if the dark sector is not ΛCDM.

**Discriminating future tests:**
1. JUNO ordering at ≥ 3σ by itself.
2. A cosmological Σm_ν detection between 59 and 70 meV.
3. Any 0νββ observation, which would falsify Dirac.
