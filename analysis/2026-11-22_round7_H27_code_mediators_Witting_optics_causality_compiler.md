### 2. Constructive 9D exact H27 error-correcting subspace in E6 27

\[
U^\dagger X U=I_9\otimes X_3,\qquad U^\dagger YU=I_9\otimes\operatorname{diag}(1,\bar\omega,\omega).

Fix the gauge factor to its zero-index basis state; the resulting 27×9 encoding `C` satisfies

\[
C^\dagger (X^aY^b)^\dagger X^cY^d C=\lambda_{ab,cd}I_9

for all nine projective Weyl errors. All 81 conditions are tested; maximum numerical deviation is below `1.1e-15`. Measure the X-shift syndrome and invert that shift; the Y-phase is scalar on the code. This implements a **nine-dimensional (log₂9 qubit equivalent) logical code for the restricted H27 error algebra** and is a concrete realization of the well-known representation-multiplicity/noiseless-subsystem principle. It is not fault tolerance to arbitrary gate, photon loss or E6 gauge fluctuations.

For contrast, for the **entire** compact-E6 unitary group acting on the multiplicity-free `78 ⊕ 27 ⊕ anti27` representation, the generated algebra is `End(78) ⊕ End(27) ⊕ End(anti27)` by the representation bicommutant theorem. ### 4. Sharp W33 80-deletion cover repair supports height 3 and height 4

The previous exact W33 identity `A²=8I−2A+4J` implies 240 edges, 160 triangles, two triangles per edge, and a maximum cut of 160 edges (spectral upper bound attained by the known 20+20 cut). Hence deleting 80 W33 links is necessary and sufficient to interpret all remaining adjacency links as covers in at least one partial order.

**Three-layer construction:** Split the 20 source-side vertices into 10 early and 10 late; retain the opposite 20 as middle. Direct every retained 160 edge between adjacent layers forward. This is a Hasse graph with 260 comparable pairs, maximum chain length 3, and interval counts: `N_open=0:160`, `N_open=2:40`, `N_open=3:32`, `N_open=4:28`.

**Four-layer construction:** On the **same** retained 160-edge bipartite graph, use breadth-first graph distance from vertex zero as the time coordinate. Because the graph is bipartite, every edge changes BFS level by exactly one. The level populations are `[1,8,19,12]`, the longest chain is 4, and every one of the **160** retained edges is still a cover. There are 287 comparable pairs with interval histogram `0:160, 2:49, 3:36, 4:30, 16:12`. No additional W33 edges must be deleted. This corrects the mistaken impression that the optimal repair forces height two or empty causal intervals.

**Matched negative control:** A deterministic seed-20261122 40-event sprinkle of independent null coordinates in a flat 1+1 Minkowski diamond yields 415 comparable pairs and a maximum chain of 10, compared with 287 and 4 in the four-layer W33 model. It remains **not manifoldlike**, and neither Lorentzian scalar curvature, spin-two propagation nor causal continuum limits have been derived. Null coordinate sprinkling and interval-based action tests follow established causal-set methods (https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.104.181301; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010465518302169).

### 5. Constructive Hessian compiler: positive and negative controls

The code now jointly optimizes a finite sequence of 27 Heisenberg representatives at each coordinate, exactly optimizing over the **648** left Hessian prefixes at every coordinate update.
