# BT1533 Fixed-ground Stabilized Carrier Law

BT1533 formalizes the fixed-ground action separated in BT1531.

The packet law acts on packets 0..3 as a square, fixes packets 4..6 as residual phase packets, and fixes packet 7 as the tetrahedral ground packet.

The 7+1 phase/ground split is preserved. This is not the regular eight-packet action.
