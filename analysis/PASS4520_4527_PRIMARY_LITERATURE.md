# Passes 4520--4527 — primary-literature boundary

The executable claims in this packet are repository-specific finite computations/proofs. The literature below is used only to locate the surrounding classical structures; it is not cited as if it contained the new apartment-coordinate statements.

1. **D. B. Chandler, P. Sin, Q. Xiang, _The permutation action of finite symplectic groups of odd characteristic on their standard modules_**, arXiv:math/0603100. This studies modular submodules arising from symplectic polar-space incidence and determines p-ranks including W(3,q). It is relevant context for the modular `PSp(4,3)` shadows, but it does not supply the 1620-apartment code, its 366-dimensional dual quotient, or the Pass-4522 quotient construction.
2. **I. Dolgachev, M. Kapranov, _Schur quadrics, cubic surfaces and rank 2 vector bundles over the projective plane_**, arXiv:alg-geom/9304005. This records the classical 27 lines and 36 Schlaefli double-sixes on a smooth cubic surface. Passes 4525 and 4527 go further only in the repository-specific sense that they give explicit permutation conjugations from the newly constructed six-dimensional orthogonal quotient to the already existing repo carriers.
3. **Point-primitive, line-transitive generalised quadrangles**, J. Group Theory, DOI 10.1515/jgth-2016-0042. This is used only for standard finite-GQ definitions/order conventions and symmetry context. The Pass-4523 primitive-six Walsh coefficients and Pass-4526 odd-t fan-kernel formula are derived in the packet and are not attributed to that paper.

Boundary: absence of the exact apartment-coordinate constructions in these targeted sources is not used as a proof of global novelty.
