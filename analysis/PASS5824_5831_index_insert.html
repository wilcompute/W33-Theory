Passes 5824–5831 — the common rational W₉ splits into distinct integral and binary forms

Integral correction: the point and heavy W₉ lattices saturate to A3³ with discriminant 2⁶, while an explicit GL4(2) coordinate relabel identifies the line W₉ lattice with A3 ⊗ A3, discriminant 2¹². Rational equivalence is therefore not integral equivalence.

Constructive line lattice: the relabel sends the nine rank-one Fourier labels to the ordinary product-nonzero 3×3 block. In the corresponding 4×4 coordinate grid, the saturated line lattice is exactly the integer arrays with every row and column sum zero, with basis (eᵢ−e₃)⊗(eⱼ−e₃).

2-primary Radon defects: on saturated W₉ bases, Rᵀ and D have Smith form 1⁵ 2² 4², giving cokernel (Z/2)² ⊕ (Z/4)² of order 2⁶. Hᵀ has Smith form 1² 2⁵ 4², cokernel order 2⁹.

Characteristic-two firewall: the ambient mod-2 ranks are rank₂ R = rank₂ D = 8, rank₂ H = 4, rank₂ K₉ = 3, while the centered cross transforms each collapse to rank 1. The common dimension 9 is a characteristic-zero statement, not a binary one.

Boundary: exact integral lattice and Smith-normal-form arithmetic. Any future CSS/code claim using the common W₉ must specify the coefficient ring and preserve these 2-primary cokernels; no physical meaning is assigned to the powers of two by themselves.
