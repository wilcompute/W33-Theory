\subsection*{Coherent determinant closure, exact binary Radon sequence, and the all-field rank-metric graph}

On $V=M_2(\mathbb F_2)$ put $q(X)=\det X$ and
$B(X,Y)=q(X+Y)+q(X)+q(Y)$.  Color ordered pairs by diagonal status,
$q(X+Y)$ and $B(X,Y)$.  Exact coherent refinement stabilizes as
\[
 \boxed{5\longrightarrow15\longrightarrow15}.
\]
The determinant stabilizer $O^+(4,2)$ has order $72$ and exactly the same
fifteen orbitals, of sizes
\[
1,6,6,6,9,9,9,12,18,18,18,36,36,36,36.
\]
Thus determinant-polar commutation together with unit-difference adjacency
recovers the full orbital coherent configuration.  Its orbital algebra has
dimension $15$, center dimension $4$, and over $\mathbb C$
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal A\cong M_3(\mathbb C)\oplus M_2(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C}.
\]
The $16$-point permutation module has multiplicity pattern
$1^3\oplus\varepsilon\oplus V_{4,a}\oplus2V_{4,b}$.  Because the vertex
orbits are $1+9+6$, this is a three-fibre coherent configuration rather than a
homogeneous association scheme.

The local Reye binary kernel $C_R=[12,4,6]$ does \emph{not} embed by support
extension into the $q=5$ footprint check code $C_F=[156,65,12]$: a weight-six
word would contradict $d(C_F)=12$.  More strongly, shortening $C_F$ to any
$12$ coordinates has dimension at most one, since every nonzero shortened word
must have all twelve coordinates in its support.  Hence the local Reye defect is
not a literal $q=5$ CSS-check subcode.  This does not rule out a relation to the
line-code logical normalizer $C_W$; no canonical map from the moving twelve
cover labels to the $156$ $W(3,5)$ point coordinates is certified here.

The correct local interface is instead integral.  For the saturated Radon map
$T_R:A_3^3\to A_3\otimes A_3$,
\[
 \boxed{T_R^TG_LT_R=4G_P},\qquad
 \det T_R=-64,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{SNF}(T_R)=1^5\,2^2\,4^2.
\]
Modulo two, its four-dimensional kernel maps objectwise to the complete Reye
code, while its image is exactly the five-dimensional radical of the line Gram
form:
\[
 \boxed{0\to C_R\to\mathbb F_2^9\xrightarrow{\bar T_R}
 \operatorname{Rad}(A_3\otimes A_3\bmod2)\to0}.
\]
The remaining four-dimensional quotient inherits the even-lattice quadratic
$q_L(x)=x^TG_Lx/2\pmod2$.  In an explicit complement basis it is literally
\[
 \boxed{q_L(a,b,c,d)=ad+bc=\det\!\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}},
\]
with $10+6$ value split.  Thus the same integral morphism places the Reye code
in its mod-two kernel and the determinant/two-qubit plus-type $9+6$ geometry in
its mod-two cokernel; these objects are related, not identified.

For every finite field $\mathbb F_r$, the unit-difference Cayley graph
\[
 X\sim Y\iff \det(X-Y)\ne0
\]
is the complement of the bilinear-forms graph $H_r(2,2)$.  Additive Fourier
characters give
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Spec}\Gamma_r=
 k^1\oplus r^{\,k}\oplus[-r(r-1)]^{(r-1)(r+1)^2}},
 \qquad k=r(r-1)^2(r+1),
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{(v,k,\lambda,\mu)=
 (r^4,\ r(r-1)^2(r+1),\ r(r^3-2r^2-r+3),\ r(r-1)(r^2-r-1))}.
\]
The binary $L_2(4)$ graph is therefore the first member of this all-field
rank-metric family.  The classical full automorphism group of $H_r(2,2)$, and
hence of its complement, is the affine semilinear left/right group extended by
factor transpose; at $r=2$ its order is $1152$.

The $72$ determinant-to-Pauli quadratic isometries also lift cleanly through the
two-qubit Clifford quotient.  Their relative group is $O^+(4,2)<Sp_4(2)$ with
orders $72<720$.  Conjugating the SPP quadratic form to the standard
non-entangling form
\[
 q_{NE}=s(x_1,z_1)+s(x_2,z_2),\qquad s(x,z)=x+z+xz,
\]
identifies its stabilizer with the $72$-element local-factor-plus-swap quotient.
Including the projective Pauli group of order $16$ gives
\[
 \boxed{16\cdot72=1152},
\]
which is Clifford-conjugate to the non-entangling two-qubit Clifford group
classified by Kubischta--Teixeira as generated by local Cliffords and SWAP.
This is a Clifford-conjugacy statement, not a claim that the original SPP frame
is already the standard tensor-product frame.

Three outside-box probes close additional structure.  For odd $r$ and quadratic
multiplicative character $\chi(0)=0$,
\[
 \boxed{\widehat{\chi(\det)}(Y)=r^2\chi(-1)\chi(\det Y)},
\]
so the transform vanishes on the singular rank-zero/rank-one labels.  This is an
explicit $2\times2$ instance of the established finite-field prehomogeneous-vector-space
Fourier functional-equation framework.  At $r=3$, exhaustive clique enumeration
of the unit-difference graph gives $648$ maximum $9$-cliques; under translations,
left/right $GL_2(3)$ and transpose they split as
\[
 \boxed{162+486}.
\]
The first orbit is exactly the affine cosets of the $18$ anisotropic two-planes
and has affine span dimension two; every clique in the $486$-orbit has affine
span dimension four, giving a genuinely nonlinear maximum-rank-distance orbit.
Finally, the Reye code dual is
\[
 C_R^\perp=[12,8,3],
\]
and its sixteen minimum weight-three supports are exactly the original Reye
triples.  The twelve minimum weight-six supports of $C_R$ are exactly the heavy
sets; their disjointness incidence has degrees $3$ and $4$ and reproduces the
second $12_4,16_3$ Reye copy.
