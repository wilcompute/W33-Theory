\subsection{Projectivity-frame closure: automorphisms, extension fields, codes, and kinematic firewalls}
\label{sec:pass5595-5602-projectivity-closure-toe}

The projectivity-graph design of Passes~5588--5594 admits several further
structural closures.  Write $X=\mathbf P^1(q)$ and
$G=\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)$, with rows $g\in G$, columns $(x,y)\in X^2$, and
incidence $y=g(x)$.

\paragraph{Pass 5595: full incidence automorphism mechanism.}
The number of common row-neighbours of two distinct columns vanishes exactly
when the two grid cells share one coordinate.  Thus the incidence structure
recovers the square rook graph on $X^2$.  Every incidence automorphism therefore
acts on columns through $(S_{q+1}\times S_{q+1})\rtimes C_2$.  Without swapping
the two rulings, $(x,y)\mapsto(ax,by)$ preserves the graph-row set exactly when
$bGa^{-1}=G$.  Hence $ba^{-1}\in G$ and
$a\in N_{S_{q+1}}(G)$, and conversely these conditions suffice.  Using the
standard natural-action normalizer
\[
N_{S_{q+1}}(\mathrm{PSL}_2(q))=\mathrm P\Gamma\mathrm L_2(q),
\]
for $q=p^f$ odd gives
\[
\boxed{\lvert\operatorname{Aut}(D_q)\rvert
 =4f\,\lvert\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)\rvert^2.}
\]
The executable verifier enumerates the complete natural action at $q=3,5$,
giving $576$ and $14{,}400$.  Thus the Reye $576$ is the first member of an
all-$q$ normalizer law, rather than an isolated order coincidence.

\paragraph{Pass 5596: the Reye and Klein-$V_4$ Latin degree-$12$ actions are

$r=(0\ 2\ 3\ 1)$.  The Reye rows are $vr^k$.  The Klein-$V_4$ Latin
autoparatopy action is the affine symmetry of $x+y+z=0$ on three labelled

\[
\boxed{\Phi(vr^k)=\bigl(k,r^k(v(0))\bigr)}

$576$-element Latin $12$-symbol action.  Their non-diagonal orbital sizes are

certificate records an abstractly isomorphic image with orbits $1+12$ but does
not retain explicit $S_{13}$ generators, so this pass does not silently claim
that final action-level intertwiner.

\paragraph{Pass 5598: the frame code projects from the footprint kernel.}
Let $C_W$ be the binary W-line incidence code.  Pass~5376 proved
$C_W=\ker(F^T)$ for the footprint matrix $F$.  For a nonsingular-class point
$p$, let $N(p)$ be its full symplectic-neighbour indicator.  Modulo two the sum
of the $q+1$ W-lines through $p$ cancels $p$ and leaves its neighbours once, so
$N(p)\in C_W$.  Restricting coordinates to the singular Segre sheet $S$ gives
precisely the projectivity row $M_p$.  Therefore
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{row}(M)
 =\pi_S\operatorname{span}\{N(p):p\in C_+\}
 \subseteq\pi_S(C_W)=\pi_S\ker(F^T).}
\]

footprint image.  At $q=3,5,7$ the restricted line-code ranks are $15,34,61$
while the projectivity-code ranks are $8,18,32$, so equality with the full
restricted line code is false.

\paragraph{Pass 5601: determinant chirality gives an exact isodual binary
code.}

class; it has $0$ or $2$ fixed points.  Hence $C_-\subseteq C_+^\perp$.  The
binary rank theorem gives both dimensions $(q+1)^2/2$, because multiplying by a
fixed opposite-coset projectivity only permutes grid coordinates.  Therefore
\[
\boxed{C_-=C_+^\perp,}
\qquad
\boxed{C_+\text{ is isodual }[(q+1)^2,(q+1)^2/2].}
\]
At $q=3$ its measured weight enumerator is

minimum distance is $6$.  No all-$q$ minimum-distance claim is made here.
