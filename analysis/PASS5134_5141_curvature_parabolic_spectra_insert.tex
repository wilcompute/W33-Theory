\[
 \Pr(\mathrm{stay}\mid S)=\Pr(\mathrm{exit}\mid S)=\frac12,
 \qquad
 \frac{1_S^TL1_S}{\lVert1_S\rVert^2}=4(q-1).
\]
Ordinary first-order Cheeger/Rayleigh data is therefore identical for every codeword support and cannot by itself prove distance.

At q=3 the first nonconstant local spectral statistic occurs at cubic order.  A chamber star has $(|S|,|E(S)|,T)=(81,324,108)$, while XORs of two chamber stars at gallery distances $1,2,3,4$ have

\]
All obey $2|E(S)|/|S|=8$, while the normalized cubic moments $6T/|S|$ are respectively
\[
 8,\ 6,\ 7,\ \frac{98}{13},\ \frac{39}{5}.
\]
Every fully selected theta-check triangle remains forbidden by parity; the varying triangles are the common-root/Tanner-six-cycle triangles.

The BT865 state/program group gauge is also intrinsic.
