## 5066 — all-q apartment/theta presentation theorem
Let Γ be the incidence graph of any finite generalized quadrangle, let A be its set of 8-cycle apartments, and let Θ be the span over F2 of the local theta triples obtained from three length-4 geodesics between the same opposite vertices.  The apartment boundary map

`∂ : F2[A] -> Z1(Γ;F2)`

induces an isomorphism

`F2[A]/Θ ~= Z1(Γ;F2)`.

Proof: fix a base vertex b and a geodesic γ_v from b to every vertex v. For an edge e={u,v}, the symmetric sum γ_u+e+γ_v has length at most 8; girth 8 implies it is either zero or one apartment. Summing these fan cycles over any 1-cycle cancels all chosen geodesics and gives the original cycle. Geodesics are unique below distance 4. Changing the chosen geodesic to an opposite vertex changes each incident fan apartment by the same local theta class, which cancels because a 1-cycle has even degree there. Thus the fan decomposition is well-defined modulo Θ and is inverse to ∂; on a single apartment choose geodesics along its two halves, leaving exactly that apartment in the fan.

For W(3,q), `dim Z1 = E-V+1 = q^4`, so the binary apartment code has length `q^4(q+1)^2(q^2+1)/8`, dimension `q^4`, and **its entire dual is generated by theta triples for every prime power q**.  This closes the all-q dimension/full-dual part of Pass5051.  The remaining all-q wall is minimum distance: the exact q=2,3,4 anchors have `d=q^4`, but a symbolic proof of `d=q^4` remains open.
