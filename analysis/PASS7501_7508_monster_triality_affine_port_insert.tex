\subsection*{Passes 7501--7508: a Monster-local triality/affine port}

The Pass7465--7472 reduction of $E_8/3E_8$ identified a classical $D_4(3)$
triality carrier with
\[
 \boxed{1120+1120+1120=3360}
\]
objects: the $1120$ singular points of $Q^+(7,3)$ together with its two families
of $1120$ maximal totally singular generators.  On the $E_8$ side, the point
family is the radical image of the $1120$ $A_2$ root subsystems and the two
generator families are the $2240$ Eisenstein $W(3,3)$ leaves.

There is now an external sporadic-group port for exactly this ambient carrier.
ATLAS lists a maximal Monster subgroup
\[
 H=(3^2{:}2\times O_8^+(3)).S_4
\]
with an intransitive permutation representation of degree $3369$.  The exact
ATLAS generators are hash-pinned by the Pass7501 verifier.  Their orbit census is
\[
 \boxed{3369=9+3360}.
\]
The image on the nine-point orbit has order $432$ and contains a normal regular
$C_3^2$, hence is the natural affine action
\[
 \boxed{AGL(2,3)=3^2{:}GL(2,3)}.
\]
The image on the $3360$-point orbit has order
\[
 \boxed{118852315545600=|O_8^+(3):S_4|}.
\]
Using the ATLAS order of $H$, the kernels of the two orbit actions have orders
$18$ and $|O_8^+(3)|$, respectively.  Both orbit images therefore have one
common quotient of order $24$:
\[
 432/18=24
 =|O_8^+(3):S_4|/|O_8^+(3)|.
\]
Thus the subgroup is the fibre product
\[
 \boxed{
 H\cong AGL(2,3)\times_{S_4}\bigl(O_8^+(3):S_4\bigr).
 }
\]
In other words, the Monster-local $3369$-point action couples an affine
$\mathbb F_3^2$ nine-sheet to the $D_4(3)$ triality carrier through one common
$S_4$ outer coordinate.  This is a genuine subgroup-action bridge, not an
integer coincidence.  ATLAS also lists $O_8^+(3):S_4$ as a maximal subgroup of
the Baby Monster, so the same $3360$-object carrier has a second sporadic port.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
The theorem identifies the ambient triality carrier containing all $2240$
Eisenstein $W(3,3)$ leaves with a standard $O_8^+(3):S_4$ permutation carrier
inside a Monster maximal subgroup.  It does not assert that the Monster
stabilizes one chosen $W(3,3)$ leaf by the leaf's internal incidence action, and
it does not derive the Moonshine module, the Griess product, a modular function,
or any physical parameter from $W(3,3)$.
