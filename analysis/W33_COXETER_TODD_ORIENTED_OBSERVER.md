## Exact loss of information and optimal repair

Consider linear amplitude dynamics generated by A, with output C selecting the 45 coordinates N. Since A has three distinct eigenvalues, the all-time observability matrix is O=[C; CA; CA²].

All 40 odd vectors are invisible. Another 15-dimensional even sector is invisible: with P₁₅=(4Q+12I−3J)/24, B P₁₅=0 for B=A[N,M]F/2. The odd and even spaces are independent. Thus rank(O)≤71. The rank modulo 101 is 71, proving rank over the rationals is exactly 71 and the invisible space has dimension 55. Within it, A has eigenvalue 3 on 45 dimensions and −9 on 10 dimensions.

Each extra scalar output contributes at most one constraint on the 45-dimensional invisible eigenspace at eigenvalue 3, so at least 45 additional scalar outputs are necessary. The certificate lists 45 coordinate outputs in M for which the extended observability matrix has rank 126 modulo 101. This attains the lower bound: exactly 45 additional ports suffice. The resulting total is 90 ports for this fixed initial observer. In fact 90 is also the global minimum for any fixed linear scalar-output observer: the full matrix A has eigenvalue 3 with multiplicity 90 (its SRG identity and trace determine the multiplicities 1,90,35). On that eigenspace, time evolution is scalar, so fewer than 90 outputs cannot be injective. The supplied 90-coordinate observer attains this universal lower bound. Time-varying output operators are outside this theorem.
