## 12282: the rule

B is an alternating bicharacter on the finite abelian group generated by a commuting pair, modulo the translations that the Plöger
conditions make harmless. Alternating bicharacters vanish on cyclic groups, and on Z_N × Z_M they take values in (1/gcd(N, M))Z/Z.

**So torsion needs a commuting pair whose orders share a factor.**

| geometry (corpus) | lines | relevant commuting pairs | torsion | measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T6/Z3, SUSY and non-SUSY | order 3 | cyclic (× central Witten Z2) | none | 37/37 consistent shifts B = 0; 160/160 relabellings invariant |
| Z6-I SUSY | order 3 | θ³ fixes the SU(3) torus: gcd(2,3) = 1 | none | — |
| Z6-II SUSY | orders 2, 3 | θ²/θ³ with fixed tori: coprime | none | **1471/1471** consistent brothers identical (153 SMs) |
| Z6-II non-SUSY | orders 2, 3 | Witten (2) × order-2 translation | **yes** | 12277, 12281 |
| Z2×Z6-I SUSY | order 2 (SO(4) torus) | Z6-I's order-2 element × e1: gcd 2 | **yes** | below |

**Z2×Z6-I (positive test in a SUSY geometry).** The rule gives B(e1, (θ'ᵏ, f)) = k V_N·λ/2. The prediction is that only the parity
of V_N·λ matters. Over the 29 Standard Models:

| brothers | result |
|---|---|
| even (V_N·λ even) | 29/29 identical |
| odd (V_N·λ odd) | 12 Standard Models with a different spectrum; 15 consistent but not Standard Models; 2 identical |

The 12 odd-parity SM brothers:

- all keep **three net generations**;
- each differs from its parent in the identification-independent (sector, multiplet, representation) content (12/12);
- none equals any of the 29 known spectra.

They are **new to the corpus**. They are not claimed new to the literature: Z2×Z6-I brother models have a literature, which I did not
search.

## 12280: SM_2496's brother is a Scherk–Schwarz twist

The only nonzero torsion is B(w, e5) = ½. It has two effects:

- untwisted states of winding n along e5 get (−1)ⁿ under the Witten element: fermion number tied to winding;
- Witten-twisted states get −1 under e5: half-integer Kaluza–Klein momenta along e5.

That is the defining data of a Scherk–Schwarz twist along e5, the order-2 translations of 12177/12180 (cited). It is derived at the
level of projection phases. A massless-level proxy for the one-loop potential:

| | parent | brother |
|---|---|---|
| n_F − n_B | 164 | 84 |
| excited-tachyon components | 8 | 14 |

The potential itself is not computed: the corpus one-loop engines are T6/Z3 only.
