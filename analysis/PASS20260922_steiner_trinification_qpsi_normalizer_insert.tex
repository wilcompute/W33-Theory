\subsection*{The complete Steiner atlas excludes non-FI $Q_\psi$ normalizers}
\label{sec:steiner-trinification-qpsi-normalizer}

Let $C$ be the $45\times27$ support matrix of the Cartan cubic.  Exact
row reduction over $\mathbb F_3$ gives
\[
 \operatorname{rank}C=21,
 \qquad\dim\ker C=6.
\]
Thus $\ker C$ is a ternary $[27,6,12]_3$ conservation code, with exact weight
enumerator
\[
 1+72z^{12}+510z^{18}+144z^{21}+2z^{27}.
\]
This is distinct from the earlier binary $[27,6,12]_2$ double-six incidence
code.  Among its $3^6=729$ words, $510$ have content $9+9+9$.  They split as
$240$ words with eighteen monochromatic and twenty-seven rainbow cubic terms,
and $270$ words with twelve monochromatic and thirty-three rainbow terms.
After the six colour relabellings these give forty and forty-five partitions,
respectively.

The constant word lies in the code.  Projectivizing its quotient gives
\[
 \mathbb P(\ker C/\langle\mathbf1\rangle)=PG(4,3),\qquad
 \boxed{121=36+40+45}.
\]
The induced order-$51840$ $W(E_6)$ action preserves a nondegenerate
five-dimensional quadratic form.  Direct support comparison identifies its
norm classes objectwise:
\[
 Q=2:\ 36\ {\rm double\!-\!sixes},\qquad
 Q=0:\ 40\ {\rm Steiner\ triads},\qquad
 Q=1:\ 45\ {\rm tritangents}.
\]
The weight-twelve supports are exactly the double-sixes.  The forty
eighteen-monochromatic partitions are exactly the classical Steiner triads
already owned by Part~MCCCXCVI and Pass~4870.  For each of the other forty-five
partitions, its twelve monochromatic cubics are exactly the star of one
tritangent.  Pass~4863 already owned the abstract $40,45,36$ norm-class sizes
and the $36$-class identification; the common code and the objectwise $40$-
and $45$-class identifications are the increment here.  The transpose has
nullity $24$, so the point-charge six-space and the prior line-holonomy
twenty-four-space are the two kernels of the same cubic incidence matrix.

Each partition has six sector assignments and $6^3$ shared-factor coordinate
labellings.  Hence the complete incidence-defined monomial atlas contains
\[
 40\cdot6\cdot6^3=40\cdot1296=51840
\]
distinct trinification charts.  The exact $W(E_6)$ action on one chart has
this whole atlas as its orbit with trivial chart stabilizer.  Equivalently,
the atlas is a regular $W(E_6)$-torsor, while an unordered partition has
stabilizer order $1296$.

In any chart write a weight as $(m,q)$, with nine-valued multiplicity address
$m$ and internal qutrit $q$; the external qutrit $p$ is unchanged by $Q_\psi$.
A diagonal normalizer of $I_9\otimes M_9(\mathbb C)$ must factor across its
multiplicity and active registers.  Therefore $D_{12}^k$ normalizes precisely
when its $9\times3$ exponent table is additively separable modulo twelve, or
equivalently when every anchored additive $2\times2$ minor vanishes.  Exhaustion
of all charts gives, for $k=0,\ldots,11$,
\[
 (51840,0,0,0,51840,0,0,0,51840,0,0,0).
\]
Thus
\[
 \boxed{\langle D_{12}\rangle\cap
 N(I_9\otimes M_9)=\langle D_{12}^{4}\rangle\cong C_3,}
\]
and matter parity $D_{12}^{6}$ is not a normalizer in any compatible monomial
chart.

The obstruction is nevertheless sharply local.  The Hamming distances of the
51,840 parity tables to the $2048$ separable binary tables have census
\[
 1^{5760}\,5^{34560}\,6^{11520}.
\]
In a best chart only one $(m,q)$ phase cell must be corrected; on Matter81 it
is repeated over the three values of $p$, so the diagonal correction has
support three.  This finite theorem neither selects a physical chart or vacuum
nor proves that the correction is dynamically available.  Separate sharp
rank bounds exclude an invertible $H_{27}$-equivariant change of basis
($9<27$) and an invertible $H_{27}\times C_3$-equivariant Matter81 compiler
($27<81$).  The remaining frozen-root dictionary must be non-equivariant,
proper-subgroup-covariant, or otherwise symmetry-changing.
