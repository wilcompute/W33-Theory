# BT1651 Guard-shell Shot Simulator

A synthetic 2048-bin photon stream exercises the 448-bin guard shell: 168 dark-reference, 168 loss-probe, and 112 parity-overflow bins.

Injected dark, loss, and parity faults remain within guard budgets and map to subtract-dark, loss-correction, and CSS-retry actions.

This is deterministic simulation, not hardware data.
