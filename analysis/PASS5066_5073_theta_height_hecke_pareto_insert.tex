For the incidence graph $\Gamma$ of any finite generalized quadrangle, let $\mathcal A$ be its $8$-cycle apartments and let $\Theta$ be the binary span of the local three-apartment theta relations.  A base-vertex fan decomposition proves the exact presentation
\[
 \mathbb F_2^{\mathcal A}/\Theta\;\cong\; Z_1(\Gamma;\mathbb F_2).
\]
Indeed, after choosing a geodesic from a base vertex to every graph vertex, each edge closes to either zero or one apartment because the graph has girth eight; summing these fan apartments around a cycle cancels the chosen geodesics.  The only geodesic choices occur at opposite vertices, and changing such a choice changes the fan by a local theta relation.  For $W(3,q)$ this gives apartment-code dimension $q^4$ and proves that theta triples generate the complete dual for every prime power $q$.  The all-$q$ minimum-distance assertion $d=q^4$ remains open; it is exact at $q=2,3,4$.
