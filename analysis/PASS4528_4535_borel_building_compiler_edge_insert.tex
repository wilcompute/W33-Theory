\paragraph{Pass 4528: the Borel is maximal by inclusion inside its splitting overgroup interval.}

\[

\]

\[

\]
with orders $162,648,648,25920$.  In particular the two maximal parabolics
intersect exactly in $H$.  Recomputing the apartment-extension section system

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}

\end{array}
\]
Hence adjoining any element outside the chamber either restores one simple
parabolic or generates all of $G$, and in every case the obstruction returns.
This proves maximality by inclusion among splitting overgroups containing this
Borel; it is not a census of unrelated subgroup conjugacy classes.

\paragraph{Pass 4529: the two obstruction bits are the two parabolic directions.}
Pass~4509 gives $H^1(G,K/J)\cong\mathbb F_2^2$.  Choose
\[
