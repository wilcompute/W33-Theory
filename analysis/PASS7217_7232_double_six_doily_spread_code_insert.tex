\subsection{Double-six sections of the $E_8/D_4$ spread code recover the doily codes}
\label{sec:pass7217-7232-double-six-doily}

Fix a Schlaefli double-six $(a_1,\ldots,a_6)/(b_1,\ldots,b_6)$ on the cubic surface.  The fifteen remaining lines are the $c_{ij}$ and hence identify with the fifteen duads, or edges, of $K_6$.  The existing exact cubic-surface classifier shows that the fifteen tritangent planes supported entirely on those lines are precisely
\[
 \{c_{ij},c_{kl},c_{mn}\},
\]
where $(ij)(kl)(mn)$ is a perfect matching of $K_6$.  They are therefore the fifteen synthemes in the classical duad--syntheme model of the doily $GQ(2,2)$.

\paragraph{A closed $K_6$ formula for the doily code.}
For $A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,6\}$ let $\delta(A)$ be the binary cut vector on the fifteen edges of $K_6$.  Then, with $A\sim A^c$,
\[
 \boxed{c(A)=\delta(A)+(|A|\bmod2)\mathbf 1_{15}}
\]
generates the self-orthogonal code
\[
 \boxed{[15,5,6]_2},
 \qquad W(y)=1+10y^6+15y^8+6y^{10}.
\]
Its even-$|A|$ sector is the $[15,4,8]_2$ simplex code.  In this model the six ovoid zero-sets are the six vertex stars of $K_6$, the fifteen perp zero-sets are one duad together with its six disjoint duads, and the ten grid zero-sets are the $K_{3,3}$ edge sets from unordered $3+3$ bisections.  The dual is $[15,10,3]_2$, and its fifteen minimum words are exactly the fifteen synthemes.

The six doily spreads are the six $1$-factorizations of $K_6$.  Consequently the two six-sets used in Passes~6533--6540 have the classical interpretation
\[
 \boxed{\text{six ovoids}=\text{six vertices of }K_6},\qquad
 \boxed{\text{six spreads}=\text{six $1$-factorizations of }K_6}.
\]
The induced actions of all $720$ permutations reproduce the earlier exceptional-$S_6$ cycle-type table exactly, including
\[
 2\leftrightarrow 2^3,\qquad 3\leftrightarrow3^2,\qquad 3\!\cdot\!2\leftrightarrow6.
\]

\paragraph{The forty-five-coordinate code contains this doily literally.}
Let
\[
 C_{\rm spread}=[45,21,5]_2
\]
be the Pass7182/7184 code on the forty-five orthogonal-$D_4$-pair / tritangent coordinates, generated by the twenty-seven ten-$D_4$ spreads.  For any one of the thirty-six double-sixes $D$, let $S_D$ denote the fifteen tritangent planes disjoint from $D$.  Exact replay over all thirty-six choices gives
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{puncture}_{S_D}(C_{\rm spread})\cong[15,10,3]_2.}
\]
This is objectwise, not parameter recognition: exactly fifteen of the twenty-seven spread generators restrict nontrivially, each restriction has weight three, and for every duad $ij$ one such row is exactly the three synthemes containing $ij$.  The other twelve generators restrict to zero.

Since Pass7182 proved $C_{\rm spread}^{\perp}=[45,24,6]_2$, puncture/shorten duality gives
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{shorten}_{S_D}(C_{\rm spread}^{\perp})
 \cong[15,5,6]_2,
 }
\]
and direct enumeration recovers $1+10y^6+15y^8+6y^{10}$.  Thus every double-six supplies the exact local hierarchy
\[
 [45,21,5]_{\rm E8\ spread}
 \xrightarrow{\rm puncture}
 [15,10,3]_{\rm doily}^{\perp},
 \qquad
 [45,24,6]_{\rm Steiner}
 \xrightarrow{\rm shorten}
 [15,5,6]_{\rm doily}.
\]

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The duad--syntheme doily, Schlaefli double-six notation, exceptional outer automorphism of $S_6$, and uniqueness of the optimal self-orthogonal $[15,5,6]_2$ code are classical.  The result here is the explicit identification among the repo's concrete cubic-surface, $E_8/D_4$ spread-code, and earlier doily-code carriers.  It is a finite binary/incidence theorem only; no physical identification is inferred.
