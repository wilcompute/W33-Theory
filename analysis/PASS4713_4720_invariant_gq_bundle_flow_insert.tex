\subsection{Invariant flag cohomology and the dual-shell $GQ(4,2)$ closure}

The apartment double cover can be placed over a base carrying the \emph{full}
$\PSp(4,3)$ action.  On the 810 selected point--line flags, the smallest
connected self-paired $\PSp$ orbital graph is 16-regular, with 6480 edges,
diameter five and
\[
 b_1=6480-810+1=5671.
\]
Its equivariant lift to the 1620 apartments is a connected regular $C_2$ cover,
also 16-regular, with exactly two lift edges above each base edge.  A base
triangle $0\to482\to367\to0$ lifts between the two apartments over flag zero,
so the deck class is nonzero.  The $\PGSp(4,3)$ outer similitude preserves both
base and lift; consequently the deck class spans an honest one-dimensional
trivial $\PGSp$ submodule of $H^1(-;\mathbb F_2)$.  This replaces the earlier
five-generator Schreier base, for which no full $\PSp$ action on the frozen
cohomology space existed.

Independently, the selected binary code reconstructs a classical generalized
quadrangle without importing the protected-45 labeling.  The 45 complements of
the dual weight-132 shell partition the 135 selected coordinates into
$45\times3$.  Projecting the 270 dual weight-three words to those packets gives
270 triples.  Their pair graph is
\[
 \operatorname{SRG}(45,12,3,3),
\]
and its maximal cliques are exactly 27 copies of $K_5$.  Every projected triple
is a three-subset of a unique $K_5$, and each $K_5$ contributes all
$\binom53=10$ triples.  Hence the dual shells intrinsically recover
$GQ(4,2)$: 45 points, 27 lines, three lines per point and five points per line.
The dual line graph is $\operatorname{SRG}(27,10,1,5)$.  If $H$ is the
$45\times270$ point--triangle incidence and $B$ the $45\times27$ point--line

\[

with Smith profiles
\[
 \operatorname{SNF}(H)_{\ne0}=1^{44}3,

\]
The reconstructed 27-vertex graph has full automorphism order 51840.

\subsection{The $S_3$ connection reconstructs the selected geometry}

The selected135 graph is a connected three-sheet cover of the
$GQ(4,2)$ point graph.  Each base edge carries a perfect matching between its
two three-point packets, hence an $S_3$ connection.  After spanning-tree gauge,
the 226 cotree voltages comprise 64 identities and 54 copies of each of the
three transpositions; the generated monodromy is all of $S_3$.

Every one of the 270 base triangles has transposition holonomy, with the three
transpositions occurring $90+90+90$ times.  Its unique fixed sheet recovers one
selected singular three-point line.  Recovering all 270 fixed-sheet triangles
reproduces the original $135_6$--$270_3$ incidence, and their intersection graph
is exactly selected270.

The 27 $GQ(4,2)$ lines are precisely the ten-vertex Petersen fibers.  Adjacent
fibers share one 45-packet.  Their 12 cross-fiber base edges split as
\[
 3K_{2,2},
\]
one $K_{2,2}$ for each of the packet's three singular coordinates.  Thus the
full selected270 router is recoverable from its 27-object quotient together
with this coordinate-preserving connection law.

The regular six-sheet $S_3$ closure gives a second canonical 270-vertex graph.
A parity gauge makes every edge voltage odd, yielding an explicit isomorphism
with the bipartite/Kronecker double cover of selected135.  Its spectrum is
\[
 \{12^1,6^{30},3^{44},0^{120},(-3)^{44},(-6)^{30},(-12)^1\}.
\]
It is \emph{not} the 1620-edge selected270 base graph, which is non-bipartite;
this closes the tempting $270=270$ identification by an action-level graph
invariant rather than by count.

\subsection{Capacity envelope and local Petersen coding}

Let one unit of base-edge service have capacity $C_b=1$ and write
$\rho=C_s/C_b$ for shortcut capacity.  The four exact all-pairs path policies
have uniform per-edge load vectors
\[
 P_0=(35,239/3),\quad P_1=(137/3,37),\quad
 P_2=(155/3,21),\quad P_3=(373/6,0).
\]
Convexifying this exact two-resource frontier gives the maximum symmetric
all-pairs demand
\[
\lambda_{\max}(\rho)=
\begin{cases}
3(\rho+2)/373,&0<\rho<63/155,\\
3(3\rho+8)/1429,&63/155<\rho<111/137,\\
3(\rho+4)/659,&111/137<\rho<239/105,\\
1/35,&\rho\ge239/105.
\end{cases}
\]
For equal capacities the optimal mixture is $13P_0/80+67P_1/80$, equalizing
both edge-orbit loads at $659/15$ and giving $\lambda_{\max}=15/659$.
Independent erasure/retransmission is incorporated, as an explicit model, by
replacing $C_i$ with $C_iq_i$; an independent M/M/1 layer gives the corresponding
queue divergence at the capacity boundary.

Finally, each 15-edge Petersen fiber carries its canonical binary cycle and cut
codes
\[
 [15,6,5]_2,\qquad [15,9,3]_2.
\]
The cycle-code enumerator is
\[
 1+12z^5+10z^6+15z^8+20z^9+6z^{10};
\]
its 12 minimum words are the Petersen five-cycles.  The code is invariant under
the full local $S_5$, and therefore under the certified $A_5/S_5$ local actions.
It corrects every pattern of at most four erased edge symbols.  Over all 27
fibers the direct sum is $[405,162,5]_2$.  This is redundancy, not free
throughput: the raw cycle-code rate is $2/5$, so no fault-free capacity increase
is inferred.
