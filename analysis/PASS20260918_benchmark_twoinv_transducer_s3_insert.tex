On the W33 controller, the determinant gate admits a much smaller compiler
once the hardware library is enlarged from reduced monomial phases to
adjoint-invariant phases.  For \(X\in\mathfrak{sp}_4(\mathbb F_3)\), the
even characteristic polynomial and Newton identities give
\[
\det X={(\operatorname{tr}X^2)^2-2\operatorname{tr}X^4\over8}.
\]
Reducing in \(\mathbb F_3\),
\[
\boxed{\det X=2\left[(\operatorname{tr}X^2)^2+\operatorname{tr}X^4\right]}.
\]
This identity has been verified on all \(3^{10}=59049\) controller states.
Hence, if direct invariant-phase primitives are available,
\[
\boxed{U_{\det}=P_{(\operatorname{tr}X^2)^2}^{\,2}\,
                  P_{\operatorname{tr}X^4}^{\,2}},
\]
only two commuting nonlinear primitives are required, versus the exact
seventeen-primitive optimum in the native reduced-monomial library.
