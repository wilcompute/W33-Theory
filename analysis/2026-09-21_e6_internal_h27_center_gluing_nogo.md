# Pass369 internal H27 cannot be center-glued to the physical external H27

The attractive identity
[
27cdot27/3=243=3^{1+4}
]
does **not** apply to the two certified H27s currently in the repo.

The Pass369/371 H27 acts regularly on the 27 minuscule E6 weights. Hence every nonidentity element, including its central C3 generator, is fixed-point-free on that set. Its Weyl projection is therefore nontrivial.

But a central element of simply connected E6 has trivial Weyl projection. On the irreducible 27 it acts by a scalar central character, so it fixes every projective weight line.

Therefore
[
Z(H_{27}^{m Pass369})
eq Z(E_6).
]
So this H27 cannot be glued through the diagonal Z3 of
[
(E_6	imes SU(3))/Z_3
]
to the center of the newly physical external-A2 H27.

Conditional on choosing an honest order-27 lift of the Pass369 Weyl subgroup, its intersection with Z(E6) is trivial. Since the E6 and external-SU3 factors commute, the two H27s then generate
[
H_{27}	imes H_{27},
qquad |,cdot,|=729,
]
not the two-qutrit Pauli group of order 243.

This is a targeted no-go, not a no-go for every possible H27 inside E6. A different internal subgroup with center exactly Z(E6) remains an open construction problem.
