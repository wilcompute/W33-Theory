## 12516: the explicit isomorphism

The other track's TL₆ braid generators at δ = √3 (SU(2)₄, ad8846e59) are built here from real two-qutrit Pauli eigenprojectors:
Rᵢ = A·I + A⁻¹√3·Πᵢ with A = e^{5πi/12}. They satisfy the TL relations, unitarity and the braid relations. Projectively each Rᵢ is
(I − Πᵢ) + ω²Πᵢ, a quadratic phase gate, so it is Clifford. A Clifford element is determined modulo scalars by its exact action on
the 81 Paulis with cube-root phases. `tl_braid_iso_exact.py` composes those actions in integers.

- The B₄ image modulo scalars has **order 648**.
- Its action on the 40 Pauli directions is **injective**, with image of order 648: exactly the transvection group G of 12507/12510,
  generated by the images of the same generators. **So the braid words give an explicit isomorphism** between the unitary SU(2)₄ /
  TL₆ braid image of four anyons (modulo scalars) and G = 3^{1+2}:SL(2,3).
- The 9-dimensional representation decomposes as **1 + 2 + 3 + 3**: the commutant has dimension 6, so the 3-dimensional irrep occurs
  twice. On each 3-dimensional block the **projective image has order 216**, the Hessian group; the kernel is the order-3 centre. The
  2-dimensional block has projective image of order 12.

This matches Cui–Wang's one-qutrit metaplectic model, whose braid image in SU(3) has order 648 with centre Z₃ and is of order 216
projectively. The earlier floating-point attempt (`tl_braid_iso.py`, keys by rounded matrices) gave non-group orders such as 443 and
5,498. That was a hashing artefact, and the exact Pauli-action representation replaces it.

## 12515: the good matchings are fixed-point pairs of polyhedral axes

`matching_axes.py` takes the stabiliser of a good matching in PGL(2,q):

- **q = 7:** the 4 matching pairs are exactly the fixed-point pairs of the **4 cyclic subgroups of order 3**, the cube's 3-fold
  (body-diagonal) axes.
- **q = 11:** the 6 pairs are exactly the fixed-point pairs of the **6 subgroups of order 5**, the icosahedron's 5-fold axes.
- **q = 3** is the exception: the order-4 elements of D₄ have no fixed points over F₃.

**Reading [I].** On the Bloch sphere, the fixed points of the octahedral (single-qubit Clifford) group's 3-fold axes are the eight
Bravyi–Kitaev **T-type magic states**, paired with their orthogonal states. The identification here is group-theoretic and exact over
F₇: the minimal even line set of W(3,7) pairs its regulus lines as the 3-fold-axis pairs of S₄. That is the combinatorics of the
magic-state pairs. A map from actual quantum states to F₇ is not claimed; √3 ∉ F₇.
