## Theorem

Let \(A\) be the W(3,3) 40-by-40 collinearity adjacency over \(\mathbb F_2\), \(C=\mathrm{im}(A)\) and \(K=\ker A=C^\perp\). Consider any diagonal transversal physical unitary
\[
U_\theta=\bigotimes_{j=1}^{40}T_j^{\theta_j},\qquad
T=\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{\pi i/4}),\quad
\theta\in(\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z)^{40}.
\]
If \(U_\theta\) preserves the CSS codespace, then **all \(\theta_j\) are even**, hence \(U_\theta\) is a product of physical phase/Pauli Clifford gates. In particular, **no code-preserving non-Clifford gate exists in this complete nonuniform diagonal \(T\)-power family**.

The proof is a necessity argument; it does not assert that every all-even pattern is codespace preserving.

### Proof

The CSS computational basis states are \(\lvert[x]\rangle=|C|^{-1/2}\sum_{c\in C}|x+c\rangle\) for \(x\in K\). A diagonal gate preserving the codespace must be constant within each \(x+C\) coset. It must therefore satisfy
\[
\sum_j\theta_j c_j -2\sum_j\theta_j x_jc_j\equiv0\pmod8
\quad(c\in C,\ x\in K).
\]
Taking \(x=0\) and subtracting shows the **necessary** condition
\[
\theta\cdot(x\circ c)\equiv0\pmod4.
\]
Generate any 16-word basis \(c_i\) of \(C\) and 24-word basis \(x_a\) of \(K\). Every overlap \(z_{ai}=x_a\circ c_i\) has even weight, since \(K=C^\perp\). Reducing \(\theta\) modulo 2 produces **384 homogeneous linear equations** \(\sum_j(\theta_j\bmod2)z_{ai,j}=0\). Their coefficient matrix has **rank 39** over \(\mathbb F_2\); its nullspace is generated by the all-ones vector. Therefore either all exponents are even, or all are odd.

Assume all odd. Write \(\theta_j=1+2s_j\pmod4\). Because \(|z_{ai}|\) is even, the same necessary condition forces
\[
\sum_j s_j z_{ai,j}=\frac{|z_{ai}|}{2}\pmod2.
\]
This system is **inconsistent**. In the deterministic canonical projective-point ordering and binary basis chosen by the script, four of its 384 equations have overlapping-support masks
\[
0x480,\quad 0x4e3,\quad0xc3,\quad0xa0
\]
with respective overlap sizes \(2,6,4,2\). The bitwise XOR of these four masks is zero, while their right-hand sides XOR to
\[
1\oplus 1\oplus0\oplus1=1.
\]
No \(s\) exists; the all-odd option is impossible. QED.
