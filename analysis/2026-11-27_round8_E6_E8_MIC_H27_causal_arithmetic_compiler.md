## 2. E8 branching matches, but full E8 symmetry does not stabilize this one-adjoint model

Restoring SU3 trace pairing in the singlet slice, with W(S)=Tr(S^2)/2+v^T S u and Tr S=0, gives S=-uv^T+(v^Tu)I/3. Substitute: W_eff=-(v^Tu)^2/3. Hence the family dependence again reduces to the single invariant v^T u; for nonzero B the six u,v directions with one invariant constraint and one complex E6 U1 gauge direction give four complex moduli if SU3 is external, not gauged. A single commuting E8-adjoint chiral field has no symmetric cubic invariant: invariant degrees 2,8,12,14,18,20,24,30 (https://abel.math.harvard.edu/~elkies/M222.23/index.html). Thus the representation correspondence does not yield this stabilizing superpotential by unbroken E8 invariance.
