# Physical FI factor-routing theorem

Combine the exact physical twin-(A_4) embedding with the FI-induced
(E_6+A_2) grading.

The organizer (A_4) has eight FI-neutral roots.  Root-set comparison gives

[
8=6+2
]

in the strongest possible sense:

- the six roots of the organizer's FI (3)-block (A_2) are **exactly** the
  six-root external (A_2) component of the FI-induced (E_6+A_2);
- the remaining two roots, the FI (2)-block (A_1), are inside the
  72-root (E_6);
- all 20 roots of the physical Wilson-line (SU(5)) (A_4) are also inside
  that same (E_6).

Moreover the gauge (A_4) and FI (A_1) are orthogonal, so inside (E_6)
there is an exact rank-five root subsystem

[
A_4^{m gauge}+A_1^{m FI}.
]

At Lie-algebra level this is the semisimple part of the familiar
(SU(5)	imes SU(2)	imes U(1)subset E_6) chain, while the FI three-block is
the external (SU(3)).

This gives a sharp physical candidate for the project's family-(SU(3)):
the threefold factor is not inserted by hand but is the (3)-block selected by
the measured anomalous-(U(1)) FI direction.
