\subsection{Raw attestation, BFT authority, nonlocal qutrit embedding, measured placement, and portable audit closure}

The execution stack now separates five previously open trust and implementation
boundaries.  First, the Holotrade verifier consumes the raw PC Client TPM event
log: the initial legacy \texttt{TCG\_PCClientPCREvent} carries the Spec-ID event,
which declares the digest widths used by subsequent crypto-agile
\texttt{TCG\_PCR\_EVENT2} records.  Informational \texttt{EV\_NO\_ACTION}
records are excluded from PCR extension, while the remaining digests are replayed
and compared with the signed TPM quote.  AK/EK X.509 provenance is typed
separately from quote validity and from an AK--EK credential-activation binding.

Second, capability epochs are no longer authorized by a static multisignature
object alone.  Five Ed25519 validators execute proposal, PREPARE-QC, and COMMIT-QC
phases with quorum four.  A four-vote timeout certificate rotates the leader;
anti-equivocation votes, prepare locks, finalization state, and the highest commit
certificate are durable across restart.  The executable partition witness shows
that a two-node side cannot finalize, the connected four-node side advances after
a timeout, and the lagging replica reconciles from the final commit certificate.
For $n=5,t=4$, two quorums intersect in at least
\[
2t-n=3,
\]
which exceeds the assumed one-Byzantine budget.  This is an executable BFT
state-machine certificate, not a claim of a production asynchronous network
stack.

Third, the qutrit fault-tolerance frontier changes qualitatively.  The previous
certificate remains valid:
\[
\boxed{\text{no monomial/zero-extension 20-edge CSS embedding exists}},
\]
because the external $H_0$ span contains weight six whereas every nonzero parent
$X$-stabilizer in the W33 edge code has weight at least twelve.  However that is
a locality obstruction, not a general symplectic obstruction.  The new
construction produces
\[
A,B\in \mathbb F_3^{20\times240},\qquad AB^T=I_{20},
\]
so
\[
(x,z)\longmapsto(xA,zB)
\]
is an injective symplectic Pauli embedding.  The seven external $H_1$ rows map
to independent logical-$X$ classes of the exact $[[240,81,3]]_3$ parent and the
two $H_0$ rows map into its $X$-stabilizer rowspace.  Thus the broad nonlocal
algebraic embedding class is \textbf{SAT}.  Fault-tolerant scheduling nevertheless
remains refused: the verified map has no low-weight/local optical compiler,
mapped decoder, or mapped threshold/noise certificate.

Fourth, Holotrade placement now closes the prediction loop.  Linux RAPL counter
deltas and Kepler Prometheus energy counters can be ingested as physically
measured energy, while strong-root storage ledgers provide realized blob-seconds
and byte-seconds.  Per-node calibration factors update from prediction error and
feed back into the next storage/recovery/energy/erasure-aware placement ranking.
CI fixtures exercise this controller but are explicitly labeled non-physical.

Finally, one portable audit verifier now accepts a bundle containing the signed
delivery receipt and its constituent evidence.  It independently re-verifies raw
TPM or SEV-SNP evidence, verifies each four-of-five epoch certificate against
trusted authority keys, recomputes the passport content identity, rebuilds the
flight-recorder root from epoch/GC/magic histories and the final artifact set,
and only then accepts the COSE/Ed25519 receipt.  The portable acceptance predicate
is therefore
\[
\boxed{\text{hardware}\land\text{authority}\land\text{passport}\land
\text{replay}\land\text{artifacts}\land\text{receipt signature}}.
\]

These closures do not constitute a fabricated photonic processor, a measured
fault-tolerance threshold, a locality-optimal symplectic embedding, or a formal
proof of BFT liveness under every asynchronous schedule.  Those remain separate
physical and protocol frontiers.
