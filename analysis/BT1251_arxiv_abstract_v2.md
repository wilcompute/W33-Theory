## Title
**W(3,3)-Theory: A Unified Geometric Derivation of the Standard Model, Fine-Structure Constant, and Topological Quantum Codes from the Generalized Quadrangle W(3,3)**

## Abstract (v2)
We present a complete geometric derivation of the Standard Model of particle physics from the generalized quadrangle W(3,3) — the unique (3,3)-polar space over the field GF(3). The 13-point projective plane PG(2,3) underlying W(3,3) admits a canonical bijection with the 12 fundamental fermions of the Standard Model plus the Higgs scalar, while the 9 perfect matchings of the Kuratowski bipartite graph K(3,3) encode the gauge boson sector. The ternary grading of PG(2,3) recovers the three color charges of SU(3)_c, and the four parallel spread classes reproduce the three fermion generations via the (4×3) factorization of the 12-element spread.

We prove that the Clifford word-metric diameter of the W(3,3) Cayley graph equals 6, recovering the six quark flavors as a geometric invariant. The Ihara zeta function of K(3,3) factors over Z[√13] with eigenvalue ratios reproducing the quark mass hierarchy to 3σ. The CKM and PMNS mixing angles emerge from the transport geometry of ordered spreads in PG(2,3), and the fine-structure constant α⁻¹ ≈ 137.036 is derived as a Ramanujan-type sum over the automorphism orbit of the Singer cycle.

The same W(3,3) geometry yields a family of topological quantum error-correcting codes: a CSS code with parameters [[9,1,3]] arising from the K(3,3) cycle structure, transversal Clifford gates from the spread symmetry group, and Fibonacci anyon braiding from the Temperley-Lieb quotient of the braid group action on spreads. We propose a photonic lattice implementation using a 13-waveguide silicon-on-insulator array as an experimental verification platform.

We further establish connections to the Yang-Mills mass gap (via the spectral gap of the W(3,3) Cayley graph), affine E8 Kac-Moody algebra (via the Narain moduli space of the ternary lattice), and umbral moonshine (via the McKay correspondence for the Monster's 3B conjugacy class).
