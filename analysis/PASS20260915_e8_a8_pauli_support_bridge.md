# Pass 2026-09-15 — the E8 A8 fixed-sector support is the two-qutrit Pauli grading support

## Why this was the next gap

The repository already had three ingredients, but not their exact algebraic weld:

- Pass 1021: the E8 root shell fibers onto W(3,3).
- Holotrade `68df33f`: the same order-three twist has fixed algebra of type A8 = sl(9), by the classical Kac classification.
- The two-qutrit Pauli algebra has 80 nonidentity labels, and the standard tensor-Pauli grading of sl(9,C) is indexed by the 80 nonzero vectors of F_3^4.

The new root-fibration/twisted-register weld identifies those 80 quotient vectors with the 80 order-three root orbits. The question here is whether the **addition/bracket support** also agrees.

## Exact root-orbit census

Let `g=c^10` be the canonical order-three element and let the 80 oriented A2 root orbits be the degrees. For every unordered pair of distinct degrees `(v,w)`, the certificate checks all nine sums of one root from the `v` orbit and one root from the `w` orbit.

The result over all `C(80,2)=3160` pairs is exactly:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\Omega(v,w) & \text{number of degree pairs} & \text{root-addition support}\\ \hline
0 & 1000 & 0\ \text{root sums}\\
1 & 1080 & 3\ \text{root sums, one target orbit}\\
2 & 1080 & 3\ \text{root sums, one target orbit}
\end{array}
\]

For every nonorthogonal pair, the unique target orbit is exactly the quotient degree `v+w`.

So at support level,

\[
[L_v,L_w]=0\ \text{can occur only on the symplectic-orthogonal pairs},
\qquad
[L_v,L_w]\subseteq L_{v+w}\ \text{otherwise}.
\]

The certificate deliberately records this as a **support theorem**, not yet an equality of Lie structure constants, because that stronger statement requires choosing and checking a Chevalley cocycle / order-three lift on the root vectors.

## Independent Pauli control

For the 80 nonzero labels of `F_3^4`, the standard two-qutrit symplectic form has the identical unordered-pair census:

\[
1000\ \text{commuting},\qquad 1080\ \text{phase }1,\qquad 1080\ \text{phase }2.
\]

For tensor generalized Pauli matrices, the commutator vanishes exactly on the symplectic-orthogonal pairs; otherwise it is a nonzero scalar multiple of the Pauli matrix of degree `v+w`.

Thus the E8 oriented-root-orbit support and the two-qutrit Pauli support coincide on every one of the 3160 pairs, not merely in aggregate counts.

## Literature boundary

Two external facts are classical/published and are not claimed here as new:

1. The order-three E8 grading with the relevant Kac class has fixed subalgebra sl(9). A modern review is Cabrera et al., *Linear models of the exceptional Lie algebra e8* (2025), which explicitly describes the Z3 grading with neutral component sl(9).
2. Pelantova, Svobodova and Tremblay, arXiv:quant-ph/0510106 / J. Math. Phys. 47 (2006), study the fine grading of sl(p^2,C) generated by tensor products of generalized Pauli matrices and show that its normalizer quotient is Sp(4,F_p) x Z2. At p=3 the grading group is F_3^4 and the symplectic symmetry is Sp(4,3).

The new corpus contribution is the exact identification of E8 root-orbit addition support with that F_3^4 symplectic Pauli support law.
