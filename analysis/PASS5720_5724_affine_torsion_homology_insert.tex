\subsection*{Passes 5720--5724: the affine gauge face complex has exact $3$-torsion}
Pass~5691 found an arithmetically exceptional rank drop for the nine-site affine gauge complex: the 54 translation plaquettes together with the 12 affine-line faces span the full $28$-dimensional cycle space over characteristic zero, but have rank only $26$ over $\mathbb F_3$.  The integral calculation now resolves the defect exactly.

Choose the star spanning tree of $K_9$.  Its $28$ chords form an integral basis of the cycle lattice.  In that basis the translation-plaquette boundary lattice has Smith invariants
\[
1^{24},
\]
whereas the full 66-face lattice has
\[
\boxed{1^{26}\oplus3^2.}
\]
Hence, for the full affine face complex $X$,
\[
\boxed{H_0(X;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z,\qquad
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)\cong(\mathbb Z/3)^2,\qquad
H_2(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{38}.}
\]
The plaquette-only complex instead has
\[
H_1(X_P;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^4,
\qquad H_2(X_P;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{30},
\]
and the 12 affine-line attachments induce Smith invariants
\[
\boxed{1,1,3,3}
\]
on this four-dimensional integral homology lattice.  Thus the line faces kill all four real plaquette modes but leave an index-$9$ residue, producing two order-three classes.

The universal-coefficient consequence is
\[
H^1(X;\mathbb Z)=0,
\qquad
\boxed{H^2(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{38}\oplus(\mathbb Z/3)^2.}
\]
Accordingly the two mod-$3$ one-homology/one-cohomology modes are torsion/Bockstein modes, not hidden real harmonic modes.

The symmetry is equally rigid.  All $432$ elements of
\[
\operatorname{AGL}(2,3)=\mathbb F_3^2\rtimes GL(2,3)
\]
preserve the face set.  On $H_1(X;\mathbb F_3)\cong\mathbb F_3^2$ the kernel is exactly the order-$9$ translation subgroup and the image is the full order-$48$ group $GL(2,3)$.  In the explicit quotient basis used by the certificate,
\[
Q(M,b)=P^{-1}\bigl(\det(M)M^T\bigr)P,
\qquad
P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
\]
so the action is independent of the affine translation and carries an essential determinant twist.

This $(\mathbb Z/3)^2$ belongs to the nine-vertex/36-edge affine face complex.  It is not Pass~5121's distinct one-dimensional $\mathbb Z/3$ saturation quotient on an $81\times108$ incidence carrier; no chain map between them is asserted.  Nor does this torsion fix the Yang--Mills coupling, repair the adjoint-trivial vertical $\mathbb Z_3$ connection, prove confinement or a mass gap, choose matter, or supply a continuum limit.  It is an exact finite homology and symmetry theorem.
