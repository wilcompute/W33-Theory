## Front 1 — Exact algebra distinction and the conditional S3 ↔ H27 bridge

The project frequently uses the number 27 for (i) the left-regular
address representation of the extraspecial group H27, (ii) the
gauge-invariant two-loop S3 Hilbert space, and (iii) three qutrits.
These have different natural operator algebras.

For the Heisenberg group H27 with elements \((a,b,c)\in\mathbb F_3^3\)
and product
\((a,b,c)(a',b',c')=(a+a',b+b',c+c'+ab')\),
the 27 left-regular 27×27 matrices are linearly independent,
commute with the 27 right-regular matrices, and generate a
**27-dimensional** associative matrix subalgebra.
The regular center element splits into three eigenspaces
of dimensions \(\mathbf{9+9+9}\).
There are nine one-dimensional and two three-dimensional irreducible
H27 representations (so \(\sum d_\alpha^2=27\)).
By contrast, the full three-qutrit Weyl algebra
\(\operatorname{Mat}_{27}(\mathbb C)\) has dimension
\(\mathbf{729}\), with 729 tensor-Pauli operators generated by three
independent qudit pairs \((X_j,Z_j)\).
The single-qutrit Pauli basis orthogonality was directly tested.

### Controlled search in the 27D S3 physical sector

The prior \((E,M,C)\) gauge Hamiltonian has a nontrivial exchange
operator; the exchange-breaking \(D=E_1-E_2\) still leaves
**18+4+4+1** numerically invariant blocks under a generic
simple-spectrum Hamiltonian.

A set of ten exact gauge-invariant raw-space operators was constructed:
\(E,M,C,D\); two single-loop Wilson characters \(W_1,W_2\);
a color-doublet antisymmetric difference \(C_{\rm diff}\);
correlated matter \(C_{\rm cross}\); a symmetrized gauge-invariant
holonomy Nielsen shear \(S_{\rm Nielsen}\) defined by
\((g,k)\mapsto(g,gk)\); and loop inversion.
All commute with the *original* S3 gauge constraints and are
Hermitian on the 144D unreduced Hilbert space.

For a finite-dimensional unital *-algebra \(\mathcal A\),
the simple-spectrum connectivity criterion is:
if \(H\in\mathcal A\) has distinct eigenvalues, any commuting
operator is diagonal in its eigenbasis. If the union graph of
nonzero offdiagonal entries of the generators is connected, all
diagonal entries must be equal. Its commutant is scalar, hence
\(\mathcal A=\operatorname{Mat}_{27}(\mathbb C)\).
The argument is exact; the nonzero tests here use floating-point
thresholds, so this is **numerical support**, not an exact
rational/interval certificate.

The ten-operator family gives a single connected 27D component.
Among the six optional operators appended to \(E,M,C,D\), an
exhaustive search by cardinality finds the first irreducible
pair: **correlated matter \(C_{\rm cross}\) plus Nielsen shear**.
This was also tested with three deterministic generic-Hamiltonian
random seeds, requiring simple spectral gaps and nonzero
transition magnitudes safely larger than 1e-8.

**Physical firewall:** The Nielsen automorphism operates on
holonomy words and is not derived as a finite-depth local interaction.
Even an exact demonstration that the extended algebra is full
\(\operatorname{Mat}_{27}\) would NOT yield a canonical physical
intertwiner to the original H27 address representation, a
nearest-neighbor qudit circuit, chiral particles or dynamics of
our universe. Any abstract 27D Hilbert isomorphism is cheap;
covariance, naturality and physically local generation are the
difficult requirements.

## Front 2 — Gauge-invariant mobile S3 matter on a square with diagonal

The new graph has four vertices, five oriented links, two triangles,
three spanning-tree links and two remaining Wilson holonomies
\(g_0,g_1\in S_3\). It contains one mobile 2D S3 color doublet
with four distinct spatial site states. Tree-gauge fixing leaves

\[
\dim\mathcal H_{\rm raw,fixed}=
|S_3|^2(4\cdot2)=288,
\quad
\dim\mathcal H_{\rm phys}
=\frac{288+2(-36)}6=\boxed{36}.
\]

For comparison, the unfixed raw space is \(6^5(4\cdot2)=62{,}208\).
The direct gauge action simultaneously conjugates the two holonomies
and acts on the mobile color doublet; exact projector idempotence,
trace 36, Hermiticity, residual Gauss commutators and physical
ground-state Gauss constraints are verified.

The Hamiltonian consists of electric class-sum shifts, two triangular
plaquette Wilson characters (holonomy words \(g_1\) and \(g_1g_0\)),
and gauge-covariant hopping on all five edges. Both
\([E,M]\ne0\) and \([E,T]\ne0\) hold in the physical sector.
The four-point parameter scan includes a nondegenerate gap for
generic nonzero couplings, but the electric-only point has a
degenerate ground state.

**Scope:** This is a dynamical single-particle finite multiloop
lattice model; no thermodynamic limit, confinement transition,
SU(3) continuum, multiple dynamical fermion sectors or GR coupling
was demonstrated.

## Front 3 — Lorentzian 4D triangulation, matter-action refinement and no-go tests

Construct the standard Freudenthal triangulation of a rectangular
four-dimensional slab, with 24 ordered four-simplices per 4D hypercube.
For subdivision n=1,2,3, the corresponding f-vectors
\((f_0,f_1,f_2,f_3,f_4)\) are:
- n=1: (16,65,110,84,24).
- n=2: (81,544,1232,1152,384).
- n=3: (256,2145,5454,5508,1944).

Each has Euler characteristic 1 (open four-ball topology).
For both timesteps 0.37 and 1.2 relative to unit spatial spacing,
every 4-simplex Gram matrix has Lorentzian inertia (1 negative,
3 positive), and the sum of simplex volumes is exactly
\(n^4\,\Delta t\).

### New causal-mesh direction artifact

Treat future-directed edges with Lorentzian squared interval
\(\Delta t^2-\sum_i \Delta x_i^2>0\) as timelike.
For \(\Delta t=.37\), no future-timelike edge moves spatially:
the timelike 1-skeleton consists of separate fixed-position
temporal chains. For \(\Delta t=1.2\), timelike spatial edges exist,
but the chosen Freudenthal simplex order allows only **positive**
spatial coordinate steps along future-timelike edges.
This apparent directionality is a triangulation-orientation
artifact, not an emergent chiral or temporal physical law.

### Nonlinear local Lorentzian scalar matter action

Over the *unit* four-box, evaluate the piecewise-linear finite-element
Lorentzian kinetic action
\[
S[\phi]=\frac12\int
[-(\partial_t\phi)^2+\sum_{i=1}^3(\partial_i\phi)^2]\,d^4x,
\qquad
\phi(x)=\prod_{\mu=0}^3\sin(\pi x_\mu).
\]
The exact continuum action is \(\pi^2/16=0.6168502751\).
For n=2,3,4,5 the **computed** finite-element actions
approach it: 0.5, 0.5625, 0.58578644, 0.59682189.
This is a real local discretized Lorentzian matter action
that converges under mesh refinement for one smooth test field,
not a nonlinear gravitational Regge action nor an Einstein equation.

### An exact topology obstruction to the previous curved S4 model

The earlier closed four-sphere triangulation from the 5-simplex
boundary has f-vector (6,15,20,15,6) and Euler characteristic **2**.
A closed compact smooth even-dimensional manifold admitting a
nowhere-degenerate Lorentzian metric must have Euler characteristic 0
(consider the time-orientation double cover if necessary).
Therefore the earlier Euclidean curved S4 model **cannot simply
receive a globally nonsingular Lorentzian signature**.
The open Lorentzian slab is not forbidden by this closed-manifold
argument. Curvature, dynamical causal constraints, graviton
propagation and a convergent nonflat Lorentzian Regge sequence
remain open.

## Front 4 — Witness-adapted MUB photonic memory, not full Witting tomography

The earlier local Witting dual witness used 40×40=1600 joint outcomes
and had a conservative Hoeffding input-pair bound 155,076 for
the p=.35 quantum-memory hypothesis and 85% per analyzer efficiency.
This is **not** an experimentally optimal shot count.

Construct the four mutually unbiased qutrit bases from the eigenvectors
of \(Z,X,XZ,XZ^2\), with Bob measured in the complex-conjugate basis.
Exactly (on the embedded 3×3 Hilbert space),

\[
C=\frac14\sum_{b=1}^4\sum_{j=0}^2
 |u_{bj}\rangle\langle u_{bj}|\otimes
 |u_{bj}^*\rangle\langle u_{bj}^*|
 =\frac{I_9+3|\Phi_3\rangle\langle\Phi_3|}{4}.
\]

The probability of same outcomes from two local analyzers is
\(c=(1+3F)/4\).
For any separable state \(F\le1/3\), so \(c\le1/2\).
The chosen depolarized qutrit-memory alternative p=.35 has
\(F=(1+8p)/9=0.4222222\), hence \(c=(1+2p)/3=0.5666667\).
With independently characterized state- and
outcome-independent detector survival \(\eta=.85\) each,
the all-trials joint matching probabilities are
\(p_0=\eta^2/2=.36125\) and
\(p_1=\eta^2(1+2p)/3=.409416667\).

The **exact IID one-sided binomial** smallest trial counts for
false-positive level <=1% and power >=95%:
- **472** ideal global (entangling) Bell projection trials.
- **891** local four-MUB-basis trials with ideal detectors.
- **1612** local four-MUB-basis input-pair trials with eta=.85 each.
- Earlier **155,076** Witting shot figure is a loose,
  distribution-free Hoeffding bound; it is not a fair direct
  detector-by-detector benchmark against these exact binomial
  sample sizes.

All four local basis unitaries are embedded in a U(4)
four-rail interferometer and passed exact Givens reconstruction.
The compiler may report diagonal-only two-mode blocks; it also
records the number of *nontrivial mixing* blocks separately.
Small independent static phase/mixer perturbations were sampled.
Those samples do not validate a separability threshold in the
presence of arbitrary calibration bias or source/detector side channels.
The ideal global Bell projector is not assumed deterministically
available with only passive single-photon optics.

This is a well-known **MUB two-design entanglement witness**,
not a new measurement-theory theorem. The useful project-specific
advance is replacing an inefficient general informationally complete
tomography protocol with a four-setting witness-specific one.

## Front 5 — Four-dimensional overlap index and the reducible SU2 guard

Build explicit Euclidean 4D 4×4 Clifford gamma matrices,
Wilson–Dirac hopping operators on L^4 periodic sites, and
uniform orthogonal 2-torus fluxes \((Q_{01},Q_{23})\).
The overlap index is
\[
\mathrm{index}\,D_{\rm ov}
=-\frac12\operatorname{Tr}\operatorname{sign}
[\gamma_5 D_W(-m)].
\]
Test the exact U(1) plaquette phases and sampled overlap
Ginsparg–Wilson residuals. At L=3, unit product flux
\(Q_{01}Q_{23}=1\) gives index +1 in the m=1 branch;
reversing one of the fluxes changes it to -1.
For m=1,3,5,7, branch indices are **+1,-3,+3,-1**
for the same flux background; these Wilson-mass choices
represent different regulator sectors.

A significant **negative cutoff control**:
at L=2, the same unit background flux yields overlap index **0**
in the m=1 branch even though \(H_W\) is gapped.
At L=3, the correct unit index is resolved.
Thus a gapped index on a very coarse lattice does not alone
validate the continuum topological charge.

Embed the Abelian U(1) links into SU(2) fundamentals as
\(\mathrm{diag}(u,u^{-1})\). This gives a **reducible SU(2)-valued
Cartan background**, and the two charge sectors contribute
the same index, producing fundamental Dirac index +2 for
\(Q_{01}=Q_{23}=1,m=1,L=3\).
It is not a noncommuting SU(2) instanton; it remains
Abelian under the chosen subgroup. It is not a chiral
gauge theory, does not solve Witten or perturbative
anomaly constraints for a proposed Weyl spectrum, and
does not select the sign of the observed Standard Model
chirality dynamically.

## Additional independent controls and epistemic constraints

**A. H27 group algebra versus Pauli basis dimension.**
Same vector-space dimension 27 does not identify a
27-dimensional group algebra with the full 729-dimensional
irreducible three-qutrit matrix algebra. The exact regular
central eigenspaces are 9+9+9.

**B. Compact Lorentzian topology obstruction.**
A closed four-sphere has chi=2 and is not a globally regular
Lorentzian spacetime, regardless of how its Euclidean
simplices are interpreted.

**C. Fake causal chirality from simplex orientation.**
Even an exactly flat Lorentzian signature can have a
directed 1-skeleton supporting only positive spatial
transport. Reverse triangulation orientation before
identifying physical parity violation.

**D. Coarse-overlap index mismatch.**
Flux and finite spectral gap do not prevent a too-coarse
Wilson lattice from missing the continuum topological
index. Lattice-spacing controls are indispensable.

**E. Information theory versus operational tests.**
A symmetric informationally complete-style readout can
have a larger estimator variance than a small,
witness-adapted set of local bases. Counts of quantum
modes do not fix achievable statistical sensitivity.
