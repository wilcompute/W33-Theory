\subsection{A $216\times540$ coherent circuit incidence geometry}
The sentinel matroid contains a $216$-orbit of five-circuits and a $540$-orbit of six-circuits.  The diagonal $\operatorname{PSp}(4,3)$ action on the $116640$ ordered cross-pairs has exactly $17$ orbitals.  By support intersection size the pair counts are
\[
|C_5\cap C_6|=0:54000,\quad 1:51840,\quad 2:6480,\quad 3:4320,
\]
carried respectively by $8,6,1,2$ orbitals.

The maximal-overlap relation $|C_5\cap C_6|=3$ is biregular of degrees
\[
\boxed{20\text{ on the }216\text{-side},\qquad 8\text{ on the }540\text{-side}},
\]
and splits into two equal $2160$-pair orbitals, each of degrees $10$ and $4$.

Fix a six-circuit.  Its six labels induce $3K_2$ in the GQ$(4,2)$ graph.  Each of its eight maximal-overlap five-circuit neighbours chooses one endpoint from each of the three matching edges, hence the local neighbour set is the full $2^3$ cube.  The order-$48$ stabilizer $C_2\times S_4$ acts on that cube through the faithful $S_4$ quotient, with central kernel $C_2$, and splits the eight vertices into two invariant tetrahedra
\[
\boxed{8=4+4}.
\]
After orienting the three matching edges these are the even- and odd-parity cube classes; reversing endpoint conventions can exchange their names but not the intrinsic unordered partition.  No identification with a separate chirality variable is made without an explicit equivariant map.
