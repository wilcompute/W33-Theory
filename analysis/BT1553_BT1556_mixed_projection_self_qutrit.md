# BT1553-BT1556: Mixed-triple projection, mu signs, product obstruction, and self-entangled qutrit

## BT1553

The 270 E6 mixed triples are projected to 24 carrier rows by six A2 axes and four orientation slots.  The projection is nonuniform: six rows receive 12 terms and eighteen rows receive 11 terms.

## BT1554

Projection-count parity gives a nonbalanced 6/18 sign shadow.  A balanced 12/12 shadow can be imposed by orientation slot, but it is not inherited from projection counts.  Packetwise mu transport remains missing.

## BT1555

The 270-triple projection increases the 12+12 product support from a 12-edge matching to a 24-edge 2-regular bipartite support.  It remains sparse and nonuniform, so product structure is still obstructed without mixed-triple degrees of freedom or U/V closure.

## BT1556

W(3,3) remains the exact two-qutrit Pauli commutation geometry.  The underexplored move is to read the two qutrit tensor factors as past/future Choi legs of one self-entangled qutrit.  This preserves the W33 carrier while changing the interpretation of the tensor factors.

## Current synthesis

The E6/Weyl bridge now has an explicit projection obstruction, and the qutrit substrate now has a sharper interpretation: two-qutrit geometry can be used as one self-entangled qutrit evaluated against its own past/future copy.
