# Charged wino selection rule at the W33 primitive monopole

Take the exact supersymmetric vacuum and its primitive magnetic charge. Work at fixed dyon electric weight and restrict asymptotic excitations to the 16 incoming and 16 outgoing lowest fermion waves. Cartan charge conservation gives a sharp obstruction and an exact multiparticle test. The replay reconstructs the weights from the canonical Hessian quotient and the unbroken gauge roots. [Core and radial channel calculation](2026-11-10_monopole_core_channels.md)

## An index-two obstruction with a unique charged gaugino

Write a Cartan weight as (c1,c2,h,y), where h = 2T3 and y = 6Y. Each side generates the full character lattice of the faithfully embedded Standard Model torus. In these coordinates the lattice has index six in Z^4 and is generated by the weights of the defining SU(5) five. The third and fourth coordinates have equal parity.

Remove the outgoing charged weak gaugino of weight (0,0,−2,0). The other outgoing weights generate exactly the kernel of the order-two character

    chi(c1,c2,h,y) = (h − 3y)/2 mod 2.

The lattice index becomes twelve in Z^4. Both gluino weights, all ordinary outgoing chiral weights and the down-type Higgsino have chi = 0. The wino alone has chi = 1. All twelve ordinary incoming quark and lepton waves have chi = 1. Consequently an ordinary fixed-dyon outgoing Fock state made from these waves must contain an odd number of charged wino particles and holes, irrespective of multiplicity. The wino and ordinary chiral outgoing weights already generate the full lattice, even without gluinos or Higgsinos. This is an electric Cartan charge rule in the specified lowest-wave space. An electrically neutral up-type Higgs VEV has weight (0,0,−1,3), zero magnetic pairing and chi = 1. Its weight alone restores the full lattice when added to the wino-free weights, so the index-two rule need not survive electroweak breaking. A charged W boson carries the same Cartan weight as the wino. Dyon recoil, extra radiation, higher partial waves and twisted sectors also change the state space of the argument. [Generalized-symmetry approach to monopole scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07318)

## Minimal ordinary final multiplicities

At fixed dyon weight, the four incoming Higgsino and gaugino waves can match single outgoing holes. The twelve other waves have no single outgoing particle or hole with the right charge. Each nevertheless has an explicit three-excitation witness preserving the conventional baryon and lepton labels separately. Since all the included charges have magnetic pairing odd, every fixed-dyon witness for one incoming excitation has odd multiplicity.

An exhaustive charge-sum enumeration through seven outgoing excitations gives the following minima. For the violation column the final total preserves the formal B−L label and changes both B and L. The seesaw matching has a Majorana term, so these labels organize candidate channels and B−L is not being assumed to be an exact symmetry of the full action. [Neutrino matching](2026-11-10_neutrino_matching.md)

| Incoming lowest wave | Minimum with B and L preserved | First with equal nonzero ΔB and ΔL |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Q, two colors and three families | 3 | 5, ΔB = ΔL = −1 |
| D^c or E^c, three families each | 3 | 5, ΔB = ΔL = +1 |
| Charged gauginos, three weights | 1 | 7, ΔB = ΔL = −1 |
| Up-type Higgsino | 1 | 7, ΔB = ΔL = +1 |

The enumeration treats repeated species as available at distinct radial momenta. The witnesses conserve Cartan charges. A dynamical calculation still needs the full centralizer intertwiners, wavefunctions, and transition amplitudes. These minima do not apply to general hadronic scattering or to states with a different dyon electric weight.

## Two exact Abelian boundary candidates

Assign the down-type Higgsino to the up-type Higgsino hole pairing and pair the three outgoing leptons with the three down antiquarks at the level of charge labels. This choice uses the weight multiplicities. In the actual light-current calculation the lepton block alone has rank two. The boundary candidates therefore require separate dynamical matching.

The twelve ordinary labels pair by the same root step alpha = (0,1,−1,−5). For each of three copies, the four distinct incoming weights sum to −2 alpha. On their bosonized occupation coordinates reflect in the hyperplane normal to the all-ones vector on the four slots. Alternatively, reflect on the twelve slots at once. Flip the remaining four outgoing signs to convert the hole coordinates back to particle coordinates. Both resulting matrices are exactly orthogonal, preserve all four Cartan currents and the formal B−L current, and act on integer incoming charge occupations. The shifts in the outgoing occupations are half the four-slot sum in each block, or one sixth the twelve-slot sum. Thus the quotient by the incoming occupations mapped to integers is (Z2)^3 for the three-block matrix and Z6 for the collective matrix. The lowest possible denominator of an orthogonal matrix satisfying Cartan conservation is two, because an integral orthogonal matrix would be a signed permutation and the twelve unmatched weights rule it out.

The different quotients show that the anomaly-free Cartan data alone do not select the boundary condition. The quotients here are quotients of occupation lattices, with a possible interpretation in the shifted-lattice treatment of twisted boundary states. Identifying physical symmetry defects or an actual scattering matrix requires the monopole dynamics. [Twisted conformal boundary states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40779)
