# BT1528 Tetrahedral Carrier Realization

K4 has 4 vertices, 6 edges, 4 triangular faces, and 24 rank-3 flags.

The flags are split into two 12-flag packets:

- a pointed Csaszar vertex-star carrier;
- a pointed Szilassi face-star carrier.

This is a flag-carrier identification, not yet a unique orientation/sign convention.
