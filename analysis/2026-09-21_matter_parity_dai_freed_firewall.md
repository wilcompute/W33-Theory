# Matter parity survives the anomaly check — with one crucial mixed-symmetry firewall

The exact W33/E6 bridge already fixes
\[
27=16_{1}+10_{-2}+1_{4},\qquad P_M=(-1)^{Q_\psi}.
\]
This makes the perturbative parent anomaly check elementary.  In the normalization
\(T(10)=1,\ T(16)=2\),
\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{grav}^2U(1)_\psi}
=16(1)+10(-2)+1(4)=0,
\]
\[
\mathcal A_{U(1)_\psi^3}
=16(1)^3+10(-2)^3+1(4)^3=0,
\]
and
\[
\mathcal A_{SO(10)^2U(1)_\psi}=2(1)+1(-2)=0.
\]
Thus the same charge whose parity distinguishes the 16 from the 10+1 is an anomaly-free parent \(U(1)_\psi\) charge on a complete 27.

The nonperturbative statement is equally sharp.  Davighi, Lohitsiri and Poovuttikul, JHEP 03 (2024) 119 (arXiv:2311.18023), compute
\[
\Omega^{\mathrm{Spin}}_5(B\mathbb Z_2)=0.
\]
Therefore an ordinary internal \(Z_2\) by itself has no four-dimensional Dai–Freed global anomaly.  Their same calculation also gives
\[
\Omega^{\mathrm{Spin}}_5(BU(1)\times B\mathbb Z_2)=\mathbb Z_4,
\]
so the statement must **not** be overextended: an independent \(U(1)\times Z_2\) can carry a mixed global anomaly.

That is exactly the right boundary for W33.  Its matter parity is not an independent add-on; it is the mod-two reduction of \(Q_\psi\).  Garcia-Etxebarria and Montero, JHEP 08 (2019) 003 (arXiv:1808.00009), independently report the Spin(10) GUT anomaly-free in their Dai–Freed analysis.

## The important non-identification

Do **not** import the famous \(\mathbb Z_{16}\) anomaly of the twisted
\(\mathrm{Spin}\times_{\mathbb Z_2}\mathbb Z_4\) / \(Z_{4,X}\) symmetry into this result.
That \(Z_4\) generator squares to fermion parity.  The W33 matter-parity object certified here is the ordinary internal \(Z_2=(-1)^{Q_\psi}\).

## What this settles — and what it does not

This removes an anomaly obstruction to the W33 matter-parity bridge at the representation/gauge-symmetry level.  It does **not** establish that a heterotic vacuum preserves the symmetry.  The latter still requires a condensate consisting only of matter-even fields that solves the D- and F-flatness conditions.  Holotrade's class-wide proof remains blocked because the raw 88-model rational charge ledgers are not committed.
