\paragraph{Pass5230--5237: the q=5 footprint dual shell exposes a rank-4 scheme.}
Let $F$ be the $156\times325$ point--dual-grid incidence matrix from Passes 5201--5203 and let $C_F=\operatorname{im}(F^T)$.  The dual remains $C_F^\perp=[325,260,8]_2$ (Pass 5213), but the complete minimum shell is now rigidly classified.  There are exactly $24375$ weight-$8$ dual words, forming a single orbit under the generated symplectic transvections.  Every support covers exactly $48$ points twice and induces $K_8$ minus a perfect matching in the $NO_5^+(5)$ block graph.  The shell span has dimension $259$.

An explicit odd dual word of weight $9$, on coordinates
\[
\{15,20,38,44,139,164,197,278,311\},
\]
therefore supplies the missing parity direction.  Consequently $C_F$ has an exact sparse parity-check presentation with $259$ independent weight-$8$ checks and one weight-$9$ check.  Since the dual minimum is $8$, this also gives odd-dual minimum $9$.

The weight-$8$ shell refines the old strongly regular relation.  For two distinct P components, let $R_1$ denote block adjacency, $R_2$ nonadjacency with weight-$8$ pair-codegree $5$, and $R_3$ nonadjacency with pair-codegree $0$.  Their valencies are
\[
(k_1,k_2,k_3)=(144,120,60),
\]
and they close to a three-class association scheme.  Selected intersection-number rows are

Thus the weight-$8$ Tanner shell sees strictly more geometry than the SRG parameters alone.

The Pass5212 Hoffman $13$-coclique is also a systematic quotient: each W-point lies in exactly one of its $13$ blocks, the restriction of $F$ to those columns has binary rank $13$, and puncturing those columns leaves rank $65$.  Hence
