# Pass 11614 — the Hesse CP order parameter is the D3 Coxeter discriminant

Reservation: `1be1b367f` (Sage track).

Passes 11607–11613 established that the sign of
`W=(x^2-y^2)(y^2-z^2)(z^2-x^2)`
can select both a Spin(10) Weyl block and one of the two E8 matter shells.
This pass identifies the exact reflection geometry carried by that sign.

## Reflection discriminant

For the six positive D3 root forms
`x-y, x+y, x-z, x+z, y-z, y+z`,

`W=-(x-y)(x+y)(x-z)(x+z)(y-z)(y+z)`.

Hence `W=0` is exactly the D3 reflection arrangement:
`x=±y`, `x=±z`, or `y=±z`.

The exact 24-element signed-permutation realization generated by simple roots
`(1,-1,0)`, `(0,1,-1)`, `(0,1,1)` satisfies

`W(gx)=det(g) W(x)`.

So the CP/chirality bit is literally the Coxeter orientation character.

## One Hesse vacuum per Weyl chamber

Act on the generic ray `(1,2,3)/sqrt(14)`. The exact orbit has 24 distinct
points. Recording signs of all six positive-root forms gives 24 distinct
root-sign signatures: one orbit point in every Weyl chamber.

At the base ray, `W=15/343`. Exactly 12 chambers have `W>0` and 12 have
`W<0`. The even A4 subgroup preserves one half and the odd coset exchanges
the halves.

Thus the 24 Hesse minima form the chamber torsor of
`W(D3) ~= W(A3) ~= S4`.

## Wall network = the S4 permutohedron

Join two chambers when they differ by one simple reflection. Exact enumeration gives:

- 24 vertices;
- 36 edges;
- degree 3 at every vertex;
- a 12+12 bipartition by `sign(W)`.

The three rank-2 parabolic subgroups have orders `6,6,4`. Their cosets give
eight hexagonal faces and six square faces, so

`V-E+F=24-36+14=2`.

This is the simple-reflection Cayley graph of S4: the 1-skeleton of the
order-4 permutohedron, combinatorially the truncated octahedron.

Every elementary wall edge reverses W. By Passes11607 and11609 it therefore
exchanges both the selected internal Weyl sign and the selected E8 shell sign.

## W^2 is a cubic discriminant

Let

`p2=x^2+y^2+z^2`,
`p3=xyz`,
`p4=x^4+y^4+z^4`.

Then `x^2,y^2,z^2` are roots of

`t^3-p2 t^2+((p2^2-p4)/2)t-p3^2`.

Its discriminant is exactly

`W^2 = (1/4)(p2^2-p4)^2(2p4-p2^2) + p2(5p2^2-9p4)p3^2 - 27p3^4`.

So the CP wall `W=0` is exactly the repeated-root/discriminant locus of the
three squared Bloch coordinates.

`Weyl-chamber orientation -> sign(W) -> E8 shell sign -> Spin(10) Weyl sign`.
