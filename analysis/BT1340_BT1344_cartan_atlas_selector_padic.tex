\begin{theorem}[Exact modular and selector closure]
Let $\mathcal H_{\mathbb Z}$ be the primitive integral form of the literal
$26$-dimensional $W(E_6)/S_5$ Hecke algebra.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item its bad-characteristic Cartan matrices are
\[
C_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&22\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C_3=\begin{pmatrix}5&1&3&0\\1&3&2&0\\3&2&5&0\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
C_5=I_6\oplus\begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\1&1&0\\1&0&2\end{pmatrix};
\]
\item the literal directed-edge carrier affords a faithful rational
$20$-dimensional Atlas-standard-generator model with generator orders
$(2,9,10)$;
\item the minimal combined literal cycle/copy selector in the length-three
through length-eight frontier has orbit size $360$ and stabilizer order $864$;
\item complete primitive idempotent systems lift to $2^6$, $3^6$, and $5^6$,
while the Smith and Loewy filtrations remain distinct invariants.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The decomposition matrices are the unique nonnegative row solutions of the
ordinary-to-modular trace congruences with the required simple dimensions.
Direct Newton lifting of quotient primitive idempotents inside
$\mathcal H_{\mathbb F_p}$ gives projective corners whose dimensions equal
$D_p^{\mathsf T}D_p$, independently proving the displayed Cartan matrices.
The rank-$20$ central character projector in the $480$-edge carrier yields
exact rational matrices $C,D$ satisfying $C^2=D^9=(CD)^{10}=I$ and the frozen
$25$-class trace row.  Literal cycle enumeration gives a smallest cycle orbit
of size $120$ at length four; a primitive multiplicity idempotent has internal
$S_3$ orbit size three, so the combined minimum is $3\cdot120=360$.
Finally, Newton--Hensel iteration gives complete orthogonal idempotent systems
at precision $p^6$ for $p=2,3,5$, while direct dimension comparison separates
the Smith saturation and radical-power filtrations.  All calculations are
recomputed by the accompanying exact certificate and regression tests.
\end{proof}
