\label{sec:bt1340-bt1344-cartan-atlas-selector-padic}

\begin{theorem}[Exact modular and selector closure]
Let $\mathcal H_{\mathbb Z}$ be the primitive integral form of the literal
$26$-dimensional $W(E_6)/S_5$ Hecke algebra.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item its bad-characteristic Cartan matrices are
\[
C_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&22\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C_3=\begin{pmatrix}5&1&3&0\\1&3&2&0\\3&2&5&0\\0&0&0&1\end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
C_5=I_6\oplus\begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\1&1&0\\1&0&2\end{pmatrix};
\]
\item the frozen rational $20$-dimensional standard-generator model is faithful,
has generator orders $(2,9,10)$, and matches a unique CTblLib character row;
\item the global minimum combined literal simple-cycle/copy selector has orbit
size $360$ and stabilizer order $864$;
\item complete primitive idempotent systems lift to $2^6$, $3^6$, and $5^6$,
while the Smith and Loewy filtrations remain distinct invariants.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The displayed dimension-compatible matrices factor the Cartan matrices as
$D_p^{\mathsf T}D_p$; their trace-congruence derivation and uniqueness are not
claimed here. Direct Newton lifting of quotient primitive idempotents inside
$\mathcal H_{\mathbb F_p}$ gives projective corners whose dimensions equal
$D_p^{\mathsf T}D_p$, independently proving the displayed Cartan matrices.
The exact frozen rational matrices $C,D$ satisfy
$C^2=D^9=(CD)^{10}=I$ and the frozen $25$-class trace row. GAP/AtlasRep
independently matches that row to one faithful degree-$20$ character. Literal
cycle enumeration gives the size-$120$
length-four orbit. The GAP path-orbit witness gives pointwise-stabilizer maxima
$6$ and $3$ on five- and six-vertex paths. Since a cyclic-order stabilizer
maps into $D_{2n}$, all simple cycles of lengths $5$ through $40$ have orbit
strictly larger than $120$. A primitive multiplicity idempotent has internal
$S_3$ orbit size three, so the global combined minimum is $3\cdot120=360$.
Finally, Newton--Hensel iteration gives complete orthogonal idempotent systems
at precision $p^6$ for $p=2,3,5$, while direct dimension comparison separates
the Smith saturation and radical-power filtrations.  All calculations are
recomputed or explicitly cross-checked by the named exact witnesses and
regression tests, subject to the boundary below.
\end{proof}
