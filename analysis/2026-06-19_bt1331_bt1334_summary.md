## 1. BT1331 — Q7 Conditional Routing Graph

Under the W63 hypothesis, the Q7 routing graph $\mathcal{G}_7$ is a 14-regular graph on 2187 nodes. The critical structural result is the **fibre-code alignment lemma**: the Q7 heptad fibre of 63 nodes corresponds exactly to the 63 physical qubits of W63, with the 7-sector decomposition matching the 7 heptad branches. The phase correction group at Q7 is $\mathbb{Z}/7\mathbb{Z}$, requiring 3 classical control bits.

## 2. BT1332 — W63 Cyclotomic Coset Search

The W63 existence question reduces to an **explicit combinatorial search** over at most 1024 candidate defining-set pairs $(T_X, T_Z)$ for length-63 BCH codes, followed by a sector-permutation test under the cyclic automorphism group (order $\geq 378$). The search is computationally tractable on a laptop. The falsification criterion is precise: if no candidate achieves $d \geq 11$ with the orthogonality condition, the BCH route fails.
