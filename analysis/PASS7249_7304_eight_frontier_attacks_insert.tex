\subsection{The double-six doily system as a spectral design and ternary shadow}

\[
N^TN=12I+3J+3A_{36},\qquad NN^T=9I+3J+3A_{45},
\]
with $A_{36}=\operatorname{SRG}(36,20,10,12)$ and $A_{45}=\operatorname{SRG}(45,12,3,3)$.  Hence
\[
\operatorname{spec}(N^TN)=180^1\oplus18^{20}\oplus0^{15}.
\]
Writing $X=N-J/3$ yields the exact primitive-module intertwiner
\[
\boxed{2A_{45}X=3XA_{36}}.
\]

A single double-six is transitive under the $36$-point $PSp(4,3)$ orbit, and the binary span of its orbit columns is the full
\[
\boxed{C_{\rm spread}=[45,21,5]_2}.
\]
Thus one local doily slice plus the ambient group action reconstructs both the spread code and the double-six overlap graph.

The $D_4\oplus D_4$ diagonal glue has nonzero classes $(v,v),(s,s),(c,c)$.  The complementary-pair stabilizer has order
\[
2\cdot192^2\cdot6=442368,
\]
where the factor $6$ is simultaneous $D_4$ triality.  It permutes the three nonzero diagonal glue classes transitively, so a bare orthogonal $D_4$ pair carries an $S_3$ torsor rather than a canonical $v/s/c$ label.

For a fixed double-six,
\[
|\operatorname{Stab}_{PSp}(D)|=720,\qquad |\operatorname{Stab}_{W(E_6)}(D)|=1440,
\]
but both images on the fifteen-tritangent doily slice have order $720$.  The extra kernel $C_2$ swaps the two sixers of the double-six and fixes the fifteen complementary $c_{ij}$ lines; it is central, not the exceptional outer automorphism of $S_6$.  Moreover the $27$ minimum words of $C_{\rm spread}$ have weight five and are exactly the $27$ cubic-line stars.  Thus the code reconstructs the full $27$-line/$45$-tritangent cubic-surface geometry from its minimum words.

\]

\subsection{A q=9 local-section obstruction and blocker-frame fingerprint}

The q=9-native local test is instead intersection with embedded symplectic $W(3,3)$ sections.  Every such intersection is a partial ovoid of $W(3,3)$ and therefore has size at most seven.  The frozen 51-point witness meets the standard $\mathbb F_3$-fixed section in four points; a deterministic scan of 20,000 embedded sections under non-$\mathbb F_3$ symplectic transvections saw maximum intersection six.  This scan is not exhaustive and is not an upper bound for $\alpha(W(3,9))$.

If $B$ is the $769\times51$ blocker-incidence matrix of the frozen witness, then

The third blocker moment is witness-specific: among the $\binom{51}{3}$ witness triads, exactly $20722$ have one common center and $103$ have ten common centers.  Those $103$ exceptional triads form a binary rank-$51$ three-uniform hypergraph.
