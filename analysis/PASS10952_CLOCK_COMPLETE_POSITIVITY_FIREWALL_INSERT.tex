\subsection{Pass 10952: the order-eight clock has a complete-positivity grading}

The Pass-10951 determinant-odd clock element has an explicit qutrit
extended-Clifford lift $A=U_SK$.  On density operators one tick is
\[
 \Theta(\rho)=U_S\rho^T U_S^\dagger .
\]
Its Choi operator has inertia $(6,3,0)$, so the map is positive and
trace preserving but not completely positive.  All eight clock powers obey
the exact parity rule
\[
 n\ \mathrm{even}:\ (1,0,8)\ \mathrm{Choi\ inertia},\qquad
 n\ \mathrm{odd}:\ (6,3,0).
\]
Hence the bare-qutrit operational clock is the even subgroup: two algebraic
ticks give one unitary Clifford step, and those steps form a projective $C_4$.

The four-tick qutrit unitary has phase-space action $-I$ but is non-scalar,
with $|\operatorname{tr}|/3=1/3$; the Spin central sign of Pass 10951 and
the qutrit parity Clifford are therefore different representations of the
same abstract group element.  The full order-eight clock becomes unitary on
$\mathcal H_3\oplus\overline{\mathcal H}_3$ via
$D=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&U_S\\U_S^*&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
with $D^2=\operatorname{diag}(V,V^*)$.  This is an operational channel
firewall, not a derivation of physical time or a thermodynamic arrow.
