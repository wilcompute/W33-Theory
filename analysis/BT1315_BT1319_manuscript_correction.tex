\subsection{Exact correction: the 432-carrier Hecke algebra and its Hashimoto bridge}
The literal coset carrier is
\(\Omega_{432}=W(E_6)/S_5=PSp(4,3)/A_5\). Its stabilizer rank is
\(26\), not \(9\). The rejected fixed-point vector
\((432,4,0,1,1)\) has Burnside average \(43/5\) and therefore cannot be
a permutation character. The exact permutation-module decomposition is
\[
 \mathbb C[\Omega_{432}]
 \cong
 \mathbf 1\oplus2\mathbf 6\oplus\mathbf{15}\oplus\mathbf{15}_a
 \oplus3\mathbf{20}\oplus2\mathbf{30}\oplus\mathbf{60}_a
 \oplus2\mathbf{64}\oplus\mathbf{81}_{-}.
\]
Consequently the complex Hecke algebra has Wedderburn block multiplicities
\(1,2,1,1,3,2,1,2,1\), dimension \(26\), and center dimension \(9\).
The 480-dimensional directed-edge module is distinct:
\[
 \mathbb C[E_{\rm dir}]
 \cong
 \mathbf1\oplus2\mathbf{15}_{-}\oplus\mathbf{15}_a\oplus\mathbf{20}
 \oplus3\mathbf{24}\oplus\mathbf{30}_{-}\oplus\mathbf{60}_a
 \oplus2\mathbf{81}_{+}\oplus\mathbf{90}.
\]
The two carriers must not be conflated, but their exact equivariant transport
space is nonzero:
\[
 \dim\operatorname{Hom}_{W(E_6)}
 \bigl(\mathbb C[E_{\rm dir}],\mathbb C[\Omega_{432}]\bigr)=6.
\]
The six basis operators are the indicator matrices of the six diagonal
\(W(E_6)\)-orbits in \(\Omega_{432}\times E_{\rm dir}\), with orbit sizes
\(51840^3\) and \(17280^3\). These statements are finite-group theorems;
they carry no independent continuum or hardware interpretation.
