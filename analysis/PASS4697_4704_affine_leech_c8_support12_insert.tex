\paragraph{Pass 4697: the three dimension-$39$ carriers.}
For the cross-shell differential
\[
D_0=\begin{pmatrix}0&R\\R^{\mathsf T}&0\end{pmatrix}
\]
the homology splits canonically as
\[
H_{\rm cross}\cong H_{27}\oplus H_{36},\qquad 15+24=39.
\]
The rank-$39$ apartment code, however, is the permutation quotient
\[
C_{\rm ap}\cong \mathbb F_2^{40}/\langle\mathbf1\rangle.
\]
Exact $\PSp(4,3)$ equivariance gives two-dimensional Hom spaces in both directions.  The three nonzero maps $H_{\rm cross}\to C_{\rm ap}$ all have rank $14$, while the three nonzero maps in the reverse direction all have rank $1$.  Hence the periodic direct sum and cross-shell homology are the same module, but the apartment code is not.

\paragraph{Pass 4698: the corrected Golay sextet stabilizer is internally affine.}
Inside the order-$138240$ sextet stabilizer $H$, the kernel of the six-tetrad action has order $192$ and element-order census
\[
1^1\,2^{63}\,3^{128}.
\]
Identity plus the $63$ involutions form a normal elementary abelian subgroup
\[
N\cong C_2^6,
\]
which acts regularly on the $64$ sextet transversals.  If $K$ is the stabilizer of the chosen zero transversal, then
\[
|K|=2160,\qquad N\cap K=1,\qquad H=N\rtimes K.
\]
The action $K\to S_6$ on the tetrads has kernel $C_3$, and the two nonzero $K$-orbits on $N$ have sizes $18$ and $45$.  This internally recovers the affine structure conventionally denoted $2^6{:}3.S_6$.

\paragraph{Pass 4699: an explicit $A_1^{24}$-to-Leech two-neighbor.}
Let
\[
N_{A_1^{24}}=\frac1{\sqrt2}\{x\in\mathbb Z^{24}:x\bmod2\in G_{24}\}
\]
and choose one corrected-sextet coordinate $i$.  For
\[
v_i=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,\ldots,1,\underset{i}{3},1,\ldots,1),\qquad v_i^2=16,
\]
define
\[
M_i=\{x\in N_{A_1^{24}}:(x,v_i)\equiv0\pmod2\},\qquad
L_i=M_i+\mathbb Z\,v_i/2.
\]
A frozen $24$-vector basis has integer numerator determinant $2^{36}$ at scale $1/(2\sqrt2)$, hence Gram determinant $1$; its Gram matrix is integral and even.  All $48$ coordinate roots of $N_{A_1^{24}}$ have odd pairing with $v_i$ and disappear.  Every vector in the new coset has odd numerator in all $24$ coordinates and therefore norm at least $3$, which evenness strengthens to at least $4$; a norm-$4$ basis vector is exhibited.  Thus $L_i$ is the rootless even unimodular $24$-lattice, i.e. the Leech lattice.  The $24$ distinguished-coordinate neighbors form one orbit under the corrected sextet stabilizer.

\paragraph{Pass 4700: the primitive-$C_8$ masses close only after adding embedding invariants.}
Put $u=s-1$.  For an apartment let $\rho$ count intersecting cross-pairs between its two external-transversal families and let $\sigma$ count intersecting transversal/single-hit outside-line pairs.  For an induced $K_{1,3}$ let $\tau$ count outside common transversals to the three leaves.  The six Pass~4635 raw masses are
\[
A_{1111|22}=16\big((4u^2+2)\rho+u^2\sigma+(4t-2)u^4+2(t-1)\big),
\]
\[
A_{1115}=64u(u^3-u^2+2u-1),\quad
A_{1133}=96u^2(u-1)^2,
\]
\[
A_{1111|4}=64(u+1)u(u-1)(t-1),
\]
\[
S_{1111|22}=48(u+1)u^3,\qquad
S_{1113|2}=48(u+1)u\tau.
\]
The primitive coefficient is one eighth of the appropriate raw sum.  W33 and its dual have the same order $(3,3)$ but
\[
(\rho,\sigma,\tau)=(0,16,1)\quad\hbox{versus}\quad(4,0,3),
\]
and exact apartment/star coefficients
\[
(712,180)\quad\hbox{versus}\quad(728,252).
\]
Therefore no formula in $(s,t)$ alone can encode primitive-$C_8$ apartment tomography for both embeddings.

\paragraph{Pass 4701: the exact apartment-code enumerator reaches support twelve.}
Let $A_w$ count all support-$12$ coefficient sets producing codeword weight $w$, and $B_w$ those containing one fixed coefficient position.  Coefficient transitivity gives the exact double count
\[
12A_w=40B_w,
\qquad A_w=\frac{10}{3}B_w.
\]
Thus only
\[
\binom{39}{11}=1676056044
\]
fixed-position subsets need be exhausted.  The native XOR/popcount census yields, after exact rescaling,
\[
\binom{40}{12}=5586853480
\]
subsets in $151$ weight classes.  The extremes are
\[
608\;(1620),\qquad 990\;(4320).
\]
Complete labelled spectra are now exact through support $12$; supports $13$--$20$ remain open.

\paragraph{Pass 4702: the Golay affine six-space is not the cubic orthogonal six-space.}
The faithful six-dimensional $K$-module carried by the translation subgroup $N$ admits neither a nonzero invariant quadratic form nor a nonzero invariant bilinear form: both full invariance systems have nullity zero.  Hence it cannot be identified with the cubic $O^-_6(2)$ module merely because both are six-dimensional.

\paragraph{Pass 4703: all support-$12$ minima are apartment corner-star thickenings.}
For an apartment $A$, let $\mathcal T(A)$ consist of all W33 lines through the four corner points of $A$.  Since there are two additional lines at each corner,
\[
|\mathcal T(A)|=4+4\cdot2=12.
\]
All $1620$ thickenings are distinct and have apartment-code weight $608$.  Pass~4701 proves there are exactly $1620$ support-$12$ minima, so these are all of them.  Each thickening contains eleven apartments; exactly one has overlap profile $\{0{:}2,2{:}8\}$ against the other ten, providing an intrinsic inverse $\mathcal T(A)\mapsto A$.

\paragraph{Pass 4704: the thickening shell generates the even apartment subcode and recovers the edge carrier.}
The $1620$ thickening coefficient masks have weight $12$ and rank $39$, hence span the full even-weight hyperplane $E_{40}\subset\mathbb F_2^{40}$.  Because the all-ones kernel also lies in $E_{40}$, their codeword span is
\[
E_{40}/\langle\mathbf1\rangle,
\]
a $38$-dimensional subcode.  The exhaustive low-weight apartment certificate gives
\[
\boxed{[1620,38,270]},
\]
with exactly $240$ minimum words, the adjacent generator-pair sums corresponding to the $240$ W33 edges.  Thus
\[
1620\text{ apartment thickenings}\longrightarrow [1620,38,270]\longrightarrow240\text{ edges}.
\]
