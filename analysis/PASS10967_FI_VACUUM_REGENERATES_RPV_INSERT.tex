\emph{(A) The most general matter parity.} Any element of the gauge $U(1)$ torus times the
space-group non-$R$ discrete group (point-group $\mathbb{Z}_6$, $\mathbb{Z}_3$, and in
$\mathbb{Z}_6$-II the $\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$ fixed-point rules) that is $-1$ on
$q,u^c,d^c,e^c$ and $+1$ on the condensing singlets, other fields unrestricted, is decided exactly
by a Smith normal form.  Such an element exists in exactly the $88$ $B-L$ models; $87$ close by one
Farkas vector, $\mathbb{Z}_6$II$_{23}$ by obstructing all $304$ FI-cancelling rays: $0/215$.
\emph{(B)} $290$ of the $302$ forced singlets obey, with single copies,
$Q(n)=-Q(u^cd^cd^c)=-Q(q\,l\,d^c)=-Q(l\,l\,e^c)$ --- the $SO(10)$ invariant $\mathbf{16}^4$ at
$\nu^c$ --- so they are odd under every matter parity.
\emph{(C)} With all orbifolder selection rules, in the $23$ $D$-flat $\mathbb{Z}_6$-I models no
cubic $R$-parity-violating coupling is allowed, yet all $9$ forced singlets of every model appear
in allowed quartics $n\,u^cd^cd^c$, $n\,q\,l\,d^c$, $n\,l\,l\,e^c$; $\mathbb{Z}_6$II$_{23}$ has
$q\,l\,d^c$, $l\,l\,e^c$ at order $3$ and $u^cd^cd^c$ at order $5$.  The FI term therefore
regenerates all three operators with strength $\langle n\rangle/M_s$, which closes b81ef8c's
condition.
