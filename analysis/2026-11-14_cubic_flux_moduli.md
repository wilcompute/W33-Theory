# Fixed-flux complex moduli and nodal monodromy of the D9 cubic vacuum

The [cubic-surface flux solution](2026-11-14_cubic_surface_flux.md) has eight real, classically massless complex-structure scalars at every smooth cubic, in addition to its six massive primitive Kähler scalars. These eight directions integrate to local families of exact classical vacua at fixed quantized flux. The smooth cubic moduli space is known independently as a complex four-dimensional orbifold. [Allcock, Carlson and Toledo](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0007048)

## Why the flux leaves the complex moduli flat

In a local smooth family of cubic surfaces, identify the differentiable fibers and their cohomology. Smooth cubic surfaces admit normalized Kähler–Einstein metrics, and the local Kähler–Einstein equation gives a smooth family because the automorphism group has zero Lie algebra. In the Monge–Ampère equation this means that the potential linearization has no holomorphic-vector-field kernel after the scale is fixed. The normalized Kähler forms obey \([\omega]=2\pi c_1/\kappa\). At fixed flux integer \(q\), both \(\kappa=14e^2/(5q^2)\) and \(f=q\kappa/e\) are independent of complex structure. The form \(F=f\omega\) therefore stays in the same integral class. Moser's argument can identify the forms themselves locally by diffeomorphisms. All these backgrounds have \(L^2\kappa=10\) and \(\mathrm{Vol}(M)=6\pi^2/\kappa^2\), since \(c_1^2=3\). The tree-level cosmological constant and Newton coefficient of the reduced theory are constant on this family. [Existence on smooth cubics](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.08960) · [Fano Kähler–Einstein moduli and its Weil–Petersson metric](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_2018__51_3_739_0/)

The tangent count can also be made directly. The normal sequence of \(S\subset\mathbb P^3\) has \(H^0(\mathcal O_S(3))\) of dimension 19. The restricted Euler sequence gives \(h^0(T\mathbb P^3|_S)=15\) and \(h^1(T\mathbb P^3|_S)=0\), using vanishing for the positive line bundles and \(H^{0,2}(S)=0\). The kernel of the induced coordinate-change map is \(H^0(TS)=0\). Hence \(h^1(TS)=4\). At the Fermat cubic, the linear quotient by coordinate changes and equation rescaling is represented by the four square-free cubic monomials \(z_i z_j z_k\) with distinct indices.

The harmonic Kodaira–Spencer representatives give transverse traceless anti-Hermitian metric variations \(h\) with \(\Delta_L h=2\kappa h\). In a local orthonormal frame their endomorphisms anticommute with the complex structure. At fixed covariant \(F\) this yields \(\delta(*F)=0\), \(\delta(F^2)=0\), and \(\delta(F_{iP}F_j{}^P)=+f^2h_{ij}\). The internal linearized Einstein equation for \(\delta g_{ij}=\phi(x)h_{ij}(y)\) then gives \(\tfrac12(-\Box_5+2\kappa)\phi h_{ij}=\kappa\phi h_{ij}\), or \(\Box_5\phi=0\). The nonzero tangent tensors have positive Einstein kinetic norm. The Maxwell perturbation and mixed metric components can vanish at this order. This derives physical massless scalars from the tangent family and does not assume a nonlinear consistent truncation to them. [Fano Kuranishi–divergence gauge, Theorem 2.2](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/620/12138-zhangyy1.pdf)

## Flux-preserving transport around a node

For a smoothing of one ordinary node, the vanishing cycle has a root class \(\delta\) with \(\delta^2=-2\), in the primitive lattice \(c_1^\perp\). The Picard–Lefschetz transport of a marked class \(a\) is \(a\mapsto a+(a\cdot\delta)\delta\). Its eigenvalues on the rank-six primitive cohomology are \(-1\) once and \(+1\) five times. It preserves \(q c_1\), and the vanishing cycle has **zero aligned Maxwell period**. Because the massive primitive sector is built from harmonic representatives of these classes, it inherits this monodromy as an identification of its integral labels and their quadratic pairing. A dynamical Berry phase or charged state requires further information. [Nodal vanishing cycles and markings](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0007048)

The exact marked intersection calculation checks this action for all 72 roots on the 27 line classes. Each reflection fixes 15 line classes and exchanges six pairs of disjoint lines. Every exchanged line retains its own period \(q\). These statements are about the smooth family and its integral class lattice. The node itself is singular, and the computation does not supply a light charged object there.
