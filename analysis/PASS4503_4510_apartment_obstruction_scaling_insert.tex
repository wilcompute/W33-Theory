\subsection{Maximal-subgroup obstruction and the corrected flag gauge (Passes 4503--4504)}
\label{sec:pass4503-4510-apartment-obstruction-scaling}

Adversarial re-execution of the Pass~4493 restricted section systems corrected a
false positive without changing the full-group nonsplitting theorem.  For the
exact extension
\[
0\to K/J\;(29)\to E=M/J\;(39)\to H_{10}\;(10)\to0,
\]
representatives of all five maximal subgroup types of $PSp(4,3)$ remain
nonsplit.  Their orders and coefficient/augmented ranks are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
|H|&[PSp(4,3):H]&\operatorname{rank}A/\operatorname{rank}[A\mid b]\\ \hline
960&27&388/389\\
720&36&386/387\\
648&40&386/387\\
648&40&387/388\\
576&45&386/387.
\end{array}
\]
The two order-$648$ rows are the line and point stabilizers respectively.  Thus
the historical $370=370$ point/line claim is withdrawn.  The canonical incident
point--line flag stabilizer has order $162$ and instead gives
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}A=\operatorname{rank}[A\mid b]=384},
\]
with a six-dimensional affine family, hence exactly $64$ equivariant sections.
Exhaustive optimization of those $64$ sections in the canonical quotient basis
gives total minimal ambient Hamming weight $42$, maximum column weight $9$, and
minimum union support $13$ lines.

\subsection{The full radical carries two obstruction bits (Pass 4505)}

For the complete apartment radical $R_{29}=K/J$, an exact full-Cayley-graph
cocycle computation gives
\[
\dim Z^1(PSp(4,3),R_{29})=31,\qquad
\dim B^1(PSp(4,3),R_{29})=29,
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{H^1(PSp(4,3),R_{29})\cong\mathbb F_2^2.}
\]
All three nonzero classes generate the same $23$-dimensional support module
\[
W/J=(K\cap\operatorname{row}N)/J,

which Pass~4492 already resolved as the route-hull/sentinel extension with
profile $8\mid(1\mid14)$.  In particular, the remaining six-dimensional radical
factor is absent from the module closure of every nonzero class.  This is
$H^1(G,R_{29})$ and must not be confused with a computation of the entire
$\operatorname{Ext}^1_G(H_{10},R_{29})$ group.

\subsection{The apartment bridge scales from $10$ to $70$ (Pass 4506)}

\[
\operatorname{rank}H=279,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}N=91,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(N^TN)=70,
\]
with the exact entrywise identity
\[
HH^T=N^TN.
\]
Consequently the apartment code has a $209$-dimensional radical and a

\operatorname{im}N/\ker N^T,

\subsection{Three outside-box consequences (Passes 4508--4510)}

First, Pass~4496 and Pass~4505 produce two different two-dimensional cohomology
spaces.  For the protected irreducible eight-core, the $PGSp$ outer involution
swaps an $H^1$ basis, leaving one nonzero class fixed.  For the full radical
$R_{29}$, the outer involution acts trivially on $H^1$, so all three nonzero
classes are outer-fixed.  Equal dimension therefore does not identify the two
outer modules.

Second, restriction of the two radical obstruction bits explains the corrected
symmetry threshold.  The order-$960$, $720$, and $576$ maximals kill no nonzero
class.  A point stabilizer kills one one-dimensional obstruction subspace, while
a line stabilizer kills a distinct one.  Their incident intersection, the
order-$162$ flag stabilizer, kills all of $H^1(G,R_{29})$.  Thus a point or line
removes one obstruction charge; a flag removes both.

Third, the $13$-line minimum support of the optimized flag section is exactly the
closed neighborhood of the fixed flag line in the W33 line-intersection graph.
Its induced graph is
\[
\boxed{K_1\vee4K_3},
\]
one center line together with the four pencils of three other lines through its
four points.  Hence the valid symmetry-broken representative register can be
chosen inside a single radius-one W33 line cell.  This is finite-graph locality,
not automatically spatial, optical or energetic locality in hardware.
