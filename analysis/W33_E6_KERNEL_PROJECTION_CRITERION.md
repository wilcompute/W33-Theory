# Exact cubic-kernel projection criterion

In the independent three-3x3-matrix E6 cubic normal form, take the 81-dimensional bracket [e_(i,a),e_(j,b)] = d_ijk epsilon_abc ebar_(k,c) and background v_n=(n+1)^2 in n=3i+a order. The 45 signed triads (18 determinant terms plus 27 cross terms) generate 1,620 ordered bracket records. Exact rational nullspace computation gives dim ker D_v=3 and rank D_v=78.

Let T be any coordinate subspace of dimension 54 in the same identified source/output basis, with orthogonal coordinate projection P_T. Skew-symmetry gives (im D_v)^perp=ker D_v. Therefore rank(P_T im D_v)=54-dim(T intersect ker D_v). Equivalently, the projection is surjective iff the restriction of the 3D kernel to the 27-coordinate complement has rank three.

For this explicit normal-form background, the kernel restrictions to each of the three 27-coordinate E6 blocks and each of the three 27-coordinate external-phase slices all have rank three. Thus any target that is the complement of one entire such block/slice has projection rank 54.

Firewall: No map from these normal-form slices to the physical Fourier-retyped 54 directions has been certified. This result is a coordinate-slice theorem, not proof of actual compiler alignment, a physical vacuum, or a Hamiltonian.
