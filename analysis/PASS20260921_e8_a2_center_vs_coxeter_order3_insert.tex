\subsection*{The external $A_2$ contains two inequivalent order-three $E_8$ actions}

The physical FI grading and the Pass~1147 colour cycle both arise from the
same external type-$A_2$ factor, but they are not the same inner automorphism.

For
\[

\]

trivially on the two adjoint blocks and by $\omega,\omega^2$ on the two
matter blocks, so its adjoint eigendimensions are
\[
\boxed{86+81+81},
\]
and its fixed algebra is $E_6+A_2$.

Pass~1147 instead uses the Weyl Coxeter three-cycle of the same $A_2$.  In
the defining triplet its eigenvalues are $1,\omega,\omega^2$, while on the
$A_2$ adjoint they occur with multiplicities $2,3,3$.  Hence its full
$E_8$ adjoint spectrum is
\[
\boxed{134+57+57},
\]
with fixed reductive algebra $E_7+\mathfrak u(1)$.

An exhaustive affine-Kac sweep in the repository's frozen $E_8$ gauge finds
exactly four normalized inner order-three spectra,
\[
80+84+84,\qquad
86+81+81,\qquad
92+78+78,\qquad
134+57+57.
\]
The two actions therefore select distinct conjugacy classes.  Their unique
Kac coordinates in the repository ordering are respectively
\[
(0,0,0,0,0,1,0,0,0),
\qquad
(1,0,0,0,0,0,1,0,0).
\]
