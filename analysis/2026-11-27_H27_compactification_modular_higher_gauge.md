## Extra 1: conditional selection of a compactification

Let H27 be exponent-3 Heisenberg group, Schr representation W with X,Z, extend V=W+1. Use dual V* and invariant cubic on each. Degree-3 invariants are sum of three cubes, xyz, singlet cube (center forces triplet degree 0 or 3, Z phase and X cyclic permutation select monomials). This is Hesse pencil plus singlet. Select F(x)=sum_{i=1}^4 x_i^3, F(y) same, H=sum d_i x_i y_i with d=(1,1,1,lambda), lambda>0. All invariant under H27 acting as V and contragredient. In P3 x P3 the three multidegrees (3,0),(0,3),(1,1) yield c1=0 by adjunction. Smoothness: each cubic surface smooth, and a singular point of H restricted to product requires d_i y_i=mu x_i^2 and d_i x_i=nu y_i^2. Both multipliers nonzero; supports equal and x_i^3=d_i^3/(nu mu^2) on support; F=0 would require sum d_i^3=0, impossible for positive d. Thus a smooth ample anticanonical divisor of product of two smooth cubic surfaces. Weak Lefschetz implies simply connected and b2=14. Prior Chern computation Euler -18, h21=23.

Every noncentral order-3 element has distinct triplet eigenvalues 1,omega,omega^2. For a monomial Schr matrix its eigenvectors are supported on one coordinate (diagonal case) or all three with cube-root phases (shift case). Choose representatives with F_triplet=s=1 or 3, dual conjugate eigenvectors paired with norm squared s. Only eigenvalue-one projective eigenspace can intersect the cubic surface: there x=(u v,t), F=s u^3+t^3=0, u nonzero; y similarly. At a fixed pair H=0 would imply lambda^3=-s, impossible. Thus all 24 noncentral elements act freely. Center fixes e.g. x=(1,-1,0,0), y=(1,1,c,0), c^3=-2. Exhaustive subgroup lattice 1 trivial,13 of order3,4 of order9,1 of order27. All larger nontrivial subgroups contain center. Exactly 12 maximal free subgroups, four inner-conjugacy orbits each size3. Standard holomorphic residue 3-form invariant since representation determinants and equations invariant. Quotient is smooth CY, pi1=C3, Euler -6. H2 of each cubic surface has trace1 for each nontrivial cyclic generator by Lefschetz fixed point theorem (three isolated fixed points, H0,H4 each trace1); invariant rank (7+2)/3=3. On product sum gives h11=6, h21=9. Standard embedding index -3, c2 matching immediate. Conditional principle: maximal quotient within this H27 action without orbifold singularities forces degree3, not a direct fit to index. No dynamics derived selecting V=W+1, Fermat coefficients, positive pairing, maximality or this group over larger automorphism groups. All 12 free subgroups have the same index, none fixed under H27 conjugation, so no inner-equivariant unique selector; flat local-system count for F3^4 is 81 for every quotient vs 1 on cover, cannot distinguish them and is not a vacuum probability. Continuous moduli remain. Classical Tian-Yau cover/free quotient already known (Bini/Favale arXiv:1104.0247); do not claim discovery of CY or generation count.

## 1. Defect fusion qudits

For successive full-H twisted boundaries use Schr representation and dual; V tensor V splits into 3 copies of V* with relative coordinate r=y-x and mean s=(x+y)/2. Diagonal X changes s, not r; diagonal Z phase depends only on s. Relative X1 X2^-1 and Z1^-1 Z2 act on r as Weyl pair, commuting with diagonal action. For triple V tensor V tensor V and fixed total Z-charge q and common-X character p, left fusion basis labeled r=y-x and right s=z-y. Constraint x+y+z=q gives s=q+r, and phase from changing reference x to y is omega^(p r) in chosen gauge. All nine character blocks verified; monomial Clifford recoupling. For V,V*,V alternating, left/right Bell bases have overlap omega^(d a-b c)/3 with labels (a,b),(c,d); exact 9x9 unitary, Clifford Fourier pairing. No physical implementation of relative Weyls as braids proved, no global F/R data or non-Clifford recoupling. ## 2. Higher gauge deformation

Choose 2-type with pi1=pi2=F3, trivial action, Postnikov class k=A cup beta(A), Bockstein from integer lifts of flat A. On oriented ordered simplices define H=delta B-k(A). For vertex shift f, A'=A+delta f mod3; t=(lift A'-lift A-delta lift f)/3. beta(A')=beta(A)+delta t. Descent C=f cup beta(A)-A cup t+f cup delta t, so k(A')-k(A)=delta C. Shift B'=B+C (also B->B+delta Lambda). H invariant exactly. Test all 81 flat edge potentials modulo constant and all 243 vertex transformations on a 4-simplex, seeded B, 98415 tetra curvature checks, 65993 nonzero. Thus action sum_t(1-cos(2pi H_t/3)) supplies a gauge-invariant non-flat face-field deformation, with A still flat; no equivalence asserted to prior type-IV state sum. Obstruction on lens L(3,1)xS1: A=x*l+y*t, beta l nonzero, two H3 components (x^2,xy). Only 3/9 flat A classes lift to H=0. Homotopy cardinality using |H2(B)||H0(B)|/|H1(B)| and divide by |H0(A)| gives 3; T4 beta=0 and cardinality 729. For continuum ordinary 2-form kinetic field in 3+1D massless polarization count C(2,2)=1, not two spin-2 graviton modes. Finite Z3 theory need not have a gapless scalar either. Gauge invariance and local curvature obtained, gravitational metric and propagating spin-two not obtained.

## Extra 2: explicit Heisenberg double modular dictionary

Use symmetric-coordinate H27 multiplication (u,z)(v,w)=(u+v,z+w+det(u,v)/2). For a type-III label with flux a=(u,z), u nonzero, map to noncentral conjugacy class rep (u,0) and 1D centralizer character with value omega^t on rep and omega^z on center, t twist exponent. If u=0,z nonzero map to central flux (0,ell3) with Schr irrep central character z. If a=0 map electric label ell to central flux ell3 and ordinary H27 character ell1,ell2. ## 4. Golay nonuniform transversal gates

Construct extended [12,6,6] ternary Golay, puncture first coordinate. Assign independent ninth-root exponents f_i(1), f_i(2), f_i(0)=0 for eleven sites. For each of 729 words require sum phases equal to fixed representative in logical coset. Linear 729x22 constraints mod9. Unit-pivot elimination rank15; residual after division by3 rank7. ## 5. Local dynamical gauge QCA and continuum control

Electric-basis link e in F3, occupations nL,nR in {0,1}; charges nL-e,nR+e. Hopping swap changes e by nR-nL; exact Gauss charge conservation. On cube all 6 face cycles divergence zero. Local hopping unitary rotates each allowed pair, electric phase diagonal, plaquette rotation from loop-shift unitary. These gates embed in 3D cubic lattice with finite edge/face coloring, yielding exact finite-depth gauge-invariant update (spinful hopping can preserve charge similarly; fermionic signs and Wilson gamma blocks need separate implementation). Simulate 4-site open plaquette with one mobile particle and static compensating charge, all 12 physical states enumerated, 10 steps of electric/hopping/magnetic update preserve norm and Gauss constraints. Matter distribution changes and expected number of nonzero electric links evolves. Not a full interacting 3D fermion simulation. Independent 3D free Wilson Hamiltonian control E^2=sum sin^2(k_i)/a^2+[m+r sum(1-cos(k_i))/a]^2; doublers at n pi components have masses m+2rn/a. Numerical O(a) continuum at selected momenta. Wilson explicitly breaks axial symmetry, does not evade Nielsen-Ninomiya for exact chiral lattice fermions. Light cone speed fixed by input hopping/time coefficients; common interacting renormalized speed not derived. No physical continuum QED or graviton.
