Let O0 be the selected BT648 24-flag orbit.

Let P_O be the diagonal projector onto O0.

Let K = CC^T = 81 A0 - 27 A1 + 9 A2 - 3 A3 + A4.

Let E4 = K/160.

Trace data:

Tr(P_O K) = 1944.

Tr(P_O E4) = 243/20.

0^6, 96^5, 104^3, 112^6, 120^4.

Rank of the restricted kernel is 18.

0,144,864,2352,3168.

Interpretation:

The selected 24-carrier is an internal coordinate carrier, not a central Bose-Mesner idempotent.

The central spectral projector for the full minus sheet remains a separate construction.
