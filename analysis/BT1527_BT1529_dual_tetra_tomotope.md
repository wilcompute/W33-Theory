# BT1527--BT1529: dual incidence, tetrahedral carrier, and tomotope 192 assembly

## BT1527

The Csaszar/Szilassi dual incidence dimensions match exactly:

- Csaszar: V=7, E=21, F=14;
- Szilassi: V=14, E=21, F=7.

The pointed Csaszar vertex-star has 12 flags and the pointed Szilassi face-star has 12 flags.  Together they give 24 flags, matching the K4/tetrahedron flag count.

## BT1528

The K4/tetrahedron carrier is explicit: 4 vertices, 6 edges, 4 faces, and 24 rank-3 flags.  The 24 flags split into two 12-flag packets matching the pointed Csaszar and Szilassi local stars.  Orientation/sign conventions remain future work.

## BT1529

The tomotope-scale assembly passes both count readings:

- 168 + 24 = 192;
- 8 * 24 = 192.

Seven 24-flag packets are interpreted as the toroidal/Fano phase shell, and the eighth as the K4 tetrahedral ground packet.  This is a flag-scale assembly test, not an abstract tomotope isomorphism theorem.
