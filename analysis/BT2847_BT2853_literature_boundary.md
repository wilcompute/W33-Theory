# Passes 2847--2853 literature boundary

The repository computations are independent exact finite calculations. The following literature only fixes terminology and claim boundaries.

- Adaptive distinguishing sequences are rooted input/output trees for finite-state-machine state identification. They can exist when preset distinguishing sequences do not and can be substantially shorter. The Pass-2852 policy is an exact instance for the 81-state affine frame machine; its four-operation depth and `94/27` mean are repository results.
- Maximum-likelihood decoding on a binary asymmetric channel depends separately on `0->1` and `1->0` transition probabilities. The Pass-2850 decoder is the direct product-channel likelihood rule for independently modelled support taps; the reported improvements are synthetic deterministic-seed experiments, not detector measurements.
- The identity `1[u != 0] = u^2` over `F3`, the 14-function collapse, the 24-sample integer-repetition optimum, the automorphism orders 32 and 6912, and the golden noisy-M36 saddle node are repository-specific results.
