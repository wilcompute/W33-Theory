## 3198 — Concurrent recursive belief virtualization

For `k` distinct depth-`n` leaves in a `40`-ary tree, each live context corresponds to one distinct
non-root prefix. Therefore

\[
N_{\min}(k,n)=\sum_{d=1}^{n}\left\lceil\frac{k}{40^{n-d}}\right\rceil,
\qquad
N_{\max}(k,n)=\sum_{d=1}^{n}\min(k,40^d).
\]

or 3,640 to 18,720 bits. The exact storage law replaces the earlier one-path estimate without
assuming global replication.
