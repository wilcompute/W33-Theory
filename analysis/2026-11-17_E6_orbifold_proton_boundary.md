# An E6 boundary condition for proton decay, and the operators that bypass it

The four-dimensional [triplet calculation](2026-11-16_E6_triplet_exchange_filter.md) cancels the two dangerous chiral-exchange blocks using an adjoint polynomial. The broken gauge current remains. The [parallel symmetry calculation](2026-11-16_TOE_fivefront_exact_modular_Bianchi_spin_optics.md) shows that hypercharge leaves every term of the E6 cubic invariant. The [parallel orbifold study](PASS12613_12615_ORBIFOLD_ROUTE.md) uses a reflection to split SU(5) multiplets. Applying that reflection to the exact E6 cubic eliminates both minimal exchange mechanisms for matter localized at the reflecting boundary. The boundary action itself admits independent decay operators.

## One involution and two exchanges

Use integer weights `w = -6 Y_SM` and `P = exp(i pi w)`. In the SU(5) fundamental its signs are `(+,+,+,-,-)`. The exact cubic derivation equations give even and odd adjoint dimensions 38 and 40. In the compact real form the even Lie algebra is `su(6) + su(2)`. The 27 splits into 15 even and 12 odd components. The neutral Higgs frame at coordinate indices 7 and 8 is even, as is the hypercharge adjoint background. For the interval mechanism and classification see [Hall and Nomura](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0103125) and [Hebecker and March-Russell](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0107039).

Put the matter Yukawa vertices at the reflecting endpoint. Take the two bulk spectator chiral fields on the sourced legs to have intrinsic reflection sign minus, so their endpoint value on an integer-weight component is projected to `(1-P)/2`. An orbifold projection is a condition on a bulk field's endpoint value. Localized matter can still be placed on that endpoint in a representation of the boundary gauge group, for example the restricted full 27 before its heavy pieces are lifted. Both sourced triplet blocks `w = +/-2` are odd under the *combined* reflection and vanish there. The light Higgs on the sourced up leg has `w=-3`, and the sourced conjugate down leg reverses this sign. Both are retained. The previous charged Yukawa tensors can be evaluated on these doublet components, with overall overlap factors to be matched in a full bulk model.

The adjoint reflection sign is `(-1)^k` in grade `k`, where `k = w_row - w_column`. The dangerous grade-5 currents vanish at the endpoint from the minimal gauge vertex. In fact all possible surviving currents made from the specified light matter have a unique baryon charge per even grade. In units of one third these are `B(0)=0`, `B(2)=-1`, `B(4)=1`, `B(6)=0` and their signed reversals, restricted to grades that exist in the adjoint. Baryon-neutral singlets are included in the check. An SM-preserving propagator pairs opposite grades, so even-sector mixing anywhere within a grade cannot produce a baryon-violating minimal current product. This argument allows arbitrary charged masses consistent with hypercharge and reflection.

## Three failure tests

**A freely acting Wilson line is insufficient.** On a circle of circumference `L`, an antiperiodic field with bulk mass `m` has coincident inverse of `-d_y^2+m^2` equal to `tanh(m L/2)/(2m)`. Its massless limit is `L/4`. The twist removes the zero mode but leaves nonzero exchange at the location of a source. On an interval of length `L` with Neumann at 0 and Dirichlet at `L`, the corresponding diagonal Green function a distance `delta` from the Dirichlet endpoint is

`G = cosh(m(L-delta)) sinh(m delta) / (m cosh(m L)) = delta + O(delta^2)`.

The endpoint node is exact. The massless expression is exactly `delta`, so point localization and a small displacement give different answers.

**Higher-derivative gauge vertices reach Dirichlet fields.** The covariant `F_(5 mu)` of an odd gauge generator is even at the endpoint and can couple there to its matter current. In the Neumann–Dirichlet example the wave numbers are `k_n=(n+1/2) pi/L` and the normalized squared endpoint derivative is `2 k_n^2/L`. For `N` modes the resulting zero-momentum kernel is `(2/L) sum k_n^2/(k_n^2+m^2)`. With a sharp mode cutoff it equals `2N/L` at zero mass. Subtracting that term and sending `N` to infinity gives `-m tanh(m L)`, by the same Green function evaluated at the other endpoint. The derivative Wilson coefficient and contact renormalization require ultraviolet matching. The cutoff sum does not by itself predict a proton lifetime. See [Hebecker's supersymmetric boundary operators](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0112230) and [Hebecker and March-Russell on the derivative proton-decay channel](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0204037).

**The boundary gauge group allows a direct chiral operator.** Form the two covariant quadratic sources `J` and `Jbar` from the repo cubic, with the down source including the dual cubic and `bar H_1`. Contract `J (1+P)/2 Jbar` directly on the boundary. The projector commutes with the boundary gauge group. As the two bilinears can carry independent flavor labels, the exact polynomial has a `QQQL` witness with tensor indices `(21,25;1,18)` and coefficient +1, and a `U^c U^c D^c E^c` witness `(6,9;10,17)` with coefficient -1 in the repo's tensor convention. These coefficients describe monomials before the independent Wilson coefficient and the neutral Higgs VEV are inserted. The boundary condition eliminates exchange through the triplet values but permits this contact operator.

There is a further constraint on a symmetry intended to remove the contact. For any additive abelian charge with superpotential charge `r_W`, allowing `J K`, `Jbar Kbar` and a constant `K Kbar` mass implies `r(J)+r(Jbar)=r_W`. The charge can forbid the contact only by changing at least one of those assumptions. For example, the tree selection rules of `Z4^R` with charge 1 for matter and 0 for the sourced Higgs fields and neutral frame allow both Yukawas and forbid the contact. They also forbid the direct spectator mass. Bulk partners of charge 2 can instead pair with charge-0 fields. This is charge arithmetic only, with the zero-mode spectrum, anomalies and symmetry breaking of that proposal still open. A family-universal rephasing leaves the derivative gauge-current vertex invariant. See [Lee et al. on the MSSM charge assignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.0905).

## What the W(3,3) context contributes

[Pass 12683](https://github.com/wilcompute/W33-Theory/commit/64050d0b3) finds ten measurement contexts, one for each choice of weak pair among the five factorizations. After choosing a context, the sign assignment is a diagonal multiplication operator on the five labels, with minus on the weak orbit. The six-element context stabilizer acts by permutations on the two orbits. The orbit-sum vector of each orbit is fixed by every such permutation. Every linear combination of those permutation matrices therefore has the same eigenvalue on both orbit sums, whereas the reflection gives them opposite eigenvalues. This rules out obtaining the required reflection from the context stabilizer's permutation algebra alone. Context incidence supplies the labels for the projector. The dynamical realization of that projector as a spacetime reflection is an additional assumption.

The exact boundary calculation does not supply a full compactification. It assumes the matter location and a compatible even vacuum, and leaves the extra bulk zero-mode spectrum, boundary gauge and supersymmetry conditions, and the independent local Wilson coefficients to be determined. A useful discriminator for the next model is whether the same selection rule that permits both Yukawa sources can forbid the explicit boundary contact while accommodating the derivative gauge current.
