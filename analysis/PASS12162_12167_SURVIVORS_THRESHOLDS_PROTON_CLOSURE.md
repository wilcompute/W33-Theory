## 12162: the survivors' own exotics cannot close the Weinberg-angle gap

**Method.** For each survivor, take its own (nsosm) hypercharge and compute the one-loop b_a of the full massless
spectrum. Then Δb_a = b_a(total) − b_a(SM with one Higgs doublet). Solve the three measured couplings exactly.

**(a) M_s at the heterotic value** (g_U·5.27·10¹⁷ GeV), with coloured and colourless exotics at independent scales.
**Every survivor needs its exotics above M_s (12/12).** The exotics push the couplings the wrong way.

**(b) One common exotic scale, M_s free.** The fit is consistent only for survivors 2, 15 and 77:

| survivor | M_s (GeV) | M_X (GeV) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 1.2·10¹³ | 1.4·10⁶ |
| 15 | 1.1·10¹² | 3.3·10⁷ |
| 77 | 3.6·10¹¹ | 1.5·10⁸ |

These lie far below the perturbative heterotic scale.

## 12166: the low-scale escape fails on the proton

In all three, **6 colour-triplet scalar components (Y = ∓1/3) have both a diquark and a leptoquark trilinear coupling**
under the full rules (exact H-momentum). Exchanged at 10⁶–10⁸ GeV, they give a proton lifetime of years. The bound is
above 10³⁴ yr. Pushing the triplets to ≳ 10¹⁵ GeV brings back case (a).

## 12164: the Fricke partner is not a simple transformation

## 12165: charm and up

With the calibrated rules the up-type rank stays 1 through quartic order in all 16 crossed models (12159). That is
consistent with the all-order AG(3,3) protection of Pass 11102. Lifting it needs the scalar potential to break that
reflection spontaneously; this is not computed here.

## 12167: closure of the T⁶/Z3 SO(16)×SO(16) W(3,3) route as a realistic Standard Model

There are three independent grounds:

1. **Fraction-free hypercharges** have k_Y ≥ 17/3 (12131), and they fail the Weinberg angle even with thresholds
   (12154, 12156).
2. **Canonical k_Y = 5/3 with fractional states lifted** (11097–11102):
   - the exotics give no usable threshold at the heterotic scale (12162a);
   - the only low-scale fits need light diquark–leptoquark scalars, so protons decay (12166).
3. **The Wen–Witten escape** (field-theoretic GUT breaking) needs a 10_H, which never appears (12161, 12163).

**What survives as mathematics and structure:**

- three chiral generations from the e6+a2 shift (12111);
- the self-duality law k_Y = 5/3 + 4n (12131);
- Witten-parity protection (12142);
- the Fricke-symmetric Witten levels and their massless fixed point at i/√3 (12149, 12152);
- the twisted-sector congruence, an instance of Wen–Witten (12150, 12161).

**Scope.** The closure is for this geometry (T⁶/Z3, prime order) with these W(3,3) shifts. Pass 11100 found the other
W(3,3) geometries tachyonic on its samples.
