\subsection*{Passes 5699--5703: tower zeta factorization, exact Ramanujan certificates, and the girth-cycle group identity}

The explicit Ramanujan 2-lift tower of Passes~5683/5693 is an Artin L-function
tower.  At every level the child spectrum splits exactly as
$\operatorname{spec}(A_{\rm child})=\operatorname{spec}(A_{\rm parent})\cup\operatorname{spec}(A_{\rm signed})$
(machine precision, max error $2.3\times10^{-14}$), so by the Bass determinant
formula the Ihara zeta factors level by level as
\[
 \zeta_{\rm child}(u)^{-1}=\zeta_{\rm parent}(u)^{-1}\,L(u,\chi)^{-1},\qquad
 L(u,\chi)^{-1}=(1-u^2)^{r-1}\det\!\bigl(I-uA_{\rm signed}+3u^2I\bigr),
\]
the Stark--Terras Artin L-function of the $\mathbb Z/2$ local system defined by
the balanced signing.  The signing is the same datum as the determinant-line
twist of Pass~5696, so the tower L-functions are the orientation-twisted sector
partition functions.  The base determinant has the closed form
\[
 \Delta_{\rm Levi}(u)=(1-u^2)(1-9u^2)(1+9u^4)^{24}(1+3u^2)^{30},
\]
the $24$-dimensional $\pm\sqrt6$ eigenspace collapsing to the quartic factor
$(1+9u^4)^{24}$ whose roots sit at $45^\circ$ angles on the critical circle.

The tower Riemann hypothesis is now an exact theorem, not numerics.  Every
signed-spectrum L-function pole lies on $|u|=1/\sqrt3$ (160/160, 320/320,
640/640), certified by exact rational LDL decompositions of
$B=12I-A_{\rm signed}^2$ (positive definite, minimum pivots $4.82$, $4.49$,
$4.52$ as exact rationals) at levels 1--3, with a 60-digit Cholesky certificate
for the $320\to640$ signing.  The apparent Sturm count $77/80$ at level 1 is
resolved: the zero eigenvalue has multiplicity $4$ and Sturm counts distinct
roots.

Exact integer trace arithmetic gives the girth-cycle group identity
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(A_{\rm Levi}^8)=193280=80\cdot 2092+25920
 =n\,M_8^{\rm tree}+|\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)|,
\]
so the Levi graph has exactly $25920/16=1620$ girth-8 cycles, counted by the
order of the automorphism simple group.  The excess shrinks under lifting
($25920\to25600\to25216\to24928$) while girth stays pinned at $8$: the tower
converges locally to the tree without destroying the defining octagons.  The
$25920$ rooted oriented 8-cycles split into exactly two $\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)$
orbits of $12960$ with $\mathbb Z/2$ stabilizers, separated by a symplectic
chirality invariant (diagonal common-neighbour count $4$ vs $0$); the cycle
stabilizer is an involution of cycle type $1^8 2^{16}$.  Whether the two orbits
merge into a regular action under the full $W(E_6)$ duality is open.

The new spectra equidistribute to the Kesten--McKay law of the $4$-regular tree
at an empirical geometric rate: the Kolmogorov--Smirnov distance halves each
level, $0.02102\to0.01079\to0.00540$, and signed moments match the tree exactly
through $M_6$ (girth-forced).  Eigenphase spacings sit near GOE rather than
Poisson.  Independently, $W(3,9)$ was rebuilt over $\mathbb F_9$ (820 vertices,
$90$-regular) and the greedy+swap optimizer class plateaus at $\alpha\ge46$,
replicating --- not breaking --- the $51\le\alpha(W(3,9))\le80$ barrier.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
All statements are finite graph, matrix, zeta-function, or group-action facts
verified by exact integer/rational arithmetic or high-precision numerics with
explicit error bounds.  No continuum limit, physical energy spectrum,
Yang--Mills mass gap, or all-level equidistribution theorem is claimed; the
$2^{-\text{level}}$ KS law and the excess-shrinkage trend are empirical on
three to four levels.
