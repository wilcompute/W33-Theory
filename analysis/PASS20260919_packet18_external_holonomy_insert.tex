\subsection{The holonomy group acts on packet18 from the left and right}
\label{sec:20260919-packet18-external-holonomy}

The packet execution group is
\[
G=\mathbb F_3^2\rtimes D_8,\qquad |G|=72,
\]
and the eighteen probe blocks are the homogeneous space
\[
X=G/H,\qquad H=\langle R\rangle\cong C_4.
\]
The earlier subgroup obstruction remains valid: the physical character group
\[
K=C_{3,\rho}\times C_{3,a}\times C_{2,b}
\]
is not a subgroup of $G$.  Nevertheless the homogeneous space carries one additional symmetry that is invisible if one looks only inside the left runtime action.

For a transitive $G$-set $G/H$,
\[
\operatorname{Aut}_G(G/H)\cong N_G(H)/H.
\]
Here exhaustive computation gives
\[
N_G(H)=\{0\}\times D_8,\qquad N_G(H)/H\cong C_2.
\]
The unique nontrivial equivariant automorphism is right multiplication by a reflection.  In the packet coordinates
\[
t=(\rho,a)\in\mathbb F_3^2,\qquad b\in\mathbb F_2,
\]
it is simply $(t,b)\mapsto(t,b+1)$ and commutes with the entire left $G$-action.

The left translation kernel $T=\mathbb F_3^2$ and this right deck involution therefore generate
\[
T_{\rm left}\times C_{2,\rm deck}\cong C_3^2\times C_2,
\]
acting regularly on all eighteen packet blocks. Thus the old two-sheet torsor bridge is the restriction of an exact regular action of the full holonomy character group; what remains false is only the stronger claim that this group sits inside the 72-element execution group.

Using $n=4a+3b\pmod 6$, the physical $C_6$ generator $(a,b)=(1,1)$ becomes one ternary translation plus one deck swap. Its packet18 cycle profile is $6^3$.
The existing control-plane ABI writes
\[
h=3\rho+a,\qquad \texttt{tick}=8h+4b+p,
\]
where $p\in\mathbb Z_4$ is the probe slot. Hence the same generator compiles lookup-free on the full 72-slot frame as
\[
a\mapsto a+1,\qquad b\mapsto b+1,\qquad \rho,p\ \text{fixed}.
\]
It has cycle profile $6^{12}$, its cube is the pure deck parity, and its sixth power is the identity.

These statements are control-plane and finite-group theorems. They do not identify an $E_8$ eigenspace with a hardware packet mode, nor do they claim that address mutation alone implements the microscopic holonomy.
