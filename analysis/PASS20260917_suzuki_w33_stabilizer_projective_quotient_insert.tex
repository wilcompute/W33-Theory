\subsection{The Suzuki block stabilizer: symplectic double cover upstairs, inner projective action downstairs}
\label{sec:20260917-suzuki-w33-stabilizer-quotient}

The full-W33 incidence tower gives a $135135$-element $2.\mathrm{Suz}$ orbit of
nondegenerate $4$-spaces $U\cong W(3,3)$ inside the twelve-dimensional
symplectic carrier, with $27$ complementary three-W33 decompositions through
each fixed $U$.  The latter carry the Schläfli/cubic-surface graph
$\operatorname{SRG}(27,10,1,5)$.  The next question is not whether that graph
\emph{admits} the usual $E_6$ symmetry, but which part of that symmetry is
actually induced by the ambient Suzuki action.

Using the vendored ATLAS matrices, the orbit-stabilizer order is
\[
 |\operatorname{Stab}_{2.\mathrm{Suz}}(U)|
 =\frac{896690995200}{135135}
 =\boxed{6635520}.
\]
A Schreier transversal over the complete orbit shows that restriction to the
four-dimensional block is surjective onto
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{Sp}(4,3)},\qquad |\mathrm{Sp}(4,3)|=51840,
\]
with kernel of order
\[
 \boxed{128=2^7}.
\]
Thus the embedded block sees the full symplectic double cover.

Projectivising changes the answer.  The action on the $27$ complementary
decompositions has image
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)},\qquad |\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)|=25920,
\]
and kernel
\[
 \boxed{256=2^8}.
\]
The computation is exhaustive rather than sampled.  A five-vertex base for the
full $51840$-element automorphism group of the local Schläfli graph is fixed;
all $270270=2\cdot135135$ Schreier generators are then classified by their
images on that base.  Every generator lies in the inner $25920$-element
projective subgroup.  No outer-coset generator occurs.

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.92\textwidth}{\small
\textbf{Extension firewall.}  Two order-$51840$ groups occur here and they must
not be identified.  The linear block action is the central double cover
$\mathrm{Sp}(4,3)\to\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)$.  The full cubic-surface Weyl symmetry is
the different extension
$W(E_6)\cong\mathrm{PSp}(4,3){:}2$.  The ambient $2.\mathrm{Suz}$ action realizes
the former upstairs and only the inner $\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)$ action on the
projective $27$-carrier.  The outer $E_6$ involution is absent from this induced
action.}}
\end{center}

This sharpens the earlier local-$E_6$ statement.  The $27$ decompositions still
form the exact cubic-surface incidence carrier, whose full graph automorphism
group is the Weyl group of type $E_6$; what is new is the actual subgroup
selected by the Suzuki embedding.  Standard cubic-surface geometry identifies
the automorphism group of the $27$-line incidence graph with $W(E_6)$, while
the repository computation determines the induced subgroup and both kernels.

The executable certificate is
\texttt{analysis/w33\_suzuki\_w33\_stabilizer\_projective\_quotient.py}, with
frozen output
\texttt{data/w33\_suzuki\_w33\_stabilizer\_projective\_quotient.json}.
The statement is finite group theory only: it does not make the absent outer
involution into a physical gate or assert that any enlargement of the Suzuki
action is dynamically available.
