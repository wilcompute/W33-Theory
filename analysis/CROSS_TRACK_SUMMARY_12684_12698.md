## Validated tool

`toe_pass12685_intermediate_scale_suite.py` provides `run()` / `mu0()`:

- reproduction of the published three-loop λ(M_Pl) fit to ≤ 1.6×10⁻⁴ (Pass 12687);
- options for vectorlike quarks and seesaw Yukawas.
