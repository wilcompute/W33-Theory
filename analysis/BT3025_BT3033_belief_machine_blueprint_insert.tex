\section*{The decoder is now a belief machine}

The previous architecture correctly replaced a fixed $29$-row diagnostic with an exact
adaptive $23/24/25$-row tree.  This pass changes the abstraction one level deeper.  A
physical detector does not emit a theorem-valued $D_4$ symbol; it emits evidence with
loss, confusion, drift and erasure.  The machine must therefore preserve likelihoods until
the decision boundary.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.0cm}p{10.5cm}@{}}
\textbf{Layer} & \textbf{Contract}\\ \hline
Geometry & $48826$ exact no/one/two-edge $D_4$ hypotheses and $120$ triangle observables.\\
Likelihood & Calibrated nine-outcome rows: eight $D_4$ symbols plus erasure.\\
Belief & At most three posterior scores after the common panel identifies a collision class.\\
Causal state & One of $457$ future-action classes, not one of $1436$ raw collision labels.\\
Action & STOP or choose both a triangle and a sensor alphabet.\\
Stream index & Omitted slot protects substitutions; ordering the same three pilots indexes edits.\\
Reset boundary & Retain next action and residual risk; reversibly uncompute the transcript.\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection*{Numbers a builder can use}

\begin{itemize}
\item The exact future-action quotient needs $9$ fixed bits instead of $11$ and saves
$1.078290855$ conditional Shannon bits under the frozen sparse prior.
\item A source-complete signed-score core handles size-two and size-three collision
classes, programmable likelihood increments, a stop threshold, and two escalation slots.
\item The substitution word still corrects four arbitrary symbol substitutions.  It does
\emph{not} correct a single insertion/deletion as an isolated cyclic block; adjacent phases
are edit-distance two.  Pilot ordering supplies the missing stream index with no new mode.
\item In the moderate synthetic channel, a six-outcome conjugacy-class sensor retains
$99.6683\%$ of the nine-outcome full-$D_4$ sensor's one-probe risk reduction.  Sensor
resolution is therefore an action variable, not a constant of the machine.
\item The static upper bound is $28$, not $29$.  A proof-producing SAT job decides whether
$27$ is impossible in the central $r^2$ restriction and verifies every SAT model against
the full nonabelian hypothesis set.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{What is exact, what is modeled, what is still absent}

The finite group calculations, causal quotient, edit obstruction, synchronization scores,
Fourier decomposition and Blackwell order are exact.  The three optical confusion
profiles and calm/burst drift process are explicit synthetic models.  The RTL is source
complete.  FPGA timing, physical D4 confusion matrices, streaming edit benchmarks,
proof-checked $27$-row UNSAT, and laboratory heat remain unobserved.

The design rule is now sharper than ``measure adaptively'':
\[
\boxed{\text{preserve evidence}\;\longrightarrow\;\text{quotient by future action}
\;\longrightarrow\;\text{spend photons only where the quotient changes}.}
\]
