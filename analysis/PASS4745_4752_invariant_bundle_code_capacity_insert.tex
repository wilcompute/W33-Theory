By contrast Pass~4713 produced a nonzero $\operatorname{PGSp}(4,3)$-fixed deck class in $H^1(\Gamma;\mathbb F_2)$. \paragraph{Exact adversarial capacity.}
Normalize every cold-edge capacity to $1$ and every hot-edge capacity to $\rho>0$.  The cold graph has edge-connectivity $12$, each Petersen fiber has edge-connectivity $3$, the $27$-vertex quotient has edge-connectivity $10$, and every quotient edge carries exactly $12$ cold physical edges.  Therefore a cut that splits a Petersen fiber costs at least $12+3\rho$, while a cut splitting no fiber is a union of whole fibers and costs at least $12\cdot10=120$.  Both bounds are attained, giving the exact worst-pair min-cut
\[
  \lambda_{\min}(\rho)=\min\{12+3\rho,120\},
\]
with the sole technology breakpoint $\rho=36$.  At equal capacities all $\binom{270}{2}=36315$ vertex pairs have min-cut $15$.  Deleting all $15$ shortcut edges in one Petersen fiber lowers the global min-cut to exactly $12$ for every $\rho>0$.  Removing one entire ten-vertex fiber leaves a connected $260$-vertex graph of min-cut $13$ and diameter $3$; removing two representative fibers leaves min-cut $11$ and diameter $3$ for both quotient-adjacent and quotient-nonadjacent pairs.
