\subsection{Pass 10964: one E6-shaped U(1) forces the sneutrino}

In each of the $23$ $W(3,3)$ $\mathbb{Z}_6$-I models with FI-cancelling D-flat singlet directions
there is an exact canonical certificate $f=sQ_{\rm anom}+\lambda\cdot Q_{\rm nonanom}$ with
$f=\tfrac{24}{5}\mu$ on every $q,u^c,e^c$, $f=\tfrac85\mu$ on every $d^c,\ell$, $\mu>0$, and
$f\ge0$ on every singlet that can be matter-even.  On Standard-Model singlets $f/\mu$ takes
only the values $-8,0,8$, on Higgs-type doublets $-\tfrac{48}{5},-\tfrac85$, and the $f<0$
singlets are exactly forced $B-L=+1$ ones at $-8$.  On the families
$f/\mu=4Q_\psi-\tfrac45Q_\chi$ with $Q_\chi=4Y-5(B-L)$; the value $-8$ at $(B-L,Y)=(1,0)$
means $Q_\psi=-3$: the right-handed-sneutrino direction of the matter-odd $16_{-3}\subset78$ of
$E_6$.  The anomalous $U(1)$ acts on singlets like the $E_6$ generator $5Q_\psi-Q_\chi$, and only
that sneutrino can balance it.
