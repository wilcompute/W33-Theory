# Pass5181 prior-art boundary — point/line duality

The P/L chart asymmetry found in Pass5177–5181 is consistent with standard classical generalized-quadrangle duality and is **not** presented as a new theorem about the isomorphism type of `W_3(q)`.

Primary accessible source used in this packet:

- Dean Crnković, Daniel R. Hawtin, Andrea Švob, *Neighbour-Transitive Codes and Partial Spreads in Generalised Quadrangles*, arXiv:2105.05833. Section 2 states that `W_3(q)` is self-dual when `q` is even, while for odd `q` its dual is `Q_4(q)`.

What is repo-derived here is the consequence for the specific opposite-pair chart-intersection/Tanner geometry: the exact q=2,4 anchors exhibit matching P and L `K_{m,m}` tensor decompositions, while q=3,5 exhibit a factorized P graph and connected L graph. The even-q transfer follows from self-duality plus the Pass5177 P-component theorem. The q=3,5 connected-L statements are exact repo computations; no all-odd-q connectedness theorem is claimed.
