\subsection{Full-apartment cubic correction and the $P$-footprint quotient}
\label{sec:pass5198_5203_q5_footprint}

The chamber-leader recursion at $q=5$ can be sharpened without assuming a
fourth-order apartment-intersection classification.  For an apartment containing
$r\in\{0,\ldots,8\}$ selected chambers one has the pointwise inequality
\[
 {\bf 1}_{\{r\ \mathrm{odd}\}}
 \ge r-2\binom r2+\frac{36}{35}\binom r3-\frac{48}{5}\binom r8.
\]
Thus the cubic coefficient may be raised from $6/7$ to $36/35$ provided fully
selected apartments are charged explicitly.  If $A_8$ denotes their number and
$P_5$ the number of selected five-edge Levi paths, then every full apartment
contains eight such windows, whereas a selected five-edge path has a unique
apartment completion.  Hence
\[
 8A_8\le P_5.
\]
A central selected edge of selected endpoint degrees $(i,j)$ supports at most
$4(i-1)(j-1)$ such paths.  Therefore, writing $e_{ij}$ for the selected
edge-type counts,
\[
 P_5\le\sum_{i,j}4(i-1)(j-1)e_{ij},
 \qquad
 A_8\le\left\lfloor\frac{P_5}{8}\right\rfloor.
\]
Combined with the existing pairwise Delsarte, $P_4$, $P_5$, $N_3$, and $N_4$
constraints, this closes chamber leaders $33$, $34$, and $35$.  The respective
new strict barriers are
\[
 \boxed{m_{\min}\ge34},\qquad
 \boxed{m_{\min}\ge35},\qquad
 \boxed{m_{\min}\ge36}.
\]
At leader $35$ the sharpest surviving relaxed layer is $N_1=57$, with certified
integer lower bound $627$, only two units above the target $5^4=625$.
Consequently this method is near its natural limit; no claim for leaders
$m\ge36$ is made here.

A separate exact quotient emerges from the opposite-point ($P$) tensor
components.  Let $F$ be the binary incidence matrix between the
$W(3,5)$ points and the $325$ $P$-component point blocks.  Each point lies in
$25$ blocks and each component block consists of $12$ points inducing
$K_{6,6}$.  Direct projective-polarity reconstruction gives
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}F=65,
 \qquad
 \dim\ker F^{T}=91.
\]
Every $W(3,5)$ line incidence vector lies in $\ker F^T$, and the binary line
incidence code also has rank $91$.  Hence
\[
 \boxed{\ker F^T=\langle\text{$W(3,5)$ line incidences}\rangle_{\mathbb F_2}}.
\]
The same rank complement is verified independently at $q=3$ and $q=7$.
For odd $q$, the parity of each $P$-component restriction is therefore a genuine
cut-gauge invariant; at $q=5$, a zero component-parity vector admits a
gauge-equivalent representative with zero point-parity vector.  This normal-form
statement does \emph{not} assert preservation of minimum chamber-leader size.

The $P$-component blocks themselves carry a strongly regular intersection
graph.  In general its parameters are
\[
 \operatorname{SRG}\!\left(
 \frac{q^2(q^2+1)}2,
 (q+1)(q^2-1),
 3q^2-q-2,
 2q(q+1)
 \right).
\]
At $q=5$ this is
\[
 \operatorname{SRG}(325,144,68,60)
\]
with nontrivial eigenvalues $14$ and $-6$.  The $25$ components in a fixed
W-point footprint form a clique, and the Delsarte bound
$1-144/(-6)=25$ proves that these footprints are maximum cliques.  Every
block-graph edge lies in exactly two distinguished point-footprint cliques.
The resulting binary footprint code has parameters $[325,65]$ at the level of
length and dimension and contains weight-$25$ point-footprint words.
Its minimum distance is \emph{not} promoted here: the exact exclusion of
nonzero weights below $25$, and the residual zero-parity apartment sector,
remain open.

\paragraph{Evidence firewall.}
Passes 5198--5200 prove only the strict-counterexample barrier
$m_{\min}\ge36$ at $q=5$; they do not prove $d=625$ or classify the weight-$625$
equality shell.  Passes 5201--5203 are $P$-side quotient and block-geometry
theorems and preserve the established odd-$q$ $P/L$ asymmetry.  No $L$-side
tensor decomposition is inferred from them.
