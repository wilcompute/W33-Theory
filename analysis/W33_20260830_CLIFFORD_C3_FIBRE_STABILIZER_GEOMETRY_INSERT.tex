\subsection{The nonsplit $C_9$ species avoids both $36$-fibre stabilizer geometries}
The free central $C_3$ action on the $216$ sentinel five-circuits produces $72$ fibres.  The quotient
\[
Q\cong\operatorname{ASL}(2,3),\qquad |Q|=216,
\]
acts faithfully with two orbits of size $36$, whose point stabilizers are respectively $S_3$ and $C_6$.  The $40$ cyclic order-three subgroups of $Q$ split into conjugacy classes of sizes $4+12+24$.

The four pure-translation subgroups have cycle shape $1^9 3^{21}$ on the $72$ fibres: each fixes nine fibres in the $Q/S_3$ orbit and none in the $Q/C_6$ orbit.  The twelve fixed-line unipotent subgroups have cycle shape $1^3 3^{23}$: each fixes three fibres in $Q/C_6$ and none in $Q/S_3$.  The twenty-four fixed-point-free nontranslation unipotents have cycle shape $3^{24}$ and fix no fibre in either orbit; these are exactly the subgroups whose full preimage is cyclic $C_9$ rather than split $C_3\times C_3$.

Since $4\cdot9=36$ and $12\cdot3=36$, every $S_3$-stabilized fibre contains the unique order-three subgroup from the translation class, and every $C_6$-stabilized fibre contains the unique subgroup from the fixed-line class.  Thus
\[
\boxed{\text{nonsplit }24\text{-class}=\text{the order-three species lying in neither fibre-stabilizer geometry}.}
\]
This is an exact finite permutation statement; a photonic coordinate interpretation requires an independently proved intertwiner.
