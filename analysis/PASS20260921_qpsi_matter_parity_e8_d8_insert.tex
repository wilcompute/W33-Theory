\[

\[
P_M=(-1)^{Q_\psi}.
\]

\[
240=112_{\rm even}+128_{\rm odd}.
\]

\[
\{\pm e_i\pm e_j\},
\]

\[
\boxed{248=120+128}

triads have only
\[

\]

the cross-repository heterotic observation that a dangerous matter-odd MSSM
operator can occur in an invariant coupling only after another odd factor,
such as the measured odd singlet insertion, is included.
