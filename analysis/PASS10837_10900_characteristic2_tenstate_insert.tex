\subsubsection*{Passes 10837--10900: modular extension defect and the ten-state projective carrier}
The order-two normalizer obstruction is now completely localized in the
$316$-dimensional $C_{13}$-fixed sector.  As $\mathbf F_2[C_2]$-modules,
\[
 \mathbf F_2[V_2]_{C_{13}\text{-fixed}}\cong 1^{64}\oplus J_2^{126},
 \qquad
 H_1(\operatorname{Levi}H(4);\mathbf F_2)_{C_{13}\text{-fixed}}
 \cong J_2^{158},
\]
where $J_2$ is the two-dimensional regular indecomposable.  Thus the exact
stable repair is
\[
 \mathbf F_2[V_2]\oplus J_2^{32}
 \cong
 H_1(\operatorname{Levi}H(4);\mathbf F_2)\oplus 1^{64}
\]
on this defect sector.  The nontrivial $W_{12}^{315}$ $C_{13}$ sector already
has the unique compatible semilinear extension.

For Wilson's explicit order-six complement $n=su$ with $s=n^4$ of order three
and $u=n^3$ of order two, the natural $\mathbf F_4^6$ module has Jordan form
\[
 J_2(1)\oplus J_2(\omega)\oplus J_2(\omega^2).
\]
Hence $\operatorname{Fix}(u)\cong\mathbf F_4^3$ and projectivizes to
$PG(2,4)$.  The fixed $H(4)$ Levi incidence is a tree selecting an affine
projective frame: one distinguished origin, one line at infinity, and five
radial projective directions.  The unique $s$-fixed $\mathbf F_4$ line gives a
scalar-gauge family of three local translations, each pairing the $64$ fixed
states into $32$ pairs.  No nonzero such translation extends
$C_{13}$-equivariantly because the order-thirteen action is irreducible.

On the Hall--Janko controller, the full $C_{13}{:}C_{12}$ normalizer acts on

\[
 1^1 3^1 4^1 6^2 12^1.
\]

$1^2 2^4$.  This is not $\mathbf F_9/\mathbf F_3$ Frobenius on

\[

 \boxed{\{0,\infty\}\;\sqcup\;\mathbf F_9^\times/\{\pm1\}}

Only this $C_2$-set/six-state quotient is identified; no $C_4$ cross-ratio or

Finally, the integers $32$ and $64$ acquire an exact modular meaning.  Since
\[

 \qquad

\]
there is a unique nonzero nonsplit self-extension
\[
 0\longrightarrow1\longrightarrow J_2\longrightarrow1\longrightarrow0.
\]

\[

 \qquad

classes whereas the homology sector carries none.  The identity
\[
 \boxed{64=2\cdot32}
\]
is therefore forced by the length-two projective extension rather than by a
cardinality coincidence.
