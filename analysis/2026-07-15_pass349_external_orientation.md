# Pass 349: The External Orientation Input — What It Must Be and What It Cannot Be

**Date:** 2026-07-15  
**Provenance:** Passes 346, 347, 333  
**Status:** Boundary mapping — no new mathematics claimed

## The Situation After Pass 346

Pass 346 proved: no PGSp(4,3)-invariant datum can select one half-spin chirality over the other. The substrate can HOST the SM's chirality but cannot EXPLAIN it. The natural follow-up: what KIND of external input could supply the selection?

## The Four Candidate Input Types

### (1) Boundary Condition
The substrate is the interior of a larger geometric structure whose boundary breaks PGSp(4,3) symmetry. The boundary selects orientation by providing an "outside" from which the half-spin families are distinguishable.

**Status:** Consistent with the no-go. The containing structure is unspecified. This is how chirality works in classical differential geometry: an orientation is a choice of which normal is "outward."

### (2) Cosmological / Initial State Selection
At some phase transition in the early universe, one chirality domain was populated. The CPT-partner domain exists but is causally disconnected. The asymmetry is historical, not structural.

**Status:** Consistent. This is the standard explanation for baryon asymmetry. It does not require any internal mechanism — just a symmetry-breaking event and sufficient causal horizon.

### (3) Dynamical Symmetry Breaking
A VEV or condensate of a field transforming under an extension of PGSp(4,3) explicitly breaks the group to a subgroup stabilizing one half-spin.

**Status:** Consistent IF the VEV is charged under the extension. Pass 346 proves that any PGSp-invariant scalar on V = 5a⊕5b is symmetric (the unique invariant bilinear form), so no PGSp-invariant VEV can do the job. The breaking field must be charged under something **outside** PGSp(4,3).

### (4) Pure Convention
The two chiralities are physically indistinguishable at the substrate level. The labeling is arbitrary — a convention choice with no physical content.

**Status:** Disfavored by the empirical fact of baryogenesis (physical matter-antimatter asymmetry exists in the observable universe). Not formally ruled out by the no-go alone, which is a statement about the substrate's internal symmetry, not about observable physics.

## What the No-Go Rules Out

Option (0): an internal substrate mechanism deriving chirality from W(3,3) geometry alone. This is what Pass 346 eliminates by theorem.

## Logical Structure

The no-go is not a failure. It is a **precise boundary**: the W33 theory explains the substrate's structure, its generation count, its code parameters, and its quantum universality. It terminates at the chirality selection boundary and hands the problem to cosmology/dynamics. This is exactly what a complete sub-theory should do.
