All-odd PSL(2,q) pair-space theorem: Steinberg square, Hecke blocks, and the mod-8 clock

Exact finite theorem. For every odd prime power q, the canonical unordered-pair space of the q+1 lines through a point is Λq = C(P¹(q),2) ≅ PSL₂(q)/N(Tsplit), with |Λq| = q(q+1)/2. Over characteristic zero its permutation module is exactly Sym²(Stq), the symmetric square of the q-dimensional Steinberg module.

The pair-space orbital rank is (3q+9)/4 for q ≡ 1 (mod 4) and (3q+7)/4 for q ≡ 3 (mod 4). Its complex centralizer algebra is C^s ⊕ M₂(C)^d, where d = floor((q+3)/8) and s = r−4d. The q=5 local fiber therefore recovers C² ⊕ M₂(C) as one member of a complete all-odd family.

A separate characteristic-2 theorem prevents overclaiming: because q+1 is even, the all-one vector lies in the augmentation kernel of F₂[P¹(q)], so the trivial submodule does not split off. Thus the characteristic-zero Steinberg cancellation cannot be reduced mod 2 to prove the open footprint-rank conjecture.

The two regimes nevertheless carry complementary arithmetic bits: the exceptional complex pair is controlled by χ₋₂(q), while the binary Weil descent is controlled by χ₂(q). Together they distinguish all four classes in (Z/8Z)×.
