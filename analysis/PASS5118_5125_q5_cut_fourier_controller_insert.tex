\[

There is also an all-$q$ support law.  The theta point graph is $8(q-1)$-regular.  Because every apartment lies in $4(q-1)$ theta checks and every codeword has even parity on each theta triple, every selected apartment has exactly $4(q-1)$ selected and $4(q-1)$ unselected theta neighbors.  Thus for every codeword support $S$,
\[
 \Gamma_\theta[S]\text{ is }4(q-1)\text{-regular},\qquad
 |\delta_\theta(S)|=4(q-1)|S|.
\]
This is a necessary intrinsic isoperimetric constraint, not a converse to the theta parity equations.
