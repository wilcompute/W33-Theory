Holotrade now closes the carrier half of the Qpsi/mu12 bridge at the level
actually supported by the two-qutrit frame geometry.

Relative to one complete factorisation frame, the 27 E6 weights are realized
as 1+10+16 frames with charges 4,-2,1.  The operator
`D12|F> = zeta12^Qpsi(F)|F>` has exact order 12, and all 45 cubic/tritangent
triples have total phase one.

Because each frame is a tensor factorisation of C^9 and W33 proves the
two-qutrit Clifford scalar center is mu12, the direct-sum 27-frame bundle has
an explicit blockwise Clifford-scalar action.  This is a genuine carrier
statement, but it includes the frame register.  It does not claim that one
scalar operator on one bare C^9 can encode the relative 1+10+16 phases.
