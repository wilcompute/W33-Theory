\subsection{Apartment parity beyond the $10$-space: module structure, zeta coefficients, and exact thermal duality}
\label{sec:pass4472-4479-apartment-module}

Passes~4461--4466 turned the $1620$ building apartments into a binary
$[1620,39]$ parity code $C$ whose nondegenerate alternating quotient has
dimension $10$.  The concurrent Passes~4469--4471 identify that quotient with
the protected $H_{10}$ label shadow by an incidence map.  The present packet
asks a different question: what does the native W33 symmetry \emph{do} on that
space, and what additional exact structures become visible once the action is
computed rather than inferred from the dimension?

\paragraph{Pass 4472: the $10$-space has a canonical $1|8|1$ filtration.}
Rebuilding the projective line action from transvections gives the inner
$\operatorname{PSp}(4,3)$ image of order $25920$ and, after adjoining a
similitude, the projective outer extension of order $51840$.  Both act
faithfully on the nondegenerate $10$-dimensional apartment quotient.  The inner
action fixes exactly one line.  If $v$ spans that line, symplectic invariance
forces the $9$-space $v^\perp$ to be invariant, and direct computation gives
\begin{equation}
  0 < \langle v\rangle < v^\perp < H_{10},
  \qquad
  \dim=(1,9,10).
  \label{eq:pass4472-filtration}
\end{equation}
The composition dimensions are therefore
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{1\mid 8\mid 1.}
  \label{eq:pass4472-181}
\end{equation}
The middle $8$-dimensional factor is faithful and irreducible for the inner
group.  Its nonzero vectors form exactly two orbits, of sizes $135$ and $120$.
Thus the new quotient is not an irreducible ``mysterious ten'': it contains a
canonical eight-dimensional core between two trivial one-dimensional factors.

\paragraph{Pass 4473: the apartment Hamiltonian is a code energy exactly.}
For line-sign bits $b\in\mathbb F_2^{40}$ define
\[
  \mathcal H(b)=-W_4(b).
\]
Since the apartment syndrome is $H^Tb$ and
$W_4=1620-2\operatorname{wt}(H^Tb)$,
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\mathcal H(b)=-1620+2\operatorname{wt}(H^Tb).}
  \label{eq:pass4473-hamiltonian}
\end{equation}
Pass~4463 proved $\ker H^T=\langle\mathbf1_{40}\rangle$, so the exact ground
space has only the two globally reversed constant signings,
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{E_0=-1620,\qquad g_0=2.}
  \label{eq:pass4473-ground}
\end{equation}
A one-line flip has code weight $162$ and energy $-1296$.  Under independent
random line signs the exact energy moments are
\[
 \mathbb E E=0,
 \quad \operatorname{Var}E=1620,
 \quad \mathbb E E^3=-12960,
 \quad \mathbb E E^4=9891720.
\]
As a deliberately labelled empirical control, $4096$ deterministic random
line signings (seed $4473$) give correlation
$\operatorname{corr}(\rho,E)=-0.27752577$ between signed spectral radius and
four-spin energy; $26.4648\%$ are Ramanujan in that sample, and their mean
energy is $6.5092$ versus $-0.1475$ overall.  This modest opposition is
consistent with the earlier warning that a spin-glass objective is not a
surrogate for Bilu--Linial spectral optimization; the correlation itself is not
a theorem.

\paragraph{Pass 4474: three different ``four-cycle counts'' must remain distinct.}

\paragraph{Pass 4475: the apartment observable is an Artin--Ihara coefficient.}
Let $B_\sigma$ be the $480\times480$ signed non-backtracking matrix for a line
signing and put $S=\sum_\ell\sigma_\ell$.  Exact directed-walk enumeration gives
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\operatorname{tr}(B_\sigma^3)=24S,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\operatorname{tr}(B_\sigma^4)=960+8W_4.}
 \label{eq:pass4475-hashimoto-traces}
\end{equation}
The fixed $960=8\cdot120$ is precisely the eight orientations of the $120$
line-internal simple $C_4$'s.  Therefore, for
$L_\sigma(u)=\det(I-uB_\sigma)^{-1}$,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \log L_\sigma(u)
 =8S\,u^3+(240+2W_4)u^4+O(u^5).}
 \label{eq:pass4475-artin-ihara}
\end{equation}
This makes the bridge to the covering-graph Artin--Ihara language literal: the
apartment Walsh Hamiltonian is the complete signing-dependent part of the
$u^4$ logarithmic coefficient after the fixed geometric-line contribution is
removed.  The determinant/L-function context is the standard finite-covering
graph framework of Stark and Terras; the W33 coefficient identity above is the
new finite calculation.

\paragraph{Pass 4476: low-weight structure of the $[1620,39]$ apartment code.}
The primal minimum distance is \emph{not} promoted to a theorem: one line gives
$d(C)\le162$, and exhaustive coefficient supports through five flipped lines
find no smaller codeword, but that does not exclude a lower-weight word with
larger coefficient support.  What is exact is the dual beginning:
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{d(C^\perp)=3,\qquad A_3(C^\perp)=2160,\qquad
 A_4(C^\perp)=84240.}
 \label{eq:pass4476-dual-lowweights}
\end{equation}
There are no dual words of weight one or two because the $1620$ apartment
columns are nonzero and distinct; exact XOR enumeration supplies the weight
three and four counts.  The projective similitude action permutes the apartment
coordinates, giving a certified automorphism subgroup of order at least
$51840$; equality with the full code automorphism group is not claimed.

\paragraph{Pass 4477: the eight-dimensional core reconstructs the old $E_8/2E_8$ carrier.}
The middle factor admits a unique invariant quadratic refinement under the

\[
  \#\{Q=0\}=136\quad(135\text{ nonzero}),
  \qquad \#\{Q=1\}=120.
\]
The polar-orthogonality graphs on the two nonzero classes are exactly
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\operatorname{SRG}(135,70,37,35),}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\operatorname{SRG}(120,63,30,36).}
 \label{eq:pass4477-e8-srgs}
\end{equation}
More discriminatingly, a nonsingular-vector stabilizer has order $216$ in the
inner group and suborbits
$1,1,1,27,27,27,36$; adjoining the outer action doubles it to order $432$ and

retraction.  Thus apartment parity supplies a new construction of that old
carrier, not merely another occurrence of the numbers $135$ and $120$.

\paragraph{Pass 4478 (outside the box): a real four-qubit Pauli phase-space shadow.}
An explicit hyperbolic basis puts the invariant quadratic into the canonical
form
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{Q(a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_4,b_4)=\sum_{i=1}^4 a_i b_i\pmod2.}
 \label{eq:pass4478-pauli-q}
\end{equation}
For real four-qubit Pauli classes this quadratic records the parity of the
number of $Y$ factors: the nonidentity even-parity/symmetric classes number
$135$, while the odd-parity/skew classes number $120$.  The executable witness
checks the isometry on all $256$ vectors.  This matches the established finite
geometry of the real four-qubit Pauli group described by Saniga, Levay and
Pracna.  It is a finite phase-space isometry only; it does not say that the
$1620$-coordinate apartment code is physically implemented by four qubits.

\paragraph{Pass 4479 (outside the box): MacWilliams is an exact thermal transform.}
Let $W_C(z)=\sum_{c\in C}z^{\operatorname{wt}(c)}$.  Since every codeword has

\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 Z(\beta)=\sum_{b\in\mathbb F_2^{40}}e^{-\beta\mathcal H(b)}
 =2e^{1620\beta}W_C(e^{-2\beta}).}
 \label{eq:pass4479-partition}
\end{equation}
Together with the two-variable MacWilliams identity
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 W_C(x,y)=|C^\perp|^{-1}W_{C^\perp}(x+y,x-y),}
 \label{eq:pass4479-macwilliams}
\end{equation}
this is a literal finite coding/statistical-mechanics duality for the apartment
Hamiltonian.  No thermodynamic-limit phase transition is asserted; the exact
content is the finite partition-function transform.
