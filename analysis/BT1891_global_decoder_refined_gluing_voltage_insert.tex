\subsection{Exact global fifth-order decoding, paired-carrier phase, and the Tutte--Coxeter lift}
\label{subsec:bt1887-1891-five-frontiers}
The fixed-coordinate decoder chart is completed by an exact complementary
codeword census.  A partner retaining the fixed coordinate is already visible
in the chart; a partner omitting it differs by a weight-$4,6,8$, or $10$
codeword containing that coordinate.  Exhausting both cases gives
\[
 \boxed{U_5=1{,}531{,}165{,}872},
 \qquad
 \boxed{A^{\rm amb}_5=4{,}747{,}680{,}912},
\]
and the exact partition
\[
 6{,}363{,}048{,}048
 =84{,}201{,}264+1{,}531{,}165{,}872+4{,}747{,}680{,}912.
\]
Thus the corrected fifth-order BSC contribution is
\[
 \boxed{1{,}531{,}165{,}872\,p^5(1-p)^{235}}.
\]
The weight-six equal-syndrome edge count remains exact, but its singleton
component count remains open.

For the separator decomposition
\[
 240=20_{\rm residual}+180_{\rm pair}+40_{\rm phase},
\]
the complete rank-$30$ fiber subcode has an exact $563$-bin
$(w_{\rm pair},w_{\rm phase})$ enumerator over all $2^{30}$ words.  The
rank-$15$ residual subcode has exactly $156$ exceptional-$S_6$ orbits with
exact $(w_{\rm residual},w_{\rm pair},w_{\rm phase})$ records.  The full mixed
$2^{45}$ refined contraction is not inferred from these marginals.

Let $A_{24}=N_{24}/2$ and $A_{90}=N_{90}/2$ be the two literal natural
$V_9$ carrier maps.  Each rank-nine image lattice has Gram Smith invariants
\[
 2,10,10,10,20,40,40,40,40
\]
and discriminant $2^{18}5^8$.  The paired orthogonal rank-$18$ lattice carries
an exact exceptional-$S_6$-equivariant complex structure
\[
 J(A_{24}v)=A_{90}v,
 \qquad J(A_{90}v)=-A_{24}v,
 \qquad J^2=-I.
\]
Its half-integral enlargement has index $2^{18}$ and therefore does not absorb
the independent $2^{35}$ maximal-order defect of the clock algebra.

On the full $24\oplus90$ carrier the exceptional-$S_6$ commutant has real
dimension $23$ and the odd real-type multiplicities obstruct a complex
structure.  Restriction to the residual clock subgroup $C_4$ gives
\[
 28\mathbf1\oplus26\,\mathrm{sgn}\oplus30\mathbb R^2_{\rm rot},
\]
with commutant
\[
 M_{28}(\mathbb R)\times M_{26}(\mathbb R)\times M_{30}(\mathbb C),
\]
of real dimension $3260$; both the $24$- and $90$-sectors then admit
$C_4$-invariant complex structures.

Finally, every one of the $180$ pair-transfer coordinates is uniquely a
nonincident syntheme--duad pair, so this sector is exactly the bipartite
complement of the Tutte--Coxeter Levi graph.  The exceptional $C_4$ fixes two
of its $90$ octagons and has $22$ free octagon orbits.  On the $90$ directed
Hashimoto states its four powers fix
\[
 90,2,10,2
\]
states.  The natural $V_9$ lies in the adjacency eigenspaces $\lambda=\pm2$
and feeds the four cyclotomic nonbacktracking factors
\[
 (x^2-2x+2)^9,
 \qquad
 (x^2+2x+2)^9.
\]
All phase and voltage statements here are finite representation and graph
holonomies; no physical time evolution or optical phase law is inferred.
