## The distinction between field size and accessible group

Work in F9=F3[z]/(z²+1), z²=2. Every element is represented exactly as
a+bz with a,b in F3, encoded a+3b. The group SL(2,9) has order
9(9²-1)=720. A native symplectic transvection is

```text
tau_w(x) = x + omega(x,w) w.
```

Two transverse directions can be represented in the symplectic plane by
u=(1,0) and v=(0,lambda). Relative to the standard alternating form they give

```text
U = [[1,-1],[0,1]],
L = [[1,0],[lambda²,1]].
```

Thus the effective lower-shear coefficient a=lambda² is necessarily a
**square in F9**. The set of nonzero squares is exactly {1,2,z,2z},
encoded {1,2,3,6}. Enumerating all generated subgroups by exact
2-by-2 matrix multiplication and breadth-first closure gives:

| Lower-shear parameter a | F9 encoding | Generated order |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 or 2 | 1,2 | 24 |
| z or 2z | 3,6 | 120 |
| 1+z, 2+z, 1+2z, 2+2z | 4,5,7,8 | **720** |

The final four a are **non-squares**, inaccessible as a=lambda² when
both transvections are constrained to the displayed unit-coefficient
native form and u=(1,0).

For a controllable coefficient in tau_(w,c)(x)=x+c omega(x,w)w,
an independent nonsquare value c supplies a lower-shear coefficient
a=c lambda² that is nonsquare; the two shears then generate the full
720-element SL(2,9). The subgroup closure is verified, not inferred
from a matching order alone: every enumerated matrix has determinant1
and all generated matrices are distinct.

## A real constraint on the TOE/Holonet proposal

A naive claim that the two-anchor q=3 model becomes fully dynamical just
by extending the field to q=9 is **false for this native coefficient-one
gate model**. The group generated by two such ticks expands from
order24 to at most120, never to720. An *additional operational knob*
for the transvection coefficient unlocks the full two-dimensional
symplectic group in this case.

This is a testable **control-architecture specification**: is a native
qutrit/pump module allowed to set independently the nonzero coefficient c
in the shear, or is its value tied to a fixed normalization? Merely
naming a qutrit phase or choosing a different projective basis does
not establish physical accessibility of c.

Important: this classifies **two-generator** groups only. It does not
forbid larger groups generated by three or more native transvections;
nor does it imply the presence of a continuum, gauge dynamics, mass
spectrum, or quantized gravity when order720 is accessible.
