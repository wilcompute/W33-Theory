\section{The Steiner three-cover is a four-class association-scheme refinement of the \texorpdfstring{$W(3,3)$}{W(3,3)} line action}
\label{sec:pass4874-steiner-association}

Pass~4870 identified forty intrinsic three-element fibers among the $120$
Steiner triangles.  Pass~4954 later corrected the degree-$40$ quotient label:
it is the intersection graph of the forty $W(3,3)$ lines, equivalently the
point graph of the nonisomorphic odd-$q$ dual $Q(4,3)$, rather than the
standard $W(3,3)$ point graph.  Pass~4874 closes the full pair algebra on the
$120$-set.  Besides the identity relation, the four symmetric relations have
valencies
\[
  2,\qquad27,\qquad36,\qquad54,
\]
and unordered-pair counts
\[
  120,\qquad1620,\qquad2160,\qquad3240.
\]
They close under matrix multiplication to a commutative imprimitive
four-class association scheme.

With relation order $(R_0,R_1,R_2,R_3,R_4)$ equal to identity, fiber, first
nonedge refinement, $Q(4,3)$-adjacency lift, and second nonedge refinement,
the first eigenmatrix is
\[
P=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&27&36&54\\
1&2&-3&6&-6\\
1&2&3&-12&6\\
1&-1&9&0&-9\\
1&-1&-3&0&3
\end{pmatrix},
\]
with primitive multiplicities
\[
  1,\qquad24,\qquad15,\qquad20,\qquad60.
\]
The fiber-constant $40$-space is exactly the first three primitive sectors.
On it, the $2160$-pair adjacency-lift relation has eigenvalues
\[
  36,\quad6,\quad-12,
\]
which are three times the common SRG eigenvalues $12,2,-4$ of the
$Q(4,3)$ line-intersection graph.  The remaining $80$ transverse directions
split as $20+60$ over characteristic zero and are annihilated by the
adjacency-lift relation.

The refinement is also exact over quotient nonedges.  Every adjacent pair of
three-element fibers contributes all nine cross pairs, i.e. a $K_{3,3}$.  For
each of the $540$ nonedges of $Q(4,3)$---equivalently each pair of disjoint
$W(3,3)$ lines---the nine cross pairs instead split canonically as
\[
  3+6,
\]
where the three-pair relation is a perfect matching and the other relation is
its six-edge complement.  Thus the Steiner layer refines adjacency and
disjointness of the W33 \emph{line} carrier, not the W33 point graph.
