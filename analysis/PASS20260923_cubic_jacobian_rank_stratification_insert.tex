\subsection*{A single cubic background reaches rank 78}

For the canonical signed $E_6$ cubic on the matter block,
\[
 [e_{i,a},e_{j,b}]=d_{ijk}\epsilon_{abc}\,\bar e_{k,c},
\qquad
D_v(x):=[v,x],
\]
the Jacobian $D_v:g_1\to g_2$ is represented, after the paired root-label
identification, by an $81\times81$ skew-symmetric integer matrix and satisfies
$D_vv=0$.

Exact fraction-free elimination gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
v & \operatorname{rank}D_v & \dim\ker D_v\\ \hline
e_0 & 20 & 61\\
(1,\ldots,1) & 54 & 27\\
(1,2,\ldots,81) & 54 & 27\\
(1^2,2^2,\ldots,81^2) & 78 & 3
\end{array}
\]
in the canonical $(e6id,\text{external phase})$ coordinate ordering.

Hence one explicit superposed matter background already supplies cubic tangent
rank $78$, exceeding by $24$ the independently proved $54$-dimensional
symmetry-retyping requirement.  Raw nonlinear rank is therefore no longer the
obstruction.  What remains is the sharper alignment problem: the rank-$78$
image has not yet been identified with, or projected surjectively onto, the
specific $54$ Fourier coordinates that must change representation type.

The repeated value $54$ for the uniform and linear backgrounds is not promoted
to an intertwiner statement: equality with the independent equivariance deficit
is presently a dimension collision only.
