\begin{remark}[Materialized $Z_2/S_3$ comparison]
\label{rem:materialized-z2-s3-comparison}
Running the BT1208--BT1210 pipeline on the uploaded repository snapshot materializes
the first actual edgewise comparison between the reconstructed center-quad
transport voltage and the Witting packet local $S_3$ matching sign.  For the
first deterministic packet-to-centerquad isomorphism the raw table is
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \operatorname{sgn}_{S_3}=0 & \operatorname{sgn}_{S_3}=1\\
\hline
Z_2=0 & 211 & 203\\
Z_2=1 & 155 & 151
\end{array}
\]
and the canonical-gauge table is
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & \operatorname{sgn}_{S_3}=0 & \operatorname{sgn}_{S_3}=1\\
\hline
Z_2=0 & 220 & 194\\
Z_2=1 & 146 & 160.
\end{array}
\]
Thus the edge-level $S_3$ sign is not equal to either raw or canonical $Z_2$ on
this labelled alignment.  More strongly, sampling $16$ packet-to-centerquad graph
isomorphisms produced $4$ distinct table signatures, so the comparison is not yet
isomorphism-invariant.  The conclusion is an obstruction: the $720$-edge packet
sign and the center-quad voltage live on isomorphic carriers, but the isomorphism
itself must be geometrically fixed before a theorem relating the two signs can be
claimed.  The BT748 half-fiber schema is now materialized, but the full
$51840$-row presentation-pair table remains a separate instrumented BT748 run.
\end{remark}
