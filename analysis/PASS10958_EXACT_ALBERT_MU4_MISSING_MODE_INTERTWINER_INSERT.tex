$U=R/2$ with $R^2=-4I_{16}$, hence $U^2=-I_{16}$ and $U^4=I_{16}$.

$(x^2+1)^8$, so the Peirce $16$ has eight $+i$ modes and eight $-i$
modes.  The exact Spin(8) chirality involution $\Chi$ anticommutes with

Pass-10954 clock characters $k=2,6$.  The producer constructs an explicit
rank-two embedding $W_2$ and an invertible $16\times16$ matrix $W$ such that
\[
UW_2=W_2A_2,\qquad \Chi W_2=W_2
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\]
and, globally, $U$ is eight copies of $A_2$ while chirality is eight copies
of the swap matrix.  Thus the Albert Peirce $16$ is exactly eight copies of
the missing $\mu_4$ clock module over $\mathbb Q(i)$.  Its square
$U^2=-I_{16}$ is the Pass-10950 $2\pi$ Spin(9) matter-parity sign.
This is a cyclic-module intertwiner only; no full $GL(2,3)$ embedding into
Spin(9) is claimed.
