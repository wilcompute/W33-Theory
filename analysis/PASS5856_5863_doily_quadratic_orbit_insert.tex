\subsection*{Doily quadratic orbits, the six-letter model, and the binary Radon radical}

The binary saturated Radon maps sharpen the integral firewall. On the common
nine-dimensional rational constituent,
\[
\operatorname{im}(R^T\bmod2)=\operatorname{im}(D\bmod2)
=\operatorname{rad}(A_3\otimes A_3\bmod2),\qquad \dim=5,
\]
while $H^T\bmod2$ has rank two and lands inside the three-dimensional heavy
radical. The three point/heavy radical generators map under $R^T$ and $D$ to
one common all-ones $3\times3$ vector, but are killed by $H^T$.

On $M_2(\mathbb F_2)$ the determinant polar form has sixteen quadratic
refinements, split as ten hyperbolic grid forms and six elliptic ovoid forms.
Their Boolean chirps satisfy
\[
\widehat{(-1)^{q_v}}=+4(-1)^{q_v}
\]
for the ten grids and
\[
\widehat{(-1)^{q_v}}=-4(-1)^{q_v}
\]
for the six ovoids. Thus the grid/ovoid hyperplane split is exactly the
positive/negative bent-Walsh sign split.

The six ovoids reconstruct the classical six-letter model internally. Any pair
of ovoids meets in one doily point. For a fixed grid, connect two ovoid labels
when that point lies in the grid. The resulting graph is $K_{3,3}$, so each
grid determines one unordered $3+3$ partition of the six labels, and all ten
partitions occur. The $720$ symplectic automorphisms induce $720$ distinct
permutations of the six ovoids, giving
\[
\boxed{Sp(4,2)\cong S_6};
\]
the ten-grid action is the induced action on $3+3$ partitions.

Every two grids meet in five points forming $K_1\vee2K_2$, equivalently two
isotropic lines through one center. All $\binom{10}{2}=45$ intersections are
distinct and coincide with the $15\binom32=45$ pointed unordered pairs of
doily lines through a point.

The unit-difference rook graph contains the determinant grid literally as the
distance-two subconstituent at zero:
\[
L_2(4)\supset L_2(3)=\operatorname{SRG}(9,4,1,2).
\]
The six neighbours of zero are the units and the nine distance-two vertices
are the rank-one matrices. The $72$-element determinant stabilizer is the full
row/column/ruling symmetry of this $3\times3$ subgrid.

Under the explicit product-coordinate relabeling, the three coordinates
removed in the simplex $[15,4,8]\to[12,4,6]$ puncture form one of exactly two
projective lines wholly contained in the six unit points. Both are
nonisotropic, so the puncture does not delete a doily context.
