\begin{remark}[Global $K3$ orientation convention]
\label{rem:k3-global-orientation}
The paper uses the complex/hyperkahler orientation of Ricci-flat $K3$.  In this
orientation the signature is $\tau=-16$ and the normalized Weyl split is
\[
  W_+^{\rm norm}=0,\qquad W_-^{\rm norm}=24.
\]
The opposite orientation changes $\tau$ to $+16$ and swaps the two entries.  All
subsequent formulas use the $\tau=-16$ convention unless explicitly stated
otherwise.
\end{remark}
