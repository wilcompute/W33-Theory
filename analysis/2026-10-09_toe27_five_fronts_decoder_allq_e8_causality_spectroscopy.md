## 2. A genuine ALL-q CSS existence theorem; why it still falls short of scalable FT

For each prime power q≥2, the classical symplectic quadrangle W(3,q) has m=(q+1)(q²+1) points, m lines, `V=2m` Levi vertices, `E=(q+1)²(q²+1)` incidence edges, degree `k=q+1` and `rank H1=E-V+1=q⁴`. By the **Solomon–Tits theorem** for rank-two spherical buildings, the q⁴ apartment eight-cycles through a fixed chamber provide a Z-basis for H1. Therefore weight-eight apartment cycle vectors span the cycle space over F3.

Each incidence edge belongs to exactly q⁴ length-eight apartments, by four independent q-way choices and the unique generalized-quadrangle collinearity completion, giving total apartment count `E*q⁴/8`. q=2 yields 45 flags,90 apartments, H1 rank16; q=3 yields 160 flags,1620 apartments, H1 rank81. This *identity itself* follows from classical incidence geometry, not a new physical observation.

Pick a matching F of r disjoint Levi edges, `1<=r<=8`, `2r<=q+1`. Since the connected regular edge-transitive Levi graph has minimum cut `q+1`, indicator cochain f is not a vertex coboundary. Some apartment c0 has pairing `f·c0 !=0` in F3. From the q⁴ apartment basis (through a fixed chamber) construct q⁴−1 independent Wilson cycles `c_i-(f·c_i)/(f·c0)*c0`. Each has support size **≤16**, lies in the cycle hyperplane ker f, and their span is exactly ker f. Pair with V−1 independent degree-(q+1) Gauss operators. Since q⁴=E−V+1, independent stabilizer rank (q⁴−1)+(V−1)=E−1: **one logical qutrit**.

A nontrivial ternary vertex gradient has support at least `q+1>=2r`, so the nontrivial X logical cochain f plus any such gradient has weight≥r. The direct f has weight r, hence d_X=r. A Z logical is a cycle (girth8) and an excluded apartment with pairing ≠0 supplies weight8, hence d_Z=8. **Exact family theorem:**

`[[(q+1)²(q²+1),1,r]]_3` for every prime-power q with `1<=r<=8` and `2r<=q+1`.

In particular q=16 yields `[[74273,1,8]]_3`. The q=3 finite code `[[160,1,8]]_3` from Round26 is **stronger than what the all-q minimum-cut bound alone certifies**; retaining this distinction prevents misstatement.

**Crucial negative result:** The theorem gives *bounded maximum distance eight* and *rate k/n→0*, hence does **not** provide a positive asymptotic rate or a robust extensive-error threshold. At q→∞ the available Gauss generators also grow in weight q+1. This is a rigorous finite-geometry existence theorem, not a scalable quantum-memory architecture.

## 3. Substantially broader E8 SU9 embedding obstruction: ANY reducible 9D carrier

`multiplicity of St81 in the E8 restricted adjoint =0`

for **every reducible nine-dimensional SL9 carrier** of G, in particular PSp4(3). This upgrades a specific 6+3 obstruction to a complete *reducible-SL9* obstruction.

## 4. A concrete 4D spacetime failure mode: exact normalized W(3,q) heat dimension

The W(3,q) Levi graph diameter is exactly **four**, independent of q. Its adjacency spectrum is

`±(q+1)^1, ±sqrt(2q)^(mr), 0^(2ms)`,

`mr=q(q+1)²/2`, `ms=q(q²+1)/2`. Define the normalized graph Laplacian `L_q=I-A/(q+1)` and heat trace per graph vertex `H_q(t)=Tr(e^{-tL_q})/[2(q+1)(q²+1)]`. Taking any fixed positive t as q grows yields exactly

`H_q(t) -> e^{-t},`

because the overwhelming nontrivial Laplacian eigenvalues converge to1, while the single λ=0 and λ=2 channels have negligible normalized multiplicities.

The standard scale-dependent spectral dimension is `d_s(t)=-2 d(log H)/d(log t)`, so the exact fixed-time limit is

`d_s(t) -> 2t.`

At t=1,2,4 the limit is **2,4,8**: a 'four-dimensional' reading at t=2 is a **single accidental crossing, not a plateau across scales**. Numerical q=1009 checks give about 1.998,3.992,7.968. This is a rigorous obstruction to **raw normalized Levi adjacency diffusion** producing a four-dimensional continuum; it does not exclude a different kinetic operator, added long-range structure, an external toroidal factor, or a real emergent relativistic field theory.

## 5. Exact eight-site spectroscopy with disorder, and a key readout correction

Starting from the Round26 full 36-state, two-boson attractive C8 Bose–Hubbard Hamiltonian at `U/t=32`, allow **site-specific onsite detuning** and **link-specific hopping errors** on all eight edges. Recompute all 36 eigenstates for **72 synthetic independent uniform noise draws at each of `σ/t =0,0.0005,0.001,0.002,0.005`**. Evaluate the smallest bound-pair band gap, degenerate-band splitting, initial doublon probability weights and leakage out of the eight-bound-level sector.

Ideal bound-pair first gap is **0.03634944 t**, and initial |2_0> scattering leakage ≈0.3872%. Across 72 seeds at each level, maximal *relative* first-gap shift was roughly:
- 0.05%/t detuning/hopping errors: **0.063%**;
- 0.10%/t: **0.364%**;
- 0.20%/t: **0.546%**;
- 0.50%/t: **4.04%**.

These are independent uniform synthetic samples, **not guaranteed tolerances** for systematic or correlated disorder. Assuming `t/h=5 MHz` gives a C8 gap frequency ≈**0.18175MHz**; ten beating periods need about **55 μs**, longer than the full 80-site W33 example at the same U/t.

**Necessary experimental readout correction:** the complex phase-sensitive doublon Loschmidt amplitude `A(t)=<2_0|e^{-iHt}|2_0>` has Fourier components at the actual band energies. The directly measurable *return probability* `|A(t)|²`, in contrast, contains **pairwise energy differences** between bands, not generally the original five band energies. A Fourier transform of probability-only return therefore cannot automatically reconstruct the five absolute band centers. Require interferometric quadrature/phase reference or independent transition spectroscopy. This is a meaningful improvement to the earlier blueprint and a possible source of false-positive peak identifications.

Real implementation still needs eight tunable nonlinear bosonic sites, independent hopping and onsite calibration, two-boson loading, coherence, resolving scattered vs bound pairs and calibrated phase-sensitive detection. **No physical prototype has been constructed or tested** in this pass.
