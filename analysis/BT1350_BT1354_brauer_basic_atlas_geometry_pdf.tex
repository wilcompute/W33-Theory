\section{Characteristic-five Brauer tree, modular basic algebras, and Atlas conjugacy}
\label{sec:bt1350-bt1354-brauer-basic-atlas}
\begin{theorem}[Exact modular representation closure]
Let $G=W(E_6)\cong U_4(2){:}2$, and let $\mathcal H_{\mathbb Z}$ be the primitive integral form of the literal $26$-dimensional $G/S_5$ Hecke algebra. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item In characteristic five, $G$ has two defect-one blocks with identical Brauer tree
\[1\;---\;24\;---\;81\;---\;64\;---\;6,\]
whose modular edges have dimensions $1,23,58,6$. The exceptional multiplicity is one; the $23$- and $58$-dimensional simples meet at the ordinary $81$-vertex.
\item The canonical Pass~1345 basic algebras of $\mathcal H_{\mathbb F_p}$ have exact Gabriel quivers and homogeneous associated-graded relations. Pass~1351 additionally freezes all projective radical and socle layers and the first minimal projective-resolution step for every simple.
\item The Repsn-afforded degree-$20$ model with standard-generator traces $(10,-1)$ is rationally conjugate to the literal carrier model. The simultaneous intertwiner space is one-dimensional and contains an invertible rational matrix $X$.
\item The $120$-element length-four selector orbit is intrinsically the set of pairs consisting of a totally isotropic line of $W(3,3)$ and one of the three perfect matchings of its four points. Thus $120=40\cdot3$, the line stabilizer is $1296=54\cdot24$, and the cycle stabilizer is $432=54\cdot8$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first assertion is read from the genuine CTblLib ordinary and characteristic-five Brauer tables for $U_4(2){:}2$; their decomposition matrices are the incidence matrices of the displayed trees. The second is obtained from the literal reduced Hecke multiplication tensor and primitive idempotents. For the third, Repsn affords the observed degree-$20$ character and exact rational nullspace calculation solves $C_{\rm Repsn}X=XC_{\rm carrier}$ and $D_{\rm Repsn}X=XD_{\rm carrier}$. For the fourth, direct polar-space enumeration gives forty totally isotropic lines, induced line action $S_4$, pointwise kernel $54$, and dihedral cycle stabilizer $D_8<S_4$.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Carrier firewall.} The ambient group Brauer tree and the characteristic-five Hecke basic algebra are different objects. The former is a four-edge defect-one tree for the group algebra; the latter is the explicitly certified Hecke corner. No identification is made beyond the stated representation-theoretic transport.
