\section{Characteristic-five Brauer tree, modular basic algebras, and Atlas conjugacy}
\label{sec:bt1350-bt1354-brauer-basic-atlas}

\begin{theorem}[Exact modular representation closure]
Let $G=W(E_6)\cong U_4(2){:}2$, and let $\mathcal H_{\mathbb Z}$ be the primitive
integral form of the literal $26$-dimensional $G/S_5$ Hecke algebra.
Then the following statements hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item In characteristic five, $G$ has two defect-one blocks with identical
Brauer tree
\[
1\;---\;24\;---\;81\;---\;64\;---\;6,
\]
whose modular edges have dimensions $1,23,58,6$, respectively.  The exceptional
multiplicity is one.  In particular, the $23$- and $58$-dimensional modular
simples are adjacent and meet at the ordinary $81$-dimensional vertex.
\item The basic algebras of $\mathcal H_{\mathbb F_p}$ for $p=2,3,5$ have
radical-power dimensions
\[
(21,17,13,7,2,0),\qquad(22,16,10,4,0),\qquad(6,2,0).
\]
Their exact Gabriel quivers, homogeneous associated-graded defining relations,
projective radical and socle layers, and minimal projective-resolution prefixes
are frozen in the accompanying certificates.
\item The Repsn-afforded degree-$20$ representation with standard-generator
traces $(10,-1)$ is rationally conjugate to the literal rank-$20$ carrier model.
The intertwiner space is one-dimensional, and the committed matrix $X$ satisfies
\[
C_{\rm Repsn}X=XC_{\rm carrier},\qquad
D_{\rm Repsn}X=XD_{\rm carrier},\qquad \det X\ne0.
\]
\item The $120$-element length-four selector orbit has the intrinsic polar-space
factorization
\[
120=40\times3:
\]
one totally isotropic line of $W(3,3)$, together with one of the three perfect
matchings of its four points.  The line stabilizer has order $1296=54\cdot24$,
and the cycle stabilizer is the preimage of $D_8<S_4$, of order
$432=54\cdot8$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first assertion is read from the genuine CTblLib ordinary and
characteristic-five Brauer tables for $U_4(2){:}2$: both defect-one blocks have
decomposition matrix equal to the incidence matrix of the displayed tree.  The
second assertion follows by reducing the literal Hecke multiplication tensor,
constructing primitive idempotents, and computing $e_iJ^ke_j$, annihilator
socle filtrations, and path-algebra kernels.  For the third assertion, Repsn
affords the observed degree-$20$ character and exact rational nullspace
calculation gives a one-dimensional simultaneous intertwiner space.  Finally,
the fourth assertion follows by enumerating the $40$ totally isotropic lines,
identifying the induced line action as $S_4$, and observing that an unoriented
Hamiltonian cycle on four points is equivalent to one of three opposite-point
matchings.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Carrier firewall.}
The ambient group Brauer tree and the characteristic-five Hecke basic algebra
are different objects.  The former is a four-edge defect-one tree for the group
algebra; the latter is the explicitly certified three-vertex exceptional corner
inside the $26$-dimensional Hecke reduction.  No identification between them is
made beyond the stated representation-theoretic transport.
