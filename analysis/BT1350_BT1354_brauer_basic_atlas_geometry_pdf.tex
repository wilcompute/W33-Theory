\begin{theorem}[Exact modular representation closure]
Let $G=W(E_6)\cong U_4(2){:}2$, and let $\mathcal H_{\mathbb Z}$ be the primitive integral form of the literal $26$-dimensional $G/S_5$ Hecke algebra. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item In characteristic five, $G$ has two defect-one blocks with identical Brauer tree
\[1\;---\;24\;---\;81\;---\;64\;---\;6,\]
whose modular edges have dimensions $1,23,58,6$. The exceptional multiplicity is one; the $23$- and $58$-dimensional simples meet at the ordinary $81$-vertex.
\item The canonical Pass~1345 basic algebras of $\mathcal H_{\mathbb F_p}$ have exact Gabriel quivers and homogeneous associated-graded relations. Pass~1351 independently reconstructs the actual basic algebras $e\mathcal H_{\mathbb F_p}e$, reproduces every projective radical and socle layer, and computes deterministic minimal projective-resolution prefixes. Every stored kernel is exact; a prefix stops before kernel construction whenever the next exact projective cover exceeds the declared dimension ceiling.
\item The $120$-element length-four selector orbit is intrinsically the set of pairs consisting of a totally isotropic line of $W(3,3)$ and one of the three perfect matchings of its four points. Thus $120=40\cdot3$, the line stabilizer is $1296=54\cdot24$, and the cycle stabilizer is $432=54\cdot8$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first assertion is read from the genuine CTblLib ordinary and characteristic-five Brauer tables for $U_4(2){:}2$; their decomposition matrices are the incidence matrices of the displayed trees. The second is obtained directly from the literal reduced Hecke multiplication tensor and lifted primitive idempotents, not from the associated-graded presentation. For the third, direct polar-space enumeration gives forty totally isotropic lines, induced line action $S_4$, pointwise kernel $54$, and dihedral cycle stabilizer $D_8<S_4$.
\end{proof}
