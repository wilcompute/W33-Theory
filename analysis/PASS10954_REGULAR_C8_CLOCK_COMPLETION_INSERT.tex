\subsection{Pass 10954: the regular $C_8$ clock lives on the oriented $A_2^4$ cover}

The minimal doubled Pin clock of Pass~10953 has $C_8$ character exponents
$\{0,1,3,4,5,7\}$, so it is exactly the regular $C_8$ representation with
the $k=2,6$ modes, $\{+i,-i\}$, deleted.  Restoring those two modes gives
trace character $[8,0,0,0,0,0,0,0]$ and eight mutually orthogonal clock
positions.

The completion is already present objectwise in the repository.  Before the
Pass~7409 quotient $\mathbb F_2^4/\langle1111\rangle$, the sixteen oriented
$A_2^4$ sign states split under the exact clock generator into two disjoint
eight-cycles.  Four ticks act as the global-complement deck transformation
$x\mapsto x+1111$, and eight ticks return to the identity.  Quotienting the
deck map produces the already-certified two four-cycles on the eight
Eisenstein $W(3,3)$ leaves.

An explicit unitary intertwiner embeds the six-dimensional Pin clock into
either regular eight-cycle with precisely the $k=2,6$ Fourier modes removed.
Those two missing modes are deck-even, not orientation-odd.  Exhausting all
$48$ elements of $GL(2,3)$ further gives an exact shared character:
determinant $+1$ preserves the Fano-hinge orientation bipartition and
determinant $-1$ swaps it.  This is the same determinant $C_2$ that separates
unitary from antiunitary extended-Clifford actions and grades the clock's
CP/non-CP powers.  No continuum time, past/future, or temporal-chirality
identification is inferred.
