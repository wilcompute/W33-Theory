# Pass5691–5698 external prior-art boundary

The packet separates repo-specific finite identities from standard or previously published context.

## Ramanujan 2-lifts

Marcus, Spielman and Srivastava, **Interlacing Families I: Bipartite Ramanujan Graphs of All Degrees**, arXiv:1304.4132, prove the existence of infinite families of regular bipartite Ramanujan graphs of every degree greater than two using good 2-lifts/interlacing polynomials.  Pass5683's infinite-tower existence statement is an application of this theorem once the W33 Levi spectrum is computed; the theorem itself is prior art.  Pass5693's contribution inside this repository is different and finite: it constructs explicit locally balanced signings at the next two W33-derived levels and verifies their spectra.

## Hessian group / order 216 context

Lei Yang, **Hessian polyhedra, invariant theory and Appell hypergeometric partial differential equations**, arXiv:math/0412065, discusses the classical Hessian collineation group of order 216 and related finite unitary reflection groups.  Therefore an order-216 Hessian/complex-projective symmetry context is not new.  The repo-specific statement in Pass5686/5696 is the explicit determinant-area 3-form on the nine affine sites, the resulting exact compact `A2` bracket on the augmentation eight, its Killing form, and the determinant-character twist that restores all 432 affine transformations as Lie-algebra automorphisms.

## Standard gauge-theory structures

Writing a connection curvature schematically as `F=da+1/2[a,a]` and forming a quadratic action with an invariant Lie-algebra form are standard gauge-theory constructions.  Pass5691 does not claim those formulas as novel.  Its repo-specific content is the exact AG(2,3) link/face rank census, the inherited `-54` Killing normalization of the finite affine eight, and the no-go that the pre-existing vertical `Z3` fiber translation acts trivially on the base-adjoint sector.

## What is not claimed

No claim is made here that the finite `su(3)` is already QCD, that a Yang–Mills coupling is predicted, that a finite Ramanujan graph gap is the Yang–Mills mass gap, that the vertical fiber qutrit is three generations, or that the Ramanujan graph metric is physical spacetime.  Those require additional dynamics, representations, scaling limits and/or explicit intertwiners beyond the finite identities in this packet.
