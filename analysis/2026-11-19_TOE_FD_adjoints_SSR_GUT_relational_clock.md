## 1. Enumerate all renormalizable singlet invariants—then actually impose F and D

Exhaustively enumerate all monomials (allowing repeated fields) of degree ≤3 whose full three-component Abelian charge vanishes. There are **zero linear, exactly two quadratic and exactly three cubic** monomials:

    AB, DE;

The most general **gauge-invariant renormalizable superpotential using these eight fields, with no neutral fields**, is therefore

    W=m AB+n DE+lambda AFE+kappa1 CEG1+kappa2 CEG2.

This is a statement of gauge permission, **not** a claim that all coefficients are physically generated by the singular compactification. ### New F/D-flatness theorem for the third family

For either `kappa1 !=0` or `kappa2 !=0` the equation `F_Gi=partial W/partial Gi=kappai C E=0` forces **C=0 or E=0**. Independently, take the Abelian charge functional `ell=(1,1,0)`. It annihilates A and B, is negative on E, and is *strictly positive* on every remaining charged singlet C,D,F,G1,G2. Its moment-map equation, with canonical positive Kähler and zero FI, is

    D_ell=|C|²+3|D|²-3|E|²+3|F|²+2|G1|²+2|G2|²=0.

If E=0, all nonnegative terms vanish and in particular C=0. Thus **every simultaneous F- and D-flat vacuum has C=S13=0 when any kappai is nonzero**, irrespective of m,n,lambda and the other field magnitudes. A source sector which insists on `<S13> !=0` as its one-spurion third-family diagonal mass generator must forbid both cubic CEG couplings or invoke modified physics.

If both gauge-permitted mass coefficients m and n are nonzero, the full F/D system is even more restrictive. `F_B=m A=0`, `F_D=n E=0`, `F_A=m B+lambda E F=0` give A=B=E=0. Then `D_ell=0` sets C=D=F=G1=G2=0. **The origin is the unique supersymmetric singlet vacuum in that eight-field truncation.**

Conversely, the earlier D-flat loop `S13*S34*S14_conj` does exist as a moment-map zero, but F-flatness forbids that all-three-nonzero point with a generated nonzero kappa. The actual topology-controlled interaction coefficient matters. Exceptional cases include a vanishing coupling, FI term, more charged fields/neutral mediators, noncanonical dynamics, singular effects or supersymmetry breaking. A generic positive Kähler metric does not always reduce to the canonical sum used here.

The symmetry-allowed one-singlet operators from the preceding commit were

    |M_X| <= (|M_octet|+|M_triplet|)/2 = 1.108531296e7 GeV.

It is **not** at MU. Since the benchmark has two light triplets and one light octet, any simple assignment into two unmixed adjoints puts at least one light triplet and octet in the same adjoint. ## 4. Gravity: an exact *anisotropic* Einstein-scalar solution with a relational clock

The prior FRW test used isotropic homogeneous spacetime. A stronger independent GR benchmark is Bianchi I with a massless canonical scalar:

    ds²=-dt² + sum_i t^(2p_i) (dx_i)²,
    phi(t)=sigma log(t),  p=(1/2,1/3,1/6),
    sum_i p_i=1, sum_i p_i²=7/18,
    sigma²=11/18.

The Einstein Hamiltonian constraint is `sum_{i<j} H_i H_j=(1/2) phi_dot²`, directional equations are `Hdot_i+theta H_i=0`, and the scalar Klein–Gordon equation is `phi_ddot+theta phi_dot=0`. All hold *exactly* for this solution; the code evaluates the residuals at six proper times to floating-point precision. This adds two anisotropic gravitational homogeneous degrees of freedom but still does **not** derive propagating gravitons, 3+1 local diffeomorphism constraints or a quantum theory.

An instructive bridge to `time = number of information exchanges`: choose exchange-coordinate n=ln(t). Then the *same spacetime* reads `a_i(n)=exp(p_i n)`, `phi(n)=sigma n`, but its proper-time lapse is `N(n)=dt/dn=exp(n)`. **Counting uniform n-steps is NOT the same as physical proper time.** The scalar furnishes gauge-independent relational observables

    a_i(phi)=exp(p_i phi/sigma).

A fundamental update theory must derive a physical relational clock and lapse, not simply identify raw iteration number with invariant proper time. This is an application of standard GR reparametrization invariance, not a proof of emergent time.
