# BT1526 Csaszar All-five Tetra Audit

All five Csaszar realizations share the same K7 triangular torus combinatorics:

- 7 vertices;
- 21 edges;
- 14 triangular faces;
- every edge in two faces;
- every vertex in six triangles.

Each has 84 flags, split as 12 pointed-vertex flags plus 72 active six-shell flags.

The two pointed 12-flag stars from the Csaszar/Szilassi pair give 24 flags, matching the tetrahedron/K4 flag count.

This is an exact combinatorial and flag-count bridge, not a metric equivalence to a tetrahedron.
