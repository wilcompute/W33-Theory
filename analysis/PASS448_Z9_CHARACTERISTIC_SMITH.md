# Pass 448 — exact characteristic-primary Smith group over \(\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\)

For the flat-section Heisenberg graph over \(R=\mathbb Z/9\mathbb Z\), the graph has
\(729\) vertices, degree \(80\), and a \(728\times728\) reduced Laplacian.

A direct \(3\)-adic elimination over \(\mathbb Z_3\) gives the exact exponent census

\[
(e=0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8)=(99,154,162,80,10,205,0,11,7).
\]

Hence

\[
K_{(3)}\cong
(\mathbb Z/3)^{154}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/9)^{162}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/27)^{80}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/81)^{10}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/243)^{205}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/2187)^{11}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/6561)^{7}.
\]

There is an exact gap at exponent six: no \(\mathbb Z/729\) factor occurs. The total valuation is \(1916\), matching the Matrix--Tree valuation from the rational spectrum.

The same algorithm reproduces the known field control at \(q=3\): exponent counts \((10,8,1,7)\), exactly the Pass 437 decomposition.

After welding the Pass 440 prime-to-characteristic layers, the full critical group is

\[
\begin{aligned}
K\cong{}&
(\mathbb Z/3)^{154}\oplus(\mathbb Z/9)^{162}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/27)^{31}\oplus(\mathbb Z/54)^6\\
&\oplus(\mathbb Z/216)^{43}\oplus(\mathbb Z/648)^{10}
\oplus(\mathbb Z/1944)^7\\
&\oplus(\mathbb Z/19440)^{198}\oplus
(\mathbb Z/174960)^{11}\oplus

\end{aligned}
\]

**Boundary.** This is an exact finite theorem for the first nilpotent rung. It is not yet a closed symbolic characteristic-primary formula for every chain length.
