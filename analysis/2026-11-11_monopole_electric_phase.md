# Electric phase quantization and light-channel traces of the W33 monopole

The primitive magnetic cocharacter is m = diag(1,0,0,−1,0). The phase character of its centralizer C = S(U(1) × U(2) × U(1) × U(1)) is z1/z4. Its connected kernel fixes the color-invariant core, so the framed bosonic phase is an angle χ of period 2π. The lift generated by m/2 reaches κ = exp(iπm) at that period. The endpoint belongs to the kernel and squares to the identity. The replay evaluates this endpoint on the actual angular weights. It acts by −1 on every one of the 32 lowest fermion waves, split equally between the two flux signs. In the m/2 lift, a state associated to a representation of the kernel has periodic or antiperiodic phase coefficients according to the action of κ. The canonical phase momentum is integral for the trivial kernel representation and half-integral for a κ-odd dressing. The shift refers to this lift and equivariance condition. It does not compute additional Berry phases or the dynamics of the dressing. [Hilbert spaces with dyon charge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01924)

Both proposed rational boundary maps pass the endpoint sign test. In outgoing particle coordinates each reflecting block reverses its summed occupation and the four hole slots also reverse their signs. The total outgoing occupation is therefore the negative of the total incoming occupation, so an integer incoming occupation sum has the same κ sign on both sides. The phase holonomy alone leaves both boundary maps possible.

## The local theta term

Use the sign convention S_theta = θ/(8π²) times the integral of the basic trace of F wedge F. The primitive embedded SU(2) has trace indices 6 on the 27 and 24 on the 78, so the basic trace restricts to Tr_27/6 and gives the squared norm 2 on m. The electric phase variation is F_0i = χdot D_iΩ with Ω at infinity equal to m/2. The Bianchi identity makes the theta integral a surface term. The asymptotic magnetic flux 2πm then gives S_theta = θ Δχ/(2π). A full phase turn contributes one unit to this local topological integral.

The rigid collective Lagrangian has kinetic term I χdot²/2. In the trivial kernel sector it therefore gives kinetic charge p−θ/(2π) and electric energy (p−θ/(2π))²/(2I), with p integral. ## What the light fermions test

A lowest wave of magnetic pairing n = ±1 has charge n/2 under the phase lift, and the sphere index assigns its radial chirality by the sign of n. In conventions where a right-moving covariant number current has divergence q E/(2π), integrating the radial electric field of a full phase turn gives the weight n²/2 in the bulk anomaly trace. Summing gives 13 from chiral matter and 3 from gauginos for the formal common occupation trace. The corresponding six traces weighted by the four Cartan charges, 3B and L all vanish. The B and L labels are formal in this calculation. A finite-domain level crossing count needs the radial boundary condition and the massive channels.
