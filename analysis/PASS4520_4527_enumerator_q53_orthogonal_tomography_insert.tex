\paragraph{Pass 4521: the $Q(5,3)=GQ(3,9)$ apartment code is exactly $[102060,279,1458]$.}
For coefficient support $S$ in the $280$-vertex line graph, write
$m=|S|$, $e=e(S)$, $p_3=p_3(S)$ and $c_4=c_4(S)$.  Pass~4515 gives
\[
 w=1458m-12\binom m2-150e+36p_3-8c_4.
\]
The line graph is $SRG(280,36,8,4)$ with nontrivial eigenvalues $8,-4$, so
\[
 \frac{m^2}{14}-2m\le e\le4m+\frac{m^2}{20}.
\]
If $t_{uv}\le4$ counts common neighbours inside $S$ for a nonedge $uv$, then
$p_3=\sum t_{uv}$ and $2c_4=\sum\binom{t_{uv}}2$, whence
$36p_3-8c_4\ge30p_3$.  Also
\[
 p_3=\sum_x\binom{d_x}{2}-3T
 \ge \frac{2e^2}{m}-9e,
\]
because $3T\le8e$.  Therefore
\[
 w\ge1458m-12\binom m2-150e+30\max\!\left(0,\frac{2e^2}{m}-9e\right).
\]
Minimizing this convex piecewise expression on the spectral edge interval gives
\[
\begin{cases}
864m-\frac{27}{2}m^2,&2\le m\le10,\\
m(789-6m),&10\le m\le91,\\
\dfrac{3m(5m^2-868m+41552)}{49},&91\le m\le140.
\end{cases}
\]
Its minimum on $2\le m\le140$ is $1674>1458$.  Global complement lets every
nonzero codeword use $m\le140$, while the $280$ support-one line-star words all
have weight $1458$.  Hence
\[
\boxed{C_{Q(5,3)}=[102060,279,1458],\qquad A_{1458}=280.}
\]

\paragraph{Pass 4523: primitive-six zeta tomography works for every thick $GQ(s,t)$.}
For the signed primitive length-six Ihara polynomial, the degree-two Walsh
coefficient for two geometric lines is
\[
\boxed{c_{\rm dis}=s(s+1)(s-1)^2}
\]
when they are disjoint and
\[
\boxed{c_{\rm adj}=s^2(s-1)^2(2t+1)}
\]
when they intersect.  Indeed a fixed pair belongs to
$\beta=s(s+1)/2$ or $\alpha=s^2t$ apartments, each apartment contributes
$2(s-1)^2$ oriented simple hexagons to the parity pair, and an intersecting pair
has the additional $s^2(s-1)^2$ triangle--triangle figure-eight family.
Therefore
\[
 c_{\rm adj}-c_{\rm dis}=s(s-1)^2(2st-1)>0
\]
for every thick generalized quadrangle, and the degree-two primitive-six Walsh
matrix reconstructs the line-intersection graph exactly.

\paragraph{Pass 4524: $Q(5,3)$ falsifies a naive nine-sheet prism extrapolation.}

six-line indicator $b$ by $b\mapsto A_*b$ gives
\[
\boxed{544320\text{ distinct images, all of weight }104.}
\]

the XOR of all ten rung pairs is in $\ker A_*$, so a three-rung prism has the
same image as its complementary seven-rung fan.  This is the precise mechanism

\paragraph{Pass 4526 (outside the box): the odd-$t$ fan-kernel theorem explains W33's exceptional collapse.}
