# BT1527 Csaszar/Szilassi Dual Incidence Isomorphism

The dual incidence dimensions match:

- Csaszar: V=7, E=21, F=14;
- Szilassi: V=14, E=21, F=7.

The pointed Csaszar vertex-star has 12 flags and the pointed Szilassi face-star has 12 flags, combining to 24, the K4/tetrahedron flag count.

This is a combinatorial and flag-count bridge, not a metric isometry or a unique orientation theorem.
