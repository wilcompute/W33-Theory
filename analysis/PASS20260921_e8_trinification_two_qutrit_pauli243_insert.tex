\subsection*{Trinification supplies the internal $H_{27}$ needed for the two-qutrit Pauli group}

The hostile-control theorem excludes the regular Pass~369 Weyl $H_{27}$ from a center-gluing construction, because its center is not $Z(E_6)$. A different internal Heisenberg group is supplied by trinification:
\[
E_6\supset (SU(3)_1\times SU(3)_2\times SU(3)_3)/\Delta Z_3,
\]
with
\[
27=(3,\bar3,1)\oplus(1,3,\bar3)\oplus(\bar3,1,3).
\]

For center exponents $(a,b,c)$, the three nonets acquire phases
\[
(a-b,\ b-c,\ -a+c)\pmod3.
\]
The diagonal $(1,1,1)$ is the quotient kernel, while
\[
\boxed{z_{E_6}\sim(2,1,0)}
\]
acts as $(1,1,1)$ and therefore as the same scalar $\omega$ on the full $27$.

With the qutrit convention $ZX=\omega XZ$, define
\[
X_{\rm int}=(X,X,I),\qquad
Z_{\rm int}=(Z^2,Z,I).
\]
Then
\[
[Z_{\rm int},X_{\rm int}]
=(\omega^2I,\omega I,I)=z_{E_6},
\]
so these generate an internal $H_{27}$ whose center is literally $Z(E_6)$.

The physical external-$A_2$ theorem supplies a second $H_{27}$ with center
\[
z_{\rm ext}=z_{\rm FI}=\exp(2\pi iQ_\psi/3).
\]
The subgroup
\[
(E_6\times SU(3)_{\rm ext})/
\langle(z_{E_6},z_{\rm ext}^{-1})\rangle
\subset E_8
\]
identifies these centers. Since the two factor groups commute before quotienting, their images form
\[
\boxed{H_{27}\circ H_{27}=3_+^{1+4}},
\qquad
\boxed{|\,\cdot\,|=243}.
\]
The explicit quotient verifier checks exponent $3$, center and derived subgroup of order $3$, and projective quotient $\mathbb F_3^4$ of order $81$.

This is an exact finite subgroup theorem. It does not prove a D/F-flat vacuum, observed family assignment, or hardware implementation.
