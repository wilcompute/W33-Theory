\subsection{The Suzuki outer involution is determinant-reversing on the six-qutrit Heisenberg carrier}
\label{sec:20260917-suzuki-outer-heisenberg-sector-swap}

The six-qutrit carrier has extraspecial Heisenberg group
\[
 H=3^{1+12},\qquad H/Z(H)\cong\mathbb F_3^{12},
\]
with two faithful irreducible Schr\"odinger sectors
\[
 \rho_r: H\to U(729),\qquad r\in\{1,2\},
 \qquad \rho_r(z)=\omega^r I.
\]
The constructive Suzuki outer completion supplies a ternary symplectic
similitude of multiplier $-1$.  Abstractly, any such similitude satisfies
\[
 \Omega(gv,gw)=-\Omega(v,w),
\]
so its lift $\tau$ to $H$ sends the center to its inverse,
\[
 \boxed{\tau(z)=z^{-1}}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{\rho_r\circ\tau\simeq\rho_{-r}},
 \qquad 1\leftrightarrow2.
\]

This immediately gives a representation-theoretic firewall.  No
complex-linear similarity can implement $\tau$ inside a single faithful sector:
similarity preserves the scalar $\rho_r(z)=\omega^rI$, whereas $\tau$ requires
$\omega^{-r}I$.  In the standard qutrit model
\[
 X|j\rangle=|j+1\rangle,
 \qquad Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,\omega,\omega^2),
\]
complex conjugation gives
\[
 X\mapsto X,\qquad Z\mapsto Z^{-1},\qquad
 \omega\mapsto\omega^{-1},
\]
and therefore realizes the determinant-reversing operation antiunitarily.
On the doubled carrier
\[
 \rho_1\oplus\rho_2,
\]
a block swap implements the same outer action complex-linearly.

This is the rank-$12$ completion of Passes~5725--5732, where the determinant
character of $GL(2,3)$ separated the unitary Clifford $SL(2,3)$ sector from a
determinant-reversing antiunitary coset.  It also sharpens the current quartic
phase proposal $\omega^{r\det X}$: the central-character label $r$ is exchanged
by the outer symmetry, so the pair $r=1,2$ is the outer-closed object; a
single-$r$ realization explicitly breaks that completion unless an antiunitary
implementation is intended.

There is a suggestive cross-repository consequence but not yet a physical
identification.  The Holotrade order-three GUT audit now finds that both the
Georgi--Glashow and flipped-$SU(5)$ routes require an even-order ingredient for
Standard-Model species to coexist.  The finite six-qutrit carrier now has a
canonical determinant-reversing $C_2$, but no certificate identifies that
$C_2$ with a heterotic space-group/deck element or proves an even-order
orbifold realization.
