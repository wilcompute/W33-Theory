\subsection{Pass 10942: the dark Strange ray opens a metaplectic universal port}

The certified dark H27 ray is Clifford-equivalent to the canonical qutrit
Strange state $|S\rangle=(|1\rangle-|2\rangle)/\sqrt2$.  An exact replay of
the two stabilizer reductions in the repository's Pauli convention gives
\[
 |S\rangle^{\otimes2}\xrightarrow[1/2]{Z_1Z_2=1}|N\rangle,
 \qquad
 |N\rangle^{\otimes2}\xrightarrow[1/4]{\omega X_1X_2=1}
 X^2|U_Z(\pi/3,2\pi/3)\rangle.
\]
The Pauli correction $X^2Z$ yields
$|R\rangle=R|+\rangle=(|0\rangle+|1\rangle-|2\rangle)/\sqrt3$ for
$R=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1)$.  A pure four-Strange batch succeeds with
probability $1/16$; buffering successful Norell intermediates costs $32$
Strange states per $R$ state on average.

Controlled-$X^2$ injection has exact branches
$K_m=3^{-1/2}RX^{-m}$.  Pauli tracking gives the three conjugate reflections
$X^mRX^{-m}$, whose projective closure has order four.  Repeat until the
desired branch succeeds consumes three $R$ resources on average.  Hence the
buffered logical cost is $96$ Strange states per injected $R$ gate.  The gate
is non-Clifford and $|R\rangle$ has maximum stabilizer fidelity $7/9$.
Adding \texttt{R\_INJECT(mode)} to the Pass 10941 $F/P/CZ$ compiler gives
approximately universal seven-qutrit Clifford+$R$ on every mode.

This converter is not a distiller.  For
$\rho_S(p)=(1-p)|S\rangle\!\langle S|+pI/3$, the exact output-equivalent error is
\[
 q(p)=\frac{4p(3p^2-7p+6)}{(p+1)(5p^2-10p+9)}
     =\frac83p+O(p^2),
\]
and $q(p)-p>0$ for $0<p<1$.  A protected or pre-distilled Strange source must
precede the circuit.

Finally, finite stabilizer processing preserves projective amplitude ratios in
$\mathbb Q(\zeta_{12})$, of degree four.  The Pass 10941 $T$ state requires
$\zeta_9$, of degree six, so exact stabilizer-only Strange-to-$T$ conversion is
impossible.  The internal Strange lane closes algebraic universality through
$R$ rather than secretly supplying the ninth-root analyzer resource.  Physical
population of the dark ray, a distillation threshold, and a laboratory
implementation remain open.
