## T2 — The theorem (with an honest refutation en route)

**First guess refuted:** the full S₆ is *2-homogeneous* on the 10 spread lines —
one orbit of size 45 on line pairs (any two triple-splits of a 6-set meet in the
pattern (2,1)). No Petersen at the S₆ level.

**The fix is the icosahedral ladder:** Aut(hemi-dodecahedron) = **A₅ exactly**, and
the spread stabilizer contains the icosahedral core A₅ = 2I/center — the
600-cell level of BT808, whose line orbits are [10, 30] with the 10 being this
very spread. Under that A₅ the 10 lines become the **duads of a 5-set**
(line-stabilizer S₃, order 6), and the 45 line pairs split

```
45 = 15 + 30   (disjoint duads + sharing duads — the Kneser split)
```

The **15-orbit graph on the 10 spread lines is the Petersen graph**
(networkx isomorphism check passes) — i.e. the edge skeleton of the
hemi-dodecahedron, the 57-cell's cell.

Dually, the hidden 6-set of the S₆ action carries K₆ = the skeleton of the
hemi-icosahedron, the 11-cell's cell (6 objects, 15 pairs = the duads again,
10 triple-splits = the spread lines themselves: **(6, 15, 10) is literally the
incidence count of the S₆ action**: 6 hidden points, 15 duads, 10 splits).
