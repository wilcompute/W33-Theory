\paragraph{Pass 4737: the $270$ residue action closes the selected270 router.}
The four-line residues of Passes~4721--4726 form one transitive
$\PSp(4,3)$-set with point stabilizer of order $96$ and permutation rank $12$.
The stabilizer subdegrees are
\[
 1,12,16,48,16,6,24,96,12,12,24,3.
\]
Its orbital centralizer algebra has dimension $12$ and center dimension $9$.
The orbital joining two residues that share exactly two W33 lines is connected,
$12$-regular, and has diameter $4$, with distance shell
\[
 1,12,67,160,30.
\]
Its characteristic polynomial is
\[
 (x-12)(x-8)^{15}(x-2)^{84}(x+1)^{64}(x+4)^{60}(x+6)^6
 (x^2-2x-12)^{20}.
\]
A second orbital has degree $3$ and decomposes into $27$ Petersen graphs of
$10$ vertices.  Each Petersen fiber consists of ten pairwise-disjoint residue
supports and partitions all forty W33 line coordinates.

This is exactly the independently constructed selected270 router of
Pass~4716.  The identification is not inferred from the common order or
spectrum: an order-$96$ selected270 point stabilizer fixes a unique involution
residue, yielding a well-defined $\PSp(4,3)$-equivariant bijection.  Under it,
the $1620$ share-two-lines edges are exactly the selected270 \emph{cold} edges
and the $405$ degree-three edges are exactly its $27$ Petersen \emph{hot}
fibers.  The quotient of the cold graph by the Petersen fibers is
$\operatorname{SRG}(27,10,1,5)$; across each quotient edge the twelve cold
edges split as three disjoint $K_{2,2}$ blocks, recovering the Pass~4716
finite-connection law from involution residues alone.

\paragraph{Pass 4738: the square-root extension mismatch is the normalizer $C_2$.}
For a four-fixing outer square root $h$, put
\[
 K=C_{\PSp(4,3)}(h),\qquad |K|=48.
\]
Exact enumeration gives
\[
 N_{\PSp(4,3)}(K)=H,\qquad |H|=96,
\]
where $H$ is the residue stabilizer.  Therefore $H/K\cong C_2$ is the intrinsic
sheet character.  The full $\PGSp(4,3)$ support-triangle stabilizer and root
centralizer are distinct order-$96$ extensions whose intersection is $K$.
Representatives may be chosen with
\[
 t^2=n^2=1,\qquad n\in H\setminus K,\qquad h=tn,\qquad nt=h^{-1},
\]
so $nhn^{-1}=h^{-1}$.  Thus the outer mismatch is the unique nontrivial
normalizer-quotient twist.  No stronger group-cohomology class is inferred.

\paragraph{Pass 4739: the $q=3$ minimum-shell theorem is exceptional as stated.}
Direct prime-field tests at $q=2,3,5,7$ give binary line-intersection adjacency
ranks
\[
 14,\quad 10,\quad 26,\quad 50,
\]
respectively; the tested odd values equal $q^2+1$.  At $q=3$ the canonical
elliptic projective involution fixes the established four-line minimum word.
At $q=5$, however, an exact MILP proves that the adjacency-kernel minimum is
\[
 \boxed{d=6},
\]
while the canonical elliptic involution fixes eight lines.  Hence the statement
``the involution fixed sets are the complete minimum shell'' does not extend
verbatim to all odd $q$.  At $q=7$ the corresponding involution supplies an
$8$-line kernel word and hence only the certified upper bound $d\leq8$ here;
no exact $q=7$ minimum is claimed.

\paragraph{Pass 4740: the standard Golay-coordinate route is blocked.}
A faithful coordinate-permutation action would embed $\PSp(4,3)$ into the
extended binary Golay coordinate automorphism group $M_{24}$, but
\[
 |\PSp(4,3)|=2^6 3^4 5
 \quad\hbox{does not divide}\quad
 |M_{24}|=2^{10}3^3 5\,7\,11\,23.
\]
The new involution geometry also supplies a canonical $24$-coordinate minor:
delete the sixteen W33 lines fixed by a $45$-class involution.  Puncturing
$H_{10}$ gives
\[
 \boxed{[24,10,6]},
\]
with enumerator
\[
 1+16z^6+87z^8+240z^{10}+336z^{12}+240z^{14}
 +87z^{16}+16z^{18}+z^{24}.
\]
Its weight-$6$ words preclude it from being a subcode of the extended binary
Golay $[24,12,8]$ code.  Shortening gives $[24,2,16]$, while puncturing
$H_{10}^{\perp}$ gives $[24,22,2]$.  This is a coordinate-code no-go only; a
non-coordinate Conway/Leech representation remains open.

\paragraph{Pass 4741: the Petersen fibers are an optimal $27$-round check schedule.}
The $27$ Petersen fibers partition all $270$ residue checks.  Each fiber contains
ten pairwise-disjoint weight-$4$ checks and covers all forty line coordinates
once.  Since every W33 line participates in exactly $27$ residue checks, any
single-use-per-coordinate schedule requires at least $27$ layers, and the
Petersen resolution attains that lower bound.  It is therefore an exact optimal
$\PSp(4,3)$-equivariant schedule.

The $270$ checks have binary rank $30$.  The $[40,10,12]$ protected code has
unique syndromes through weight five.  At weight six the only collisions are
the complementary $6+6$ halves of the forty weight-$12$ codewords: exactly
$18{,}480$ syndrome classes are doubletons and there are no triple collisions.
The distinct leader count at weight six is $3{,}819{,}900$.

\paragraph{Pass 4742: the redundant checks carry a $[270,240,3]$ circuit code.}
The dependency code of the $270$ residue masks is
\[
 \boxed{[270,240,3]}.
\]
Its complete minimum shell consists of exactly $540$ weight-three dependencies.
Every such triple has pairwise two-line intersections and six-line union, and

second tempting $540=540$ identification is false: the circuit-triangle

\[
 1^1 2^{19}3^8 4^{12}6^8
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 1^1 2^7 3^8 4^{24}6^8,
\]
so the two subgroups are not conjugate and the two $540$-sets are not the same

\paragraph{Pass 4743: a finite commuting four-body parity model.}
For each residue $R$ define
\[
 S_R=\prod_{i\in R}Z_i,
 \qquad
 \mathcal H_R=-\sum_R S_R.
\]
The $270$ commuting checks have rank $30$, so the common $+1$ ground space has
computational-basis degeneracy $2^{40-30}=2^{10}=1024$ and is exactly
$H_{10}$.  The syndrome code is
\[
 \boxed{[270,30,27]}.
\]
An exact MILP proves the minimum $27$, and a second exclusion MILP proves that
the complete weight-$27$ shell is the forty single-line syndrome rows.  Hence
this finite model has ground energy $-270$, gap $54$, and first-excited
degeneracy $40\cdot2^{10}=40{,}960$.  One line violates $27$ terms; two skew
lines violate $48$, while two meeting lines violate $54$.  Thus the two-defect
spectrum resolves W33 line incidence.  The $540$ Pass~4742 circuits become
operator identities $S_aS_bS_c=I$, and Pass~4741 gives the optimal $27$-layer
measurement schedule.  No microscopic or thermodynamic-limit interpretation is
claimed.

\paragraph{Pass 4744: the second canonical $24$-coordinate family also misses Golay.}

observations, but these two canonical coordinate intertwiners are now
structurally excluded.
