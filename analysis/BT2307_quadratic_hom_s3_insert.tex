\section*{The complete quadratic map space as an $S_3$-module}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The complete quadratic map space as an S3-module}

The complete Pass-2301 basis of fifty $PSp(4,3)$-equivariant quadratic maps
from the signed-edge $90$ carries a simultaneous phase action $r$ and outer
involution $s$ satisfying
\[
r^3=s^2=1,\qquad srs=r^{-1}.
\]
Thus the multiplicity spaces form a module for $C_3:C_2\cong S_3$.  Exact GAP
character decomposition gives
\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{Sym}}
 \cong13\,\mathbf1\oplus3\,\mathrm{sgn}\oplus5\,\mathrm{std},
\]
\[
\mathcal H_{\Lambda}
 \cong3\,\mathbf1\oplus13\,\mathrm{sgn}\oplus4\,\mathrm{std},
\]
and hence
\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{quad}}
 \cong16\,\mathbf1\oplus16\,\mathrm{sgn}\oplus9\,\mathrm{std}.
\]
The associated characters on $(1),(123),(12)$ are
$(26,11,10)$, $(24,12,-10)$, and $(50,23,0)$.

This one decomposition explains both frozen dimension ledgers.  A reflection
has one eigenvalue of each sign on every standard copy, so
\[
\dim\mathcal H_+=16+9=25,
\qquad
\dim\mathcal H_-=16+9=25.
\]
The one-dimensional representations are fixed by $C_3$, while the nine
standard copies give eighteen rotating dimensions, so
\[
50=32_{C_3\text{-fixed}}+18_{C_3\text{-rotating}}.
\]

The order-six phase on the $90$ reduces to order three on bilinear inputs
because its central sign acts twice.  This is an exact representation-theoretic
logic reduction.  The fifty maps are not physical couplings, and the
multiplicities $16$ and $9$ are not identified with unrelated W33 counts.
