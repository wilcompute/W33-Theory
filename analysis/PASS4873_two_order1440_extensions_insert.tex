\section{Two order-$1440$ six-set extensions: a necessary non-isomorphism firewall}
\label{sec:pass4873-two-1440}

The marked-double-six chart of Pass~4869 and the earlier duad--syntheme
transfer of Pass~1848 both expose symmetry scales of order $1440$.  They do
\emph{not} expose the same group.

For the marked double-six, the six opposite columns carry the direct product
\[
  G_{\mathrm{res}}\cong S_6\times C_2,
\]
where the central involution fixes all duads and complements every triad.  Its
exact element-order census is
\[
  1^1\,2^{151}\,3^{80}\,4^{360}\,5^{144}\,6^{560}\,10^{144}.
\]
In particular $|Z(G_{\mathrm{res}})|=2$ and there are no elements of order $8$.

The frozen Pass~1848 duad-to-syntheme bijection instead reconstructs the
exceptional outer automorphism class of $S_6$: a transposition of cycle type
$(2,1,1,1,1)$ is carried to a triple transposition of type $(2,2,2)$.  After an
explicit inner adjustment one obtains an involutive representative of the
outer class and hence
\[
  G_{\mathrm{out}}\cong\operatorname{Aut}(S_6)=S_6{:}\operatorname{Out}(S_6).
\]
Its exact order census is
\[
  1^1\,2^{111}\,3^{80}\,4^{360}\,5^{144}\,6^{240}\,8^{360}\,10^{144}.
\]
Thus $Z(G_{\mathrm{out}})=1$, it has only $111$ involutions, and it contains
$360$ elements of order $8$.

Therefore
\[
  \boxed{S_6\times C_2\not\cong\operatorname{Aut}(S_6),}
\]
despite the common order $1440$.  The first $C_2$ is a central triad-complement
operation; the second is the exceptional outer-automorphism direction.  Their
numerical equality must not be used as an identification.
