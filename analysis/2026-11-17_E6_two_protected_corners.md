# A six-dimensional E6 Higgs spectrum that passes the interval index obstruction

The [interval calculation](2026-11-17_E6_reflection_proton_scan.md) gives two toral E6 boundary actions that remove the minimal proton-decay vertices, both of which fail the 5D spectrum test. Put those actions at two corners of a flat $T^2/\mathbb Z_2$ instead. The two corners then differ by a Wilson line equal to the Spin(10) matter sign. A second Wilson line changes the chiral projection. Four choices pass a full SM representation and integer index test, with two admitting a solution containing just four charged bulk hypermultiplets. The spectrum test assumes unbroken $Z_4^R$ and vanishing magnetic flux. See [Hall et al. on 6D orbifold unification](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0108071) and [Kobayashi, Raby, and Zhang on E6 and discrete Wilson lines](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0409098).

## Projection and index

Use point inversion on the torus and order-two translations $U_x,U_y$. The four corner actions are $P,PU_x,PU_y,PU_xU_y$. The two protected gauge actions fix $P$ and $PU_x$, with $U_x$ the matter sign. A commuting $U_y$ in the fixed toral SM centralizer has eight choices. A constant zero mode must be periodic under both translations and have the correct inversion sign. For a hypermultiplet the $R=0$ superfield and its conjugate $R=2$ superfield have opposite inversion signs and the same real translation signs. Their signed zero-mode difference is the average of the four signed corner characters. The chiral adjoint and vector contribute to that same index with opposite signs. The $T^2/\mathbb Z_2$ point inversion preserves the orientation of the two compact directions.

The Smith calculation uses the full character on $SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$, resolving all highest weights by the Weyl denominator. It includes the gauge tower, mandated sourced (27) and conjugate fields, any number of auxiliary (27,78,351,351',650) fields or conjugates, and brane blocks of (27) and conjugate representations. SM-singlet charge-0 or charge-2 fields are allowed. The target index has one weak doublet at each $w=\pm3$ and the Standard Model gauginos. Four choices have integer solutions. The other four have rational-span witnesses that require a net index change of one on a specific SM highest weight or combination.

## Bulk gauge and gravity anomaly check

The covering-space spectrum with four charged fundamental hypers has a simple Green–Schwarz lattice candidate. In (27)-trace normalization the exact traces give $A_{78}=4$, $C_{27}=1/12$, $C_{78}=1/2$, using the absence of an independent quartic E6 invariant. With `lambda_E6=6`, the local 6D factorization conditions are $a\cdot b=0$ and $b^2=-2$. In the even unimodular hyperbolic plane (U), $a=(-2,-2)$ and $b=(1,-1)$ also give $a^2=8$, as required for $T=1$. The irreducible bulk gravitational anomaly equation then requires 214 neutral hypers in addition to the four dimension-27 fields. This is a candidate bulk factorization and lattice embedding, with neutral-sector projection, fixed-point (R) anomalies, inflow and ultraviolet origin yet to be determined. In the charged bulk pairs the ordinary four-dimensional localized gauge-anomaly traces cancel between conjugate reps with the same intrinsic signs. The fields added at the corners must be checked separately.
