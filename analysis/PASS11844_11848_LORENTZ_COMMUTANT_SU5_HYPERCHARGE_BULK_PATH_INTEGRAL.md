## 11845 — which lift: su(5), su(3) ⊕ su(2), and the hypercharge between them (corrects 11843)

| lift of the finite Lorentz group | group | commutant in E₈ |
|---|---|---|
| adjacent Tits words n_i n_{i+1} (the A₅ chain) | **exactly A₆**, order 360 | **su(5)**: dim 24, rank 4, semisimple |
| permutation matrices on C⁶ = 1 + 5 inside SU(6) | A₆, order 360 | su(5), a different copy |
| all Tits words n_a n_b, as in Pass 11843 | A₆ extended by order-2 torus elements of SU(6) | su(3) ⊕ su(2) (dim 11) |
| A₆ (adjacent) plus the torus signs h_β(−1) | same extension | su(3) ⊕ su(2) |

* **Correction to Pass 11843.** Its "finite Lorentz group" was the group generated by all ordered pairs n_a n_b. That
  group contains order-2 elements of the SU(6) torus, so it is an extension of A₆ and not A₆ itself. The su(3) ⊕ su(2)
  commutant is correct for that group. **The faithful A₆ commutes with su(5)**, and the torus 2-elements break it to
  su(3) ⊕ su(2). The 11843 note, the paper paragraph and the ledger row are corrected accordingly.
* **The commutants nest:** su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊂ su(5)_A₆.
* **The hypercharge emerges.** The centraliser of su(3) ⊕ su(2) inside su(5) is **one-dimensional**. Its ad-spectrum on
  all of E₈, normalised to maximum 1, is

  0³⁶, (±1/6)³⁰, (±1/3)³⁰, (±1/2)²⁰, (±2/3)¹⁵, (±5/6)⁶, (±1)⁵

  This is **exactly the Georgi–Glashow hypercharge pattern** of E₈ ⊃ SU(5) × SU(5). So the u(1) that the torus signs
  break, i.e. the one direction separating the two lifts, is precisely the Standard-Model hypercharge Y.

* **One finite action gives exactly the Standard-Model algebra.** Add to the faithful A₆ a hypercharge rotation
  exp(2πi·6Y/7). It commutes with A₆ because Y lies in A₆'s commutant. The E₈ commutant of the group they generate is
  **dimension 12, derived algebra 11, centre 1, rank 4, containing su(3)_A₂ and su(2)_α**, i.e. exactly
  **su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1)_Y**.
* **Cross-track reconciliation.** Parallel Round 32 §III (`analysis/w33_20261010_toe32_e8_extension_monotonicity.py`)
  proves that every overgroup H of an embedded Lorentz group L has c(H) ⊂ c(L). It concludes that su(3) ⊕ su(2) and a
  centre cannot coexist. Its input was Pass 11843's group, which is the torus extension, with c = su(3) ⊕ su(2) of
  dimension 11. The monotonicity lemma is right, but for the faithful lift c(L) = su(5), dimension 24. So the no-go does
  not apply to it, and the overgroup A₆ × ℤ₇(Y) realises su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1)_Y inside the bound.

## 11844 — translations: no Minkowski subspace, but Minkowski with a central ℤ₃

* **No 4-dimensional invariant subspace.** W(A₅)′ acts on E₈/3E₈ = F₃⁸ through the Weyl group. Its invariant subspaces
  have dimensions 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 only: there is **no 4-dimensional one**. So the tangent Minkowski module M of
  Pass 11833 cannot be realised as order-3 torus elements on its own.
* **The 5-dimensional submodule.** The unique 5-dimensional submodule is the A₅ lattice mod 3. Its form has rank 4, and
  its radical is the invariant line (1,…,1). The quotient's nonzero orbits are **20 + 30 + 30**, exactly M. **The
  translations come as a non-split extension ℤ₃ · M**: Minkowski translations with a central ℤ₃, which cannot be
  removed because 3 | 6.
* **Commutant of this finite Poincaré group.** The commutant of (ℤ₃ · M) ⋊ Lorentz inside E₈ is **su(3) ⊕ su(2)**
  (dim 11, rank 3, centre 0), for both the A₆ lift and the extension. It contains the A₂ and the su(2)_α. The
  translations, like the torus signs, break su(5) → su(3) ⊕ su(2): **the full finite Poincaré group commutes with exactly
  the non-abelian Standard-Model algebra.**

## 11847 — a finite bulk path integral

* **Bulk field.** A Gaussian field on the 36 reversals with the crosscap propagator G = 6I + 2A − J = 12·Π₁₅
  (Pass 11839).
* **Boundary operators.** O_x = Σ_p N(x,p) φ_p, summed over the reversals orthogonal to context x.
* **Two-point function.** ⟨O_x O_y⟩ = 81 (same) / −27 (contexts that meet) / 9 (opposite) = **216 × the projector onto
  the boundary 15** (the −4 eigenspace of the context collinearity graph SRG(40,12,2,4)). The bulk field is dual to the
  boundary 15 mode, and the hidden bulk 20 is invisible (N·Π₂₀ = 0).
* **Tree three-point function.** The cubic contact Witten diagram W(x,y,z) = Σ_p K(x,p) K(y,p) K(z,p), with K = NG, is
  nonzero (7 distinct values). It equals **186624 × the boundary-intrinsic cubic** Σ_w Π₁₅(x,w) Π₁₅(y,w) Π₁₅(z,w),
  exactly. Multiplicity one of S15³ (Pass 11841) forces this agreement between the bulk and the boundary computations.
* **One loop.** The bubble G∘G has components 60 / 24 / 24 on the constant, the 15 and the 20. **The hidden 20
  circulates in loops** even though it never reaches the boundary at tree level. The one-loop two-point function is
  still ∝ the boundary 15 projector (ratio 288 to tree), so it only renormalises the normalisation.
