\subsection{The 200-cover orbital dictionary and Gram reconstruction}
The 200 nine-tritangent covers split canonically as 40 point-covers and 160 flag-covers. Exact cover overlap is constant on the 19 point/flag orbitals. The point fiber has overlap values 9, 3, 1 on identity, collinear, and noncollinear pairs, while the eight flag relative positions have overlaps 9, 3, 0, 0, 0, 3, 4, 1. The full integer cover Gram reconstructs the two fibers, W33 collinearity, the 40 maximal four-point lines, and every incident point-line flag. Its cross-fiber level 6 is the previously certified 480-incidence cover graph. After integer centering, the 40 point-covers and 160 flag-covers are separately tight frames for the same tritangent degree-24 constituent, with frame operators 300 P24 and 1200 P24 respectively. These are finite incidence statements only.
