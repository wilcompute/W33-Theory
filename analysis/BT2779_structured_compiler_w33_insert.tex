\section*{A $40\times12$ controlled-add scheduler and a deterministic two-copy M36 boundary}
The $480$ right cosets of the controlled-add centralizer admit an exact geometric
factorization. Each conjugate of the rank-two unipotent is specified by one of the
$40$ Lagrangian lines of $W(3,3)$ and one of the $12$ invertible symmetric
$2\times2$ forms over $\mathbb F_3$ having determinant $2$. Every pair occurs
exactly once, and for every $g\in\operatorname{Sp}(4,3)$ the decoder verifies
\[
 g\,\mathrm{CX}=r_{L,S}\,\mathrm{CX}\,c,
 \qquad c\in C_{\operatorname{Sp}(4,3)}(\mathrm{CX}).
\]
This replaces an $829{,}440$-bit dense dispatch table by $20{,}168$ structured
bits, a compression factor exceeding $41$.

A complementary search enumerates all $5{,}355$ binary $[[4,2]]$ stabilizer codes
and all four syndromes for two identical depolarized Witting resources. Logical
basis vectors are constructed from rank-one joint Pauli projectors and phased by
a deterministic pivot convention. Among branches closing onto the $36$-ray orbit
in this frozen canonical logical Pauli decoder gauge, exact
$\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)$ certificates find no fidelity-improving branch in any
magic-witness interval. Arbitrary logical Clifford decoders and larger or adaptive
protocols are not exhausted by this statement.
