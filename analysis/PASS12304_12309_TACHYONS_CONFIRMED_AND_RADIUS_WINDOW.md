# Passes 12304–12309: the tachyons are real; the 12299 suspicion retracted; the radius window

Claude track. Producer: `analysis/w33_pass12304_to_12309_tachyons_confirmed_and_radius_window.py`.

## 12304: what the engine calls a tachyon

The engine's TachyonSolver was run with its hard-coded verbose flag switched on (`~/orb/nsoverb/nsodump_verbose`). In SM_810's parent it
finds right-moving tachyonic vacua q = (0, ±⅓, ∓⅓, 0) with **m_R² = −1/6** in the Witten-twisted θ² and θ⁴ sectors. The 16 "excited
tachyon" states are left-movers matched to that level with an oscillator excitation. **They are genuine tachyons.**

The brother has the same right-moving vacua, but no left-moving partner survives the projection: T_NFIELDS = 0.

**Retraction.** 12299 re-derived these states' levels from the W and R lines that `nsoq` prints. For these states those lines are not
the solver's tachyonic configuration, and the massless controls could not detect that. The suspicion is withdrawn, and the
"tachyon-free" statements of 12281, 12286 and 12292 stand.

## 12307: the radius window

In the brother, the parent's tachyons carry winding ½ along e6, so α′m²/4 = −1/6 + R²/(16α′). The brother is tachyon-free only for
**R₆ ≥ 4√(α′/6) ≈ 1.63√α′**. The normalisation of R depends on conventions; the −1/6 is the engine's own value.
