Passes 5720–5724 — the affine gauge complex has exact 3-torsion

Integral theorem: for the nine-site AG(2,3) complex with K9 links, 54 translation plaquettes and 12 affine-line faces, the full boundary lattice has Smith invariants 1^26, 3^2. Therefore H1(X;Z) = (Z/3)^2 and H2(X;Z) = Z^38.

Mechanism: the plaquette-only complex has H1 = Z^4. The 12 line faces attach with Smith invariants 1,1,3,3: they kill all four real modes but leave index 9, so the two mod-3 modes are torsion/Bockstein modes rather than real harmonic zero modes.

Symmetry: all 432 elements of AGL(2,3) preserve the face complex. On the two-dimensional mod-3 homology, the kernel is exactly the 9 translations and the image is all of GL(2,3), with action conjugate to det(M) M^T.

Firewall: this is distinct from Pass5121's one-dimensional Z/3 saturation module on an 81×108 carrier. No chain map identifies them. The result also does not determine a Yang–Mills coupling, confinement, matter content, a continuum limit or a physical discrete gauge symmetry.
