# Pass 2973 addendum — the exact 540 clock/frame counting bridge

Pass 2416 constructs 540 frames as unordered pairs of disjoint isotropic W33 lines. A fixed isotropic line meets 12 of the other 39 lines and is disjoint from the remaining 27, so

\[
N_{\rm skew\ frames}=\frac{40\cdot27}{2}=540.
\]

The Pass-2973 curvature clock has an independent factorization. Translation by the order-three vector `a` partitions `F_3^4` into 27 cosets; the clock does not change the two encode/check sectors; and the global slot rotation remains within one of ten spread lines. Thus

\[
N_{\rm clock\ cycles}=2\cdot27\cdot10=540.
\]

The common factor 27 is exact and structurally meaningful. On the frame side it is the number of lines disjoint from a fixed line. On the clock side it is the quotient of 81 frame states by the order-three clock translation.

This does not yet identify the two 540-sets. Their labels and ambient group actions differ. The next objectwise test is to construct or rule out a bijection intertwining a common subgroup of the PSp frame action and the native clock-controller action.
