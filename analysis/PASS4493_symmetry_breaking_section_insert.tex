\subsection{Corrected symmetry-breaking boundary for the nonsplit apartment extension (Passes 4493, 4503--4504)}
\label{sec:pass4493-symmetry-breaking-section}

The full $PSp(4,3)$ action forbids an equivariant section of
\[
0\to K/J\;(29)\to E=M/J\;(39)\to V=M/K\cong H_{10}\;(10)\to0,
\]
with the reproducible full-group ranks
\[
\operatorname{rank}A=389,\qquad\operatorname{rank}[A\mid b]=390.
\]
A later adversarial re-execution (Pass~4503) found that the originally frozen
Pass~4493 restricted-subgroup table was a false positive.  The historical
$370=370$ point/line claim is withdrawn.  Exact reconstruction from the same
$GF(3)^4$ geometry gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
H & |H| & \operatorname{rank}A/\operatorname{rank}[A\mid b]\\ \hline
\text{line stabilizer} &648&386/387\\
\text{point stabilizer}&648&387/388\\
\text{incident flag stabilizer}&162&384/384\\
\text{apartment setwise stabilizer}&16&357/358.
\end{array}
\]
Thus neither order-$648$ maximal geometric stabilizer selects a protected
complement.  The canonical incident-flag stabilizer does: its section family is
six-dimensional, hence contains exactly $2^6=64$ equivariant sections.

Pass~4503 extends the correction beyond these geometric examples: representatives
of all five maximal subgroup types of $PSp(4,3)$, of orders
\[
960,\ 720,\ 648,\ 648,\ 576,
\]
retain the obstruction.  Pass~4504 exhausts the $64$ flag-equivariant sections
and finds a canonical-coordinate optimum of total ambient Hamming weight $42$,
maximum column weight $9$, and union support $13$ lines.  These are software
gauge representatives in $E=M/J$, not physical measurement or decoder costs.

The corrected architectural statement is therefore
\[
\boxed{\text{full symmetry and every maximal subgroup type tested}\Rightarrow
\text{protected sector remains quotient-only},}
\]
whereas
\[
\boxed{\text{fix an incident point--line flag}\Rightarrow
\text{an equivariant 10D representative section exists}.}
\]
The order-$162$ flag is a verified splitting subgroup; this does not by itself
classify every subgroup or prove $162$ is the globally largest splitting order.
