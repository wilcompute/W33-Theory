# Pass5619-5626 external prior-art boundary

Primary-source checks used for scope, not for importing results into certificates:

1. Guy Henniart and Chun-Hui Wang, *Weil representations over finite fields and Shintani lift*, arXiv:1303.5141. Finite symplectic groups over odd finite fields carry distinguished Weil representations. The repo's explicit qutrit matrices are a finite realization of this classical representation-theoretic setting; no novelty is claimed for the existence of the Weil representation.

2. Shamgar Gurevich and Ronny Hadani, *The categorical Weil representation*, arXiv:1108.0351. This provides a canonical finite-field Weil-representation framework via Heisenberg intertwiners. It supports the scope distinction between an intrinsic Heisenberg/Weil object and arbitrary coordinate-section phases.

3. Pablo Arrighi, Marcelo Forets, and Vincent Nesme, *The Dirac equation as a quantum walk: higher dimensions, observational convergence*, arXiv:1307.3524. Causal homogeneous lattice quantum walks can converge to the Dirac equation. Pass5624 therefore does not claim novelty for discrete causal Dirac propagation; its contribution is the audit of the repo's specific split-step support cone, finite-step anisotropy, and the physical c calibration boundary.

4. Pablo Arnault, Armando Pérez, Pablo Arrighi, and Terry Farrelly, *Discrete-time quantum walks as fermions of lattice gauge theory*, arXiv:1807.08303. Discrete-time walks can realize ultralocal fermionic lattice-gauge dynamics. This is relevant background for the repo's finite gauge/Dirac direction, but no equivalence to a continuum Standard Model gauge theory is asserted.

No source located in this check turns the q=3 W33 deck-odd 16 into a physical D5 half-spin, derives the SI speed of light from W33 counts, proves the repo's E6 horizontal/vertical firewall is a physical Yukawa selection rule, or supplies a spacetime Weyl law for the many-cell Gaussian limit. Those remain repo-specific open interpretation/derivation problems.
