\subsection*{The order-three defect closes by a three-character Fourier compiler}

Let $G=SL(2,3)$ be the common physical split complement.  The two
$36$-state carriers decompose as
\[
36_{\rm ordinary}=4+4+4+24,
\qquad
36_{\rm safe}=4+4+4+8+8+8.
\]
The three $4$-orbits have the same order-six stabilizer and pair objectwise.
The ordinary $24$-orbit is the regular $G$-set, while all three safe
$8$-orbits have the same stabilizer $H\cong C_3$.  Hence
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Reg}(G)
\cong
\bigoplus_{\chi\in\widehat H}\operatorname{Ind}_{H}^{G}\chi .
}
\]

The executable compiler realizes this identity directly.  For each of the
eight right cosets $rH$ and the three characters $j=0,1,2$,
\[
|r,j\rangle\longmapsto
\sum_{k=0}^{2}\omega^{-jk}|rh^k\rangle .
\]
The three safe $8$-orbits carry the characters
$1,\chi,\chi^2$, with monomial phase
\[
g|r,j\rangle=\omega^{jm(g,r)}|r',j\rangle,
\qquad
gr=r'h^{m(g,r)}.
\]

The resulting $36\times36$ matrix has entries in $\mathbf Q(\omega)$,
exact rank $36$, and $84$ nonzero entries.  Its columns are exactly orthogonal,
with squared norms
\[
1^{12}\oplus3^{24}.
\]
All $24^2\cdot36=20{,}736$ twisted-target representation-law checks and all
$24$ intertwining identities pass exactly.  The order-three target trace moves
from $9$ before twisting to $3$ after twisting, matching the ordinary carrier;
the order $1,2,4,6$ character strata were already identical.

\paragraph{Architecture reading.}
Twelve states compile objectwise.  The remaining $24$ are eight $C_3$ fibers
whose three Fourier modes are the three character-labelled safe sheets.
Thus the finite $SL(2,3)$ representation-level compiler closes after one
explicit ternary character twist.

\paragraph{Scope firewall.}
Multiplying the Fourier columns by $1/\sqrt3$ gives the usual complex-unitary
normalization.  This finite intertwiner does not prove that E8 dynamics, a
heterotic vacuum, or a photonic device physically selects the twist.
