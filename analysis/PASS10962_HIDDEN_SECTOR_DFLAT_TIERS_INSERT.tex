\subsection{Pass 10962: the hidden sector does not rescue matter parity}

% Repo prior art: Pass 10960 (singlet closure); Holotrade ed67ee5 (Kac enumeration).
Pass 10960's one loophole --- Standard-Model singlets charged under the hidden gauge group,
whose parity a hidden gauge element can compensate --- is closed in tiers.  Tier A (exact,
every hidden direction): a parity-preserving vacuum is D-flat for the hidden maximal torus
with component parities $3(B-L)\cdot q+2\eta\cdot w$, $t$ and $\eta$ free; for $32$ of the $88$
models the ever-even components carry an exact Farkas vector.  Tier B (exact over explicit
generators): by Luty--Taylor and Kempf--Ness a parity-preserving D-flat vacuum exists iff an
FI-cancelling product of matter-even hidden invariants does; over singlets, all quadratic
invariants, $SU(N)$ baryons and $SU(5)$ cubics, $55$ more models close by exact Farkas vectors
(completeness of the generators is not claimed).  Tier C: in the last model all $2846$
FI-cancelling rays of $62{,}309$ are parity-obstructed (Smith normal form).  The four
anomaly-free models admit no matter parity at all.  A pilot scan shows that $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$-I
also carries the $W(3,3)$ twist ($1167$ modular-invariant shifts, all with Z3 part
$(A_8,D_7{+}U_1)$) and hosts Standard Models, whose first spectra fail the same test.
