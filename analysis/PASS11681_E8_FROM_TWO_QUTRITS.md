## The construction

Let V = C⁹ be two qutrits. Then

  **e₈ = sl(V) ⊕ Λ³V ⊕ Λ³V\***

* **sl(V)** is the two-qutrit operators: Hamiltonians and Paulis.
* **Λ³V** is the three-fermion states of 9 modes (creation).
* **Λ³V\*** is their duals (annihilation).

The brackets are:
* [A, B] = AB − BA;
* [A, x] = A·x and [A, ξ] = −Aᵀ·ξ;
* [x, y] = ∗(x ∧ y) and [ξ, η] = ∗(ξ ∧ η);
* [x, ξ] = γ·(x·ξ)_traceless.

**The Jacobi identity fixes γ = −1** and then holds on all 27 type-triples (residual 1.3·10⁻¹³). The underlying decomposition is classical: it is Kac's Z₃-grading of E₈ with grade-0 part sl(9). Pass 2026-09-15 already identified the Pauli grading of the sl(9) part.

## Reading

> **E₈ is the Lie algebra generated by two-qutrit operators together with the creation and annihilation of three fermions in the nine two-qutrit modes.**

* **Its Cartan subalgebra** is the Pauli-singlet three-fermion states.
* **Its roots** are labelled by Pauli charges, three per nonzero phase-space point.
* **Its root geometry**, read on the Cartan, is the Witting polytope. The polytope's 40 rays are the 40 points of W(3,3), with orthogonality meaning commutation.
* **The repository's 6:1 fibration** of E₈ roots onto W(3,3) (Pass 1021) is exactly this reading: 6 roots per ray, 3 for v and 3 for −v.
* **The symmetric counterpart** of the Cartan, Pauli-singlet bosonic triples, is the Burkhardt space of Passes 11651–11659.
* **The two exceptional geometries in one space.** Witting (odd) and Burkhardt (even) are the two halves of one Pauli-invariant cubic space (Pass 11680).

**Time reversal.**
* Complex conjugation of the qutrit amplitudes is an antilinear automorphism, so it selects the split real form E₈₍₈₎. This agrees with the repo's earlier E₈₍₈₎ involution (Killing inertia (128, 120)).
* On the Witting coordinates of the roots it acts by complex conjugation.

## What is not established

* **The intertwiner to Codex's rays.**
  * Codex's Hilbert-space Witting rays (odd parts of Pauli projectors, Pass 11663) form a Witting configuration with the same labels and the same orthogonality.
  * The naive basis map T_u − T_{−u} ↦ |u⟩ − |−u⟩ matches only 16 of 40 rays.
  * The representations are complex conjugate, so the two are related by an antilinear intertwiner. That intertwiner is not constructed.
* **The full Witting group.** The order-3 complex reflections of G₃₂ = μ₆·PSp(4,3) are E₈ Weyl-group elements outside the Clifford image.
* **No physics claim.** No Standard Model content is claimed. The three-fermion reading is a reading of the grading, not a dynamical statement.
