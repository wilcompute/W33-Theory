# Pass 11693 — what we were missing: the finite geometry is the skeleton of the E₈ that two qutrits generate

Synthesis of Passes 11641–11692 (Claude track), with the Codex/Sage packets 11550–11679 and the repository's earlier E₈
passes. This note claims no new theorem; it states the picture the theorems add up to, and what is still missing.

## 1. The single object

Take two qutrits, V = C⁹. Then:

  **e₈ = sl(V) ⊕ Λ³V ⊕ Λ³V\***

That is, E₈ consists of:
* the operators of two qutrits;
* the creation of three fermions in the nine two-qutrit modes;
* their annihilation.

The Jacobi identity fixes the one free constant (Pass 11681). It closes only for dim V = 9 (Pass 11691).

**So the "W(3,3) ↔ E₈" correspondence of the repository is not an analogy or a count match.** W(3,3) is the
torus-normaliser skeleton of a concrete E₈, the one generated by two qutrits and three fermions:
* **its points** are root directions;
* **its lines** are SU(3)⁴ centralisers;
* **its factorisations** are D₄ × D₄ dual pairs;
* **its symmetry group** is the part of the Weyl group realised by unitary qutrit gates.

## 3. What this explains

* **Why the same numbers keep reappearing** (27, 40, 45, 36, 51840, 240): they are E₈ data seen through the Pauli grading.
  This accounts for the repository's chronic rediscovery problem. Many passes found different faces of one object.
* **Why selection keeps failing "from inside".** Every selection the physics needs breaks a symmetry the substrate has:
  * the point p, i.e. which E₆ × SU(3);
  * the line, i.e. which trinification;
  * the magic direction, i.e. which SU(2) × U(1) ⊂ SU(3)_p;
  * the chirality, i.e. which sign of ε·τ.

  A symmetric finite structure cannot choose; only a state or a dynamics can. Pass 346 met this for chirality. Pass 11689 shows the obstruction is C × T, and names the lowest order parameter that can lift it.
* **Why stabiliser (Clifford) physics is "too symmetric".** In 45 000 sampled Clifford-only ticks, none leaves an
  SM-shaped unbroken subalgebra su(3) ⊕ su(2) (⊕ u(1)⁵). With one magic gate, such ticks appear, at about 0.2% (Pass 11692).
  The non-stabiliser resource is what breaks the symmetric skeleton.

## 4. What is still missing (honestly)

This is **not** a Theory of Everything. The finite and algebraic layer is now one coherent object, but the physics needs
several things it does not supply.

1. **Dynamics.** We need an action, or an evolution law, whose ground state picks p, a line, a magic direction and a
   chirality.
   * The one-gate tick is a candidate microscopic law: it is an E₈ group element.
   * What would select a particular tick is unknown.
   * Fixed-radius selectors exist (Passes 11600, 11649, 11663), but a stable radial completion does not. Pass 11649's
     unbounded potential was refuted (Codex 11663 intake).
2. **Spacetime.** Nothing here produces four Lorentzian dimensions. The D₄ = so(8) factors and their triality are
   suggestive (so(8) is the transverse little algebra of ten-dimensional light-cone physics), but **no spacetime is
   derived**.
3. **The continuum and scales.** The two-qutrit E₈ is a genuine continuous Lie algebra, which bridges the
   finite-to-continuous gap at the level of symmetry. Masses, couplings and the cosmological constant remain unexplained.
4. **Three chiral generations.** The family SU(3)_p and its magic breaking 3 → 2 + 1 are structural. Chirality still
   requires a C × T-odd order parameter to condense, and nothing yet makes it do so.

## 5. The research programme this suggests

**Pose physics as dynamics on the two-qutrit E₈:** a state or tick sequence of two qutrits whose unbroken centraliser,
after symmetry breaking, is the Standard Model algebra, and whose C × T-odd order parameter selects one chirality. The
finite W(3,3) results then become the classification of the possible symmetry-breaking patterns: centralisers of Pauli
subgroups and of ticks.

Two concrete targets:
* a dynamical principle that prefers the 0.2% SM-shaped ticks, or a natural tick that has exactly that centraliser;
* the condensation of Im⟨P⟩ as the chirality order parameter.
