\subsection{Passes 4936--4937: exact chamber matrix units and a finite adjoint controller}
\label{subsec:pass4936-4937}

The rank-$48$ chamber packet of Passes~4324 and~4334 now has literal rational
matrix units.  In the packet algebra
\[
 \mathcal A_{48}=\langle\Pi_{48},X,\Omega,X\Omega\rangle_{\mathbb Q},
 \qquad X^2=6\Pi_{48},\quad \Omega^2=-60\Pi_{48},\quad
 X\Omega=-\Omega X,
\]
the point and line carriers are
\[
 Q_p=\frac12\Pi_{48}+\frac18X+\frac1{48}X\Omega,
 \qquad
 Q_\ell=\frac12\Pi_{48}+\frac18X-\frac1{48}X\Omega.
\]
Putting
\[
 e_{11}=Q_p,\quad e_{22}=\Pi_{48}-Q_p,\quad
 e_{21}=(\Pi_{48}-Q_p)Q_\ell Q_p,\quad
 e_{12}=\frac{64}{15}Q_pQ_\ell(\Pi_{48}-Q_p)
\]
gives all sixteen relations
\[
 e_{ij}e_{k\ell}=\delta_{jk}e_{i\ell}.
\]
Hence
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal A_{48}\cong M_2(\mathbb Q)}
\]
on the packet representation: one exact two-state multiplicity algebra repeated
on $24$ representation lanes.  This is not a construction of $24$ physical
qubits.

The bridge to the checked-in chamber ISA is deliberately aggregate.  If the
three deterministic point-panel transitions are denoted $HP_i$ and the three
line-panel transitions $HL_i$, their chart-independent family sums are $P$ and
$L$.  Their packet compressions
\[
 H_p=\frac{\Pi_{48}P\Pi_{48}-\Pi_{48}}2=2Q_p-\Pi_{48},
 \qquad
 H_\ell=\frac{\Pi_{48}L\Pi_{48}-\Pi_{48}}2=2Q_\ell-\Pi_{48}
\]
satisfy
\[
 H_p^2=H_\ell^2=\Pi_{48},\qquad
 H_pH_\ell+H_\ell H_p=-\frac12\Pi_{48}.
\]
For $T=H_pH_\ell$ one has
\[
 2T^2+T+2\Pi_{48}=0,\qquad
 \chi_T(t)=t^{112}\left(t^2+\frac12t+1\right)^{24},
\]
so the aggregate turn has infinite order on the packet.  These identities
certify the complete $HP$ or $HL$ family only; no individual selector is
asserted to preserve the packet.

Pass~4864 identified the remaining ternary ten-space with
$\mathfrak{sp}_4(\mathbb F_3)$, and Pass~4871 later proved that its bracket is
already forced up to nonzero scalar by the Levi-incidence action.  Pass~4861
showed that full local port matching leaves one global $PGSp(4,3)$, while
Pass~4872 compiled each of the $45$ local $S_3$ matchings as one trit plus one
bit.  Pass~4937 composes the ten-space and global frame into a different
object: a ten-trit affine correction register.  Over
$R=\mathbb F_3[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$ it verifies the split
sequence
\[
 1\longrightarrow\mathfrak{sp}_4(\mathbb F_3)^+
 \longrightarrow Sp_4(R)\longrightarrow Sp_4(3)\longrightarrow1,
\]
whose elementary-abelian tangent kernel has order $3^{10}=59049$.  Port
matching instead gives the faithful affine controller candidate
\[
 \mathbb F_3^{10}\rtimes PGSp(4,3),\qquad
 v\longmapsto vA_g+w,\qquad
 |\mathbb F_3^{10}\rtimes PGSp(4,3)|=3{,}061{,}100{,}160.
\]
The $59049$ offsets form exactly $17$ $PGSp(4,3)$-orbits.  The dual-number
group has the same order as this affine group but is not the same group: their
centers have orders $2$ and $1$, respectively.  Moreover, multiplication in
the square-zero kernel is addition, so its group commutator is zero; the
nonabelian Lie bracket is tangent data inherited from Pass~4864, not a hidden
kernel commutator.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
The two native GAP witnesses replay $20/20$ and $30/30$ exact checks and emit
byte-stable certificates.  Pass~4936 proves a finite rational packet algebra
and a chart-independent selector-family checksum.  Pass~4937 proves a finite
characteristic-three tangent group, affine semidirect product, and orbit
atlas.  Neither pass implements an individual chamber selector, a HoloBox
guest transition, a compiler lowering, an isolation or security guarantee,
hardware timing, a continuum gauge field, or a particle, mass, or coupling.
