# BT1564-BT1566 Operator-on-photon Packet

## BT1564

One Choi leg is formalized as the photonic mode state. The other Choi leg is formalized as the encoded optical action. The circuit is modeled as a relation on one photon’s own registers.

## BT1565

A finite self-applied circuit model is added. Gates I, X, Z, F3, and S are represented as encoded optical actions on the operator leg and checked by trace-Choi overlap against the state leg.

## BT1566

The OAM operator witness distinguishes passive spatial labels from active internal operator behavior. It includes label-only control, operator-leg activation, basis covariance, and self-vs-external control tests.

## Current synthesis

The photon is no longer only a probe moving through an external circuit. In the finite model, the photon carries a state leg and an operator leg. External optics become preparation/readout handles for an internal state-operator relation.
