\paragraph{Exact finite triality theorem.}
The totally singular subspaces occur in the exact counts
\[
  135,\qquad 1575,\qquad 2025,\qquad 270
\]
in vector dimensions $1,2,3,4$.  The $270$ maximal totally singular $4$-spaces
split into two families of $135$.  Thus the three outer $D_4$ types are
\[
  \mathcal P_{135},\qquad \mathcal S^+_{135},\qquad \mathcal S^-_{135}.
\]
For any two distinct outer types, let $M$ be the $135\times135$ incidence matrix.
Every row and column has weight $15$, and each outer type carries the same relation
\[
  A=\operatorname{SRG}(135,70,37,35),
  \qquad
  \operatorname{spec}(A)=70^1\oplus7^{50}\oplus(-5)^{84}.
\]
The incidence Gram law is
\[
  MM^{\mathsf T}=15I+3A.
\]
More strongly, for cyclically ordered outer types,
\[
  M_{12}M_{23}=J+6M_{13}.
\]
After removing the trivial all-ones constituent with the integral centering
\[
  D_{ij}=9M_{ij}-J,
\]
the three maps satisfy the exact triality algebra
\[
  \boxed{D_{12}D_{23}=54D_{13}}
\]
cyclically, and
\[
  D_{ij}D_{ij}^{\mathsf T}=1215I+243A-135J=54^2E_{50},
  \qquad \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}D_{ij}=50.
\]
This is an explicit rank-$50$ intertwiner layer, not a numerical coincidence of
three cardinalities.

\paragraph{A useful obstruction, not an identification.}
A selected singular line lies in six maximal singular $4$-spaces and has six W33
apartment lifts.  The equality $6=6$ does \emph{not} define a canonical bridge.
Its stabilizer has order $96$.  On the six apartments it acts transitively; on the
six maximal generators its orbit sizes are $3+2+1$.  The two actions have the same
image order $12$ and even the same kernel of order $8$, but their fixed-point
characters differ.  Therefore there is no stabilizer-equivariant bijection between
those two six-sets.
