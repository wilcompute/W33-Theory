The protected binary quotient reconstructed from the W33 line/apartment system has
$256$ classes and a plus-type quadratic form.  Pass~4579 had already shown that its

\paragraph{Exact finite triality theorem.}
The totally singular subspaces occur in the exact counts
\[
  135,\qquad 1575,\qquad 2025,\qquad 270
\]
in vector dimensions $1,2,3,4$.  The $270$ maximal totally singular $4$-spaces
split into two families of $135$.  Thus the three outer $D_4$ types are
\[
  \mathcal P_{135},\qquad \mathcal S^+_{135},\qquad \mathcal S^-_{135}.
\]
For any two distinct outer types, let $M$ be the $135\times135$ incidence matrix.
Every row and column has weight $15$, and each outer type carries the same relation
\[
  A=\operatorname{SRG}(135,70,37,35),
  \qquad
  \operatorname{spec}(A)=70^1\oplus7^{50}\oplus(-5)^{84}.
\]
The incidence Gram law is
\[
  MM^{\mathsf T}=15I+3A.
\]
More strongly, for cyclically ordered outer types,
\[
  M_{12}M_{23}=J+6M_{13}.
\]
After removing the trivial all-ones constituent with the integral centering
\[
  D_{ij}=9M_{ij}-J,
\]
the three maps satisfy the exact triality algebra
\[
  \boxed{D_{12}D_{23}=54D_{13}}
\]
cyclically, and
\[
  D_{ij}D_{ij}^{\mathsf T}=1215I+243A-135J=54^2E_{50},
  \qquad \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}D_{ij}=50.
\]
This is an explicit rank-$50$ intertwiner layer, not a numerical coincidence of
three cardinalities.

\paragraph{What the $1620$ W33 apartments do inside this triality geometry.}
For an apartment $\{a,b,c,d\}$ in the W33 line graph, its two opposite line pairs
give two singular quotient vectors; together with the apartment's Pass~4579
singular fiber label they form one totally singular projective line.  The resulting
map has exact multiplicities
\[
  1620\longrightarrow270\quad(6\text{-to-}1),
\]
where the selected $270$ singular lines form one $\PSp(4,3)$ orbit.  They define a
regular incidence structure
\[
  135_6\;\text{--}\;270_3,
\]
and the apartments are a two-sheet cover of its $810$ point--line flags.

\paragraph{The apartment-selected singular graph.}
Let $N\in\{0,1\}^{135\times270}$ be the incidence matrix of the $135$ singular
points against those $270$ selected lines.  Then every row has weight $6$ and
every column has weight $3$.  Writing
\[
  NN^{\mathsf T}=6I+A_{\rm sel},
\]
the selected point graph is $12$-regular on $135$ vertices and has exact spectrum
\[
  \boxed{12^1\oplus6^{15}\oplus3^{20}\oplus0^{60}\oplus(-3)^{24}\oplus(-6)^{15}}.
\]
Its annihilator is
\[
  (x-12)(x-6)(x-3)x(x+3)(x+6).
\]
Every adjacent pair has exactly one common neighbor.  Hence every edge lies in a
unique triangle and the graph has exactly $810/3=270$ triangles.  These are
precisely the $270$ selected totally singular lines, so the apartment image can be
recovered from the graph alone.  Moreover,
\[
  \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}N=120,
  \qquad
  \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}N=119.
\]

\paragraph{The tempting $120=120$ identification is false.}
The protected quotient also has $120$ anisotropic points.  Pass~4591 compares the
actual $\PSp(4,3)$ characters rather than the dimensions.  On the deterministic
five-transvection generating set, all five generators happen to have trace $3$ on
both the rational rowspace of $N$ and the anisotropic permutation module, so a
shallow generator check is misleading.  The short order-$6$ word $g_4g_0$ separates
them:
\[
  \chi_{\operatorname{row}_{\mathbb Q}N}(g_4g_0)=-1,
  \qquad
  \chi_{\rm aniso}(g_4g_0)=3.
\]
Therefore the two $120$-dimensional rational $\PSp(4,3)$ modules are not
isomorphic.  This rules out a cardinality/rank promotion while leaving nonlinear
or subquotient relationships as separate questions.

\paragraph{A useful obstruction, not an identification.}
A selected singular line lies in six maximal singular $4$-spaces and has six W33
apartment lifts.  The equality $6=6$ does \emph{not} define a canonical bridge.
Its stabilizer has order $96$.  On the six apartments it acts transitively; on the
six maximal generators its orbit sizes are $3+2+1$.  The two actions have the same
image order $12$ and even the same kernel of order $8$, but their fixed-point
characters differ.  Therefore there is no stabilizer-equivariant bijection between
those two six-sets.

\paragraph{The positive replacement.}
Under the projective W33 group the $270$ maximal singular generators decompose as
\[
  135\;\sqcup\;36\;\sqcup\;36\;\sqcup\;36\;\sqcup\;27.
\]
The three degree-$36$ orbits were checked as $G$-sets rather than named from their
sizes: for each orbit, a representative stabilizer has order $720$ and fixes
exactly one of the $36$ W33 spreads.  Hence each is genuinely equivariantly
isomorphic to the W33 spread carrier.  The degree-$27$ orbit is deliberately left
unnamed until the same stabilizer/permutation-character test identifies it.
