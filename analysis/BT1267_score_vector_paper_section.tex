\subsection{Tomography score vector and validator}
\label{sec:bt1267-tomography-score-vector}

The Clifford tomography ladder can be compressed into a five-component score vector
\[
S=(C,D,P,E,L),
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
C&=1 &&\text{iff the candidate closes to } |Sp(4,3)|=51840,\\
D&=1 &&\text{iff the symmetric word diameter is }14,\\
P&=1 &&\text{iff the polar/nonpolar edge split is }P_4/P_4,\\
E&=1 &&\text{iff there is a unique all-channel diameter endpoint,}\\
L&=1 &&\text{iff the labelled geodesic tensor has nonzero channel spread.}
\end{aligned}
\]

The reference BT1233/BT1258 target has
\[
S=(1,1,1,1,1),
\]
so it scores \(5/5\).  The full-order regimes score as follows:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{regime} & S & \text{score}\\
\hline
\operatorname{diam}=14\text{ polar path} & (1,1,1,1,1) & 5/5\\
\operatorname{diam}=12 & (1,0,0,0,1) & 2/5\\
\operatorname{diam}=10_A & (1,0,0,0,1) & 2/5\\
\operatorname{diam}=10_B & (1,0,0,0,0) & 1/5\\
\operatorname{diam}=10_C & (1,0,0,0,0) & 1/5
\end{array}
\]

This yields an operational validator:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{band} & \text{rule}\\
\hline
\mathrm{pass} & \text{all five gates true}\\
\mathrm{review} & \text{full closure plus at least one additional gate, but not all gates}\\
\mathrm{fail} & \text{missing closure or below review threshold.}
\end{array}
\]

Thus full closure is necessary but not sufficient.  A wrong full-order regime is review-only; a closure-only candidate fails; only the diameter-14 polar path tetrahedron passes as the recovered finite Clifford target.
