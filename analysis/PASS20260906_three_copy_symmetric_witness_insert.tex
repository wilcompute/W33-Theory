% PASS20260906 -- three-copy exact stabilizer witnesses (symmetric-family exhaustion)
\section*{Current magic frontier: the two-copy no-go does NOT extend to three copies}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Current magic frontier: the two-copy no-go does NOT extend to three copies}

\noindent\textbf{Scope.}
An exact finite search result about the stated distillation condition.  A witness here
certifies possibility under the exact condition (annihilate the single-error subspace,
keep the clean ray with nonzero weight); it is not yet a decoder, a yield, or a
fidelity curve, and it is not a physical device claim.

\paragraph{The search that was asked for.}
Pass 2861 exhausted all $5{,}355$ two-copy $[[4,2]]$ codes: zero super-linear branches.
Pass 2881 sampled $30{,}000$ random six-qubit stabilizer groups at three copies: zero;
Pass 4680 then proved that sample had at most about one percent power unless witnesses
number in the tens of thousands.  The recorded ask was an \emph{exhaustive search over
a chosen code family}.  The chosen family is canonical: the exact condition is
copy-permutation invariant, so the search exhausts ALL $S_3$-invariant stabilizer
groups.  By the isotypic decomposition $\FF_2^{12}=\FF_2^4\otimes(\mathrm{triv}\oplus
\mathrm{std})$, every such group is $V_t\oplus W_T$ with $V_t,T$ isotropic in the
$2$-qubit label space ($31$ choices each, automatically mutually orthogonal), giving
exactly
\[
961 \text{ groups},\qquad 27{,}391 \text{ signed syndrome projectors per ray}.
\]

\paragraph{The result.}
Exact integer arithmetic in $\ZZ[\zeta_{12}]$ (Walsh--Hadamard over each group's
$2^r$ sign assignments, with the full Pauli operator-product phase cocycle ---
dropping it was two false starts) against
\[
\operatorname{Tr}(PJ)=0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\langle mmm|P|mmm\rangle\neq 0 ,
\]
finds, for the $36$ $\mathrm{M}_{36}$ rays:
\[
\boxed{\text{104 witnesses, every reported one independently re-verified by a dense
projector construction on a separate code path.}}
\]
Per family: seven rays carry $2$ witnesses and two rays carry $6$.  All witnesses are
maximal (rank $6$).  A representative for ray $\mathrm{A}_{00}=(0,1,-1,1)/\sqrt3$:
the group generated by
\[
X_1^{\otimes 3},\quad X_2^{\otimes 3},\quad
Z_2^{(13)},\quad Z_2^{(23)},\quad Z_1^{(13)},\quad Z_1^{(23)},
\]
with generator signs $(-,+,+,+,+,+)$, annihilates all nine single-error inputs and
keeps the clean ray.

\paragraph{Why the earlier searches missed it.}
Every one of the $36$ rays has \emph{trivial local Pauli stabilizer} (computed
exactly): no single generator stabilizes the clean input, so witnesses work only by
multi-generator splitting of the error subspace --- a thin algebraic condition,
invisible to random sampling at the power Pass 4680 computed, and excluded at two
copies by Pass 2861's exhaustive rank argument.

\paragraph{What changes.}
The blueprint's open problem 2 is answered in the affirmative \emph{at the level of
the stated condition}: three-copy super-linear distillation of $\mathrm{M}_{36}$ is
possible.  The open problem becomes the decoder, the yield, and whether the
$8\times$-$S$ guest overhead and the acceptance inequality $p<(8-2\sqrt3)/9$ survive
the witnessed branch.  Fully asymmetric projectors are not covered by this family;
they are no longer the first place to look.
