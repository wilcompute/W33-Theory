# Theta periods and center glue of the faithful W33 gauge group

The faithful parent is G = (E6_sc × SU(3))/Z3, with the diagonal subgroup generated by the positive E6 center and the inverse family center. On closed spin four-manifolds the two continuous theta angles have an index-three period lattice at fixed discrete counterterm. A simultaneous 2π shift is a period. Shifting either angle by itself requires 6π. [Faithful group and Higgs stabilizer](2026-11-10_global_gauge_topology.md)

## The center quadratic form in the W33 chart

Use the basic trace convention of the electric-phase calculation, so a long coroot has squared norm two. The E6 center coweight is H/3, where H has charges 4, −2 and 1 on the 1, 10 and 16 of the defining 27. The trace Tr_27(H²) is 72. Dividing by the index six of that representation and by nine gives coweight norm 4/3. The inverse family center has coweight diag(−1,−1,2)/3 of norm 2/3. The replay checks both endpoint phases in the actual representation and reconstructs the full E6 coroot lattice from the commutators of the root matrices. [Electric phase trace convention](2026-11-11_monopole_electric_phase.md)

The covering lattice E6 ⊕ A2 has determinant nine. Adjoining the paired center coweight gives an even integral lattice of determinant one and rank eight. Its exact norm-two count is 78 in the covering coset and 81 in each of the two additional cosets. The two 81-element sets match the W33 defining bifundamental weights and their negatives, weight for weight. This identifies the quotient cocharacter lattice with E8. The standard branching of the E8 adjoint gives index one for both simple factors, so the basic E8 Chern–Weil charge restricts to the sum of the two charges. ## Periods and a spin witness

Write w for the common obstruction in H²(M,Z3) and write s = ⟨w cup w,[M]⟩ in Z3. With positive trace convention, the center quadratic form gives

    Q_E6 ≡ 2s/3 mod 1
    Q_family ≡ s/3 mod 1.

The inverse family center has the same quadratic value as the positive center. The topological weight is exp[i θ_E6 Q_E6 + i θ_family Q_family + 2π i p s/3] with p in Z3. Hence a shift by 2π(a,b) multiplies it by exp[2π i (2a+b)s/3]. At fixed p the full period lattice is a ≡ b mod 3, generated by (1,1) and (0,3). Allowing p to change compensates a shift by p → p−(2a+b). The necessary condition already occurs on the spin manifold S² × S². Use the paired cocharacter and a line bundle with first Chern class x+2y. Its square evaluates to four, giving s = 1 and exact charges (8/3,4/3). Liftable bundles with unit instantons in either simple factor fix the 2π units. [Fractional instanton numbers and the center quadratic form](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.09117)

For a line with the generator of magnetic obstruction, the canonical Witten electric relabeling under the two unit theta shifts adds trialities (1,0) and (0,1) respectively, using the positive center of each simple factor to measure electric labels. The relative triality changes by a−b, which is congruent to −(2a+b). A simultaneous shift adds a genuine Wilson charge of the quotient since its trialities coincide. This gives the same period condition from the relative center charge. [Line operators and theta shifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0318)

For the topological weight alone, orientation reversal sends both theta angles and p to their negatives. At the four angle pairs (0,0), (0,π), (π,0), and (π,π), the compatible p values are respectively 0, 1, 2, and 0. This table tests the topological couplings. Whether orientation reversal is a symmetry of the full action also depends on the matter couplings.

The spin witness has signature zero, so the fermion Dirac indices can be checked directly from the integer cocharacter weights. They are 84 for each bifundamental or its conjugate, 64 for the E6 adjoint, 8 for the family adjoint and 20 for the conjugate family decuplet. The total over the canonical chiral fields and both gauginos is 576 in this orientation. This is an index in a specified bundle, with no assumption about which zero modes are saturated by interactions.

The Standard Model stabilizer embeds through the simply connected cover of the parent. Any parent bundle reduced everywhere to that stabilizer therefore has w = 0. The smooth primitive monopole and its phase probe the liftable sector. The new period condition concerns the full parent theory, including bundles that cannot carry the canonical Higgs vacuum everywhere. [Smooth electric phase](2026-11-11_monopole_electric_phase.md)

## Global gauge anomalies on spin spacetime

The group also has Ω5^Spin(BG) = 0. The ordinary homology Serre sequence for B(E6_sc × SU(3)) → BG → K(Z3,2) has no entries at total degree five. The base has H1 = H5 = 0, and the simply connected cover has classifying-space homology zero in degrees one through three and in degree five. For the degree-five vanishing, the basic degree-four class of each simple factor gives a six-connected map to K(Z,4), using π4(E6_sc) = π4(SU(3)) = 0. The target has H5 = 0. It follows that H5(BG,Z) = 0. [Low homotopy of the simple groups](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.36.3268) · [Integral homology of K(Z3,2), Table 3.5](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/bf4b85dc-2fc1-4e24-8b34-a9b9b4a86bf4/content)

The covering map induces an isomorphism on mod-two homology because its classifying-space fiber BZ3 is mod-two acyclic in positive degrees. In the spin Atiyah–Hirzebruch spectral sequence the only potentially nonzero term of total degree five is therefore H4(BG,Z2) = (Z2)², at bidegree (4,1). Each of its generators is in the image of a BSU(3) map. One comes from the family factor. The other comes from a regular long-root A2 in E6, whose index is one because its coroots have the same basic norms. The replay finds this A2 in the W33 chart. On BSU(3) the differential from (6,0) to (4,1) is surjective, dual to Sq²(c2 mod 2) = c3 mod 2 after reduction of the integral source. Naturality therefore kills both target generators. No term survives at total degree five. Together with the canonical perturbative anomaly cancellation, this removes a residual global gauge anomaly for the faithful group on ordinary spin spacetime. The argument uses spin manifolds with G bundles as its background data.
