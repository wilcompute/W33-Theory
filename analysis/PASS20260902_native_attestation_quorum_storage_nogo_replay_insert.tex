\section{Native attestation, quorum authority, storage-aware placement, and replay receipts}
\label{sec:native-attestation-quorum-storage-replay}

This pass closes five runtime frontiers while preserving the distinction between
mathematical certificates, software security policy, and fabricated hardware.
The resulting execution chain is
\[
\text{native hardware evidence}
\to \text{execution passport}
\to \text{quorum epoch authority}
\to \text{storage-aware placement}
\to \text{signed replay receipt}.
\]

\paragraph{Native TPM2 and SEV-SNP verification.}
The Holotrade sibling repository now contains
\texttt{js/w33-native-attestation.js}.  For SEV-SNP it parses the 1184-byte
attestation report, binds the 64-byte \texttt{REPORT\_DATA} field to the W33
challenge and runtime public-key digest, enforces launch-measurement and
reported-TCB policy, converts the firmware P-384 signature representation to
DER, and verifies the report directly with SHA-384.  It supports either an
explicitly pinned VCEK key or an in-process ARK--ASK--VCEK certificate chain.
For TPM2 it parses \texttt{TPMS\_ATTEST} for a quote, binds
\texttt{extraData} to the W33 challenge, verifies the AK signature, replays
supplied event digests into PCR banks, and recomputes the quoted PCR digest in
the selected-PCR order.  Thus the preferred path no longer depends on the
older normalized ``external verifier said yes'' envelope.  A pinned AK/VCEK
remains a deployment trust anchor; vendor provenance is stated separately from
cryptographic signature validity.

\paragraph{Quorum-certified authority epochs.}
\texttt{analysis/w33\_quorum\_epoch\_authority.py} replaces the single-signer
authority transition with a $4$-of-$5$ Ed25519 quorum certificate transported
through the same neutral $36$-state ST81/ST64 fabric.  Honest members enforce a
one-vote-per-predecessor rule.  With $n=5,t=4$, any two quorums intersect in at
least
\[
2t-n=3
\]
members.  Under the explicit assumption of at most one Byzantine authority,
at least two members of that intersection are honest, so conflicting certified
successors require violation of the honest one-vote rule.  If conflicting valid
certificates are nevertheless presented, the runtime emits content-addressed
fork evidence naming the intersecting signer set.  This is quorum
certification, not a complete asynchronous BFT protocol and not an aggregated
threshold-signature primitive.

\paragraph{Persistence enters placement.}
Holotrade's \texttt{js/w33-storage-aware-placement.js} now augments the ordinary
ExecutionEngine placement score with forecast terms for live strong roots,
retained reversible history, checkpoint retention, expected recovery storage,
expected recovery compute time, energy-policy cost, and irreversible-erasure
policy cost.  The objective is exposed rather than collapsed into an opaque
number:
\[
C_{\rm total}=C_{\rm base}+C_{\rm strong\ root}
 +\mathbb E[C_{\rm recovery}]+C_{\rm energy}+C_{\rm erase}.
\]
The strong-root term is measured in blob-seconds/byte-seconds before a market
tariff is applied.  No blob tariff or erasure tariff is identified with
Landauer work; measured device energy remains a distinct quantity.

\paragraph{A restricted but exact $[[20,7,2]]_3$ no-go.}
The appropriate type-compatible parent for the external qutrit code is not the
$40$ W33 point set (whose points are two-qutrit Pauli classes), but the exact
$240$-edge qutrit CSS code with $H_X=d_1$ and $H_Z=d_2^T$.  The executable
certificate \texttt{analysis/w33\_qutrit\_20\_7\_2\_edge\_css\_no\_go.py}
computes the W33 graph edge connectivity by Stoer--Wagner and obtains
\[
\lambda(W(3,3))=12.
\]
For any vertex potential $y\in\mathbb F_3^{40}$, a nonzero parent
$X$-stabilizer $y^Td_1$ is supported precisely on edges joining unequal
potential values.  Hence every nonzero word in $\operatorname{row}(H_X)$ has
weight at least $12$; a one-vertex potential attains weight $12$.  By contrast,
the frozen external $H_0$ span contains a nonzero weight-$6$ stabilizer.
Coordinate permutation, injection, and nonzero $\mathbb F_3$ rescaling preserve
support weight.  Therefore
\[
\boxed{\text{no literal CSS-preserving monomial zero-extension }20\to240
\text{ intertwiner exists}.}
\]
This is an UNSAT certificate for that adapter class, not a theorem excluding
all embeddings.  General symplectic Clifford maps that mix $X/Z$, introduce
ancillas, or encode external coordinates nonlocally remain open.

\paragraph{Attested replay flight recorder.}
Holotrade's \texttt{js/w33-attested-replay-receipt.js} builds one Merkle-like
content commitment over the execution passport, deployment digest, native
hardware-attestation evidence, complete epoch-transition chain, wait-for root,
cancellation root, deterministic wake-schedule root, temporal-GC root
evolution, magic-token reservation/consumption events, and final artifact set.
That flight-recorder root is embedded in the existing deterministic CBOR/COSE
Ed25519 delivery receipt.  Reordering, omitting, or substituting any bound
history changes the signed payload.  The receipt proves integrity and identity
of the supplied evidence; it does not by itself prove that a fabricated sensor
or external authority was truthful.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The five additions materially tighten the runtime trust chain, but they do not
claim a fabricated SEV-SNP platform, a certified TPM deployment, secure physical
media erasure, complete asynchronous Byzantine consensus, or a W33-specific
fault-tolerant magic-state-distillation threshold.  In particular the new
weight-$6$ versus weight-$12$ obstruction closes only the literal CSS-preserving
monomial embedding class; the broader symplectic/ancilla-assisted adapter search
is now the correct remaining target.
