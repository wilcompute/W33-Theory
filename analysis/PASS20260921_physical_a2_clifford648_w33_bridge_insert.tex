\subsection*{The physical external-$A_2$ qutrit normalizer is the W33 point stabilizer}

The physical external-$A_2$ theorem supplies
\[
H_{27}=\langle X,Z,\omega I\rangle,
\qquad
ZX=\omega XZ,
\qquad
\omega I=\exp(2\pi iQ_\psi/3).
\]
Pass~5730 gives the standard Fourier and quadratic-phase normalizer actions.
On $H_{27}/Z(H_{27})\cong\mathbb F_3^2$ in the basis $(X,Z)$ they are
\[
F=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
They generate all of $SL(2,3)$, of order $24$, so the finite normalizer has
order
\[
27\cdot24=648.
\]

Pass~1054 independently computes the selected W33 point stabilizer, extracts
its normal extraspecial $H_{27}$, and records the complete conjugation image of
an $SL(2,3)$ complement on the quotient.  Re-running that witness yields
literally the same set of $24$ determinant-one matrices, including the two
generators above.  Therefore the physical external-$A_2$ qutrit normalizer and
the W33 point stabilizer agree at the exact quotient-action level:
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Stab}_{W33}(p)\cong H_{27}:SL(2,3).}
\]

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No particular $3\times3$ complex conjugating matrix to the W33 permutation
model is serialized here, and the theorem makes no low-energy gauge or
hardware claim.
