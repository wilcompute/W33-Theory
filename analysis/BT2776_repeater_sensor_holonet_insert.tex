\section*{Physical lift sensing and repeater-grade remote controlled-add}
A phase-stable path ancilla controls $U$ and $U^2$ on the nine-mode register.
Uniform mode randomization and path-$X/Y$ readout estimate
$\operatorname{Tr}(U^k)/9$, while the determinant is tracked from the programmed
Clifford word. The four quadratures reconstruct
$\Theta_k=\operatorname{Tr}(U^k)^9/\det(U^k)$ for $k=1,2$. A conservative
$99\%$-confidence design uses $29{,}579$ events per quadrature; phase drift and
visibility remain experimental calibration obligations.

For isotropic qutrit Bell pairs, bilateral SUM purification obeys
\[
 P_{\rm acc}=\frac{27F^2-6F+11}{32},\qquad
 F'=\frac{33F^2-2F+1}{27F^2-6F+11}.
\]
Its fixed points are $1/9$, $1/3$, and $1$, and it improves fidelity precisely
for $F>1/3$. Nested swapping, memory decay, classical latency, and heralded
erasures are explicit in the controller. Under the stated scenario, a
$1{,}280\,$km link is optimized by eight segments with elementary and post-swap
purification, yielding a distillable end-to-end pair at a sub-hertz rate. This is
a modeled design point, not a measured repeater or fault-tolerance threshold.
