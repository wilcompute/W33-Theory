```

  (νR optional/BSM: not counted in minimal SM)

But with both chiralities and antiparticles:
  u:  uL(3) + uR(3) = 6 DoF

  6 quarks × 3 colors + 3 leptons = 18+3 = 21 Weyl DoF per chirality
  Both chiralities: 21×2 = 42...
```

**36 = 12 quark color states × 3 (u,d,s-like) — no, wrong generation.**
