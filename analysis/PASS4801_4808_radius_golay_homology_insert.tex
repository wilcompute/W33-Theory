The 45-point $GQ(4,2)$ triangle carrier from Passes 4795--4800 supports a sharper local-to-global code decomposition, while the binary $H_{10}$ covering-radius problem also closes exactly.

\paragraph{Exact covering radius.}
Pass 4794 had proved $14\le \rho(H_{10})\le 15$ and exhibited a distance-$14$ coset.  A distance-$15$ coset would contain a leader $x$ of weight exactly $15$.  For each $c\in H_{10}$,
\[
 d(x,c)=15+\mathrm{wt}(c)-2|\operatorname{supp}(x)\cap\operatorname{supp}(c)|,
\]
so a weight-$15$ leader is exactly a $40$-variable cardinality-feasibility problem.  Coordinate transitivity fixes one selected coordinate.  Its intersection with the corresponding weight-$12$ neighborhood codeword has size $a\in\{1,\ldots,6\}$.  The order-$648$ point stabilizer has respectively
\[
1,2,3,6,6,7
\]
orbits on those neighbor subsets.  Exhaustive exact propagation over the resulting $25$ symmetry representatives rejects every case.  Hence
\[
\boxed{\rho(H_{10})=14.}
\]
The independent PySAT producer \texttt{w33\_pass4801\_h10\_covering\_radius\_sat.py} encodes the same finite decision directly.

The $270$ columns of $B$ are distinct weight-three vectors.  Consequently the ordered $X/Z$ syndrome pair uniquely identifies all
\[

\]

0\longrightarrow L/C\longrightarrow C^\perp/C\longrightarrow H_1(\operatorname{Levi};\mathbb F_3)\longrightarrow0,

\paragraph{First nonlocal logical sector.}
Modulo $L$, each $K_5$ block has $81$ possible point-sum syndromes with minimum lift costs distributed as $1$ state of cost $0$, $20$ of cost $1$, and $60$ of cost $2$.  An exact symmetry-broken MILP excludes every nonzero global homology class of triangle-lift weight at most $5$.  Weight $6$ is attained by induced $K_{3,3}$ subgraphs of the $27$-line intersection graph: opposite signs are assigned to the two three-line parts, and each line contributes the triangle of its three intersections with the opposite part.  There are exactly $360$ induced $K_{3,3}$ subgraphs, furnishing a canonical projective witness family.  Therefore
\[
\boxed{d_{\mathrm{lift}}(C^\perp/L)=6.}
\]
The $360$ witnesses are not claimed to exhaust the full weight-six quotient shell.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}

This is an exact finite-code statement.  It does not imply a fault-tolerance threshold, a microscopic Hamiltonian, or a physical energy scale.
