# BT1548 E6 Cubic vs K4/Toroidal Signs

The E6 cubic sign tensor has profile 23/22, while K4/toroidal carriers have 12/12 and 96/96 profiles.

The E6 profile has imbalance 1. The K4/toroidal profiles have imbalance 0.

A simple aggregate sign-profile normalization bridge is therefore obstructed. Any deeper bridge must use Weyl-equivariant structure-constant signs or additional gauge data.
