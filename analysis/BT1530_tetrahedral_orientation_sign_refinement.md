# BT1530 Tetrahedral Orientation/Sign Refinement

The K4 24-flag carrier receives a balanced sign rule:

`flag_sign = vertex orientation sign * oriented face-boundary sign`.

The carrier has sign profile 12 positive and 12 negative.

Each pointed 12-flag half has six positive and six negative flags, and each tetrahedral edge has a 2/2 sign split.

This supplies a consistent sign refinement for the carrier; it does not prove a unique physical orientation convention for the toroidal realizations.
