\section{The hidden torus as a finite d'Alembertian}
\label{sec:bt3385-hidden-torus-dalembertian}
For the hidden $27$-state signed-torus block,
\[
 D=A(C_3)_{3}-A(C_3)_{1}-A(C_3)_{2}.
\]
Since $\Delta_i=2I-A(C_3)_i$, one has the exact identity
\[
 \boxed{D+2I=\Delta_1+\Delta_2-\Delta_3.}
\]
Thus the shifted hidden block is a finite discrete d'Alembertian on $C_3^3$, with two positive Laplacian factors and one negative factor.  At Fourier frequency $k\in\mathbb F_3^3$,
\[
 \lambda(k)=3\bigl([k_1\ne0]+[k_2\ne0]-[k_3\ne0]\bigr),
\]
so its spectrum is
\[
 \boxed{6^4,\;3^{12},\;0^9,\;(-3)^2}.
\]
The rank is $18$ and the nullity is $9$.  The null modes consist of the constant character together with four frequencies satisfying $k_1=0$, $k_2,k_3\ne0$, and four satisfying $k_2=0$, $k_1,k_3\ne0$.  Hence the exact null profile is $1+4+4$.  This is a finite signed-graph identity only; no physical spacetime, Lorentzian continuum, causal cone, or laboratory claim is made.
