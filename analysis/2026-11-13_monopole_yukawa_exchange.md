# Native semileptonic Yukawa exchange on the W33 monopole

The neutral up- and down-Higgs directions mediate a flavor tensor between the four Weyl charge slots of the proposed winding-saturation operator. This calculation uses native Yukawa matrices evaluated along the real core and explicit regular trial fermion packets. It extends the two-Higgs radial inverse to different, matrix-valued sources at its two ends. [Winding charge ledger](2026-11-13_monopole_rotor_winding_modes.md) · [Scalar inverse](2026-11-13_monopole_higgs_radial.md)

The quark slots are Q of weight (1,−1,1,1) and U^c of weight (−1,1,0,−4). The lepton slots are L of weight (0,0,−1,−3) and E^c of weight (0,0,0,6). Each pair plus its corresponding neutral Higgs direction has zero full Cartan weight. The fermion flavor frames are orthonormalizations of the three light vacuum lifts. For L the down-Higgs covector defines the lepton kernel in the upper weak component, which is then transported by weak lowering. At each sample the source matrices are third derivatives of the complete superpotential at the local monopole background, with two fermion flavor directions and the radius-dependent horizontal Higgs direction. Symmetric polarization in the two fermion directions and a complex step in the Higgs direction compute these derivatives. The replay checks a second polarization step and verifies full vacuum rank at both ends. [Native light Yukawas](2026-11-10_light_yukawa_completion.md) · [Supersymmetric Yukawa convention](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9709356)

In reduced radial coordinates the two trial fermion factors have shape r exp(−r²/(2w²)). Their product divided by r gives the reduced scalar source envelope, up to normalization. The computed exchange tensor C has four three-dimensional flavor slots and contracts the up and lepton sources with the static holomorphic scalar inverse. Antisymmetrizing three copies of C independently in all four slots tests saturation of the twelve flavor variables. For a factorized C = A ⊗ B this cubic invariant is 3! det(A) det(B), up to the choice of Grassmann ordering. This calculation tests all components of the tensor and allows cancellations between them. [Semileptonic operator convention](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.4884)

All six packet and mesh combinations gave a nonzero flavor contraction with either boundary condition. With the appended free exterior, the finest mesh gave absolute cubic coefficients of 4.53×10⁻¹⁴, 8.52×10⁻¹², and 7.50×10⁻¹³ at widths 0.08, 0.16, and 0.32. These depend on the chosen packet normalization. An independent permutation sum reproduces the cubic invariant. Keeping only the leading singular component of C at width 0.32 overestimates the magnitude by a factor of 4.28, even though that component carries more than 97 percent of the squared Frobenius norm. The sign of the contraction depends on flavor orientation.

The calculation freezes the gauge background and projects onto two scalar directions. The radial fermion packets are prescribed, with the flavor vectors carried into the core as fixed lifts. The result therefore tests whether the native flavor structure permits exchange and winding saturation in this projection. A scattering coefficient needs radial solutions of the coupled Weyl equations with their physical angular contractions and flux normalization. A compact winding contribution also needs the actual winding-mode wavefunctions and integration over the rotor history.
