\subsection{Carrier-spinorial symmetry, gauge-action obstruction, and finite sheet dressing}

The intrinsic deck-odd $16$ is a genuine central-sign representation, but only
of the vector stabilizer of the Segre carrier.  Direct generation gives
\[
 |\PSp(4,3)|=25920,\qquad |G_{\rm Segre}^{\rm proj}|=48,
 \qquad |\Sp(4,3)|=51840,\qquad |\widetilde G_{\rm Segre}|=96,
\]
and the central $-I\in\Sp(4,3)$ acts on the signed space as $-I_{16}$.
Consequently this representation cannot descend to an ordinary $\PSp(4,3)$
module and is not either of the previously constructed $D_5$ half-spin
$16$-modules.  Under $\widetilde G_{\rm Segre}$ it decomposes over $\mathbb C$ as
\[
 V_{16}\cong2A\oplus2\overline A,\qquad \dim A=4,
\]
with magnetic eigenspace pattern
\[
 -6:A,\qquad -3:\overline A,\qquad 3:A,\qquad 6:\overline A.
\]

The equivariant complex commutant has dimension eight.  Its real symmetric and
skew parts both have dimension four, so the $K$-odd Hermitian Hamiltonians
compatible with the full carrier stabilizer form a four-real-dimensional space.
An explicit equivariant particle-hole-compatible perturbation moves the
unperturbed $6/3=2$ level ratio.  Thus that ratio is an exact feature of the
chosen magnetic operator, not a symmetry-protected mass prediction.

The $E_6$ bundle selector also sharpens negatively.  Local $\mathbb Z_3$ gauge
covariance preserves both the horizontal thirty-six-term cubic sum and the nine
vertical full-fiber cubics, hence gauge symmetry permits
\[
 S_{\rm cubic}=g_H\sum_{T\in H_{36}}C_T+g_V\sum_{b=1}^{9}V_b.
\]
Therefore gauge invariance and total $\mathbb Z_3$ neutrality do not force
$g_V=0$.  Suppression of the vertical nine requires an additional
horizontality/locality, $L_\infty$/Jacobi, representation-theoretic, or dynamical
principle.  Moreover the affine base has nine sites and complete one-skeleton
$K_9$ with thirty-six links; its incidence rank is eight and cycle-space
dimension twenty-eight.  The bad nine naturally occupy site/$C^0$ degree rather
than link/$C^1$ connection degree, obstructing their identification with
propagating gauge bosons by count alone.

The simplest cover-based continuum iteration fails exactly.  The connected
$270$-vertex Pass4719 carrier is already the bipartite double cover
$G\times K_2$, and
\[
 (G\times K_2)\times K_2
 =G\times(K_2\times K_2)
 =2(G\times K_2).
\]
Hence the next naive $C_2$ lift has two connected components and duplicates the
existing spectral support rather than generating a new scale.  A connected
refinement tower must use a new nontrivial voltage/cohomology class at each
level.

The signed block has the Majorana/BdG algebraic form $H=iS$ with $S$ real skew
and $KHK^{-1}=-H$, $K^2=1$.  Searching for a second carrier-equivariant
antiunitary $T=UK$ commuting with $H$ gives the simultaneous linear equations
$UR_g=R_gU$ and $UH+HU=0$, whose solution space has dimension zero.  Thus the
carrier supplies no equivariant Kramers $T^2=-1$ symmetry; in tenfold-way
vocabulary its finite algebraic pattern is class-D-like rather than DIII-like.
This does not constitute a relativistic spin-statistics theorem.

For the $q=5$ selected cover, every $1+12$ permutation action satisfies the
module identity
\[
 \operatorname{centered}(1\oplus\operatorname{Perm}_{12})
 \cong \operatorname{Perm}_{12}.
\]
Under such an intertwiner the invariant direction
$12e_{\rm fixed}-\sum e_i$ maps to the constant vector $\sum e_i$ on the moving
orbit.  Therefore, even if the still-pending direct moving-$12$ GAP conjugator
closes the Latin/$F_4$ action gate, the $q=5$ distinguished direction can map
only to a collective $q=3$ orbit-average singlet, not to a distinguished $q=3$
point; the two isomorphic $q=3$ twelve-orbits also leave the target nonunique.

Finally, ordering the intrinsic vector lift by its two sheets gives
\[
 H_{32}=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ B&A\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 H_+=A+B,\qquad H_-=A-B.
\]
Up to the $\omega\leftrightarrow\omega^2$ convention, $A$ is precisely the old
section-dependent sixteen-state magnetic operator.  It therefore has a precise
new role as a bare one-sheet block rather than an intrinsic projective
observable.  Eliminating the opposite sheet gives the exact Feshbach operator
\[
 \boxed{H_{\rm eff}(E)=A+B(E-A)^{-1}B}.
\]
Since $[A,B]\ne0$, the self-energy is not a scalar renormalization of $A$ and
has poles at its fifteen distinct one-sheet energies.  This is an exact finite
energy-dependent dressing/decimation flow, not yet a Wilsonian spacetime RG.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
These statements are finite group-action, representation, bundle-gauge,
graph-cover, cochain, BdG-algebra, and resolvent results.  They do not prove
physical spin-statistics, a Standard-Model mass prediction, physical gauge
bosons, a continuum Weyl law, a Wilsonian fixed point, or a $q=5$ physical
vacuum identification.
