Holotrade closure contract was executed without sampling.  With

Smith-normal-form parity obstruction on every FI-cancelling ray.  The singlets that
are never even carry $3(B-L)=\pm3$ fixed by the Standard Model itself: every D-flat

exactly the case in which matter parity cannot survive (Martin's criterion).  An
independent scan of all $1024$ $\mathbb{Z}_2$ characters of the charge lattice that are
odd on $q,u^c,d^c,e^c$ gives no even D-flat direction either.  The exact machinery
reproduces the prior census of $23+105$ D-flat models and $88$ $B-L$ models.  Scope:
singlet directions; hidden nonabelian composite directions remain the one loophole.
