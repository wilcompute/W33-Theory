# BT1551 Enriched Jordan Product Using E6 Signs

The 12+12 incidence product was enriched with a deterministic sign shadow.

This creates a signed matching with profile 7 plus and 5 minus over 12 nonzero pairs, but the support is still just a matching with 132 zero entries.

It still lacks U/V maps, triple closure, pair identities, and a canonical projection from cubic or mixed-triple data.
