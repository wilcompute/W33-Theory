# Passes 5675–5682 — external prior-art boundary

This packet does not claim novelty for the general BdG/class-D, Pfaffian, graph 2-lift, or voltage-cover machinery.  The new claims are only the exact specialization of those structures to the repository's W33 carriers.

## Class-D / Majorana / Pfaffian language

Alexei Kitaev, *Periodic table for topological insulators and superconductors*, arXiv:0901.2686.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.2686

Kitaev formulates quadratic free-fermion Hamiltonians in a Majorana basis using real skew matrices and classifies gapped free-fermion phases by symmetry class and dimension.  Pass5680 uses this standard zero-dimensional class-D/Pfaffian vocabulary only to test the finite deck-odd block.  The result is a W33-specific negative: the stabilizer-enforced fourfold level multiplicities make the Pfaffian sign identical on all three gapped multiplicity-matrix signature components.

## Graph 2-lifts and signed spectra

Yonatan Bilu and Nathan Linial, *Constructing expander graphs by 2-lifts and discrepancy vs. spectral gap*, arXiv:math/0312022.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0312022

A 2-lift contains the base spectrum plus a new spectrum controlled by a signed adjacency matrix.  Pass5678 uses exactly this standard decomposition.  Its W33-specific contribution is the deterministic Levi single-chord tower calculation and the resulting two-edge bottleneck; it does not claim a new general theorem about 2-lifts.

## Voltage-derived graph covers

Sophia Gonet, *The Jacobian of Cyclic Voltage Covers of K_n*, arXiv:2108.02022, gives a modern primary-source treatment of graphs derived from edge voltages in a finite group, including single-voltage constructions.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.02022

Pass5677 uses only the standard voltage-cover idea and the elementary connectivity criterion that a nontrivial cycle voltage generates the Z2 deck group.  The W33-specific content is that the Levi graph starts with beta_1=81 and therefore supports an indefinitely iterated connected tower when a fresh nonzero cohomology class is chosen after every lift.
