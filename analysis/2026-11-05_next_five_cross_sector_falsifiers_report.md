## Front 1 — Proton Z9 survives added scalar charges, but scalar-only axion quality is insufficient

The previous anomaly-free \(X=3(B-3L_e)\) model has \(X_T=-9,X_S=-18\), and both vevs preserve a \(\mathbb Z_9\) discrete gauge group. With additional scalar \(A\) carrying \(X_A=9n\), its vev still preserves a \(\mathbb Z_9\). Every gauge-invariant phase-sensitive scalar monomial linear in \(A\) has integer exponents \(k,\ell\) of the scalar fields \(S,T\) obeying
\[
X_A + k X_S+\ell X_T=0,\qquad n=2k+\ell.
\]
Allow conjugates for negative exponents. The minimum degree is exactly
\[
d_{A,\min}=1+\min_{k,\ell\in\mathbb Z,\;2k+\ell=n} (|k|+|\ell|)
=1+\left\lceil \frac{|n|}{2}\right\rceil.
\]
The executable independently enumerates all 32 candidates \(n=1,\ldots,32\), including \(X_A=216\) (\(n=24\)), for which the first **A-dependent scalar-only** operator occurs at dimension **13**, e.g. \(A S^{12}\).

**But the old low-dimension \(S^\dagger T^2\) remains allowed and breaks the original scalar-relative phase.** The calculation does not establish that the additional phase of \(A\) is a physical QCD axion: the eaten gauge mode, independent global phase symmetries, colored heavy fermion Yukawas, mixed PQ-breaking operators, discrete gauge anomalies, dark-matter cosmology and heavy relic decays remain to be analyzed. A dimension-13 *scalar-only* A invariant is not a dimension-13 *full-model PQ-violation* theorem.

A single standard-model-vectorlike but \(X\)-chiral QCD triplet pair \((P,P^c)\) with mass Yukawa \(A P P^c\) has \(X(P)+X(P^c)=-X_A\). Its \(SU(3)_c^2U(1)_X\) mixed anomaly contribution is \(-X_A/2\), nonzero for \(X_A\ne0\). Introducing an opposite-chiral pair with \(A^\dagger P'P'^c\) gives \(+X_A/2\), canceling this *one* mixed anomaly. If **both** Dirac masses are generated by the same axion phase and the accidental PQ acts identically to the scalar's phase on both Yukawas, their QCD-PQ anomaly contributions also cancel: a viable construction therefore needs a different PQ-charge structure or heavier sector, not merely a second mirror pair. Other \(X^3\), mixed Abelian, and gravitational anomalies require explicit checks.

This is an exact necessary-condition/obstruction analysis, **not** a successful high-quality axion model. Compare Davighi, Greljo, Ponce Díaz, *Axion Quality from Exact Proton Stability* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01712 ; Di Luzio et al., *Reconciling axion quality with post-inflation cosmology* (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16110.

## Front 2 — What an actual predictive flavor symmetry has to do

In the unconstrained type-I seesaw with \(X=3(B-3L_e)\), the general allowed symmetric Majorana matrix remains fully populated, so previous algebra shows no generic PMNS prediction.

Impose a **new** generalized CP symmetry—mu/tau reflection—on the effective light Majorana mass matrix. Its invariant form is
\[
P_{\mu\tau}M_\nu^*P_{\mu\tau}=M_\nu,\quad
M_\nu=\begin{pmatrix}
a & b & b^*\\ b & c & d\\ b^* & d & c^*
\end{pmatrix},
\quad a,d\in\mathbb R,\quad b,c\in\mathbb C.
\]
With nondegenerate masses, nonzero solar/reactor angles, and the charged-lepton mass basis correctly aligned, the eigenmodes of \(M_\nu^\dagger M_\nu\) satisfy \(|U_{\mu i}|=|U_{\tau i}|\). This forces
\[
\theta_{23}=45^\circ,\qquad |\sin\delta_{\rm CP}|=1.
\]
Five independently selected complex mass matrices verify these identities to numerical precision. Perturbing the \(e\tau\) entry off the generalized-CP invariant texture shifts the recovered atmospheric angle to approximately \(48.396^\circ\), breaking the equal-amplitude prediction.

**Important:** We *supplied* this generalized CP symmetry; we did NOT derive it from the existing \(X\)-charges, Higgs sector, spontaneous alignment, or high-scale seesaw. The sample complex matrices do not satisfy all NuFIT mass-splitting/angle constraints and are not data fits. The calculation shows the kind of parameter-reducing constraint a unified theory would need to predict CP, rather than merely accommodate it. A true advance requires a consistent enlarged symmetry and observed-angle likelihood validation.

Prior art: generalized mu/tau reflection neutrino CP literature, including Grimus and Lavoura (2004); NuFIT v6.1 comparison.

## Front 4 — Why the causal-action path-sum measure matters: exact multiplicities

Previously the path sum covered all 15-bit forward-edge presentations on six chronologically ordered events, \(2^{15}=32{,}768\) possibilities, assigning each equal weight before taking its transitive closure. But different forward-edge DAGs can encode **identical causal orders**. Deduplicating the transitive closures yields exactly **4,824 distinct naturally labeled partial orders** on the fixed chronological index.

An exact combinatorial identity explains the bias. For any such order \(C\), let \(R(C)\) be its number of comparable pairs and \(L(C)\) its number of Hasse-diagram cover relations. Every DAG representation must contain all the cover edges, while each of the \(R-L\) transitively implied edges may independently be included or omitted. Hence
\[
m(C)=2^{R(C)-L(C)}.
\]
The producer enumerates all 32,768 histories, confirms this formula for all 4,824 distinct posets, and finds multiplicities as large as **1,024**. Uniform **DAG-presentation** weighting is therefore exponentially biased toward orders with more transitively implied edges.

The impact is not small. For the previous 4D BDG **bracket** \(B_4=N-N_1+9N_2-16N_3+8N_4\), at the arbitrary unit test phase coefficient \(\kappa=1\):
\[
|Z_{\rm DAG}| \simeq0.1149878020,\qquad
|Z_{\rm distinct\,posets}|\simeq0.0273445250.
\]
The mean longest-chain statistic shifts from **3.8617** (DAG measure) to **2.8804** (distinct-poset measure). This is a fully exact finite enumeration and an important guard against reading physical meaning into an arbitrary graph-presentation counting measure.

**Neither** equal-weight measure is a physical diffeomorphism-invariant causal-set path integral: fixed natural labels are not quotiented by all isomorphisms/automorphisms, the BDG action needs actual dimensional prefactors, boundary terms and scaling, and the dynamical quantum measure has not been derived. There is no continuum \(3+1\)D Einstein limit or observation of manifoldlike-order suppression in this experiment.

Prior art: Sorkin causal-set sum-over-histories; *Causal sets and an emerging continuum*, GRG (2024), https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-024-03281-1 ; Adamson and Wallden, Phys Rev Research 8 023188 (2026), https://doi.org/10.1103/qdst-yrh6.

## Front 5 — Remove the externally scheduled SWAP operations, enforce energy conservation and Gauss law

The previous 4-matter-site, 3-link \(\mathbb Z_2\) lattice-gauge sector has 128 microscopic matter/link basis states but an exact 8-dimensional selected physical Gauss sector, corresponding to three logical link qubits. A local *dressed* link flip simultaneously toggles one link's electric \(X\) eigenvalue and both adjacent matter parities; it maps one physical gauge state to another. An undressed link flip would violate two local Gauss constraints.

Introduce three external gauge-neutral radiation qubits and a **single fixed Hermitian exchange Hamiltonian**
\[
H_{\rm emit}=\sum_{j=1}^3 g_j
(\sigma^+_{{\rm source},j}\sigma^-_{{\rm rad},j}
+\sigma^-_{{\rm source},j}\sigma^+_{{\rm rad},j}).
\]
The source operators are defined through the exact 8-dimensional Gauss isometry; **no** state leaves the physical gauge sector. Let \(N_{\rm total}=N_{\rm source}+N_{\rm radiation}\) and \(H_0=\omega N_{\rm total}\). This is energy conserving: \([H_{\rm emit},H_0]=0\) exactly, in the chosen *excitation-energy* model. Crucially, there are **no externally scheduled emission gates**—all three source/radiation pairs interact autonomously and continuously under the same Hamiltonian.

Start with three gauge-logical source excitations and the radiation vacuum. For equal couplings \(g_j=1\), entropy and source energy evolve as exact independent quantum Rabi oscillations:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
t & S_{\rm rad}\;\text{(bits)} & \langle E_{\rm source}\rangle/\omega\\\hline
0 & 0 &3\\
\pi/8&1.8026&2.5607\\
\pi/4&3&1.5\\
3\pi/8&1.8026&0.4393\\
\pi/2&0&0\\
\pi&0&3
\end{array}
\]
At \(t=\pi/2\), the coherent information has transferred to radiation, and the source is empty. But by \(t=\pi\), excitations and source energy have **returned completely**: a closed small Hamiltonian reabsorbs the radiation. With slightly detuned couplings \((1,1.03,1.11)\), the radiation entropy remains **0.21494 bits** at \(t=\pi/2\). Exact perfect discharge depended on finely matching the couplings. GHZ-state superposition and 24 dressed-link locality checks also pass.

**What this does and does not solve:** a fixed, Gauss-constrained, unitary, excitation-energy-conserving emission model, stronger than the previous externally clocked SWAP circuit. It is **not** a dynamical black-hole horizon, ADM energy, spacetime backreaction, a local 3+1D QFT, a thermal Hawking spectrum, or an irreversible evaporation mechanism. The central barrier is now sharp: a physical coarse-grained arrow of emission needs a large radiation continuum, causal boundary and dynamically derived coupling density; finite Rabi modes recur.
