## Result A: Forty exact W33 inequalities and the pure-state spectral selection rule

Producer: analysis/w33_20261010_toe42_purity_W33_spectral_identity.py

Frozen data: data/w33_20261010_toe42_w33_purity_variance_spectral.json

For a trace-one two-qutrit density operator \(\rho\), label the 40 projective nonidentity Pauli operators \(W_p\), one for every point of \(W(3,3)\), and set

\[
q_p=2\left|\operatorname{Tr}(\rho W_p)\right|^2.
\]

Let \(A\) denote the W33 commuting adjacency with eigenvalues \(12^1,2^{24},(-4)^{15}\). Fix \(p\), write \(\Pi_j\) for the three rank-three spectral projections of \(W_p\), put \(\rho_j=\Pi_j\rho\Pi_j\), and \(t_j=\operatorname{Tr}\rho_j\).

The 27 Weyl operators in \(p^\perp\subset\mathbb F_3^4\) give a Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonal basis of the block-diagonal commutant. Parseval gives

\[
\sum_{v\in p^\perp}|\operatorname{Tr}(\rho W_v)|^2
=9\sum_{j=0}^2\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_j^2).
\]

The three-element subgroup generated by \(p\) contributes \(1+2|\operatorname{Tr}(\rho W_p)|^2\). The Fourier coefficients of the probabilities \(t_j\) obey
\(3\sum_jt_j^2=1+2|\operatorname{Tr}(\rho W_p)|^2\).
Subtracting the subgroup contribution proves the **40 exact state inequalities**

\[
\boxed{(Aq-2q-2\mathbf1)_p
=9\sum_{j=0}^2\big[\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_j^2)-t_j^2\big]\le0.}
\]

Every \(\rho_j\) is positive semidefinite, so \(\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_j^2)\le t_j^2\). In a **pure state**, all three blocks are rank at most one, hence

\[
\boxed{Aq=2q+2\mathbf1,\qquad P_{-4}q=0.}
\]

The complete Weyl basis gives \(\sum_{p=1}^{40}q_p=9\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2-1\); for pure states it equals 8. Therefore \(q-\mathbf1/5\) lies **exactly in the 24-dimensional eigenvalue-2 adjacency space**, with no contribution to W33's 15-dimensional eigenvalue-\(-4\) sector.

For arbitrary density matrices, summing the 40 inequalities yields

\[
\boxed{\sum_p(Aq-2q-2\mathbf1)_p=90(\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2-1).}
\]

The sum is zero **if and only if** \(\rho\) is pure. This is a W33 commutation-context *purity witness*. It uses established Pauli tomography/Parseval information and does not imply a new measurement technology.
