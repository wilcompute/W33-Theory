\section{Selector Gelfand pair, Terwilliger algebra, and Schur defect}
\label{sec:bt1360-bt1364-gelfand-terwilliger}

\paragraph{Exact scope.}
Let
\[
X=\{(L,M):L\text{ is a totally isotropic line of }W(3,3),
\ M\text{ is one of the three perfect matchings of }L\}.
\]
Thus \(|X|=40\cdot3=120\).  The following theorem concerns only the exact
finite permutation and matrix algebras on \(X\).

\begin{theorem}[Selector Gelfand--Terwilliger theorem]
Let \(G=\operatorname{PGSp}(4,3)\cong W(E_6)\) act naturally on \(X\), and
let \(H=G_x\) be the stabilizer of one selector.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item
\[
|G|=51840,\qquad |H|=432,
\]
and the five \(H\)-subdegrees are
\[
1,\ 2,\ 36,\ 27,\ 54.
\]
The corresponding orbital algebra is commutative, so \((G,H)\) is a
Gelfand pair.  The multiplicity-free constituent degrees of
\(\mathbb C[X]\) are
\[
1,\ 15,\ 24,\ 20,\ 60.
\]

\item
For the Terwilliger algebra \(T(x)\), generated by the Bose--Mesner
algebra and the five diagonal shell idempotents at \(x\),
\[
\dim_{\mathbb Q}T(x)=79,
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb Q}Z(T(x))=10.
\]
An exact word basis closes by word length six.  Of the elementary triple
products \(E_i^\ast A_jE_k^\ast\), exactly \(53\) are nonzero, so
\(26\) further directions are generated by multiplication.

\item
The stabilizer \(H\) has \(83\) orbitals on \(X\times X\).  Hence
\[
T(x)\subsetneq\operatorname{End}_{H}(\mathbb C[X]),
\qquad
83-79=4.
\]
The Schur closure of \(T(x)\) is the full \(83\)-dimensional orbital
algebra.  The defect is localized as
\[
(2,2):\ 8-6=2,
\qquad
(4,4):\ 16-14=2,
\]
and every other shell block has zero defect.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Two-prime split fingerprint.}
Modulo each of the good primes \(1000003\) and \(1000033\), a
deterministic generic central element splits into ten distinct linear
factors and gives simple block sizes
\[
1,1,1,2,2,3,3,3,4,5.
\]
The associated module multiplicities are
\[
3,12,14,1,2,4,4,8,8,1.
\]
This is recorded as an exact finite-field fingerprint, not as a rational
splitting-field theorem.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The theorem supplies no preferred selector inside a three-element fiber,
no \(H_4\) or \(600\)-cell adjacency, and no cosmological, Standard-Model,
or laboratory consequence.  The 2024 flag scheme of
Colangelo--Monzillo--Siciliano concerns \(160\) incident point-line flags
of a generalized quadrangle and is a nearby but different construction;
no literature-priority claim is made here.
