\subsection{Finite curvature versus a K3 realization}
\label{subsec:k3-realization-boundary}

The current repository contains an explicit finite curved precomplex on the $45$-point
transport quotient,
\[
 C^0(\mathbb F_3^2)\xrightarrow{d_0}C^1(\mathbb F_3^2)
 \xrightarrow{d_1}C^2(\mathbb F_3^2),
 \qquad K=d_1d_0\pmod3.
\]
Its adapted off-diagonal curvature block is a genuine internally constructed object; the
current source records rank $36$, and its triangle-row localization is supported on $2428$
transport triangles.  These statements are finite consequences of the W33 transport data.

They must, however, be distinguished from a realization by an actual K3 geometric/cochain
object.  The historical $2428\times36$ witness scan was corrected because it scanned a newly
allocated zero matrix, and the later deformation script explicitly inserts a nonzero entry by
hand while acknowledging that existence ``on the actual K3 side'' remains open.  Accordingly
we record the evidence boundary
\[
 \boxed{\text{finite curved precomplex closed; K3 realization open}.}
\]
A K3 promotion requires a named source object and hash, an explicit basis/coordinate map into
the finite cochain carrier, compatibility of the K3 differential/connection with $d_0,d_1$,
and an independent comparison of the induced curvature with the finite off-diagonal block.
No dimension or support-count coincidence substitutes for that map.
