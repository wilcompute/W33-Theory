\subsection{Exceptional-$S_6$ outer doily transfer clock}
Let $D$ be the $15\times15$ syntheme--duad incidence matrix and let
$P\in GL_{15}(\mathbb Z)$ be the exact Pass~1859 permutation matrix realizing
the exceptional outer automorphism of $S_6$. Define $T=P^{\mathsf T}D$.
Then
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}T=
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}T=10,
 \qquad \dim\ker T=5,
\]
and its ten nonzero Smith invariants are all $1$. Its exact polynomials are
\[
 \chi_T(x)=x^5(x-3)(x-2)(x+2)^2(x^2+4)(x^4+16),
 \qquad
 \mu_T(x)=x(x-3)(x^8-256).
\]
On the balanced nonzero image
\[
 W=\operatorname{im}(T)\cap\mathbf1^\perp,
 \qquad \dim W=9,
\]
one has
\[
 \boxed{(T/2)^8=I_W},
 \qquad
 (T^8-256I)T=1261J,
\]
and
\[
 \operatorname{tr}(T^n)=3^n+(-2)^n+8\,2^n[8\mid n]
 \qquad(n\ge1).
\]
For the exceptional automorphism $\alpha$ frozen in Pass~1859,
\[
 T\rho(g)=\rho(\alpha^{-1}(g))T
 \qquad(g\in S_6).
\]
Finally
\[
 T^{\mathsf T}T=D^{\mathsf T}D,
 \qquad
 TT^{\mathsf T}=T^{\mathsf T}T.
\]
Thus $T$ is normal but nonsymmetric and $T/2$ is orthogonal on $W$. The fold
preserves the doily singular spectrum $9^1+4^9+0^5$, and
$T^{\mathsf T}T-3I$ is the $\operatorname{SRG}(15,6,1,3)$ point graph of
$W(2)$. The clock terminology is strictly finite and representation-theoretic;
no physical time evolution or continuum dynamics is inferred.
