# BT654 Minus-Sheet Trace Bridge

BT651 separates the central recurrence-module sheet from the Levi coordinate
carriers:

Pi_minus is central in the endpoint recurrence module.

P_O is an internal coordinate carrier inside the 160 Levi flags.

The bridge is therefore not a projector equality.  It is a trace bridge.

## Central recurrence side

The endpoint recurrence has a central minus sheet with scalar trace contribution

24(-1)^n.

Equivalently, the Lagrange idempotent Pi_minus has amplitude 24 in the endpoint
sequence.

## Coordinate-carrier side

BT644 and BT652 show that the internal S4 action has six regular 24-flag orbits.
For each regular orbit O_i, define the sign operator S_i=-I on that 24-dimensional
carrier.  Then

Tr(S_i^n)=24(-1)^n.

Thus each O_i individually realizes the same trace law.

## Canonical all-six bridge

Without choosing a hinge/base flag, use the averaged all-six trace functional:

T_minus(n) = (1/6) sum_{i=1}^{6} Tr(S_i^n).

Then

T_minus(n) = (1/6) * 6 * 24(-1)^n = 24(-1)^n.

So the bridge is:

Pi_minus endpoint trace -> averaged trace of six internal S4 sign carriers.

## Boundary

This is canonical at the trace level after the S4 subgroup is fixed.  It does not
claim that Pi_minus equals the coordinate projector onto any one orbit, or onto
the 144-flag union.  BT652 shows that even the 144-union is structured but not
central.
