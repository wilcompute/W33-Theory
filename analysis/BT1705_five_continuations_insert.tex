\subsection{Integral-frame five-continuation theorem}
Let $M$ be the $540\times240$ frame--edge incidence matrix and let $K$ be the
$45\times240$ incidence matrix of the intrinsic induced $K_{4,4}$ octets.
The $30$-dimensional Bockstein torsion module has a nonsplit composition series
with factors
\[
  1,\ 6,\ 8,\ 1,\ 14,
\]
where the head $14$ and the factor $6$ are absolutely irreducible over
$\mathbb F_2$, while the irreducible factor $8$ has endomorphism field
$\mathbb F_4$.

The octet exact-cardinality relations yield $240$ independent global XOR
strengthenings of the nine-colour frame-resolution system:
\[
  \operatorname{rank}_{2}:2100\longrightarrow2340.
\]
The certified four-cover packing and its anti-symplectic mirror form two free
$PSp(4,3)$ orbits of size $25920$, fused into one $PGSp(4,3)$ orbit of size
$51840$; their binary Bockstein signatures coincide.

For the free rank-$15$ lattice, the saturated unsigned-to-oriented transition
has
\[
  \operatorname{SNF}=1^{14}\oplus2,
  \qquad |\det|=2.
\]
Finally, the joint action on $540$ frames and $45$ octets is a two-fibre
coherent configuration of rank
\[
  32+3+5+5=45.
\]
For the half-incidence cross orbital $J$,
\[
  (H-32I)(H-14I)(H-2I)J=0,
  \qquad \operatorname{rank}[J,HJ]=69.
\]
These are exact finite-module and lattice statements. They do not decide the
existence of a global nine-cover resolution.
