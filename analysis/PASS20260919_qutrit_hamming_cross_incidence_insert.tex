\subsection{The translated \(CZ_3\) cross bundle has determinant \(80\)}
\label{sec:20260919-qutrit-hamming-cross-incidence}

The nine translated \(CZ_3\) zero-phase frames from the Hamming packet theorem
form a square incidence operator on \(\mathbb F_3^2\).  For general \(q\), let
\[
 C_{a,b}=\{(x,y):x=a\ \hbox{or}\ y=b\}
\]
and define
\[
 B_q[(a,b),(x,y)]=1_{(x,y)\in C_{a,b}}.
\]
Then
\[
 B_q=I+A(H(2,q))
    =J_q\otimes I_q+I_q\otimes J_q-I_{q^2}.
\]
The Hamming spectrum gives
\[
 \operatorname{spec}(B_q)
 =\{2q-1\}^{1}\cup\{q-1\}^{2(q-1)}
   \cup\{-1\}^{(q-1)^2},
\]
and hence
\[
 \det B_q=(-1)^{(q-1)^2}(2q-1)(q-1)^{2(q-1)}.
\]
At \(q=3\),
\[
 \boxed{\det B_3=80=3^4-1=\dim\mathfrak{sl}_9.}
\]
The equality with \(q^4-1\) is unique for integer \(q\ge2\).  This is an exact
arithmetic lock between the translated-\(CZ\) frame incidence and the
independently certified nonzero two-qutrit Pauli carrier; it is not by itself
an equivariant identification.

The integral Smith form is
\[
 \operatorname{SNF}(B_3)=
 \operatorname{diag}(1^5,2^3,10).
\]
Reducing its row span modulo two gives a binary
\[
 [9,5,3]
\]
code with weight enumerator
\[
 1+6z^3+9z^4+9z^5+6z^6+z^9.
\]
Thus the same five-dimensional number that appears as the \(CZ_3\) zero-phase
block also appears as the binary rank of the nine-frame incidence, without
identifying the corresponding vector spaces.

There is a second \(q=3\) lock in native characteristic.  For odd prime \(q\),
\[
 B_q=-I+N,\qquad
 N=J_q\otimes I_q+I_q\otimes J_q,
\]
and over \(\mathbb F_q\),
\[
 N^3=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}N=2q-2,\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}N^2=1.
\]
Therefore
\[
 \dim\ker N=(q-1)^2+1.
\]
This equals the \(CZ_q\) zero-support size \(2q-1\) precisely when
\[
 (q-1)(q-3)=0,
\]
so among \(q>1\) it singles out \(q=3\).

Finally the exact inverse lies in the Hamming Bose--Mesner algebra:
\[
 B_q^{-1}
 =\frac{3-q}{q-1}I+\frac1{q-1}A
  -\frac{2}{(q-1)(2q-1)}J.
\]
At \(q=3\) the explicit identity coefficient vanishes,
\[
 \boxed{B_3^{-1}=\frac12A(H(2,3))-\frac15J.}
\]
Thus the nine translated frame sums can be inverted from rook-neighbour sums
and one global mean, with no separate identity-channel term in this basis.
