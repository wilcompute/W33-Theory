\section*{Pass 4970: Ihara zeta function of $W(3,3)$}
For $W(3,3)=\operatorname{srg}(40,12,2,4)$,
\[
\operatorname{Spec}(A)=\{12^{(1)},2^{(24)},(-4)^{(15)}\},\qquad |E|=240.
\]
Bass' determinant formula gives
\[
\zeta(u)^{-1}=(1-u^2)^{200}(1-12u+11u^2)(1-2u+11u^2)^{24}(1+4u+11u^2)^{15}.
\]
The exact quadratic roots are
\[
1-2u+11u^2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad u=\frac{1\pm i\sqrt{10}}{11},
\]
\[
1+4u+11u^2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad u=\frac{-2\pm i\sqrt7}{11}.
\]
Thus the nontrivial root fields are $\mathbb Q(i\sqrt{10})$ and $\mathbb Q(i\sqrt7)$, not the previously stated $\mathbb Q(i\sqrt{43})$ and $\mathbb Q(i\sqrt{107})$. Both nontrivial root families have modulus $1/\sqrt{11}$.

The Ramanujan statement survives because $2,4<2\sqrt{11}$. The earlier class-number interpretation based on the incorrect discriminants is withdrawn.
