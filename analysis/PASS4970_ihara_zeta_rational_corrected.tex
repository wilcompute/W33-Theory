\subsection*{Ihara Zeta Function}

For a $k$-regular graph $\Gamma$ on $v$ vertices with eigenvalues $\lambda_i$, the
Ihara zeta function satisfies:
\[
\zeta_\Gamma(u)^{-1} = (1-u^2)^{|E|-v} \prod_{i=1}^{v}(1 - \lambda_i u + (k-1)u^2)
\]
For $W(3,3)$: $v=40$, $k=12$, $|E|=240$, $|E|-v = 200$.

\begin{align}
\zeta_{W(3,3)}(u)^{-1} &= (1-u^2)^{200} \cdot (1 - 12u + 11u^2)^1 \nonumber\\
&\quad \cdot (1 - 2u + 11u^2)^{24} \cdot (1 + 4u + 11u^2)^{15}
\end{align}

Factoring the quadratics over $\mathbb{Q}$:
\begin{align}
1 - 12u + 11u^2 &= (1-u)(1-11u) \\
1 - 2u + 11u^2 &= \text{irreducible over } \mathbb{Q},\; \text{roots at } u = \tfrac{1 \pm i\sqrt{43}}{11} \\
1 + 4u + 11u^2 &= \text{irreducible over } \mathbb{Q},\; \text{roots at } u = \tfrac{-2 \pm i\sqrt{107}}{11}
\end{align}
