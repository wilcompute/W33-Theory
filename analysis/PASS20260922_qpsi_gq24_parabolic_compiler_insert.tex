\subsection*{The $Q_\psi$ clock is compiled by one $GQ(2,4)$ anchor}

Let $A$ be the adjacency matrix of the $27$-point cubic-surface meet graph.
For any anchor $a$, define
\[
\boxed{q_a(i)=1+3\delta_{ai}-3A_{ai}.}
\]
The anchor has charge $4$, its ten neighbours have charge $-2$, and its
sixteen nonneighbours have charge $1$.  Thus every anchor gives
\[
27=1_{4}+10_{-2}+16_{1}.
\]
The five tritangents through $a$ have pattern $(-2,-2,4)$.  By the generalized
quadrangle axiom, each of the other forty tritangents contains exactly one
neighbour of $a$ and has pattern $(-2,1,1)$.  Hence all $45$ cubics conserve
$Q_\psi$ directly from incidence.

The six simple reflections generate $W(E_6)$ of order $51840$ and act
transitively on these $27$ clocks.  The five reflections fixing the
highest-weight anchor have the $D_5$ Cartan matrix and generate its complete
order-$1920$ stabilizer:
\[
\boxed{W(E_6)/W(D_5)=27.}
\]
Consequently, choosing the $SO(10)\times U(1)_\psi$ chart is precisely choosing
one minuscule weight/cubic-surface line.

On the address register this gives the executable clock
\[
D_{12}(a)|i,p\rangle
=\zeta_{12}^{\,1+3\delta_{ai}-3A_{ai}}|i,p\rangle,
\]
using only equality and adjacency predicates.  The formula does not select an
anchor physically and does not supply the still-missing change of basis to the
trinification Pauli execution tensor factor.
