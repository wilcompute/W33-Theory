# Pass 12076: time rotates SO(10) inside E6

Claude track. Builds on 12064, 12066 and 12073.

## Results

For two non-collinear clocks:

1. **Three SO(10)s.** For each singlet f_i, the E6 roots orthogonal to f_i form D5 (40 roots). The 27 then splits as
   **1 + 10_i + 16_i**, with **10_i = 8_i + f_j + f_k** and **16_i = 8_j + 8_k**. The three D5 meet in the D4 of the four
   common neighbours.
2. **The ticks rotate them.** The triality ticks (12066) cycle f₀ → f₁ → f₂ and carry D5_i onto D5_{i+1}.

## Reading, and a correction

The "families from triality" reading of 12066 and 12073 is **corrected**. The three octets are not three generations.
A tick of time rotates *which* SO(10) is unified, and it moves an octet from the matter 16 into the Higgs 10.
