\subsection{Apartment parity and the protected binary label space (Passes 4469--4471)}
\label{sec:pass4469-apartment-h10-bridge}

Let $N$ be the $40\times40$ point--line incidence matrix of $W(3,3)$ and
let $H$ be the $40\times1620$ line--apartment incidence matrix.  Passes
4463--4464 established
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}H=39,
 \qquad
 \dim\operatorname{rad}(\operatorname{im}H^T)=29,
 \qquad
 H H^T=N^TN=A_*\pmod2,
\]
with $A_*$ the dual line-collinearity adjacency.  Pass~201 independently
owns the binary sentinel code
\[
 C=\ker N^T=[40,15,8],
 \qquad C^\perp=\operatorname{im}N,
\]
and the $10$-dimensional logical-label quotient
$H_{10}=C^\perp/C$ of the $[[40,10,4]]$ CSS code.

\begin{theorem}[Canonical apartment--$H_{10}$ symplectic bridge]
The incidence map induces an exact isometry of nondegenerate alternating
spaces
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{\operatorname{im}H^T}{\operatorname{rad}(\operatorname{im}H^T)}
 \;\cong\;
 \frac{\mathbb F_2^{40}}{\ker(N^TN)}
 \xrightarrow{\ [b]\mapsto[Nb]\ }
 \frac{\operatorname{im}N}{\ker N^T}
 =H_{10}.}
\]
Its kernel statement and pairing statement are literal:
\[
 Nb\in\ker N^T\iff N^TN b=0,
\]
\[
 \langle H^Tb,H^Tc\rangle
 =b^TN^TNc
 =\langle Nb,Nc\rangle\pmod2.
\]
The executable witness checks the quotient dimensions, the three Gram
matrices, and all $2^{10}$ quotient classes.
\end{theorem}

The two natural Hamming quadratic refinements,
\[
 q_{\rm ap}([y])=\frac{\operatorname{wt}(y)}2\pmod2,
 \qquad
 q_{H_{10}}([x])=\frac{\operatorname{wt}(x)}2\pmod2,
\]
both descend and both have $528$ singular classes including zero; hence
both are of plus type $O^+(10,2)$.  The raw incidence isometry is not,
however, a quadratic isometry.  Their difference is one nonzero linear
functional $\ell(v)=B(v,a)$.  Pass~4470 identifies the unique isotropic
defect class under the bridge with
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{im}(A_{\rm point}\bmod2)/C,}
\]
the fixed one-dimensional layer already owned by Pass~187 inside the
uniserial $H_{10}=1\mid8\mid1$ filtration.  The single symplectic
transvection
\[
 T_a(v)=v+B(v,a)a
\]
repairs the quadratic refinement on all $2^{10}$ classes, so
$T_a\circ\Phi$ is an equivariant quadratic isometry.  This transvection
is a comparison-map correction; it is not being promoted to a physical
gate.

The same mechanism has a sharp generalized-quadrangle boundary.  For a
finite $GQ(s,t)$, Pass~4465 gives
\[
 HH^T=(r-\beta)I+(\alpha-\beta)A_*+\beta J,
 \quad
 r=\frac{(s+1)s^2t^2}{2},\quad
 \alpha=s^2t,\quad
 \beta=\frac{s(s+1)}2,
\]
whereas point--line incidence always gives
$N^TN=(s+1)I+A_*$.  Reduction modulo two yields
\[
 \boxed{HH^T=N^TN\ \text{ over }\mathbb F_2
 \iff s\equiv3\pmod4\ \text{ and }t\text{ is odd}.}
\]
Thus $W(3,3)$ and the displayed $GQ(3,9)$ orientation satisfy the bridge,
while the dual parameter orientation $GQ(9,3)$ does not.  The statement
is therefore an orientation-sensitive characteristic-two theorem, not a
duality invariant and not an explanation of the empirical Ramanujan
signing percentages of Pass~4457.
