\begin{remark}[Hyperkahler orientation and Weyl chirality]
\label{rem:k3-hyperkahler-weyl-chirality}
The global orientation in Remark~\ref{rem:k3-global-orientation} is the natural
hyperkahler one.  The three parallel Kahler forms span the chosen self-dual
three-plane.  Since a Ricci-flat Kahler surface is scalar-flat Kahler, the
self-dual Weyl block in this convention vanishes:
\[
  W_+^{\rm norm}=0.
\]
Thus the nonzero integrated Weyl basket
\[
  W_-^{\rm norm}=24
\]
is the opposite chirality forced by the hyperkahler orientation.  The chirality
is therefore not a free sign choice in the paper: it is fixed once the complex
orientation of $K3$ is fixed.
\end{remark}
