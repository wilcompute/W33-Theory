Passes 5388–5395 · All-q Levi flag Hodge projector

For every generalized quadrangle of order (q,q), the flag line graph has distance shells 1, 2q, 2q², 2q³, q⁴ and intersection array {2q,q,q,q; 1,1,1,2}. Its terminal -2 eigenspace is exactly the oriented Levi cycle space, with dimension q⁴.

The resulting Hodge/Bose–Mesner projector is

E_-2 = (q⁴A0 - q³A1 + q²A2 - qA3 + A4) / ((q+1)²(q²+1)).

The terminal first-eigenmatrix row is q-independent: (1,-2,2,-2,1). The terminal second-eigenmatrix column is (q⁴,-q³,q²,-q,1). At q=3 this recovers the existing W33 H1=81 Levi Hodge projector and its (81,-27,9,-3,1) kernel exactly.

Prior-art boundary: the underlying symmetric 4-class flag fusion and its valencies are known in Colangelo–Monzillo–Siciliano (Discrete Mathematics 347 (2024), 114054). The repo contribution here is the explicit all-q cycle-space/Hodge identification and its exact W33 specialization. No code-distance or physical-protection claim is implied.
