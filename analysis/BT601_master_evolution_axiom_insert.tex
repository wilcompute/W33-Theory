\subsection{Master Evolution Axiom: Hodge Projection of Ihara Propagation}\label{subsec:master-evolution-axiom}

The preceding computations suggest the following structural axiom for the W33
substrate.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Master Evolution Axiom.}
Physical evolution on the protected W33 cycle frame is not raw entrywise
nonlinear propagation.  It is Hodge projection of Ihara/nonbacktracking
propagation back onto the protected Levi cycle idempotent.
\end{quote}

The ingredients are as follows.  The W33 point-line Levi graph has
\[
|V(L)|=80,\qquad |E(L)|=160,\qquad \beta_1(L)=81,
\]
and its oriented simple-$8$-cycle incidence matrix satisfies
\[
\frac1{160}CC^T=E_4,
\]
so the protected Gram is
\[
G=\frac1{81}CC^T=\frac{160}{81}E_4.
\]
The raw cubic transform detects an Ihara/nonbacktracking shadow.  For the W33
collinearity spectrum
\[
12^1,\qquad 2^{24},\qquad (-4)^{15},
\]
the odd closed-walk moments obey
\[
M_3=\operatorname{Tr}(A^3)=960,\qquad
M_5=\operatorname{Tr}(A^5)=234240,
\]
so
\[
\frac{M_5}{M_3}=244.
\]
The nonbacktracking outdegree is
\[
p_{\rm Ih}=12-1=11,
\]
and hence
\[
\boxed{
\frac{M_5/M_3}{p_{\rm Ih}^2}=\frac{244}{121}.
}
\]
This is precisely the raw cubic leakage ratio into the companion sectors.

Thus the raw cubic transform has the sector flow
\[
E_4\longrightarrow E_0+E_1+E_2+E_3+E_4,
\]
where
\[
E_1+E_2+E_3
\]
is the nonbacktracking companion shadow.  The physical/repaired evolution applies
the minimal sector filter
\[
P_{E_0+E_4},
\]
then removes $E_0$ and renormalizes.  Consequently
\[
G\longmapsto G.
\]
In this form, the repair is not ad hoc: it is the Hodge selection rule removing
the Ihara shadow while preserving the protected W33 Levi cycle idempotent.
