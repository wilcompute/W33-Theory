# Passes 7217–7232 — a double-six cuts a literal doily out of the E8/D4 spread code

## Executive result

Fix any one of the 36 double-sixes

\[
(a_1,\ldots,a_6)/(b_1,\ldots,b_6).
\]

The 15 remaining cubic lines are the \(c_{ij}\), hence are naturally the 15 duads / edges of \(K_6\). The repo's existing exact classifier proves that the 15 tritangent planes entirely among those lines are precisely

\[

That already explains the old \(S_6\) symmetry. The stronger new result is that the **recent 45-coordinate E8/D4 spread code restricts to this doily exactly**.

## 1. Closed K6 formula for the [15,5,6] doily code

Let the 15 coordinates be the edges of \(K_6\). For \(A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,6\}\), let \(\delta(A)\) be its cut vector and let \(\mathbf1\) be the all-ones edge vector. Then

\[
\boxed{c(A)=\delta(A)+(|A|\bmod2)\mathbf1},
\qquad A\sim A^c.
\]

The 32 resulting words form a self-orthogonal binary code

\[
\boxed{[15,5,6]_2}
\]

with weight enumerator

\[
\boxed{1+10y^6+15y^8+6y^{10}}.
\]

Its even-\(|A|\) sector is the \([15,4,8]_2\) simplex code.

The old hyperplane dictionary becomes elementary K6 graph theory:

- the 6 ovoid zero-sets are the six 5-edge vertex stars;
- the 15 perp zero-sets are one duad together with the six duads disjoint from it;
- the 10 grid zero-sets are the \(K_{3,3}\) edge sets associated with unordered \(3+3\) bisections.

Consequently the weight-10 supports are \(K_5\) edge sets, the weight-8 supports are \(K_{2,4}\) cuts, and the weight-6 supports are pairs of disjoint triangles.

The dual is the familiar

\[
\boxed{[15,10,3]_2},
\]

whose 15 weight-three words are exactly the synthemes / perfect matchings.

\[
\boxed{\text{six doily ovoids}=\text{six vertices of }K_6},

\boxed{\text{six doily spreads}=\text{six 1-factorizations of }K_6}.

\[

Thus this is not recognition by parameters or weight enumerator. The restricted generators are literally the dual-doily incidence rows.
