# Quaternionic repair of the two 36-state Hesse carriers

The two natural 36-object carriers fail to be isomorphic as physical
\(SL(2,3)\)-sets only on the order-three class. Restrict to the normal
Sylow-two subgroup
\[
Q_8\triangleleft SL(2,3).
\]
The obstruction disappears completely.

Both 36-sets decompose as
\[
\boxed{36=8+8+8+4+4+4}
\]
under \(Q_8\). The three 8-orbits are regular \(Q_8\) torsors. The three
4-orbits have stabilizer equal to the unique central involution and are all
copies of \(Q_8/Z(Q_8)\). Hence the two \(Q_8\)-sets are objectwise
isomorphic.

The executable certificate constructs one deterministic frozen-gauge bijection
and verifies
\[
f(gx)=g f(x)
\]
for all \(8\times36=288\) action pairs.

The repair is exact but not canonical. Pairing three regular source orbits with
three regular target orbits contributes \(3!\,8^3\) choices. Pairing the three
\(Q_8/Z\) orbits contributes \(3!\,4^3\). Thus
\[
\boxed{N_{\rm Q8\text{-}equivariant}=1{,}179{,}648=2^{17}3^2.}
\]

So the compiler problem has narrowed again: a quaternionic objectwise core is
already present, and only the ternary order-three extension is missing. The
remaining selector problem is to choose or constrain one of the
\(1{,}179{,}648\) \(Q_8\)-equivariant gauges in a way compatible with the
order-three qutrit shear.

This is a finite representation/control result. It does not physically select
one bijection and does not identify the two carriers as the same geometric
objects.
