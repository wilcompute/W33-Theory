\subsection{Pass 10974: the established $R$-rules close the plane-rotation door}

% Correction of Pass 10968; rule from Bizet et al. arXiv:1301.2322.
The per-plane $R$-charge rule used by orbifolder~1.2 holds only on prime planes of factorizable orbifolds;
on non-prime planes the $\gamma$-phase contributes (Bizet et al.).  Keeping only the established $\mathbb{Z}_3^R$,
$\mathbb{Z}_2^R$ of the $\mathbb{Z}_6$-II lattice, and separately adding the $\gamma$-corrected G2-plane charge, all
$304$ FI-cancelling directions of $\mathbb{Z}_6$II$_{23}$ break the parity: $\mathbb{Z}_6$-II is $0/128$.  The new
$\mathbb{Z}_3\times\mathbb{Z}_6$ family ($5$ SMs) has no parity; $\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_6$-I ($29$) and
$\mathbb{Z}_6\times\mathbb{Z}_6$ ($10$; two models open only under uncorrected $R$-rules admit no MSSM-viable parity
even then) have none that survives; $\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_6$-II yields no Standard Model in $51\,000$ tries.  The $9$ $\mathbb{Z}_{12}$-I verdicts that used per-plane rules become undetermined.
