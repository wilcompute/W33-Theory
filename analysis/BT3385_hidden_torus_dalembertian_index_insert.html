The hidden torus is an exact finite d’Alembertian

After a scalar shift, the 27-state hidden operator satisfies D + 2I = Δ₁ + Δ₂ − Δ₃ on the ternary torus C33. Its exact spectrum is 64, 312, 09, (−3)2, with rank 18 and nullity 9.

The null Fourier set splits exactly as 1 + 4 + 4: one constant mode and two four-mode rulings. This is a finite signed-graph theorem; it is not a claim about physical spacetime, Lorentz symmetry, causality, or a continuum limit.
