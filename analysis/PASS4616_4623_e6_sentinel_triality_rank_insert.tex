\subsection{The forty-five carrier closes, the sentinel shell becomes self-describing, and outer symmetry glues the exceptional six-space (Passes 4616--4623)}
\label{sec:pass4616-4623-e6-sentinel-triality-rank}

\paragraph{Pass 4616: the protected forty-five is exactly the center-quad/$E_6$ forty-five.}
The forty-five $16$-line supports constructed from the protected singular shell
in Pass~4585 and the older forty-five antipodal center-quad quotient points are
not merely graphs with the same parameters.  In the common W33 model the
stabilizer of a protected support has order $576$ and fixes exactly one old
center-quad quotient point.  Sending $gU_0\mapsto gQ_0$ therefore gives an
explicit $PSp(4,3)$-equivariant bijection of the two $45$-sets; generator
checks certify well-definedness and equivariance.  Under this bijection the
Pass~4586 $\operatorname{SRG}(45,32,22,24)$ is exactly the established
center-quad transport/disjointness graph.  Thus the former parameter-only
$E_6$/tritangent comparison is now an exact finite $G$-set intertwiner.

\paragraph{Pass 4617: the $45\times40$ transport is the complete minimum shell of the sentinel code.}
The same row permutation strengthens the previous graph identity to a matrix
identity: a transport row is exactly the eight-point support of the corresponding
center-quad quotient point.  Let $T$ be this $45\times40$ support matrix and let
$N$ be W33 point--line incidence.  Over $\mathbb F_2$,
\[
 \operatorname{rank}T=15,\qquad \operatorname{rank}N=25,\qquad TN=0.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{\ker T=\operatorname{im}N},\qquad
 \boxed{\operatorname{row}T=\ker N^T}.
\]
The latter is the sentinel $[40,15,8]$ code.  Every row of $T$ has weight eight,
and the exact sentinel enumerator has exactly forty-five weight-eight words, so
these rows are the \emph{entire} minimum shell:
\[
 1+45z^8+720z^{12}+6930z^{16}+17376z^{20}
 +6930z^{24}+720z^{28}+45z^{32}+z^{40}.
\]

\paragraph{Pass 4618: the three exceptional six-spaces form an $S_3$ multiplicity line, but the W33 outer involution singles out one.}
Pass~4583 found exactly three $PSp(4,3)$-invariant six-submodules in
$Q_{12}=K_{27}/K_{15}$.  Their full $PSp$ commutant has dimension four over
$\mathbb F_2$, hence sixteen elements; exactly six are units, and those six
realize all six permutations of the three submodules.  Thus the multiplicity
space is a projective line with
\[
 \operatorname{Aut}_{PSp}(Q_{12})_{\rm units}\cong GL(2,2)\cong S_3,
\]
and no individual factor is canonical under the inner group.  The actual W33
outer similitude acts on the three factors with cycle type $1+2$: it fixes one
and swaps the other two.  The fixed factor has inner image order $25920$ and
outer image order $51840$, giving a unique $PGSp(4,3)$-stable six-submodule.
The commutant $S_3$ is a multiplicity-space symmetry; no identification with
geometric $D_4$ triality is made here.

\paragraph{Pass 4619: both half-spinor families now have concrete W33 lifts.}
The $270$ maximal totally singular four-spaces of the protected $O^+(8,2)$
geometry have $PSp(4,3)$ orbit sizes
\[
 \boxed{27,36,36,36,135}.
\]
For the transitive $135$-orbit, a representative stabilizer has order $192$ and
fixes exactly three maximal size-eight W33 partial spreads.  Those are exactly
the three eight-line orbits in its line-orbit decomposition
$8+8+8+16$, and each has full stabilizer $192$.  Thus this half-spinor object
has a concrete three-valued lift by maximal partial spreads.  For the other
half-spinor family, the degree-$27$ orbit is explicitly the $27$ center-quad/$E_6$
quotient lines via a unique fixed object under the order-$960$ stabilizer, while
each of the three degree-$36$ orbits is the W33 spread $G$-set via a unique
fixed spread under the order-$720$ stabilizer.  This covers all $270$ generators
without collapsing the genuine inner-orbit decomposition.

\paragraph{Pass 4620: the elliptic binary-rank conjecture is exactly a Hermitian generator-code problem.}
The duality
\[
 Q^-(5,q)=GQ(q,q^2)\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 H(3,q^2)=GQ(q^2,q)
\]
turns the same incidence matrix into the generator--point matrix of the
Hermitian surface.  The conjectural all-odd-$q$ rank becomes
\[
 \boxed{\dim_{\mathbb F_2}C_1(H(3,q^2))
       =q^4+q^2+1=\theta_2(q^2)=|PG(2,q^2)|},
\]
equivalently $C^\perp\subseteq C$ with
$\dim C^\perp=q(q^2-q+1)$.  For odd $q$ the line-graph SRG coefficients are all
even, so its adjacency satisfies $A^2=0$ over $\mathbb F_2$ automatically.
The exact $q=3,5,7$ anchors continue to satisfy the proposed dimensions, but
square-zero alone does not determine rank and no all-$q$ proof is claimed.

\paragraph{Pass 4621 (outside the box): the sentinel minimum shell is self-describing.}
Forget the construction and retain only the forty-five weight-eight sentinel
words.  Two minimum words meet in either zero or two coordinates; declaring
intersection two to be adjacency recovers exactly the center-quad/$E_6$
transport graph, with pair counts $270$ and $720$.  Dually, two of the forty
coordinate positions occur together in either one or three minimum words;
declaring co-occurrence three to be adjacency recovers exactly the \emph{point}
side W33 graph, with pair counts $540$ and $240$.  The recovered point graph is
explicitly distinct from the protected line-side $A_*$.  Thus the complete
minimum shell alone reconstructs both sides of the $45\times40$ incidence.

\paragraph{Pass 4622 (outside the box): the $135$ maximal partial spreads carry a second canonical three-cover.}
Pass~1100's $135$ maximal/unextendable size-eight partial spreads are transitive.
Pass~4619 shows that an order-$192$ stabilizer $H$ fixes exactly three of them,
and each of those three has full stabilizer $H$.  Hence there are
$135/3=45$ conjugacy packets of such stabilizers.  Orbit--stabilizer gives
\[
 |N_G(H)|=576,\qquad N_G(H)/H\cong C_3,
\]
and the quotient acts regularly on the three spreads in each packet.  This is a
second exact $135\to45$ cover with a $C_3$ deck action.  It is deliberately not
identified with the center-quad forty-five without a new explicit intertwiner.

\paragraph{Pass 4623 (outside the box): outer symmetry makes the exceptional $6\mid6$ extension nonsplit.}
The unique outer-stable six-space of Pass~4618 is a submodule, not an
outer-equivariant direct summand.  Indeed $Q_{12}$ restricts to $PSp(4,3)$ as
$U_6\oplus U_6$ and has exactly three inner-invariant six-spaces; the outer
involution fixes only one of them.  Any $PGSp$-stable complement would therefore
be a second outer-stable member of that exhaustive three-element set, which does
not exist.  Thus
\[
 \boxed{0\longrightarrow U_6\longrightarrow Q_{12}
        \longrightarrow U_6\longrightarrow0}
\]
splits over $PSp(4,3)$ but is nonsplit over $PGSp(4,3)$.  Accordingly the
orthogonal-pair wedge map of Pass~4583 is canonically outer-equivariant into
$Q_{12}$, whereas projection to one six-space necessarily drops to inner
equivariance or breaks the outer symmetry.
