# Literature boundary for the sparse W33 apartment Hamiltonian and q=2 parity control
**2026-11-03; attribution-focused audit.** This note distinguishes general mathematical prior art from the repo-specific finite optimization and does not assert research priority beyond the stated computational witness.

## What is established elsewhere
1. **Line-graph flat bands and compact localized loop states are known.** For a bipartite graph with unsigned vertex-edge incidence U, its line-graph adjacency satisfies A=U^T U-2I. Even cycles supply alternating-sign compact localized states in the -2 eigenspace. Examples and detailed physics: *Spin-Orbit-Induced Topological Flat Bands in Line and Split Graphs of Bipartite Lattices* (2020), https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_3314193_4/component/file_3617229/content and *Line-Graph Lattices: Euclidean and Non-Euclidean Flat Bands, and Implementations in Circuit Quantum Electrodynamics*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.02794 . The W33's 81-dimensional -2 band and the physical interpretation of loop modes must be credited to that broader construction and to older repo BT545–BT551.
2. **Apartment cycles generating top building homology are known.** The Solomon–Tits theorem identifies the Steinberg module with the top reduced homology of the Tits building; apartment homology classes generate it. Relevant survey: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Steinberg_module ; symplectic treatment: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00029-025-01051-8 . In the repo, BT744 already proved an 81-apartment spanning result for W33, so the existence of an apartment basis is not new.

## What the new finite certificates actually contribute
- \`analysis/2026_11_03_optimal_5load_apartment_hamiltonian.py\`: construct an explicit GF(2)-independent set of 81 shortest apartments on 160 Levi flags whose maximum cycle participation is 5, matching the unconditional averaging bound ceil(648/160). The noncanonical basis gives a finite-rank-one-circulation Hamiltonian H=BB^T, with D H=H D^T=0, and with H not scalar on the protected subspace.
- \`analysis/2026_11_03_q2_parity_apartment_load.py\`: show that the naive average bound fails sharply at q=2. For 16 shortest apartments on the 45 flags of GQ(2,2), trivalent parity forces maximum participation at least 4, and a constructive GF(2) basis attains 4.
- This difference between q=2 (parity bound stronger than mean) and q=3 (mean bound sharp) is a concrete new finite-family obstruction within this repository's optimization program. No claim of being the first result on the topic in the full mathematical literature is made.

## Physical firewall and falsifiers
The matrix H=BB^T is a chosen quadratic sum of oriented 8-edge circulation terms. Calling its terms *finite-supported* is justified: each rank-one term has exactly eight flags of support. Calling the resulting 160-flag model a relativistic local field theory, emergent Lorentzian spacetime, dynamically selected clock, experimentally protected code, or derived photon Hamiltonian is not. The construction breaks the full PSp(4,3) symmetry through a selected basis; generic onsite potentials can still create cycle-cut leakage. It also says nothing about a continuum limit until an explicit compatible refinement family with growing spatial diameter, finite interaction range and controlled spectral scaling is supplied.

To test a physical interpretation, predeclare a local refinement rule, a kinetic continuum limit and a perturbation model, then measure in-band dispersion, leakage, symmetry breaking and causal propagation without retuning the Hamiltonian separately at each size.
