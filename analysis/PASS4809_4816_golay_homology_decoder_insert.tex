\section{Complete nonlocal Golay--Levi shell and decoder reconstruction}
\label{sec:pass4809-4816-golay-levi}

The local-to-global filtration of the intrinsic qutrit triangle code can be sharpened substantially.  Write
\[
 C\subset L:=G_{10}^{\oplus 27}\subset C^\perp,
 \qquad
 C^\perp/L\cong H_1(\operatorname{Levi}(GQ(4,2));\mathbb F_3),

where $G_{10}=[10,6,4]_3$ is the punctured ternary Golay code on a single $K_5$ fibre.

\paragraph{Complete minimum nonlocal shell.}
The quotient has triangle-lift distance six.  Exhausting all local cost patterns of total cost six shows that the previously exhibited $360$ induced $K_{3,3}$ witnesses are complete: all mixed cost-one/cost-two patterns are empty.  Thus there are exactly $360$ projective minimum classes.  They form one orbit under $PSp(4,3)$, with stabilizer order $72$, and one orbit under the full order-$51840$ outer action, with stabilizer order $144$.

\paragraph{Exact hierarchical component decoder.}
A single ten-triangle fibre has exactly $81$ point-syndrome states, whose minimum local lift costs occur with multiplicities
\[
 1\times0+20\times1+60\times2.
\]
Consequently exact minimum-Hamming-weight decoding of one ternary $X$- or $Z$-error component reduces to choosing one of $81$ states on each of the $27$ fibres, minimizing the sum of their exact local costs, and imposing the $45$ global point-sum constraints modulo three.  The exact weight-two census is
\[
 145260=140400+3\cdot1620,
\]
so every weight-two ambiguity is local Golay.  At weight three the syndrome multiplicity census is
\[
 1^{23385600},\quad 2^{7200},\quad 3^{842400},\quad 12^{2160}.
\]
The $7200$ twofold classes are precisely the first genuinely global ambiguity sector: they are the two complementary weight-three halves of the $720$ nonzero scalar $K_{3,3}$ weight-six logicals.  This statement concerns one ternary component; independent component decoding is not asserted to minimize joint qutrit Pauli support.

\paragraph{Global Golay extension chirality.}
The two projective one-coordinate extension directions $G_{10}\to G_{11}$ transform under the local $S_5$ by its sign character: every even permutation fixes both directions separately and every odd permutation exchanges them.  Globally the resulting $54$ directions form two $27$-orbits under $PSp(4,3)$ but one transitive $54$-orbit under the full order-$51840$ group.  Choosing either inner sheet gives a monomially $PSp$-equivariant direct sum
\[
 G_{11}^{\oplus27}=[297,162,5]_3,
\]
while adjoining both directions gives the self-dual full-outer-equivariant
\[
 G_{12}^{\oplus27}=[324,162,6]_3.
\]
The full stabilizer of a base fibre has structure $2^4{:}S_5$ and order $1920$; the direction stabilizer is the sign-kernel preimage $2^4{:}A_5$ of order $960$.  Hence the $54\to27$ map is the orientation cover
\[
 W(E_6)/(2^4{:}A_5)\longrightarrow W(E_6)/(2^4{:}S_5).
\]
In particular this is not an untwisted disjoint union of two full-$W(E_6)$ invariant $27$-sets; an identification with the $27$ and dual-$27$ modules would require a representation-level intertwiner.

The decoder sees the same geometry intrinsically.  For a projective signed $K_{3,3}$ logical, its two nonzero scalars and ten unordered $3+3$ support partitions produce exactly $20$ twofold weight-three syndrome collisions.  Therefore
\[
 360\cdot20=7200,
\]
