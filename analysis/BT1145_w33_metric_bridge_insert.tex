\begin{theorem}[K3--W33 metric bridge]
\label{thm:k3-w33-metric-bridge}
The product coefficient in the corpus-normalized Ricci-flat lane is
\[
C_4=440\cdot24+16320/2=18720=240\cdot6\cdot13=E q!\Phi_3.
\]

$440$ and $16320$ come from the finite spectrum
$0^{122},4^{240},10^{48},16^{30}$ of $D_F^2$.
The scalar, spin, and Hodge rows in Remark~\ref{rem:k3-a4-convention-table}
are operator-specific probes; they are not substitutes for this corpus-normalized
K3 curvature input.
\end{theorem}
