\subsection{A repo-native 2026 Vogel split-Casimir certificate}
\label{subsec:vogel-e6-split-casimir}

The Vogel connection can be tested at operator level rather than by matching dimensions.
Starting from the integral Chevalley realization of the minuscule $E_6$ module $V_{27}$,
commutator closure inside $\operatorname{Mat}_{27}(\mathbb Z)$ gives the full
$78$-dimensional Lie algebra.  For an integral basis $X_a$ let
\[
 G_{ab}=\operatorname{Tr}_{27}(X_aX_b)
\]
and use the trace-dual basis to form the split Casimir on
$V_{27}\otimes\mathfrak e_6$.  In the deterministic basis used by the verifier,
$18G^{-1}$ is integral, so if
\[
 O_{18}=18\sum_a\rho_{27}(X_a)\otimes\operatorname{ad}(X^a),
\]
then $O_{18}$ is an integral sparse $2106\times2106$ matrix.  Exact integer
multiplication gives
\[
 \boxed{(O_{18}-3I)(O_{18}+12I)(O_{18}+36I)=0}.
\]
With the Vogel normalization $\widehat C=O_{18}/72$, the spectrum is therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 \left(\frac1{24}\right)^{1728}
 \oplus\left(-\frac16\right)^{351}
 \oplus\left(-\frac12\right)^{27}},
\]
recovering the characteristic identity for the $E_6$ specialization of
$27\otimes78$ in the 2026 split-Casimir literature.  The corresponding exact
spectral projectors are
\[
 P_{1728}=\frac{(O_{18}+12I)(O_{18}+36I)}{585},\qquad
 P_{351}=-\frac{(O_{18}-3I)(O_{18}+36I)}{360},
\]
\[
 P_{27}=\frac{(O_{18}-3I)(O_{18}+12I)}{936}.
\]
They are pairwise orthogonal, idempotent, sum to the identity, and have traces
$1728,351,27$.  The exact moments are
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}\widehat C=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}\widehat C^2=\frac{39}{2},\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}\widehat C^3=-\frac{39}{8}
 =-\frac14\operatorname{Tr}\widehat C^2.
\]
This is an $E_6$ operator certificate on a repo-native carrier.  It does not prove
existence of Vogel's conjectural universal Lie algebra, nor does a dimension match by
itself define a W33--Vogel morphism.  We therefore distinguish arithmetic observations,
universal specializations, operator certificates such as the present one, and the still
open stronger problem of constructing an explicit W33-derived Vogel intertwiner.
