\subsection{The quartic determinant is a mod--3 matroid basis count, and every building apartment has phase one}
\label{sec:20260917-det-matroid-apartment}

The preceding controller analysis left one exact finite candidate for a genuinely
nonquadratic phase: for a collection of Pauli directions
$V=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_m]\in\mathbb F_3^{4\times m}$, form
$S=VV^T$ and $X=SJ$, where $J$ is the standard symplectic form.  The
single-three-label weight--two operator has $\operatorname{rank}X\le3$ and hence
zero determinant, while two operators can supply four independent directions.
The following observation makes the latter statement coordinate free.

\begin{theorem}[Determinant--Tutte basis law]
Let $M(V)$ be the column matroid represented by $V$ over $\mathbb F_3$.
Then
\[
 \boxed{\det((VV^T)J)=T_{M(V)}(1,1)\pmod3.}
\]
Equivalently, the determinant is the number of rank--four column bases modulo
three.  In particular, with exactly four projective directions the determinant
is nonzero iff they span $\mathbb F_3^4$, and then it equals $1$.
\end{theorem}

\noindent
Indeed, Cauchy--Binet gives
\[
 \det(VV^T)=\sum_{|I|=4}\det(V_I)^2.
\]
Every nonzero square in $\mathbb F_3$ is $1$, and $\det J=1$; the sum therefore
counts the bases of $M(V)$ modulo $3$.  Since $T_M(1,1)$ is the standard Tutte
basis evaluation, the proposed synchronized-sector phase can be written
\[
 \omega^{r\det X}=\omega^{rT_M(1,1)}.
\]
Thus its exponent also inherits deletion--contraction modulo three.

The exhaustive $PG(3,3)$ census is
\[
 \binom{40}{4}=91{,}390=63{,}180+28{,}210,
\]
with $63{,}180=|GL(4,3)|/(2^4 4!)$ projective bases and $28{,}210$
dependent quadruples.  For all $658{,}008$ five-subsets the numbers of bases
are distributed as
\[
 0^{51{,}480},\qquad
 3^{252{,}720},\qquad
 4^{252{,}720},\qquad
 5^{101{,}088},
\]
so the determinant values occur with multiplicities
\[
 \boxed{0^{304{,}200},\qquad1^{252{,}720},\qquad2^{101{,}088}.}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Apartment determinant bridge]
Every one of the $1620$ induced W33 quadrangles/apartments is a projective basis
of $\mathbb F_3^4$ and satisfies
\[
 \boxed{\det((VV^T)J)=1.}
\]
Each such point quadrangle lifts uniquely to one alternating point--line Levi
$8$-cycle.  Hence the determinant candidate is uniform on the complete
apartment set: its value is $\omega^r$ in central-character sector $r$.
\end{theorem}

\noindent
For a cyclic apartment $p_1-p_2-p_3-p_4-p_1$, adjacent symplectic pairings
vanish and the two opposite pairings
$a=\langle p_1,p_3\rangle$, $b=\langle p_2,p_4\rangle$ are nonzero.  Therefore
\[
 \det(V^TJV)=(ab)^2=1
\]
in $\mathbb F_3$, proving directly that the four points form a basis.  This
statement uses the repository's corrected Pass--4474 semantics: the $1620$
\emph{induced} quadrangles are the apartments, while the separate $120$ simple
four-cycles lying inside geometric $K_4$ lines are not included.
