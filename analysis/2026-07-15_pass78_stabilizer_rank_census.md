## The 15 Vectors and Their Pauli Structure

In the two-qubit Pauli group, the 15 non-identity elements (modulo phase) form the doily. They split by type:

| Type | Count | Pauli operators |
|------|-------|----------------|
| XX-type (both X) | 3 | XX, XY, XZ (sort of) |
| ZZ-type | 3 | ZZ, ZY, ZX |
| Mixed (one X, one Z) | 9 | XZ, ZX, YY, etc. |

More precisely, the 15 operators are all {I,X,Y,Z}^{∇2} \ {II} = 15 operators. They form the W(3,2) doily under the symplectic structure: two operators commute iff their symplectic inner product is 0.

## Stabilizer Rank Classes

A stabilizer state on 2 qubits is determined by 2 independent commuting Pauli operators. The stabilizer group has rank 2.

The doily has structure: **5 totally isotropic lines** (maximal cliques where all pairs commute) forming a **spread** of W(3,2). Wait: W(3,2) has 15 lines, each with 3 points. The maximum cliques (totally isotropic subspaces) have size... in W(3,2), a maximal totally isotropic subspace has dimension 2, giving a **Lagrangian** = 2^2-1 = 3 points = a line. So all 15 lines are Lagrangians, and a spread consists of 5 disjoint Lagrangians covering 15 points.

The **stabilizer rank** of a state is the minimal number of Pauli generators needed. For a 2-qubit stabilizer state: rank = 2 (always, since we need 2 commuting generators for the 2-qubit space).

## The Doily Attack: What Pass 70 Found

Pass 70 identified the following structure in the 40-point W(3,3):
- The 15 F_2-rational points (doily) form a sub-GQ
- The remaining 40-15 = 25 points form a "shell"
- The 15 doily points split into 5 groups of 3 (the 5 spreads) or 3 groups of 5 (the 3 ovoids)

For the [[40,10,4]] code:
- A stabilizer group of rank 15 (using all 15 doily generators) has stabilizer state dimension 2^(15-2) = 2^{13} = 8192 in a 2-qubit system... this dimension doesn't make sense for 2 qubits.

**Reframing for 10-qubit system** (k=10 logical qubits, n=40 physical qubits):
- The [[40,10,4]] code has a stabilizer group of rank n-k = 30
- The 15 doily generators generate HALF the stabilizer
- The full stabilizer is generated by the doily generators plus 15 additional generators from the W(3,3) shell

This suggests a **bipartite stabilizer structure**: 15 doily generators + 15 shell generators = 30 total stabilizers.
