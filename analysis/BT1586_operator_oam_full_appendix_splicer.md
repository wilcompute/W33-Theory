# BT1586 Operator/OAM Full Appendix Splicer

BT1586 upgrades the earlier dry-run splice into a full bounded appendix splice for the main `photonic_holonet.tex` paper. The block includes the BT1564-BT1585 operator/OAM inserts, the BT1586-BT1588 synthesis insert, and the BT1589-BT1591 radial/lane/front-end insert, plus the BT1592-BT1594 lab/mode/Hesse witness-loop insert and the BT1595-BT1597 Witting fuel-object insert, plus the BT1598-BT1600 full Witting-cycle insert and the BT1601-BT1603 physical/Fano/universal-closure insert. It remains idempotent on repeated application.
