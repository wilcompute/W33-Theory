# Residual color and Pauli constraints at the W33 monopole

The asymptotic magnetic charge is m = diag(1,0,0,−1,0) in the defining SU(5) chart. Its centralizer in the unbroken Standard Model group is C = S(U(1) × U(2) × U(1) × U(1)). The U(2) acts on the two equal-magnetic-charge color entries. The connected group is generated by the four Cartan directions and this color SU(2), so testing complete SU(2) representations at fixed central weights implements C, including the inherited identifications between its center and torus. This concerns the stabilizer of a chosen asymptotic charge. Color rotations that change m require a separate monopole-bundle analysis. ## An exact net wino rule and a Pauli obstruction

Write a weight as (c1,c2,h,y,b,L) with h = 2T3, y = 6Y and b = 3B. On the outgoing lowest waves the additive label

    Nw = (y − 3h − 4b)/6

is zero on every excitation except the outgoing charged wino and its hole, where it is +1 and −1. This strengthens the earlier mod-two rule by including the formal baryon label. At fixed dyon Cartan weight,

    Nw(out) = (y_in − 3h_in − 4b_in)/6 − 2 ΔB.

This identity does not require conservation of B−L. For incoming Higgsinos and gluinos the first term is zero. Any nonzero integer ΔB then needs at least two same-sign winos. A derivative-free local normal-ordered product has only one Grassmann generator for each wino sign. It therefore cannot realize those transitions at any fermion multiplicity. More generally a net count of magnitude r requires at least r(r−1)/2 derivatives, from occupying distinct derivative orders on the same wino sign. This is a contact-operator constraint. Scattering states with distinct momenta can carry repeated winos.

The standard monopole phase transforms under C by z1/z4, the character of the root between the two distinguished lines. Its kernel has z1 = z4 and is connected: z1 and U in U(2) can be chosen freely and z5 is then fixed by the determinant. Thus a purely phase-like core excitation, trivial under that kernel, exchanges integer multiples k alpha of the root weight. If outgoing minus incoming fermion Cartan charge is k alpha, the right-hand side of the wino identity acquires the additional term −k/3. In particular an integer baryon-number change still requires k divisible by three within this restricted exchange sector. This charge statement leaves the phase moment of inertia, excitation spectrum, and energy thresholds open.

## Full residual color in the local operator count

Let V be the direct sum of the sixteen outgoing lowest-wave species and their holes, with separate lepton and Higgsino labels even when their gauge weights coincide. Local zero-derivative products with k outgoing fields carry the exterior character of wedge^k V. With one derivative the character is that of wedge^(k−1) V tensor V. The replay constructs both by exact charge addition. A highest weight w of residual SU(2) appears with multiplicity n(w)−n(w+alpha_c), where alpha_c = (2,−1,0,0) and n is the weight multiplicity at the same central and B,L labels.

The first local products with formal ΔB = ΔL nonzero are as follows. The multiplicity counts equivariant maps per fixed incoming family or gaugino multiplet. ## Both rational gluing candidates survive a color-current test

Each side has three matter color doublets and one gaugino color doublet in its charged waves. The bosonized raising currents have exponent vectors e_high−e_low. For both candidate gluings, the reflecting normals are perpendicular to the matter color differences and exclude the gaugino slots. The hole sign flip takes the gluino exponent to the conjugated outgoing pair with the same color root. The replay checks all four exponents for each gluing. This is compatibility at the level of the Cartan currents and color-root vertex exponents. Core matching and full boundary-state data, including phases and consistency in twisted sectors, remain necessary to distinguish the two candidates.
