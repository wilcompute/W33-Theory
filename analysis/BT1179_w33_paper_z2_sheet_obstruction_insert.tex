\begin{remark}[What the triple-$45$ bridge does not yet predict]
\label{rem:triple-fortyfive-z2-obstruction}
The triple-$45$ bridge identifies the Boolean relation sector, the tritangent
layer model, and the $W(3,3)$ center-quad quotient at the labeled point-graph
level.  It also identifies the complement transport graph.  It does not yet
predict the raw $Z_2$ sheet voltage on transport edges.  The existing transport
audit shows that this voltage is finer than the induced local $S_3$ matching: it
is not determined by the permutation nor by its parity.  Therefore the Boolean
$60=15+45$ module must be lifted by an additional sheet coordinate before it can
claim the raw voltage data.
\end{remark}
