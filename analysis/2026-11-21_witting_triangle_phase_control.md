# Witting triangle phases and a control that fails positivity

Using the 40 normalized rays of BT1408, form G_ij = <r_i|r_j>. The unordered triangle loop is G_ij G_jk G_ki, whose phase changes sign on reversing orientation. A numerical census over all 9880 triples gives 6640 zero loops; among nonzero loops, 2880 have absolute principal phase pi/6 and 360 have pi/2. The Gram rank is four. The conjugate realization has the same pairwise transition probabilities and reverses every oriented loop phase, so it supplies a physical magnitude-matched control for observables odd under conjugation. This does not distinguish the two realizations up to antiunitary equivalence. The entrywise magnitude matrix has minimum eigenvalue 1-sqrt(3), so erasing all phases is not a physical Gram-matrix control. Indeed |G| = I + (J-I-A)/sqrt(3), with A the orthogonality adjacency, and its A-eigenvalue-2 nonuniform sector yields 1-sqrt(3). The triangle census was computed from the BT1408 coordinate formula; no claim of novelty over the full repo or of chart-transport implementation is made.
