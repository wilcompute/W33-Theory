## Mathematical freedom

Let a deterministic 9D-to-2D recovery be represented by five 2×9 Kraus operators R_j, with Σ_j R_j†R_j=I9. For any unitary G in U(5), define

R'_j = Σ_{k=1}^5 G_{jk} R_k.

For **every** nine-dimensional input density operator rho, Σ_j R'_j rho R'_j† = Σ_j R_j rho R_j†. This is the standard unitary ambiguity of Kraus representations, or equivalently a change of environment basis in a minimal Stinespring dilation; see https://qubit.guide/9.5-stinesprings-dilation-and-krauss-ambiguity and https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1701036/1701036.pdf.Show.

The physically implemented 10×9 isometry DOES change by (G tensor I2). Completing it to a 10×10 unitary and recompiling with the same exact Reck triangular construction can rearrange how each rail encounters lossy two-mode couplers. Thus one ideal quantum recovery channel can have inequivalent lossy photonic implementations.

## Fully specified finite optical benchmark

For each of Petz and the new rank-five near-SDP-optimal Witting recovery:
- Use the same stored qubit encoder and 4→9 Witting channel.
- Complete the five-Kraus 10×9 isometry into a 10×10 unitary with one orthogonal column.
- Decompose that unitary into an adjacent two-mode Reck mesh with **45 active two-mode rotations**.
- After each active gate, multiply the amplitudes in both touched modes by sqrt(0.98), leaving idle modes untouched. No other waveguide, preparation, detector or readout loss is imposed.
- Compute the trace-decreasing recovery on the logical code after discarding the five-level auxiliary and keep photon loss as a separate failure, not a renormalized success.
- Explore **200 seeded independent Haar U(5) rotations per decoder**, each generated by QR of a complex Ginibre matrix with diagonal-phase correction. Include the original Kraus basis as a baseline.
- Independently check the CP map before and after Kraus rotation on a nonuniform complex nine-dimensional input density operator.

An independent local sandbox reproduced these results. The exact seeded sample, optical loss implementation, and the full best 5×5 complex gauge matrices are generated by the committed producer; the GitHub CI workflow was queued when this report was authored.

The previous finding that Petz beat the optimized recovery at 2% loss per active element was **correct for the two originally selected meshes**, but it is **not invariant under equivalent Kraus representations**. Merely rotating the five Kraus labels and recompiling the corresponding unitary moves the optimized recovery back ahead of Petz in the supplied sample.

This is the relevant engineering discovery: **quantum recovery optimization and physical interferometer synthesis should be optimized jointly over the hidden environment-unitary gauge**. The gauge is a legitimate compiler degree of freedom, not a new physical quantum channel.

## Boundaries and falsifiers

This is a random-search *constructive example*, not a global physical-layout optimum. The best Petz and optimized samples are not asserted to be their true optimum over all U(5). Results are sensitive to routing, the choice of one remaining orthogonal unitary-completion column, intensity loss profile, and the cost of physical couplers. The model assumes no coupler-induced phase errors or crosstalk. The 2% loss number is a hypothetical model parameter, not a measured chip.

The simulator does not establish scalable BQP by one photon across 16 paths. That separate mode-only encoding requires exponentially many paths to simulate multiple logical ququarts. Moreover, unitary ambiguity of Kraus operators is standard quantum information theory; the original contribution is exploiting it here as a concrete **Witting decoder hardware compilation objective**.
