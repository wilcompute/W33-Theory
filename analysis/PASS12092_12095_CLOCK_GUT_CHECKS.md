## 12092: the SO(10) splits are the factorisations through the event

For the E6 vacuum of the three clocks at an event p, the 27 SO(10)-making splits (12089) are exactly the 3 split
involutions of each of the 9 factorisations that contain p. Each of those factorisations holds 3 far points of p.

## 12093 (negative): a Z3 circle does not help Hosotani

On T⁵ (four Z2 holonomies plus one order-3 shift; all 6561 shifts; 40 bases per type), the bosonic minimum is still d4.
That holds with any shift and with non-trivial shifts only: d4 at −437.3 against a4 at −428.7.

## 12094 (negative): the characters do not protect the proton

The X/Y character pairs are (Q, u e) ×5 and (L, d N) ×10. So all the SU(5) X/Y couplings to quark–quark and quark–lepton
pairs survive the grading selection rule.

## 12095 (negative): the Eisenstein unit gives no family Z3

ω fixes none of the 255 classes, so no Standard-Model grading is ω-invariant. The ω-orbit of an SM grading is three SM
gradings, each with a different su(3) and su(2).
