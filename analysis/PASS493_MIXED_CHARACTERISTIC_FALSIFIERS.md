# Pass 493 — mixed-characteristic exact falsifiers

The extension `d(R)=|P^1(R)|` from cyclic rings `Z/p^n` to arbitrary local Frobenius rings is false.

Exact fraction-free cyclotomic determinants give:

| ring | `|R|` | `v_lambda(|R|)` | `|P^1(R)|` | attained depth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `Z/9[x]/(3x,x^2-3)` | 27 | 18 | 36 | **18** |
| `GR(9,2)` | 81 | 24 | 90 | **24** |
| `Z/9 x F_3` | 27 | 18 | 48 | **18** |

The character is generating and the flat determinant formula survives in every case. Ring size and character order therefore do not determine depth alone. These are exact attained witnesses; they refute the size-only extension but do not prove the global minimum over every section.
