% Exact local restriction theorem for the central C3 Clifford extension.
\subsection{The central $C_3$ Clifford cover is locally $C_9$ on three fifths of the order-three directions}

The exact point-stabilizer audit gives a nonsplit central extension
\[
1\longrightarrow Z\cong C_3\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow 1,
\qquad |K|=648,\qquad |Q|=216,
\]
where $Q$ is the projective one-qutrit Clifford/Hessian $\operatorname{ASL}(2,3)$ quotient.  Its certified lift-order census contains $32$ order-three elements of $Q$ with lift orders $(3,3,3)$ and $48$ with lift orders $(9,9,9)$.

For $q\in Q$ of order three and any lift $g\in K$, define the central cube charge
\[
\omega(q):=g^3\in Z.
\]
This is independent of the lift, since $(gz^a)^3=g^3z^{3a}=g^3$ for central $z^3=1$, and it obeys $\omega(q^{-1})=\omega(q)^{-1}$.

The full preimage of $\langle q\rangle$ has order nine.  If $\omega(q)=1$, all lifts have order three and the preimage is $C_3\times C_3$; if $\omega(q)\neq1$, all lifts have order nine and the preimage is $C_9$.  Since the $80$ order-three elements of $Q$ form $40$ cyclic subgroups, the census therefore yields the exact local split
\[
\boxed{40=16_{\,C_3\times C_3}+24_{\,C_9}}.
\]
Consequently the global extension class restricts nontrivially to
\[
\boxed{24/40=3/5}
\]
of the cyclic order-three subgroups of $Q$.  After choosing a generator $z$ of $Z$, inversion pairs the two nonzero charges and forces the $48$ nonsplit oriented generators to divide as
\[
\boxed{24\text{ with }g^3=z\quad+\quad24\text{ with }g^3=z^2}.
\]
Thus the previously global nonsplitting obstruction localizes on concrete order-three Clifford directions: $24$ cyclic $C_3$ directions thicken to $C_9$ in the three-sheeted cover, while $16$ restrict trivially.  This is an exact finite-group/cohomology statement; identifying the deck charge with a measured optical phase requires an additional physical intertwiner.
