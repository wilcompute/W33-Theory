\paragraph{Exact route quotient.}
A $D_4$ connection on the $45$ edges of $K_{10}$ has nine spanning-tree edges and
$36$ fundamental chords.  Tree gauge fixing leaves the $36$ chord holonomies,
complete up to one simultaneous conjugation.  Burnside's lemma gives
\[
\left|D_4^{36}/\!\sim\right|=\frac{2\,8^{36}+6\,4^{36}}8
=81{,}129{,}638{,}418{,}148{,}456{,}557{,}941{,}239{,}054{,}336.
\]
The binary sign-curvature code is the abelian projection of this nonabelian
connection data.

\paragraph{Constructive $S_6$ two-graph.}
The ten spread modes identify with the ten unordered $3+3$ partitions of a
six-set.  Choosing one symbol identifies them with the two-subsets of a five-set,
with Petersen adjacency given by disjointness.  Triples spanning an odd number of
Petersen edges form a $2$-$(10,3,4)$ two-graph with $60$ blocks, point replication
$18$, pair replication $4$, and even parity on every four-set.  All $720$ elements
of the degree-ten $S_6$ action preserve this system, and an explicit relabelling
matches it to the W33 spread-router curvature.

\paragraph{General-isotropic M36 frontier.}

\[

\paragraph{Golden selector.}
The symmetric controller-word census shows that $R_4^2U_6$ is the shortest
expanding word preserving a rational line.  It negates that line and induces
\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}
\]
on the quotient, with polynomial $t^2-t-1$.  Thus the golden ratio is selected
by shortest rational-line reduction and Fibonacci quotient action, not by minimum
spectral radius among all words.  The mechanical word of slope $1/\varphi^2$ is
balanced, has no consecutive expensive slots, and has factor complexity $n+1$.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
Modulo two, the Fibonacci quotient has order three and cycles the three nonzero
vectors of $\mathbf F_2^2$, so $V_4\rtimes C_3\cong A_4$.  In contrast,
$\operatorname{Aut}(D_4)$ has order eight and no order-three element.  Golden
control therefore acts on the abelian syndrome shell and cannot lift faithfully
to the nonabelian $D_4$ core.
