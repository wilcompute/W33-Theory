\subsection{Controlled determinant gate and outer-complete coherence}
\label{sec:20260917-controlled-det-outer-coherence}

The determinant controller target can now be stated as an exact unitary rather
than a verbal missing bridge.  Let \(X\in\mathfrak{sp}_4(\mathbb F_3)\) label
the certified ten-qutrit controller basis and let
\[
z=\operatorname{diag}(\omega,\omega^{-1})
\]
act on the doubled \(r=1,2\) central-character factor.  Define
\[
\boxed{
U_{\det}=\sum_X |X\rangle\!\langle X|\otimes z^{\det X}.
}
\]
This is unitary.  Since the determinant assumes all three values in
\(\mathbb F_3\), it is not a product gate.  For a controller superposition of
one \(\det X=0\) and one \(\det X=1\) basis state and logical
\(|+\rangle=(|1\rangle+|2\rangle)/\sqrt2\), the two logical branches have
overlap
\[
\langle +|z|+\rangle=-{1\over2},
\]
so the output has Schmidt rank two.

The gate is also non-Clifford on the controller.  For prime qutrits, a
cube-root diagonal Clifford has a quadratic polynomial exponent.  The exact
adjoint invariant calculation already proves that the only invariant
quadratic is \(q_2(X)=\operatorname{tr}(X^2)\), while all nine pairs
\((q_2,\det)\in\mathbb F_3^2\) occur.  Hence \(\det X\) is not any quadratic
function on the controller, and each logical-\(Z\) block
\(\omega^{\pm\det X}\) is non-Clifford.

This does not contradict the single-sector Schur no-go.  On a fixed
\(729\)-dimensional Schr\"odinger sector the determinant phase is scalar.  The
new unitary uses both controller dependence and the doubled logical sector.
What remains physically open is an actual VOA/OPE, Hamiltonian, photonic, or
fault-tolerant implementation of \(U_{\det}\).

The doubled-sector coherence question also closes algebraically.  Let
\(H=3^{1+12}\) and adjoin the certified outer involution \(s\) with
\(szs^{-1}=z^{-1}\).  The two \(729\)-dimensional Schr\"odinger irreducibles
\(V_1,V_2\) have distinct central characters and satisfy
\[
V_1^s\simeq V_2\not\simeq V_1.
\]
For the index-two normal extension
\[
G=H\rtimes\langle s\rangle,
\]
Mackey--Clifford irreducibility therefore gives
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Ind}_H^G V_1\ \text{irreducible},\qquad
\left.\operatorname{Ind}_H^G V_1\right|_H=V_1\oplus V_2.}
\]
Thus the certified \(1458\)-dimensional doubled carrier is one irreducible
outer-complete module.  The projectors \(P_1,P_2\) onto the two central
characters obey
\[
sP_1s^{-1}=P_2
\]
and are not central projectors of the enlarged representation algebra.
Therefore \(r=1\) versus \(r=2\) is not an \emph{intrinsic} superselection
label once the outer operation is admitted.

The physical boundary is precise: if the outer coset is not dynamically
realizable and the observable algebra is restricted to operations commuting
with \(z\), then the two labels remain operationally superselected.  The open
question is implementation of the outer operation, not representation theory.
