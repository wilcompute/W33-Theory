\section{The route memory has two layers}
\label{sec:bt2974-bt2983-blueprint}

\begin{plain}
The ten routing modes do not merely carry one error bit per link.  After local
slot labels are removed, the complete connection is thirty-six small nonabelian
holonomies.  Their parity bits remain useful as a fast outer syndrome, but they
do not contain the whole route state.  The machine should therefore keep a
protected $D_4$ route-memory core and expose only a smaller abelian shell to
ordinary scheduling logic.
\end{plain}

\begin{spec}[Pass 2974 --- complete discrete route quotient]
A spanning tree of $K_{10}$ uses nine of the forty-five links.  Gauge fixing the
tree leaves thirty-six chord holonomies.  They classify the connection up to one
simultaneous $D_4$ conjugation.  The exact quotient contains
\[
81{,}129{,}638{,}418{,}148{,}456{,}557{,}941{,}239{,}054{,}336
\]
states.  The existing thirty-six-bit curvature word is its sign projection, not
a replacement for the nonabelian state.
\end{spec}

\begin{spec}[Pass 2975 --- why the ten-mode curvature has $S_6$ symmetry]
The ten modes are the ten unordered $3+3$ partitions of six symbols.  Choosing
one symbol turns them into the ten two-subsets of five symbols.  Disjoint pairs
are Petersen neighbors, and the sixty triples containing an odd number of
Petersen edges reproduce the router's odd-curvature faces.  This gives an exact
$2$-$(10,3,4)$ design and an explicit degree-ten $S_6$ action.
\end{spec}

\begin{spec}[Pass 2976 --- pair-conditioned adaptive receiver]
For a known conjugate pair, the ideal optimized receiver uses Bayesian posterior
updates after each binary measurement and reaches
\[
P_{\rm success}(n)=\tfrac12\left(1+\sqrt{1-3^{-n}}\right).
\]
The local Pauli receiver remains cheaper but starts at $0.788675\ldots$ rather
than the unrestricted one-copy value $0.908248\ldots$.  Readout-flip and erasure
figures in the certificate are synthetic engineering profiles.
\end{spec}

\begin{spec}[Pass 2977 --- honest M36 search boundary]
The full non-CSS search contains $213{,}648{,}435$ rank-four isotropic subspaces
and is divided into $495$ duplicate-free pivot shards.  The completed
$649{,}940$-subspace pilot found six non-CSS collinearity projectors, but all six
accepted stabilizer states.  The full shard sweep remains a release-blocking
open computation, not a completed theorem.
\end{spec}

\begin{plain}[Why the golden controller belongs outside the core]
The shortest controller word that reduces to a two-dimensional rational quotient
is $R_4^2U_6$, and that quotient runs the Fibonacci recursion.  Its balanced
mechanical word is useful for spreading expensive calibration events.  Modulo
two it rotates the three nonzero parity labels, but there is no corresponding
order-three automorphism of $D_4$.  The golden controller can schedule and rotate
the outer syndrome shell without rewriting the protected nonabelian memory.
\end{plain}

\begin{spec}[Passes 2979--2981 --- golden shell]
The outer parity labels form $V_4$.  The Fibonacci quotient acts as $C_3$, giving
$V_4\rtimes C_3\cong A_4$.  Since $|\operatorname{Aut}(D_4)|=8$ and its element
orders are only $1,2,4$, this $C_3$ action has no faithful lift to the route core.
The intended hierarchy is therefore
\[
\text{balanced golden scheduler}\longrightarrow A_4\text{ syndrome shell}
\longrightarrow D_4\text{ route-memory core}.
\]
\end{spec}
