# Pass 1138: Explicit Cubic-Incidence Bridge

## Result

Pass 1135 determined the character of the \(45\)-point cubic carrier and
subtracted it from the \(2240\)-point \(A_2\)-triple carrier.  That proved the
module difference but did not construct the named map.  Pass 1138 supplies the
missing object.

Use doubled integral coordinates for the \(240\) roots of \(E_8\).  Fix an
\(A_2\) root triple and let \(W(E_6)\) be generated by reflections in the
\(72\) roots orthogonal to it.  In a \(27\)-root shell, the constant-sum triples
form the \(45\) cubic supports.  Define

\[
 M\colon \mathbb Q^{2240}\longrightarrow\mathbb Q^{45},\qquad
 M_{s,t}=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&\langle r,u\rangle=0
       \text{ for every }r\in s,\ u\in t,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}
\]

where \(s\) is a cubic support and \(t\) is an unordered zero-sum \(A_2\)
root triple.  GAP 4.12.1 constructs the full \(45\times2240\) matrix and proves

\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}M=45,
 \qquad
 \text{row sums}=32,
 \qquad
 \text{column sums}=0^{2000},6^{240}.
\]

The \(240\) nonzero columns are exactly the unique \(W(E_6)\)-orbit of size
\(240\) in the complete orbit census

\[
 1,1,27^6,240,270,270,432,432,432.
\]

Every basis vector in all three \(432\)-orbits is killed.  This is stronger
than merely finding no degree-\(81\) constituent in the image.

## Exact Gram identity

Let \(A_{45}\) be the disjointness adjacency matrix on the cubic supports.
The already established graph is
\(\operatorname{SRG}(45,32,22,24)\); Pass 1138 uses that relation but
reconstructs it from the \(E_8\) supports.  Exact multiplication gives

\[
 \boxed{MM^{\mathsf T}=24I_{45}-6A_{45}+8J_{45}}.
\]

Two disjoint supports have row inner product \(2\), while two intersecting
supports have row inner product \(8\).  The corresponding distribution is
\(2^{720},8^{270}\), and

\[
 \operatorname{spec}(MM^{\mathsf T})
   =\{192^1,48^{20},12^{24}\}.
\]

In particular, the Gram matrix is positive definite, giving a second exact
proof that \(M\) is onto.

\[
 \mathbb Q[240]
  \cong 1\oplus15_a\oplus20\oplus24\oplus30\oplus60_a\oplus90.

The subscript labels use the character-table crosswalk already fixed by

\[
 \boxed{\ker(M|_{\mathbb Q[240]})
   \cong15_a\oplus30\oplus60_a\oplus90},

The \(2000\) dead basis vectors plus this \(195\)-dimensional internal kernel

## Equivariant \(E_8\)-support/W33-octet bridge

The verifier independently constructs \(W(3,3)\), its projective similitude
group of order \(51840\), and its \(45\) intrinsic induced \(K_{4,4}\) octets.
The stabilizer of a cubic support and the stabilizer of an octet both have
order \(1152\) and GAP structure

\[
 ((C_2^3){:}C_2^2){:}(S_3\times S_3).
\]

An exact isomorphism \(W(E_6)\cong PGSp(4,3)\) takes the first stabilizer to
a conjugate of the second.  Coset transport therefore constructs a
\(W(E_6)\)-equivariant bijection

\[
 \{\text{\(45\) \(E_8\) cubic supports}\}
 \;\longleftrightarrow\;
 \{\text{\(45\) intrinsic W33 \(K_{4,4}\) octets}\}.
\]

It transports the relation exactly:

\[
 s\cap s'=\varnothing
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 |\mathcal O_s\cap\mathcal O_{s'}|=2.
\]

This is an exact equivariant existence theorem, not a canonical labeling:
the displayed bijection depends on a chosen group isomorphism and stabilizer
conjugator.

## The complete \(2240\to45\to40\) bridge

Let \(N\) be point/octet incidence after transporting the octets into the
cubic-support ordering.  The prior point/octet theorem gives

\[
 NN^{\mathsf T}=8I_{40}+J_{40}+2A_{W33},
 \qquad
 N^{\mathsf T}N=8I_{45}+2A_{45}.
\]

Pass 1138 constructs the composite

\[
 C=NM\colon\mathbb Q^{2240}\longrightarrow\mathbb Q^{40}
\]

and verifies

\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}C=25,\qquad
 \boxed{CC^{\mathsf T}
   =96I_{40}+24A_{W33}+336J_{40}}.

Its Gram spectrum is

\[
 \operatorname{spec}(CC^{\mathsf T})
   =\{13824^1,144^{24},0^{15}\}.

Thus \(C\) lands exactly in the \(1+24\) W33 point sector.  The
\(20\)-dimensional cubic sector is the intermediate component killed by
\(N\), and \(\dim\ker C=2215=2195+20\).

## Sixfold W33-line factorization

The composite has a sharper combinatorial form.  Its \(2000\) dead columns
are zero.  Every live column has value \(1\) on \(36\) W33 points and value
\(3\) on four points; those four points form a totally isotropic W33 line.
All \(40\) lines occur, exactly six times each.  Consequently the live
submatrix factors as

\[
 \boxed{C_{\mathrm{live}}=J_{40\times240}+2R},
\]

where \(R\) is the pullback of W33 point-line incidence along an equivariant
six-to-one map

\[
 \{\text{live \(240\) \(A_2\) triples}\}
 \longrightarrow
 \{\text{\(40\) W33 lines}\}.
\]

Indeed \(RR^{\mathsf T}=6(4I+A_{W33})\), and the row sum of \(R\) is \(24\).
This immediately explains

\[
 CC^{\mathsf T}
 =240J+96J+4\!\left(24I+6A_{W33}\right)
 =96I+24A_{W33}+336J.
\]

The sixfold line fibration is the strongest new structural result of the
pass: the rank-\(25\) composite is a weighted line-incidence pullback, not
an unexplained spectral coincidence.
