# Pass 559 — radial-anchor and quadratic Hjelmslev packets

The Pass-554 affine packet family is enlarged from `F3^8` to `F3^10` by two symmetry-selected functions:

- a common deep-anchor amplitude on the four nonprimitive classes;
- a common quadratic character `q ell_b(u)^2` on the 36 primitive lifts.

The exact spectral images over the seven ordered layers are

`13, 26, 96, 336, 921, 3056, 9266`.

The corresponding minimal future-complete state counts are

`41, 122, 365, 1094, 3281, 9841, 9266`.

The radial packet is spectrally Markov at the coarse polynomial level. The quadratic packet creates one ambiguous parent polynomial. The old 6,561-section family is exactly the `d=q=0` face, but its 921 terminal polynomials refine to all 3,281 projective affine histories once the new futures are admitted.
