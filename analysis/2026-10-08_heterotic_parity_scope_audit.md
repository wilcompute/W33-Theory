# W33 versus published heterotic R-parity benchmarks (scope audit)

Pass10960's frozen dataset concerns **215 specific W33-twist Z6-I/II models**, not every heterotic Z6-II compactification. Its stated matter-even FI-cancelling singlet no-go is scoped to that ledger, rational charges, support constraints and selection rules.

A distinct model class is studied by Lebedev *et al.*, *Heterotic road to the MSSM with R parity*, Phys. Rev. D 77, 046013 (2008), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.77.046013 and https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2691. The authors report exact R parity and vectorlike exotic decoupling along D-flat directions. This does not refute the W33-subclass census absent an explicit model identification.

**Pending overlap gate:** normalize and compare full E8xE8 gauge shifts, Wilson lines, space group, B-L and hypercharge lattices, singlet support, F/D constraints, and established plane-R selection rules. A shared Z6-II order or SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1) gauge label proves no inclusion.

**Logical firewall:** the statement "no FI-cancelling *matter-even singlet* vacuum in the selected W33 dataset" does *not* imply "no parity-preserving heterotic Z6-II models" or a quantitative proton lifetime. Pass10960 already cites the Lebedev paper; this is a precision audit, not a new external discovery.
