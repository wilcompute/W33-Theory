\subsection{Dual completion, induced-subgraph enumerators, and higher zeta tomography (Passes 4511--4518)}
\label{sec:pass4511-4518-dual-enumerator-ihara-q53}

Passes~4495--4502 proved $C_{\rm ap}=[1620,39,162]$, identified the $2160$
minimum dual words with triangular prisms, and left a $366$-dimensional gap
between their rank-$1215$ span and $\dim C_{\rm ap}^{\perp}=1581$.  The present
packet closes that dual gap, identifies the prism-induced $240$-set by an
explicit equivariant map, pushes the signed Ihara hierarchy through lengths
six--eight, and separates what can and cannot yet be proved about the complete
weight enumerator and the larger $GQ(3,9)$ apartment code.

\paragraph{Pass 4511: weight four closes the entire missing dual sector.}
There are exactly $84240$ weight-four words of $C_{\rm ap}^{\perp}$.  Their
binary span has dimension
\[
 \boxed{1580},
\]
which is exactly the even-coordinate-weight subcode of the $1581$-dimensional
dual.  Adding any odd weight-three prism word therefore gives all of
$C_{\rm ap}^{\perp}$.  Equivalently,
\[
 \boxed{\langle C^\perp_{(4)}\rangle=C^\perp_{\rm even},\qquad
        \langle C^\perp_{(3)},C^\perp_{(4)}\rangle=C^\perp.}
\]
Under $PGSp(4,3)$ the $84240$ weight-four relations split into nine orbits of
sizes
\[
1620,1620,3240,6480,6480,12960,12960,12960,25920.
\]
The final orbit alone supplies all $366$ dimensions missing from the prism span.
Under the inner $PSp(4,3)$ action that same $25920$-set is a regular orbit, so a
single weight-four seed and its full inner orbit already span the complete
$1580$-dimensional even dual code.

\paragraph{Pass 4512: the full enumerator reduces exactly to four W33 statistics, but is not yet numerically closed.}
For a coefficient subset $S$ of the forty dual-W33 line vertices write
$m=|S|$, let $e(S)$ count induced dual-W33 edges, $p_3(S)$ induced $P_3$
triples, and $c_4(S)$ induced apartment quadrangles.  Expanding odd apartment
intersection parity by
\[
 {\bf1}_{j\ {m odd}}={j\choose1}-2{j\choose2}+4{j\choose3}-8{j\choose4}
\]
and using the exact apartment incidences gives
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{wt}(H^T1_S)
 =162m-12{m\choose2}-42e(S)+12p_3(S)-8c_4(S).}
\]
Hence the complete enumerator is exactly the induced-subgraph sum
\[
 \boxed{W_C(z)=\frac12\sum_{S\subseteq V(W33)}
 z^{162|S|-12{ |S|\choose2}-42e(S)+12p_3(S)-8c_4(S)}.}
\]
The factor $1/2$ is the global-complement kernel.  Burnside on
$\operatorname{Aut}(C)=PGSp(4,3)$, whose action on the forty coefficients has
$25$ cycle types, leaves $21578952$ subset orbits and
\[
 \boxed{10789604}
\]
codeword orbits after also quotienting by complement.  This is a rigorous
reduction of the remaining exact computation, \emph{not} a completed numerical
coefficient table.  The full weight enumerator therefore remains open.

\paragraph{Pass 4513: the prism $240$-orbit is exactly the W33 edge action.}
Let a prism have six-line indicator $b$.  For every prism there is a unique
adjacent dual-line pair $i\sim j$ such that
\[
 \boxed{A_*b=A_*(e_i+e_j).}
\]
Conversely all $240$ adjacent pairs occur.  Thus the protected $240$-set of
Pass~4502 is equivariantly the dual-W33 edge set, rather than merely another
set of cardinality $240$.  Every edge has exactly nine prism preimages.  Its two
endpoints lie outside each six-line prism and each endpoint meets exactly three
of the prism lines.  The inner edge stabilizer has order $108$ and the previously
observed suborbit fingerprint
\[
1,1,2,2,18,18,18,18,27,27,54,54,
\]
while the outer extension has stabilizer order $216$ and suborbits
$1,1,4,18,18,18,18,27,27,108$.  In particular this explicit edge map prevents
a count-only identification with the distinct $E_8$ root action.

\paragraph{Pass 4514: primitive lengths six--eight have exact parity-support decompositions.}
A meet-in-the-middle diagonal nonbacktracking trace, transported by directed-edge

\[

These are exact finite orbit sums, not sampled prime statistics.

\paragraph{Pass 4515: $Q(5,3)=GQ(3,9)$ scales the code, but its distance equality remains open.}
Pass~4506 already owns the exact $112$-point, $280$-line, $102060$-apartment
construction and its $70$-dimensional apartment/protected quotient.  The new code
question starts from its rank-$279$ apartment incidence matrix.  Every line row
has weight $1458$, and for a coefficient subset $S$ in the $280$-vertex
line-intersection graph,
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{wt}(H^T1_S)
 =1458m-12{m\choose2}-150e(S)+36p_3(S)-8c_4(S).}
\]
The exact support-two weights are $2754^{5040}$ and $2904^{34020}$; the
support-three spectrum is
\[
3888^{13440},\quad4074^{136080},\quad4188^{1088640},\quad4338^{2381400}.
\]
A universal lower bound excludes every coefficient support size $2\le m\le12$
from beating the line word.  After fixing complement symmetry, any counterexample
to $d=1458$ must therefore have support between $13$ and $140$.  Accordingly
\[
 \boxed{d\le1458\quad\text{and equality remains OPEN}.}
\]
The dual side does close: its minimum distance is $3$, with exactly
\[
 \boxed{544320=4536{10\choose3}}
\]
minimum words, again the three rectangular apartment faces of triangular prisms.
Here $4536$ is the number of noncollinear point pairs and the ten choices around
such a pair are the $t+1$ common neighbors.

\paragraph{Pass 4516 (outside the box): each W33 edge carries an affine nine-sheet prism fiber.}
The order-$108$ inner edge stabilizer acts on the nine prism preimages with image
order $36$ and kernel order $3$.  The image has element-order census
\[
1^1,\quad2^9,\quad3^8,\quad4^{18}.
\]
Its identity plus eight order-three elements form a normal regular translation
subgroup $C_3^2$, while a fiber-point stabilizer is cyclic of order four.  Thus
\[
 \boxed{G_{\rm fiber}\cong C_3^2{:}C_4,\qquad
        1\to C_3\to G_{\rm edge}\to C_3^2{:}C_4\to1.}
\]
This is an action-theoretic explanation of the nine-to-one prism projection, not
a physical nine-state subsystem.

\paragraph{Pass 4517 (outside the box): one regular orbit generates the even dual code.}
A representative weight-four relation in the regular inner orbit uses eight
lines whose induced dual-W33 subgraph has fourteen edges, two triangles and
degree sequence
\[
3,3,3,3,4,4,4,4.
\]
Its four apartment supports have pairwise intersection sizes
$0,0,2,2,2,2$.  The $25920$ $PSp(4,3)$ translates of this one relation span
rank $1580$.  Hence a single orbit seed is a symmetry-complete generator for the
entire even dual sector.

\paragraph{Pass 4518 (outside the box): the sixth Ihara coefficient reconstructs W33.}
Inside the primitive length-six Walsh polynomial, the degree-two coefficient is
$252$ for the $240$ adjacent line pairs and $48$ for the $540$ disjoint pairs.
Therefore its off-diagonal quadratic-coefficient matrix is exactly
\[
 \boxed{48(J-I)+204A_*.}
\]
So the two-body Walsh layer of $C_6$ alone reconstructs the dual-W33 adjacency
matrix.  Together with Pass~4513 this gives two independent reconstructions of
the same substrate: the prism projection recovers its edge action, while the
length-six zeta coefficient recovers its adjacency.
