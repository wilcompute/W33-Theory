# Global gauge group and defects of the canonical W33 vacuum

The canonical light vacuum has connected faithful stabilizer `S(U(3) × U(2))` inside the faithful parent `(E6_sc × SU(3))/Z3`. Its gauge orbit has integer monopole charge and a `Z3` string charge. These statements use the actual VEV tensors and distinguish the faithful parent from its simply connected cover. [Canonical vacuum](2026-11-09_canonical_sm_flatness.md), [The same parent quotient inside E8](https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/atmp/2010/0014/0002/ATMP-2010-0014-0002-a001.pdf)

## Finite stabilizer calculation

Use `(g,f)` in the simply connected cover. Preservation of the three separate fundamental VEVs forces

```text
g e0 = a e0, g e1 = a e1, f = a^-1 I3, a^3 = 1.
```

Indeed, the first two rank-one tensors fix two family lines and the third fixes their orthogonal family line. Its internal factor `e0+e1` forces equality of the two internal phases. The conjugate VEVs impose no further restriction on this subgroup, and the adjoint adds the condition that `g` commute with `Y`. There is a central element of `E6_sc` acting by each cube root on the 27. Dividing out the diagonal ineffective center reduces the stabilizer computation to the pointwise internal stabilizer of `e0,e1` that centralizes `Y`.

The replay constructs a primitive internal circle generator `H` with charge pattern `1_4 + 10_-2 + 16_1`. In the trace metric the moment of the first weight ray is `H/18`. The first ray stabilizer is therefore the compact centralizer of `H`, since its charge-four eigenspace is one-dimensional. The root chart gives its `D5` semisimple part. The embedded `Spin(10)` is faithful because its center acts faithfully on the half-spin 16. The circle at angle `pi/2` has phases `(1,-1,i)`, the action of a generator of the `Spin(10)` center. This identifies the intersection as `Z4`. The circle phase on `e0` is the fourth power, so fixing the vector gives precisely `Spin(10)`. The angle `2pi/3` acts as one common primitive cube root throughout the 27, which also verifies the nontrivial `E6` central action used above. [Compact E6 and its D5 centralizer](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/node17.html)

The second vector `e1` lies in a single half-spin weight line. The half-spin weights are minuscule, so this is a pure-spinor ray. Its projective stabilizer in `Spin(10)` is the connected double cover of `U(5)`, described by `z²=det U`. The spinor phase is `z` or its inverse. Fixing the vector therefore gives `SU(5)`. The replay identifies its faithful five-dimensional block and reads `6Y = diag(-2,-2,-2,3,3)` there. Centralizing `Y` gives exactly `S(U(3) × U(2))`, a connected group. [Pure spinor stabilizer](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0210073)

In the cover the full stabilizer is `S(U(3) × U(2)) × Z3`, with the second factor the ineffective center. In the faithful quotient it is just the connected first factor. Both descriptions give the same gauge orbit. The local field content allows this distinction in ultraviolet global form, which matters for line operators.

## Monopole and string charge

Let `G` be the faithful parent and `H_SM` the faithful stabilizer. The inclusion of `H_SM` lifts to the simply connected cover, by sending it to `(h,1)`. Therefore the map `pi1(H_SM)=Z → pi1(G)=Z3` is zero. The long exact sequence then gives `pi2(G/H_SM)=Z` and `pi1(G/H_SM)=Z3`.

The Standard Model covering map sends `(A,B,e^{iθ})` to `diag(e^{-2iθ} A,e^{3iθ} B)`. Its kernel is generated by the color and weak centers together with `θ=pi/3`. A primitive monopole loop is `exp(iφ m)` with `m=diag(1,0,0,-1,0)` and `0≤φ≤2pi`. Its color-block determinant winds once. Its Abelian projection is `−Y`. This gives a concrete cocharacter for the unit integer sector.

For a subsequent neutral electroweak VEV the stabilizer is `(SU(3) × U(1)_em)/Z3`. On the same five-block, `Q=diag(-1/3,-1/3,-1/3,1,0)`. The monopole loop already lies in this subgroup. Its electromagnetic projection is `−Q`, with color compensation `diag(2/3,-1/3,-1/3,0,0)`. In the convention `e Q A` the magnitude of the Abelian flux is `2pi/e`, accompanied by the color-center flux. The electromagnetic subgroup and Standard Model subgroup have the same primitive color-determinant winding, so their fundamental groups map isomorphically. This electroweak step adds neither a topological string charge nor a monopole charge.

If the parent quotient is realized as an intermediate subgroup of simply connected compact `E8`, the first breaking has `pi2(E8/G)=Z3`. In the fibration `G/H_SM → E8/H_SM → E8/G`, the boundary map from this `Z3` to the low-stage string `Z3` is an isomorphism. The high-stage monopole sector therefore supplies endpoints for the low-stage strings. This statement is conditional on that ultraviolet breaking sequence. Formation rates and defect masses require dynamics.
