\paragraph{Pass 4536: the missing tenth direction is coefficient parity.}
Let $A_*$ be the dual-W33 line-intersection matrix over $\mathbb F_2$.  Pass~4534
showed that the protected edge vectors $A_*(e_i+e_j)$ span the unique

the connected line graph's endpoint vectors span the full even-weight hyperplane

\[
 \boxed{\pi(A_*b)=\sum_i b_i\pmod2}
\]
is a well-defined nonzero functional on $H_{10}$ with
\[
 \ker\pi=V_9=A_*(\text{even coefficient vectors}).
\]
Thus any single line-star $A_*e_i$ supplies the missing tenth direction.  The
complete parity-refined protected weight enumerator is
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}

A_w&1&135&240&135&1
\end{array},\qquad
\begin{array}{c|ccc}

A_w&40&432&40.
\end{array}
\]
The $240$ even weight-$20$ vectors are exactly the protected edge shell; the
forty odd minimum vectors are exactly the forty line-stars.
