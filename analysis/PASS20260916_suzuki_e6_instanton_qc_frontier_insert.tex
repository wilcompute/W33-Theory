\subsection{Suzuki--$E_6$ incidence, instanton support, and quantum-computation frontiers}
\label{sec:20260916-suzuki-e6-instanton-qc}

The exact $12$-dimensional $\mathbf F_3$ representation of $2.\mathrm{Suz}$ now exposes a second local geometry inside the $32760$-point Leech tight orbit.  Fixing one tight-orbit point, the $165$ certified full $W(3,3)$ four-spaces through it form
\[
 \operatorname{srg}(165,36,3,9),
\]
the $U_5(2)$ polar graph (equivalently the point graph of $GQ(4,8)$).  Its spectrum is
\[
 36^1+3^{120}+(-9)^{44},
\]
so the permutation module splits as $165=(1+44)+120=45+120$.  This is an exact spectral coincidence with the repository's $45$ tritangent and $120$ Steiner carriers; no equivariant identification of those modules is asserted without a separate intertwiner.  The $36$ neighbours of a base four-space split as nine non-isotropic projective lines through the chosen point, four neighbours on each line.

The $1216215$ orthogonal three-$W33$ decompositions carry a natural refactorization graph: two frames are adjacent when they share one $W33$ block.  Each frame has $3(27-1)=78$ neighbours, hence the graph has $47432385$ edges.  The complete local move libraries fixing the blocks generate $\mathrm{Suz}$ projectively and $2.\mathrm{Suz}$ in the symplectic lift.  They do not generate the full six-qutrit Clifford group:
\[
 [\mathrm{Sp}(12,3):2.\mathrm{Suz}]
 =16054507443872541110987520.
\]
Thus refactorization is an exact large Clifford compiler primitive, but it is neither full-Clifford universal nor a non-Clifford resource by itself.

On the cubic side, an individual $\zeta_{12}$ phase assigned to one of the $45$ $E_6$ tritangent triples is not invariant under the homogeneous rephasing allowed by the exact $E_8\to$Pauli cocycle lift.  Let $R$ be the $27\times45$ line--tritangent incidence matrix and $N$ the canonical $45\times36$ tritangent--double-six matrix.  The earlier integral theorem $RN=3Q$, together with
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbf F_3}R=21,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbf F_3}N=14,
\]
gives
\[
 \dim\ker R=24,
 \qquad
 \dim H_1=10.
\]
Hence single-triangle Pauli-center phases cannot supply magic.  If an enriched Pauli/VOA transport exists, its first gauge-invariant global cubic phase target is the ten-dimensional mod-$3$ homology sector.

A separate totally isotropic $PG(3,3)$ section of the Suzuki orbit meets the tight set in $PG(3,3)\setminus PG(1,3)$.  Its $81$ internal graph lines identify with $M_2(\mathbf F_3)$.  Rank-two difference gives
\[
 \operatorname{srg}(81,48,27,30)
\]
with spectrum $48^1+3^{48}+(-6)^{32}$.  Exactly $18$ two-dimensional MRD subspaces occur; their nine affine cosets give $162$ distinct nine-line parallel frames, each closing with the deleted line to a ten-line projective spread.  These are projective spreads in a totally isotropic four-space and are not the $36$ symplectic $W33$ spreads.

The local $165$ carrier also reconstructs all $297$ lines of $GQ(4,8)$ as five-element cliques, with nine lines through each point and every collinear pair on a unique line.  This is the same finite $U_5(2)$ degree-$165/297$ incidence carrier that admits the classical quaternionic tight-projective-$3$-design realization in $\mathbf H P^4$.  The present theorem identifies the finite $G$-set only; a physical complex/fusion-frame realization remains open.

On the heterotic branch, the Rule--4/Rule--5-safe exotic channels have positive classical $A_2^3/\mathbf Z_3$ worldsheet-instanton kernels.  Writing
\[
 K_S(T)=\sum_{m,n\in\mathbf Z}e^{-2\pi T(m^2-mn+n^2)},\qquad
 K_D(T)=\sum_{m,n\in\mathbf Z}e^{-2\pi TQ(m+1/3,n+2/3)},
\]
both are strictly positive for $T>0$.  The surviving sextic and nonic aggregate factors are products and positive sums of these kernels, so classical-instanton cancellation does not erase the combined structural rank-nine support.  This is still not a numerical low-energy mass matrix: Kahler metrics, singlet VEVs, quantum/OPE normalization and physical-state phases remain to be supplied.

Finally, the exact all-level twining identities
\[
 Z^{A_8^3}_{(123)}=T_{3C},\qquad
 Z^{A_8^3}_{(12)}=T_{2A}+80
\]
do not imply a direct $A_8^3$ orbifold construction of Moonshine.  The factor $3$-cycle fixes diagonal $\mathfrak{sl}_9\subset\mathfrak{sl}_9^3$, of dimension $80$, while $V^\natural_1=0$, and its permutation-twisted vacuum shift is $8/9$.  The certified $\mathbf Z_9$ center/simple-current glue cannot remove those currents because the center is trivial on the adjoint.  The Monster match is therefore an exact twining shadow; any literal surgery requires structure beyond factor permutation plus center glue.
