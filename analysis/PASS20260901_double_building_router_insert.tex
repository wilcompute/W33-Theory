\subsection{The two building homologies and their common obstruction router}
The order-$25920$ group carried by the construction has two rank-two building
realisations.  Their Levi graphs have
\[
 (V,E)=(80,160),\qquad (72,135),
\]
so their first-homology dimensions are respectively
\[
 160-80+1=81,\qquad 135-72+1=64.
\]
The first is the $W(3,3)$ building.  The second is the $GQ(4,2)$ building,
and the existing objectwise atlas identifies its $27+45$ vertices and $135$
incidences with the cubic-surface $27$ lines and $45$ tritangent planes, and
simultaneously with the selected $E_8$ ten-$D_4$ charts and $D_4\oplus D_4$
packets.  The two homology characters have norm one and inner product zero.
By the Solomon--Tits interpretation these are the degree-$81$ and degree-$64$
Steinberg modules for the two exceptional rank-two presentations; the finite
character calculations themselves do not depend on that name.

The older $81$-dimensional rational clique-Hodge sector is now identified
without a dimension argument: its oriented simplicial Lefschetz character is
equal, element by element on all $25920$ group elements, to the $W(3,3)$
Levi-building $H_1$ character.  Its value profile is
\[
 -3^{810},\;-1^{3240},\;0^{16640},\;1^{5184},\;9^{45},\;81^1,
\]
and its character norm is one.  Thus the characteristic-zero harmonic
$81$-space used earlier in the paper is exactly the same irreducible
Steinberg module as the building cycle space.

On the degree-$1080$ obstruction carrier the two building modules occur with
multiplicity three each,
\[
 3\,H_1(W(3,3))\oplus3\,H_1(GQ(4,2))
 =3\cdot81\oplus3\cdot64
\]
as part of the full Wedderburn decomposition.  In contrast, the two
nonconjugate degree-$216$ source shells select them complementarily: the
five-circuit shell contains the $81$ once and the hemisystem shell contains
the $64$ once.

The degree-$2160$ BT796/$48$-packet carrier contains nine copies of the
$81$ module and five copies of the $64$ module.  Projecting its complete
$113$-dimensional equivariant Hom space to the two multiplicity-three target
blocks gives
\[
 \dim\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}(81^3)=9,
 \qquad
 \dim\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}(64^3)=9.
\]
Consequently one finite geometric carrier generates both complete multiplicity
algebras,
\[
 \operatorname{End}_G(81^3)\oplus\operatorname{End}_G(64^3)
 \simeq M_3(\mathbb Q)\oplus M_3(\mathbb Q).
\]

\subsubsection{Chain-level materialisation}
This statement is no longer only character-theoretic.  Exact oriented boundary
matrices give integral cycle bases
\[
 Z_{81}\in\mathbb Z^{160\times81},\qquad
 Z_{64}\in\mathbb Z^{135\times64}.
\]
For each of the three primitive rank-$81$ obstruction projectors and each of
the three primitive rank-$64$ projectors, a source-chamber/obstruction orbital
incidence map was projected and composed with the appropriate cycle basis.
This produces six explicit rational intertwiners
\[
 H_1(W(3,3))\longrightarrow P_i\mathbb Q^{1080},\quad i=1,2,3,
\]
\[
 H_1(GQ(4,2))\longrightarrow Q_i\mathbb Q^{1080},\quad i=1,2,3.
\]
Every map is generator-equivariant and has full column rank, independently
verified modulo $1000003$ and $1000033$.  Thus all six multiplicity copies are
concrete images of building cycles rather than labels inferred from equal
dimensions.
