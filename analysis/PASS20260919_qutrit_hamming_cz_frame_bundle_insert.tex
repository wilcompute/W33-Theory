\subsection{The 72-slot packet frame is the full qutrit Hamming frame of the \(CZ_3\) entangler}
\label{sec:20260919-qutrit-hamming-cz-frame}

The punctured-Hesse packet group admits a sharper interpretation at \(q=3\).
The nine affine bins are the two-trit computational grid
\[
\mathbb F_3^2,
\]
whose Hamming graph \(H(2,3)\) joins basis labels differing in exactly one
coordinate.  The packet-frame group is
\[
\mathbb F_3^2\rtimes D_8
\cong AGL(1,3)\wr C_2
\cong S_3\wr C_2
=\operatorname{Aut}H(2,3),
\]

full local classical relabelling group of the two-qutrit computational basis.

For
\[
CZ_3|x,y\rangle=\omega^{xy}|x,y\rangle,
\]
the zero-phase support is the five-state cross
\[
Z_0=\{(x,y):xy=0\}.
\]
Its stabiliser inside the 72-element local-frame group is exactly the linear
monomial \(D_8\).  The orbit of \(Z_0\) consists of the nine translated crosses
\[
Z_{a,b}=\{(x,y):(x-a)(y-b)=0\},
\]
one centered on each affine Hesse bin.  Hence
\[
G_3/D_8\cong\mathbb F_3^2
\]
is literally the bundle of nine \(CZ\) zero-phase frames.

The two retained infinity points of the punctured plane are
\[
[1:1:0],\qquad [1:-1:0].
\]
For a linear monomial frame change
\[

\]

\[
xy\longmapsto \chi(L)\,xy,\qquad \chi(L)=\alpha\beta.
\]

swap).  Because every nonzero element of \(\mathbb F_3\) is self-inverse,
\[
\frac{\beta}{\alpha}=\alpha\beta.
\]
Therefore the two infinity points swap if and only if
\[
CZ_3\longleftrightarrow CZ_3^{-1}.
\]
The existing runtime alphabet
\[

\]

centers plus the two nonzero \(CZ\) phase sectors.
