\paragraph{Pass 4976: shell-three correlations.}
On the 360 edges of the double-six graph $H_{36}$, the Pass4859 switching cochain is even on exactly the
1080 non-Steiner triangles and odd on the 120 Steiner triangles.  The 1080 even triangle vectors span
all $324$ dimensions of $K^\perp$.  Hence the extremal signed-shell values are rigid:
\[
T_3=+1080\Rightarrow T_j=A_j,\qquad
T_3=-1080\Rightarrow T_j=(-1)^jA_j .
\]
In particular, the Pass4960 truncated-moment witness with $T_3=-1080$ and $T_4=-1936$ is not a
character of $K^\perp$: it would have to satisfy $T_4=10530$.  This does not yet lower the rigorous
covering-radius ceiling $173$.

\paragraph{Pass 4977: the PGSp outer twist does not restore the dark 15.}
The point and line permutation modules both extend from $\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)$ to
$\mathrm{PGSp}(4,3)$.  Therefore conjugation by the multiplier-minus-one outer element fixes the
isomorphism class of the unique $15$-dimensional constituent in each module.  The twisted point-line
Hom spaces remain zero.  A true point/line correlation, if used, requires structure outside these
ordinary PGSp permutation extensions.

\paragraph{Pass 4978: Witting phase compensates the quadratic outer sign.}
The two-dimensional quadratic Hom multiplicity space of Pass4875 carries outer action $-I_2$, while
the oriented Witting phase line of Pass4966 carries the same nontrivial quotient character.

\[
H_{\rm comp}=H\otimes\varepsilon_W,\qquad (-I_2)(-1)=+I_2,

so the compensated quadratic multiplicity space is PGSp-even.  Unlike the intrinsic quartic bracket
of Pass4941, this cancellation does not select a preferred projective quadratic channel.

\paragraph{Pass 4979: the 45 tritangents read the missing $1+20$ spread sector.}
Let $M$ be the $45\times36$ matrix with $M_{TD}=1$ when the tritangent $T$ meets the corresponding
double-six in exactly two of its three cubic lines.  Every row has weight $24$, every column weight
$30$, and
\[
\rank M=21,\qquad M^{\mathsf T}M=18J+12I+3A_{H_{36}}.
\]
Thus $M$ transmits the $1+20$ sector and kills the $15$.  The Pass4967 spread-line matrix $C$ obeys
$CC^{\mathsf T}=4J+6I-3A_{H_{36}}$, hence
\[
\boxed{18I_{36}=CC^{\mathsf T}+M^{\mathsf T}M-22J_{36}} .
\]
The stacked $40+45$ readout has rank $36$: W33 lines plus cubic tritangents reconstruct the complete
36-spread/double-six carrier.

\paragraph{Passes 4980 and 4982: the local nine-spread gauge.}
At every W33 line, the nine incident spreads have the canonical Steiner partition $3+3+3$.  The full
line stabilizer has order $1296$ and acts faithfully as the complete imprimitive wreath product
$S_3\wr S_3$.  Therefore the block system is canonical but its labels are not.  If the partition is
treated as one parallel class of $\mathrm{AG}(2,3)$, there are exactly $12$ affine-plane completions;
the local wreath product acts transitively on all twelve, with completion stabilizer order $108$.
Thus a qutrit compiler must fix a finite gauge rather than inherit canonical affine coordinates.

\paragraph{Pass 4981: tetrahedral Bianchi closure.}

\[
H_{abc}H_{acd}H_{adb}=P_{ab}H_{bcd}P_{ba}.
\]

\paragraph{Pass 4983: the geometric meaning of $A_3(K^\perp)=1080$.}
Among the $1200$ triangles of the one-overlap graph on the 36 spreads, exactly $120$ have one common
W33 line and exactly $1080$ have empty triple intersection.  The former are the Steiner triangles;
the latter are exactly the complete weight-three shell of $K^\perp$.  Thus a pairwise one-overlap
spread triple is a weight-three dual check precisely when its common W33-line intersection is empty.
