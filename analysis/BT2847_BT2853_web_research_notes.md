# Passes 2847--2853 web research notes

Web research was used to calibrate terminology, not to supply the repository theorems.

- Hierons et al. (ACM SAC 2008, DOI 10.1145/1363686.1363850) establish that adaptive distinguishing sequences can replace preset sequences in checking constructions and may be exponentially shorter.
- Robinson-Mallett et al. (A-MOST 2005, DOI 10.1145/1083274.1083283) formulate optimal distinguishing-sequence generation through model checking.
- Rivest and Schapire (STOC 1989, DOI 10.1145/73007.73047) study no-reset automaton inference using homing sequences.
- Qureshi et al. (arXiv:1611.10268) study maximum-likelihood equivalence regions for binary asymmetric channels.

These sources support the use of “adaptive distinguishing experiment,” “no-reset,” and “binary asymmetric maximum likelihood.” The exact depths, sample counts, groups, recurrences, and thresholds in this pass range were derived independently from the W33 repository model.
