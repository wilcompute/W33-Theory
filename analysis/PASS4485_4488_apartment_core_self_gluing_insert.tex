\subsection{The repeated eight-core and the nonsplit apartment extension (Passes 4485--4488)}
\label{sec:pass4485-apartment-core-self-gluing}

Let $M=\mathbb F_2^{40}$ be the W33 line module, $A_*=N^TN\bmod2$,
$K=\ker A_*$, $I=\operatorname{im}A_*$, $R=\ker N$,
$U=R\cap I$, and $J=\langle\mathbf1\rangle$.  Pass~4481 gives
\[
 \dim(J,U,I,K,M)=(1,9,10,30,40).
\]
Since $A_*^2=0$, $I\subset K$, while $A_*$ induces the equivariant
isomorphism
\[
 \overline A_*:M/K\xrightarrow{\sim}I.
\]
The protected quotient $M/K\cong H_{10}$ therefore has the literal filtration
\[
 0<J<U<I,
 \qquad 1\mid8\mid1.
\]
The protected middle eight-space itself is \emph{not} new here: Pass~176
already proved the incidence-fixed-line quadratic isometry from
$f^\perp/\langle f\rangle$ onto the route hull $U/J$ (in its route-code
notation), including the plus-type $136/120$ census.  The new apartment-side
statement is that this same pre-existing $U/J$ also sits as an invariant
submodule of the newly discovered apartment radical $K/J$.  Thus one owned
eight-core appears on both sides of
\[
 0\to K/J\;(29)\to M/J\;(39)\to M/K\;(10)\to0.
\]

Pass~4486 compares the two \emph{newly juxtaposed} form structures.  On the
radical occurrence, the apartment polar form and its Hamming quadratic
refinement vanish identically on $U/J$.  On the protected occurrence, the
Pass-176 form can be written through $\overline A_*$ as
\[
 B_{\rm prot}(A_*b,A_*c)=b^TA_*c,
 \qquad
 q_{\rm prot}(A_*b)=\frac{\operatorname{wt}(Nb)}2\pmod2.
\]
These expressions are lift-independent.  On $U/J$ the protected polar form has
rank eight and the quadratic has
\[
 136=1+135\text{ singular classes},\qquad120\text{ anisotropic classes},
\]
reproducing the prior Pass-176 plus-type $O^+(8,2)$ result.  The new conclusion
is therefore the contrast: the same owned module is form-null in the apartment
radical and nondegenerate when viewed through the protected quotient.

Pass~4487 is a reconciliation certificate, not a new claim of the eight-core
itself.  It places the independent Passes 4477--4478 implementation in the same
ambient line coordinates and proves
\[
 \boxed{B_{10}v=\mathbf1,\qquad B_{10}v^\perp=U.}
\]
In the resulting quotient basis, the parallel $F_8$ Gram matrix equals the
Pass-176/lift-defined protected Gram matrix entry by entry, and the parallel
invariant quadratic $q_8$ equals $\operatorname{wt}(Nb)/2$ on all $256$
classes.  Hence Passes 176 and 4477--4478 are exact coordinate realizations of
the same route-hull $U/J$; Passes 4485--4487 add its apartment-radical
placement and cross-packet consistency check.  No E8 dynamics or Pauli hardware
is imported into the radical copy.

Finally, Pass~4488 tests whether the apartment extension splits equivariantly.
Write $E=M/J$, $V=M/K$, and $\Pi:E\to V$.  A $PSp(4,3)$-equivariant section
$S$ would obey
\[
 \Pi S=I_{10},\qquad G_ES=SG_V
\]
for four generators.  The resulting $1660$ affine binary equations in $390$
unknown entries satisfy
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{rank}A=389,\qquad
        \operatorname{rank}[A\mid b]=390,}
\]
so no equivariant section exists.  Moreover
\[
 \dim\operatorname{Hom}_{PSp(4,3)}(V,E)=1.
\]
Its unique nonzero map has rank nine, projects to zero in $V$, and is exactly
\[
 \boxed{T([b])=[A_*b]\pmod J,}
\]
with image $I/J$ inside the radical and one-dimensional kernel.  Thus the only
nonzero symmetry-preserving return channel folds the protected module back into
the radical; there is no invariant ten-dimensional protected complement.
This nonsplitting result is new relative to the earlier route-hull bridge and
does not forbid non-equivariant decoding or symmetry-breaking hardware.
