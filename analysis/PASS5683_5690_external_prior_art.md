## Ramanujan 2-lifts

Adam Marcus, Daniel A. Spielman, Nikhil Srivastava, *Interlacing Families I: Bipartite Ramanujan Graphs of All Degrees*, arXiv:1304.4132.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.4132

MSS prove the existence of infinite families of regular bipartite Ramanujan graphs in every degree greater than two by proving the existence of suitable good 2-lifts. Pass5683 uses this theorem only after independently verifying that the W33 point-line Levi graph is a 4-regular bipartite Ramanujan graph.

Yonatan Bilu, Nathan Linial, *Constructing expander graphs by 2-lifts and discrepancy vs. spectral gap*, arXiv:math/0312022.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0312022

Bilu–Linial introduced the 2-lift spectral framework in which a lift retains the old spectrum and gains a signed-adjacency new spectrum, and connected discrepancy with the spectral gap. Pass5688's signing comparisons live squarely inside that standard framework.

## Hessian / finite SU(3) group context

W. Grimus, P. O. Ludl, *Principal series of finite subgroups of SU(3)*, arXiv:1006.0098.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.0098

This paper develops the exceptional finite subgroups customarily denoted Sigma(36x3), Sigma(72x3), and Sigma(216x3), including their subgroup/representation structure. No claim is made that the existence of order-216/648 Hessian-family groups in SU(3) is new.

Lei Yang, *Hessian polyhedra, invariant theory and Appell hypergeometric partial differential equations*, arXiv:math/0412065.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0412065

Yang treats the Hessian collineation group of order 216 and its finite unitary reflection-group lifts. This is relevant historical context for the order-216 affine-orientation group appearing in Pass5686.
