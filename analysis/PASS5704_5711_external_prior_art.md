## L-infinity / strongly homotopy Lie identities

Tom Lada and Martin Markl, **“Strongly homotopy Lie algebras”**, arXiv:hep-th/9406095, gives the standard strongly-homotopy Lie framework and higher Jacobi identities. In the ordinary uncurved convention, the arity-three identity contains the unary-bracket terms involving `l1(l3)` and `l3(l1,...)` together with the `l2∘l2` Jacobiator. Therefore setting `l1=0` removes the `l3` terms from that identity and forces `l2` to satisfy strict Jacobi. Pass5707 applies that standard identity to the repo's own declaration `l1=0`; the correction is the repo-specific consequence, not a new definition of L-infinity algebras.
