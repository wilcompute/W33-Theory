# Witting defect controls and critical Gauss ring

Baseline: ba6ab74. For the rank-one chart defect, the chart cyclic space consists of the three nonzero projections of the marked vertex onto the adjacency eigenspaces. In their normalized basis the free chart energies are (0,9 Delta,15 Delta), and the defect is vv^T with v=(1,sqrt(20),sqrt(24))/sqrt(45). Tensoring with the 16 physical gauge states gives an exact 48-dimensional active block. The remaining blocks are 19 copies of H_g+9 Delta I and 23 copies of H_g+15 Delta I. Independently reconstructed ground energies at coupling 0,0.3,1,2 were -4.6068216091,-4.6097679266,-4.8530247457,-5.5230234442. At positive infinite coupling the chart factor localizes on the marked vertex and the sector weights tend to (1,20,24)/45.

All 78 strongly regular graphs with parameters (45,12,3,3) have the same one-mark block (Coolsaet, Degraer and Spence, EJC 13 R32). For several diagonal marks, the spectral projector Gram matrices on the marked set depend only on its induced adjacency. A graph-level control beyond pair types: test whether every edge lies in a 5-clique. The three common neighbors must form a triangle, so such a clique is unique for each edge. If the test passes, each point is on three lines, and an external point can touch at most one vertex of a line, by uniqueness of the edge clique (any adjacent pair on that line already has all three common neighbors on the line). The five line vertices each have eight external neighbors; the 40 outside vertices therefore each touch exactly one line vertex. This recovers the generalized quadrangle axioms of order (4,2). This is a proof of a recognition criterion, not an executed catalogue census.

For a ring, solve Gauss law in the link-X basis: matter Z at v equals the product of the adjacent link signs, while dressed hopping flips a single link sign. At electric and Wilson couplings zero, and equal unit mass and hopping, the reduced Hamiltonian is the critical periodic transverse-field Ising chain. Sparse diagonalization gave gaps 0.397824734759, 0.263304995175, 0.196982806714, 0.157403413649, 0.131086925630 for lengths 4,6,8,10,12. They match 2 tan(pi/(4L)); L times the gap tends to pi/2. This is a bare 1D control, not evidence of chart-induced criticality, a continuum gauge theory in higher dimensions, or gravity.
