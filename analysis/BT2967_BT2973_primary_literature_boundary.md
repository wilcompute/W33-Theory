# Passes 2967–2973 primary-literature boundary

## Optical calibration

- M. Malik, M. Mirhosseini, Z. Shi, and R. W. Boyd, “Towards Quantum Communication with more than 4 bits/photon: Near-Perfect Sorting of the Orbital Angular Momentum Modes of Light,” QIM 2014, DOI 10.1364/QIM.2014.QW3A.6. Reports an OAM separation efficiency above 92%; it does not calibrate the proposed W33 device.
- G. F. Walsh et al., “Parallel sorting of orbital and spin angular momenta of light in a record large number of channels,” *Optics Letters* 43, 2256–2259 (2018), DOI 10.1364/OL.43.002256. Characterizes operational range and neighboring-channel crosstalk; it motivates keeping a full crosstalk matrix.
- L. M. Serino et al., “Programmable time-frequency mode-sorting of single photons with a multi-output quantum pulse gate,” *Optics Express* 33, 5577 (2025), DOI 10.1364/OE.544206. Detector tomography reaches fidelity up to 0.958±0.030 in 3 and 5 dimensions; it does not provide the four-slot Holonet transfer matrix.

The packet therefore freezes a calibration-ready inference engine with synthetic component-resolved counts. No experimental numbers from these different platforms are multiplied into a claimed device efficiency.

## Reversible and fault-tolerant compilation

- C. Jones, “Novel constructions for the fault-tolerant Toffoli gate,” arXiv:1212.5069. Provides a four-T measurement-assisted Toffoli resource point.
- P. Selinger, “Quantum circuits of T-depth one,” arXiv:1210.0974. Gives a T-depth-one Toffoli with four ancillas.
- D. Maslov, “On the advantages of using relative phase Toffolis with an application to multiple control Toffoli optimization,” arXiv:1508.03273. Establishes circuit identities permitting lower-cost relative-phase substitutions in suitable compute/uncompute structures.

These papers support the resource-model rows in Pass 2969. They do not select a winner without a physical code, measurement latency, factory cost, and hardware connectivity model.
