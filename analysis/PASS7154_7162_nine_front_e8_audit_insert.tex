\subsection{The Gram big cell, the hexad lifting invariant, and an audited $E_8$ boundary}
\label{sec:pass7154-7162-nine-front-e8}

The normalized rank-four Gram state space from Pass~7139 has a simple matrix stratification.  Writing
\[
 (1,a,b,c)\longleftrightarrow
 \begin{pmatrix}1&a\\ b&c\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad a,b,c\in\mathbb F_9^\times,
\]
there are exactly
\[
 \boxed{512=448+64}
\]
states, with $64$ rank-one boundary matrices ($c=ab$) and $448$ invertible matrices.  The invertible states are precisely the all-entry-nonzero big cell of $\mathrm{PGL}_2(9)$ after normalizing the upper-left entry to one.  The involution $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ acts freely on this big cell, yielding $224$ of the $360$ nodes of $\mathrm{PGL}_2(9)/\langle D\rangle$; the remaining $136$ quotient nodes lie outside the big cell.  Thus the Gram and involution-quotient models have an exact overlap but are not the same finite object.

The code/geometry symmetry gap from Pass~7152 also admits a concrete resolution.  The two complementary triple columns split the six generators into two $3$-sets, and the nine cross-pair column types form $K_{3,3}$.  Hence the abstract outer group is
\[
 \operatorname{Aut}(K_{3,3})\cong S_3\wr C_2,
 \qquad |S_3\wr C_2|=72.
\]
The actual geometric product $AF$ acts on the six witnesses as the cycle
\[
 0\to1\to5\to4\to2\to3\to0.
\]
Its edges are a Hamiltonian $C_6\subset K_{3,3}$; the three omitted cross edges form a perfect matching.  Since $K_{3,3}$ has six perfect matchings, the stabilizer of one complementary Hamiltonian $C_6$ has order $72/6=12$ and is $D_{12}$.  Consequently a code-outer symmetry lifts to the ambient hexad geometry exactly when it preserves this selected $C_6$ (equivalently, the omitted perfect matching).

The two-channel involution theorem extends to all symplectic ranks.  For odd $q$, let
\[
 V=U\oplus U^*,\qquad
 B((u,w),(u',w'))=u(w')-u'(w),\qquad
 D(u,w)=(u,-w),
\]
with $\dim U=n$.  Then the fixed projective locus has size
\[
 2\frac{q^n-1}{q-1},
\]
the number of nonisotropic transversals is
\[
 q^{n-1}\frac{q^n-1}{q-1},
\]
and the number of eligible $D$-pair nodes is
\[
 \boxed{\frac{q^{n-1}(q^n-1)}2}.
\]
For two eligible orbits represented by $X,Y$, a conflict occurs exactly when
\[
 B(X,Y)=0\quad\text{or}\quad B(X,DY)=0.
\]
The extra $\mathrm{PGL}_2(q)$ Schreier description of Pass~7147 is special to $n=2$ and is not asserted in higher rank.

The $q=9$ witness orbit itself is the homogeneous bundle
\[
 \mathrm{P}\Gamma\mathrm{Sp}(4,9)/C_2
 \longrightarrow
 \mathrm{P}\Gamma\mathrm{Sp}(4,9)/D_{12},
\]
whose fibers are the six vertices of the natural $D_{12}$ hexagon.  The base has $573{,}868{,}800$ hexads and the total space $3{,}443{,}212{,}800$ witnesses, with the order-six $AF$ element transporting the $C_6$ structure equivariantly from fiber to fiber.

There is also a new binary-projective $40$-point object.  The $248$ code coordinates collapse to $23$ distinct nonzero columns of $\mathbb F_2^6$: all six weight-one words, all fifteen weight-two words, and two complementary weight-three words.  If the complementary triples define $\mathbb F_2^6=A\oplus B$ with $\dim A=\dim B=3$, then these $23$ points are
\[
 (A\setminus\{0\})\ \cup\ (B\setminus\{0\})\ \cup\
 \{e+f:e\in E_A,\ f\in E_B\},
\]
where $E_A,E_B$ are the chosen three-point bases.  Thus
\[
 \boxed{23=7+7+9},\qquad
 \boxed{63-23=40=(7\times7)-(3\times3)}.
\]
The full $\mathbb F_2$-linear setwise stabilizer of this $23/40$ split is exactly
\[
 \boxed{(S_3\times S_3)\rtimes C_2=S_3\wr C_2},\qquad |G|=72.
\]
Indeed, $A\setminus\{0\}$ and $B\setminus\{0\}$ are the only projective planes wholly contained in the $23$-set.  If a mixed three-space $H$ existed, every mixed nonzero vector would have to be a grid vector $(e_i,f_j)$.  Any two such vectors differing in both row and column sum to a forbidden mixed vector, so all mixed vectors of $H$ lie in one grid row or one grid column; but three in one row or column also have a forbidden sum.  Hence $H$ has at most two mixed nonzero vectors.  On the other hand, if $r_A=\dim(H\cap A)$ and $r_B=\dim(H\cap B)$, then $r_A+r_B\le3$ and a genuinely mixed $H$ has at most four pure nonzero vectors, so its seven nonzero vectors require at least three mixed ones, a contradiction.  Therefore a linear stabilizer must preserve or swap the two Fano planes.  The cross grid then singles out the three basis points in each plane, leaving exactly $S_3\wr C_2$.

On the $40$-point complement this full linear stabilizer has five orbits
\[
 \boxed{1+6+6+9+18}.
\]
It is therefore not transitive and cannot be permutation-isomorphic to the certified Pass~1021 $W(3,3)$ point action, which is transitive of rank three with subdegrees $1,12,27$.  Hence the attractive equality $40=(7\times7)-(3\times3)$ does \emph{not} identify the new binary complement with the $40$-point base of the $E_8$ root fibration through the natural projective-linear route.

The most direct coding-theoretic $E_8$ readings of the new length $248$ code are likewise obstructed.  First, the $23$ distinct columns contain no affine $3$-flat of eight points.  Indeed, if $v+H$ were such a flat, summing Hamming weights over the coset gives $4s+8f$, where $s$ is the support size of the $3$-space $H$ and $f$ counts fixed-one coordinates outside that support.  The available column types force $f=0$ and $s=4$.  The four possible hyperplane-coset weight distributions in $\mathbb F_2^4$, according to normal weight $1,2,3,4$, are
\[
 (1,3,3,1),\quad(2,4,2,0),\quad(3,3,1,1),\quad(4,0,4,0)
\]
in weights $1,2,3,4$.  Each contains either a weight-four word or at least two weight-three words, whereas the $23$-set contains no weight-four word and at most one of its complementary weight-three words on any four-coordinate support.  Hence no such affine flat exists, so the natural eight-column $[8,4,4]$ puncture route is absent.

Second, for the full binary code $C=[248,6,51]_2$ one has
\[
 GG^{\mathsf T}=I_6\pmod2.
\]
Thus $C$ is not self-orthogonal, and its weight-$51$ generators also show that it is not doubly even.  The full code therefore does not realize the standard self-dual doubly-even binary Construction-A route to the $E_8$ lattice.

Finally, although the numerical Lie-algebra identity is
\[
 \dim\mathfrak e_8=248=240+8,
\]
with $240$ root spaces and an $8$-dimensional Cartan subalgebra, the full coordinate-permutation automorphism group of the code has coordinate orbits
\[
 \boxed{192,36,18,2}.
\]
No union of these orbits has size $8$ or $240$.  Hence the code carries no full-symmetry-invariant coordinate split that can canonically be interpreted as ``$240$ roots plus $8$ Cartan directions.''  The same subset-sum obstruction rules out an invariant coordinate realization of the standard dimension pattern $248=78+8+81+81$.

These negative statements complement, rather than contradict, the earlier certified $E_8$ result in the repository: Pass~1021 proves a canonical six-to-one Eisenstein fibration of the $240$ $E_8$ roots onto the $40$ \emph{points} of $W(3,3)$.  What the present audit shows is that the new $248$-code does not recover that $E_8/W33$ bridge by any of the three most direct routes tested here: a canonical $240+8$ coordinate split, a standard binary Construction-A realization, or the natural projective-linear identification of its $40$-point complement.

The eight target-$48$ Gram clique decisions are run by a separate exact parallel SAT certificate.  No value $\alpha(W(3,9))=51$ is inferred here unless that independent certificate records UNSAT in all eight cases.
