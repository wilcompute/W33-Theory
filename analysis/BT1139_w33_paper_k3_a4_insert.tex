\begin{proposition}[Normalized Ricci-flat $K3$ $A_4$ closure]
\label{prop:k3-a4-normalized-closure}
In the corpus-normalized Ricci-flat $K3$ lane, take
\[
  A_0=1,\qquad A_2=0,\qquad
  A_4^{\rm norm}=\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\int_{K3}|{\rm Rm}|^2\,d{\rm vol}.
\]
For a Ricci-flat $K3$ metric, Chern--Gauss--Bonnet gives
\[
  \frac{1}{8\pi^2}\int_{K3}|{\rm Rm}|^2\,d{\rm vol}=\chi(K3)=24,
\]
so
\[
  A_4^{\rm norm}=24.
\]
Substituting into the product coefficient of
Proposition~\ref{prop:ricci-flat-product-heat-slot} gives
\[
  C_4^{\rm norm}=440\cdot24+8160=18720.
\]
This integer is not isolated: it factorizes completely through the $W(3,3)$
packet as
\[
  \boxed{
  C_4^{\rm norm}=18720=E\,q!\,\Phi_3=240\cdot6\cdot13.
  }
\]
Equivalently,
\[
  \frac{C_4^{\rm norm}}{E}=q!\Phi_3=78.
\]
Thus the first normalized metric-dependent target in the $K3$ continuum lane is
again a pure substrate integer: the edge shell $E=240$ multiplied by the master
permutation count $q!=6$ and the fermion-mixing cyclotomic scale
$\Phi_3=13$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For Ricci-flat $K3$, $R=0$ and ${\rm Ric}=0$.  The four-dimensional
Chern--Gauss--Bonnet identity reduces to
\[
  \chi(K3)=\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\int_{K3}|{\rm Rm}|^2\,d{\rm vol}.
\]
Since $\chi(K3)=24$, the normalized curvature slot is $A_4^{\rm norm}=24$.
The Ricci-flat specialization of Proposition~\ref{prop:ricci-flat-product-heat-slot}
is
\[
  C_4=440A_4+8160A_0.
\]
With $A_0=1$ and $A_4=A_4^{\rm norm}=24$ this gives
\[
  C_4^{\rm norm}=440\cdot24+8160=10560+8160=18720.
\]
Finally $E=240$, $q!=6$, and $\Phi_3=q^2+q+1=13$, hence
\[
  E q!\Phi_3=240\cdot6\cdot13=18720.
\]
\end{proof}
