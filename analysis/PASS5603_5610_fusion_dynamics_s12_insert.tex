\subsection{Projectivity fusion, exact distance, and the phase-sensitive dynamical frontier}

\paragraph{Pass 5603: the fixed-point fusion is an all-odd Bose--Mesner algebra.}
For odd prime powers $q>3$, fuse pairs $g,h\in\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)$ according to the
number $0,1,2$ of fixed points of $g^{-1}h$ on $\mathbf P^1(q)$.  With
\[
k_0=\frac{q(q-1)^2}{4},\qquad k_1=q^2-1,\qquad
k_2=\frac{q(q+1)(q-3)}4,
\]
the first eigenmatrix and multiplicities are
\[
\boxed{P=\begin{pmatrix}
1&k_0&k_1&k_2\\
1&0&q-1&-q\\
1&-(q-1)^2/4&0&(q-3)(q+1)/4\\
1&q&-(q+1)&0
\end{pmatrix}},
\]
\[
\boxed{m=\left(1,\frac{(q-3)(q+1)^2}{4},q^2,\frac{(q-1)^3}{4}\right).}
\]
The symbolic certificate verifies
$P^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{diag}(m)P=|\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)|\operatorname{diag}(1,k_0,k_1,k_2)$
and reconstructs all Pass~5599 intersection polynomials from the standard
character formula for $p_{ij}^k$.  At $q=3$ both
\[
\boxed{k_2=0,\qquad m_1=0,}
\]
so the exceptional Reye/Latin member is an actual degeneration of the generic
Bose--Mesner algebra: one relation and one primitive idempotent disappear.
The character-table background is the standard projective-line treatment in
Long--Plaza--Sin--Xiang, arXiv:1608.07304.

\paragraph{Pass 5604: exact all-odd minimum distance.}
Let $C_+$ and $C_-$ be the square- and nonsquare-determinant binary
projectivity spans from Pass~5601, so $C_-=C_+^\perp$.  Through a fixed grid
cell pass $r=q(q-1)/2$ opposite-coset projectivity graphs, while a compatible
pair lies on $\lambda=(q-1)/2$ of them.  If a nonzero support $S$ of size
$s\le q$ contained a cell $c$, then at least
\[
r-(s-1)\lambda=\frac{q-1}{2}(q-s+1)>0
\]
dual generators would meet $S$ exactly once, contradicting orthogonality.
A generator has weight $q+1$, hence
\[
\boxed{d(C_+)=d(C_-)=q+1}
\]
for every odd prime power $q$, with parameters
\[
\boxed{[(q+1)^2,(q+1)^2/2,q+1]_2.}
\]
Independent exact MILPs give $d=8$ at $q=7$ and $d=10$ at $q=9$.

\paragraph{Pass 5605: maximally symmetric Hodge and Hashimoto scaling is atomic.}
The all-$q$ Levi flag Hodge spectrum is
\[
0^{q^4},\quad(q+1-\sqrt{2q})^{q(q+1)^2/2},\quad
(q+1)^{q(q^2+1)},\quad(q+1+\sqrt{2q})^{q(q+1)^2/2},\quad(2q+2)^1.
\]
The full normalized spectral measure tends $\delta_0$; after deleting the
cycle kernel and scaling by $q+1$, the nonzero measure tends $\delta_1$.
Likewise, for every $k$-regular graph the nonbacktracking matrix is locally
permutation-equivalent to $J_k-I_k$, so its singular values are only
$k-1$ and $1$.  The $W(3,q)$ Ihara--Bass roots also have a finite atomic
normalized limit.  Thus these maximally symmetric families do not supply a
Weyl-law continuum spectrum without further phase, refinement, defects, or
symmetry breaking.

\paragraph{Pass 5607: full projective symmetry forbids a dispersive Segre wave operator.}
On $X=\mathbf P^1(q)\times\mathbf P^1(q)$, 2-transitivity gives
$\operatorname{End}_{\mathrm{PSL}_2(q)}\mathbb R[\mathbf P^1(q)]

For $n=q+1$ the canonical indefinite ruling operator
\[
\boxed{\square_q=(nI-J)\otimes I-I\otimes(nI-J)}
\]

\[
\boxed{0^{q^2+1}\oplus(+n)^q\oplus(-n)^q.}
\]
Thus exact independent projective symmetry is too restrictive for a dispersive
continuum d'Alembertian; additional phase/holonomy, directed transport, chart
order, or symmetry breaking is mathematically necessary.

\paragraph{Physical boundary.}
The packet closes finite-group, coding, and spectral statements and identifies
a concrete phase mechanism that defeats the simplest spectral-flatness no-go.

heat-kernel/dispersion limit and whether the required phase embedding can be
selected rather than chosen.
