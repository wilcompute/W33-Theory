\section{The two $120$-object carriers are not the same \texorpdfstring{$PGSp$}{PGSp} set}
\label{sec:pass4877-maxcut-steiner}

Pass~4867 found exactly $120$ maximum cuts of weight $216$ in the double-six
graph, while the Steiner layer contains exactly $120$ maximal/Steiner
triangles.  Pass~4877 tests the equality rather than promoting it.

A marked-double-six $K_6$ chart constructs a maximum cut with a Hamilton
$C_6$ duad state and exactly two triad completions.  Its full $PGSp(4,3)$
orbit has size $120$.  Thus both the maximum-cut set and the Steiner-triangle
set are transitive $PGSp$ sets with stabilizer order
\[
  51840/120=432.
\]
The stabilizers are nevertheless nonconjugate.  The maximum-cut stabilizer
fixes no Steiner triangle and has element-order census
\[
  1^1\,2^{45}\,3^{26}\,4^{54}\,6^{90}\,8^{108}\,12^{108},
\]
whereas a Steiner-triangle stabilizer fixes exactly its defining Steiner
triangle and has census
\[
  1^1\,2^{87}\,3^{26}\,4^{72}\,6^{210}\,12^{36}.
\]
In particular, the first stabilizer has $108$ elements of order $8$ and the
second has none.  Hence
\[
  \boxed{\{
  \text{maximum cuts}\}\not\cong_{PGSp}
  \{\text{Steiner triangles}\}.}
\]
There is no $PGSp$-equivariant bijection between the two $120$-sets.  Their
equal cardinalities and equal stabilizer orders are not an identification.
