\subsection{Pass 10955: the clock determinant exchanges the two $D_4$ half-spin sheets}

The tetracode clock group $GL(2,3)$ has a faithful four-dimensional signed
permutation representation obtained directly from its frozen monomial action.
All $48^2$ products are checked, and every image preserves the standard
$D_4$ root shell $\{\pm e_i\pm e_j\}$.

The sixteen orientation signs split as the two eight-weight half-spin
demicubes $S_+$ and $S_-$, according to even or odd sign parity.  Every
determinant-$+1$ element preserves the vector, $S_+$ and $S_-$ weight sets
separately; every determinant-$-1$ element fixes the vector set and exchanges
$S_+\leftrightarrow S_-$.  Thus the outer image is the single transposition
inside $\mathrm{Out}(D_4)\cong S_3$, not a triality three-cycle.

For the exact clock generator, one tick exchanges the two half-spin sheets,
two ticks preserve them, four ticks equal $-I_4$, and eight ticks return to
the identity; on $S_+\cup S_-$ there are two eight-cycles.  Consequently the
extended-Clifford/complete-positivity grading, the Pass~10954 Fano orientation
grading, and the $D_4$ half-spin-sheet grading are the same determinant
character on the shared $GL(2,3)$.  This is a finite root/weight theorem, not
an identification with observed fermion chirality or continuum spin geometry.
