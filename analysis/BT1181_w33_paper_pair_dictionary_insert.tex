\begin{theorem}[Intrinsic pair dictionary]
\label{thm:intrinsic-pair-dictionary}
The earlier labeled dictionary can be replaced by a genus-two construction.  A
natural six-set is supplied by the six odd quadratic refinements on
$\mathbb F_2^4$.  Its unordered pairs give $15$ objects, matching the $15$
nonzero masks.  Thus the mask-pair dictionary is intrinsic up to relabeling of
that six-set.
\end{theorem}
