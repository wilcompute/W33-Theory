# BT1208 -- R3 Architecture Dependency Graph

## Why this exists

BT1202 reframed R3 as a finite-to-continuum convergence residual. BT1208 makes the logical dependency explicit: the metric/K3 spacetime continuum branch is downstream of the finite W33 seed, but it is not upstream of the holonet computer branch.

## Dependency graph

The graph has one finite seed and two downstream branches:

\[
W(3,3)_{\rm finite}
\longrightarrow
\text{symplectic/CV computer},
\]

and

\[
W(3,3)_{\rm finite}
\longrightarrow
\text{metric/K3 spacetime candidate}.
\]

The symplectic branch feeds the single-photon demonstrator and the GKP/Steinberg fault-tolerant machine. The metric branch feeds the R3 checklist and the spacetime-continuum claim.

## Verified cut statement

Removing the metric/R3 branch removes the spacetime-continuum claim but leaves both computer targets reachable:

\[
\text{finite seed}
\to
\text{symplectic/CV computer}
\to
\text{single-photon demonstrator},
\]

and

\[
\text{finite seed}
\to
\text{symplectic/CV computer}
\to
\text{GKP/Steinberg fault-tolerant stack}.
\]

So failure of R3 would be serious for the spacetime interpretation, but it would not erase the finite holonet architecture.

## New connection

This makes the page-36 two-continuum split operational. The holonet is the symplectic/metaplectic/CV continuum of the substrate. The spacetime story is the metric/K3/spectral-action continuum of the same seed. They are coupled by the finite source, but neither should be used to smuggle proof of the other.

## Boundary

The dependency graph is not a proof that the metric branch converges. It is a proof of architectural isolation: R3 is an external convergence residual, not a hidden prerequisite of the computer architecture.
