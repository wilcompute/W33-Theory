# Zero-energy Weyl profiles for the W33 winding charge slots

The grand-spin-zero radial shooting now covers the four charge slots entering the semileptonic winding vertex. It uses the native holomorphic and gauge Yukawa blocks on the real, 160-element background at phase zero. For each slot the embedded root generates the partner magnetic weight. The exterior mass kernel is split by the radial flux operator. Incoming amplitudes are normalized to the identity in that kernel. The outer radius is 5, followed by the constant vacuum exterior used in the prior triplet calculation. The origin regular subspace and the exterior decaying and light subspaces are propagated toward the middle using QR factorization. On the exterior side the basis is ordered as decaying, outgoing, incoming. The triangular factors preserve the quotient by the first two blocks, so their lower-right blocks transport the prescribed incoming amplitudes to the middle. After matching, triangular back-substitution reconstructs the states on both radial meshes. The replay checks outgoing flux normalization and the zero net standing flux at every node, including off-diagonal matrix elements between incident channels.

The norm reported for each sampled radius is the Frobenius norm of the matrix of reduced radial spinors, summed over all unit-flux incident states in the charge sector. It includes massive evanescent components. It is invariant under unitary changes of the exterior incident basis. The largest reflection unitarity error is below 3.3×10⁻¹². A separate massless control with constant K = 1 reproduces the regular analytic reduced norm 2r/R for the two incident modes.
