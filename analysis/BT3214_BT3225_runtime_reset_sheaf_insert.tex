\section{Runtime closure, epistemic reset, and the finite-port cohomology}
\label{sec:pass3214-runtime-reset-sheaf}

The larger instruction-set programme now has a proof-carrying exhaustive surface.  A canonical
plan contains all $194$ universal five- and six-opcode subsets of the frozen ten-generator
affine library.  Each of thirty-two shards is bound to the plan digest and must compute a directed
breadth-first search over all $4{,}199{,}040$ elements of $\mathrm{ASp}(4,3)$.  The aggregate
refuses missing or duplicate shards, stale plan identities, incomplete growth series, or any
record whose reached order is not exactly $4{,}199{,}040$.  Consequently frame connectivity and
frame information can no longer be promoted to a global runtime optimum.  At this source stage
the exhaustive aggregate remains pending.

The curvature-aware controller is compiled independently into its canonical Moore quotient.
Reconstruction of the $48{,}826$ frozen hypotheses, $46{,}284$ base signatures and $1{,}436$
collision classes gives $770$ initial classes and $876$ recursive states.  Equality of the
complete output/action/child signature is the bisimulation certificate.  The resulting ROM has a
ten-bit address and a $102$-bit word containing eight child addresses, an outcome mask, the three
curvature counts, the selected triangle action and a terminal flag.  Source generation does not
constitute an FPGA placement or timing result.

The merged finite-port factorisation also defines a cellular complex.  Its forty-five block
vertices, seven hundred twenty pair edges and two hundred forty support-port triangles satisfy
\[
 C_2\xrightarrow{\partial_2}C_1\xrightarrow{\partial_1}C_0,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}\partial_1=44,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}\partial_2=240.
\]
The face boundaries have disjoint edge supports, so over $\mathbb F_2$, $\mathbb F_3$ and
$\mathbb F_5$ the Betti numbers are
\[
 (b_0,b_1,b_2)=(1,436,0).
\]
A phase-decorated edge assignment $a$ is flat only when
$\partial_2^{\mathsf T}a=0$.  Nonzero port flux therefore obstructs a flat lift, but zero flux
leaves $p^{436}$ gauge-inequivalent constant-coefficient lifts over $\mathbb F_p$.  This is a
large moduli space, not a canonical replacement for the falsified direct forty-five-object
identification.  It is also not advertised as a complete contextuality invariant.

Taking $H_X=\partial_1$ and $H_Z=\partial_2^{\mathsf T}$ gives the exact binary CSS complex
\[
 [[720,436,2]],\qquad d_X=2,\qquad d_Z=3.
\]
The degree-thirty-two block graph contains $5{,}280$ triangles, of which only $240$ are filled
port faces; the other $5{,}040$ provide weight-three non-boundary cycles.  Two edges of one
filled face give a weight-two dual logical.  The construction is therefore a high-rate
provenance/checksum fabric, not a fault-tolerant quantum memory.

Finally, epoch synchronization and epistemic reset are distinct operations.  Crossing the
$876$ belief states with twelve phase states gives $10{,}512$ product states.  Every phase-only
letter acts identically on the belief coordinate, so every phase-only word has image rank at least
$876$.  The optimal marker attains that rank by collapsing the phase factor, but it cannot reset
belief.  A rank-one reset removes the full state capacity
\[
 \log_2 876=9.7747870596\ \text{bits}.
\]
For a uniform or maximally mixed $876$-state ensemble at $300\,\mathrm K$, the corresponding
Landauer floor is $2.8063207254\times10^{-20}\,\mathrm J$
($0.1751567627\,\mathrm{eV}$).  The actual thermodynamic cost depends on the input distribution;
this value is not a distribution-independent reset charge or an engineering energy estimate.  The
hardware contract therefore permits a marker to lock phase while requiring separate authorization
and a matching proof root before a belief-reset pulse.

Runtime and rank-three M36 shards are committed through domain-separated SHA-256 Merkle leaves
that bind plan, engine, source, result and candidate identities.  This supplies integrity,
provenance, deterministic inclusion challenges and actionable counterexamples; it does not prove
honest computation or replace independent certification.  Likewise, no alternative ISA is
promoted over the current four-opcode fallback without observed placement and calibration evidence
for every compared mode.
