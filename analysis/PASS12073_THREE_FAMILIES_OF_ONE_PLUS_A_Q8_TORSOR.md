# Pass 12073: three families of 1 + a Q₈-torsor

Claude track. Builds on 12064, 12066 and 12067.

## Results

For two non-collinear clocks, with K = C₂ × Q₈:

1. **Three families.** Each singlet f_i sits at inner product −2/3 from every weight of exactly one octet. This gives
   **27 = F₀ + F₁ + F₂, with F_i = 1 + 8**, and the triality ticks permute the three families cyclically.
2. **Q₈-torsors.** K acts on each octet through Q₈ (element orders 1:1, 2:1, 4:6), regularly.
3. **Yukawa monomials by family.**
   - 45 = 12 family-diagonal monomials (4 per family, of the form f_i·x·y with x, y in 8_i) + 33 that take one weight
     from each family.
   - The diagonal monomials pair each octet perfectly, by the orbits of the Q₈ centre.
   - The cross monomials are f₀f₁f₂ (invariant) plus 4 orbits of 8 of type 8₀8₁8₂.

## Reading

A vacuum value along the second clock's monomial gives family-diagonal mass terms, one parameter ⟨f_i⟩ per family.
K allows the three values to differ; triality relates them. No hierarchy is predicted.

## Scope

Weyl level only. Reading the octets as physical families is an interpretation.
