\subsection*{The regular $E_6$ $H_{27}$ does not central-product with the physical external $H_{27}$}

The tempting arithmetic
\[
27\cdot27/3=243=3^{1+4}
\]
would require the central $C_3$ of the Pass~369/371 Heisenberg group on the
$E_6$ minuscule $27$ to equal $Z(E_6)$.  It does not.

The Pass~369 group acts regularly on the $27$ minuscule weights.  Hence every
nonidentity element, including its central generator, acts fixed-point-freely
and has nontrivial Weyl projection.  By contrast a central element of $E_6$
has trivial Weyl projection and acts by a scalar on the irreducible $27$.
Therefore
\[
Z(H_{27}^{\rm Pass369})\neq Z(E_6).
\]

Consequently this particular internal $H_{27}$ cannot be glued through the
diagonal $Z_3$ kernel of $(E_6\times SU(3))/Z_3$ to the center of the physical
external-$A_2$ Heisenberg group.  Conditional on an honest order-$27$ lift of
the Pass~369 Weyl subgroup, the intersection with $Z(E_6)$ is trivial, so the
two commuting factors generate
\[
\boxed{H_{27}\times H_{27}}
\]
of order $729$, not an extraspecial group of order $243$.

\paragraph{Remaining opening.}
This excludes the already-certified regular Pass~369 torsor from the
center-gluing construction.  It does not exclude a different $H_{27}\subset
E_6$ whose center is literally $Z(E_6)$.
