\subsection{The odd-$q$ footprint matrix is a binary two-periodic complex}
\label{sec:pass5350-footprint-homology}
The integral Gram identity of Pass~5267 reduces for odd $q$ to
\[
 \boxed{FF^{\mathsf T}=J\pmod2}.
\]
Hence $\langle F^{\mathsf T}x,F^{\mathsf T}y\rangle$ is simply the product of
the parities of $x$ and $y$; in particular $\ker F^{\mathsf T}$ is contained in
the even point module and the induced bilinear form on the footprint image has
rank one.

There is a complementary identity.  Two carriers are
$C_H=\PG(H)\cup\PG(H^\perp)$ and $C_K=\PG(K)\cup\PG(K^\perp)$.  Orthogonal
complementation pairs $H\cap K$ with $H^\perp\cap K^\perp$ and pairs
$H\cap K^\perp$ with $H^\perp\cap K$; their projective point counts therefore
occur twice.  Thus every two carrier columns have even intersection and
\[
 \boxed{F^{\mathsf T}F=0\pmod2}.
\]
Writing $V_0$ for the even point module and $U$ for the carrier module gives the
binary two-periodic complex
\[
 U\xrightarrow{F}V_0\xrightarrow{F^{\mathsf T}}U.
\]
Pass~5376 closes $\operatorname{im}F=C_W^\perp$, $\ker F^{\mathsf T}=C_W$, and
$\operatorname{rank}F=g=q(q^2+1)/2$.  Since the restriction
$F^{\mathsf T}|_{V_0}$ has rank $g-1$, the point-side homology is canonically
\[
 \boxed{H_V=C_W/C_W^\perp,\qquad \dim H_V=q^2+1.}
\]
This is exactly the logical quotient of the binary CSS family in Pass~5379, so
its logical dimension has a canonical chain-homology realization rather than
being only a rank subtraction.
