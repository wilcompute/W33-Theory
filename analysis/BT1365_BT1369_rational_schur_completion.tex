\section{Rational Wedderburn closure and geometric Schur completion}
\label{sec:bt1365-bt1369-rational-schur}

\paragraph{Exact scope.}
Let $X$ be the $120$-element selector set consisting of a totally isotropic
line of $W(3,3)$ together with one of its three perfect matchings.  Fix a
selector $x$, let $H=G_x$ be its order-$432$ stabilizer in
$G=\operatorname{PGSp}(4,3)\cong W(E_6)$, and let $T=T(x)$ be the
$79$-dimensional Terwilliger algebra from Section~\ref{sec:bt1360-bt1364-gelfand-terwilliger}.

\begin{theorem}[Rational selector Schur-completion theorem]
The following statements hold over $\mathbb Q$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The Terwilliger algebra is split semisimple, with
\[
T\cong
\mathbb Q^3\oplus M_2(\mathbb Q)^2\oplus M_3(\mathbb Q)^3
\oplus M_4(\mathbb Q)\oplus M_5(\mathbb Q).
\]
Its simple sizes are
\[
1,1,1,2,2,3,3,3,4,5,
\]
and the corresponding multiplicities in $\mathbb QX$ are
\[
3,12,14,1,2,4,4,8,8,1.
\]

\item There are two symmetric stabilizer-orbital matrices $S_2,S_4$,
supported respectively in shell blocks $(2,2)$ and $(4,4)$, with the
following intrinsic definitions.  For $S_2$, the two carrier lines are
disjoint, their intersections with the base line form a base-matching edge,
and transversal transport aligns their matchings.  For $S_4$, the carrier
lines meet at $q$, and the partners of $q$, pulled back through the two
base-line transversals, coincide.  Their ordered-pair supports have sizes
$108$ and $432$.

Writing $S=S_2+S_4$,
\[
\dim\langle T,S_2\rangle=81,
\qquad
\dim\langle T,S_4\rangle=81,
\qquad
\dim\langle T,S\rangle=83.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\langle T,S\rangle=\operatorname{End}_H(\mathbb QX).
\]

\item The full stabilizer orbital algebra is split semisimple, with
\[
\operatorname{End}_H(\mathbb QX)
\cong
\mathbb Q^7\oplus M_2(\mathbb Q)^2\oplus M_3(\mathbb Q)^3
\oplus M_4(\mathbb Q)\oplus M_5(\mathbb Q).
\]
The seven non-scalar Terwilliger blocks remain unchanged.  The three scalar
Terwilliger isotypic sectors split as
\[
3=1+2,
\qquad
12=4+8,
\qquad
14=2+4+8,
\]
with splitter eigenvalues
\[
3,-3;
\qquad
-3,0;
\qquad
-4,2,-1,
\]
respectively.  Hence
\[
83-79=14-10=4:
\]
the entire Schur defect is an exact center-refinement defect.

\item The $83$ orbitals form a five-fiber coherent configuration with fiber
sizes $1,2,36,27,54$.  It has $29$ symmetric orbitals, $27$ transpose
pairs, and $4277$ nonzero intersection constants.  Its ordinary adjacency
algebra is generated by $A,D,S$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The exact orbital intersection tensor converts multiplication of
stabilizer-invariant matrices into rational arithmetic on $83$ coordinates.
The $10$-dimensional center of $T$ contains a generic element with ten
distinct rational eigenvalues, producing a complete rational central
idempotent system.  The component dimensions are
\[
1,1,1,4,4,9,9,9,16,25.
\]
For every central component $z$, a shell idempotent $E_s^*$ gives an
idempotent $zE_s^*$ whose rank equals the asserted split multiplicity.  A
non-split central division factor of degree $d>1$ would force every nonzero
idempotent rank to be divisible by an additional factor $d$; thus every
component has division degree one.

The two stated geometric predicates each select one self-transpose
$H$-orbital.  Exact closure modulo two good primes gives dimensions
$81,81,83$; since all matrices lie in the known $83$-dimensional rational
orbital algebra, the last lower bound is an equality in characteristic
zero.  Every rational central idempotent of $T$ commutes with $S$.  On the
three scalar sectors the exact annihilating polynomials are
\[
(B_3-3)(B_3+3)=0,
\qquad
B_{12}(B_{12}+3)=0,
\]
\[
(B_{14}+4)(B_{14}-2)(B_{14}+1)=0.
\]
Lagrange interpolation yields seven rational orthogonal idempotents of
ranks $1,2,4,8,2,4,8$.  Together with the seven unchanged non-scalar
central idempotents they form the complete $14$-element rational central
system of the orbital algebra.  The coherent-configuration assertions are
then verified directly from the $83$ stabilizer orbitals and their exact
intersection tensor.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This theorem is an exact finite rational matrix-algebra statement.  It does
not select a preferred perfect matching in a fiber, construct an
$H_4$/600-cell adjacency, establish literature priority, or promote any
cosmological, Standard-Model, or laboratory claim.
