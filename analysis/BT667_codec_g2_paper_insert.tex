\subsection{The Fano--Tomotope Codec Chart and the Six-Carrier Weyl Quotient}

The verified raw complement to the six regular \(S_4\)-carriers is
\[
C_{16}^{\rm raw}\cong K_4\sqcup K_4\sqcup K_4\sqcup K_4.
\]
The hypercube \(Q_4\) is therefore not a raw Levi-flag adjacency graph.  It enters only after choosing a secondary product-codec chart
\[
C_{16}=A\times B,
\qquad A\cong B\cong\mathbb F_2^2.
\]
A square structure on a four-point factor is determined by the omitted perfect matching, equivalently by a nonzero direction in \(\mathbb F_2^2\).  Hence each factor has three square structures, and the product chart has
\[
3\cdot3=9
\]
codec gauges.  Every one of these gauges produces a graph isomorphic to \(Q_4\), and hence an antipodal quotient isomorphic to \(K_{4,4}\).  Thus graph isomorphism alone does not select the physical chart.

The Fano--tomotope chart fixes this ambiguity.  Let the tetrahedral hinge axis be \(000\in\mathbb F_2^3\).  The four hinge-adjacent odd axes are
\[
\{001,010,100,111\},
\]
while the three nonadjacent even toroidal axes are
\[
\{011,101,110\}.
\]
Identify the three nonzero directions in \(\mathbb F_2^2\) with these even axes:
\[
01\leftrightarrow011,\qquad
10\leftrightarrow101,\qquad
11\leftrightarrow110.
\]
The Fano-compatible gauges are exactly the diagonal ones,
\[
(01,01),\qquad(10,10),\qquad(11,11),
\]
so the codec ambiguity reduces as
\[
\boxed{9\longrightarrow3\longrightarrow1,}
\]
where the final reduction is a choice of tomotope hinge/orientation.

The same three diagonal directions match the three metric carrier-pair channels:
\[
011\leftrightarrow\mathrm{far},\qquad
101\leftrightarrow\mathrm{middle},\qquad
110\leftrightarrow\mathrm{active}.
\]
Thus the six regular carriers split as
\[
6=2_{\rm far}+2_{\rm middle}+2_{\rm active}.
\]
Writing the paired carriers as
\[
F_+,F_-,M_+,M_-,A_+,A_-,
\]
define the secondary carrier graph by connecting every plus carrier to every minus carrier.  This gives
\[
\{F_+,M_+,A_+\}\;--\;\{F_-,M_-,A_-\}\cong K_{3,3}.
\]
The verified metric pairing
\[
\{F_+F_-,M_+M_-,A_+A_-\}
\]
has stabilizer of order \(12\) inside \(\operatorname{Aut}(K_{3,3})\).  Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(K_{3,3},M_{\rm metric})\cong D_6\cong W(G_2).
\]
This is the precise secondary \(G_2\)-sized quotient associated with the codec chart.  It is not yet a full Weyl action on the \(160\) Levi flags.
