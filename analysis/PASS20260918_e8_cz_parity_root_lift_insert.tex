\[L:(x,k)\mapsto(Qx,k+\delta_x)\pmod6\]

\[L^3=d^3=-I.\]

\[\langle d,C\rangle\cong C_6\times C_3.\]

Therefore $40\neq36$ forbids identifying $d^3$ with the physical parity gate.
