# Pass 455 — ordinary and twisted Frobenius–Schur indicators

The faithful degree-three irreducible characters were computed on both extraspecial groups of order 27:

- the exponent-three Heisenberg group \(H_3\);
- the exponent-nine modular group \(R_9=\langle x,y\mid x^9=y^3=1,\ yxy^{-1}=x^4\rangle\).

For the canonical center-inverting involutions

\[
\tau_H(a,b,c)=(a,-b,-c),
\qquad
\tau_R(x^ay^b)=x^{-a}y^b,
\]

every faithful degree-three character satisfies

\[
\boxed{\nu(\chi)=0,\qquad \nu_\tau(\chi)=+1.}
\]

The involutions were verified on every group pair before evaluating the indicator sums.

**Resolution.** The exponent-three/exponent-nine distinction is invisible at four successive levels: graph isomorphism, PDS eigenvalue fingerprint, ordinary Frobenius–Schur indicator, and canonical center-inverting twisted indicator. Any surviving distinction must occur in finer integral lattices, extension data, tensor-category structure, or a different twisting automorphism.
