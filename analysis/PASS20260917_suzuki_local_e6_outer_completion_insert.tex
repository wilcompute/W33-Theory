\subsection{The local cubic carrier sees $PSp(4,3)$ in $2.\mathrm{Suz}$ and the full $W(E_6)$ in $2.\mathrm{Suz}{:}2$}
\label{sec:20260917-suzuki-local-e6-outer}

The $32760$-point Suzuki/Leech tight shell contains a transitive family of
$135135$ full nondegenerate $W(3,3)$ subspaces.  Fix one such subspace $U$.
Its $54$ orthogonal full-$W33$ partners pair by symplectic complement into
$27$ unordered decompositions
\[
   \mathbf F_3^{12}=U\perp V\perp V^{\perp(U\oplus V)}.
\]
The parent incidence certificate proves that these $27$ decompositions carry
$\operatorname{SRG}(27,10,1,5)$; its complement is the Schl\"afli graph of the
$27$ lines on a smooth cubic surface.

The exhaustive stabilizer computation already separates two order-$51840$
extensions which must not be conflated.  On the four-dimensional block itself,
$\operatorname{Stab}_{2.\mathrm{Suz}}(U)$ restricts onto the symplectic double
cover $\mathrm{Sp}(4,3)$, order $51840$, with kernel $2^7$.  After
projectivizing to the local $27$ decompositions, however, all $270270$
Schreier generators lie in the inner subgroup
\[
  \boxed{\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)\cong U_4(2)},\qquad |\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)|=25920,
\]
with projective kernel $2^8$.  The outer Weyl coset is genuinely absent from
$2.\mathrm{Suz}$.

The new result constructs that missing coset explicitly.  We vendor the ATLAS
$12$-dimensional $\mathbf F_3$ representation of $2.\mathrm{Suz}{:}2$.
Its second standard generator $D$ is literally the old Suzuki generator $B$.
The new generator $C$ satisfies
\[
  CJC^T=-J,
\]
so it is a symplectic similitude of multiplier $-1$ rather than an element of

that $C(U)$ is also reached from $U$ by the old-group word
\[
 \boxed{\texttt{BABABABABABBABABABABBABABA}}
\]
of length $26$.  If $T$ is the matrix of that word, then
\[
   h=CT^{-1}
\]
fixes $U$ projectively while retaining multiplier $-1$.  Thus $h$ is an
explicit outer-coset block-stabilizer element, not merely an order argument.

The published ATLAS subgroup structure closes the proof.  The degree-$135135$
Suzuki point stabilizer is
\[
  2^{1+6}.U_4(2),
\]

\[

\[
  6635520=128\cdot51840.

\[
  \boxed{\operatorname{Im}_{2.\mathrm{Suz}:2}(27)=U_4(2){:}2

\]
This is exactly the classical automorphism group of the Schl\"afli/cubic-surface
