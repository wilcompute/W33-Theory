\section{Exact Mackey decomposition of the selector algebra}
\label{sec:mackey-selector-decomposition}

Let $X$ be the $120$ line--matching selectors of $W(3,3)$ and let $H$ be the stabilizer of one selector in $\operatorname{PGSp}(4,3)$.  The literal permutation action gives
\[
H\cong C_3^3\rtimes(D_8\times C_2).
\]
Write $N=O_3(H)\cong C_3^3$ and $K=H/N\cong D_8\times C_2$.  The action of $K$ on the dual group $\widehat N$ has six orbits of sizes
\[
1,2,4,4,8,8,
\]
with little groups of orders $16,8,4,4,2,2$ and types
\[
D_8\times C_2,\quad D_8,\quad V_4,\quad V_4,\quad C_2,\quad C_2.
\]
Exact little--group induction in $\mathbb Z[\omega]$, where $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$, produces all $27$ irreducible characters.  Every value is a rational integer, and the degree census is
\[
1^8,\qquad 2^6,\qquad 4^9,\qquad 8^4.
\]
Hence
\[
\mathbb QH\cong
\mathbb Q^8\oplus M_2(\mathbb Q)^6\oplus
M_4(\mathbb Q)^9\oplus M_8(\mathbb Q)^4.
\]

The permutation character of $H$ on $X$ contains fourteen irreducibles.  Their degrees are
\[
1,1,1,2,2,2,4,4,4,4,8,8,8,8,
\]
and their multiplicities are
\[
1,1,1,1,1,1,1,2,2,3,3,3,4,5.
\]
Therefore
\[
\sum_i d_i m_i=120,
\qquad
\sum_i m_i^2=83,
\]
so the orbital commutant dimension is recovered directly from the character decomposition.

For each nonzero constituent $\chi$, the exact projector
\[
e_\chi=
\frac{\chi(1)}{|H|}
\sum_{h\in H}\chi(h^{-1})\rho_X(h)
\]
coincides exactly with one of the fourteen rational primitive central projectors of the orbital algebra.  Thus
\[
\operatorname{End}_H(\mathbb Q^X)
\cong
\mathbb Q^7\oplus M_2(\mathbb Q)^2\oplus
M_3(\mathbb Q)^3\oplus M_4(\mathbb Q)\oplus M_5(\mathbb Q),
\]
and the matrix sizes are precisely the selector multiplicities.

The selector Terwilliger center has dimension ten rather than fourteen.  Seven Mackey sectors are already separated, whereas the remaining seven fuse into scalar packets of sizes $2,2,3$.  Consequently
\[
(2-1)+(2-1)+(3-1)=4=14-10=83-79.
\]
The geometric splitter resolves these three packets.  It acts by scalars only on multiplicity--one orbital blocks; on repeated constituents it remains a genuine matrix in the multiplicity space.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This is a finite rational representation--theory statement reconstructed from the literal permutation group.  It does not identify particle multiplets, gauge fields, generations, hardware channels, or laboratory observables.
