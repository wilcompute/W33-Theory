## Setting

The 240 roots fibre 6:1 over the forty points (Pass 1021). The fibres are the Eisenstein lines {±α, ±ωα, ±ω²α}, the
orbits of c⁵ for a Coxeter element.

**Collinearity is root orthogonality.** Two points are collinear iff all 36 inner products between their fibres vanish
(spectrum 12, 2²⁴, −4¹⁵).

**How an involution reads on the points.** An inner involution θ_h (h ∈ E₈/2E₈) has root signs (−1)^⟨h,α⟩, and
dim 𝔨 = 8 + #even roots. On a fibre α + ωα + ω²α = 0. So each point has either all six roots even, or exactly one
even ± pair.

## Theorem A (exact): real forms = two-qutrit structures

| real form | involutions | all-even point set | W(3,3) structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| E₈₍₋₂₄₎ (clock, dim 𝔨 = 136) | 120 = **40 × 3** | the 12 neighbours of one point (4 triangles) | a **context / event**; the 3 are the tick lines of Theorem 7.1 |
| E₈₍₈₎ (split, dim 𝔨 = 120) | 135 = **45 × 3** | 8 points inducing K₄,₄ = H₁ ⊥ H₂ | a **tensor factorisation** C⁹ = C³ ⊗ C³ |

The names "clock" and "split" are exact:
* the clock form is anchored at a single event;
* the split form is anchored at a subsystem decomposition.

**What is ours.** Pass 11831 owns the 45 factorisations ↔ symplectic involutions at the Sp(4,3) level. The lift to the
E₈ real forms is new.

## Theorem B (exact): which dynamics is compatible with which real form

**The group.** G₃₂ = 3 × Sp(4,3) (order 155,520, enumerated) acts on the 255 involutions. The central Eisenstein
element ω fixes **none** of them, so the physical group is the determinant-one Sp(4,3) (51,840 elements). Call a real
form compatible with a dynamics g when g fixes its involution.

**Full census (frozen).**
* **Transvection** (order 3, centre p; 80 elements): it is the elementary Clifford tick. It fixes **exactly the 3
  clock involutions at p and no split involution.** The same holds for −transvections and for the order-12 class with
  one fixed point.
* **Orders 4 and 8:** no fixed point, but an invariant factorisation. They fix **only split** involutions.
* **Regular unipotents (order 9), orders 5 and 10:** they fix none.

**Overall:** 10,240 elements are clock-only and 9,900 are split-only.

## Reading

**The question.** Open problem 2 asked whether a principle on the forty points selects E₈₍₋₂₄₎ over the split form,
i.e. whether "the geometry chooses one time".

**The answer, at the level of a selection rule given the dynamics:**
* the **elementary tick** (a symplectic transvection) is compatible with exactly one real form, the clock form,
  anchored at the tick's centre;
* no tick preserves a split real form;
* a dynamics that preserves a subsystem decomposition but no event would select the split form instead.

**What it means.** Time is chosen together with the dynamics: a theory whose evolution is generated by elementary ticks
has one time per tick centre.

## Scope

* **What is proved:** a selection rule, given the dynamics. No action principle is derived.
* **Involutions:** inner ones only; E₈ has no outer automorphisms.
* **Compatibility:** means a fixed involution.
