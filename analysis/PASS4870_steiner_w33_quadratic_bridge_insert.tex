\label{sec:pass4870-steiner-w33-quadratic}

directions: the $120$ Steiner/maximal triangles form the full
$H_2(-;\mathbb F_3)$ coordinate module of the double-six clique complex, but
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{PSp}(H_2,Q_{10})
  =\operatorname{Hom}_{PSp}(Q_{10},H_2)=0.
\]
The linear obstruction is exact.  Pass~4870 determines the first nonlinear
layer rather than bypassing that obstruction.

The action of $PSp(4,3)$ on unordered pairs of Steiner triangles has four
orbits, of sizes
\[
  120,\qquad1620,\qquad2160,\qquad3240,
\]

\[

triangles.

intrinsically into forty three-element fibers.  The $2160$-pair relation is
then exactly the complete $K_{3,3}$ lift between adjacent fibers.  Quotienting
by the three-element fibers gives a graph with
\[
  v=40,\qquad k=12,\qquad\lambda=2,\qquad\mu=4.
\]

symplectic $W(3,3)$ collinearity graph, and the induced projective symplectic

\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{PSp}(\operatorname{Sym}^2 H_2,Q_{10}).
\]
For a permutation module, $\operatorname{Sym}^2 H_2$ splits into the diagonal
permutation module and the four unordered-pair orbit modules above.  The

\[
  \boxed{\dim\operatorname{Hom}_{PSp}
  (\operatorname{Sym}^2H_2,Q_{10})=2.}

lifts $W(3,3)$ adjacency.  Since $Q_{10}$ is irreducible, every nonzero map in

This gives a precise characteristic-three answer to the preceding obstruction:
the first equivariant Steiner-to-adjoint bridge is quadratic, not linear, and
