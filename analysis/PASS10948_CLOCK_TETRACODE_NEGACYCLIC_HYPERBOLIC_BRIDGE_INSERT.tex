\subsection{Pass 10948: the clock tetracode is a reciprocal negacyclic hyperbolic pair}

The exact oriented clock code from Pass~10946,
\[
 C=\{(a,b,a+b,b-a):a,b\in\mathbb F_3\},
\]
is not negacyclic in its frozen orientation.  However, flipping either the
second or the fourth projective-ray representative gives the two codes
\[
 C_+=(x^2+x-1),\qquad C_-=(x^2-x-1)
 \subset \mathbb F_3[x]/(x^4+1),
\]
where
\[
 x^4+1=(x^2+x-1)(x^2-x-1).
\]
Thus the two standard negacyclic gauges are precisely the two reciprocal
irreducible factors of $\Phi_8$ modulo $3$.

For the negacyclic shift
\[
 N(c_0,c_1,c_2,c_3)=(-c_3,c_0,c_1,c_2)
\]
one has $N^4=-I$ and $N^8=I$ on both sectors, so the algebraic clock has
exact order eight.  Each sector is a self-dual $[4,2,3]_3$ tetracode.
They meet only at zero and pair perfectly across one another: in polynomial
bases $(g_+,Ng_+)$ and $(g_-,Ng_-)$ the cross Gram matrix is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\2&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \det=2\pmod3.
\]
Cyclotomic inversion $x\mapsto x^{-1}=-x^3$ exchanges the two sectors.

Exhaustive signed-coordinate enumeration gives eight distinct tetracode
images under the order-$384$ signed permutation group.  Exactly two images
are standard negacyclic; each has $48$ presentations, hence $96$ of the
$384$ signed gauges yield negacyclicity.  This $48$ is the same monomial
stabilizer scale already attached to the clock tetracode.

The mechanism is objectwise compatible with the older Pass~9961--9984
$\Phi_8$ split branch: that pass uses the same reciprocal factors to obtain
two conjugate $\mathbb F_9$ spaces with vanishing restricted forms and
nondegenerate cross-pairing.  Pass~10948 therefore identifies the four-ray
clock code as the rank-one finite shadow of that reciprocal-prime mechanism,
not merely a repeated factor count.

This is a reversible coding/cyclotomic statement.  The order-eight
negacyclic shift is not a thermodynamic arrow and does not by itself promote
the finite clock to physical time.
