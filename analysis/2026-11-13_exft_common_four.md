# The shared M/IIB four-space and its effective ExFT gauge action

For the representative shared four-space S = span(0, 1, 2, 3), contraction with the signed cubic has common annihilator A = span(0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9). The contraction matrices are signed partial permutation matrices, so this identifies A for arbitrary real vectors by ruling out cancellation within each contraction. In A/S, write the transverse coordinates as x along 4 and (y,z) along (5,9). The only nonzero products are the (4,5) and (4,9) cross terms, with independent targets 26 and 25 and opposite signs. The self-section constraint is xy = xz = 0. Its solution is exactly the union of the IIB line and M plane. A linear section containing S must lie entirely in one of those two branches, because adding a nonzero vector from the line to a nonzero vector from the plane violates the constraint. The exact result applies to all 1,080 coordinate four-spaces by the transitive signed cubic action. In particular the full M and IIB derivative directions cannot both be activated while retaining this strong section constraint. [Coordinate-orbit proof](2026-11-13_e6_tits_coordinates.md)

## Affine parameter cohomology

The affine operator has a 23-dimensional space of invisible constant parameters, because a constant acts only through its four anchor components. The linear potentials in the trivial-parameter map generate 20 of those directions. Their image equals the annihilator of A in the generalized-vector space. Combining this with exactness for Jacobians leaves a three-dimensional quotient of invisible affine parameters by trivial parameters from degree-at-most-two potentials. Representatives are the constant components dual to the three transverse directions. Equivalently this quotient is the dual of A/S. This result concerns affine parameters on a coordinate chart. Periodicity or other global boundary conditions change the permitted potentials.

On a flat four-torus with smooth periodic parameters and potentials, each nonzero Fourier mode is exact in the kernel of the generalized derivative. At a single nonzero coordinate covector the operator and potential symbols have ranks 17 and 10 and zero composition. The conformal stabilizer projects onto the full linear Lie algebra on S, hence its connected action covers SL(4, R) on S and is transitive on nonzero covectors. The cubic and its inverse tensor scale reciprocally, so the rank and exactness statements transport to every nonzero Fourier covector. Constant rank on the unit covector sphere gives a uniform bound for the inverse on the image, and scaling with frequency preserves smoothness of the reconstructed potential. Zero modes are different. Derivatives of periodic potentials have zero mean, so all 23 anchor-zero constant parameters survive in the periodic quotient.
