# External source notes for the Clebsch neighborhood

These notes distinguish standard external graph facts from repository-derived controller results.

- Standard graph references identify the Clebsch graph as the unique `SRG(16,5,0,2)`, the folded 5-cube, with intersection array `{5,4;1,2}`, spectrum `5^1 1^10 (-3)^5`, automorphism group `2^4:S5`, and Petersen second subconstituent.
- Algebraic-geometry references identify a degree-four del Pezzo/Segre quartic as having 16 lines and describe the corresponding projective-cube line-incidence graph; removing one line and its five neighbors leaves a Petersen graph.
- Repository-derived results are the 22-state envelope occupancy, minimum seven-bit systematic parity extension, replicated-controller fault counts, gauge-fusion law, and block-distance `M22` PSD reduction.

No physical identification between hardware states and algebraic-surface lines is asserted.
