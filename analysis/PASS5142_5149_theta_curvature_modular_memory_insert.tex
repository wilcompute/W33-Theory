\subsection*{Passes 5142--5149: exterior theta parity, curvature, centered spectral inversion, and modular root-coset arithmetic}
Pass~5119 already establishes that every apartment-code support is half-regular in the intrinsic theta graph.  The complementary exterior law is stronger than that selected-vertex statement: every unselected apartment has an even number $t_v$ of selected theta-neighbours, and the number of active theta triples is exactly
\[
A_{\rm active}=2(q-1)|S|,
\qquad
\sum_{v\notin S}t_v=4(q-1)|S|.
\]
Writing $b_{2r}=\#\{v\notin S:t_v=2r\}$ gives $\sum_r r b_{2r}=2(q-1)|S|$.  A chamber star has only the $b_2$ term, with $b_2=2(q-1)q^4$.

This exterior parity yields a quantized second-moment curvature theorem.  Put $D=8(q-1)$, $k=D/2=4(q-1)$, and let $A_\Theta$ be theta adjacency.  For $x=\mathbf1_S$,
\[
x^T A_\Theta^2 x\ge k(k+2)|S|,
\]
and the exact defect is
\[
\Delta_2=x^T A_\Theta^2x-k(k+2)|S|
       =\sum_{v\notin S}t_v(t_v-2)\in8\mathbb Z_{\ge0}.
\]
Equality holds precisely when every exterior boundary apartment sees two selected theta-neighbours.  Chamber stars attain equality at $q=2,3,4,5$.  At $q=2$, exhaustive enumeration of all $65535$ nonzero words finds exactly $45$ zero-defect words, all of weight $16$, with next positive defect $64$.

First-order expansion itself is blind: every nonzero codeword support has theta conductance $1/2$.  Equivalently a one-step theta walk stays in the support with probability $1/2$.  At two steps the curvature appears:
\[
\Pr[X_2\in S]\ge\frac14+\frac1D,
\qquad
\Pr[X_2\in S]-\left(\frac14+\frac1D\right)
=\frac{\Delta_2}{D^2|S|}.
\]
Thus the sharp chamber-star values are $3/8,5/16,7/24,9/32$ for $q=2,3,4,5$.

The apparent first-order spectral blindness disappears after removing the constant mode.  Let $N$ be the apartment count, $\alpha=|S|/N$, and $y=x-\alpha\mathbf1$.  The centered theta Rayleigh quotient satisfies
\[
\rho(S)=\frac{y^TA_\Theta y}{y^Ty}
       =D\frac{1/2-\alpha}{1-\alpha},
\qquad
\alpha=\frac{D/2-\rho}{D-\rho}.
\]
Hence the centered quotient determines codeword weight exactly; the remaining distance problem is a bound on the attainable centered quotient, not ordinary Cheeger conductance.

On the root-coset arithmetic side, the $q=4$ incidence matrix has exact Smith form
\[
1^{180}\oplus2^4\oplus0^{72},
\qquad
\operatorname{coker}M_4\cong\mathbb Z^{72}\oplus(\mathbb Z/2)^4.
\]
Thus its characteristic-two rank loss is four genuine integral torsion directions.  More generally every root-coset column has $q$ ones, so the constant left functional supplies one explicit defining-characteristic relation.  Comparing the already certified native rank drops $0,1,4,8,10$ at $q=2,3,4,5,7$ shows that additional hidden modular defect begins at $q=4$; at least $3,7,9$ dimensions remain beyond that one displayed augmentation relation at $q=4,5,7$.

Finally, two statistics of the positive-root poset organize the derivative and memory stories.  The root count $N=|\Phi^+|$ controls the big-cell volume $q^N$ and root-direction derivative $Nq^{N-1}$, while the total root height $H=\sum_{\alpha>0}\mathrm{ht}(\alpha)$ controls the top Jennings degree $(p-1)H$ in the previously established safe range.  For $A_2,C_2,G_2$ the pairs $(N,H)$ are $(3,4),(4,7),(6,16)$.  Thus the $C_2$ derivative $q^4\mapsto4q^3$ and the $C_2$ group-ring depth $7(p-1)$ are related through the same root system but are different invariants.
