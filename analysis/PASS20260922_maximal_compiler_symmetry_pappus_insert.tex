\subsection*{The maximal address--operator common symmetry is a qutrit plane, with 36 Hesse--Pappus choices}

Let
\[
K=H_{27}^{\rm address}\times C_3^{\rm ext},
\qquad
D=[K,K]=Z(H_{27})\times\{0\}.
\]
The regular address character and the landed matter-$81$ operator character
restrict identically to a subgroup $L\le K$ exactly when $L\cap D=1$.
Such an $L$ injects into $K/D\cong\mathbf F_3^3$ as a totally isotropic
subspace for the rank-two commutator form, so
\[
\boxed{|L|\le 9.}
\]
Exhaustion gives $49$ order-nine subgroups, of which exactly
\[
\boxed{36}
\]
avoid $D$.  All $13$ maximal order-$27$ subgroups contain $D$, so the bound is
sharp and global.

In the fixed physical Clifford lift gauge, the twelve possible noncentral
$H_{27}$ cyclic lifts are symplectic-dual to the twelve affine lines of
$AG(2,3)$ through
\[
\langle d,v\rangle_{\rm symp}=c-c_0(d).
\]
Each safe plane has one of three center lines complementary to $D$, hence the
maximal compiler choices form a $12\times3$ three-sheet Hesse controller.
The fixed-center automorphism image has order $216$ and orbit split
$12+12+12$.

There is a second exact incidence theorem.  The $36$ noncentral $C_3$
subgroups of $K$, incident with the $36$ maximal safe planes by containment,
form a cubic bipartite graph on $72$ vertices.  It has four connected
components of size $18$, and every component is the Pappus graph.  On one
projective direction this is simply
\[
c=\alpha+\beta p
\]
on nine points $(c,p)\in\mathbf F_3^2$: $AG(2,3)$ with one parallel class
deleted.

\paragraph{Scope firewall.}
This is not an identification with the classical $36$ doily/double-six
carrier: the safe-plane order-three-intersection graph is $17$-regular,
whereas the established E6/double-six graph is
$\operatorname{SRG}(36,20,10,12)$.  The earlier Witting-packet Pappus theorem
also lives on a different carrier (affine-foliation leaf incidence).
The result is a finite compiler-symmetry theorem; it selects no physical plane,
vacuum, continuum limit, or hardware realization.
