## 3545 — a factorization-field construction compiler for M57

Let \(n\) be even. Suppose there are \(n\) one-factorizations \(F_0,\ldots,F_{n-1}\) of \(K_n\), with every pair sharing exactly one perfect matching:

\[
|F_i\cap F_j|=1.
\]

Write \(m_{ij}\) for that shared matching. Construct vertices

\[
x,y,\quad r_i,\quad c_a,\quad z_{i,a},
\]

and edges

\[
x\sim y,r_i,
\qquad y\sim c_a,
\qquad z_{i,a}\sim r_i,c_a,z_{j,m_{ij}(a)}.
\]

The resulting graph has

\[
n^2+2n+2=(n+1)^2+1
\]

vertices and degree \(n+1\).

The pairwise-intersection property removes all two-row four-cycles. The graph has girth at least five provided every three-row holonomy and every simple four-row holonomy is fixed-point-free. A regular degree-\(n+1\) graph of girth at least five on \((n+1)^2+1\) vertices meets the Moore bound exactly and is therefore a Moore graph.

For \(n=56\), this compiler requires

\[
56\text{ one-factorizations},
\quad \binom{56}{2}=1540\text{ pair intersections},
\]

\[
\binom{56}{3}=27{,}720\text{ triangle checks},
\]

and

\[
3\binom{56}{4}=1{,}101{,}870\text{ simple four-cycle checks}.
\]

It would produce exactly 3250 vertices of degree 57.

**Boundary:** pairwise unique intersection is a strong sufficient branch condition. It is not proved necessary for every involutive Moore chart.
