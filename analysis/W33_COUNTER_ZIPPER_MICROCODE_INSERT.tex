\subsection{Preemptible authenticated counter microcode}
\label{ssec:counter-zipper-microcode}
The existing authenticated counter ISA admits a persistent path-context
implementation, specializing Huet's zipper rather than asserting a new
data-structure theorem. A trusted continuation carries the committed guest
state, both fibre tags, a phase, and cursor, stack and result roots.
\texttt{SCAN} consumes one carry/borrow digit; \texttt{UNWIND} rebuilds one;
\texttt{COMMIT} emits the existing portal route. Each tick opens at most one
canonical bit node and writes at most one. Only a verified final continuation
changes the committed guest registers and program counter.

A propagated prefix of length $k$ takes $2k+3$ ticks. The finite-prefix
scan and unwind terminate and refine the existing macro instruction.
For a $4096$-bit carry or borrow, the witness gives $8195$ ticks, with the
same result as the independent macro verifier; all $512$ small cases agree.
A short increment commits after three one-tick scheduling rounds while a
long carry remains uncommitted. This bounds bit-node work, not wall-clock
latency, program validation, wire bytes, RAM or physical gate cost.

Retention must cover the continuation, not just the visible guest registers:
a paused borrow has $17$ nodes under the committed roots but $24$ under the
complete live roots. A full-closure checkpoint retains both fibre tags,
recovers under a trusted continuation identity, and survives collection while
its archive reference is strong. A hash-only audit reference does not retain
its bytes. The scalable counter semantics supply the universality argument;
a finite controller or fixed finite hash space alone does not.
The executable, frozen certificate and adversarial recovery tests are
\texttt{w33\_counter\_zipper\_microcode.py},
\texttt{w33\_counter\_zipper\_microcode\_certificate.json} and
\texttt{test\_w33\_counter\_zipper\_microcode.py}.
Prior art, the phase argument and implementation limits are recorded in
\texttt{analysis/W33\_COUNTER\_ZIPPER\_MICROCODE.md}.

Compiler admission is a separate obligation from content identity. In the
dynamic sparse witness, replacing the valid Wasm constant $77$ by $78$
preserved every opcode but formerly left the counter target returning $77$.
The exact-witness compiler now checks decoded operands and module bindings,
rejecting unsupported inputs before target generation. Three regression tests
cover differential memory execution, five same-opcode mutations and an
accepted custom-section variation. This enforces the documented restricted
source language; it is not a general compiler-equivalence theorem.

The process microstep owner now separates reusable arithmetic evidence from
consumption of a particular process cursor. Eight identical-value forks use
$19$ inner proofs for $152$ accepted process-bound submissions; all $152$
immediate duplicate submissions reject. Final values agree with an independent
macro verifier while all eight histories and continuation identities differ.
The trusted serialized owner publishes guest state only at macro commit.
Seven owner tests plus the seven process-kernel and nine zipper tests pass.
This is an in-memory execution protocol, not durable exactly-once external I/O;
see \texttt{analysis/W33\_PROCESS\_MICROSTEP\_OWNER.md} for the executable,
prior ownership, retention roundtrip and scope.

A subsequent SQLite adapter publishes the consumed cursor and its complete
live bit closure in one transaction. Killing a worker before commit recovers
tick $8$ and permits one retry; killing it after commit recovers tick $9$ and
rejects the retry. Both borrow executions finish in $35$ ticks at $65535$.
The final macro commit also survives a lost reply without committing twice.
Five durability tests and seven existing owner tests pass, including a
two-connection submission race and restart after every tick. These are process
exit experiments, not power-loss tests or exactly-once external effects.
The database is trusted authority and each tick writes a full snapshot;
see \texttt{analysis/W33\_DURABLE\_MICROSTEP\_OWNER.md}.

Exact accounting further separates work, content and observable information.
Writing $s(x)$ for binary popcount, $L$ increments from $a$ take
$5L+2s(a)-2s(a+L)$ microticks. From zero, preemption adds $M-s(M)$ bit-write
operations relative to the prior macro backend, but adds no distinct bit nodes
when all earlier counter values remain retained. For a uniform $n$-bit initial
value and $L=2^m$, $0\le m<n$, individual completion times reveal
$m+2-2^{1-(n-m)}$ bits, while total batch time reveals only
$2-2^{1-(n-m)}$ bits. The proof identifies the transcript with the low $m$
bits and the trailing-one count of the remaining high part.
An explicit reversible XOR permutation erases the correlated timing record
using the retained final counter: its conditional entropy is zero even when
its marginal entropy is positive. This supplies no measured heat or zero-energy
device claim. Exact backend counts, $45$ timing partitions and a $1024$-state
permutation witness are in \texttt{analysis/W33\_CARRY\_TIMING\_INFORMATION.md}.

\paragraph{Radix, noisy observations, and synthesized erasure.}
The executable extension
\texttt{w33\_computation\_resource\_frontiers.py} checks an abstract radix-$b$
zipper law
\[
 T_b(a,L)=3L+\frac{2}{b-1}\bigl(L+s_b(a)-s_b(a+L)\bigr).
\]
For $X$ uniform on $0,\ldots,b^n-1$ and $L=b^m$, the individual timing entropy
is $m\log_2 b+h_b(n-m)$, whereas the total duration has entropy $h_b(n-m)$,
where
\[
 h_b(d)=(1-b^{-d})\left(\frac{b}{b-1}\log_2 b-\log_2(b-1)\right).
\]
Forty finite ensembles check this identity; classical radix symbols do not
establish coherent qutrit hardware. For mixed binary arithmetic, each crossing
of the edge $u-1\leftrightarrow u$ costs $3+2v_2(u)$ ticks. A closed cycle at
$2^k$ costs $4k+6$, preventing a uniform constant amortized bound for both
operations using a counter-state potential.

For exact timing $Z$, final counter $Y=X+L$, and independent additive observation
noise $E$, the noisy record $O=Z+E$ satisfies $H(O\mid Y)=H(E)$.
Thus noise can reduce input leakage while leaving randomness that $Y$ cannot
uncompute. Four timings with independent jitter $(-2,0,2)$ of probabilities
$(1/4,1/2,1/4)$ leak about $2.005$ bits but retain six conditional record bits.
For the exact record, a prefix circuit reconstructs the statistic $(r,k)$ from
$Y$ with $d=n-m$ clean ancillas, $2d$ NOT gates, $2(d-1)$ Toffoli gates, and
$m+2+2d-\operatorname{popcount}(d)$ CNOT gates, then restores its ancillas.
Twenty-eight sizes pass exhaustive clean-ancilla checks. The five-bit example
uses eighteen gates. This is a basis-permutation construction, not measured
physical dissipation. The accompanying report separately attributes a published
colloidal-trap work measurement and keeps Holonet calibration open.

\paragraph{Redundant counters and eight observation experiments.}
The follow-up \texttt{W33\_EIGHT\_COMPUTATION\_EXPERIMENTS.md} reports seven
executed computational investigations and one blocked physical collection.
An authenticated redundant counter stores $P-N$ with $P\geq N$ and increments
only $P$ or $N$; canonical root equality supplies its zero test under the trusted
invariant. For $U$ increments, $D$ successful decrements and $Z$ zero decrements,
\[
 T=Z+5(U+D)+2s(P_0)+2s(N_0)-2s(P_f)-2s(N_f).
\]
All 320 tested six-operation paths pass. At the $2^{64}$ boundary, 128 alternating
cycles take 1390 arithmetic microticks rather than 33536, at the cost of growing
representation state; wrapper work and integration into the guest ABI remain
outside this claim.

Retaining reversible noise seeds permits noisy-record erasure, but reusing a
common additive seed exposes timing differences: four timings again reveal all
three input bits in the tested ensemble. A ternary eraser uses eight controlled
modular-shift gates on nine qutrits; its 19683-state permutation and a complex
superposition preserve amplitudes while clearing the intended record and
ancillas in simulation. Sixteen padding policies supply an exact finite
latency--information frontier. Holotrade separately exports signed pencil
preimages and rational primal--dual certificates with real $\ell_1$ optima 5
and 8, so the selected profiles' negativity survives fractional relaxation.
These statements neither construct blockers nor measure physical heat. The
reset-work protocol and analyzer reject synthetic provenance, and physical
collection remains blocked without instrument data.
