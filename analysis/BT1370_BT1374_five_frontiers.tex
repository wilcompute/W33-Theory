\section{Matrix units, stabilizer structure, modular degeneration, and the selector--Levi boundary}
\label{sec:bt1370-bt1374-five-frontiers}

Let $X$ be the $120$ line-matching selectors of $W(3,3)$, let
$H$ be the stabilizer of one selector in $G=\operatorname{PGSp}(4,3)$,
and let
\[
T=T(x)\subset \mathcal A=\operatorname{End}_H(\mathbb Q^X)
\]
be the selector Terwilliger and full orbital algebras.

\begin{theorem}[Five-frontier selector-algebra theorem]
The following exact statements hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The algebra
\[
\mathcal A\cong
\mathbb Q^7\oplus M_2(\mathbb Q)^2\oplus M_3(\mathbb Q)^3
\oplus M_4(\mathbb Q)\oplus M_5(\mathbb Q)
\]
admits an explicit rational system of all $83$ matrix units. For each
simple block, bases of $\mathcal Ae$ and $e\mathcal A$ are dualized under
$e\mathcal A\times\mathcal Ae\to e\mathcal Ae=\mathbb Qe$, giving
$E_{ij}E_{k\ell}=\delta_{jk}E_{i\ell}$ and $\sum_iE_{ii}=z$ exactly.

\item The order-$432$ selector stabilizer is
\[
H\cong C_3^3\rtimes(D_8\times C_2).
\]
The extension splits, and the complement acts faithfully on
$\mathbb F_3^3$ with a $1+2$ invariant decomposition.

\item Exhausting all $8\cdot16=128$ orbital pairs across the two defect
blocks shows that the minimum support of an ordinary-multiplication
completion of $T$ is $540$. Exactly two minimum symmetric solutions
occur, with orbital sets
\[
\{18,63\},\qquad \{18,64\}.
\]
Thus the previously selected splitter is support-minimal and has exactly
one equally minimal geometric sibling.

\item The integral $A,D$ word lattice is not saturated in characteristics
$2,3,5$: its generated reductions have dimensions $42,54,74$. Their
Jacobson-radical power dimensions are
\[
(22,6,0)\quad(p=2),
\qquad
(48,36,22,13,4,0)\quad(p=3),
\]
and the $p=5$ reduction is semisimple. For the full $83$-dimensional
orbital algebra the corresponding radical powers are
\[
(45,16,0)\quad(p=2),
\qquad
(72,49,27,14,4,0)\quad(p=3),
\]
while characteristic five is semisimple.

\item The natural selector--flag cross-orbital space has dimension four,
with orbit sizes
\[
480,\ 1440,\ 4320,\ 12960.
\]
Every such equivariant map annihilates the terminal $81$-dimensional flag
idempotent. Hence the natural $120$-selector/$160$-flag bimodule is not a
Morita bridge to the Levi Steinberg sector; the verified bridge remains
the $2160$ rectangle/apartment selector-sheet construction.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
These are finite group, rational-algebra, modular-algebra, and equivariant
bimodule statements. No literature-priority, cosmological, Standard-Model,
optical-hardware, or laboratory claim is made.
