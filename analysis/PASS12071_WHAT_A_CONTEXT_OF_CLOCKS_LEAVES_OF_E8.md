# Pass 12071: what a context of clocks leaves of E8

Claude track. Builds on 12052, 12062 and 12068.

## Method

Commuting clock involutions fix h + span(roots even under all of them). Every choice of clock at each event was
checked.

## Results

| Clocks | Fixed subalgebra | Dimension |
|---|---|---|
| one | e₇ + a₁ | 136 |
| two collinear | d₆ + 2a₁ | 72 |
| two non-collinear | e₆ + u(1)², when the product is a clock (6 of 9); d₆ + 2a₁, when it is split (3 of 9) | 80 / 72 |
| three at one event | e₆ + u(1)² | 80 |
| three on a line | d₄ + 4a₁ | 40 |
| a whole context (all 81 choices) | **su(2)⁸** | 24, rank 8 |

## Reading

- A context of clocks leaves eight SU(2)s and no su(3). It does not leave a Standard-Model algebra.
- Two incompatible clocks whose product is a clock leave e₆ + u(1)², the grand-unified algebra of E6(p).

## Scope

Which clocks a tick condensate would pick is not a tick datum (12072).
