\section{Symmetry, Phase, and Cubic Leakage}
\label{sec:symmetry-phase-cubic-leakage}

The Levi flag geometry of $W(3,3)$ separates three structures which are easily
confused if only their orders are compared: the true incidence symmetry, the
nonzero phase double, and the nonlinear cubic leakage stack.

\begin{theorem}[Levi symmetry/phase split]
For the $q=3$ point-line Levi frame of $W(3,3)$,
\[
\operatorname{Aut}_{\rm flag}=\mathrm{PSp}(4,3),
\qquad
|\operatorname{Aut}_{\rm flag}|=25920.
\]
The Weyl-group scale
\[
51840=2\cdot25920
\]
is not an extra point-line duality of the $q=3$ incidence geometry.  It is the
balanced nonzero $\mathbb F_3$ phase double
\[
+1:25920,
\qquad
-1:25920.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Levi flag set has $160$ flags and flag stabilizer $162$, hence
\[
160\cdot162=25920.
\]
The minimal logical vector pairing has
\[
320\cdot3240=1036800
\]
vector pairs with phase distribution
\[
0:984960,\qquad +1:25920,\qquad -1:25920.
\]
Thus the nonzero phase sector has size
\[
25920+25920=51840,
\]
while each nonzero sheet separately has the true flag-frame symmetry order.
\end{proof}

The projective support incidence count is
\[
160\cdot81=12960.
\]
Lifting by the two nonzero scalars on the $X$ side and the two nonzero scalars
on the $Z$ side gives the scalar factor $4$:
\[
4\cdot12960=51840.
\]
The deck involution
\[
\tau(a,b)=(-a,b),\qquad a,b\in\mathbb F_3^*,
\]
swaps the two nonzero phase sheets.

\begin{theorem}[Cubic leakage resonance]
Let the nontrivial W33 SRG spectrum be
\[
12^1+2^{24}+(-4)^{15}.
\]
Then
\[
\frac{\sum m_\lambda\lambda^5}{\sum m_\lambda\lambda^3}=244.
\]
Moreover
\[
244=40\cdot7-4\cdot9=v\Phi_6-\chi q^2.
\]
Since the Ihara non-backtracking outdegree is $p_{\rm Ih}=11$, the cubic leakage
ratio is
\[
\boxed{
\frac{244}{121}=
\frac{\left(\sum m_\lambda\lambda^5\right)/\left(\sum m_\lambda\lambda^3\right)}{p_{\rm Ih}^2}.
}
\]
\end{theorem}

Finally, let $A$ be the Levi flag adjacency operator.  Its eigenvalues are
\[
6,\quad 2+\sqrt6,\quad 2,\quad 2-\sqrt6,\quad -2
\]
with multiplicities
\[
1,\quad24,\quad30,\quad24,\quad81.
\]
The protected $H_1=81$ sector is the $-2$ eigenspace.  Its projector is
\[
P_{E_4}(A)=
\frac{(A-6I)(A-2I)(A^2-4A-2I)}{320}.
\]
The leakage-killer retaining only the uniform and protected sectors is
\[
P_{E_0+E_4}(A)=
\frac{(A-2I)(A^2-4A-2I)(A^2-4A-6I)}{320}.
\]
For the cubic Gegenbauer Hamiltonian
\[
H_3=C_3(G),\qquad G=\frac1{81}CC^T=\frac{160}{81}E_4,
\]
this filter kills the entire companion stack $E_1+E_2+E_3$.  After subtracting
the uniform component and renormalizing the diagonal, the repaired cubic flow
returns exactly to the protected Gram matrix $G$.
