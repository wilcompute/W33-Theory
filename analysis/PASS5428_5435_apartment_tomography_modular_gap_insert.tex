\subsection{Canonical apartment tomography and the orientation Hodge filter}

Let $C$ denote the signed flag--apartment incidence matrix of a generalized
quadrangle $GQ(q,q)$, with every Levi edge oriented from point to line, and put
\[
 N=(q+1)^2(q^2+1).
\]
Pass~5396 gives $CC^{\mathsf T}=N E_{\rm cyc}$, where $E_{\rm cyc}$ is the
orthogonal projector onto the $q^4$-dimensional Levi cycle space.  Consequently
all nonzero singular values of $C$ equal $\sqrt N$, and the Moore--Penrose inverse
on the cycle image is
\[
 \boxed{C^+=\frac1N C^{\mathsf T}.}
\]
Thus every real cycle flow $z$ has the canonical minimum-$\ell_2$ apartment
reconstruction
\[
 a_{\min}=\frac1N C^{\mathsf T}z,
 \qquad z=Ca_{\min},
 \qquad \|a_{\min}\|^2=\frac{\|z\|^2}{N}.
\]
Equivalently, $\sum_A\langle c_A,z\rangle^2=N\|z\|^2$.  Since each apartment
column has squared norm eight, the normalized apartments form a unit-norm tight
frame of bound $N/8$.

For the unsigned matrix $B=|C|$, Pass~5404 gives a positive five-sector Gram
spectrum.  Its largest eigenvalue is $8q^4$ and its smallest is
$(q-1)^2(q^2+1)$ for every $q>1$, so
\[
 \boxed{\kappa(BB^{\mathsf T})=\frac{8q^4}{(q-1)^2(q^2+1)}.}
\]
The exact inverse is the primitive-idempotent spectral sum and
$B^+=B^{\mathsf T}(BB^{\mathsf T})^{-1}$.  Changing only the odd-distance shell
signs sends the full-rank unsigned kernel
\[
 q^4A_0+q^3A_1+q^2A_2+qA_3+A_4
\]
to
\[
 q^4A_0-q^3A_1+q^2A_2-qA_3+A_4=N E_{\rm cyc}.
\]
Hence orientation is an exact algebraic Hodge spectral filter: it removes the
Levi cut-space dimensions while preserving the diagonal trace.  This terminology
is purely spectral and carries no thermodynamic claim.

\subsection{Integral unsigned spectrum and exact Gram volume}

The conjugate eigenvalues
\[
 \lambda_{\pm}=2q^2(q-1)(q+1\pm\sqrt{2q})
\]
are the roots of
\[
 Q_q(x)=x^2-4q^2(q-1)(q+1)x+4q^4(q-1)^2(q^2+1).
\]
With $f=q(q+1)^2/2$ and $g=q(q^2+1)/2$, the complete characteristic polynomial is
\[
 \boxed{(x-8q^4)Q_q(x)^f(x-2q^2(q-1)^2)^{2g}(x-(q-1)^2(q^2+1))^{q^4}.}
\]
In particular the radicals cancel in the global determinant.  Writing
$P=(q+1)(q^2+1)$,
\[
 \boxed{\det(BB^{\mathsf T})=
 2^{2P+1}q^{4P}(q-1)^{2f+4g+2q^4}(q^2+1)^{f+q^4}.}
\]
At $q=3$ this is $2^{456}3^{160}5^{105}$.

\subsection{Characteristic-two defect module and the $q=5$ post-minimum gap}

For odd $q$, let $M_0$ be the even-weight point module, $K=\ker(F^{\mathsf T})$,
and $C_W$ the binary $W$-line code.  Pass~5350 gives $C_W\subseteq K\subseteq M_0$
and identifies $\operatorname{Rad}(\operatorname{im}F^{\mathsf T})$ with
$F^{\mathsf T}(M_0)$.  Hence
\[
 \boxed{0\longrightarrow D_q:=K/C_W\longrightarrow M_0/C_W
 \longrightarrow \operatorname{Rad}(\operatorname{im}F^{\mathsf T})\longrightarrow0.}
\]
If $r=\operatorname{rank}_2F$ and $g=q(q^2+1)/2$, then $\dim D_q=g-r$.
Thus the open all-odd footprint-rank theorem is exactly $D_q=0$.  This uses no
splitting of $\mathbb F_2[P^1(q)]$ and respects the Pass~5358 nonsplitting firewall.

At $q=5$, $K_0=[73125,560,1000]_2$.  Pass~5284 classifies every block-minimum
word: there are $2340$, all of weight $1000$.  Every other word has at least $26$
active P-components, each nonzero local even component having weight at least $40$.
Therefore
\[
 \boxed{d_2(K_0)\ge1040,}
\]
so weights $1001,\ldots,1039$ are absent.  No weight-$1040$ witness is asserted.

\subsection{The $q=3$ provenance decoder reaches eventual radius ten}

Pass~5381 closes eventual radius nine.  At weight ten, fix a false candidate by
apartment transitivity and write $V$ for its vote.  The four incident charts are
disjoint away from the candidate, so $V\le4$.  The four sectors close as follows.
For $V=1$, an exact fixed-coordinate stopping-set MILP is infeasible.  For $V=2$,
an exact MILP enforcing two local false votes and at least three spoiled charts at
every true error is infeasible.

For $V=3$, the local false-center weights $6,7,8,9,10$ occur with counts
\[
 32,\ 256,\ 832,\ 1536,\ 1856.
\]
A single outsider supplies at most two new local chart directions.  The exact
local direction deficits are already too large for weights seven through ten, so
only weight six plus four outsiders can survive the necessary spoiled-chart test.
Its complete completion census contains
\[
 \boxed{1296}
\]
rooted candidates, and every one clears under the actual deterministic decoder.

For $V=4$, the raw rooted count is
\[
 16\binom{1599}{2}+64\cdot1599+96=20{,}544{,}048.
\]
An outsider must share a chart with another true error or singleton provenance
eliminates the false center.  This exact prune leaves
\[
 341{,}664+7{,}808+96=\boxed{349{,}568}
\]
necessary survivors, and all $349{,}568$ clear under the actual decoder.
Therefore every weight-ten error either makes a nonempty true-only first
correction and falls into the radius-nine basin, or is one of the exhaustively
classified false-survivor cases and self-heals.  Hence
\[
 \boxed{r_{\rm eventual}=10.}
\]
The monotone true-only guarantee remains sharply seven because the radius-eight
echo family still makes false first corrections.  Radius eleven remains open.
