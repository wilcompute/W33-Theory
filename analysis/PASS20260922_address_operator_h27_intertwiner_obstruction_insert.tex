\subsection*{The regular address $H_{27}$ and operator $H_{27}$ admit no invertible equivariant basis change}

The two landed order-$27$ Heisenberg actions are not merely different as
permutation actions; they are non-isomorphic complex modules.  The regular
address module decomposes as
\[
\mathbf C[H_{27}]
\cong
\bigoplus_{\chi\in\widehat{H_{27}/Z}}\chi
\oplus3V_\omega\oplus3V_{\omega^2},
\]
whereas the center-correct trinification action on the $E_6$ minuscule $27$ is
\[
27\downarrow H_{27}^{\rm op}=9V_\omega.
\]
Consequently
\[
\dim\operatorname{Hom}_{H_{27}}
\!\left(\mathbf C[H_{27}],9V_\omega\right)=27,
\]
but every equivariant map has
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}T\le 9.}
\]
The bound is sharp, so no invertible $27\times27$ $H_{27}$ intertwiner exists.

Equivalently, the address center acts with eigenvalue multiplicities
$9+9+9$, while the operator center is scalar on all $27$ dimensions.
Automorphism twisting cannot remove the obstruction because the center is
characteristic.

\paragraph{Scope firewall.}
This rules out an $H_{27}$-equivariant frozen-root basis conjugacy between the
two landed actions.  It does not rule out a non-equivariant coordinate
dictionary, a smaller common subgroup, or an enlarged correspondence.
