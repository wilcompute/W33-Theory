\section{Continuation frontiers: singleton orbits, chiral $A_2$ fibers, and the Schlaefli weld}
\label{sec:bt2707-continuation}

The complete exact-cover signature carrier admits a further exact refinement.  Writing
\[
 u=3t-4\mathbf 1,
\]
the 720 centered signatures have norm distribution
\[
144^{45}\oplus216^{270}\oplus360^{135}\oplus432^{270}
\]
and decompose into 45 affine rank-two fibers of size 16.  In an integral basis with Gram matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}72&36\\36&72\end{pmatrix},
\]
each fiber is one center, an $A_2$ root hexagon, one chiral weight triangle, and the doubled root hexagon:
\[
720=45(1+6+3+6).
\]

The 45 local two-planes lie in the common 20-dimensional octet constituent. If
\[
N_i=2a_ia_i^T-a_ic_i^T-c_ia_i^T+2c_ic_i^T,
\qquad P_i=N_i/108,
\]
then
\[
2\sum_iN_i=3U_0^TU_0,
\qquad
\sum_iP_i=\frac92E_{20}.
\]
They form a two-angle tight fusion frame over $\operatorname{SRG}(45,12,3,3)$: adjacent plane pairs have squared principal cosines $(1/4,0)$ and nonadjacent pairs have $(1/16,1/16)$.

The three noncentral shells form the exact local tower
\[
S_3,\qquad S_3/C_2,\qquad S_3,
\]
with coherent-closure ranks $21,8,21$.  Modulo two, every 16-point fiber refines one Schlaefli tritangent class as
\[
16=7+3+3+3.
\]
The seven-point class contains the center and doubled roots; each of the three heavy triples contains two roots and one chiral weight and is indexed by one incident Schlaefli line.  The global incidence has 45 tritangents, 27 lines, degrees $(3,5)$, and line graph $\operatorname{SRG}(27,10,1,5)$.

On the coding frontier, a deterministic exact 5,000-support search followed by exact $PGSp(4,3)$ orbit classification certifies
\[
U_6^{\mathrm{singleton}}\ge
596\cdot51{,}840+3\cdot25{,}920
=30{,}974{,}400.
\]
This remains a lower bound.  On the chromatic frontier the exact statement remains
\[
10\le\chi(H)\le11;
\]
no unsuccessful ten-colour search is interpreted as an impossibility proof.

Finally, the unique full-group degree-seven self-covariant restricts on primitive $C_5$ character lines by
\[
k\mapsto7k\equiv2k\pmod5,
\]
giving the projective cycle $1\to2\to4\to3\to1$.  This is a self-covariant of the faithful four-dimensional module and is not asserted to be an $E_8$--coexact carrier map.
