\subsection{Line signings as an exact apartment-parity observable}
\label{sec:pass4461-line-signing-apartment}

The signing and building threads admit a sharper common formulation than the
numerical Wilson-loop comparison alone.  Let $G$ be the $40$-vertex
collinearity graph of $W(3,3)$ and let $\mathcal L$ be its $40$ geometric
lines.  The six edges of each $K_4$ line are disjoint from the six-edge sets of
the other lines, so the $40$ line supports partition all $240$ graph edges.
Choose a sign $\sigma_\ell\in\{\pm1\}$ for each $\ell\in\mathcal L$, and give
every collinearity edge on $\ell$ that sign.  Write $A_\sigma$ for the signed
adjacency matrix and $N$ for the $40\times40$ point--line incidence matrix.
Then the signed graph has the exact incidence factorization
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 A_\sigma=N\,\operatorname{diag}(\sigma)N^{T}
           -\operatorname{diag}(N\sigma).}
 \label{eq:pass4461-incidence-factorization}
\end{equation}
The subtraction removes the diagonal point-wise signed line sums.

There are exactly $1620$ simple quadrangles in the point collinearity graph.
Indeed, the graph has $540$ nonedges; each nonedge has $\mu=4$ common
neighbours, so choosing two common neighbours gives
$540\binom42/2=1620$ quadrangles.  For every such quadrangle its four edge-lines
are distinct and induce a quadrangle in the dual line-collinearity graph.
Equivalently, inserting the four side-lines produces the corresponding simple
$8$-cycle in the point--line Levi graph.  Thus the $1620$ point-graph
quadrangles, the $1620$ dual line-graph quadrangles, and the $1620$ Levi
apartments are three shadows of the same rank-two-building apartments.

For an apartment $a$, define its line parity
\[
 \chi_a(\sigma)=\prod_{\ell\in a}\sigma_\ell,
 \qquad
 W_4(\sigma)=\sum_{a}\chi_a(\sigma).
\]
A direct closed-walk count gives the exact moment identities
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\operatorname{tr}(A_\sigma^2)=480,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\operatorname{tr}(A_\sigma^3)=24\sum_{\ell}\sigma_\ell,}
 \label{eq:pass4461-low-moments}
\end{equation}
and, crucially,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\operatorname{tr}(A_\sigma^4)=12000+8W_4(\sigma).}
 \label{eq:pass4461-fourth-moment}
\end{equation}
If $f_4$ is the fraction of apartments with negative parity, then
$W_4=1620(1-2f_4)$ and therefore
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\operatorname{tr}(A_\sigma^4)=24960-25920f_4.}
 \label{eq:pass4461-frustration-law}
\end{equation}
The coefficient $25920=16\cdot1620$ is used here only as the combinatorial
coefficient in the trace identity; its numerical equality with
$|\operatorname{PSp}(4,3)|$ is not a group-theoretic inference.  Likewise,
Pass~4434 remains correct that fourth-moment pressure is a generic even-moment
feature of signed graphs.  The $W(3,3)$-specific statement is instead that the
\emph{entire variable part} of the fourth moment is exactly the apartment-parity
observable of the building.  This is the precise geometric content behind the
length-four signal without reintroducing the withdrawn claim that $\mu=4$
causes spectral optimization in general.

The apartment family itself has a compact Bose--Mesner certificate.  Let
$H\in\{0,1\}^{40\times1620}$ be line--apartment incidence and $A^\ast$ the dual
line-collinearity adjacency matrix.  Each line lies in $162$ apartments, each
intersecting pair of lines in $27$, and each disjoint pair in $6$.  Hence
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{HH^T=156I+21A^\ast+6J,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\operatorname{spec}(HH^T)=648^1\oplus198^{24}\oplus72^{15}.}
 \label{eq:pass4461-apartment-gram}
\end{equation}
This gives an explicit point/line-dual carrier for the apartment observable,
even though the projective point and line actions should not be conflated.

\paragraph{Exact random-line baseline.}
The $1620$ apartment supports are $1620$ distinct four-subsets of the $40$ line
bits.  Under independent Rademacher line signs the apartment parities are
therefore distinct Walsh characters.  Orthogonality immediately gives
\[
 \mathbb E W_4=0,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E W_4^2=1620,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E f_4=\frac12,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{sd}(f_4)=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{1620}}.
\]
Pass~4462 computes the higher null moments by exact symmetric-difference counts:
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \mathbb E W_4^3=12960,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E W_4^4=9891720.}
 \label{eq:pass4462-null-moments}
\end{equation}
Thus the line-signing null is not exactly Gaussian: its standardized skewness is
$0.1987615979\ldots$ and its excess kurtosis is
$0.7691358025\ldots$.  The corresponding fourth-trace null has
\[
 \mathbb E\operatorname{tr}(A_\sigma^4)=12000,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}\!\left(\operatorname{tr}(A_\sigma^4)\right)=103680.
\]
This is an analytic control distribution, not a search result.  Its purpose is
to make the baseline mandatory and reproducible before any optimized line
signing is called exceptional.

\paragraph{Apartment-parity tomography.}
Encode the line signs by $b\in\mathbb F_2^{40}$ through
$\sigma_\ell=(-1)^{b_\ell}$.  The full apartment-parity record is then simply
\[
 y=H^Tb\in\mathbb F_2^{1620}.
\]
Pass~4463 row-reduces the exact incidence matrix and obtains
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}H=39,}
 \qquad
 \boxed{\ker(H^T)=\langle\mathbf 1_{40}\rangle.}
 \label{eq:pass4463-tomography}
\end{equation}
Hence the $1620$ apartment parities determine all $40$ line signs up to the one
unavoidable ambiguity $\sigma\mapsto-\sigma$.  Global reversal is necessarily
invisible because every apartment contains four lines; the rank theorem says
there is no second hidden signing gauge.  Equivalently, the apartment-parity
image is a binary $[1620,39]$ code.  A one-line generator has Hamming weight
$162$, while the XOR of two line generators has weight $270$ for intersecting
lines and $312$ for disjoint lines.  Reducing
Eq.~\eqref{eq:pass4461-apartment-gram} modulo two gives the especially compact
identity
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{HH^T=A^\ast\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_2.}
 \label{eq:pass4463-mod2-gram}
\end{equation}
This is a finite-geometric identifiability theorem, not a statement that an
optical experiment automatically measures all $1620$ apartment products.
