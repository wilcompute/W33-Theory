# Pass 2828: Distance-Four Doubled Support Observer

The shortest six-operation preset observer is exact but has trajectory-code minimum distance one.  The next search concatenates two members of the eight-word shortest family and treats the resulting 12-operation sequence as one uninterrupted preset experiment.

There are

\[
8\times8=64
\]

ordered concatenations.  Their minimum-distance profile is

\[
\boxed{d_{\min}=2\text{ for }40,\qquad d_{\min}=3\text{ for }16,\qquad d_{\min}=4\text{ for }8.}
\]

Thus exactly eight doubled observers reach distance four.

## Canonical distance-four word

One canonical choice is the ordered pair of shortest-word indices `(0,5)`:

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{CX}_{p\to f},F_p,Z_p,F_p,Z_p,\mathrm{CX}_{p\to f},
\mathrm{CX}_{f\to p},F_p,Z_p,F_p,Z_p,\mathrm{CX}_{f\to p}
}.
\]

Observing the initial support and the support after all 12 prefixes gives

\[
13\times4=52
\]

binary trajectory bits.  The resulting 81-word code has

\[
\boxed{d_{\min}=4},
\]

with 18 state pairs at distance four.

Therefore nearest-neighbor decoding guarantees:

\[
\boxed{\text{detection of up to 3 arbitrary support-bit errors}}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{\text{correction of 1 arbitrary support-bit error}.}
\]

This is a single uninterrupted diagnostic sequence; it does not require resetting the frame between two experiments.

## Exact family boundary

The eight distance-four ordered pairs are

\[
(0,5),(0,6),(1,2),(1,3),(4,5),(4,6),(7,2),(7,3).
\]

The theorem is exhaustive inside the `8 x 8` family generated by concatenating shortest injective words.  It does not claim that length 12 is globally shortest among all distance-four observers, nor that 52 raw bits are the minimum distance-four telemetry payload.

## Architectural consequence

The hardware now has two exact operating modes:

- **fast/noiseless:** six operations, eight selected support taps, 81-entry direct ROM;
- **protected:** twelve operations, full 52-bit trajectory, nearest-neighbor decoding with one-bit correction.

The remaining engineering target is to compress the 52-bit protected trace while preserving distance four and to synthesize the nearest-neighbor decoder against actual detector error statistics.
