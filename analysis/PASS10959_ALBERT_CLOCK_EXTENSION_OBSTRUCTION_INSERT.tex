\subsection{Pass 10959: one Albert 16 cannot carry the full clock; $32=16+16$ is minimal}

Restricting every irreducible complex character of $GL(2,3)$ to the frozen
clock $C_8=\langle g\rangle$ gives an exact central-odd law:
if the central $-I$ acts as scalar $-1$, then the odd Fourier
multiplicities obey
\[
m_1=m_3,\qquad m_5=m_7.
\]
The exact Albert half-turn $U$ of Pass~10958 has only two distinct admissible
$\mu_8$ phase-lift spectra compatible with $g^4=-I$: eight copies of
$\{1,5\}$ or eight copies of $\{3,7\}$.  Exhausting all $25$
central-odd degree-$16$ character combinations proves that neither spectrum
extends to a full $GL(2,3)$ representation.

The minimal completion is dimension $32$.  Combining the two conjugate
Albert phase lifts gives multiplicity eight on each odd $C_8$ character,
exactly the restriction of eight copies of the faithful four-dimensional
signed $D_4$ representation from Pass~10955.  Over
$\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)$ an explicit invertible $32\times32$ matrix $J$
intertwines
\[
\operatorname{diag}(\zeta_8^{-1}U,\zeta_8 U)
\quad\text{with}\quad
R_4(g)^{\oplus8},
\]
so transporting $R_4^{\oplus8}$ supplies a faithful full $GL(2,3)$ action
on the doubled Albert vector space.  This is a finite complex-representation
result; no Spin(9), Spin(10), Jordan-product, or physical Dirac identification
is claimed.
