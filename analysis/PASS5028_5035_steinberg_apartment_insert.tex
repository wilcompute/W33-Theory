\textbf{COVER-CHAMBER PROJECTOR AND APARTMENT FRAME (Passes 5028--5035).}
The $160$ incident point--line covers are the chambers of the $C_2$ building of $W(3,3)$. If $\ell(f,g)$ is gallery distance, define
\[
R_{fg}=(-1)^{\ell(f,g)}3^{4-\ell(f,g)}.
\]
Its five values are $81,-27,9,-3,1$, and exact multiplication gives $R^2=160R$ with zero panel sums and trace $160\cdot81$. Thus $P_{\rm St}=R/160$ is the rank-$81$ chamber-cycle projector. Together with Pass~5023 this is the canonical W33 Hodge $H_1$ carrier, now realized inside the flag--flag coherent-algebra corner.

For the signed chamber-by-apartment matrix $X$ and unsigned apartment-by-cover matrix $Y$,
\[
XX^\top=160P_{\rm St},\qquad \operatorname{rank}X=81,
\]
so the $1620$ apartments form a tight frame for the protected $81$-space. Also $\operatorname{rank}Y=160$ and
\[
\operatorname{spec}(Y^\top Y)=0^{40}\oplus40^{81}\oplus72^{15}\oplus144^{15}\oplus(243-9\sqrt{409})^{24}\oplus(243+9\sqrt{409})^{24}\oplus1296^1.
\]
Hence the same $81$ is already the eigenvalue-$40$ block on the full $200$-cover carrier.

The $1620$ apartments form one $PSp(4,3)$ orbit, with setwise stabilizer orders $16$ in $PSp$ and $32$ in $PGSp$. Their line-incidence matrix obeys
\[
B^\top B=156I+21A_{\rm line}+6J.
\]
The Levi graph and pendant-cover support graph have critical group
\[
(\mathbb Z/4)^6\oplus(\mathbb Z/40)^{22}\oplus\mathbb Z/160,
\]
while full edge subdivision gives
\[
(\mathbb Z/2)^{52}\oplus(\mathbb Z/8)^6\oplus(\mathbb Z/80)^{22}\oplus\mathbb Z/320.
\]
Finally the cube-complex rational homology representation is
\[
H_0=\mathbf1,\qquad H_1=\mathrm{St}_{81},\qquad H_2=\mathbf1\oplus V_{24}\oplus V_{15}.
\]
The separate $240$-object constructions remain distinct unless an explicit action-preserving map is supplied.
