# Exact fourth-order stability of the saved F32 regulus stabilizer

The saved ten-qubit stabilizer has objective 11 and 17 flat real tangent coordinates. The [exact Hessian](2026-11-14_regulus_stabilizer_variation.md) is positive on the other 2029 tangent coordinates. Eliminating their second-order response leaves a nonnegative quartic on all 17 flat coordinates. Its zero set is the union of 12 coordinate subspaces, with largest dimension six.

Use the normalized projective coordinate chart (psi + u + v)/sqrt(1 + ||u+v||^2), where u is flat and v has positive curvature. The stabilizer characters require the xor of the tangent labels in every monomial to vanish modulo the Lagrangian. No triple of flat characters has this property, so the pure cubic on the flat space vanishes. The homogeneous quartic restricted to u is the sum of squares of the quadratic Pauli expectations minus 11||u||^4. The terms linear in v in the cubic are sum_j g_j(u) v_j, with g_j quadratic and rational. If h_j is the positive quadratic coefficient of coordinate j, the minimizing second-order choice is v_j = -g_j(u)/(2h_j). Thus the reduced quartic is the flat quartic minus sum_j g_j(u)^2/(4h_j). This operation is the degree-four part of eliminating the positive directions by the implicit function theorem.
