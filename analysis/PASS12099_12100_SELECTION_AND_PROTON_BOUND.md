# Passes 12099–12100: one-loop selection and a proton bound

Claude track. Builds on 12086, 12091 and 12094.

## 12099 (negative): one loop does not pick the three-family shift

A Z3 holonomy on a circle has S(0) = 2ζ(5) and S(±1/3) = −(240/243)ζ(5). So V_B depends only on the fixed dimension,
and it is strictly decreasing in it.

Among the 242 shifts compatible with an SM grading, the fixed dimensions are {134, 92, 86, 80}:

- bosons pick 134;
- fermion domination picks 80;
- the three-family shifts (86) are picked by neither.

## 12100 (estimate): a bound, not a prediction

The X/Y bosons are odd under a Z2 Wilson line, so M_X = 1/(2R). The Super-K bound with α_G = 1/25 then gives:

- **M_X ≳ 6 × 10¹⁵ GeV**;
- **1/R ≳ 1.2 × 10¹⁶ GeV**.

This is an order-of-magnitude bound on the compactification scale. The framework does not fix R.
