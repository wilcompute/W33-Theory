\[

(u_A)_v=\mathbf1_{v\in A},\quad
(s_A)_v=
\begin{cases}
+1&v\in A\text{ a point},\\
-1&v\in A\text{ a line},\\
0&v\notin A.
\end{cases}
\]

For distinct cycles \(A,B\), put \(m(A,B)=|A_P\cap B_P|-|A_L\cap B_L|\). Direct multiplication shows

\[
\boxed{[C_A,C_B]=m(A,B)\bigl(u_A s_B^T-u_B s_A^T\bigr).}
