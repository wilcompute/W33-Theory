# BT1514 Toroidal Incidence Test

The 7/21/3 bridge supports a legal Szilassi-style incidence model:

- seven face classes;
- twenty-one edge/flag classes;
- six edges per face;
- two faces per edge;
- forty-two total face-edge incidences;
- three two-edge local sectors per face.

This is incidence-count compatibility only; it does not yet identify BT1504 classes with a concrete Szilassi realization.
