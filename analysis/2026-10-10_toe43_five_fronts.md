### Front 1 — Elementary-abelian F3^4 two-qutrit SIC endpoint

Producer `analysis/w33_20261010_toe43_field9_sic_search.py`; certificate `data/w33_20261010_toe43_sic_search.json`.

Use 40 projective nonidentity two-qutrit Pauli classes generated by tensor-product `X,Z` with exponents in \(\mathbb F_3^4\). A fiducial must obey \(|\langle\psi|W_p|\psi\rangle|^2=1/10\) for **every** \(p\). We executed 48 seeded independent trust-region nonlinear least-squares starts on 18 real state parameters with exact numerical normalization. No start reached zero; best objective (sum of 40 squared overlap-squared deviations) was **0.04444444444445**, with max deviation about 0.1. Its 90-frame purity variance is **0.00131687242798** (near rational \(8/6075\)), whereas zero is required for a SIC.

**Status: OPEN, not proved.** These nonconvex searches do not provide a global positive lower bound, algebraic impossibility proof, or exhaustive coverage. Cyclic dimension-nine \(Z_9^2\) WH fiducials are a distinct representation from this elementary-abelian two-qutrit question. Numerically close-to-zero is not itself a SIC; the best state and all 48 residuals are archived.

### Front 2 — A fully operational ten-MUB W33 purity measurement protocol (simulated)

Producer `analysis/w33_20261010_toe43_purity_mub_protocol.py`; certificate `data/w33_20261010_toe43_purity_shots.json`.

Construct the full 40 W33 isotropic lines on the 40 projective Pauli points, select a disjoint ten-line spread, and explicitly form nine rank-one stabilizer projectors per line using the commuting Pauli pairs. Numerical rank, completeness, and all 45 pairwise MUB relations are checked. Thus **ten experimentally measurable nine-outcome settings cover all 40 projective Pauli classes without incompatible measurements within a setting.**

Let \(Y_b,Y'_b\) be independent outcomes sampled from setting \(b\) on two copies of the same state. With \(m\) disjoint pairs per setting,

\[
\widehat{\mathcal P}=\sum_{b=1}^{10}\frac1m\sum_{s=1}^m \mathbf1\{Y_{bs}=Y'_{bs}\}-1,\qquad
\mathbb E\widehat{\mathcal P}=\mathrm{Tr}\rho^2.
\]

This follows from the complete-MUB two-design identity \(\sum_{b,j}p_{bj}^2=1+\mathrm{Tr}\rho^2\). Per setting, the four q-values satisfy the stronger W33 relation \(\sum_{p\in L}q_p=9\Pr(Y=Y')-1\). Exact enumeration over 81 pairs of outcome labels verifies that relation for every setting.

For \(m=160\) pairs per basis (total **3,200 state copies**), 1,500 Monte Carlo replications produced clean predicted versus measured standard errors:
- stabilizer pure state: predicted **0.074536**, observed **0.074813**, sample mean **0.999575**;
- maximally mixed state: predicted **0.078567**, observed **0.078681**, sample mean **0.114100** (true \(1/9\));
- half-depolarized state and calibrated 5% classical nine-outcome confusion were also simulated.

For \(5\%\) known white-confusion readout, \(p'=(1-\varepsilon)p+\varepsilon/9\), exact bias correction is

\[
\widehat{\mathcal P}_{\mathrm{cal}}
=\frac19+\frac{\sum_b\widehat c_b-10/9}{(1-\varepsilon)^2},
\]

with \(\mathrm{Var}=\sum_bc'_b(1-c'_b)/[m(1-\varepsilon)^4]\). This correction depends on **knowing** the readout model and \(\varepsilon\). An ideal entangling two-copy SWAP measurement uses fewer preparations for a given uncertainty (for the same 1,600 pairs, its standard error is \(\sqrt{(1-\mathcal P^2)/1600}\)); the advantage of the MUB protocol is availability of single-copy stabilizer measurements, **not a claimed shot-number advantage**.

### Front 3 — Explicit E6 ideal-CCZ schedule/layout co-design

Producer `analysis/w33_20261010_toe43_e6_layout_proxy.py`; certificate `data/w33_20261010_toe43_e6_hardware.json`.

For all **520** certified five-layer native 45-CCZ parallelisms, retain the same 27 signed registers and ideal nine-disjoint-gates-per-layer primitive. Declare two fixed physically motivated but **highly simplified** connectivity proxies:
1. 27 native-indexed optical channels in a row-major **3×9 square nearest-neighbor grid**, routing load of one three-site gate = sum of its two smallest terminal Manhattan separations (terminal MST).
2. The same registers on a **27-site nearest-neighbor ring**, load = 27 minus the largest gap between its cyclically ordered three sites.

For each schedule, compute the **peak per-layer sum** of nine triple routing loads; independently find the order of its five spreads minimizing native phase-sign switches. Exhaust all \(520\times120\) spread/order combinations and preserve all results.

| Quantity (proxy units) | TOE41 native schedule | Best available across 520 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 3×9-grid peak layer routing load | 74 | **69** |
| 27-ring peak layer routing load | 119 | **113** |
| Per-site positive/negative cubic pulse switches | 57 | **38** |

The minima need **not** occur in the same schedule. The grid-first, ring-second, sign-third lexicographic schedule gives peak (69,119) and 52 sign flips. 20 Pareto-nondominated schedule records were identified. The total routing proxy over all 45 terms is **fixed at 345** on the grid and **531** on the ring, independent of schedule; therefore there is no claim that scheduling reduces *total* network connection length. The proxy omits occupied intermediate sites, SWAP decomposition, actual three-qutrit native gates, pump engineering, OAM/time-bin losses, component placement, optical path delay, physical phase-gauge freedom and crosstalk. This is a **layout sensitivity benchmark**, not a realized photonic compiler.

### Front 4 — Equal-purity anisotropic noise classification

Producer `analysis/w33_20261010_toe43_equal_purity_noise.py`; certificate `data/w33_20261010_toe43_noise_fingerprints.json`.

Construct four channels acting on \(|00\rangle\), all yielding *exactly* \(\mathrm{Tr}\rho^2=1/2\):
- isotropic depolarization with \(\eta=\sqrt{7/16}\), output \(\eta|00\rangle\langle00|+(1-\eta)I/9\);
- 50:50 random-unitary dephasing between \(|00\rangle\) and a basis state;
- 50:50 random-unitary mixture with a two-site superposition orthogonal to \(|00\rangle\);
- 50:50 random-unitary mixture with an entangled three-site state orthogonal to \(|00\rangle\).

All random-unitary channels use an explicit normalized Householder reflection \(U\), with \(U|00\rangle=|\phi\rangle\) and \(U^\dagger U=I\). The map \(\mathcal E(\rho)=\frac12\rho+\frac12U\rho U^\dagger\) is CPTP.

The exact W33 \(-4\)-projector \(\Pi_{-4}=(A-12I)(A-2I)/96\) acts on the 40-component Pauli *powers* \(q_p=2|\mathrm{Tr}(\rho W_p)|^2\). The spectral norms \(\|\Pi_{-4}q\|\) are:

| Noise family | \(\|\Pi_{-4}q\|\) |
|---|---:|
| isotropic depolarization | \(<10^{-15}\) |
| entangled three-site | 0.306186218 |
| two-site superposition | 0.530330086 |
| basis dephasing | 0.918558654 |

All four states have identical global purity and identical W33 deficit sum \(-45\), so the \(-4\)-sector adds **noise-pattern information beyond global purity**. There is a firm negative control: for **every pure input** \(|\psi\rangle\), isotropic depolarization obeys \(q_{\mathrm{out}}=\eta^2q_{\mathrm{pure}}\), so \(\Pi_{-4}q_{\mathrm{out}}=0\) despite mixedness. Hence the spectral norm is **not a standalone purity witness**, nor a complete noise-channel classification. Measurement noise/finite-sample confidence not yet assigned.

### Front 5 — Independent SU(9)/84 gauge-vector Coleman–Weinberg audit

Producer `analysis/w33_20261010_toe43_e8_vector_cw_audit.py`; certificate `data/w33_20261010_toe43_e8_one_loop_audit.json`.

Use the existing repository's full \(\Lambda^3\mathbb C^9\) Cartan-trivector embedding from Pass11681, but **independently** construct all 80 Frobenius-orthonormal anti-Hermitian \(\mathfrak{su}(9)\) generators. For unit Frobenius-norm vevs \(T\), define the positive real gauge-vector mass Gram matrix

\[
Q_{ab}=\operatorname{Re}\langle G_a\!\cdot T,G_b\!\cdot T\rangle
\qquad(a,b=1,\ldots,80).
\]

It is proportional to physical squared vector masses only once the gauge coupling and field normalization are fixed. We diagonalized Q at four Witting-ray Cartan representatives and eight seeded generic Cartan vevs:
- Witting: exactly **24 zero eigenvalues** each, agreeing with \(\mathfrak{su}(3)^3\) dimension 24;
- Generic Cartan: **0 zero eigenvalues** in all eight cases;
- \(\mathrm{tr}\,Q\) and \(\mathrm{tr}\,Q^2\) are constant across all 12 samples to \(<2\times10^{-14}\) in the \(\mathrm{tr}\,Q^2\) test;
- The dimensionless **vector-only** logarithmic shape \(F(T)=\sum_{a=1}^{80}e_a^2\log e_a\), \(e_a=\lambda_a(Q)\), is not constant. All four tested Witting rays give \(F=-6\log3=-6.591673732\), explicitly anticipated by Pass11884–11886 and subsequently reproduced in its landed implementation. The maximum variation across the 12 TOE43 samples is **0.3593072**.

At the usual one-loop vector level, \(\overline{\mathrm{MS}}\)/Landau-gauge normalization is proportional to \((3/64\pi^2)\sum_a m_a^4[\log(m_a^2/\mu^2)-5/6]\). Thus **holomorphic invariant-degree 12 does not protect the entire nonholomorphic genus-two shape modulus from one-loop gauge contributions**; the phase symmetry of a *common overall rephasing* of T remains a distinct issue. The entire gauge-, scalar-, ghost-, fermion- and counterterm-complete potential may differ. Numerical ray sampling does **not** prove the Witting rays are global quantum vacua, nor that CP is broken. The parallel Pass11884–11886 report claims global selection based on five randomized optimizer/polishing runs and finite local-direction checks; those are meaningful numerical evidence, **not a rigorous global minimization theorem or positive-definite full quantum Hessian**. Its heavy-scalar proportionality claim has not been independently rederived here, and charged fermions could alter the selection.

A separate exploratory check of 56 mixed sextic contractions \(\mathrm{SU}(9)\)-invariant under ordinary tensor contractions found **no Cartan shape variation** in its sampled candidates. This is **not** a proof that all sextic contractions are constant or a substitute for the loop calculation.

### External research / limits

The complete MUB purity relation is a standard projective-2-design result; this research concretely instantiates it in native W33 and tests a noise calibration model rather than claiming it as a newly discovered quantum-estimation theorem.

The generic one-loop effective-potential formula with gauge-boson constant 5/6 and the need to include scalar/fermion contributions are conventional, as discussed in Phys. Rev. D 107, 015016 (2023), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.107.015016 . Physical vacuum identification additionally requires gauge-/scheme-aware analysis.

Standard dimension-nine Weyl–Heisenberg SIC literature, e.g. Bengtsson's review at https://doi.org/10.1007/s10701-017-0078-3, largely describes the cyclic \(\mathbb Z_9^2\) representation. That does **not** settle the elementary-abelian \(\mathbb F_3^4\) endpoint.
