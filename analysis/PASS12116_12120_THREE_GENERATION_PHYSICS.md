## 12116: three quark generations

**Method.** A second order-3 Wilson line was added to the SO(10) three-generation models, using the original non-SUSY
engine (60 consistent candidates, 19 of which load).

**Result.** 4 models have:

- gauge group **SU(3) × SU(2) × SU(2) × U(1)s**;
- **net 3 quark doublets (3,2)** and **net 6 colour-antitriplet singlets**, i.e. three u^c-type plus three d^c-type;
- no tachyons, and cancelled anomalies.

**Scope.** These are three-generation **quark** spectra. Leptons are partly vector-like and hypercharge is not yet
identified, so they are not a full Standard Model.

## 12117: Bose–Fermi count

- The cosmological constant itself is not computed.
