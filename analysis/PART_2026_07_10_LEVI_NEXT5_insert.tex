\subsection{Fourier rank theorem, trade-module bridge, and executable middleware}
\label{sec:levi-next5}

Let $M_q$ be the point--line incidence matrix of $W(3,q)$ over $\mathbb F_2$,
where $q$ is an odd prime power, and put
$A_P=M_qM_q^T$, $A_L=M_q^TM_q$. A central-translation Fourier
decomposition gives one affine-plane block and $q-1$ nontrivial additive-
character blocks. On a nontrivial block, Fourier conjugation sends the point
operator to
\[
Y\longmapsto Y+Y^T\qquad(Y\in\operatorname{Mat}_q),
\]
so its rank is $q(q-1)/2$. The transformed incidence columns span
$\operatorname{Sym}_q$, of dimension $q(q+1)/2$, while their Gram form has
rank $q$ because off-diagonal terms cancel in characteristic two. The
trivial affine-plane block has ranks $q^2+1$, $q^2+q+1$, and $q+1$,
respectively. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{rank}_2M_q=\frac{q(q+1)^2+2}{2},\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_2A_P=\frac{q(q^2+1)}2+1,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_2A_L=q^2+1.
\]
Together with $D_q^3=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&J\\J&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$
and $D_q^4=0$, this proves
\[
D_q\sim J_4^{\,2}\oplus J_3^{(q^3+2q^2+q-4)/2}\oplus J_1^{q(q-1)^2/2},
\]
with no $J_2$ blocks, for every odd prime power $q$.

\[
L_{-4}/2L_{-4}\cong\mathbf1\oplus U_{14}^-

the quotient is faithful and irreducible, and the intertwiner space to the
$U_{14}^-$ summand of line homology is one-dimensional with invertible
nonzero element.
