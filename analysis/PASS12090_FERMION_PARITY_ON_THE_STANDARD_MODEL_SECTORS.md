# Pass 12090: fermion parity on the Standard-Model sectors

Claude track. Builds on 12087 and 12089.

## Results

Of the 15 split elements sigma of the flag-built Standard-Model subspace:

- **5 are clean.** The gauge and X/Y sectors are even, and exactly **4 sectors of each matter type** are odd. That gives
  four complete fermionic copies of a generation, Q, (u, e), (d, N) and L, each with its conjugate. The bosonic
  leftovers are 1 Q, 1 (u, e), 6 (d, N) and 6 L sectors, the last being Higgs-like doublets.
- **The other 10** make the X/Y sector odd.

## Reading

These are the right kinds of fields with the right statistics. But there are **four** families (5 = 4 + 1 from the
commutant SU(5)'), not three, and they are vector-like. Chirality and the family number need the orbifold projection.
