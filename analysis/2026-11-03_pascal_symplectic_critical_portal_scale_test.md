## Scientific verdict and falsifiable next developments

This pass unifies Pascal counting, symplectic W33 incidence, a local line-variance kinetic action, lattice Green functions and a *candidate* relative-scale mechanism in a single quantitative workbench. It does **not** unify the physical interactions, select d=4, produce a Lorentzian action, derive c, ħ, G, gauge couplings, particle masses or chirality.
