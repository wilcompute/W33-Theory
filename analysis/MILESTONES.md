# Release milestones — criteria, not momentum

*Releases fire when gates close, not when a round feels productive. Gates may be overtaken by results; when that happens, replace the gate and say so.*

## v1.4-characteristic-and-length-three-rung — shipped

Passes 445–452 close or sharply replace the next gates.

1. **The exponent-9 companion PDS and q=5 orbit question — resolved by Passes 445–447.** The known `(27,10,1,5)` graph also has a flat section-plus-centre PDS realization in the exponent-9 extraspecial group. At q=5 exact Burnside gives 20,592 section orbits, and a 400-sample census gives 396 spectra; orbit and spectrum classifications are nearly injective rather than small.
2. **Characteristic-primary first nilpotent rung — closed exactly by Pass 448.** For the Z/9 flat Heisenberg graph, direct 3-adic elimination gives exponent counts `(99,154,162,80,10,205,0,11,7)`, total valuation 1916, and the complete welded critical group. A symbolic all-length characteristic-primary formula remains open.
3. **A useful q=5 nonlinear taxonomy — closed by Pass 449.** All 625 homogeneous binary cubic sections fall into five exact spectrum-and-Smith classes indexed by cubic factorization type. The zero section and 24 nonzero pure cubes are spectrally and integrally indistinguishable from flat. This is a structured subfamily theorem, not a replacement for the full 20,592-orbit census.
4. **Central Fourier formalization — advanced by Pass 450.** Lean now contains finite convolution, character-eigenvector, scalar-cancellation, and conductor-arithmetic scaffolds, integrated into the W33 package without `sorry` or custom axioms. The full Heisenberg irreducible representation and integral lattice remain open.
5. **Blind hardware custody — advanced by Pass 451.** A hash-bound exact-arithmetic packet freezes calibration format, commitments, predictions, reveal, threshold, and endpoint; the 96-sample synthetic rehearsal is perfect. A measured optical transfer matrix and unchanged blind holdout remain the experimental gate.
6. **Length-three Hjelmslev geometry — closed by Pass 452.** AHG(2,Z/27Z) is built explicitly with Gram spectrum `972^1,243^8,81^72,27^648`; the nested Gram law identifies all three conductor depths with geometric resolution levels.

The permanent Passes 448–452 workflow regenerates all certificates, runs focused regressions, and compiles the integrated Lean package in a separate pinned job.
