\subsection*{Passes 7517--7524: the projective $E_8$ Weyl action inside the Monster-local carrier}

The classical exceptional-group identification by itself is not new: one has
\[
W(E_8)\cong 2.O_8^+(2).2.
\]
The new question is whether the \emph{current} $E_8$ action on the $1120$ $A_2$
subsystems and $2240$ Eisenstein $W(3,3)$ leaves can be located explicitly inside
the $3360$-object $Q^+(7,3)$ triality carrier of Pass7465 and the Monster-local
carrier of Pass7501.

Reconstructing the eight simple reflections gives a faithful projective action
on the $1120$ $A_2$ subsystems of order
\[
\boxed{348364800=|W(E_8)/\{\pm I\}|}.
\]
The $2240$ leaves split into the two generator-family halves of size $1120$.
Every simple reflection fixes the singular-point family as a type and exchanges
the two generator families.  Hence the induced type action is $C_2$, with kernel
\[
\boxed{174182400=|O_8^+(2)|}.
\]
Thus the current objects realize, rather than merely quote,
\[
\boxed{W^+(E_8)/\{\pm I\}\cong O_8^+(2)},
\qquad
\boxed{W(E_8)/\{\pm I\}\cong O_8^+(2):2}.
\]
Combined with the $D_4(3)$ triality closure and the Monster-local port, this yields
the nested carrier chain
\[
\boxed{
O_8^+(2):2
< O_8^+(3):S_4
< (3^2{:}2\times O_8^+(3)).S_4
< \mathbb M.
}
\]
The projective $E_8$ subgroup uses only the outer involution that swaps the two
generator families; the ambient $D_4(3)$ symmetry enlarges this to the full outer
$S_4$.  The index of the projective $E_8$ image in the $3360$ ambient group is
\[
\boxed{341172=2^2\,3^8\,13}.
\]

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
The abstract $O_8^+(2)$ exceptional isomorphism is classical and was already
partly discussed in the historical Pass1301.  The new theorem is the explicit
placement of the current $A_2$/W33-leaf action in the current $Q^+(7,3)$ carrier
and then in the Monster-local subgroup action.  It implies no moonshine or
physical parameter relation by itself.
