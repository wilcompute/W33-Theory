# Matter 81 under the explicit E8 Pauli243 subgroup

The repeated number 81 is not an objectwise identity.

For the trinification realization
\[
27=(3,\bar3,1)\oplus(1,3,\bar3)\oplus(\bar3,1,3),
\]
the internal Heisenberg shift has nine 3-cycles on the 27 weight rays, while its clock gives the corresponding qutrit phases. Hence
\[
27\downarrow H_{27}^{\rm int}=9\,\mathcal H_3.
\]
Tensoring with the external physical qutrit gives
\[
\boxed{(27,3)\downarrow 3_+^{1+4}=9\,\mathcal H_9}.
\]

The exact group character is supported only on the common center:
\[
\chi(1)=81,\qquad \chi(z)=81\omega,\qquad \chi(z^2)=81\omega^2,
\]
and
\[
\chi(g)=0
\]
for all 240 noncentral elements.

Projectively, the 81 matter weight rays have nine orbits of size nine. In contrast,
\[
G/Z(G)\cong\mathbf F_3^4
\]
is an 81-element regular translation G-set with one orbit of size 81. Therefore no G-equivariant bijection exists between matter rays and Pauli phase-space points.

The correct relation is representation-theoretic:
\[
81_{\rm matter}=9\times\mathcal H_9,\qquad
81_{\rm operators}=\mathbf F_3^4.
\]
