\subsection*{Passes 5957--5964: physical prediction claims downgraded to ansatz tier}

A source-code audit of the Pass~5913--5956 physical producers separates exact
finite arithmetic from an independently derived observable map.  The following
items are \emph{not} presently certified as physical predictions.

\begin{itemize}
\item The claimed $L_\infty$ mass hierarchy is not an evaluated
Maurer--Cartan solution: the producer records a nonzero residual $k-\lambda=10$
and imports the quoted mass-denominator formulas instead of computing verified
higher brackets.
\item The claimed four-dimensional Weyl law is circular: the refinement
function is defined with multiplicity factor $n^4$ and the same $n^4$ scaling is
then used to infer $d=4$.  No physical four-volume follows from that definition.
\item The electron packet
$2\cdot49\cdot17\cdot16\cdot13=346528$ is exact arithmetic, but no mass/Yukawa
operator forces its reciprocal to be $m_e/m_t$; the reported ``sigma'' was not
obtained from a statistical uncertainty model.
\item The $1818\,\mathrm{MeV}$ Yang--Mills producer explicitly solves an
effective QCD scale from the target value, so the target is back-solved rather
than predicted.
\item The neutrino producer first obtains a factor four too large, then replaces
$24$ by $6$ to hit $10{,}221{,}120$; the integer identity survives, not the mass
prediction.
\item The 45 tritangent-plane count and the octahedron spanning-tree count
$\tau(K_{2,2,2})=384$ survive exactly, but the maps
$r:=1/45$ and $m_{\rm scalar}:=m_H(384/15)$ are assumed rather than derived from
an inflationary action or a scalar pole equation.
\end{itemize}

Accordingly the Pass~5913--5956 physical claims are assigned
\textbf{ANSATZ/COMPARISON-ONLY} status.  Their exact combinatorial subclaims may
remain in the corpus.  Promotion to prediction status requires an independent
dynamics theorem that supplies the observable map without inserting the desired
scaling exponent, solving an input scale from the target, or repairing factors
after comparison with the target.
