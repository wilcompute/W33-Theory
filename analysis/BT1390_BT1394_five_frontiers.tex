\section{Five exact selector frontiers: modular, Fourier, apartment, and integral}
\label{sec:selector-five-frontiers-1390-1394}

Let $X$ be the $120$ line--matching selectors, let $H$ be a selector stabilizer, and let $\mathcal A=\operatorname{End}_H(\mathbb Q^X)$ be the $83$--dimensional orbital algebra.

\paragraph{Modular boundary.}
The fourteen primitive rational central projectors have denominator LCMs
\[
36,18,54,18,54,18,54,54,54,27,54,54,54,108.
\]
Consequently one projector localizes at $2$, none localizes at $3$, and all fourteen localize at $5$.  The exact radical-power dimensions are
\[
(45,16,0)\quad(p=2),
\qquad
(72,49,27,14,4,0)\quad(p=3),
\]
whereas the characteristic-five algebra is split semisimple with regular factor census
\[
1^7,\ 2^4,\ 3^9,\ 4^4,\ 5^5.
\]
The Loewy extensions are independently computed modular data; they are not inferred from characteristic-zero orbit sizes alone.

\paragraph{Six dual orbits.}
The complement $D_8\times C_2$ has a unique invariant line and complementary plane in $\mathbb F_3^3$.  In exact coordinates,
\[
L=\langle(1,0,1)\rangle,
\qquad
P=\langle(0,1,0),(1,0,2)\rangle,
\qquad
Q_P=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The six dual orbits are therefore zero, pure hinge charge, neutral square diagonals, neutral square axes, charged square diagonals, and charged square axes, of sizes
\[
1,2,4,4,8,8.
\]

\paragraph{Exact selector Fourier transform.}
A deterministic rational basis matrix $U\in GL_{120}(\mathbb Q)$ splits $\mathbb Q^X$ into isotypic dimensions
\[
1,2,2,4,4,8,8,2,4,12,12,24,32,5.
\]
It satisfies $U^{-1}U=I$ exactly and block diagonalizes the selector adjacency, shell operator, and minimum geometric splitter.  The maximum denominators of $U$ and $U^{-1}$ are $54$ and $3$, respectively.

\paragraph{Gauge-fixed Steinberg bridge.}
The hinge-selected $2160\times160$ apartment sheet is boundaryless and has rank $81$.  Composing it with the natural selector--rectangle incidence and either binary side/edge character gives four boundaryless $120\times160$ maps, each of rank
\[
\boxed{81}.
\]
Every one of the fourteen Mackey source sectors survives with its full source dimension.  The images overlap in the common $81$--dimensional Levi cycle sector.  Since the hinge and parity choices break full $G$--equivariance, this does not contradict
\[
\operatorname{Hom}_G(\mathbb Q^{120},E_4\mathbb Q^{160})=0.
\]

\paragraph{Integral commensurability.}
Let $O$ be the orbital $\mathbb Z$--order and let
\[
M=\mathbb Z^7\oplus M_2(\mathbb Z)^2\oplus M_3(\mathbb Z)^3
\oplus M_4(\mathbb Z)\oplus M_5(\mathbb Z)
\]
be the split order selected by the frozen rational matrix units.  Neither order contains the other.  For $L=O\cap M$,
\[
[M:L]=2^{38}3^{113},
\qquad
[O:L]=4,
\]
and
\[
2O\subset M,
\qquad
108M\subset O.
\]
The reduced-trace discriminant is
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{disc}(O)=2^{72}3^{226}}.
\]
Hence $O$ is nonmaximal at $2$ and $3$ and passes the discriminant maximality test at $5$.  No conductor into the selected $M$ is claimed because $O\not\subset M$.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
These are exact finite representation-theoretic and integral-algebraic statements.  They make no particle, hardware, laboratory, or literature-priority claim.
