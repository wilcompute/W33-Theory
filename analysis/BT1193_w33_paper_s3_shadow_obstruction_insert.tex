\begin{remark}[The $S_3$ route is active, but the abelian-shadow theorem is not yet proven]
\label{rem:s3-shadow-carrier-obstruction}
The $S_3$ port-transport layer is the current active source for the missing sheet
data.  On its own carrier it is exact: the $54$ pockets carry a $\mathbb Z_3$
label distribution $(17,11,26)$, and the $270$ transport edges have cocycle
profile $(201,33,36)$ with only generator $g_3$ shifting the sheet.  However, the
raw $Z_2$ voltage lives on the $45$-vertex, $720$-edge complement transport
graph.  Thus the $S_3$ package is a genuine refinement, but the statement that
raw $Z_2$ voltage is its abelian shadow requires a carrier map from the $720$
complement edges to the $270$ $S_3$ transport edges.  Until that map is built,
the paper records an obstruction rather than an abelian-shadow theorem.
\end{remark}
