# Grand-spin-zero Yukawa currents on the W33 monopole

The two projected Higgs directions and the four winding Weyl charge slots define a local angular matching problem. At each radius the scalar direction is the normalized horizontal projection used in the two-Higgs radial inverse. For each fermion lift, the embedded root gives its opposite magnetic component in the same isodoublet. This construction carries vacuum flavor lifts into the core. It supplies the angular contraction needed by a future calculation with dynamical radial spinors. [Grand-spin reduction](2026-11-13_monopole_grand_spin.md) · [Trial-packet Yukawa exchange](2026-11-13_monopole_yukawa_exchange.md)

In the north-pole chart let F be the native Yukawa matrix for the first fermion at pairing +1 and the second at −1. Let G be the matrix with the pairings exchanged using the root-related partners, with the two flavor slots in their original order. This convention puts the exterior QU^c matrix in G and the exterior LE^c matrix in F. Define the singlet and triplet matrices S = (F−G)/2 and T = (F+G)/2. The scalar Higgs direction has pairing zero.

For each isodoublet use the normalized spin–isospin tensors ε/√2 and σ·rhat ε/√2, with ε = iσ₂ and angular measure dΩ/(4π). Write the reduced radial coefficients as (u,v) and (p,q). The spin contraction before the overall factor 1/2 obeys

    [(u I+v σ·rhat) ε]^T ε [(p I+q σ·rhat) ε]
      = [(up−vq) I+(uq−vp) σ·rhat] ε.

In the north-pole frame the two terms pair opposite magnetic components. Contracting with the Yukawa matrix and restoring the factor 1/2 gives the source S(up−vq) + T(uq−vp). With independent flavor-dependent radial vectors, each matrix multiplies the corresponding bilinear. Simultaneous spatial and embedded gauge rotation transports this expression away from the pole. If the scalar is a monopole-isospin singlet, the source is in its grand-spin-zero channel. An opposite-helicity exterior pair selects one of F or G, with a sign fixed by the angular tensor convention. A regular origin solution has v and q subleading, so the leading source there samples S. [Lowest partial waves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.11207)

The two projected Higgs directions have zero computed root action at every sample on both background meshes. Thus the embedded-root angular stiffness of the two-Higgs trial frames vanishes there. The native Yukawa matrices both have full rank at the origin. On the 160-element mesh the smallest singular values of S are 1.05456 for QU^c and 0.11742 for LE^c. The triplet matrices vanish at the origin within 5×10⁻¹⁵. Near radius 0.0506 the lepton triplet norm reaches 7.174, while the singlet norm is 8.746. The two radial products can therefore contribute on similar scales once the subleading components of the spinors grow. The triplet coefficient describes the two-fermion contraction in the monopole background, with the external Higgs still an isospin singlet. It also checks the Pauli identity with complex coefficients and several axes. The flavor matrices and angular identity by themselves supply no winding amplitude. The incoming and outgoing radial coefficients still have to satisfy the coupled Weyl equations and the physical normalization conditions.
