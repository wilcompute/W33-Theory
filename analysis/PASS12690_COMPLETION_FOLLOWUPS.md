## (1) B's collider handle: a compressed T–B–T′ triplet whose splitting is y′

For equal Dirac masses M, the charge-2/3 masses are √(M² + m²/4) ± m/2 with m = y′v/√2. So **ΔM(T′ − T) = y′v/√2 exactly**, with the charge −1/3 state B at M in the middle. At y′ = 0.51:
- ΔM(T′ − T) = 88.8 GeV, for any M (1.65, 2, 3 TeV);
- B − T ≈ T′ − B ≈ 44 GeV < m_W, so the internal decays B → T W* and T′ → B W* are off-shell three-body decays with soft products.

The signature is a compressed vectorlike-quark triplet. Inverting, y′ = √2 ΔM/v. A ΔM resolution of 5 (2) GeV gives δy′ = 0.029 (0.011), enough to test B's radius-independent prediction y′ = 0.51 ± 0.02.

## (3) A's leptogenesis vs ACT-compatible inflation

A PQ-generated Majorana mass M_N = y_N f_a/√2 (f_a = 10¹¹ GeV) feeds negative running into the PQ quartic: b_N = −c·3y_N⁴/(16π² λ_σ). It must be over-compensated by the extra scalar to leave b ≈ +0.03 (Pass 12688). The cancellation factor |b_N|/b_needed, for c = 2:

| M_N | y_N | ξ = 1 | ξ = 10 | ξ = 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10⁸ GeV | 0.0014 | 0.01 | 10⁻⁴ | 10⁻⁶ |
| **10⁹ GeV** (Davidson–Ibarra) | 0.014 | **110** | **1.3** | 0.013 |
| 10¹⁰ GeV | 0.14 | 10⁶ | 10⁴ | 130 |

> **Result.** Thermal leptogenesis (hierarchical N₁, M_N ≳ 10⁹ GeV) inside completion A is natural only for **ξ ≳ 10**. At ξ ≈ 1 the extra scalar would have to cancel the N_R running to ~1%, so resonant or non-thermal leptogenesis would be needed. This ties A's inflation parameter to its baryogenesis route. The bound depends on c (an O(1) group factor) as c^{1/4}.
