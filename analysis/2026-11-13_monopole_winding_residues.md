# Light-channel residues of the W33 monopole resonances

Both near-threshold resonance pairs appear in the analytic continuation of the light-channel reflection matrix for the frozen real core. The reflection amplitudes use unit incident flux and are defined at radius 5 in a constant vacuum exterior. [Outgoing matching poles](2026-11-13_monopole_winding_poles.md)

At complex energy, the exterior basis contains massive decaying modes followed by outgoing and incoming light modes. Propagating it with QR factorization preserves the incoming quotient through the triangular factors. The matching solve sets the incoming amplitudes to the identity. Reversing the triangular factors then recovers the outgoing amplitudes in the fixed exterior basis. This back-substitution cancels the intermediate QR choices, so the reflected matrix is meromorphic in the energy disk of interest. Real-axis checks test unitarity at zero and at 0.002.

For a pole z, evaluate the contour mean of (E−z) times the reflection matrix at equally spaced points on a circle around z. This is a trapezoidal approximation of its residue. Circles with radii 0.00008 and 0.00004 use 12 and 16 points, respectively. The replay also extrapolates the symmetric Laurent difference h[S(z+h)−S(z−h)]/2 from two step sizes. The procedures use different evaluations of the reflection matrix and give independent quadrature and differencing checks. A simple outgoing pole can contribute at most one independent residue direction. [Contour methods for nonlinear eigenvalue problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.1580)

The norm of the residue is its single nonzero singular value. The corresponding right and left singular vectors identify the coupled incident and outgoing combinations. Numerical eigenspace basis coordinates by themselves are arbitrary under unitary rotation. In the triplet sector the charge-frame replay instead computes the weights of the incident D^c and conjugate H_u, and of the outgoing L and H_d. This is the magnetic orientation of the L block used in the pole solver. The charge-frame construction uses its own root-related lifts in the same coordinates as the numerical exterior basis. [Physical triplet flavor frame](2026-11-13_monopole_triplet_charge.md)

The quark and lepton-doublet residue norms are 0.000511992 and 0.001203439, respectively. The ratios to the corresponding pole widths −2 Im z are 1.02275 and 1.06139. At each pole there is one resolved singular direction. On the L block the squared incident weights are 0.89564 in D^c and 0.10436 in conjugate H_u. The squared outgoing weights are 0.27003 in L and 0.72997 in H_d. The two reflected-energy poles give the same grouped weights. These are normalized singular-vector projections and depend on the specified vacuum flavor decomposition. Cross sections would also include the regular reflection amplitude and its interference with the poles.

These are one-particle scattering quantities in the 80-cell finite-cut model. The interaction coefficient for the compact rotor still requires the coupled radial wavefunctions at both native Yukawa vertices and the scalar–gauge propagator.
