\subsection*{The ramified Clifford compiler lifts to a ternary $[45,12,6]_3$ cubic-holonomy code}

Let $T_{36}$ be the exact full-Clifford Hesse compiler.  Over
$\mathbf Q(\omega)$ it is a direct sum of twelve three-point Fourier
transforms and satisfies
\[
 T_{36}^{*}T_{36}=3I_{36}.
\]
Reduce its Eisenstein coefficients at the ramified prime
$\pi=1-\omega$.  Since
\[
 \mathbf Z[\omega]/(1-\omega)\cong\mathbf F_3,
 \qquad \omega\longmapsto1,
\]
each Fourier block collapses to an all-ones matrix.  The reduced compiler
$\overline T_{36}$ has
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbf F_3}\overline T_{36}=12,
 \qquad
 \dim\ker\overline T_{36}
 =\dim\ker\overline T_{36}^{T}=24,
 \qquad
 \overline T_{36}^{T}\overline T_{36}=0.
\]
Its twelve distinct column supports are triples which partition the ordinary
$36$ tritangents, three triples in each of the four noncentral Hesse
directions.

The compiler nullity is on safe-plane coordinates and is not identified with
the independent $24$-dimensional line kernel of the $27\times45$ Cartan-cubic
incidence matrix.  The exact bridge uses only
$\operatorname{im}\overline T_{36}$.  In the frozen Clifford gauge this
$12$-space lies in the ordinary-$36$ projection of the cubic line kernel.
The incidence submatrix on the distinguished fiber nine has rank nine, so
every reduced Fourier triple admits a unique fiber correction.

The twelve corrected relations each contain three ordinary and three fiber
tritangents.  They span
\[
 \boxed{R_{12}\subseteq\ker_{\mathbf F_3}C_{27\times45},
 \qquad R_{12}\cong[45,12,6]_3.}
\]
Exhaustion of all $3^{12}=531441$ words proves that the minimum shell consists
of exactly the $24$ nonzero scalar multiples of the twelve generator hexads.
Every ordinary tritangent occurs once across the basis and every fiber
tritangent occurs four times.  The generator Gram matrix has rank three and,
after grouping by Hesse direction, is
\[
 (J_4-I_4)\otimes J_3.
\]
More precisely, the fiber nine is the affine plane
$H_{27}/Z(H_{27})\cong AG(2,3)$ and the twelve correction supports are all
twelve affine lines, with coefficient $2$ on each line.  The ordinary parts
are disjoint triples, so after permuting coordinates
\[
 G=\left[,2A_{AG(2,3)}\ \middle|\
 I_{12}\otimes(1,1,1)\,\right],
 \qquad
 \operatorname{wt}(cG)=3\operatorname{wt}(c)
 +\operatorname{wt}(cA_{AG(2,3)}).
\]
This formula independently reproduces the complete enumerator.

\paragraph{Scope firewall.}
This is a finite arithmetic bridge in a fixed Clifford gauge.  The two
dimension-$24$ kernels remain different objects; no $W(E_6)$-invariance,
physical noise process, particle assignment, or global coding-theory novelty
is claimed.  In particular, the parameters $[45,12,6]_3$ also occur in a
published split-local-ring Gray-image code; equivalence with that unrelated
construction has not been tested.
