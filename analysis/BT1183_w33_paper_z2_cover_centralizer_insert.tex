\begin{remark}[The missing sheet coordinate and the $96=2\cdot48$ split]
\label{rem:z2-cover-centralizer96}
The raw transport voltage requires a binary lift of the $45$-point complement
transport graph.  Since that graph has parameters $(45,32,22,24)$, the minimal
sheet lift has $90=2\cdot45$ vertices.  This has the same structural form as the
BT748 fiber result: the full centralizer has order $96=2\cdot48$, with the
inner order-$48$ subgroup acting on each chirality half-fiber.  Thus both lanes
exhibit a $Z_2$ sheet over an inner sector.  The paper records this as a
compatible sheet pattern, not yet as an equality of the raw transport voltage
with the BT748 chirality bit; that equality requires a direct correlation test in
root-triple coordinates.
\end{remark}
