# The cubic del Pezzo quiver on the dual W(3,3) geometry

The directed nine-node quiver for a D3-brane probe of the cubic del Pezzo cone gives a concrete choice of 27 cubic single-trace monomials from the 45 tritangents of the cubic surface. Transport of this choice by the (E_6) Weyl group produces 40 supports. Their overlap geometry recovers the 45 tritangents, the 27 lines, and the cubic monomial support at every point of (Q(4,3)).

## The gauge-index selection rule

The input is the dictionary from bifundamentals (X_{ab}) to line classes in Eq. (3.34) of [Franco, Hanany and Kazakopoulos](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0404065). Use the Picard basis (H,E_1,\dots,E_6) with diagonal intersection form ((1,-1,\ldots,-1)). The three arrow blocks are (123\to456\to789\to123), with all nine arrows in each block. Each cubic closed trace takes one node from each block, for (3^3=27) triples. For comparison, enumerate all triples of pairwise intersecting lines. There are 45. The 18 triples outside the closed traces are precisely the six perfect matchings in each of the three arrow blocks. They have the same support pattern as the determinant terms in the (3\times3) matrix presentation of the (E_6) cubic. The trace rule imposes gauge-index contraction before any superpotential coefficients are assigned.

This identifies the published quiver's closed cycles with the 27 mixed triples in the repo's [Steiner trinification partition](2026-09-22_steiner_trinification_qpsi_normalizer.md). The earlier [Steiner quotient calculation](../docs/pass4870-steiner-w33-quadratic.html) gives (Q(4,3)) from the 40 classical Steiner triads. Here we obtain it from a directed quiver and a count of shared candidate interactions. [Dolgachev's account of the cubic surface and its Steiner triads](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/idolga/wp-content/uploads/sites/1334/2024/08/CAG.21.pdf) gives the classical incidence background.

## Orbit, orientation, and reconstruction

Reflect the line classes in (E_i-E_{i+1}\) for (1\le i\le5) and (H-E_1-E_2-E_3), and propagate the induced permutations on tritangents. The orbit has 40 supports. A fixed support shares 18 triples with 12 others and 15 triples with 27 others. Joining supports that share 18 gives an explicit graph isomorphism with the points of (Q(4,3)), the line-intersection graph of (W(3,3)). Independent triads distinguish the two polar point graphs. The overlap graph has 1,080 independent triads with zero common neighbors and 2,160 with two. The (W(3,3)) point graph has 2,880 with one and 360 with four.

For each tritangent, take the set of supports omitting it. In the explicit coordinates, that 16-point set is a hyperplane section of (Q(4,3)). These sets give all 45 hyperbolic sections, once each. Two distinct sections meet in four quadric points exactly when the corresponding tritangents share a cubic-surface line. All other pairs meet in seven. The graph joining sections that meet in four points has exactly 27 maximal cliques, all of size five. Each is the set of tritangents containing one line. Thus the quadric with a chosen point determines the (27\)-line/(45\)-tritangent incidence and the candidate cubic terms for that point. A term belongs to the support precisely when its section avoids the chosen point.

The Weyl stabilizer of a support has order (51{,}840/40=1{,}296). Arbitrary node permutations within blocks and cyclic permutations of blocks give 648 directed relabelings. The other 648 block and node permutations act on the 27 line labels by globally reversing the arrows. All 1,296 preserve line intersection and the cubic support, verified over every pair of lines. The Weyl action is faithful on the line configuration and the cubic-surface incidence group is (W(E_6)), so these exhaust the support stabilizer and the directed subgroup has index two.

This calculation uses formal single-trace candidate monomials. At special couplings some coefficients can vanish. The permutations of the support are candidates for symmetries of the couplings, and Weyl transport of a triangle support by itself supplies no equivalence of quantum theories. The superpotential construction and its dependence on the cubic surface are treated by [Wijnholt](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0212021).

## Reproduce

Run `python -B analysis/toe_20261115_cubic_quiver_steiner.py` with NetworkX installed. The [certificate](../data/toe_20261115_cubic_quiver_steiner.json) lists the input arrow dictionary, the 40 support masks, the quadric coordinates and the 45 hyperplane covectors. Bit (j) of a mask marks inclusion of tritangent (j), with zero-based indices. A covector vanishes at a quadric point when the corresponding tritangent is absent there. The script also recovers the 27 line stars by enumerating all maximal cliques of the section-intersection graph.
