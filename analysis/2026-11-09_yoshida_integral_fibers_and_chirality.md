## The matrix and all cones

The Yoshida coordinates are products of the nine positive-root forms in each $A_2^3$ subsystem, up to signs. This identifies the monomial matrix $M$ with the incidence matrix used in Pass 12634, with subsystem rows and root columns. The 40 products are defined in Section 6 of [Positive del Pezzo Geometry](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.13604v3#S6). Tropicalization keeps the exponents and discards the signs. The implementation uses $Q_i=M_i-M_{39}$ for the projective chart.

Doubled $E_8$ coordinates give a 36-element positive $E_6$ root set. Rank and closure are exact modulo 1009 because every nonzero square minor of a matrix of these rank-six roots has absolute value at most 512 by Hadamard's bound. The minimal building set consists of the connected flats. A maximal nested tree inside a connected flat is formed by choosing a relative hyperplane and independently taking a maximal nested tree in each of its components. The component ranks add to the hyperplane rank, which is one below the parent rank. Include the full flat as the tree root and discard that root in the projective fan. [Feichtner and Sturmfels, Matroid polytopes, nested sets and Bergman fans](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0411260)

This gives 750 proper connected flats and 142,560 maximal nested cones. Each source cone's projected rays have nonnegative coefficients in a single target face. The exact counts by cardinality of the supporting target ray set are 1,620 of size three and 140,940 of size four. Conversely, every target maximal cone is covered. For this direction, split its nonnegative orthant into the 24 coordinate-order chambers. Each chamber is generated by four zero-one suffix indicators. The calculation finds a single nested cone in which all four indicators have nonnegative **integer** preimages. The target ray matrix is saturated in its rational span on all 1,215 maximal cones. Thus every integral point of the Naruki fan lifts to an integral point of the projective nested-set fan.

The two cone types have 156 and 111 source maximal cones with full target support per target cone, respectively. Those counts include images of smaller rank. All 1,620 ridges are three-valent. Since the target maximal ray bases are saturated, the extra ray in each adjacent cone gives a primitive normal modulo the ridge. Their sum belongs to the integral span of the ridge, so the reconstructed fan balances with weight one. This supplies an exact support and lattice check alongside the original Naruki fan literature. [Ren, Shaw and Sturmfels, Tropicalization of Del Pezzo Surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.5651)

The real slice of a five-dimensional source cone over a generic target point is empty or a line segment or ray. Solve each slice by exact rational linear algebra, then glue endpoints with the same coefficients on the shared nested face. ## The global order-three obstruction carries chirality

The unprojectivized incidence matrix has Smith diagonal $1^{15},3$ in its nonzero part, in agreement with the published Yoshida lattice index. In the projective quotient this becomes $1^{14},3$. Thus the global image lattice has index three in its saturation, even though every cone has integral lifts. [Cueto and Deopurkar, Anticanonical tropical cubic del Pezzos](https://ananddeopurkar.org/papers/lines_on_tropical_cubics.pdf)

The certificate gives integral vectors $k,v$ with $Qk=3v$, and reduction modulo three shows that $v$ lies outside the image lattice. A class in the saturation quotient vanishes precisely when its reduction lies in the column space of $Q$ over $\mathbb F_3$, since that quotient is killed by three. Permute the 40 Yoshida rows by each root reflection and project again. Every reflected witness represents $-v$. An independent server calculation in a different root and subsystem ordering gives the same 36 inversions and the same modular rank drops.

The two nonzero classes therefore carry the determinant character of $W(E_6)$. They form the same two-element Weyl set as the two $PSp(4,3)$ orbits of pezzotopes. After choosing which primitive class labels one chirality, every double-six reflection that exchanges the two pezzotopes over a codeword also exchanges those labels. This is an equivariant label for the two chiralities of the combinatorial chamber model. An orientation of the label still requires a choice. No physical time-reversal operator or Standard Model symmetry breaking follows from this lattice calculation.

## The real regions and their facet labels

The second producer enumerates all 25,920 projective chambers of the $E_6$ reflection arrangement. It sends each root sign vector to the 40 signs of its Yoshida products and identifies a common sign reversal. There are exactly 432 patterns, with 60 source chambers per pattern. Each is realized by a real point of the marked moduli space. Signs cannot change on a connected component of the real very affine Yoshida variety. The published component count is 432, so the patterns give a bijective labeling of those components. Fixed sign conventions for the 40 products only translate the sign patterns. [Positive del Pezzo Geometry, Theorems 8.1 and 8.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.13604v3#S8)

For each sign pattern, take its 60 root chambers in turn. In positive simple-root coordinates assign a small parameter $t$ to a single simple coordinate, or to an adjacent pair, and set the rest to one. A positive root relative to that chamber is a nonnegative linear combination of simple roots. Its valuation is one exactly when its support is among the coordinates assigned $t$. The two arc types therefore give the indicator of an $A_1$ or $A_2$ flat. Under the Yoshida map these reach the relative interiors of type-(a) or type-(b) rays. The first arc gives a root facet and the second gives a cubical cusp facet.

The union over the 60 chambers gives exactly ten distinct root facets and five cusp facets for each sign pattern. These exhaust the published numbers of each kind. The ten root facets are exactly the ten unique triple intersections of the five cusp subsystems. The five cusps give a maximal partial spread for every region, and all 432 cusp sets are distinct. This identifies the actual real components with Claude's combinatorial pentads by their facet labels.

Determinant parity is constant among all 60 lifts of a region. The even and odd regions form sets of size 216. The two pentads over each even ten-line support have the same 20 covered points and opposite determinant parity. The unique root absent from both pentads' combined subsystem support is the root whose spread is disjoint from the codeword. Its signed action sends each real Yoshida sign pattern to its partner. Their projective Hamming distance is 20 in all 216 pairs. The two nonzero lattice torsion classes therefore also label the two chiralities of the actual real regions, after choosing one orientation.
