\section{Binary torsion filtration and the frame--octet coherent bridge}
\label{sec:bt1611-1615}

Let $M\in\{0,1\}^{540\times240}$ be the canonical frame--edge matrix,
$K\in\{0,1\}^{45\times240}$ the intrinsic $K_{4,4}$ octet matrix, and
\[
J=\frac12MK^{\mathsf T}\in\{0,1\}^{540\times45}.
\]
The elementary two-torsion of the frame cokernel is a $30$-dimensional
$\mathbb F_2PSp(4,3)$-module.  Exact orbit-span tests give the composition
series
\[
0<V_1<V_9<V_{10}<V_{16}<V_{30},
\]
with successive factor dimensions
\[
\boxed{1,8,1,6,14}.
\]
The $8$-factor has endomorphism field $\mathbb F_4$; the $6$- and $14$-factors
have scalar endomorphism field $\mathbb F_2$.

The exact-cover equations imply a useful limitation.  For every exact-cover
indicator $x$,
\[
M^{\mathsf T}x=\mathbf1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
J^{\mathsf T}x=8\mathbf1_{45}.
\]
Thus every four-packing has signature $32\mathbf1$, while the complementary
$300$-frame carrier has signature $40\mathbf1$.  The Bockstein signature is
therefore orbit-blind and cannot decide packing extendibility.

For the integral free-$15$ bridges, saturated coordinates give
\[
\operatorname{SNF}(C)=1^{10}\oplus3^5,
\qquad
\operatorname{SNF}(F)=1^{10}\oplus3^4\oplus6.
\]
The unique saturated map $Q$ satisfying $QR_C=R_F$ is integral with
\[
\boxed{|\det Q|=2}.
\]
Hence the signed orientation bridge differs from the unsigned bridge by one
and only one parity coset.

Finally, the octet action is the rank-three scheme
$\operatorname{SRG}(45,32,22,24)$ and
\[
J^{\mathsf T}J=66I+3A_{45}+6\mathbf J,
\qquad
\operatorname{spec}(J^{\mathsf T}J)=432^1\oplus72^{24}\oplus54^{20}.
\]
The two-fiber frame/octet coherent configuration has
\[
\boxed{32+3+5+5=45}
\]
relations.  The incidence $J_{fo}=1$ is one cross orbital of valencies $6$ and
$72$.  The coarse five-valued partition of $JJ^{\mathsf T}$ is not closed under
matrix multiplication; the full frame orbital algebra is required.

A reduced solver sidecar contains $270$ native XOR equations, thirty per color,
and reproduces the exact global rank increase $2100\to2340$.  These equations
strengthen the unresolved Hoffman nine-coloring instance but do not decide it.
