\subsection{The $A_8$ fixed-sector support is the two-qutrit Pauli support}
The order-three $E_8$ weld also closes the support-level form of an old
algebraic gap.  Index the 80 oriented root orbits by the nonzero vectors
$v\in\mathbb F_3^4$.  The exact certificate
\texttt{w33\_e8\_a8\_pauli\_support\_bridge.json} checks every one of the
$\binom{80}{2}=3160$ unordered pairs.  With $\Omega$ the induced symplectic
form, the complete census is
\[
\Omega=0:\ 1000\text{ pairs and no root sum},\qquad
\Omega=1:\ 1080\text{ pairs},\qquad
\Omega=2:\ 1080\text{ pairs},
\]
and every nonorthogonal pair has exactly three root sums, all in the unique
oriented orbit of degree $v+w$.  The standard tensor-Pauli grading of
$\mathfrak{sl}_9$ on two qutrits has the identical $1000/1080/1080$ census:
its bracket vanishes exactly for $\Omega(v,w)=0$ and otherwise has degree
$v+w$.  Thus the $E_8$ root-orbit addition support and the
$\mathbb F_3^4$ Pauli-grading support agree pair-by-pair.
The classical order-three $E_8$ grading has fixed algebra $A_8=\mathfrak{sl}_9$;
and Pelantov\'a--Svobodov\'a--Tremblay
(\texttt{arXiv:quant-ph/0510106}) prove that the tensor-Pauli fine grading of
$\mathfrak{sl}(p^2)$ has normalizer quotient
$\Sp(4,\mathbb F_p)\times C_2$.  The new result here is the exact root-orbit
support identification at $p=3$.  A Chevalley cocycle/order-three lift remains
to be fixed before equality of Lie structure constants is claimed.
