# Correction to Pass 1079: the frame orbitals are not all self-paired

Pass 1079 correctly found the three imprimitivity systems on the 540-frame action and correctly decomposed the degree-117 frame graph into seven orbitals. Its self-pairing test was wrong.

Pass 1082 reconstructs every ordered-pair orbital directly. For an orbital represented by `(f0,f)`, it tests self-pairing by the exact condition that some group element sends `f0 -> f` and `f -> f0`. The result is:

- 12 self-paired orbitals;
- 20 non-self-paired orbitals;
- 10 transpose pairs.

The outer `GSp(4,3)` action still has rank 22, but its ten fusions are not simply the transpose closure. The full inner and outer intersection tensors are generated by `analysis/w33_pass1082_frame_coherent_configuration.py`.

The original Pass-1079 GAP source remains in history as the record of the imprimitivity computation; its pairing conclusion must not be reused.
