\subsection{The $1080$ obstruction carrier: full Wedderburn decomposition and three explicit Steinberg copies}
\label{sec:20260901-obstruction-wedderburn}

The equivariant factorization of the depth-three obstruction carrier as
$27\times40$ has degree $1080$, orbital rank $59$, and point stabilizer order
$24$.  Its exact orbital algebra has center dimension $15$ and closes the
ordinary complex decomposition:
\[
\begin{split}
\mathbb C^{1080}\cong{}&1\oplus6\oplus2(15)\oplus3(20)\oplus2(24)\oplus30\\
&\oplus2(30\oplus\overline{30})\oplus(40\oplus\overline{40})
\oplus(45\oplus\overline{45})\\
&\oplus3(60)\oplus3(64)\oplus3\,\mathrm{St}_{81}.
\end{split}
\]
The three non-rational central factors---of degrees $30$, $40$, and $45$---all
split over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$.  In particular the Eisenstein field appears
at the full obstruction-module level rather than from a matching count.

The Steinberg isotypic component has rank $243$.  A deterministic symmetric
orbital operator $B$ has multiplicity-space eigenvalues $4,0,-4$, so with $E$
the central Steinberg projector the three rational primitive projectors are
\[
 P_+=\frac{B(B+4E)}{32},\qquad
 P_0=E-\frac{B^2}{16},\qquad
 P_-=\frac{B(B-4E)}{32}.
\]
They are pairwise orthogonal, each has rank $81$, and their sum is $E$.
Thus the three Steinberg copies are represented by explicit equivariant
projectors, not only by a character multiplicity.
