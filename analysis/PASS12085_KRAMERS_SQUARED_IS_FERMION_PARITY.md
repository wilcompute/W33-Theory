# Pass 12085: Kramers squared is fermion parity

Claude track. Builds on 12080 and 12084.

## Results

1. **In W(E8).** The 72 Kramers reversals satisfy e² = −1 (central; it acts as α → −α). The 1080 CPs satisfy e² = +1.
2. **Lifted to E8.** Every lift of −1 is an involution conjugate to the Chevalley involution, whose fixed algebra has
   dimension |Φ⁺| = 120. That is the **split** class (clocks fix dimension 136). So the square of any lift of a Kramers
   reversal is a split involution.
3. **The split class.** For every split class, 248 = so(16) (8 + 112 even roots) ⊕ **128**, and the 128 odd roots form
   one minuscule W(D8)-orbit: the half-spinor.

## Reading

An observer's time reversal squares to the involution that is +1 on so(16) and −1 on the spinor 128, a fermion-parity
operator. The corpus non-SUSY models use exactly this involution (12084), as in the SO(16)×SO(16) string, which is the
fermion-parity orbifold of E8×E8.
