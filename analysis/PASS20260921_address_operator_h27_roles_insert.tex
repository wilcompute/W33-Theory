\subsection*{The address and operator $H_{27}$ roles are distinct and executable}

In physical normal form $Z^aX^b\omega^c$, the regular address Heisenberg group has five distinguished order-three directions
\[
\langle\omega\rangle,\ 
\langle\omega X\rangle,\ 
\langle\omega Z\rangle,\ 
\langle ZX\rangle,\ 
\langle\omega^2ZX^2\rangle.
\]
Their $45$ right cosets form
\[
\operatorname{SRG}(27,10,1,5)=GQ(2,4)
\]
and map exactly to the $45$ tritangents on the complete-factorisation-frame carrier.

After adjoining the external phase $C_3$, slopes $\pm1$ over each base direction give ten directions in $H_{27}\times C_3$. Their $270$ right cosets are exactly the lifted matter cubics,
\[
270=45\cdot3!,
\]
with one of each external phase. The physical Clifford-648 preserves this address geometry, with line orbits $9+36$ and, when phase labels are fixed, instruction orbits
\[
27+27+216.
\]

The center-correct trinification Heisenberg group has a different role. On
\[
27=(3,\bar3,1)+(1,3,\bar3)+(\bar3,1,3)
\]
there is a basis chart $(m,q)$ with
\[
X_{\rm int}|m,q\rangle=|m,q+1\rangle,\qquad
Z_{\rm int}|m,q\rangle=\omega^q|m,q\rangle.
\]
Tensoring the external qutrit gives
\[
\boxed{\mathbb C^{81}\cong
\mathbb C^9_{\rm mult}\otimes\mathbb C^3_{\rm int}\otimes\mathbb C^3_{\rm ext}.}
\]
The central-product Pauli group acts as $I_9\otimes\mathcal H_9$, so its generated algebra and commutant are
\[
\boxed{I_9\otimes M_9(\mathbb C)},\qquad
\boxed{M_9(\mathbb C)\otimes I_9}.
\]

The two $H_{27}$ actions are not permutation-isomorphic: the center of the address action is fixed-point-free, while the operator center is scalar and fixes all basis rays projectively.
