\subsection*{Passes 5888--5895: exact W33 Ihara factorization and graph-identity firewall}

Passes~5880--5887 are superseded because they conflated the canonical
$W(3,3)$ collinearity graph, a separate signed $4$-regular 40-line mesh, and a
33-vertex circulant surrogate.  The canonical collinearity graph is
\[
  \operatorname{SRG}(40,12,2,4),\qquad |E|=240,
\]
with adjacency spectrum
\[
  12^1\oplus 2^{24}\oplus(-4)^{15}.
\]
Its Hashimoto operator therefore has $480$ directed-edge states, outdegree
$11$, and exact Ihara--Bass factorization
\[
\det(I-uB)=
(1-u^2)^{200}
(1-12u+11u^2)
(1-2u+11u^2)^{24}
(1+4u+11u^2)^{15}.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\operatorname{spec}(B)=
11^1\oplus 1^{201}\oplus(-1)^{200}
\oplus(1\pm i\sqrt{10})^{24}
\oplus(-2\pm i\sqrt7)^{15}.
\]
Thus the exact surviving equal-modulus statement is
\[
\boxed{\text{the 78 adjacency-induced nontrivial modes have }|\lambda|=\sqrt{11}.}
\]
The structural $\pm1$ modes and Perron mode are not part of this shell.

The same finite spectrum contains only four distinct nontrivial phases, with
multiplicities $24,24,15,15$, so no eigenphase-equidistribution theorem follows.
In particular Ihara data alone does not determine a physical free spectral
range, Fabry--Perot finesse, Shannon capacity, or absolute loss/Q.  Those require
an independent propagation/coupling model specifying delays or lengths,
dispersion, coupler amplitudes/losses, boundary conditions, and the map from the
dimensionless spectral parameter to physical frequency.

\paragraph{Graph-identity firewall.}
The symbols must remain distinct: the canonical $W33$ collinearity graph has
$(v,k,q_{\rm nb})=(40,12,11)$; the Pass~5706 signed 40-line mesh is a separate
$4$-regular object with $q_{\rm nb}=3$; and the Pass~5880 surrogate is the
33-vertex $4$-regular circulant with jumps $\pm1,\pm11$, which is not canonical
$W(3,3)$ and is not Ramanujan.  No theorem may be transported between these
objects by the label ``W33'' alone.
