\section{Certifiable predictive execution}\label{sec:bt3133-certifiable-predictive}

The adaptive controller must not convert the exact twenty-three-triangle panel into a hard
fault label once the physical symbols are noisy.  Over the complete universe of
$48{,}826$ no-fault, one-edge and two-edge $D_4$ hypotheses, the panel produces $46{,}284$
signatures: $44{,}848$ hypotheses are unique immediately and the remaining collision
classes have size at most three.  Pass~3133 therefore retains the full posterior through
all twenty-three observations.  For the explicit positive synthetic channels used in the
certificate, a batch log-domain update and the streaming normalized update agree within
$3.45\times10^{-15}$.

This distinction is operational.  Under the frozen prior
\[
 P_0=0.995,\qquad P_1=0.0045,\qquad P_2=0.0005,
\]
a transcript equal to a rare fault's noiseless signature may still have maximum posterior
at no fault when the detector is noisy.  The exact-symbol collision class is therefore an
optimization of the noiseless model, not the state variable of the physical controller.

\subsection{Independent certification of a rank-three magic branch}

A rank-three stabilizer group on six qubits defines an eight-dimensional accepted code.
The Pass~3134 certifier accepts three signed binary symplectic generators and independently
checks rank, commutation, projector Hermiticity and idempotence, trace eight, annihilation
of all nine single-error vectors, nonzero clean success, and non-stabilizerness of the
accepted clean output by the complete Pauli-expectation criterion.  The committed negative
control has rank three and trace eight but is rejected because its range is not contained
in the single-error complement.  The certifier is not a search: a parallel candidate is
not promoted until it passes this independent path.

\subsection{Spectral prediction and future-action state}

For conjugation-invariant $D_4$ noise, prediction decomposes into four one-dimensional
Fourier gains and one scalar two-dimensional block.  The fixed-point RTL uses Q1.15
approximations to
\[
 \frac{43}{50},\quad\frac{22}{25},\quad\frac9{10},\quad
 \frac{89}{100},\quad\frac{89}{100},
\]
with maximum coefficient error below $1.47\times10^{-5}$.  The same datapath updates the
nine-bit future-action causal state.  Likelihood multiplication and Bayesian normalization
remain explicit nonlinear operations; they are not hidden in the Fourier block.

\subsection{Streaming synchronization after one edit}

The per-tick index is the pair consisting of the omitted slot and the ordering of the three
already-present curvature pilots, hence a $4\times6=24$ symbol alphabet.  Assuming phase
was locked before the edit, exhaustive enumeration covers
\[
 12(23+24+1)=576
\]
single substitutions, insertions and deletions.  Of these, $565$ relock within two received
symbols and the remaining eleven within three.  Those eleven cases are precisely
insertions equal to the expected symbol.  Thus one edit is tracked with delay at most three
without another optical mode.  This is not blind acquisition and is not a multiple-edit
claim.

\subsection{The action-rate--distortion envelope}

Pass~3137 jointly varies the syndrome alphabet, zero through six chirality copies, raw
versus causal versus immediate-action retention, and the exact mean address-transport cost
of $1.275$ symplectic shears.  The frozen dimensionless model contains $105$ policies and
has eleven points on its lower convex envelope.  The envelope moves through no additional
route measurement, $V_4$ sensing, conjugacy-class sensing and finally full $D_4$ sensing.
Full nonabelian resolution is optimal only at the extreme low-distortion end; it is not the
unconditional default.

\subsection{Certified forgetting and recursive belief virtualization}

For the moderate positive prediction kernel, the Dobrushin coefficient is
$0.9969072165$ and Birkhoff's projective contraction coefficient is $0.9992044551$.
The resulting conservative uniform memory horizons are $1{,}487$ steps to total-variation
error at most $10^{-2}$ and $4{,}461$ steps to $10^{-6}$.  These are model-derived upper
bounds, not measured coherence times.

A raw post-base guest requires eleven fixed bits for $1{,}436$ class labels.  The exact
future-action quotient has $470$ states and needs nine.  Recursive virtual belief machines
therefore save two fixed bits per guest, or thirty-two bits across sixteen guests.  Updates
on different guest contexts commute because they act on different Cartesian-product
coordinates; the shared spectral predictor is read-only with respect to every context.

\subsection{When fewer generator collisions are worth a longer program}

The current computing ISA has $45$ generator collisions among $324$ labeled outgoing
edges; the minimum-collision computing set has $18$.  The reduction is exactly
\[
 \frac{45-18}{324}=\frac1{12}
\]
per dispatch.  At the measured mean program length $14.175585$, this avoids
$1.18129875$ collision exposures per program.  If one collision-resolution event costs the
same as one instruction, a lower-collision ISA may therefore spend $1.1813$ additional mean
instructions and still break even.  The statement is a decision boundary; the alternative
ISA's actual path-length distribution and placed hardware remain separate evidence.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
All posterior, synchronization, contraction and rate--distortion values above are exact
for their stated finite objects or explicit synthetic kernels.  Rank-three candidate
existence, FPGA timing, compiled front doors, laboratory likelihoods, physical edit rates
and dissipated heat remain independently gated.
