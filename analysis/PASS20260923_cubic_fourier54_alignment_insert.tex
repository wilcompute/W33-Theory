\subsection*{The cubic interaction covers all $54$ retyped Fourier directions}

The canonical $E_6$-id cubic labels are first transported through the explicit 27-point gauge conjugacy to the current physical-Clifford $H_{27}$ address chart.  This bridge maps all $45$ canonical cubic triads to the current five-direction right-coset system and all nine firewall fibers to the current center spread.

The source regular module decomposes as
\[
 \operatorname{Reg}(K)=S_1^{(27)}\oplus S_2^{(27)}\oplus L^{(27)},
\]
with $S_1$ the unique operator-compatible isotypic sector.  Thus the minimal
compiler must access the quotient
\[
 \operatorname{Reg}(K)/S_1,
 \qquad \dim=54.
\]

For $D_v(x)=[v,x]$, the correct alignment criterion is
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\!\left(S_1+\operatorname{Im}D_v\right)=81,
\]
not merely $\operatorname{rank}D_v\ge54$.

Constructing the $27$ canonical $S_1$ matrix coefficients from the frozen
$qutrit$ Schrödinger representation and reducing the resulting
$\mathbb Z[\omega]$ matrices at both split primes $103$ and $109$ gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
v & \operatorname{rank}D_v &
\operatorname{rank}(S_1+\operatorname{Im}D_v) &
\operatorname{rank}_{\rm quotient} &
\dim(S_1\cap\operatorname{Im}D_v)\\ \hline
e_0 & 20 & 47 & 20 & 0\\
(1,\ldots,1) & 54 & 63 & 36 & 18\\
(1,2,\ldots,81) & 54 & 72 & 45 & 9\\
(1^2,2^2,\ldots,81^2) & 78 & 81 & 54 & 24
\end{array}
\]
at both primes.

Because an $81\times81$ minor remains nonzero after reduction, it was already
nonzero over $\mathbb Q(\omega)$.  Hence for the explicit quadratic background
\[
 v_n=(n+1)^2
\]
the projection
\[
 \operatorname{Im}D_v\longrightarrow\operatorname{Reg}(K)/S_1
\]
is surjective of rank $54$.  The finite tangent-level alignment obstruction is
therefore closed: one cubic background accesses every Fourier direction that
must change representation type.

This does not assert $\operatorname{Im}D_v=S_2\oplus L$; the image intersects
$S_1$ in dimension $24$.  Nor does it select the quadratic background as a
physical vacuum or exponentiate the tangent map into a hardware gate.
