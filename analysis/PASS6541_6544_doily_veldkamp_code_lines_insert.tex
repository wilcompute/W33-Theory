\subsubsection{The code reconstructs all $155$ Veldkamp lines}
\label{sec:doily-code-veldkamp-lines}

The coding realization above extends from Veldkamp points to the full incidence
space. Since $C$ has dimension five over $\mathbb F_2$, its $31$ nonzero words
are the points of $PG(4,2)$ and every projective line is
\[
 \{u,v,u+v\}.
\]
Exact enumeration yields $155$ such triples. Classifying the common zero-set
core of each triple by the doily incidence geometry reconstructed from
$C^\perp$ gives the complete five-type census
\[
 \boxed{15+15+60+20+45=155}.
\]
More explicitly, the core/type and code-weight compositions are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{core} & \text{hyperplane composition} & \#\\ \hline
\text{single point} & (8,10,10) & 15\\
\text{collinear triple} & (8,8,8) & 15\\
\text{unicentric triad} & (6,8,10) & 60\\
\text{tricentric triad} & (8,8,8) & 20\\
\text{pentad} & (6,6,8) & 45
\end{array}
\]
The only ambiguity left by weights alone is the $35$ all-perp
$(8,8,8)$ lines. Minimum dual supports resolve it canonically:
\[
 \boxed{35=15_{\rm collinear}+20_{\rm tricentric}}.
\]
Thus the same code that reconstructs the $31$ hyperplanes also reconstructs
all $155$ Veldkamp lines and all five core types.

The corresponding addition/fusion laws are
\[
6+6\to8,\qquad10+10\to8,\qquad6+10\to8,\qquad8+8\to8,
\]
with pair counts $45,15,60,105$, respectively. These are finite-code
statements. The abstract identification $\mathcal V(W(2))\cong PG(4,2)$ and
its five Veldkamp-line types are prior art (arXiv:0704.0495); the claim here is
the executable reconstruction from the explicit quadratic-evaluation code and
its dual, not a literature-priority claim.
