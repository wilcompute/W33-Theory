# BT812 — The Five Vacua of W(3,3)

Every maximal subgroup class of PSp(4,3) induces a canonical vacuum
decomposition of the substrate.  The complete table (GAP-witnessed orbit
anatomies on the 40 points and 40 isotropic lines):

```text
index 27  (2^4:A5)      points [40]          lines [40]
index 36  (S6)          points [40]          lines [10, 30]
index 40  (point parab) points [1, 12, 27]   lines [4, 36]
index 40  (line parab)  points [4, 36]       lines [1, 12, 27]
index 45  ((2Tx2T):2)   points [8, 32]       lines [16, 24]
```

## Readings

1. **The icosahedral vacuum is the unique HOMOGENEOUS one** — transitive
   on both points and lines.  The F4² register's symmetry flattens the
   substrate completely; its point stabilizer has order 960/40 = 24 = f.
   A featureless vacuum carried by the icosahedron.
2. **The holonet split is the point-parabolic vacuum**: 40 = 1 + 12 + 27
   (self + gauge shell + matter shell), with line split 4 + 36; the line
   parabolic mirrors both under duality.  The architecture's foundational
   decomposition is one of five, not the only one.
3. **The spread vacuum** keeps points homogeneous and fibers the lines
   10 + 30 (fibration + BC layer) — the 600-cell's reading.
4. **The polar vacuum** is binary: points 8 + 32, lines 16 + 24 = mu² + f
   (the cross-transversal grid + remainder) — the 24-cell's reading.

## The pentaptych

One group, five self-portraits: homogeneous (icosahedral), fibered
(spread), pointed (building, twice, dual to each other), and binary
(polar).  Physics built on the 1+12+27 split is the choice of the
parabolic vacuum; the existence of the other four — especially the
featureless icosahedral one — is the substrate's vacuum-degeneracy
statement: the SAME geometry supports five inequivalent "ways of being
decomposed," indexed by the Schläfli inventory 27/36/40/40/45 (BT810).

## Boundary
