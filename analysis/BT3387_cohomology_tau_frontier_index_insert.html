Minimum-defect normal forms and the hidden signed ternary torus

The minimum weight-two gauge defects give an exact word metric on the flat C35 connection space. The full flat-space diameter is 480; exact sphere counting bounds the switching-class Cayley diameter by 389 ≤ D ≤ 480. The same generators present the 2,180-dimensional cohomology with 3,600 generator coordinates and 1,420 independent relations.

The 135 τ-orbit species in the ternary Hamming cube have only 108 distinct block-one-hot Q15 barycentres, with fibre profile 81 singletons plus 27 double fibres. The ambiguous fibres form a canonical two-sheeted cover of the 27-point fixed affine flat, distinguished by the correlation bit h=(x1+x4)(x2+x3-1).

Its magnetic character block has cubic trace 31,302 instead of 31,680, certifying a genuine nonseparable cohomological weighting while leaving the chromatic boundary at 10 ≤ χ(H) ≤ 11.
