\subsection{Pass 4941: the quadratic ambiguity cancels at degree four}
Passes~4870 and 4875 give a two-dimensional $PSp$-equivariant quadratic Hom
plane
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{PSp}(\operatorname{Sym}^2H_2,Q_{10})
\]
on which the outer $PGSp/PSp$ involution acts as $-I_2$.  Pass~4871 supplies
the unique intrinsic equivariant Lie bracket on $Q_{10}$.  For any basis
$q_1,q_2$ of the quadratic Hom plane define
\[
  \mathcal F(x)=[q_1(x),q_2(x)].
\]
Then $\mathcal F$ is homogeneous of degree four, and under a basis change
$A\in GL_2(\mathbb F_3)$ it scales only by $\det A$.  Thus it defines a
canonical projective quartic line.  The two outer minus signs cancel, so the
quartic is $PGSp$-even.  Direct exact evaluation finds three-support inputs
whose images span all ten dimensions of $Q_{10}$:
\[
  \boxed{\dim\langle\mathcal F(x)\rangle=10.}
\]
Every two-support input vanishes.  Moreover each quadratic channel separately
annihilates the entire $40$-dimensional fiber-constant subspace, because the
sum over every complete $3\times3$ lift of a $W(3,3)$ edge is zero in
$\mathbb F_3$.  Hence the quartic construction is genuinely carried by the
non-semisimple three-fiber layer rather than by the quotient alone.
