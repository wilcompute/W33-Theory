\[

\]

\[
S(U(3)\times U(2)\times U(1)^4),
\]

\[
\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)^5,
\qquad \dim=16.
\]

Four further local abelian directions remain and must be removed or lifted by the rest of the heterotic compactification.
