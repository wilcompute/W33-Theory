\subsection*{Passes 5052--5059: the $q=4$ apartment code, bicycle lift, and orientation channels}
The binary apartment-code law survives a third exact anchor.  A direct reconstruction of $W(3,4)$ gives $13600$ apartments and a rank-$256$ apartment-evaluation code.  Its $54400$ theta checks span the full $13344$-dimensional dual, and exact even-set optimization gives minimum distance $256$:
\[
\mathcal A_4=[13600,256,256]_2.
\]
Together with the previously frozen $q=2,3$ cases, the first three prime-power anchors satisfy $\dim\mathcal A_q=d(\mathcal A_q)=q^4$.

At $q=3$ the minimum shell is now classified completely.  Excluding all $160$ chamber-star words leaves the weight-$81$ feasibility problem inconsistent, so those $160$ words are the entire minimum shell.  Their intersections are $27,9,3,1$ at gallery distances $1,2,3,4$.  Hence the abstract code recovers the chamber graph; its $80$ maximal $K_4$ panel cliques recover a $40+40$ bipartite $4$-regular girth-$8$ graph isomorphic to the original W33 Levi graph.

The first two-adic lift of the subdivided-Levi Jacobian is the binary bicycle module
\[
\mathcal B_{29}=\operatorname{im}B^{\mathsf T}\cap\ker B.
\]
Its point and line halves have composition profiles $1\mid14$ and $1\mid8\mid6$.  Thus $\mathcal B_{29}$ has a natural $23$-space with factors $14,1,8$ and a six-dimensional quotient.  This $29$ must not be identified with the older apartment radical $R_{29}$ merely by dimension: the exact intertwiner space has dimension one and its unique nonzero map $R_{29}\to\mathcal B_{29}$ has rank only $15$, with $14$-dimensional kernel.

There is also an orientation correction to the unsigned apartment permutation statement.  The setwise stabilizer $H$ of an apartment has order $16$, and an oriented apartment cycle affords a nontrivial character $\varepsilon:H\to\{\pm1\}$, with eight orientation-preserving and eight orientation-reversing elements.  Both $\mathrm{St}^{H}$ and $\mathrm{St}^{H,\varepsilon}$ have dimension five.  In the twisted space an integral basis has supports $8,16,8,40,40$; the ordinary signed apartment is one distinguished support-eight channel, not a unique Steinberg copy of the unsigned permutation module.

Finally, the local cubic-surface $V_{24}$ compiler has been arithmetically optimized.  Among all $2672$ rank-preserving one-row substitutions in the fixed-point apartment star, replacing frozen row $7$ by row $53$ lowers the exact inverse denominator from $9360$ to $3120$.  The full $162$-row point-star lattice has largest nonzero Smith invariant $780$, showing a further arithmetic floor if unrestricted integral row combinations are allowed; those large-coefficient Smith combinations are not promoted as a hardware schedule.

Two additional finite-geometry facts sharpen the decoder picture.  At $q=4$ the theta relations give $54400$ binary projective lines on the apartment orbit but no complete contained seven-point projective plane.  The apartment/theta Tanner graphs have no four-cycles; their girths at $q=2,3,4$ are $8,6,6$, with respectively $0,4320,108800$ six-cycles.  These are finite code/building statements only; no physical dynamics follows from the matching channel counts.
