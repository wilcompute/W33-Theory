\section{Exact Mackey decomposition of the selector algebra}
\label{sec:mackey-selector-decomposition}

Let $X$ be the $120$ line--matching selectors of $W(3,3)$ and let $H$ be one selector stabilizer.  The literal permutation action gives
\[
H\cong C_3^3\rtimes(D_8\times C_2).
\]
Writing $N=O_3(H)\cong C_3^3$ and $K=H/N\cong D_8\times C_2$, the action of $K$ on $\widehat N$ has orbit sizes
\[
1,2,4,4,8,8
\]
with little groups of types
\[
D_8\times C_2,\quad D_8,\quad V_4,\quad V_4,\quad C_2,\quad C_2.
\]
Exact induction in $\mathbb Z[\omega]$ constructs all $27$ irreducible characters.  Their degree census is
\[
1^8,\qquad2^6,\qquad4^9,\qquad8^4,
\]
and all character values are rational integers.

Exactly fourteen irreducibles occur in $\mathbb Q^X$.  Their degrees and multiplicities are
\[
(1,1,1,2,2,2,4,4,4,4,8,8,8,8)
\]
and
\[
(1,1,1,1,1,1,1,2,2,3,3,3,4,5).
\]
Thus
\[
\sum_i d_i m_i=120,
\qquad
\sum_i m_i^2=83.
\]
Each exact Mackey character projector coincides with one rational primitive central projector of the orbital algebra, so
\[
\operatorname{End}_H(\mathbb Q^X)
\cong
\mathbb Q^7\oplus M_2(\mathbb Q)^2\oplus
M_3(\mathbb Q)^3\oplus M_4(\mathbb Q)\oplus M_5(\mathbb Q).
\]

The fourteen orbital sectors fuse under the Terwilliger algebra into ten packets. Seven remain singletons and the other seven form packets of sizes $2,2,3$, giving
\[
(2-1)+(2-1)+(3-1)=4=14-10=83-79.
\]
The geometric splitter resolves the three packets with scalar eigenvalue sets
\[
\{-3,3\},\qquad\{-3,0\},\qquad\{-4,-1,2\}.
\]

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This is a finite rational representation--theory theorem reconstructed from the literal permutation group. It uses no database character table or floating eigensolver and makes no particle, hardware, or laboratory identification.
