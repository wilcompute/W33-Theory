\subsection{The apartment tight frame carries an exact basis-volume budget}

Put $r=q^4$ and $N=(q+1)^2(q^2+1)$.  For the signed flag--apartment matrix
$C$, Pass~5396 gives $CC^{\mathsf T}=N E_{\rm cyc}$.  If $Q$ is any
orthonormal basis of the Levi cycle space and $D=Q^{\mathsf T}C$, then
$DD^{\mathsf T}=NI_r$.  Cauchy--Binet therefore yields
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{|S|=r}\det(C_S^{\mathsf T}C_S)=N^r,
 }
\]
where dependent $r$-subsets contribute zero.  Thus the full apartment family has
an exact coordinate-free squared-volume budget.

Passes~5439 and 5441 give a cardinality-minimal fundamental apartment basis and
the Levi tree number
\[
 \tau(L)=(q^2+1)^{f-1}(q+1)^{2g},
 \quad
 f=\frac{q(q+1)^2}{2},\quad
 g=\frac{q(q^2+1)}2.
\]
Let $\Lambda=Z_1(L;\mathbb Z)$.  For any independent $r$-apartment set $S$,
its columns generate a finite-index sublattice $\Lambda_S\le\Lambda$, so
\[
 \boxed{
 \det(C_S^{\mathsf T}C_S)=\tau(L)\,[\Lambda:\Lambda_S]^2.
 }
\]
Consequently the real Cauchy--Binet identity refines to the integral sum-of-squares
law
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{|S|=r}m_S^2=\frac{N^r}{\tau(L)},
 \qquad
 m_S=[\Lambda:\Lambda_S],
 }
\]
with $m_S=0$ for dependent sets.  At $q=3$ the right side is
$2^{322}5^{58}$.

At $q=3$, the full $1620$-apartment signed frame has redundancy $20$ and
condition number one on its $81$-dimensional image.  The deterministic
$81$-apartment BFS basis of Pass~5443 has Gram minimum/maximum eigenvalues
$1$ and $160$, hence singular condition number $\sqrt{160}$.  Its inverse norm
is therefore worse than the full-frame Moore--Penrose inverse norm by exactly
$\sqrt{160}$.  This compares that specific BFS basis with the full frame; no
optimal-conditioning claim over all $81$-apartment bases is made.

\subsection{The two binary footprint kernels live at different stages}

For odd prime powers, Pass~5376 gives
\[
 \ker(F^{\mathsf T})=C_W,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}_2F=g,
 \qquad
 C_F=\operatorname{im}F=C_W^\perp.
\]
Hence $F^{\mathsf T}$ induces
\[
 M/C_W\cong\operatorname{im}F^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Pass~5350 identifies the radical, giving
\[
 \boxed{
 0\to M_0/C_W\to\operatorname{im}F^{\mathsf T}\to\langle\mathbf1\rangle\to0,
 }
\]
with dimensions $0\to(g-1)\to g\to1\to0$.  This is the point/transposed
stage.  By contrast, Passes~5421 and 5377 give the apartment stage
\[
 \boxed{
 0\to D_{\rm ap}=K_0\to H_1(L)^*\to C_F\to0,
 }
\]
with dimensions $0\to(q^4-g)\to q^4\to g\to0$.  Thus the all-odd point-kernel
closure does not kill the nonzero apartment residual.

\subsection{The $q=5$ weight-$1040$ support is forced into an exotic corner}

A weight-$1040$ word of $K_0$ would have exactly $26$ active P-components, each
a local even minimum of weight $40$.  Let $(a,b,c)$ count its internal block
pairs in the three Pass~5232 relations $R_1,R_2,R_3$, whose weight-eight-shell
pair codegrees are $25,5,0$.  The no-singleton shell law gives
\[
 4a-c\ge1235.
\]
Delsarte positivity in the rank-four block association scheme gives
\[
 \frac a{10}-\frac{39}{5}\ge0,
 \quad
 \frac{2c}{15}-\frac{26}{75}\ge0,
 \quad
 \frac{481}{15}-\frac a{10}-\frac{2c}{15}\ge0.
\]
Together with $a+b+c=325$, only $21$ integer pair profiles remain; all satisfy
$310\le a\le316$, so only $9$--$15$ of the $325$ block pairs lie outside $R_1$.
Moreover, exhaustive extension of every one of the $156$ known $25$-block point
footprints by every one of its $300$ outside blocks gives zero no-singleton
survivors.  Hence any weight-$1040$ word, if it exists, has an exotic $26$-block
support.  Nonexistence is not claimed.

\subsection{The $q=3$ radius-eleven decoder frontier}

Pass~5430 proves global eventual radius ten.  At weight eleven, exact MILPs rule
out false-center sectors $V=1$ and $V=2$.  For $V=3$, local weights $6$ through
$11$ occur with counts
\[
 32,\ 256,\ 832,\ 1536,\ 1856,\ 1536.
\]
The two-new-direction cap per outsider eliminates all local weights at least
$8$.  Only weight six plus five outsiders and weight seven plus four outsiders
remain; exact MILPs exhibit necessary survivors in both branches, and the sampled
witnesses self-heal.

For $V=4$, the raw rooted space has
\[
 \boxed{10{,}963{,}673{,}616}
\]
cases.  Requiring every outsider to share a chart with the local core or another
outsider leaves exactly
\[
 \boxed{35{,}218{,}336}
\]
necessary cases.  A necessary-survivor witness again self-heals, but the remaining
census has not been exhaustively decoded.  Thus radius eleven remains open.

\subsection{A negative-Pell square clock for the unsigned Gram determinant}

Pass~5434 implies that for odd $q$ the square class of
$\det(BB^{\mathsf T})$ is
\[
 \boxed{\frac{q^2+1}{2}}.
\]
Therefore the determinant is a perfect integer square exactly when
\[
 \boxed{q^2-2m^2=-1.}
\]
The first nontrivial Pell $q$-coordinates are $7,41,239,1393,8119,\ldots$;
only prime-power coordinates are admissible generalized-quadrangle parameters.
For even $q$, the square class is $10$ at $q=2$ and $2$ for $q\ge4$, so the
unsigned Gram determinant is never square.

Reducing the negative-Pell equation modulo $16$ forces $q\equiv\pm1\pmod8$.
Passes~5361 and 5406 independently identify exactly this congruence class as the
branch where the two binary logical Weil factors are individually defined over
$\mathbb F_2$.  Hence
\[
 \boxed{
 \det(BB^{\mathsf T})\text{ square at odd }q
 \Longrightarrow q\equiv\pm1\pmod8
 \Longrightarrow \text{split }\mathbb F_2\text{ Weil branch}.
 }
\]
The converse fails, e.g. $q=9$.
