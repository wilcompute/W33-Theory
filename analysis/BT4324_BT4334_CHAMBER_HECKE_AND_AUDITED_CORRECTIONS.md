## 2. Exact chamber theorem (Passes 4324--4327)

Let \(\mathcal F\) be the 160 incident point--line chambers of \(GQ(3,3)\).  Let
\(P\) hold the point and change the line, let \(L\) hold the line and change the
point, and put \(C=P+L\).  On \(\mathbb Q^{\mathcal F}\), the GAP witness proves

\[
P^2=2P+3I,\qquad L^2=2L+3I,\qquad PLPL=LPLP,
\]

and the algebra generated by \(P,L\) has dimension 8.  Its chamber-module census is

\[
1\chi_{(3,3)}+15\chi_{(3,-1)}+15\chi_{(-1,3)}
 +81\chi_{(-1,-1)}+24V_2.
\]

With the two orientations of a Levi edge ordered as blocks, the non-backtracking
operator factors exactly as

\[
B_{\mathrm{Levi}}=\begin{pmatrix}0&L\\P&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B_{\mathrm{Levi}}^2=\operatorname{diag}(LP,PL).
\]

Thus \(K=LP\) is a binary 9-out oriented distance-two half, \(K^T=PL\) is its
disjoint reverse, and

\[
K+K^T=C^2-2C-6I,
\]

\[
\chi_K(x)=(x-9)(x-1)^{81}(x+3)^{30}(x^2+9)^{24}.
\]

The critical kernel
\(\ker((K+3I)(K^2+9I))\) has dimension 78 and satisfies \(K^4=81I\).

The chirality operator \(\Omega=LP-PL\) has

\[
\chi_\Omega(x)=x^{112}(x^2+60)^{24},\qquad
\Pi_{48}=-\Omega^2/60,
\]

where \(\Pi_{48}\) is an exact symmetric idempotent of rank 48.  On its image,
\((\Omega/\sqrt{60})^2=-I\), and

\[
\Omega(C-2I)=-(C-2I)\Omega,
\]

so the \(C\)-eigenspaces with eigenvalues \(2+\sqrt6\) and \(2-\sqrt6\) are
exchanged.  This is the exact conjugate \(24+24\) channel.  It is not, by itself, a
\(W(G_2)\)-action construction.

Finally, put \(X=(C-2I)\Pi_{48}\) and
\(F=\Pi_{48}(T B_{W33}^3T^T)\Pi_{48}\).  In the exact four-element packet basis
\(\{\Pi_{48},X,\Omega,X\Omega\}\),

\[
F=-68\Pi_{48}-31X-\frac{21}{2}\Omega+\frac23X\Omega,
\]

\[
\left(-\frac{21}{2}\Omega+\frac23X\Omega\right)^2=-6455\Pi_{48},
\]

and

\[
F^2+136F+5313\Pi_{48}=0,
\qquad (F+68\Pi_{48})^2=-689\Pi_{48},\quad 689=13\cdot53.
\]

These are identities of explicit finite rational matrices.  They prove neither a
continuum limit nor a mass/coupling identification.

### 2.1 Object-level meaning of the \(24+24\) channel (Pass 4334)

Let \(A_p\) and \(A_\ell\) be the point graph and dual line graph adjacency
matrices.  Both have spectrum \(12^1,2^{24},(-4)^{15}\), so their exact
rank-24 projectors onto the eigenvalue-two spaces are

\[
E_p=-\frac{(A_p-12I)(A_p+4I)}{60},\qquad
E_\ell=-\frac{(A_\ell-12I)(A_\ell+4I)}{60}.
\]

Let \(R_p,R_\ell\) pull point and line functions back to the 160 incident
chambers, and define the orthogonal chamber projectors

\[
Q_p=\frac14R_pE_pR_p^T,\qquad
Q_\ell=\frac14R_\ell E_\ell R_\ell^T.
\]

Exact GAP arithmetic proves that both have rank 24, their images meet only in
zero, and

\[
\operatorname{im}\Pi_{48}
=\operatorname{im}Q_p\oplus\operatorname{im}Q_\ell.
\]

The summands are not orthogonal.  Instead they are uniformly isoclinic:

\[
Q_pQ_\ell Q_p=\frac38Q_p,\qquad
Q_\ell Q_pQ_\ell=\frac38Q_\ell.
\]

Thus all 24 principal angles have cosine \(\sqrt{3/8}=\sqrt6/4\).  If
\(Q=Q_p+Q_\ell\), then

\[
Q^2-2Q+\frac58\Pi_{48}=0,
\qquad
\boxed{\Pi_{48}=\frac85(2Q-Q^2)=-\frac{\Omega^2}{60}}.
\]

This closes the earlier object-level gap: `24+24` is literally the point and
line eigenvalue-two carriers on the chamber space, joined at one exact angle.
It remains a finite rational carrier theorem, not a selected opcode or a
physical two-sector model.

## 3. Audited corrections (Passes 4328--4333)

### 3.1 Ihara poles and the withdrawn irregular-Ramanujan score

For a finite graph,

\[
\det(I-uB)=(1-u^2)^{E-V}\det(I-uA+u^2(D-I)).
\]

For a connected non-circuit graph of minimum degree \(d_{\min}\ge2\) and maximum
degree \(d_{\max}\), Kotani--Sunada Theorem 1.3(2) gives the non-real-pole annulus

\[
(d_{\max}-1)^{-1/2}\le |u|\le(d_{\min}-1)^{-1/2}.
\]

For the corresponding roots \(\lambda=u^{-1}\) of the Hashimoto determinant, this is

\[
\sqrt{d_{\min}-1}\le|\lambda|\le\sqrt{d_{\max}-1}.
\]

For the shipped graph, \(d_{\min}=2,d_{\max}=8\), hence
\(1/\sqrt7\le|u|\le1\), equivalently \(1\le|\lambda|\le\sqrt7\), for the
corresponding non-real roots only.  Pass 4239 instead used the unsquared band
\(q^{-1}\le|u|\le p^{-1}\), or \(p\le|\lambda|\le q\), and turned spectral
spread inside that wrong interval into a “closest irregular Ramanujan” metric.  Both the
formula and that conclusion are withdrawn.

The primary source is Motoko Kotani and Toshikazu Sunada, “Zeta Functions of Finite
Graphs,” *J. Math. Sci. Univ. Tokyo* **7** (2000), 7--25, especially Theorems 1.1 and
1.3: <https://www.ms.u-tokyo.ac.jp/journal/pdf/jms070102.pdf>.  The paper's exact
Ramanujan/RH circle statement is for regular graphs.  Its irregular degree annulus is not
an equality, does not say that poles fill the annulus, and does not certify the repo's
internal circle \(|\lambda|=\sqrt{\rho(B)}\).

### 3.2 The stored \(\mathbb Z_{2731}\) cover has girth 8, not 16

In zero-based vertex labels, the stored Pass-4253 voltage assignment contains the simple
base 8-cycle

\[
(3,52,4,48,8,66,18,53).

Its zero-based edge indices are
\((48,49,33,34,104,105,54,52)\), its signed voltages are
\((0,0,0,0,1328,-542,801,-1587)\), and their sum is \(0\pmod{2731}\).  The

This is a certificate-specific retraction.  It does not retract the distinct

### 3.5 Degree 33: exact algebraic conjugacy, not intrinsic localization

Pass 4237's exact quadratic-pencil factorization places the shipped growth root
\(\rho(B)\approx5.7468726799\) in a monic degree-33 factor.  The correction witness
checks that factor is irreducible over \(\mathbb Q\) and, by exact Sturm counts, has
exactly one real root in each interval

\[
574/100<\rho(B)<575/100,
\qquad
3349/1000<\lambda_{\mathrm{slow}}<3350/1000.
\]

Together with the frozen identification of the slow real Hashimoto root, this proves that
the two numbers are roots of the same irreducible polynomial: each has algebraic degree
33 and they are Galois conjugates.  It does **not** say that the full operator minimal
polynomial has degree 33, construct the rational primary projector, or make the
Pass-4244 coordinate hyperplane intrinsic.  In particular, an eigenvalue theorem and an
eigenvector-support theorem are different claims.

## 5. Highest-leverage genuinely open connection

The missing object is an **equivariant full-ISA lift to an incidence carrier**.  The
linear symplectic opcodes act on projective points, lines, and flags, but the affine load
translations do not.  To connect the degree-33 instruction-graph factor, the 160-chamber
Hecke algebra, the linear-opcode mismatch census, and any universal-cover/Ramanujan question, one
must construct an enlarged carrier \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}\) and maps

\[
\varphi:\operatorname{ASp}(4,3)\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Aut}(\widetilde{\mathcal F}),
\qquad
\pi:\widetilde{\mathcal F}\longrightarrow\mathcal F,
\]

such that every shipped affine opcode has a certified lift, \(\pi\) preserves the relevant
incidence/non-backtracking structure where defined, and the linear subgroup reduces to
the existing flag action.  A proof that no finite lift with these properties exists would
be equally decisive.

This is the highest-leverage open connection because, until such a map exists, the
degree-33 ISA spectrum and the chamber Hecke spectrum are adjacent computations rather
than spectra of one constructed machine.  The Pass-4331 census covers only the
linear opcode tests, and “the zeta's domain is a machine register” over-reads the load
port.  Once the carrier is constructed (or ruled out), the next exact tasks are:

1. build the rational projector onto the degree-33 primary factor and test its covariance
   under the lifted action;
2. define and compute the appropriate infinite universal-cover spectral benchmark for
   the resulting irregular graph; and
3. only then formulate an irregular Ramanujan/RH comparison.  The finite
   \(|\lambda|=\sqrt{\rho(B)}\) circle and the Kotani--Sunada degree annulus are not
   substitutes for that benchmark.
