Five qutrit switches and a determinant-one unit spiral

Exact: five disjoint K4,4 cover switches commute, generating 3^5 = 243 distinct 60-frame exact covers of the 240 W33 supports.

Exact obstruction: every local OA(16,3,4,2) is Klein-four type, but each inter-block K3,3 has six matchings. Around a support triangle all six S3 holonomies occur equally often, so bare incidence does not select curvature.

Golden lift: the golden plane of R4^2 U6 admits a determinant-one quarter-turn companion with eigenvalues i phi and -i/phi. Its square is an exact O(1,1)-type boost of rapidity 2 log(phi).
