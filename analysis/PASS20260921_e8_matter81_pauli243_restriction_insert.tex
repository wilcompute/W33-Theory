\subsection*{The matter $81$ is nine Schr\"odinger modules, not the Pauli phase space}

For the trinification realization of the $E_6$ minuscule representation, the internal Heisenberg shift has nine three-cycles on the $27$ weight rays and the clock supplies the corresponding qutrit phases. Thus
\[
27\downarrow H_{27}^{\rm int}=9\,\mathcal H_3.
\]
Tensoring with the external physical qutrit gives
\[
\boxed{(27,3)\downarrow 3_+^{1+4}=9\,\mathcal H_9}.
\]
The exact character is
\[
\chi(1)=81,\quad \chi(z)=81\omega,\quad \chi(z^2)=81\omega^2,
\]
and vanishes on all $240$ noncentral elements.

On projective matter weight rays the Pauli subgroup has nine orbits of size nine. By contrast its quotient
\[
G/Z(G)\cong\mathbb F_3^4
\]
is a regular $81$-element translation set. Therefore the two appearances of $81$ are not equivariantly identical:
\[
\boxed{81_{\rm matter}=9\,\mathcal H_9,\qquad 81_{\rm operators}=\mathbb F_3^4.}
\]
