\paragraph{Pass 4552: a third exact elliptic-quadric rank anchor, and a literature-scope correction.}
An independent construction of $Q^-(5,5)=GQ(5,25)$ gives $756$ points and
$3276$ lines and, over $\mathbb F_2$,
\[
 \operatorname{rank}N=651,\qquad \operatorname{rank}(N^TN)=546.
\]
Together with the exact $q=3,7$ anchors this gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}q&3&5&7\\\hline
\operatorname{rank}N&91&651&2451\\
\operatorname{rank}(N^TN)&70&546&2150
\end{array}
\]
and all three agree with
$\operatorname{rank}N=q^4+q^2+1$ and
$\operatorname{rank}(N^TN)=(q^2+1)(q^2-q+1)$.  The closed odd-$q$ formulas
remain open.  In particular, the Bagchi--Brouwer--Wilbrink $O(5,q)$ binary-code
paper concerns the dual of the square symplectic quadrangle $Sp(4,q)$, not the
elliptic $Q^-(5,q)=GQ(q,q^2)$ family, so it is not used as a proof here.

\paragraph{Pass 4553: the $1\mid8\mid1$ chain is intrinsic parity geometry.}
Let $j$ be the all-ones protected vector.  Pass~4541 gives
$\pi(x)=B(x,j)$, hence
\[
 \boxed{0<\langle j\rangle<j^\perp=V_9<H_{10}}.
\]
On $V_8=V_9/\langle j\rangle$ the formula
\[
 \boxed{q_8([x])=\frac{\operatorname{wt}(x)}4\pmod2}
\]
is well defined under $x\mapsto x+j$ and polarizes exactly to $B$.  It has
$136$ singular classes including zero and $120$ anisotropic classes, so this is
an explicit plus-type $O^+(8,2)$ model.  The fixed line and nine-space are now
located without cyclic-submodule search; irreducibility of the middle eight is
inherited from the independent Passes~4477/4496 certificate.

\paragraph{Pass 4554: the 108 local bases form a basis-switching codebook.}
The one-spoke exchange graph on the $108$ Pass~4543 bases is $15$-regular with
$810$ edges, diameter three, distance distribution $(1,15,48,44)$, and spectrum
\[
 15^1,\quad9^8,\quad3^{27},\quad0^{16},\quad(-3)^{56}.
\]
The order-$162$ Borel has two basis orbits of sizes $81$ and $27$ with equitable
quotient
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}12&3\\9&6\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Every specified spoke is omitted by $27$ bases; two erased spokes in distinct
pencils are simultaneously omitted by six bases, while two in one pencil are
never simultaneously omitted.  An erased three-spoke set has exactly one
surviving basis iff it meets three distinct pencils.

\paragraph{Pass 4555: one primitive-$C_8$ selector layer suffices for the whole protected bootstrap.}
Pass~4551 proves that coefficient $712$ at Walsh degree four selects exactly the
$1620$ apartments.  Keeping only that Boolean selector already gives
\[
 C_8^{(4)}[712]\to H\to HH^T=A_*\to W33^\vee\to H_{10}
 \to (j,\pi,V_9).
\]
Thus no $C_6$ or $C_7$ layer is required once this $C_8$ selector is available.
This is a sufficiency theorem, not an absolute lower bound on all possible
encodings of the same geometry.

\paragraph{Pass 4556: no linear equivariant shortcut from $H_{10}$ to the exceptional six-space.}
The protected module has composition factors $1,8,1$, while the faithful
$O^-_6(2)$ module $U_6$ from Passes~4522/4544 is simple of dimension six.
Therefore
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Hom}_{PSp(4,3)}(H_{10},U_6)=
        \operatorname{Hom}_{PSp(4,3)}(U_6,H_{10})=0.}
\]
Any nonzero map would force a six-dimensional simple composition factor in
$H_{10}$.  The Schlaefli/double-six incidence intertwiner acts on different
$27$- and $36$-point permutation carriers and does not evade this obstruction.

\paragraph{Pass 4557 (outside the box): cross-shell Hamming distance recovers line pencils.}
For an even edge vector $e_{jk}=s_j+s_k$ and the forty odd line-stars, the
cross-shell distance profile is
\[
 d=12:2,\qquad d=20:36,\qquad d=28:2.
\]
The two distance-$12$ stars are exactly $j,k$; the two distance-$28$ stars are
exactly their common neighbors.  These four vertices form the unique $K_4$
line pencil through the geometric intersection point.  Thus protected Hamming
geometry reconstructs vertex--edge incidence and point pencils, not merely the
SRG adjacency relation.

\paragraph{Pass 4558 (outside the box): apartments uniformly lift the singular $O^+(8,2)$ shell.}
The $1620$ apartment indicators have only $135$ distinct protected images under
$b\mapsto A_*b$.  Every image has weight $16$, every image has exactly twelve
apartment preimages, and these images are precisely the $135$ nonzero singular
classes of $V_8$.  In each twelve-element fiber, join two apartments when they
share one line.  Every fiber is
\[
 \boxed{K_{4,4,4}},
\]
with degree eight, $48$ intersecting pairs and $18$ disjoint pairs.

\paragraph{Pass 4559 (outside the box): the $240$ edges are a two-sheeted anisotropic lift.}
Every protected edge has weight $20$, hence represents an anisotropic class of
$V_8$.  Addition of $j$ pairs the $240$ edge vectors into $120$ classes.  On the
W33 carrier this involution sends an edge to the opposite edge in the unique
four-line $K_4$ pencil through the same point.  Therefore
\[
 \boxed{240\longrightarrow120}
\]
is the canonical opposite-edge double cover of the anisotropic shell; its polar
orthogonality quotient is again $\operatorname{SRG}(120,63,30,36)$.
