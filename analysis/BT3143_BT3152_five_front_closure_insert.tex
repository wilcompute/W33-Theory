\subsection{Five-front closure: sparse inference, edit epochs, and the universal ISA price}
\label{sec:bt3143-3152-five-front}

The controller is now organized as
\[
 \text{candidate}\to\text{independent certifier}\to\text{sparse posterior}
 \to\text{costed action}\to\text{edit state}\to\text{recursive context}
 \to\text{calibrated dispatch}\to\text{reset}.
\]
This section records the finite claims and, equally importantly, the boundaries that keep
source calculations separate from candidate existence, placed hardware, and laboratory
performance.

\paragraph{Candidate intake is fail closed.}
No Pass~3125 rank-three candidate is presently available.  The intake reports
\texttt{NO\_INPUTS\_DISCOVERED}; this is not a no-go theorem.  Any later candidate is passed
through the independent rank-three certifier before promotion.

\paragraph{The noisy posterior is exactly sparse.}
For the frozen 23-row panel, a hypothesis contains zero, one, or two faulty edges.  Write
$L_0$ for the no-fault likelihood and $U_a=L_a/L_0$ for an atomic edge/group fault.  Two
distinct edges are either disjoint or share one triangle.  Therefore
\[
 \frac{L_{ab}}{L_0}=U_aU_b C_{ab},
 \qquad
 C_{ab}=1
\]
unless the unique shared triangle is one of the 23 measured triangles.  On that triangle,
for observed symbol $y$,
\[
 C_{ab}(y)=
 \frac{P(y\mid s_{ab})P(y\mid 1)}
      {P(y\mid s_a)P(y\mid s_b)}.
\]
There are one baseline factor, 315 unary factors, and
$23\cdot3\cdot7^2=3381$ pair corrections, for 3697 dynamic values instead of 48826.  This
is a $92.4282\%$ reduction, or a $13.2069\times$ compression.  Thirty-two noisy regression
transcripts gave posterior error below $1.67\times10^{-15}$ and the same one-step
mutual-information action in all 32 cases.  The algebraic factor identity, not the finite
regression set, is the proof.

\paragraph{Blind acquisition and edit correction require different resources.}
The 24-symbol phase word has twelve distinct cyclic length-two words, so clean blind
acquisition needs two symbols.  Blind insertion/deletion correction without an epoch is
impossible: adjacent phase words are related by deleting the first symbol and appending
one symbol, hence have Levenshtein distance at most two at every finite length.  With phase
locked before a six-source-symbol burst, exhaustive enumeration of 41641 distinct traces
containing at most two substitutions, insertions, or deletions gives the relock census
\[
 34411\text{ in one symbol},\quad
 7148\text{ in two},\quad
 81\text{ in three},\quad
 1\text{ in four}.
\]
Every trace relocks.  The hardware therefore carries a trusted epoch and a private
three-layer, twelve-phase Levenshtein mask per recursive guest.

\paragraph{Correction to the collision frontier.}
The earlier 18-collision set
$\{CX_{fp},Z_0,Z_1,Z_2\}$ is connected on the 81 frame vectors but generates only 243
affine transformations.  It is not a universal ISA.  Classifying all
$\binom{10}{4}=210$ frozen four-generator sets by linear subgroup order, invariant
translation dimension, affine order, and collision count gives 24 universal sets and a
true universal minimum of 36 collisions.  Eight sets attain it; the selected representative
is
\[
 \{CX_{fp},CX_{pf},F_f,Z_0\}.
\]
Full breadth-first enumeration on all 4199040 affine transformations gives

\paragraph{Recursive physical controller.}
The source RTL combines the five fixed-point Fourier lanes, sixteen private causal states,
sixteen private edit-mask triples, a frozen action-rate selector, explicit per-context
reset, both ISA dispatch maps, and two 72-entry calibration banks with atomic bank commit.
At eighteen bits per log factor, storage falls from 878868 to 66546 bits.  A one-factor-per-
cycle design point at 100~MHz would rise from 2048.09 to 27048.96 posterior sweeps per
second.  The frequency is a design assumption; simulation, synthesis, placement, timing,
optical rates, heat, and calibrated likelihoods remain separately gated.
