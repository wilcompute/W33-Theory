# Pass 558 — the three five-cube fibres form a PG(3,2) triality

The three 80-word fibres with translation dimension four share the same 16-word translation subgroup. Quotienting by it produces 15 points: all nonzero points of a four-dimensional binary vector space. The three fibres become three disjoint five-caps, each the unique zero set of a nonsingular quadratic form on those 15 points.

Their partition stabilizer in `GL(4,2)` has order 60 and presentation

`C15 semidirect C4`, with `s r s^-1 = r^2`.

It acts as the full `S3` on the three elliptic quadrics. The kernel is `D10`, exactly matching the physical component action found in Pass 553. The quartic values therefore form the `1 + 2` permutation representation of `S3`; they are a single quartic-covariant orbit, not a degree-based `Z3` grading.
