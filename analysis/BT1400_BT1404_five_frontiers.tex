\section{Modular Mackey localization, selector Fourier transform, and the apartment bridge}
\label{sec:bt1400-bt1404-five-frontiers}

Let $X$ be the $120$ line-matching selectors, let
$H\cong C_3^3\rtimes(D_8\times C_2)$ be a selector stabilizer, and put
$\mathcal A=\operatorname{End}_H(\mathbb Q^X)$.

\begin{theorem}[Five-frontier selector theorem]
The following exact statements hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The fourteen rational Mackey projectors have denominator lcms
\[
36,18,54,18,54,18,54,54,54,27,54,54,54,108.
\]
Thus one projector is integral at $2$, none is integral at $3$, and all are
integral at $5$.  The full orbital algebra has radical powers
$(45,16,0)$ at $2$ and $(72,49,27,14,4,0)$ at $3$, while its reduction at
$5$ is split semisimple with factor census $1^7,2^4,3^9,4^4,5^5$.

\item The complement action on $\widehat{C_3^3}\cong\mathbb F_3^3$ has one
invariant axis and one invariant plane with a nonsingular square form.  Its
six orbits are zero, hinge charge, two neutral square-norm classes, and their
two charged lifts, of sizes $1,2,4,4,8,8$.

\item There is an explicit $U\in GL_{120}(\mathbb Q)$ whose column blocks have
dimensions
\[
1,2,2,4,4,8,8,2,4,12,12,24,32,5.
\]
Its inverse is exact, and $U^{-1}AU$, $U^{-1}DU$, and $U^{-1}SU$ are block
diagonal across the fourteen Mackey sectors.

\item The hinge-selected $1110_{r0}$ matrix has $2160$ boundaryless apartment
rows and rank $81$, hence spans the full Levi cycle space.  Four gauge-fixed
selector--apartment compositions are boundaryless and have rank $81$.  This
does not contradict the vanishing natural $G$-equivariant selector--Steinberg
Hom, because the hinge and sign characters break full $G$-equivariance.

\item Let $O$ be the orbital order and let
\[
M=\mathbb Z^7\oplus M_2(\mathbb Z)^2\oplus M_3(\mathbb Z)^3
\oplus M_4(\mathbb Z)\oplus M_5(\mathbb Z)
\]
be the split maximal order selected by the frozen rational matrix units.
The two orders are incomparable.  For $L=O\cap M$,
\[
[M:L]=2^{38}3^{113},\qquad [O:L]=2^2,
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{disc}(O)=2^{72}3^{226}.
\]
Moreover $2O\subset M$ and $108M\subset O$, so only $2$ and $3$ obstruct
maximality; the two orders agree locally at $5$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The Fourier bases within repeated copies remain a rational gauge; the apartment
bridge is not a natural Morita equivalence; and the selected split maximal order
need not be a maximal overorder of the orbital order.  These are finite exact
statements with no physics or literature-priority claim.
