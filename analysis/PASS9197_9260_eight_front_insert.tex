\subsection*{Carrier symmetry, selector sign, and the transverse-glue phase space}

The three rank-$24$ carriers of the same $W(3,3)$ quotient remain sharply distinguishable after one resolves their carrier symmetries.  For the line-supported $E_6^4$ carrier, four independent local order-nine phase actions induce a group of order $27$ on the quotient, while the signed tetracode monomial automorphisms induce the complete $S_4$ permutation action on the four visible line points.  Together they generate the full line stabilizer,
\[
 |G_{\rm line}|=1296\quad\hbox{in }\Sp(4,3),
 \qquad
 |\overline G_{\rm line}|=648=3^3\!:\!S_4
\]
projectively.  Thus the four-point $E_6^4$ root shadow is not merely a support set: its carrier centralizer realizes the complete $W(3,3)$ line symmetry.

The Niemeier automorphism-quotient images provide a second independent discriminator.  Modulo the root Weyl groups, the $E_8^3$ carrier survives as the order-three factor cycle in $S_3$; the diagonal $E_6^4$ carrier dies completely in the $GL_2(3)$ quotient; and the $A_2^{12}$ carrier survives as an order-three signed monomial element of $2.M_{12}$ projecting to the fixed-point-free $M_{12}$ class $3B$ of cycle shape $3^4$.  Hence the common $W(3,3)$ quotient does not determine the umbral image.

\paragraph{A marked line is not a Suzuki selector.}
The exact $M_{12}{:}2$ polarization inside the $2.\mathrm{Suz}$ six-qutrit module produces $7371$ nondegenerate two-spaces $A$, and each candidate

Consequently identifying the distinguished Niemeier root-shadow line with the first polarization line leaves all $7371$ candidates; the exact $C_4\times A_5$ similitude controller still has $90$ nondegenerate candidate orbits. \[

This is a characteristic-three finite-field complex structure generated by the pair of Niemeier glues; it is distinct from the characteristic-two modular modules denoted $U_6$ elsewhere in this manuscript.
