\subsection{The thirty-six doily sections are a second generating system for $C_{\rm spread}$}
\label{sec:pass7241-7248-double-six-slice-generator}

Let $M$ be the canonical $45\times36$ tritangent/double-six incidence matrix, with
\[
 M_{tD}=1
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 |t\cap D|=2.
\]
For each double-six $D$, let the $D$-column of $N$ indicate the fifteen tritangent planes disjoint from $D$, equivalently the fifteen syntheme coordinates of the doily section isolated in Section~\ref{sec:pass7217-7232-double-six-doily}.  A tritangent meets a double-six in either zero or two lines, so over $\mathbb F_2$ one has the exact incidence identity
\[
 \boxed{N=J+M}.
\]

Exact reconstruction gives
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{2}M=20,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}_{2}N=21.
\]
More strongly, on the same forty-five tritangent coordinates,
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{col}(M)=C_{\rm spread}^{\rm even}},
 \qquad
 \boxed{\operatorname{col}(N)=C_{\rm spread}=[45,21,5]_2}.
\]
Thus the Pass7184 decomposition $C_{\rm spread}=\mathbf1\oplus V_{20}$ has a direct cubic-surface incidence realization: the even twenty-space is generated by the ordinary tritangent/double-six selector columns, while complementing those columns supplies the odd line and yields the full twenty-one-space.

The thirty-six doily sections themselves form a two-intersection family.  Every section contains fifteen tritangents and every tritangent lies in twelve sections.  For two distinct double-sixes $D,D'$,
\[
 |S_D\cap S_{D'}|=
 \begin{cases}
 6,& |D\cap D'|=6,\\
 3,& |D\cap D'|\ne6.
 \end{cases}
\]
There are respectively $360$ and $270$ such pairs, exactly the edge/nonedge split of the standard $36$-vertex double-six graph $H_{36}$.

Consequently the same code now has two explicit geometric generating systems:
\[
 \boxed{27\ \text{ten-}D_4\text{ spread words}}
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \boxed{36\ \text{double-six doily-section words}},
\]
with identical binary span.  This is an exact finite incidence/coding identity; no continuum or physical claim is inferred.
