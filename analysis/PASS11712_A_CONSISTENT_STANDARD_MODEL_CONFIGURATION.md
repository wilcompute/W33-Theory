## What changed relative to Pass 11703

* **Pass 11703 was compatibility in the wrong grade.** Its line-trinification Standard Model makes colour out of *three-fermion* generators. **No pure-state vacuum can leave that colour unbroken** (11706, exact: the vacuum's centraliser is su(8) ⊕ u(1) in the operator grade).
* **The consistent configuration is the operator-type Standard Model.** Colour and weak rotate Hilbert-space levels, and the vacuum sits on a level they do not touch.
* **Hypercharge is no longer a free choice.** It is the unique generator that annihilates the vacuum, and it is the Georgi–Glashow one.

## What it still does not explain

* **Why these clocks.** A dynamics choosing the clock pair, or the single tick R² ⊗ R⁻¹, is not derived. The clock-Hamiltonian ground-state principle fails (11706).
* **Which hand, and which sign of Y.** Both remain unpredicted. The configuration is consistent with either outcome; it does not choose the observed one.
* **Families, masses, couplings and spacetime.** Not addressed. The five SU(5) levels carry the hypercharges of one 5̄ (or 5) multiplet. This is a statement about quantum numbers of levels, not a derivation of three generations of fermion fields.
* **Relation to the Weil split.** It is open whether the 5 + 4 level split is related to the Weil parity split 9 = 5 + 4 (`analysis/THE_SELECTION_LAYER.md`, Pass 2448).
