\subsubsection*{Passes 9465--9528: the glue pair is unitary, oriented, and root-shadow periodic}

alternating form $K$, the exchange $S$, and $R=KS$ with $R^2=-I$.  Setting

\[
 C_{\operatorname{Sp}(12,3)}(R)\cong U(6,3),\qquad
 |U(6,3)|=182699779456696320.
\]

halves are mutually $B$-orthogonal minus-type six-spaces and are simultaneously
Lagrangian for $K$.  The involution $S$ preserves $B$, reverses $K$, and obeys
$SRS=-R$.  Exhausting signed coordinate monomials gives a further rigidity:
although the $E_6$-relative glue alone has $62208$ such symmetries, the common
stabilizer of the ordered pair of concrete glue codes is only $\{\pm I\}$.
