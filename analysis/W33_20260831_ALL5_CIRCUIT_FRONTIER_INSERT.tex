\subsection{All-five circuit frontier closure: Clifford restriction, Eisenstein sixes, kernel arithmetic, and rank-76 coherent geometry}
\label{sec:all5-circuit-frontier-20260831}

Let $M$ be the $216\times540$ maximal-overlap incidence matrix between the sentinel five- and six-circuit shells, and let $M_\pm$ be its two $\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)$ orbital colours.  With
\[
M_+M_+^{\mathsf T}=M_-M_-^{\mathsf T}=10I+A_{30},\qquad
M_+M_-^{\mathsf T}+M_-M_+^{\mathsf T}=4A_{20},
\]
the commuting relations $A_{30},A_{20}$ have the previously certified joint-sector dimensions
\[
1,15,15,20,24,60,81.
\]
The present audit closes five outstanding structural questions simultaneously.

First, let $K$ be the order-$648$ W33 point stabilizer with centre $Z(K)=C_3$.  Exact rational spectral projectors and traces on all elements of $K$ give the central-isotypic dimensions $(1,\omega,\omega^2)$ and restricted-character norms
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\dim & (d_1,d_\omega,d_{\omega^2}) & \langle\chi,\chi\rangle_K\\ \hline
15&(3,6,6)&3\\
15&(3,6,6)&3\\
81&(27,27,27)&12\\
20&(8,6,6)&3\\
60&(18,21,21)&12\\
24&(12,6,6)&5\\
1&(1,0,0)&1
\end{array}
\]
without guessing irreducible labels for the reducible restrictions.  In particular the $24$-sector agrees with the sharper decomposition
\[
24\downarrow_K=1\oplus3\oplus8\oplus6_\omega\oplus6_{\bar\omega}.
\]
On its $12$-dimensional rational nontrivial-central carrier, the canonical deck-orientation operator
\[
J=z-z^2
\]
satisfies the exact identity
\[
\boxed{J^2=-3I}.
\]
Thus scalar extension to $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})=\mathbb Q(\omega)$ splits this rational carrier into the conjugate six-dimensional $\omega$ and $\omega^2$ eigenspaces.  Six explicitly certified central deck fibres provide a rational basis witness, giving a concrete Eisenstein realization of the two sixes.

Second, over characteristic zero,
\[
\operatorname{rank}M=\operatorname{rank}M_+=\operatorname{rank}M_-=216,
\]
so all three right kernels in the $540$-dimensional six-circuit space have dimension $324$.  The stacked colour matrix has rank $372$, hence
\[
\boxed{\dim(\operatorname{row}M_+\cap\operatorname{row}M_-)=60},
\]
while
\[
\boxed{\dim(\ker M\cap\ker M_+\cap\ker M_-)=168},
\]
and every pairwise kernel intersection is the same $168$-space because $M=M_++M_-$.  The right Gram spectrum is
\[
0^{324}+4^{96}+16^{35}+28^{60}+64^{24}+160^1.
\]
The numerical match between the common colour row-space dimension $60$ and the existing $60$-dimensional left spectral sector is recorded as a target, not yet promoted to an equality of modules.

Third, the modular ranks of $(M,M_+,MM^{\mathsf T})$ at $p=2,3,5,7,11,13$ are respectively
\[
(156,201,0),\ (216,216,216),\ (216,216,215),\ (216,216,156),\ (216,216,216),\ (216,216,216).
\]
The exact Gram determinant has prime valuations
\[
v_2=601,\qquad v_5=1,\qquad v_7=60.
\]
Cauchy--Binet together with full row rank modulo $5$ and $7$ therefore forces every nonunit Smith invariant of $M$ to be a power of $2$; the mod-$2$ rank fixes their number:
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{SNF}_{\mathrm{nonzero}}(M)=1^{156}\oplus(2\text{-power})^{60}.}
\]
Likewise the colour incidence has
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{SNF}_{\mathrm{nonzero}}(M_\pm)=1^{201}\oplus(2\text{-power})^{15}.}
\]
The individual positive $2$-adic exponents are not asserted here.

Finally, the complete two-fibre Schurian coherent configuration has same- and cross-fibre orbital ranks
\[
r_{55}=10,\qquad r_{66}=32,\qquad r_{56}=r_{65}=17,
\]
and hence exact total rank
\[
\boxed{76}.
\]
Inside the $10$-orbital five-circuit commutant, $A_{30}$ is a single orbital whereas $A_{20}$ is the fusion of exactly two transpose-paired directed orbitals of valency $10+10$.  For
\[
K_c=M_+M_-^{\mathsf T},
\]
the two directed orbitals carry constant coefficients $1$ and $3$, so
\[
\boxed{K_c+K_c^{\mathsf T}=4A_{20}},
\]
but
\[
[K_c,A_{20}]\neq0,\qquad [K_c,A_{30}]\neq0.
\]
Therefore the seven-sector algebra is a symmetric commutative fusion of a genuinely noncommutative orbital algebra; the directed orientation datum is erased by symmetrization rather than supplied by an additional commuting eigenoperator.
