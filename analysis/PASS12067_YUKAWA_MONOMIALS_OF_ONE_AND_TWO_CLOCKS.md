# Pass 12067: Yukawa monomials of one and two clocks

Claude track. Builds on 12064 and 12066.

## Results

The 45 tritangents of the 27 of E6(p) are the monomials of the cubic invariant (27³). Their orbits under each vacuum
symmetry:

| Vacuum | Symmetry | Orbits on the 45 | Invariant monomials |
|---|---|---|---|
| one clock | 432 | 9 + 36 | none; the 9 are the clock's own far-point monomials |
| two non-collinear clocks | C₂×Q₈ | 1 + 8 + 4+4+4 + 8+8+8 | **one**, the second clock's |
| … plus triality ticks | 48 | 1 + 8 + 12 + 24 | one |
| two collinear clocks | ±1×S₃ | 1+1+1 + 3¹⁰ + 6² | **three**, disjoint and outside the 9, and exactly the three 3-orbits of matter (27 = 3+3+3 + 6+6+6) |

## Reading

- An incompatible clock pair leaves one invariant Yukawa coupling: a rank-one texture. That is the shape the corpus's
  Weber law gives at leading order (11872/11938).
- A compatible pair leaves three disjoint invariant couplings: a diagonal three-family texture.

## Scope

Invariance under the finite vacuum symmetry only. Magnitudes and the generation assignment are not derived.
