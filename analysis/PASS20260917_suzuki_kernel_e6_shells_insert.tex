\subsection{The hidden $E_6$ binary carrier inside the Suzuki W33 kernel}
\label{sec:20260917-suzuki-kernel-e6-shells}

The stabilizer calculation identifies the linear kernel of one embedded W33 block as the
extraspecial minus group
\[
 K_{\rm lin}\cong 2_-^{1+6},\qquad |K_{\rm lin}|=128.
\]
For an extraspecial $2$-group the quotient by the center carries the canonical quadratic
form $q(\bar x)=x^2\in Z(K)\cong\mathbf F_2$.  Minus type therefore gives
\[
 K_{\rm lin}/Z(K_{\rm lin})\cong \mathbf F_2^6
\]
with a nondegenerate minus quadratic form.  Its $63$ nonzero vectors split as
\[
 \boxed{63=27+36},
\]
with $27$ nonzero singular vectors and $36$ nonsingular vectors.

This is not only a count.  The $27$ singular vectors form the elliptic quadric
$Q^-(5,2)=GQ(2,4)$; polar orthogonality gives
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{SRG}(27,10,1,5)}.
\]
The executable certificate constructs this graph and an explicit vertex-by-vertex
isomorphism to the repository's independent W33 center-quad quotient
\[
 90\longrightarrow45\longrightarrow27,
\]
whose $27$ quotient lines carry the same cubic-surface incidence graph.  Thus the new
Suzuki stabilizer kernel itself contains a canonical binary realization of the old
W33$\to E_6$ $27$-line carrier.

The complementary $36$ nonsingular vectors are equally suggestive.  Under the inner
$U_4(2)\cong\mathrm{PSp}(4,3)$ action their stabilizer has order
\[
 25920/36=720,
\]
matching the already certified transitive $36$-object family
\[
 \text{W33 spreads}\leftrightarrow\text{Schl\"afli double-sixes}
 \leftrightarrow\text{doily complements}
 \leftrightarrow\text{Pfaffian sections}.
\]
Under the full orthogonal action the stabilizer doubles to
\[
 51840/36=1440,
\]
which is exactly the classical full double-six stabilizer.  At this stage the $27$ bridge
is objectwise, while the $36$ bridge is an exact orbit/stabilizer fingerprint; the explicit
nonsingular-vector-to-spread intertwiner remains open.

Most importantly, the extension puzzle is now structural.  The full automorphism group of
the minus quadratic quotient is
\[
 \operatorname{Out}(2_-^{1+6})\cong O_6^-(2)\cong W(E_6),
\]
whereas the Suzuki W33 block stabilizer acts through only the index-two subgroup
\[
 \Omega_6^-(2)\cong U_4(2)\cong\mathrm{PSp}(4,3).
\]
So the ``missing'' outer $E_6$ involution is not mysterious: it is an intrinsic automorphism
of the extraspecial kernel that is not induced by conjugation from the ambient Suzuki block
stabilizer.  This explains simultaneously why the local $27$-carrier admits the full Weyl
symmetry and why the actual $2.\mathrm{Suz}$ action sees only the inner half.

The executable artifacts are
\texttt{analysis/w33\_suzuki\_w33\_kernel\_structure\_reconciliation.py} and
\texttt{analysis/w33\_suzuki\_kernel\_e6\_quadratic\_shell\_bridge.py}, with frozen JSON
certificates of the same names under \texttt{data/}.  These are finite-group and finite-
geometry statements; no physical implementation of the missing outer automorphism is inferred.
