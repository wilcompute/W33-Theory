\subsection{Passes 5300--5307: the exact 576 bridge and the post-$q=9$ frontier}

The numerical coincidence $576=576$ between the Hoffman-cover stabilizer and the
number of order-four Latin squares admits a precise refinement.  Let $H$ be the
setwise stabilizer of the thirteen-cell Hoffman cover in $\operatorname{PSp}_4(5)$.
Then
\[
 |H|=576,\qquad Z(H)\cong C_2,
\]
and the computation identifies
\[
 H\cong 2_{+}^{1+4}:(S_3\times C_3).
\]
The full autoparatopy group of the Klein $V_4$ Latin square also has order $576$,
but it is a different group,
\[
 \operatorname{AutPar}(L_{V_4})\cong 2^4:(S_3\times S_3).
\]
Their centers, derived subgroup orders, and element-order distributions differ.
The genuine bridge appears one level down: if $L^+$ denotes the index-two subgroup
of even parastrophes, then an explicit $\mathrm{GL}_4(2)$ change of basis gives
\[
 \boxed{H/Z(H)\cong L^+\cong 2^4:(S_3\times C_3).}
\]
Thus the Hoffman order-$576$ group is a central double lift of the same affine
$\mathbb F_2^4$ symmetry carried by the orientation-even half of the Klein Latin
chart; equality of the integers alone is not used as evidence.

This exposes an exact connection with the earlier toroidal-knight construction.
The sixteen cells of the Klein $4\times4$ chart are
\[
 \mathbb F_2^2\times\mathbb F_2^2\cong\mathbb F_2^4,
\]
the same sixteen-point substrate on which the toroidal knight graph was proved to
be $Q_4$.  The Latin affine-plane completion partitions the fifteen nonzero
translation directions into five $PG(3,2)$ lines: row, column, symbol, and two
additional orthogonal-mate directions.  The Hoffman/Latin-even linear quotient
has direction orbits $9+6$, exactly three of these spread lines plus two spread
lines.  In contrast, $Q_4$ uses only four basis directions, two from the row line
and two from the column line.  The order-$288$ affine quotient does not preserve
that four-direction set, so the shared theorem is the common $\mathbb F_2^4$
substrate, not an equality of edge graphs.

The order-four Latin census itself is
\[
 576=144+432,
\]
with a $144$-element Klein-$V_4$ isotopy class and a $432$-element cyclic-$C_4$
class.  The complete three-MOLS$(4)$ geometry of the Klein chart is a five-line
spread of $PG(3,2)$.  There are exactly $56$ such spreads; $\mathrm{GL}_4(2)$ is
transitive on them and a spread stabilizer has order $360$.  Hence the
$A_5$-invariant five-line spread independently found in every $q=5$ $K_0$ fiber is
$\mathrm{GL}_4(2)$-conjugate to a complete-MOLS$(4)$ spread.  A canonical
objectwise identification of the particular labels remains open.

The Hoffman shortening frontier also contracts.  The $715$ four-subsets of the
thirteen cover cells form only ten stabilizer orbits, with orbit-size/rank pairs
\[
 (3,33),(12,33),(16,35),(144,35),(96,38),(144,38),
 (36,39),(48,40),(72,40),(144,40).
\]
Pass 5294 already proves every span involving at most three cells has minimum
forty.  Therefore the first unresolved layer has ten four-cell representatives.
Bounded exact searches find weight-forty words but do not certify the absence of
weights $28,32,36$; accordingly the shortened distance remains
\[
 d_{\rm sh}\in\{28,32,36,40\}.
\]

On the all-odd footprint side, the exact rank formula gains a new finite anchor:
for $q=13$ the point/P-component incidence matrix has $2380$ rows, $14365$
columns, point-footprint weight $169$, and
\[
 \operatorname{rank}_2F=1105=\frac{13(13^2+1)}2.
\]
Together with $q=3,5,7,9,11$, this gives six exact anchors.  The general theorem
remains equivalent to the same-point generation statement $K_0=D_q$.

At $q=11$, every P-carrier has size $24$ and any two distinct carriers intersect
in zero or two W-points.  Hence a hypothetical dual word supported on twenty
carriers has, writing $d_p$ for point multiplicities and $e$ for selected
carrier-graph edges,
\[
 2e=\sum_p\binom{d_p}{2},\qquad \sum_p d_p=480,\qquad d_p\equiv0\pmod2,
\]
so
\[
 \boxed{e\ge120}.
\]
Equality holds precisely when $240$ W-points are covered twice, in which case the
selected graph is $12$-regular.  The conjectural $K_{10,10}$ plus two-factor
support saturates this lower bound.  Symmetry-broken bounded MILPs produced no
incumbent but no infeasibility certificate, so neither a weight-twenty dual shell
nor $d(C_F)=121$ is promoted here.

Finally, the complete $2340$-word $q=5$ minimum $K_0$ shell has a finer symmetry
than its six Hamming distances reveal.  The transitive $\operatorname{PSp}_4(5)$
action has point stabilizer order $2000$ and orbital rank
\[
 \boxed{21},
\]
with subdegree histogram $1^3,4^3,50^9,125^3,500^3$.  Thus the Hamming-distance
partition is a fusion of a rank-$21$ coherent configuration.  In a separate
negative theorem, the two previously noted $64$-dimensional modules are not
isomorphic: a symplectic transvection fixes dimension $4$ on
$\ker_2N/\langle\mathbf1\rangle$ but dimension $24$ on the footprint hull.  Hence
there is no $\operatorname{PSp}_4(5)$-equivariant invertible $64\times64$
intertwiner between them.

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
The $q=5$ apartment distance $625$ and the $q=9$ footprint distance $81$ remain
closed theorems.  Passes 5300--5307 do not prove the general odd-$q$ generation
statement $K_0=D_q$, do not prove a $q=11$ weight-twenty dual shell or footprint
distance $121$, and do not promote the Hoffman-shortened distance to $40$.
The Latin/Hoffman/toroidal statements are finite group and affine-geometry
identifications; no physical dynamics are inferred from them.
