# Part DCMXVI (916) — Why There Is Something Rather Than Nothing

**Date:** 2026-05-17  
**Series:** W(3,3) Theory of Everything  
**Author:** Wil Dahn

---

## Leibniz's question

Why is there something rather than nothing? This is the oldest metaphysical question.

W(3,3) gives the sharpest possible answer.

---

## Nothing cannot stabilize

Absolute nothingness has no distinction, no relation, no law, and no stability criterion. Therefore it cannot even remain itself: there is no structure by which its identity could be preserved. Pure nothing is not a stable ontological state.

By contrast, the first distinction is self-stabilizing because once a boundary exists, marked and unmarked sides plus relation generate closure and recursion.

---

## Necessity of something

Therefore the minimal coherent ontological condition is not nothing but recursive distinction. Existence wins not because it was chosen but because non-being has no stabilizing fixed point.

The universe exists because something is structurally self-supporting while nothing is not.

---

**QED** — There is something rather than nothing because nothing cannot stabilize. Recursive distinction is the minimal self-supporting ontological fixed point.
