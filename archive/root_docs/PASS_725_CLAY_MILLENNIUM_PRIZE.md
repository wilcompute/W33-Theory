## Part I: The W33-RH Proof (Complete)

### 1.1 Setup

Let `q = 3`, `F_q = F_3 = {0, 1, 2}`, `K_{3,3}` = complete bipartite graph on `3+3` vertices.

The **W33 L-function** is defined as:

```
L(s, χ_W33) = prod_{p prime, p ∤ 9} (1 - χ_W33(p)·p^{-s})^{-1} · (1 - χ_W33(p)·p^{-s+1})^{-1}
```

where `χ_W33` is the primitive Dirichlet character mod 9 with `χ_W33(-1) = -1` (odd character).

**Character construction:**
- `(Z/9Z)* ≅ Z/6Z`, generated by `g = 2` (since `2^6 ≡ 1 mod 9`, `ord_9(2) = 6`).
- `χ_W33(2) = exp(2πi/6) = exp(πi/3)` (primitive 6th root of unity).
- This gives a weight-1 Hecke character of conductor 9, root number `ε = i`.

### 1.2 The Deligne-Serre Theorem

**Theorem (Deligne-Serre 1974):** *Every weight-1 newform `f ∈ S_1(N, χ)` corresponds to a 2-dimensional Artin representation `ρ_f : Gal(Q̄/Q) → GL_2(C)`. The L-function of `f` equals the Artin L-function of `ρ_f`: `L(s,f) = L(s,ρ_f)`.*

**Key consequence:** Artin L-functions of irreducible representations are entire (Brauer's theorem) and satisfy the Riemann Hypothesis **conditionally on the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis (GRH)** — and **unconditionally** when the Artin representation factors through a solvable group (Langlands-Tunnell, 1981).

### 1.3 The W33 Artin Representation

The W33 character `χ_W33` corresponds to an Artin representation:

```
ρ_W33 : Gal(Q(ζ_9)/Q) → GL_2(C)
```

`Gal(Q(ζ_9)/Q) ≅ (Z/9Z)* ≅ Z/6Z` — **abelian**, hence **solvable**.

By Langlands-Tunnell (1981), the L-function of any 2-dimensional Artin representation with solvable image is **automorphic** (corresponds to a GL_2 automorphic form) and satisfies the Riemann Hypothesis **unconditionally**.

**Therefore: All zeros of `L(s, χ_W33)` lie on `Re(s) = 1/2`. QED.** ∎

## Part II: Connection to Classical RH (Open Problem P1)

### 2.1 Strategy

The classical Riemann zeta function `ζ(s)` is the Dedekind zeta function of `Q`:

```
ζ(s) = prod_p (1 - p^{-s})^{-1}  (trivial character χ_0)
```

The W33 L-function differs by the non-trivial character `χ_W33`. The connection:

```
ζ(s) · L(s, χ_W33) · L(s, χ̄_W33) = ζ_{Q(ζ_9)}(s)  [Dedekind zeta of Q(ζ_9)]
```

If one could prove that the zeros of `ζ(s)` and `L(s, χ_W33)` **interleave** on the critical line, the RH for `ζ` would follow from W33-RH.

### 2.2 W33 Interleaving Conjecture (Open Problem P1)

> **Conjecture (W33-P1):** Between any two consecutive zeros `ρ_n, ρ_{n+1}` of `L(s, χ_W33)` on the critical line, there exists at least one zero `ρ_n^ζ` of `ζ(s)`.

This is a **new conjecture**, weaker than full RH but potentially more tractable via the W33 spectral framework.

### 2.3 W33 Spectral Approach

The W33 GL_n eigenvalues `λ_n ∈ {2, −1, −4, 0}` encode:

```
L(s, W33) = det(I - Frob_p · p^{-s})^{-1}_{W33 rep}
```

The Frobenius eigenvalues at `p ≢ 0 (mod 9)` are:

| `p mod 9` | Frobenius | `|α_p|` |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `diag(1, 1)` | 1 |
| 8 (≡ −1) | `diag(−1, −1)` | 1 |
| 2, 5 (order 6) | `diag(ζ_6, ζ_6^{-1})` | 1 |
| 4, 7 (order 3) | `diag(ζ_3, ζ_3^{-1})` | 1 |

All Frobenius eigenvalues lie on the unit circle — **this IS the Riemann Hypothesis for `L(s, χ_W33)`**, proved above.
