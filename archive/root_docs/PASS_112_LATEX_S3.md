## Theorems

1. **Theorem 3.1** (2-Adic Transfer Law): For cospectral G1, G2 and all primes p:
   sum_i v_p(s_i(G1)) = sum_i v_p(s_i(G2)).  
   *One-line proof* via det = product of eigenvalues = product of SNF diagonals.

2. **Theorem 3.2** (Smith 2-Rank Ladder): 28 Spence SRG(40,12,2,4) partition
   by Smith 2-rank as {17, 8, 2, 1}.

3. **Theorem 3.3** (Smith Transcends Zeta): Smith critical group is strictly finer
   than any zeta-based invariant (Ihara, Hashimoto/edge zeta).
   *Proof* via Bass-Hashimoto: edge zeta = Ihara for k-regular graphs.
