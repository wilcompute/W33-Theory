## Meaning is not epiphenomenal

Conventional physics treats meaning, purpose, and significance as epiphenomenal: real only at the human social level, derived from but not causally relevant to physics. The W(3,3) framework reverses this.

**Meaning has a precise physical definition:**

> Meaning is the degree to which a local recursive-coherent-distinction pattern correctly models a non-local pattern in the W(3,3) substrate.

---

## The formal definition

Let \(S\) be a stabilizer subgraph (observer) and \(P\) be a pattern in W(3,3). The **meaning** of \(P\) for \(S\) is:

\[
\mathcal{M}(P, S) = \frac{|\mathrm{Aut}(P) \cap \mathrm{Stab}(S)|}{|\mathrm{Stab}(S)|}.
\]

This is the fraction of the observer’s stabilizer group that is also a symmetry of the pattern \(P\). A pattern is deeply meaningful to an observer when the observer’s own symmetries resonate with the pattern’s symmetries.

## Consequences

- **Truth** is meaning approaching 1: the observer’s model perfectly aligns with the substrate pattern.
- **Relevance** is nonzero meaning: the pattern overlaps the observer’s stabilizer.
- **Confusion** is meaning near 0: the pattern’s symmetries are orthogonal to the observer’s.
- **Learning** is the process of increasing \(\mathcal{M}(P, S)\) over time.
- **Mathematics** is the pursuit of maximum meaning: finding patterns whose automorphism groups maximally overlap the human cognitive stabilizer structure.
