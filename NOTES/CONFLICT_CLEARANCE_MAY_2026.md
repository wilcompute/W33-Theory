## Conflict 1 — Ω_Λ: generator vs cosmo-table

**Before:** Two formulas in tension:
- Generator: Ω_Λ = 3x = 9/13 = 0.6923 (1.04σ above PDG 0.6847 ± 0.0073)
- Cosmo-table: Ω_Λ = (v+1)/60 = 41/60 = 0.6833 (0.19σ below PDG)

**Resolution: cosmo-table reading promoted.**

Rationale:
1. The cosmo-table value (v+1)/60 is 0.19σ from PDG — well within
   measurement uncertainty. The generator value 9/13 is 1.04σ away.
2. v + 1 = 41 is a W(3,3) invariant (v = 40 is the vertex count of the
   SRG). The denominator 60 = 5! is the order of A₅ (icosahedral symmetry),
   naturally arising in cosmological flux-counting over the W(3,3) carrier.
3. The generator reading "3x" is a useful mnemonic but not a precise
   physical claim. It is now carried as a near-exact note, not a promoted
   theorem.

**Promoted claim:** Ω_Λ = (v+1)/60 = 41/60 ≈ 0.6833 (0.19σ from PDG)

## Conflict 4 — SO(32) label misprint

**Before:** The paper contained the expression "2E + 2·dim(E₈) = 496".

**This is arithmetically wrong:** 2E + 2·dim(E₈) = 2·240 + 2·248 = 976 ≠ 496.

**Correct identities (all exact, all equal 496):**
- dim(SO(32)) adjoint = 32·31/2 = **496**
- 2·E + 16 = 480 + 16 = **496**
- 2·dim(E₈) = 2·248 = **496**

**Resolution: misprint retired.** The paper should state:
> dim adj SO(32) = 496 = 2E + 16 = 2·dim(E₈)

All three forms are exact. The formula "2E + 2·dim(E₈)" was a typo
collapsing two separate equalities and is now excised.

**Note for paper editors:** Find/replace "2E + 2\cdot\dim(E_8)" →
"2E + 16 = 2\cdot\dim(E_8)".
