\subsection{What stands, and what closed}

Two different kinds of result are in this paper, and they deserve to be read differently.

\emph{The mathematics stands.} Everything in Sections~2--8 is exact and independent of any physical
reading: the forty points as the commutation geometry of a qutrit acting on itself, the finite light
cone, the four clocks and the tetracode, $E_8$ from nine histories and its $6{:}1$ fibration over the
forty points, the Freudenthal quartic and its $57$-dimensional cone, the real form in which time has one
dimension, and the Albert algebra with its $\so(1,9)$ and chiral $\mathbf{16}$. None of it depends on a
string construction, and none of it was touched by the corrections below.

\emph{The string route closed.} Section~9 asked whether heterotic orbifolds carrying the forty-point twist
give our universe. The answer, reached by exact computation over nine orbifold families and checked
against the selection rules the worldsheet establishes, is no in all nine: two of them yield no Standard Model at all; in six, their supersymmetric vacua
cannot keep the proton stable; in the ninth, $\Z_3\times\Z_3$, the parity survives and the vacua are
supersymmetric, but a counting argument over hidden representations leaves six massless particles of charge
$1/3$ in every one. That is a finished negative result --- it tells the programme where the forty points do
\emph{not} lead, and it isolates what a successful route must do differently: provide a vacuum in which the
Fayet--Iliopoulos term is cancelled by something other than a sneutrino-like field, with a hidden sector whose
representations pair up.

\subsection{Seven open problems, stated sharply}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.},leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=5pt]
\item \textbf{A realistic vacuum.} Every parity built from the gauge torus, the space group and the
      established rotation symmetries is excluded in $\Z_6$-I, $\Z_6$-II, $\Z_{12}$-I (where the $E_6$
      lattice itself fixes the admissible rule), $\Z_2\times\Z_6$, $\Z_3\times\Z_6$ and $\Z_6\times\Z_6$.
      The one family where it survives is $\Z_3\times\Z_3$: its $28$ parity vacua are supersymmetric
      ($W=F(p)$, $\Z_3$-graded) but carry six massless charge-$\tfrac13$ states; its $54$ parity vacua
      with the hidden $SU(4)$ condensed cure that count but leave at least $120$ fractional charges
      massless, protected by an unbroken discrete $3$-group. Baryonic and mixed condensates do not free
      them, and neither does any resolution of the orbifold or any breaking of supersymmetry. The $87$
      non-supersymmetric twins of the $\Z_6$-I Standard Models are consistent three-generation models,
      but every one is tachyonic. What remains:
      \begin{itemize}
      \item for the $SO(16)\times SO(16)$ models with the $\W$ shift, a source of the light-generation
            hierarchies (the $\Delta(54)$ flavour symmetry keeps $m_c=m_u$ and $m_\mu=m_e$ at the same order under
            every alignment of the scalar vacuum tried; with a single Higgs triplet a residual $S_3$ locks $m_c=m_u$
            exactly at every minimum of its general $\Delta(54)$-invariant potential, while a misaligned flavon
            triplet splits charm from up but needs an order-one misalignment for the observed size; and the
            stabilised family modulus $T=\rho$ gives $m_c=m_u=m_t/2$) and a
            vacuum at all (no minimum in the tachyon-free region: the Wilson-line moduli roll into a winding
            tachyon and the dilaton runs to weak coupling---but $21$ survivors have a neutral exit and in six of them
            every nearby instability is Standard-Model-neutral; there the potential drives them steeply to it, the
            condensate never touches the up sector but, for a single light Higgs, lowers $m_b/m_t$ to $\epsilon^2$ with
            the tau at the same order in $14$ of $21$ models; the exit is SM-neutral on the whole $B$ circle (exact
            hyperbolic disks at the $\Gamma_0(3)$ points, the duality group itself), its endpoint is the
            non-supersymmetric $U(16)$ string, not a nearby vacuum, every symmetric point of the modulus is tachyonic or a runaway,
            and the light generations stay degenerate under every vacuum and Higgs choice tried);
      \item twists and lattices not yet scanned;
      \item a mechanism other than a parity for proton stability.
      \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{A dynamical principle.} Apart from the vacuum selections of Section~\ref{sec:siegel} --- the
      one-loop minimum at the forty Witting rays (trinification) and the soft-supertrace choice of the
      $\log\tfrac85$ Clifford orbit, both computed for an assumed field content rather than derived from
      $\W$ --- every result in this paper is kinematics. What
      action, Hamiltonian or spectral principle on $\W$ has the structures found here as its
      symmetries, and what does it predict? In particular: does such a principle select the
      clock real form $E_{8(-24)}$ over the split form --- does the geometry
      \emph{choose} one time?
\item \textbf{A continuum limit.} The finite light cones (in $2+1$, $3+1$, $1+9$ and the
      quartic $57$) are exact algebraic objects. Is there a limit, a tower over $W(3,q)$ or a
      lattice construction, in which one of them becomes a smooth Lorentzian manifold with
      dynamical curvature?
\item \textbf{Chirality from outside.} Corollary~9.7 shows that reversing time's orientation,
      acting antiunitarily and exchanging chirality are one coset, so fixing an arrow makes
      chirality conserved but does not choose it. The chamber law now supplies an exact candidate
      order parameter: the two nonsplit cocycle orientations $s=\pm1$ are exchanged by qutrit
      complex conjugation, $\Psi(x\star_s y)=\Psi(x)\star_{-s}\Psi(y)$, and their class-three
      central tick reverses sign. \cert{w33\_20261001\_u81\_antiunitary\_orientation.py}
      The remaining problem is dynamical rather than combinatorial: what selects one sign, and does
      that selected finite orientation couple to the unique global chirality character of
      $\PGSp(4,3)$ (Holotrade 98cafff, P11091) in a physical vacuum? Theorem~9.8 gives the outside datum a
      name: the antiunitary coset is the generalised CP of the genus-two modulus, so the orientation is set by
      whether the vacuum value of $\Omega$ lies off the CP-real locus, and the open question becomes what
      stabilises $\Omega$.
\item \textbf{A physical $54$ chamber gate.} The literal $H_{27}$-equivariant
      $54\times54$ intertwiner is impossible (rank ceiling $36$), but the symmetry-changing
      replacement is now explicit. Four single-foliation $27$-triangle factors transport through
      both nonzero $K_{81}\to U_{81}$ cocycle orientations and remain rank $54$, with
      $S_2\oplus L$ projection ranks $27+27$ at both split primes. Thus the algebraic
      class-three transducer exists. \cert{w33\_20261001\_u81\_54d\_cocycle\_transducer.py}
      What remains is physical: derive a local Hamiltonian or optical circuit whose finite
      evolution realises one of these transported weighted factors, with an error model and
      measurable calibration protocol.
\item \textbf{Numbers.} The genus-two modulus of Section~\ref{sec:siegel} fixes the \emph{shape} of the
      flavour data but not its size. The up texture obeys an exact Weber law,
      $m_um_t/m_c^2=|\mathfrak f(\tau_1)\mathfrak f(\tau_2)|^8/4$, which every elliptic-point home misses
      (it gives at least $1$; measured $0.54$); near-product homes fit all eight quark observables, but with
      twelve parameters; the reactor angle needs $\mu$--$\tau$ breaking in the neutrino sector; and the
      strong-CP problem of the chain is open, although with CP-real theta moduli one complex vectorlike row
      suffices to fit the quark data (Pass~12005) --- a Nelson--Barr compatibility witness, not a protected
      solution. \cert{w33\_pass12005\_cp\_real\_theta\_barr.py}
      Can any mass ratio or mixing angle be computed, with an error, from a stabilised vacuum with fewer
      parameters than data?
\item \textbf{The machine.} Specify a magic-state injection circuit for the $36$ Witting
      rays with a decoder and a threshold, and build the two-qutrit photonic core.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{keyidea}
The forty points do not yet give a theory of everything. They give something rarer: a
single finite object in which quantum measurement, a light cone, clocks, $E_8$, the
octonionic Albert algebra, ten-dimensional Lorentz symmetry, a chiral spinor, matter parity
and a quantum computer are exact aspects of one structure --- with every claim checkable,
and the remaining distance to physics mapped rather than hidden.
\end{keyidea}
