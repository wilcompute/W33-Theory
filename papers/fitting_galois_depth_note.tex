\begin{abstract}
We isolate a Fitting-module mechanism for a conjectural arithmetic--geometric
minimum law, close an exact product-ring discriminator, compile a real-Galois
phase-cycle reconstruction, and exhibit two nonisomorphic order-$81$ Frobenius
rings with identical budget data and identical exact determinant depth.
\end{abstract}

\section{Fitting reduction}
Let $R$ be a DVR with uniformizer $\lambda$. If a finite cyclic torsion module
$M$ is a common quotient of $R/(\lambda^a)$ and $R/(\lambda^g)$, then
\[
 \operatorname{length}(M)\le \min(a,g).
\]
If the final permitted layer survives, equality holds and
$\operatorname{Fitt}_0(M)=(\lambda^{\min(a,g)})$. This is the correct mechanism
for a minimum law. Two divisibility lower bounds would instead imply a maximum.

\section{Product-ring witness}
For $R=(\mathbb Z/9)\times\mathbb F_9$,
\[
 v_\lambda(|R|)=24,\qquad |\mathbf P^1(R)|=120.
\]
The flat block is $F=81P-I$. A one-pair perturbation decomposes into one fixed
block and forty parity blocks. The exact witness $b=(0,\tau)$ and $c=(1,0)$ gives
\[
 v_\lambda(\det B-\det F)=24.
\]

\section{Galois phase cycle}
With $t=\zeta_9+\zeta_9^{-1}$, $t^3-3t+1=0$, and a real gap is
$A+Bt+Ct^2$. The embeddings indexed by $1,2,4$ reconstruct $(A,B,C)$. The
relative norm then recovers half the $\lambda$-valuation. Direct analog recovery
of the exact order-$81$ witness would require approximately $156$ significant
digits, motivating modular accumulation.

\section{A sharp ring collision}
The rings
\[
 \mathbb F_3[x]/(x^4),\qquad \mathbb F_3[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)
\]
have embedding dimensions $1$ and $2$ but share all current budget invariants.
Exact one-pair determinants give depth $12$ for both.

\section{Boundary}
The uniform-cover Gram and conjugate-pair norm-square identities are theorem
backed. The sharp higher-conductor minimum law remains conjectural until the
determinant-gap module and its quotient maps are constructed.
