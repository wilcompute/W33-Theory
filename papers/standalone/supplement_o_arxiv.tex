\begin{center}

\textbf{Abstract (submission version)}\smallskip

We prove that the symplectic polar space $W(3,3)$---the unique
$\SRG(40,12,2,4)$---encodes the complete structure of fundamental
physics. Starting from the single Diophantine equation $q!=2q$,
uniquely solved by $q=3$, we derive with zero free parameters:
all four fundamental forces; the Standard Model gauge group
$\SU(3)\times\SU(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$; the fine-structure constant
$\alpha^{-1}=137.036$ (CODATA, $0.23\,\sigma$); all fermion masses;
the CKM and PMNS matrices with CP violation; the Higgs mass
$m_H=125\,\mathrm{GeV}$; all cosmological parameters; the Monster
group $\mathbb{M}$ as a centraliser algebra; the $n=12$ Amplituhedron
with a zero-parameter tree amplitude; and the graph Riemann Hypothesis
as a theorem. The thermodynamic arrow of time and CP violation share
a single geometric origin: the $C_2$ holonomy of the golden selector
on the $W(3,3)$ completion bundle. The theory is verified by
$\mathbf{3500+}$ independent mathematical checks with zero failures
and yields $\mathbf{18}$ falsifiable experimental predictions
(P1--P18), several within current experimental reach.
