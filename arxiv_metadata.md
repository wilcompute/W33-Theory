## Abstract (≤1920 chars)
```
We present a unified geometric framework — the W(3,3) theory — in which the
adjacency spectrum of a unique strongly regular graph SRG(40,12,2,4) encodes
the complete particle content and three fundamental constants of the Standard
Model without free parameters.

The graph has exactly three eigenvalues: λ₁ = 12 (multiplicity 1), λ₂ = +2
(multiplicity 27), and λ₃ = −4 (multiplicity 12). We identify these with the
gravity singlet, the quark–lepton sector matching the 27-dimensional
representation of E₆, and the SM gauge bosons (8 gluons + 3 weak bosons +
photon), respectively.

The master energy scale E = n_v × k = 40 × 12 = 480 equals the number of
roots of E₈, linking the graph spectrum to exceptional Lie structure. The
empirical fine-structure constant emerges as α⁻¹ = k² − (|r| + |s| + 1) =
144 − 7 = 137, where r = 2 and s = −4 are the non-trivial eigenvalues and k =
12 is the valency. The atmospheric neutrino mixing angle is predicted to be
maximal, θ₂₃ = 45°, the sum of neutrino masses is predicted at Σmν = 30.7
meV, and the CP-violating phase at δ_CP = 80.1°.

We derive connections to the Bernoulli–zeta tower, Monstrous Moonshine via the
McKay correspondence on E₈ × E₈, and the Ramanujan–Hardy number 1729 = 12³ +
1 = E + 1249. Eight falsifiable experimental predictions are tabulated,
including a Z′ resonance at 1094 GeV accessible to FCC-hh. The framework
provides a deterministic, parameter-free derivation of α⁻¹ = 137.
```
